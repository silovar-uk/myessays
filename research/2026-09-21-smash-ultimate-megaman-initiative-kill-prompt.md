# Mega Man Initiative / Kill Conversion — Research & Writing Prompt

## Goal
Write a serious but lively essay explaining how Mega Man in Super Smash Bros. Ultimate can keep initiative, make neutral feel one-sided, and convert that control into reliable KOs.

## Research sequence
1. Verify current game version and latest balance-affecting patch from Nintendo.
2. Verify frame data for Mega Buster, Bair, Fair, Uair, Up Tilt, Fsmash, Metal Blade, Crash Bomber from Ultimate Frame Data and SmashWiki.
3. Separate universal facts from character/percent/stage-dependent confirms.
4. Research competitive player practice and current specialist footage; use community sources as practical evidence, not as primary mechanics authority.
5. Re-check every surprising claim against a second source where feasible.

## Core thesis
Do not frame initiative as "attack first."
Frame it as "make the opponent answer first."

Build the chain:
pellet -> forced response -> second question -> take space -> ledge -> Bair / confirmed Up Tilt cash-out.

## Required surprises
- The 2% pellet is strategically more important than its damage suggests.
- "One-sided" does not mean a long true combo; it means the opponent has little clean neutral restart time.
- Fsmash looks like the obvious finisher but Bair and confirmed Up Tilt are usually better conceptual defaults.
- Crash Bomber should be explained as a decision deadline.
- Metal Blade changes jobs from zoning projectile to kill-confirm bridge.

## Uneven U
For major paragraphs, move roughly:
L4 problem -> L3 analysis -> L2 situation -> L1 verified mechanic -> L2/3 reinterpretation -> L4 claim -> L5 implication.
The paragraph must end at a stronger idea than it began with.

## Editorial constraints
- Distinguish fact / interpretation / proposal.
- Do not promise universal kill percentages or guaranteed confirms without conditions.
- Explain jargon on first use.
- Use jokes from factual mismatch and escalation, not random punchlines.
- Headings should state conclusions.
- Keep the practical training plan at the end.
- End by returning to the opening question with a changed model of Mega Man.
