## 2. Research conclusion

FA should not be framed as a transfer event.

Best working definition for the article:

**FA is a system that expands the set of clubs an eligible player may negotiate with after the player exercises the right.**

Important distinctions:

- `ELIGIBILITY ≠ DECLARATION`
- `DECLARATION ≠ TRANSFER`
- `DOMESTIC FA ≠ OVERSEAS FA`
- `FA ≠ FREE-CONTRACT STATUS`
- `A/B/C RANK ≠ TALENT RANK`
- `CURRENT RULE ≠ PROPOSED FUTURE CHANGE`

## 4. Evidence classification

### A. VERIFIED OFFICIAL STRUCTURE — what FA means

NPB official explainer:
https://npb.jp/announcement/2019/fa_about.html

NPB defines:

- Domestic FA: right to conclude a player contract with any NPB club.
- Overseas FA: right to conclude a contract with domestic or foreign professional clubs.

Learning interpretation:

**The principal freedom is negotiation-market access, not automatic transfer.**

The explainer page is old, so use it for stable conceptual structure, not as sole evidence for every 2026 detail.

### A. VERIFIED OFFICIAL PROCEDURE — eligible vs declared

2025 official qualified list:
https://npb.jp/announcement/2025/fa_qualified.html
https://npb.jp/news/detail/20251022_03.html

- 104 FA-qualified players were officially listed for 2025.

2025 official declaration notice:
https://npb.jp/news/detail/20251112_01.html

- 8 of 104 qualified players declared FA.
- 4 domestic, 4 overseas.

Learning implication:

**ELIGIBILITY ≠ DECLARATION.**

### A. VERIFIED OFFICIAL PROCEDURE — declaration does not equal transfer

Official 2025 declared-player page:
https://npb.jp/announcement/2025/fa_filed.html

Official signing-agreement page:
https://npb.jp/announcement/2025/fa_signed.html

Examples of declaring and re-signing with former club:

- 東浜巨 → 福岡ソフトバンク
- 辰己涼介 → 東北楽天
- 松葉貴大 → 中日

Learning implication:

**FA DECLARATION ≠ TRANSFER.**

Staying is still a valid choice after the negotiation market opens.

### A/B. CURRENT PUBLIC GUIDANCE — domestic eligibility clock

JPBPA current transfer overview:
https://jpbpa.net/transfer/

Published table states first domestic FA eligibility:

- players under the older cohort and high-school entrants under the post-2007 draft regime: 8 seasons;
- university/social-company entrants under the post-2007 draft regime: 7 seasons;
- repeat FA: 4 seasons.

NPB official explainer also describes 145 active-roster registration days as one season, with partial seasons aggregated.

Critical wording:

Do not write:

- `大学・社会人選手はプロ7年目で必ずFA`

Write:

- `基本は登録日数を積み上げ、145日を1シーズン相当として資格時計が進む`

### A/B. CURRENT PUBLIC GUIDANCE — overseas eligibility

NPB official explainer:

- overseas FA: normally 9 registration seasons;
- special relation applies after previous exercise of FA.

2026 supporting observed case:

- 坂本誠志郎 was reported on 2026-04-12 as reaching overseas FA qualification after 9 years of active-roster registration.

Source:
https://www.nikkansports.com/baseball/news/202604120000304.html

Classification:

- eligibility structure: official historical explainer + current observed case;
- avoid pretending the 2019 explainer is itself a 2026 regulation edition.

### A. VERIFIED CURRENT PUBLIC COMPENSATION FRAME

JPBPA current transfer overview:
https://jpbpa.net/transfer/

Ranks are based on salary ordering among Japanese players in the former club:

- A: ranks 1–3
- B: ranks 4–10
- C: ranks 11 and below

C rank: no FA compensation under the published table.

A/B: monetary compensation and, depending on the selected form, player compensation can apply.

Important:

**A/B/C is not a quality rating.**

It is a salary-rank classification used in the compensation mechanism.

Do not introduce detailed compensation percentages in the beginner article unless they directly help the system question; they are easy to turn into memorization clutter and are an active reform area.

## 5. 2026 Rule Change Audit — compensation reform

### PUBLIC REPORT / NEGOTIATION IN PROGRESS

May 27–28, 2026:

- NPB and JPBPA discussed removing player compensation (commonly called 人的補償).
- replacement mechanisms including draft-related ideas were being examined.
- reporting said earliest target was the following season, not immediate current-rule repeal.

Sources:

July 3, 2026:

- NPB presented multiple replacement ideas, including special selection rights around the second or third round of the following draft.
- JPBPA expressed concern that replacements must not create a larger mobility barrier.
- no final agreement reported.

Source:
https://www.nikkansports.com/baseball/news/202607030000651.html

July 28, 2026:

- after the players association extraordinary meeting, compensation and rank design were still described as requiring further discussion.

Source:
https://www.nikkansports.com/baseball/news/202607280001186.html

Therefore as of 2026-08-31:

**Do not write that player compensation has already been abolished.**

Correct status:

**reform is under discussion; current published compensation structure remains the baseline until a new rule is formally agreed/published.**

This is the main Rule Change Watchpoint for Episode 8.

## 6. Free agency vs free-contract status

Episode 7 source:
2025 Baseball Agreement, contract reservation structure.

A non-reserved controlled player can become a free-contract player under a different institutional path.

Therefore:

- FA: earned eligibility + exercise of right;
- free-contract status: contract/reservation status creates open negotiation through another route.

Both can produce broader negotiation access, but they are not the same mechanism.

## 10. Beginner Check

A beginner should be able to explain:

- FA is not a transfer itself.
- qualification is not declaration.
- declaration is not transfer.
- domestic and overseas FA open different markets.
- service time, not merely calendar age, creates eligibility.
- re-signing with the old club is possible.
- A/B/C are salary-based compensation categories, not ability grades.
- FA and free-contract status are different paths.
- compensation rules are a current reform watchpoint in 2026.
