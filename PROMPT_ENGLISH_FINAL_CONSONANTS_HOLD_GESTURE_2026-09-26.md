# 再リサーチ用プロンプト：英語の子音を「伸ばす」のではなく「保つ」

作成日: 2026-09-26
対象記事: `english-final-consonants-hold-the-gesture`

## 目的

日本語話者が英語の語末子音、とくに鼻音を発音するとき、「日本語の撥音『ん』を一拍追加する」より「子音の調音ジェスチャーを必要な区間だけ保つ」と考えると有効ではないか、という教育的仮説を、音声学・音韻論・第二言語習得の一次資料で検証する。

## プロンプト

```text
You are a researcher working across phonetics, phonology, second-language pronunciation, and Japanese linguistics.

Research this hypothesis:
“For Japanese learners of English, it may be more useful to imagine certain English consonants as sounds/gestures to be sustained rather than as a Japanese moraic ‘ン’ appended after a vowel.”

Requirements:

1. Terminology
   - Distinguish 撥音便 from 撥音.
   - Define Japanese moraic nasal /N/, mora, English /m n ŋ/, coda, syllabic consonant, and connected speech.
   - Do not translate technical terms halfway. Give a natural Japanese term first and English in parentheses when needed.

2. Falsification first
   - Actively search for evidence against “English final nasals are longer than Japanese final nasals.”
   - Separate absolute duration, relative duration, segment position, stress, speech rate, and following segment.
   - Report study design, sample, material, and measurement method before generalizing.

3. Articulation
   - Explain how English /m n ŋ/ are physically produced.
   - Explain how Japanese moraic /N/ changes place according to context.
   - Separate phonemic representation from phonetic realization and assimilation.

4. “Hold” as pedagogy
   - Treat “hold/sustain” as a learner-facing motor-attention cue, not a literal universal duration rule.
   - Classify nasals, fricatives, approximants, stops, and affricates by what can physically be sustained.
   - Explain why stops cannot be “stretched” in the same way as /n/ or /s/.

5. Syllabic consonants
   - Research English syllabic /n l m/.
   - Include accent/style variation and schwa-containing variants.
   - Do not claim that every speaker always deletes the weak vowel.

6. Connected speech
   - Investigate coarticulation, linking, assimilation, reduction, and word-boundary timing.
   - Test whether “do not abandon the consonant gesture before the next gesture is ready” is compatible with this literature.

7. Japanese learners
   - Search Japanese-L1 English production/perception studies separately.
   - Do not infer a production error merely from contrastive phonology.
   - Distinguish evidence from pedagogical proposal.

8. Output
   - Structure each major claim as FACT / INTERPRETATION / PEDAGOGICAL PROPOSAL.
   - Provide minimal/near-minimal examples, including sum/sun/sung if reputable dictionary IPA confirms them.
   - Finish with a 5-minute practice protocol based on articulatory attention rather than exaggerated length.
   - Attach DOI, publisher, university, or academic-society links to all major empirical claims.
```

## 検証上の注意

- 「英語はストレス拍、日本語はモーラ拍」の一文だけで発音時間を説明しない。
- 「ネイティブは必ず〜」と書かない。
- 学習上の比喩と音響的事実を別ラベルで扱う。
- 「伸ばす」は長音化ではなく、調音状態を途中で捨てないという意味に限定する。
