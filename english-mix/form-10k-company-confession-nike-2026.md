# Inside the 10-K――ナイキの155億ドルから企業開示を読む

*2026年10月26日調査。Japanese-first English Mix。日本語正本と節・段落を対応させた。*

## 守｜10-Kとは、「今年はうまくいきました」だけでは済まない年次報告書

NIKE's 2026 filing contains an unusually concrete fact: about **$15.5 billion in endorsement-related contractual obligations**, including related marketing commitments. 約17億ドルが12か月以内に支払われる見込みだ。広告に登場するアスリートを、financial commitments（将来の金銭的約束）から眺める景色は少し違う。[ナイキの原資料](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm)。

But $15.5 billion is **not** NIKE's annual advertising expense, nor simply bank debt. 将来支払いが生じる契約上の義務の概算である。この違いが大切だ。**Form 10-K** is the annual filing submitted to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). 会社の経営、リスク、財務を一定の形式で開示する資料である。[SECの解説](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/how-read)。

## 守｜株式会社が情報を公開するのは、知らない人からお金を集めるためでもある

A corporation can raise capital by issuing shares. 株式を保有する人が株主で、上場株式は市場で売買される。ただし、経営者は買い手より会社の内情を知っている。この差が **information asymmetry（情報の非対称性）** だ。投資家は知らないままお金を出すことになる。

The goal is not to publish only good news. SEC reporting rules require covered companies to disclose business, financial results and material risks on an ongoing basis. Form 10-K is the detailed annual filing. **It is not a report required of every U.S. business.** 公開文書は[EDGAR（米国の電子開示システム）](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/going-public/exchange-act-reporting-registration)で探せる。

The label **10-K** is a form identifier, not a 10,000-word limit. Annual report can also mean a separate shareholder-facing publication. NIKE listed its 2026 Form 10-K on July 15, and its shareholder annual report (ARS) on July 22. 名前が似ても、両者を同一視してはいけない。[ナイキの開示一覧](https://investors.nike.com/sec-filings/annual-reports)。

## 守｜1930年代の証券法から電子公開へ、制度は不信を扱う技術として育った

The securities laws of **1933 and 1934** grew out of distrust after the 1929 market crash. 1933年法は証券の募集・販売時の開示、1934年法は流通市場の規制と継続報告の土台をつくった。SECも1934年に生まれる。現代の10-Kという様式がその年に現在の姿で完成したわけではない。[SEC法令資料](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/statutes-regulations)。

EDGAR's mandatory electronic filing phase-in began in 1993 and ended in May 1996. 2002年のサーベンス・オクスリー法（Sarbanes-Oxley Act）を受け、CEOとCFOが定期開示を認証する仕組みも導入された。Disclosure became searchable and accompanied by executive accountability. [EDGARの歴史](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/1999/01/rulemaking-edgar-system)／[2002年の制度化](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2002-128-commission-approves-rules-implementing-provisions-sarbanes-oxley-act-accelerating-periodic-filings)。

The format keeps changing. In 2020, the SEC modernized business and risk disclosures and eliminated the old mandatory five-year selected-financial-data table. 古い入門記事にその表が載っていても、現在の一律の必須項目とは限らない。A guide has its own publication date. [事業・リスクの改正](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2020/08/modernization-regulation-s-k-items-101-103-105)／[財務開示の改正](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2020-290)。

## 守｜10-Kは「何を売る会社か、何を恐れるか、いくら稼いだか」を別々に尋ねる

Form 10-K follows a common structure. **Item 1: Business** explains what the company sells and where it operates. **Item 1A: Risk Factors** identifies important uncertainties. **Item 7: MD&A (management's discussion and analysis)** explains financial results from management's perspective. 同じ会社を「事業」「不安」「実績の理由」から読む設計だ。[公式ガイド](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/how-read)。

**Item 8** contains financial statements, notes and the auditor's report. **Item 15** points to exhibits such as important contracts or subsidiaries. Some governance and compensation information may be incorporated from a later **proxy statement（委任状説明書）**. 読み切ったつもりでも、別文書への参照が残る。[公式ガイド](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/how-read)。

## 守｜利益と現金は別物。三つの財務諸表はそれぞれ違う質問に答える

Three statements answer different questions. The **income statement（損益計算書）** tracks revenue and expenses over a period. The **balance sheet（貸借対照表）** shows assets and liabilities at a point in time. The **cash flow statement（キャッシュ・フロー計算書）** tracks actual cash movement. 同じ会社を測っているが、物差しは異なる。

Imagine a ¥1 million delivery this month with payment next month. Revenue recognition and cash receipts can differ. これが**発生主義会計（accrual accounting）**を理解する入口になる。The **notes to the financial statements（財務諸表の注記）** explain accounting policies and estimates. 数字の出どころを確かめる場所だ。

## 破｜ナイキを三か所だけ読むと、売上の「横ばい」の意味が変わる

Read three places in [NIKE's fiscal 2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm). The financial statement shows revenue rising slightly from **$46.309 billion** to **$46.398 billion**. But MD&A says revenue was **down 2% on a currency-neutral basis（為替変動の影響を除いたベース）**. 「売上横ばい」は一つの読み方であって、営業実態のすべてではない。

NIKE Brand wholesale revenue increased **6%**, while **NIKE Direct fell 6%** year on year. 全体の横ばいの内側で、販売経路は逆向きに動いている。Item 1A also describes risks including competition, seasonality and currency moves. We can observe a channel shift, but we cannot claim that its strategy will succeed. [原資料](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm)。

## 破｜155億ドルの契約を見て、支出と負債を混同しないためにもう一段調べる

Back to the strange number: NIKE discloses roughly **$15.5 billion in endorsement-related obligations**, about **$1.7 billion due within a year**. 取引相手は選手、著名人、チーム、リーグなど。These are contractual future commitments, **not cash already spent**. [Item 7の原文](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm)。

A contractual commitment is not automatically the same as a recognized balance-sheet liability. 会計処理は契約内容、履行、認識の条件に左右される。Before interpreting the number as debt or annual marketing spend, cross-check the balance sheet and notes. 数字を正確に読むほど、スポンサー契約の経済的な重みが見えてくる。

## 破｜監査済みでも「SECのお墨付き」ではない。信頼の範囲を区別する

A 10-K can contain **audited financial statements**, and the CEO and CFO provide required certifications. But **the SEC does not guarantee that the report is accurate**. Management prepares the filing; an auditor gives an opinion on financial statements; the SEC oversees disclosure compliance. 監査意見は未来の事業戦略まで保証しない。[SECの説明](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/how-read)。

NIKE's auditor opines on financial statements and internal control over financial reporting. That does not mean every strategic forecast in the document has been audited in the same way. **Ask what is assured, by whom, and within what scope.** 監査を過大評価しないことが、むしろ資料への信頼を正しくする。[監査報告](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm)。

## 破｜開示は多ければ多いほど親切か。古い入門資料そのものを再点検する

More disclosure is not always better disclosure. Risk factors may be lengthy, generic, and unable to quantify each probability or loss. SEC reforms in 2020 explicitly sought to reduce repetition and improve readability. 情報の量と意思決定への有用性は同義ではない。[SECの改正資料](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2020/08/modernization-regulation-s-k-items-101-103-105)。

An older SEC guide may still mention a mandatory five-year data table. That blanket requirement was removed in the 2020 reform. The normal Form 10-K deadline is **60, 75 or 90 days** after fiscal year-end, depending on filer status. つまり、10-Kはリアルタイムニュースではない。Always check the date and the current rule. [改正の説明](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2020-290)／[10-K提出様式](https://www.sec.gov/files/form10-k.pdf)。

## 破｜再調査で分かったのは、ひとつの数字の前後に別の問いが潜むことだった

Research sequence: first the SEC's beginner guide, then the official filing requirements and amendments, and finally NIKE's actual 2026 report. 当初はRisk Factorsが主役だと思った。しかし、より発見があったのはliquidity and capital resources（流動性と資金需要）の契約義務だった。It translated business relationships into future commitments.

The next check rejected a tempting but incorrect interpretation: $15.5 billion ≠ annual ad spend. 次に損益計算書の売上と、MD&Aの為替調整後の説明を対比した。The filing is most useful when we **triangulate evidence** rather than quote one impressive figure.

## 離｜EDGARで迷ったら、まず三つの項目だけ開けばいい

Find the company in [EDGAR](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/getting-started/researching-investments/using-edgar-research-investments), filter by **10-K**, and check both the fiscal period and filing date. Start with **Item 1 → Item 7 → Item 8**, then visit risks and notes as your questions develop. 最初から全ページを読むのではなく、問いから逆引きする。

Other labels matter: **10-Q** for quarterly filings, **8-K** for certain current events, **10-K/A** for amendments, and often **20-F** for foreign private issuers. XBRL provides machine-readable tagging, and the SEC publishes financial statement data sets. ただし、データが読めることと、比較条件がそろうことは別だ。[SECの書類案内](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/getting-started/researching-investments/using-edgar-research-investments)／[公開データ](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/financial-statement-data-sets)。

## 離｜競合調査では、開示の「ない会社」をどう扱うかも重要になる

In sports tech research, many businesses will not have their own Form 10-K. 非上場企業、国外企業、親会社傘下の事業では開示義務も様式も違う。**No 10-K does not automatically imply misconduct.** Search the legal entity, parent company, and reporting status before drawing conclusions.

When available, a 10-K can reveal segment revenue, cash needs, customer concentration, acquisition accounting, and competition. これらを並べると、単なる機能比較からbusiness model analysis（事業モデル分析）に進める。ただし製品別利益など非開示の数字は、勝手に実績へ変えてはいけない。An estimate must remain an estimate.

## 離｜読むべきなのは会社の数字だけでなく、その数字が語れない範囲である

At first, the **$15.5 billion** commitment was the surprise. 調べたあとは、支払時期、会計上の認識、経営者の説明のほうが気になった。A 10-K is rich in facts, but the boundary of what it does **not** establish matters just as much.

Skepticism does not require disbelief. Check responsibility, audit scope, accounting definitions, and reporting lag. その区別を積み重ねれば、company disclosure（企業開示）は自分の判断材料になる。Form 10-K is a structured way to read a company's claims, evidence, and limits together.
