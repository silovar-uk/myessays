# なぜサメの服を着ているのか、本人にも分からない――The Business of mofusand

## From a strange shark-suited cat to global licensing, retail and animation

There is a cat wearing a shark costume. その名はサメにゃん。Why the shark suit? According to the publisher's 2024 character profile, **even the cat doesn't know**. It's an orange-and-white cat, not an actual shark. The central visual joke comes with no detailed explanation. [Publisher's announcement](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000002581.000013640.html)

A business usually wants a clear story. ブランドを広げるなら、設定や世界観をきっちり説明しなければいけないと思う。But here is a counterexample worth examining: a cat with an unexplained costume became the face of merchandise, dedicated retail stores, books, a 2026 animated series and international licensing programs.

The question changes. 「なぜサメを着ているのか」より、「how did an unexplained image become a commercial system?」が気になってくる。本稿は2026年10月16日までの公表情報を検証し、facts, interpretations and proposalsを分ける。Private contracts, total revenue and the creator's unreported intentions are not assumed.

## 1. mofusandは「サメにゃん一匹」ではなく、猫たちの表現を束ねた作品群である

**mofusand is the wider body of work; Same-nyan is one character within it.** 作品を描くのはイラストレーターのぢゅの（Juno）。The official character description presents fluffy cats in cute, slightly surreal situations, often wearing unexpected outfits. [Official character profile](https://grayparkaservice.com/pages/characters/mofusand)

There are other cats, too: シャチにゃん、ちびにゃん、さまざまな装いの猫たち。The 2024 book contains roughly 80 four-panel comics, introducing character profiles and everyday settings. [Shogakukan release](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000002581.000013640.html)

Two kinds of attachment may be operating here: **visual recognition** and **narrative attachment**. 一目見てぬいぐるみが欲しくなることと、4コマを読んで人物関係が好きになることは別の体験だ。Both are possible, but we do not have public buyer research proving which drives sales. A plausible explanation is not yet an established cause.

## 2. サメと猫の組み合わせは、設定の説明より先に視覚で理解できる

The design is easy to decode: shark teeth and fins surround a clearly recognisable cat face. 「猫なのにサメ」というズレが、文字なしで伝わる。

One hypothesis is that such **visual portability** can help a character travel across languages. セリフを完全に理解しなくても、姿は認識できる。But export performance also depends on distribution, pricing, marketing and local fandom. We cannot turn an attractive theory into a proven reason for international success.

A stranger detail remains. **The lack of explanation is itself part of the published profile.** 書籍でもアニメでも「本人にも分からない」という説明が残る。That doesn't prove the artist deliberately designed ambiguity as a marketing technique. It does mean viewers can enjoy the contrast without being handed a detailed origin story. [Book](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000002581.000013640.html) / [Anime character page](https://mofusand-anime.jp/character/samenyan/)

## 3. 歴史は「SNSで有名になった」で終わらない――売り場の形が変わっていった

An exact birth date for mofusand wasn't established in the verified first-party sources. 2021年にはサメにゃんの絵が投稿された記録があるが、first documented appearance is not necessarily the first creation. [Archived post record, secondary source](https://twiman.net/user/932946882623561728/illust/1350043584511180801)

The verifiable commercial milestones are more concrete.

- **January 2022:** Kiddy Land began mofusand pop-up shops in Harajuku and Umeda. SNSの絵が実際の店頭商品になっていた。[Announcement](https://www.atpress.ne.jp/news/4906679)
- **2022:** A temporary mofusand-themed café brought the characters into food and physical space. 商品を買う以外のファン体験ができた。[Contemporary report](https://actresspress.com/report-mofusandcafe202205/)
- **March 8, 2023:** The first official shop opened at Tokyo Character Street. The operator reported more than 360,000 followers on the creator's X account **at that time**. 現在のフォロワー数ではない。[Store release](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000075.000095831.html)
- **June 2023:** 『mofusandの英会話』 was published. According to the publisher, circulation reached 22,000 copies and four printings within approximately one month. This is a publisher-reported cumulative figure, not independently audited sell-through. [Publisher](https://www.atpress.ne.jp/news/361384)
- **April 26, 2024:** The comic-and-character book 『サメにゃん』 launched, with an additional printing announced before release. [Shogakukan](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000002581.000013640.html)
- **October 24, 2025:** A concept-focused permanent shop, mofusand TOKYO, was announced for Harajuku. 東京駅の店とは別の展開。[Juice](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000130.000074811.html)
- **January 7, 2026:** Short-form animation began streaming. 動かないイラストから、声と動きのある作品へ。[Anime official](https://mofusand-anime.jp/news/anime_key/)
- **October 13, 2026:** International publishing partnerships were announced. Books became another channel for expansion beyond Asia. [License Global](https://www.licenseglobal.com/publishing/spiralcute-international-signs-global-publishing-deals-for-mofusand-and-koupen-chan)

The order matters: **illustrations and consumer products came before the anime**. すべての人気キャラクターが「テレビアニメ→玩具」という順に成長するわけではない。Retail can help create the audience for later media, rather than only the other way around.

## 4. 作者・権利管理会社・商品会社・店舗運営会社を一つに混ぜると、商売が見えなくなる

Who owns mofusand? これに会社名一つで答えるのは危険だ。The public record shows different operational roles, not a full legal rights register.

- **Juno（ぢゅの）** — the illustrator and creative origin of the cat artwork. [Official brand profile](https://grayparkaservice.com/pages/characters/mofusand)
- **Spiralcute（スパイラルキュート）** — character management and licensing. It appears in the 2023 official-store announcement and in overseas publishing deals. [2023 release](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000075.000095831.html) / [2026 publishing](https://www.licenseglobal.com/publishing/spiralcute-international-signs-global-publishing-deals-for-mofusand-and-koupen-chan)
- **Gray Parka Service（グレイ・パーカー・サービス）** — merchandise creation, sales and operation of the official Mofumofu Market store. [Company](https://grayparkaservice.com/) / [Store operator disclosure](https://mofusand-mofumofu-market.jp/pages/contract)
- **Marimo Craft and Juice** — companies involved in the Tokyo Station official shop and Harajuku concept-store projects respectively. [Marimo Craft](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000075.000095831.html) / [Juice](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000130.000074811.html)
- **Jazwares and overseas publishers** — partners for specific categories and territories. [Toy agreement](https://www.licenseglobal.com/character/exclusive-spiralcute-international-names-jazwares-master-toy-partner)

**Copyright, trademarks and licenses are different things.** 著作権は創作的表現、商標権は商品やサービスの識別標識、ライセンスは一定条件で利用を認める契約を指す。The name on a product label does not automatically identify the ultimate rights owner.

We could not verify the underlying royalty rate, ownership splits, exclusivity clauses or creator approval rights. これらは秘密契約の可能性があり、推測で埋めるべきではない。A manufacturer, a licensing manager and a copyright owner may be different parties.

## 5. 収益は「絵が売れる」一種類ではない――誰が何にお金を払うのか

A typical licensing chain involves the creator or rights-management side, licensed manufacturers, publishers, distributors and retailers. 消費者に届くまでには、design approval, production, inventory, logistics and selling costsがある。

Some agreements use royalties, minimum guarantees, advances or territory restrictions. ただし、これらは**一般的な契約概念**であり、mofusandの秘密契約に含まれると確認したものではない。

The observable categories include merchandise, permanent and temporary stores, books, collaborations and animated media. [Official shop](https://mofusand-mofumofu-market.jp/) / [Information site](https://mofusand-info.jp/index.html) / [Anime](https://mofusand-anime.jp/news/anime_key/)

**A plush toy is one purchase but many business activities.** ぬいぐるみ一つの背後で、創作、監修、製造、卸、在庫管理、販売促進が分かれている。We found no sufficiently detailed public breakdown of mofusand's total sales, profit or animation revenue. 「人気がある」と「どの企業が儲かるか」を同一視してはいけない。

## 6. 海外進出の実態は「世界で人気」という一言より、商品分類と地域で読むべきだ

In December 2025, **Spiralcute International and Jazwares** announced a multiyear toy and collectibles partnership outside Asia for several brands, including mofusand. An initial fall-2026 rollout was planned. [License Global](https://www.licenseglobal.com/character/exclusive-spiralcute-international-names-jazwares-master-toy-partner)

On October 13, 2026, publishing deals were announced with **Penguin Random House** and other companies. Penguin Random House's agreement for mofusand covers the world **excluding Asia**. Specific books and formats have their own timetables. [Publishing announcement](https://www.licenseglobal.com/publishing/spiralcute-international-signs-global-publishing-deals-for-mofusand-and-koupen-chan)

Other licensing agents were appointed for territories including the UK, Oceania, North America, Brazil, Mexico and South Africa. 地域ごとに代理店と小売網を組むという構造だ。[Agent announcement](https://www.licenseglobal.com/character/spiralcute-international-names-licensing-agents-in-seven-markets-for-chiikawa-mofusand-and-koupen-chan)

In Singapore, Starbucks announced a local mofusand collaboration beginning August 25, 2026. これは特定市場の実際の商品展開を確認できる例だ。[Starbucks Singapore](https://www.starbucks.com.sg/menu/merchandise/mofusand-starbucks)

**Announcement ≠ launch ≠ sustained sell-through ≠ profit.** 契約の数と、売上の額と、顧客が買い続けているかは別々に測る。Global licensing is best read by **territory × product category × distribution channel × contract period**, not a single claim of worldwide popularity.

## 7. 比べるべき相手はちいかわだけではない――「先に物語があるか」という成長経路の違い

Different character brands take different routes. Some begin with anime or games; mofusand's visible chronology starts with illustration, social distribution and consumer products before short-form animation.

**The first touchpoint shapes the funnel.** SNSで知る人、店頭で一目惚れする人、4コマを読む人、アニメで初めて出会う人。These groups may overlap, but treating them as the same customer is a measurement error.

The reported 360,000-plus followers in 2023 and the reported 22,000 book copies show meaningful activity at distinct touchpoints. しかし、誰が何度購入したか、国別売上、ライセンス使用料は分からない。[Shop announcement](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000075.000095831.html) / [Book publisher](https://www.atpress.ne.jp/news/361384)

The real comparison should concern conversion, retention and licensing economics—not which fictional creature is cuter.

## 8. 批判すべき論点は「かわいい商品が多い」ではなく、誰がリスクと費用を負うかだ

**First: scarcity and collectible purchasing.** 2023年の東京駅ショップでは購入金額に応じた特典があり、8,800円以上で種類を選べないぬいぐるみバッジが案内された。[Store announcement](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000075.000095831.html)

This is documented commercial practice, **not proof of unlawful conduct or harmful addiction**. 問うべきは価格・数量・抽選条件の分かりやすさ、消費者が支出を把握できるかという設計である。

**Second: creator control at scale.** 量産と国際展開にはspeed, quality assurance and approvalsが必要になる。Spiralcute has described an artist-first approach, but public statements alone do not disclose actual approval clauses, workloads or compensation for this particular character. [Executive interview](https://www.licenseglobal.com/character/how-spiralcutes-artist-first-approach-redefines-global-character-licensing)

**Third: overextension and product quality.** 多くのメーカーに展開すると、商品の品質差、在庫リスク、ブランドの過剰露出を管理する必要がある。These are general risks, not findings that mofusand has suffered them.

**Fourth: infringement and counterfeits.** 海外展開では販売元の真偽や地域別の権利確認が必要だ。However, we found no verified primary evidence for a specific major counterfeit scandal and will not invent one.

Good criticism is not accusation by default. 何が判明していて、何が未検証かを切り分けること自体が批評になる。

## 9. 実務では「ファンが増えた」から一段降りて、接点ごとの計測をする

For a business analyst, the useful task is measurement design, not explaining away cuteness. 以下は**提案**であり、mofusandの実際の社内運用を記述するものではない。

- **Awareness:** 投稿閲覧、保存率、動画の視聴完了率、地域別接触。Follower counts are not unique buyers.
- **Product economics:** 商品種類ごとの販売数量、返品率、在庫消化率、定価販売比率。A sold-out product does not reveal unmet demand on its own.
- **Channels:** Official e-commerce, permanent stores, pop-ups and partner retailers. 売上だけでなくgross marginを比べる。
- **Licensing:** Territory, category, licensee, contract duration, approval rights and guaranteed payments. 契約台帳をつくり、更新時期と採算を確認する。
- **Customer behaviour:** First purchase, repurchase and purchases across different product types. 同じファンが何度買ったかを数える。
- **Creator sustainability:** Approval lead time, revision rates, rights-management response time. 創作側の負担を見えなくしない。

To evaluate a new shop, compare similar regions with and without a launch while controlling for seasonality. アニメの効果を見るなら、同時期の商品投入と宣伝活動を考慮する。**Correlation is not causation.** 「売上が増えた」と「その施策が増やした」は違う。

## 10. 用語を整理すると、キャラクターの「人気」と「事業」が別物に見えてくる

- **IP / 知的財産:** rights connected with works and brands; business usage often refers more broadly to the exploitable character franchise. 法的権利と商業的ブランドを混同しない。
- **Character licensing / キャラクターライセンス:** permission to use a character under specified terms. 商品区分、地域、販路、期間が重要。
- **Licensor / Licensee:** the party granting rights / receiving permission. 許諾する会社が最終権利者と同じとは限らない。
- **Royalty / ロイヤルティ:** a contract-based licensing payment; not necessarily one universal percentage. 使用料率は契約ごとに異なる。
- **Minimum guarantee (MG) / 最低保証:** a contractual payment floor; not confirmed in mofusand's private agreements.
- **Master toy partner / 主要玩具提携先:** a primary toy manufacturer or distributor for a defined territory or scope. 独占性は契約条件次第。
- **SKU / 商品種類:** the inventory unit distinguishing variants such as colour and size. 商品ごとの採算を見る単位。
- **Sell-through rate / 販売消化率:** the share of stocked or supplied units sold within a defined period. 分母と測定期間を統一する。
- **Permanent store / Pop-up store:** a long-term retail location / temporary retail activation. 費用構造と目的が異なる。

## 11. 結局、あのサメスーツは「理由がない」のではなく、事業の入口になっている

The cat doesn't know why it wears a shark suit. その一方で、事業者は誰がぬいぐるみを作り、どの国で本を出し、どの店で売るかを細かく決める。

The artwork can remain strange; the commercial architecture cannot remain vague. 作品の余白と、契約の精度は両立する。

At the beginning, the question was: *Why is this cat dressed as a shark?* 調べ終えると問いが変わる。*What makes a playful image understandable to customers, yet expandable across so many companies?*

The costume needs no reason. 世界に届けるための権利、流通、利益、責任には理由がいる。

## 出典・調査上の限界

**Research cut-off: October 16, 2026.** 公式サイト、事業者・出版社の発表を一次資料として優先し、海外契約は業界メディアによる当事者発表・取材を参照。We did not interview the illustrator, inspect private licensing contracts or survey buyers. Claims about precise ownership, revenues, buyer psychology and creator compensation are therefore left open.
