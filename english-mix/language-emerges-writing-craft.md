# Words Do Not Wait Until the Sentence Is Finished
## ことばは勝手に出てくるのか

When I write, I do not always know the whole next sentence.

最初の数文字を書く。

Then another phrase appears.

そのphraseを書いたことで、さらに次が出てくる。

It feels less like manufacturing language word by word and more like uncovering a connection that was already forming.

そこで疑問が出る。

**Do we really learn how to “generate” words when we study writing?**

Psycholinguistics gives this feeling a surprisingly concrete starting point.

2000年のeye-tracking研究では、話者が絵を説明するとき、これから名前を言う対象を見るのは、その語を口にする1秒未満前だった。

Speech does not look like a fully rendered audio file waiting for playback.

It unfolds.

言葉は、走りながら線路を敷いている。

[Griffin & Bock, “What the Eyes Say About Speaking,” 2000](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/1467-9280.00255/)

---

## 1. Language production is incremental / 全文完成後に話すわけではない

Researchers use the word **incrementality**.

話し始める前に、意味やstarting pointはある程度準備される。

But planning continues after speech begins.

2012年の研究では、how far ahead speakers plan can change depending on recent experience with structures and words.

So the process is not simply:

complete thought → complete sentence → speaking.

むしろ、

meaning → starting structure → speaking → more planning → more speaking.

The words already produced become part of the context for the next words.

一文目は思考のoutputであると同時に、二文目を生むinputにもなる。

[Konopka, “Planning ahead,” 2012](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0749596X11000957)

[“Planning in sentence production,” 2010](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2930890/)

---

## 2. “Spontaneous” does not mean “from nowhere” / 勝手に出る、は無から出るではない

A word that “just appears” still has a history.

読んだ本。

Conversations.

昨日考えたこと。

Frequently used phrases.

The sentence already on the screen.

Creative-cognition research often separates **spontaneous processes** from **controlled processes**.

連想やmind-wanderingのように、候補が自動的に浮かぶ過程がある。

Then executive control evaluates, redirects and selects.

So “words come by themselves” and “I think deliberately” are not opposites.

**Generation and selection can belong to different parts of the process.**

[“Measuring spontaneous processes in creativity research,” 2019](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352154618301396)

---

## 3. Writing changes the thought that produced it / 書くと、考えも変わる

The classic Flower–Hayes model treated writing as recursive problem solving.

Planning.

Text generation.

Reviewing.

Back to planning.

Goal changes.

Revision.

もう一度書く。

Writing is not a clean three-step conveyor belt.

文章を書いたことで、「自分はこれを言いたかったわけではない」と気づくことがある。

The visible sentence becomes something the writer can think with.

だからwriting is not only a record of thought.

**It is also a machine for producing the next thought.**

[Hayes & Flower, “Writing as Problem Solving,” 1980](https://journals.uc.edu/index.php/vl/article/view/5308)

---

## 4. So do we need to study writing at all? / 文章術はいらないのか

Here the romantic answer fails.

Writing instruction works.

Graham and Perin’s meta-analysis of writing instruction for grades 4–12 found positive effects for strategies such as explicit writing strategy instruction, summarization, peer assistance, specific product goals, sentence combining, prewriting and studying models.

2021年の別のmeta-analysisでも、初等教育のwriting interventionに平均的なpositive effectが確認された。

So this claim is too strong:

“Words emerge naturally, therefore writing craft is unnecessary.”

文章術は必要やけど、役割を別に考えたほうがいい。

It may not install a “generate beautiful sentence” button.

It gives us more ways to **guide, evaluate and reshape what emerges**.

[Graham & Perin, “A Meta-Analysis of Writing Instruction for Adolescent Students,” 2007](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.3.445)

[Kim et al., 2021 meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9390887/)

---

## 5. Maybe writing craft has three jobs / 材料・方向・選択

### Material / 材料

Read. Watch. Talk. Experience. Take notes. Think.

言葉が勝手に出るなら、出てくるcandidateを増やす。

### Direction / 方向

Who is this for?

何を残したい？

What should the reader understand or feel differently afterward?

Goal setting narrows the field of possible language.

### Selection / 選択

Do not treat the first phrase as the final phrase.

削る。比較する。具体化する。順番を変える。

Feedback research also shows that feedback aimed at deeper content and structure can improve deeper writing outcomes.

Maybe craft is less about commanding generation and more about **designing the conditions around generation**.

[“How effective is feedback for L1, L2, and FL learners’ writing?,” 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959475224000884)

---

## 6. “I can’t write” is not one problem / 書けない理由を分解する

Sometimes “I cannot write” means no material.

Sometimes it means no direction.

Sometimes the internal editor kills every sentence before it appears.

Sometimes there are too many sentences and no selection rule.

「文章力がない」で全部まとめると、diagnosisが雑になる。

Different bottlenecks need different solutions.

材料不足にtemplateを足しても薄い。

No amount of vocabulary fixes an unclear purpose.

And telling an over-generating writer to “write freely” can create even more noise.

---

## 7. Words can emerge by themselves. Good writing does not. / 勝手に良い文章にはならない

My original thought survives, but in a different shape.

ことばは、かなり勝手に出てくる。

We do not always build the whole sentence before beginning.

書くこと自体が、次の言葉や次の考えを呼ぶ。

But writing craft still matters.

ただし、学ぶのは「無から一文を生成する魔法」ではない。

**Build the reservoir. Set the direction. Select again.**

材料を持つ。

方向を決める。

出てきたものを選び直す。

That makes the blank page a little less frightening.

最初の一文は完成品ではない。

It is simply the first stone that gives the next word somewhere to land.
