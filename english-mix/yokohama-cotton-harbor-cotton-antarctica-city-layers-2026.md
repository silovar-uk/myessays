# 綿の港を調べていたら、南極とごま油と砲台が出てきた

*Research date: October 24, 2026. Desk research using public archives, city planning materials, company records and official facility information. No field measurements or new interviews were conducted.*

**Cotton Harbor. A harbor made of cotton?**

横浜の水辺にある住宅地の名前としては、ずいぶん soft だ。High-rise residences, sea breeze, white buildings. The name suggests comfort before it suggests industry.

ところが調べると、an Antarctic research ship appears. Then a sesame-oil factory. And a coastal battery from the late Edo period. えっ、綿はどこへ行った。

![浅野ドックの記念碑とコットンハーバーの住宅群（2014年）](https://yokohamasannpo.up.seesaa.net/02729123N000000001/140638551604353238228_ysan025-12.jpg)

*Photo: externally linked image from [よこはまさんぽ（2014）](https://yokohamasannpo.seesaa.net/article/201407article_4.html). Not a photograph of the site in 2026.*

This is not a story about a fake name. It is about **how a place can have several lives while presenting only one face at a time.**

## 守｜そもそもコットンハーバーという住所は存在しない

The first surprise is administrative. コットンハーバーは正式な町名ではなく、橋本町・星野町・山内町の一部を指す development-area nickname. The official city-planning unit is the **山内ふ頭周辺地区**, about **7.1 hectares**. Its plan combines offices, commerce and urban housing. [横浜市の地区計画](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/kurashi/machizukuri-kankyo/toshiseibi/plan-rule/chikukeikaku/kubetsu/kanagawa/c-062.html)

An address answers *Where is this?* A place brand answers *What sort of place should this feel like?* 住所と愛称は同じ役割をしていない。So the name alone will never tell us everything about the land beneath it.

## 守｜「コットン」は作り話ではない。昔、本当に「棉花町」があった

The cotton connection is not entirely invented. 横浜市役所文書課の1939年刊『横浜市町名沿革誌』には、神奈川区の旧町名として**「棉花町」**が載る。The archival index uses the character **棉**, not 綿. [横浜市立図書館の1939年資料](https://archive.lib.city.yokohama.lg.jp/museweb/detail?cls=collect_01&pkey=00002759)

Local walking accounts also describe the surviving bridge name, 棉花橋. But an important limit: the exact establishment and abolition dates of the old town have **not** been independently checked against the original municipal notices in this research. 地域の記事に書かれた年号を、行政の一次資料で確認済みとは扱わない。[旧町名の現地調査](https://citywalk2020.hatenablog.com/entry/2024/10/21/102100)／[市立図書館の地名調査案内](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/kurashi/kyodo-manabi/library/shiru/chimei.html)

A 2013 interview with city officials reports two sources for the new nickname: the historical 棉花町 and the notion of the pleasant texture of washed cotton. In other words, **the name translates a piece of the past into a promise about future living.** 史実の痕跡と販売・開発の構想が、ひとつの言葉に重ねられた。[はまれぽ（2013）](https://hamarepo.com/story.php?story_id=1697)

## 守｜現在の海辺は、南極へ行く船を改造した場所だった

Before high-rises, there was **浅野ドック（Asano Dock）**, a repair and conversion facility with roots in Asano's shipbuilding business. 浅野学園の史料室によれば、浅野造船所は1916年に設立され、ドックは1995年に閉鎖された。その跡地がコットンハーバー地区に再開発された。[浅野学園史料室](https://www.asano.ed.jp/historical/)

Its most unexpected job involved **宗谷（Soya）**. The ship was built in 1938 elsewhere; **it was not newly built here**. In 1956, 日本鋼管浅野船渠 converted it for service as Japan's first Antarctic research vessel. Work began with a March 12 ceremony and reached completion on October 10. The conversion included an icebreaking bow, diesel engines and a helicopter flight deck. [船の科学館・詳細な改造記録](https://nippon.zaidan.info/seikabutsu/2002/00032/contents/013.htm)／[国立科学博物館の技術史資料](https://sts.kahaku.go.jp/sts/detail.php?no=909991160001)

A waterfront housing essay has somehow become an engine-replacement story. でも、これは脱線ではない。A harbor once prepared ships for journeys outward; the same waterfront later became a place to return home every day. **The value of the location changed, not the location itself.**

## 守｜高層住宅が建っても、港の仕事はなくなっていない

The industrial waterfront has not vanished. 横浜市によると山内ふ頭は1928年着工・1932年整備。It later served overseas shipping and has also been used for recycling-related logistics. Nearby, 横浜市中央卸売市場 continues the broader area's trading and distribution functions. [横浜市港湾局「山内ふ頭」](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/kanko-bunka/minato/yokohamako/gaiyo/yamauti.html)

More unexpectedly, **岩井の胡麻油（Iwai Sesame Oil）** is based nearby. The company traces its founding to 1857, moved to Yokohama in 1893, established a Hoshinocho factory in 1914 and relocated its headquarters and factory to Hashimotocho in 2005. [同社の公式沿革](https://www.iwainogomaabura.co.jp/company)

Cotton, Antarctic ships, and sesame oil. One neighborhood. 一見笑える取り合わせだが、都市計画上はかなり真剣な問題でもある。Industrial operations and residential life need to coexist. A 2012 factory interview described rooftop deodorizing equipment installed with neighbors in mind; current equipment and actual odor conditions were **not** independently checked. [工場への取材（2012年）](https://hamarepo.com/story.php?page_no=2&story_id=1540)

The view from a waterfront apartment is only one layer. **The systems keeping a city fed and supplied are another.**

## 破｜2004年の資料を読むと、開発は「名前を変えた」だけではなかった

Let's inspect the developer's numbers instead of treating a redevelopment brochure as atmosphere. In a July 7, 2004 release, the three developers described a district of about **7 hectares**, a core residential site of about **2.1 hectares**, and **four towers with 920 planned apartments**. Their design aimed to give every unit a south-facing, sea-facing orientation. [JFE都市開発・三菱地所・野村不動産の共同発表](https://www.nomura-re.co.jp/cfiles/news/o2004070700681.pdf)

But the completed-property record now lists **926 apartments**. [三菱地所ハウスネット](https://www.mec-h.com/selection/detail/M00320)

Six apartments: a tiny difference, yet a vital distinction. **920 is a plan; 926 is the completed property's reported total.** And 7.1 hectares and 2.1 hectares describe different boundaries. 数字は大きいか小さいかより、何を数えたのかが重要になる。

The deeper transformation concerns what counts as value. For a dock, water access mattered because ships could enter. For housing, the same water became a **view**. One landscape, different economic logic. This does not prove that every initially planned amenity was completed unchanged.

## 破｜「補助金ゼロだった」という話は、土地の使い道の経済学でもある

A 2013 local interview quotes city officials explaining that the redevelopment received no city subsidy, because a change from an industrial-only land-use framework toward mixed uses helped make the project financially viable. ただし、これは当時の担当者による説明であって、本稿が全事業の会計を監査した結論ではない。[はまれぽ（2013）](https://hamarepo.com/story.php?page_no=1&story_id=1697)

Land is not valuable solely because it is close to the sea. **Permitted uses, infrastructure and market demand** shape what can be built and paid for. 都市計画は単なる建物の配置ではなく、土地の可能性を組み替える制度でもある。

Still, rising land value is not a complete scorecard. Residents, businesses, existing workers and local government experience redevelopment differently. Who benefits, who adjusts, and who maintains the public realm? その問いを省いて「成功」とは言い切れない。

## 破｜古い記事の「温浴施設跡地」は、現在の街を説明しない

Historical sources can trap readers in the past. A 2013 article described the closed INSPA Yokohama facility and uncertain future plans. それを2026年現在の状況として引用すれば誤りになる。

The official corporate history confirms that **RAKU SPA BAY 横浜 opened on December 5, 2024**, inside the INSPA Yokohama facility, with the spa area operated by the Gokurakuyu group. Its official website was posting facility information and shuttle-bus notices in October 2026. [極楽湯の企業沿革](https://www.gokurakuyu-holdings.co.jp/corporate/history.html)／[開業発表](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000103.000108434.html)／[施設公式サイト](https://rakuspa.com/yokohama/)

Redevelopment is not a one-time event with an end screen. Buildings open, businesses close, and operating models change. **A city's completion date is not the end of its story.**

## 破｜さらに隣で「次のコットンハーバー」を作っているのか

Nearby, the **東高島駅北地区（Higashi-Takashima Station North District）** has a separate land-readjustment project. City documents describe urban-planning approval in 2017 and the start of the project in 2018. A city-official interview reflecting May 2025 expected project completion in fiscal 2027, but this is a schedule, not a guarantee. [横浜市・整備状況](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/kurashi/machizukuri-kankyo/toshiseibi/toshin/keihin/higashitakahima.html)／[市担当者インタビュー](https://kanagawa.itot.jp/higashi-kanagawa/interview01)

A critical warning: this is **not the same project** as Cotton Harbor. The latter's district-plan area is about **7.1 ha**; the northern readjustment project covers about **7.5 ha**. Those figures should not be combined into one Cotton Harbor total.

Here is the hypothesis, not a forecast: if surrounding blocks become better connected, the practical meaning of the older waterfront development may change. Better walking routes could matter as much as spectacular views. だが徒歩時間や価格上昇の実測・推計はしていない。**The next development can change the usefulness of the previous one.**

## 離｜さらに時間を戻すと、横浜の海を守る砲台が埋まっていた

Go back even further, to **1860**. The Tokugawa government commissioned the Iyo-Matsuyama domain to build the **神奈川台場（Kanagawa coastal battery）**, designed by 勝海舟（Katsu Kaishū）. The city says the coastal-defense facility was later retired in 1899, and reclaimed land obscured much of its original shape. Parts of the stonework can still be seen around 星野町公園. [横浜市・神奈川台場跡](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/kanagawa/shokai/rekishi/spot/kanagawadaibaato.html)

![神奈川台場の記念碑](https://sp-ao.shortpixel.ai/client/to_webp%2Cq_glossy%2Cret_img%2Cw_1200%2Ch_900/https%3A/www.sumaitoseikatsu.yokohama/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/sumaitoseikatsu_hoshinotyokouen-14.jpg)

*Externally linked photograph: [横浜で暮らそう](https://www.sumaitoseikatsu.yokohama/archives/37090).*

The waterfront has served distinct purposes: **defending against ships, repairing ships, moving goods, and making the sea a place to live beside**. 同じ海でも、人との関係は時代によって違う。And because land reclamation changed the coastline, the boundary between sea and city is itself historical, not fixed.

## 離｜「港に住む」とは、港を手放すことなのか

A name cannot be a complete archive. 「コットンハーバー」は、棉花町の記憶を残す一方で、宗谷の改造、物流の現場、ごま油工場、砲台跡までは語らない。That is not necessarily dishonest; names always select.

Imagine the opposite: “Cotton–Dock–Antarctica–Sesame–Battery Harbor.” もう住所欄に入らない。

The question is not whether every history appears in the name. It is whether visitors and residents can still **discover the stories that did not fit**. Public memorials, archives and operating industries keep parts of those stories available.

So perhaps a better definition of successful redevelopment is not “everything old is preserved,” but “the functions of a place can change without making its past impossible to read.” これは筆者の解釈であり、開発の優劣を測定した結果ではない。

## やりすぎ調査｜名前を「棉花・造船・住宅」の三つの時代に戻してみる

For a small desk experiment, I compared three records written for different purposes: **a 1939 town-name directory, a 1956 ship-conversion account, and a 2004 residential development release**.

- **Town-name record:** 棉花町 appears as an administrative place identifier.
- **Engineering record:** ship's bow, engines, construction sequence and handover date dominate.
- **Housing document:** apartments, orientation, earthquake-resistant construction and shared amenities become the relevant facts.

Different sources, different units of attention. 資料の目的が違うため、言葉の出現頻度をそのまま時代の世論と考えることはできない。それでも、**the language of the waterfront shifts from classification to production to residential experience**. That change in vocabulary is itself a clue to the change in value.

A field visit could test the remaining questions: from public routes, record the bridge sign, dock memorial, battery stones, existing industrial frontage and pedestrian links. Compare weekday and weekend conditions without trespassing or photographing residents intrusively.

## 再検証｜分かったこと、まだ分からないこと

What is supported: 棉花町 appears in the 1939 city record; Soya was converted here in 1956; the dock closed in 1995; 920 apartments were planned in 2004 while the completed-property record reports 926; the sesame-oil factory remains nearby; and a spa-area business opened in December 2024.

What is **not** established: original municipal notices for the exact old-town dates, full project financial accounts, current pedestrian counts, contemporary industrial noise and odor measurements, or quantitative effects from the adjacent redevelopment.

2004年の予定、2013年の聞き取り、2026年の現況をひとつの現在形に混ぜない。それがこの文章の最低限の検証原則だ。

## 結び｜街は、古い名前を忘れたのではなく、別の言葉で話し始めた

At first, “Cotton Harbor” sounded like a pleasant waterfront label. 調べた後には違って聞こえる。It recalls a real historical place name, but tells only one part of the story.

The dock, the Antarctic vessel, the factory, the logistics quay and the coastal battery did not all disappear the moment housing arrived. Their traces sit at different depths, sometimes visible, sometimes preserved only in records.

**The harbor did not simply stop being one thing and start being another. Its purposes accumulated across time.**

次に白い高層住宅を見るとき、私は1956年の造船所を思い浮かべるだろう。Beneath a very soft name, the city has done some remarkably hard work.
