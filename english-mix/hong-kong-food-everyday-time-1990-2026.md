# 香港の食卓には、なぜ二つの時計があるのか
*English Mix: Two clocks at the table — fast cafés, leisurely yum cha and daily markets*

> [前編：香港はなぜ中国でありながら、中国本土と違うのか](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/#/essay/hong-kong-1990-2026-boundaries-of-autonomy)

## 要旨――香港料理とは、料理の名前だけでなく、時間の使い方である

Most food guides tell you *what to eat*: dim sum, wonton noodles, egg tarts or milk tea. でも香港の生活を理解するなら、*how people use time while eating* も重要や。A quick meal at a cha chaan teng（茶餐廳）, a shared yum cha table（飲茶）, and buying ingredients at a wet market（街市）are different social practices. Their common theme is not simply East meets West. 香港の食文化は、急ぐ時間、話す時間、家庭の食事を準備する時間が重なってできている――これが本稿の解釈の出発点になる。

## 1. 茶餐廳の奇妙な献立は、味の「混血」だけでは説明できない

A **cha chaan teng（茶餐廳／チャーチャンテン）** is a Hong Kong-style diner. Milk tea, toast, macaroni soup, instant noodles, rice dishes and pineapple buns may appear on one menu. 観光局はその系譜を1950年代の**bing sutt（冰室／ビンサッ）**にたどり、現在の茶餐廳には豊富な献立と素早いサービスという特徴があると説明する。[Tourism Board](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/food-and-drink/must-visit-cha-chaan-tengs.html)

It is tempting to call this **fusion cuisine（融合料理）** and stop there. But consider the customer: a breakfast before work, a short lunch break, or a late-afternoon snack. 一軒の店が多様な生活の時間帯を引き受けている。The menu is not only a collection of foreign influences; it also makes the diner useful in different moments of daily life. That is a plausible reading of the format, not proof that all diners operate identically. [Example of customizable noodle dishes](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-wai-kee-noodle-cafe.html)

## 2. 「パイナップルが入っていないパン」は、名前と実物のずれを教える

A **pineapple bun（菠蘿包／ボーローバウ）** does not usually contain pineapple fruit. The name points to the cracked, golden crust resembling the fruit's surface. 2024年に更新された香港の無形文化遺産目録では、甘い皮を載せて焼く製作技術そのものが紹介されている。[Hong Kong heritage inventory, p. 93](https://www.icho.hk/documents/Intangible-Cultural-Heritage-Inventory/2024/ich_inventory_2024_en.pdf) The lesson is small but useful: names, ingredients and craftsmanship are not the same category.

Hong Kong-style milk tea（港式奶茶）is not just ordinary British tea with milk. The local technique uses tea filtered through cloth and **evaporated milk（無糖練乳）**. 「絲襪奶茶」という呼び名は濾し器の見た目に関わり、実際の靴下を用いるという意味ではない。**Yuen yeung（鴛鴦／ユンヨン）** mixes tea and coffee. These drinks demonstrate how an imported habit can become a distinctly local craft. [Heritage inventory](https://www.icho.hk/documents/Intangible-Cultural-Heritage-Inventory/2024/ich_inventory_2024_en.pdf)

In 2017, Hong Kong-style milk tea making entered Hong Kong's own Representative List of Intangible Cultural Heritage（無形文化遺産の代表一覧）. これは**UNESCO's global inscription（ユネスコの世界遺産登録）とは別**の仕組みだ。The recognition says something important about everyday knowledge: a drink can be easy to buy yet difficult to reproduce without learned technique. [Hong Kong representative list](https://www.icho.hk/en/web/icho/past_representative_list_of_the_ich_inventory.html)

## 3. 飲茶は点心を食べる行為より、卓を共にする行為に近い

**Yum cha（飲茶／ヤムチャ）** literally means drinking tea; **dim sum（点心／ディムサム）** refers to small dishes often enjoyed with it. Har gow（蝦餃／エビ入り蒸し餃子）, siu mai（焼売）, and char siu bao（叉焼包／豚肉入り饅頭）are familiar examples. 蒸し物をワゴンから選ぶ古い形式もあるが、今は注文票や電子メニューの店もある。[Tourism Board: dim sum](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/food-and-drink/unmissable-hong-kong-dim-sum-restaurants.html)

At a shared table, several dishes become topics for conversation and choices made together. But **leisurely dining（ゆっくり食事すること）** is not a rule for every table or every family. 川龍の茶楼では、早朝から山歩きの人や家族が訪れる。[Choi Lung Restaurant](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-choi-lung-restaurant.html) The venue changes meaning depending on who uses it, so the value of yum cha cannot be reduced to one imagined “traditional” timetable.

## 4. 街市は、レストランの背後にある「まだ料理になっていない食文化」である

A **wet market（街市／ガイシー）** is a place to buy fresh ingredients, not a restaurant. At Graham Street Market（嘉咸街市）in Central, stalls selling vegetables, seafood and everyday groceries occupy several adjoining streets. レストランの完成した一皿では見えない仕入れ、家族の献立、売り手と買い手の関係がそこにはある。[Graham Street Market](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-graham-street-market.html)

At Chun Yeung Street（春秧街）in North Point, a tram passes through a narrow commercial street lined with food stalls. The street is photogenic, but it is also a functioning place of trade and transport. 「昔ながらの風景」と呼ぶだけで済ませると、歩行者の安全、衛生、店が続く条件を忘れる。Heritage is not merely something to photograph; it is something that people must still be able to use. [Chun Yeung Street](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-chun-yeung-street-wet-market.html)

## 5. 屋台の懐かしさは、営業できる場所が変わった歴史を隠しやすい

A **dai pai dong（大牌檔／ダイパイドン）** is a form of open-air or semi-outdoor food stall known for practical furniture and cooked-to-order meals. It should not be used as a name for every kind of street restaurant. [Tourism Board](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/food-and-drink/must-visit-dai-pai-dongs.html)

The Woosung Street Temporary Cooked Food Hawker Bazaar（呉松街臨時熟食小販市場）opened in 1984 and reopened after renovation in March 2021. 料理や屋台文化が続いても、店舗の建物や設備は変わり得る。[Official visitor guide](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-woosung-street-temporary-cooked-food-hawker-bazaar.html) That distinction matters. Preservation is not necessarily about freezing every wall and table. It may also mean providing conditions under which people can keep cooking.

Outdoor food can feel spontaneous and lively, but questions of hygiene, ventilation, safe access and working conditions remain. 問題を「規制か文化か」という二択にしない。The harder design question is how to support street-level dining without ignoring the people who live and work around it. [Government hawker information](https://www.fehd.gov.hk/english/pleasant_environment/hawker/hawker.html)

## 6. 上環の乾物と大澳の海老醤は、香港が海辺の交易都市だったことを思い出させる

Sheung Wan's Dried Seafood Street（上環の海味街）has shops selling dried scallops, shrimp, squid and fish products. These ingredients appear in soups, congee and braised dishes. 干して保存するという技術は、見栄えのよい料理より前にある、海との関係を思い出させる。[Tourism Board: dried seafood](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/jp/place-to-go/travel.guide-dried-seafood-street-and-tonic-food-street.html)

In Tai O（大澳／タイオー）on Lantau Island, stilt houses and local shrimp paste（蝦醤）reflect the area's fishing history. 都心のミルクティーも、島の海産物も、同じ香港にある。Food is a practical way to discover that a city famous for finance is also a coastal collection of neighbourhoods with different livelihoods. [Tai O Village](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-tai-o-village.html)

## 7. 香港の食卓には、広東料理だけでなく、多様な移動の履歴がある

Hong Kong's cuisine has strong Cantonese roots, but Chiu Chow（潮州）, Hakka（客家）and South Asian food traditions also form part of the city. Chungking Mansions（重慶大厦）in Tsim Sha Tsui is known for its international shops and restaurants.[Tourism Board](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-chungking-mansions.html) 「中国料理＋英国料理」という二つの箱だけでは、香港を行き来した多様な人々を説明できない。

Not every community's food becomes a celebrated “Hong Kong classic.” What appears in travel guides is shaped by media attention, location and popularity. だから「地元の味」という言葉を聞いたとき、**whose local experience?（誰にとっての地元か）**と問い直してみる。This question does not invalidate famous dishes; it shows why a longer menu can still be an incomplete social history.

## 8. 2017年から2026年の「食の保存」は、味ではなく技術の継承を問う

Hong Kong's representative heritage list included local milk tea craftsmanship in 2017. The overall local intangible heritage inventory expanded to 507 items in December 2024. 2026年の企画展では、エッグタルト、パイナップルパン、ミルクティー、豆製品の調理過程が科学的にも紹介された。[Heritage inventory update](https://www.icho.hk/en/web/icho/ich_inventory_of_hong_kong.html)／[2026 exhibition](https://www.icho.hk/en/web/icho/ICHxScience_Decoding_ICHFood.html)

A recipe is information, but the cook's movements are also **tacit knowledge（暗黙知）**. 店で培われた手順を知る人が引き継がなければ、料理の名前だけ残っても同じ作業は再現されない。Protecting food culture therefore means more than protecting an old photograph or a celebrated shop name. It also raises the question of how the next generation can keep making the food.

## 9. 守破離――香港の食文化は、都市が誰に時間を渡すかを映す

**守 / Facts.** Cha chaan tengs, dim sum teahouses, wet markets, open-air stalls, dried seafood shops and diverse migrant cuisines all coexist in Hong Kong. 提供される料理も、利用者が求める時間も一様ではない。

**破 / Interpretation.** Think of food culture as a way of **organizing urban time（都市の時間を編成すること）**. A quick lunch and a shared weekend meal meet different needs. これは観察資料から導いた概念仮説であり、客の滞在時間を統計的に検証した結果ではない。

**離 / Transfer.** When designing public spaces or services, it helps to ask not only *What is offered?* but also *What kind of time does the place allow?* 誰でも短時間で使える場所と、目的なく長居できる場所では生まれる交流が変わる可能性がある。This is a transferable question, not an established business rule.

## はじめて読む人のための用語辞典

- **Cha chaan teng（茶餐廳）**: Hong Kong-style everyday diner.
- **Bing sutt（冰室）**: earlier refreshment shop associated with the diner tradition.
- **Pineapple bun（菠蘿包）**: sweet bun named after its cracked crust, not pineapple filling.
- **Hong Kong-style milk tea（港式奶茶）**: strong filtered tea with evaporated milk.
- **Yuen yeung（鴛鴦）**: tea mixed with coffee.
- **Yum cha（飲茶）**: drinking tea with shared small dishes.
- **Dim sum（点心）**: a range of small dishes, including dumplings and buns.
- **Wet market（街市）**: fresh-food market.
- **Dai pai dong（大牌檔）**: a traditional open-air or semi-outdoor food-stall form.
- **Dried seafood（海味）**: preserved marine ingredients.
- **Shrimp paste（蝦醤）**: fermented shrimp seasoning.

## 散歩から始める再リサーチ計画

Compare (1) Graham Street Market and Sheung Wan's dried seafood stores; (2) a quick-service cha chaan teng and a teahouse; (3) a traditional open-air food format and a renovated food bazaar. 場所ごとにメニュー、席の使い方、価格、客層、利用時間を記録する。店舗の営業状況は訪問前に別途確認する。
