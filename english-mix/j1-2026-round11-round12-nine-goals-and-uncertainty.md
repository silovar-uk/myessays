# Nine goals conceded. One match, three days later.
## J1第11節・第12節――The strange week when scorelines tried to tell the whole story

*Research cutoff: October 24, 2026, before Matchday 12 begins. Round 11 is complete; Round 12 remains a preview. 事実と仮説を分けて読むためのEnglish Mix版。*

Two teams conceded nine goals on the same Wednesday. 広島は長崎に0-4。清水は町田に0-5。そして、たった中2日後の10月24日、**広島と清水が直接対戦する**。

This is not a joke fixture. It is an ordinary league schedule that looks unusually dramatic because of what happened immediately before it. しかも広島は前節まで首位、町田は5-0の勝利で新しい首位となった。The more interesting question is not who should be embarrassed, but **what a dramatic scoreline really tells us**.

## 守｜Eleven matches are not an entire season

First, define the competition. The 2026/27 J1 season runs from August 7, 2026 to June 6, 2027. 20 clubs play 38 matches each, 380 matches overall, in a home-and-away league. A win earns three points, a draw one. 第11節終了時でも、各クラブにはまだ27試合が残る。[Official competition rules](https://www.jleague.jp/outline/j1/)

After Round 10, Hiroshima, Machida and FC Tokyo all had **23 points**. 広島が得失点差で首位、町田が2位、ＦＣ東京が3位。On October 21, Machida beat Shimizu 5–0; Hiroshima lost 0–4 to Nagasaki; FC Tokyo drew 0–0 with Kashiwa. The new top three became **Machida 26, FC Tokyo 24, Hiroshima 23**. 神戸と柏は22点で続く。[Round 10 standings](https://newsdig.tbs.co.jp/articles/-/3020494?page=2) / [Official standings after Round 11](https://www.jleague.jp/j1/standings/)

But look at goal difference. Machida moved from **+15 to +20**, while Hiroshima fell from **+19 to +15**. The gap between those two clubs reversed from Machida being four goals behind to five goals ahead: a **nine-goal swing**. 9つ順位が変わったのではなく、得失点差の相対的な差が9点分動いた。A number becomes meaningful only after we say exactly what it measures.

These ten matches produced **32 goals**. Source: [J.League official match data](https://data.j-league.or.jp/SFTP01/).

## 破｜The 32-goal headline hides concentration, not an evenly attacking league

The headline is tempting: “32 goals in one matchday!” しかし、そのうち**9点は町田と長崎の2試合**に集中した。That is **28.1% of all goals in only 20% of the games**. The other eight games produced 23.

A second check matters. Official cumulative data after Round 11: **324 goals in 110 matches**. Subtract the 32 Round 11 goals and we get **292 goals in the previous 100 matches**, or 2.92 per game. Round 11 averaged 3.20. Difference: just **0.28 goals per game**. これだけで「リーグ全体が急に攻撃的になった」とは言えない。[Official J.League Data Site](https://data.j-league.or.jp/SFTP01/)

What about the two red cards? Shimizu's Matsuzaki was sent off in minute 69, **after Machida already led 3–0**. Hiroshima went down to ten players in minute 66, **after Nagasaki already led 2–0**. In both games, the advantage existed before the dismissal, but the final score includes minutes played with a numerical disadvantage. [Shimizu–Machida](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/102110/) / [Nagasaki–Hiroshima](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/102115/)

This forces a distinction: **a red card can amplify a scoreline without having caused the initial lead**. 反対に「退場がなければ何対何だった」と推定することも、試合記録だけでは不可能だ。We observed one match, not a controlled experiment with two alternative endings.

## 破｜67% possession, fifteen shots, and zero goals

Nagasaki against Hiroshima gives us a remarkable statistical contrast. Hiroshima recorded **67% possession, 15 shots and 2 shots on target**. Nagasaki had **33% possession, 10 shots and 4 shots on target**. The final score was **Nagasaki 4–0 Hiroshima**. [Official match statistics](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/102115/)

![Possession, shots and goals: Nagasaki versus Hiroshima](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/j1-2026-r11-nagasaki-hiroshima-stats.svg)

*Original comparison diagram. Full-match data includes the period after the dismissal.*

Possession is control of the ball, not ownership of the scoreboard. ボールを保有する、前進する、決定機を生む、枠内へ打つ、得点する――これらは連続した過程でも同義語ではない。A team may possess the ball for long periods without creating clear chances; another can attack less often but finish efficiently.

Does this prove that Nagasaki's tactical plan was superior in every respect? **No.** We would need the quality of chances, the locations and types of shots, game-state context and video evidence. 広島の15本のシュートは、そのまま15回の良い決定機ではない。The real insight is methodological: the more dramatic the result, the more carefully we should distinguish output from the process that generated it.

## 破｜Points and goal difference tell different versions of the same team

The table contains a smaller paradox. Urawa are **8th with 18 points**, despite a **−3 goal difference** and a record of **6 wins, 0 draws, 5 losses**. Yokohama F. Marinos are **9th with 17 points**, but their goal difference is **+4**, with **4 wins, 5 draws, 2 losses**. [Official standings](https://www.jleague.jp/j1/standings/)

Urawa win more often, but also lose more often. 横浜Ｆ・マリノスは引き分けが多く、負けは少ない。League points reward wins more than draws, while goal difference measures the net total of goals over every game. Neither metric is simply “the true measure of strength”. They answer different questions.

Tokyo Verdy take the paradox further: **0 wins, 5 draws, 6 losses** after eleven games. Against Yokohama, they rescued a 2–2 draw with goals in minutes 73 and **90+3**. Dramatic, yes. But only one league point. 第12節の相手は18位の福岡。What feels like a heroic comeback in one match may still leave a team at the bottom of the standings. [Official Round 11 summary](https://www.jleague.jp/news/article/35174/)

The standings compress different kinds of performance into a single order. 順位を見るときは、その圧縮で失われた「勝ち方・負け方・引き分け方」をもう一度取り出す必要がある。

## 離｜A Wednesday crowd is not the same measurement as match quality

Ten official crowd figures add up to **185,466 spectators** on October 21. Yokohama versus Tokyo Verdy at MUFG National Stadium drew **40,765**, about **22.0%** of the round's entire attendance. Fukuoka versus Gamba Osaka drew **6,105**. [Official attendances](https://data.j-league.or.jp/SFTP01/)

One headline might be “40,000 on a Wednesday night.” Another might be “just over 6,000 at a Wednesday game.” Which is the truth? Both are facts, neither is a complete explanation. 収容人数、場所、対戦相手、料金、告知、特別開催かどうかを統一せずに比較しても、「集客力の差」と「条件の差」は分けられない。

The sporting spectacle and the live event are connected, but not identical. 横浜Ｆ・マリノスは2点差を追いつかれてドロー、東京Ｖは未勝利のまま。それでも4万人以上が足を運んだ。The league exists both as a competition measured in points and as a public event measured in attendance, experiences and local engagement.

## Matchday 12｜Ten matches as ten tests, not ten predictions

Rather than predicting winners, this preview asks: **which observation could change what we think we learned from Round 11?** 日程は[公式第12節一覧](https://www.jleague.jp/j1/match/search-list/?enddate=2026-10-25&period=custom&startdate=2026-10-24)を確認した。

**Saturday, October 24**

- **14:00 Hiroshima vs Shimizu:** A combined nine goals conceded on Wednesday. 退場がなかった時間帯の守備をどう修正するか。
- **15:00 Kashima vs Kashiwa:** Sixth versus fifth, only two points apart. Direct competition in a crowded upper table.
- **15:00 Tokyo Verdy vs Fukuoka:** Twentieth versus eighteenth. Can Verdy turn draws into a first league win?
- **15:00 Kobe vs Machida:** Fourth versus first. Is Machida's five-goal performance a repeatable pattern or an extreme outcome?
- **16:00 Gamba Osaka vs Kyoto:** Gamba ended their winless run; Kyoto rescued a late draw. A test of continuity, not just one emotional result.

**Sunday, October 25**

- **14:00 Nagoya vs Yokohama F. Marinos:** Both gave up a lead on Wednesday. What does protecting a lead look like?
- **15:00 Urawa vs Nagasaki:** The difference between winning frequently and scoring efficiently. 長崎の4得点は再現されるか。
- **15:00 Cerezo Osaka vs Chiba:** Both without a win in multiple matches. Identify the mechanism, not simply the streak.
- **16:00 FC Tokyo vs Mito:** FC Tokyo are unbeaten in ten, with six consecutive clean sheets; Mito have lost five straight. How does an apparent favorite actually create chances?
- **16:00 Kawasaki vs Okayama:** Both won on Wednesday. Look for repeatable attacking patterns rather than treating victory itself as the explanation.

## Rechecking the claim｜A big scoreline is evidence, not an experiment

The research moved in stages. First: “The former leader lost by four.” Second: “The red card changed the conditions.” Third: “But Hiroshima were already two goals down before the red card.” Fourth: “And they still finished with 67% possession and no goals.” Each additional fact removes an overly convenient explanation.

For further analysis, the workflow should be concrete: compare events **before and after the dismissal**, check five-match trends, classify shots by chance quality, and compare Round 12 with the hypotheses written **before kickoff**. 必要なら想定得点（expected goals / xG）も確認する。ただし今回は計測値を取得していないため、数字を作らない。

Verified here: official scorelines, standings, event timelines, match statistics, attendance and fixtures, plus arithmetic recomputation. Not verified here: full-video tactical coding, controlled estimates of red-card effects, or individual chance quality. **An observed correlation is not a proven cause.**

## 離｜The next match starts at 0–0, but our expectations do not

Hiroshima and Shimizu carry nine conceded goals in the historical record, but their next match kicks off at **0–0**. Their points and goal differences remain; the conditions that produced Wednesday's scorelines do not automatically repeat.

That is the real reason this accidental fixture is fascinating. 私たちは大差を見ると、その試合の結果をチームの恒常的な実力に読み替えたくなる。But a score is an outcome, not an entire model of performance.

By the end of the research, the strange thing was no longer that two heavily beaten teams were meeting three days later. The stranger habit was ours: **expecting the next 90 minutes to behave like a replay of the last 90.**
