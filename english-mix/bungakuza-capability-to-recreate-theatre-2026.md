# 文学座はなぜ続くのか――Recreating Theatre Across Generations

**Research date: November 10, 2026.** This is a Japanese-based English Mix essay, not a full translation. 事実は公式資料、演劇史の事典、関係者への取材、公開された資金調達記録に基づく。Interpretations and proposals are explicitly separated from evidence.

### Abstract

Why does a theatre company survive for nearly ninety years? 名作や名優を数えるだけでは答えにならない。A script can survive on paper, but a performance must be made again by living people. 文学座には本公演に加えて、experimental productionsを担うアトリエ、trainingを担う研究所、long-term supportersを結ぶ支持会がある。Yet none of these structures automatically guarantees financial stability. 1963年の分裂と2021年の経営危機が、その単純な物語を崩す。本稿の問いは “What has been preserved?” から “Who can create the next performance, and under what conditions?” へ進む。

## 1｜守：Shingeki was once a movement to make theatre new

The word 新劇（shingeki, modern Japanese theatre）sounds old today. But its origin was a challenge to existing theatrical conventions. 明治末期以降、Western modern plays and new forms of everyday dialogue entered the Japanese stage. 新劇は一つの思想や演技様式だけで説明できないが、生活感情や人物の内面を演劇で表現しようとした。その運動が後に「伝統」と呼ばれる。This gives us a paradox: **a tradition can begin as an act of renewal.** [Source: Performing Arts Network Japan](https://performingarts.jpf.go.jp/article/76780/)

In September 1937, 岸田國士、久保田万太郎、岩田豊雄（獅子文六）founded Bungakuza. Their stated ambition was to create theatre close to the feelings of contemporary people. 創立は日中戦争の開始と同年であり、theatre was being created under mounting political pressure. 作品の芸術性を重視する立場は、politics had no influence on their choices という意味ではない。The founding ideal becomes more interesting when understood as a series of difficult artistic choices, not just a noble slogan. **An institution reveals its principles through what it chooses to stage.** [Official history](https://www.bungakuza.com/about_us/index.html)

## 2｜守：A canonical play survives by being reinterpreted, not merely repeated

A repertory play is not a recording on repeat. 森本薫の『女の一生』は1945年に初演され、杉村春子と文学座を象徴する作品として戦後も上演された。The text can remain stable while the actors, audiences, and social context change. 一つの戯曲が別の時代に新しい問いを投げるなら、repertory is not just preservation. It is the ability to reinterpret inherited material in public. **Continuity may depend on making the familiar unfamiliar again.** [Bungakuza history](https://www.bungakuza.com/about_us/index.html)

Film and theatre preserve different things. 映画では撮影された映像自体が作品として再生される。一方、舞台では台本が同じでも俳優と観客がその都度上演を成立させる。A filmed performance is valuable evidence, but it does not fully transfer rehearsal judgment or responsiveness to another performer. This is why an acting company cannot be understood only as an archive of plays. **What must endure is also embodied expertise—the practical ability to act, direct, and collaborate.**

## 3｜守：The Atelier places experimentation inside the institution

Tradition does not necessarily eliminate experimentation. 文学座は1949年にアトリエ公演を始め、1950年に信濃町のアトリエを完成させた。The official record lists productions of Beckett's 『ゴドーを待ちながら』 and Ionesco's 『犀』 in 1960, followed by works associated with つかこうへい in the 1970s. 老舗の劇団の内部で、当時の前衛劇が上演されていた。That matters because experimentation was not simply outsourced to people outside the company. **The harder question is whether experimental work can influence the institution's main repertoire and practices.** [Atelier history](https://www.bungakuza.com/about_us/atelier1.html)

The Atelier resembles a research-and-development function, but that is an analogy, not proof of financial return. 小規模公演が多いだけで、innovation が生じたとは限らない。To test the claim, trace which Atelier productions were revived, which directors gained later opportunities, and whether new methods reached main-stage or regional productions. This moves the evaluation from “How many experiments?” to **“What knowledge and artistic possibilities moved forward?”**

## 4｜守：The Institute produces people whose value may spread beyond Bungakuza

Training can create public value without keeping all the talent in one organization. 文学座の俳優教育は1938年に始まり、途中に閉鎖や再開を経て、現在につながる附属演劇研究所が1961年に設けられた。Its official history reports more than 4,000 alumni, including 樹木希林、松田優作、桃井かおり、大塚明夫、内野聖陽、長谷川博己. But celebrity alumni are not causal evidence of training quality by themselves. Selection, individual effort, and subsequent experience also matter. **Educational impact and financial benefit to the school are different outcomes.** [Institute history](https://www.bungakuza.com/newinstitute/aboutus.html)

The 2027 intake will introduce a one-class undergraduate training system, according to the Institute's announcement. 研究生一人ひとりに深く向き合うことが理由として示されている。Whether that improves career outcomes, or changes costs and enrollment, remains unverified. Still, the decision raises a serious management question: does growth always mean teaching more students, or can a smaller cohort create better learning? **Measure not just how many graduate, but what capabilities travel with them into theatre and other media.** [2027 policy](https://www.bungakuza.com/newinstitute/67ki.html)

## 5｜守：The two ruptures of 1963 prevent a heroic survival story

Longevity is not evidence that every internal decision was wise. 1963年1月、芥川比呂志や岸田今日子ら29人が文学座を離れ、劇団「雲」の結成へ進んだ。同年11月には、三島由紀夫の『喜びの琴』の上演中止をめぐる対立が起こり、三島と複数の団員が去った。The two ruptures had distinct circumstances and should not be reduced to one motive. Creative freedom, casting, governance, and artistic judgment may pull an organization in different directions. **Survival tells us that a company continued; it does not settle who was right.** [Historical encyclopedia](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%96%87%E5%AD%A6%E5%BA%A7-128323)

This tension is familiar in creative businesses and even technology companies. A star creator may expand an organization's reputation while questioning its decision rights. 対立を単なる「人間関係の問題」と片づければ、institutions lose an opportunity to examine how disagreement is governed. However, retrospective management language cannot replace careful historical evidence. **The transferable question is how a mission-driven group can make room for disagreement without dissolving its common work.**

## 6｜破：A supporters' association may sell continuity, not just admission

A theatre ticket buys access to one performance. A supporters' association can form a relationship with an organization before its next play is known. 文学座支持会は1938年に始まり、1947年には会員600人を数えた。The 2026 A membership costs ¥16,000 and includes invitations, early booking, and a monthly newsletter. This is not identical to buying a season of individual tickets. It may help turn an occasional audience into long-term participants. ただし、会員が全員、新作の挑戦を支持しているとは断定できない。**Repeat purchasing and support for institutional continuity are related but different forms of commitment.** [Supporters' association](https://www.bungakuza.com/supporters.html)

COVID-19 made that distinction visible. 公演中止、座席制限、観客減少に直面した文学座は、2021年に資金調達を行い、820人から22,772,000円を集めた。The completion report states that the funds helped cover maintenance and production costs. The result shows support beyond ordinary ticket transactions, but one successful campaign does not prove recurring financial sustainability. **Emergency solidarity and ordinary operating economics must be evaluated separately.** [Fundraising results](https://readyfor.jp/projects/bungakuza) / [Completion report](https://readyfor.jp/projects/bungakuza/accomplish_report)

## 7｜破：Different activities operate on different time horizons

One accounting period cannot capture every type of value a theatre company produces. 公式の組織案内には、公演制作、俳優の映像出演のマネジメント、研究所、会報、ワークショップが並ぶ。The upcoming 90th-anniversary production 『みつ豆』 lists a general ticket of ¥7,000, U-30 tickets at ¥4,500, school-age admission at ¥1,000, and ¥3,000 gift-ticket contributions to widen access. 公演には当期収入以外の目的が組み込まれ得る。Yet price differentiation alone does not establish that new audiences were acquired. **Separate current revenue, future audiences, and future creators before claiming that one activity subsidizes another.** [Company structure](https://www.bungakuza.com/guide.html) / [Production information](https://www.bungakuza.com/mitsumame/index.html)

The public organization page lists approximately 170 actors and 40 people in directing and stage-related departments, with 鵜山仁 as company representative. これらは常勤社員数と同義ではなく、公開資料から部門別利益率も分からない。An attractive story—“screen appearances pay for experimental theatre”—would be easy to write but is not evidenced here. **Multiple revenue-related functions do not, by themselves, demonstrate cross-subsidy or financial resilience.**

## 8｜破：The name “Shingeki” carries both credibility and a label

A trusted tradition can become a restrictive category. In 2024, five Bungakuza directors—松本祐子、五戸真理枝、西本由香、稲葉賀恵、生田みゆき—discussed their careers and the meaning of 新劇 in an interview by the Japan Foundation. 外から「新劇」という一つのラベルを与えられることと、内部で多様な演出を行うことは必ずしも一致しない。The same name that signals accumulated technique can obscure individual artistic differences. **Institutional reputation is both an asset and a filter through which outsiders misread the present.** [Interview part 1](https://performingarts.jpf.go.jp/article/74791/) / [part 2](https://performingarts.jpf.go.jp/article/76780/)

It would be premature to prescribe a “younger marketing campaign.” 先に調べたいのは、未観劇者が文学座という名前から想像するものと、実際の公演後に感じるものの差だ。If expectations and experience diverge, messaging alone may not solve the problem. The design of the first encounter matters. **Renewal may require more ways to reveal contemporary work, not a denial of the past.**

## 9｜離：A theatre company resembles an academy, a sports club, and a craft workshop

A sports club has competitive matches, youth development, fan communities, and experiments in play. 文学座の本公演、研究所、支持会、アトリエとの対応は考えやすい。But actors are not athletes, and performance rights, employment relationships, and training compensation work differently. The analogy is useful only if it sharpens the same question: **How can short-term public performance coexist with long-term capability building?**

Universities add another distinction: the institution's economic benefit is not the same as the social value of its graduates. 大学出身の研究者が別の場所で成果を出しても、教育が無意味だったとは言えない。The same may apply to an actor who leaves a troupe and works in films. Yet the school still needs funds to operate. **Organizations that send capabilities into society must explain both their public contribution and their own financial sustainability.**

Consider craft knowledge as well. A manual can preserve procedures, but not every judgment made in an unfamiliar situation. 舞台上で相手の呼吸に応じる演技、医療現場の判断、顧客の微妙な反応を読む仕事には、written rules と practiced judgment の両方が要る。Therefore the key is not merely recording expertise. **It is creating settings where successors can practice, make decisions, receive feedback, and become independent.**

## 10｜The 90th anniversary asks who can make the next play

Bungakuza's first announced 90th-anniversary production is 『みつ豆』, scheduled to open on November 28, 2026, written by 秋之桜子 and directed by 高橋正徳. 秋之桜子は俳優・山像かおりとして文学座に所属した後、2017年に退団している。An alumnus returning as an outside playwright suggests that the company's creative relationships need not end at its formal boundary. That does not yet tell us whether the production will succeed commercially. **The meaning of an anniversary lies partly in whether past relationships can become new work.** [Official production page](https://www.bungakuza.com/mitsumame/index.html)

Why has Bungakuza lasted nearly ninety years? We can document plays, people, the Atelier, the Institute, and the supporters' association. しかし、それぞれが存続へどれだけ寄与したか、因果的な強さを示す資料は足りない。It is a mistake to turn survival itself into an explanation. The stronger question is forward-looking: **Who will inherit the judgment, skills, audiences, and resources required to create the next performance?**

## 11｜Proposal：Five tests of continuity

以下は確認済みの施策ではなく、research proposals である。

1. **Separate business economics.** 公演別収支、研究所費用、映像出演関連の収入、助成、会費、寄付を同じ年度で比較し、cross-subsidyを推測で決めない。
2. **Measure audience continuity.** First-time attendance, repeat visits, membership retention, and age profilesを追い、discounted admissionと長期的なファン化を区別する。
3. **Trace creative transfer.** アトリエ公演の作品・作家・演出家が、reprises, main-stage productions, tours, or other companiesにどう展開したか確認する。
4. **Follow graduates, not only enrollment.** 研究所の修了、所属、外部活動、職業的自立を追い、2027年の一クラス制の前後を比較する。
5. **Test the gap between brand and experience.** 未観劇者の印象と初観劇者の評価を聞き比べ、伝統が支持を生む場面と制約になる場面を分ける。

These tests would help us replace a comfortable story with a falsifiable one. 老舗だから強い、名優がいたから残った、という説明だけでは、次の時代の意思決定に使えない。**Cultural continuity is not simply about where old works are stored. It is a decision about how to keep people, places, and resources capable of making new work.**
