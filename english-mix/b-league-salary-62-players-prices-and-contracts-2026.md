# 1億円プレーヤーが62人いるのに、年俸の上限は8億円？
### The Price of Talent — salary bands, contract risk and a strange transparency puzzle

**Research / writing: November 11, 2026.** 日本語原本を基にしたEnglish Mix。Confirmed data and hypotheses are kept separate.

Imagine opening a company salary sheet and finding 62 people in the “¥100 million or more” bracket. まず入力ミスを疑い、次に経理の人の顔を思い浮かべる。But this is a real number in Japanese professional basketball. しかもクラブごとの選手報酬には原則8億円の上限がある。Sixty-two players above ¥100 million, yet a ¥800 million cap per team? 電卓を持ち出したくなる。

On November 10, 2026, Bリーグ（B.LEAGUE）published the first salary distribution for its new top tier, Bプレミア（B.LEAGUE PREMIER）. [The official report](https://www.bleague.jp/files/user/2026-27_salary.pdf) also covers contract duration, each club's aggregate pay, and spending rules. 年収ランキングのように見えるが、実はもっと深い資料だ。It shows how an industry prices talent, discloses information, and limits competition for talent.

## 0｜電卓をたたく前に、二つの数字を分ける

First, 62 players is a league-wide count across 26 clubs. Divide it by 26 and you get about 2.4 per club, but actual team-level distributions are undisclosed. The ¥800 million cap, meanwhile, applies **per club**. 全体の62億円と各クラブの8億円を直接比較するのは、分母が違う。No contradiction is required.

Second, the “62 players” statistic counts only **fixed base pay**. Signing fees, housing and travel allowances, and performance incentives are excluded. Club salary-cap totals cover a wider set of fixed costs. A multi-year deal uses that season's amount; short deals are not automatically annualized. 同じ「年俸」という言葉でも、what gets counted is different. The small notes on [pages 6 and 10 of the league report](https://www.bleague.jp/files/user/2026-27_salary.pdf) matter.

This is useful far beyond sport. A company's average base salary is not its total labor cost; bonuses and benefits make the definitions diverge. **Before comparing numbers, ask exactly what is being added up.** 地味だが、ここを飛ばすと議論の土台がなくなる。

## 1｜守：給料は、頑張った量に貼られる値札ではない

Why do clubs pay athletes so much? Not simply because they run farther or practice longer. Clubs compete to sign scarce talent that may help them win and attract fans and sponsors. こうした選手とクラブの取引の場を**労働市場（Labor Market）**という。Pay reflects bargaining, scarcity, age, contract terms and alternatives—not just personal ability.

A player with the same scoring output may be more valuable to a team that urgently needs that role. Roster restrictions can also shape the value of Japanese-registered players. ただし、1億円以上62人のうち日本人登録が7人という数字だけでは、差別や実力差は証明できない。We would need group sizes, playing time, positions and performance.

Be careful with the phrase **market value**. It is the price of acquiring someone's services under particular conditions, not a measure of the person's worth. 年俸という値札が見えても、人の価値の全部が見えたわけではない。

## 2｜守：そもそも、なぜ十年前には難しかったのか

Commissioner Shinji Shimada recalled that ¥20 million salaries were unusual a decade ago. The history matters. Japanese men's basketball once had separate corporate-team and professional leagues, including the bj League launched in 2005. 統括体制の問題を背景に、2014年には日本バスケットボール協会が国際バスケットボール連盟（FIBA）から資格停止処分を受けた。After reform, the unified Bリーグ began on September 22, 2016. See the [official history](https://www.bleague.jp/corporate/about/history/).

Ten years later, all 55 clubs together recorded ¥90.4 billion in operating revenue in 2025–26; adding league revenue of ¥10.9 billion gives about ¥101.3 billion. That is roughly five times the inaugural scale of ¥20.1 billion. **Revenue is not profit.** アルバルク東京の約92.4億円には新アリーナ関連事業も含まれ、単純なチケット売上と同一ではない。[Financial reporting](https://www.bleague.jp/media_news/detail/id=666263) explains the scope.

New revenue streams include food, premium seating, corporate services and arena events. だから高額報酬は、事業規模の拡大と切り離せない。But rising revenue can still be outweighed by operating costs. **A bigger industry does not automatically mean profitable clubs.**

## 3｜守：八億円の天井と五億円の床

From 2026–27, the league's B.革新 reform created Bプレミア, Bワン and Bネクスト. Entry and continuing eligibility increasingly depend on business scale, attendance and arena conditions, not simply on league position. [The official criteria](https://www.bleague.jp/new-bleague/examination/) show the shift. 従来型の自動昇降格をなくしても、規定違反による降格の可能性は残る。

The reform introduced a **選手報酬総額の上限制度（Salary Cap）** and **最低投資額制度（Salary Floor）**. For Bプレミア, the limits are generally ¥800 million and ¥500 million. The goal is **戦力均衡（Competitive Balance）**: prevent a rich team from signing everyone, while discouraging extreme underinvestment. 最低年俸は原則800万円で、新人には別の規定がある。See the [official salary-cap guide](https://www.bleague.jp/new-bleague/system/salary-cap/).

The ¥800 million figure is not a universal ceiling on every yen actually received by every athlete. Under the **star-player exception**, one top-paid player earning at least ¥150 million may count as ¥150 million for cap purposes. 実際の支払額、報酬階級を決める基本報酬、上限計算上のみなし報酬は別々の数字だ。Rules also define how contracts are counted.

## 4｜数字を見直すと、年俸の高い人より多い人がいる

Here are all 350 players across six base-pay bands: 62 at ¥100 million or above; 29 at ¥80–100 million; 21 at ¥60–80 million; 29 at ¥40–60 million; 81 at ¥20–40 million; and 128 below ¥20 million. That makes 222 players, or 63.4%, above ¥20 million. Yet the largest single band is **below ¥20 million**. 派手な数字が主役のニュースでも、人数の中心は別にある。

![Players by base-pay range in B.PREMIER: 128 below ¥20m, 81 at ¥20–40m, 29 at ¥40–60m, 21 at ¥60–80m, 29 at ¥80–100m, 62 at ¥100m+.](/myessays/assets/b-league-salary-2026/distribution.svg)

What is a typical player's salary? Positions 175 and 176 in the sorted list fall in the ¥20–40 million band. Thus **the median lies within that band**, not necessarily at ¥30 million. We cannot calculate the mean either because the top band has no disclosed maximum. データがない部分を想像で埋めない。

At opening, the highest club-level deemed fixed-pay total was about ¥821.24 million for Altiri Chiba; the lowest was about ¥501.62 million for Shiga Lakes. But exceeding the cap at this snapshot does not mean an immediate sanction. Injury replacements can be handled under special provisions, and final accounting comes after the season. 「上限」という一語より制度運用は複雑だ。

## 5｜破：全員が同じ天井を見たら、強いクラブは何を買うのか

Now the question flips. If every club faces the same pay ceiling, will competition become fair? Not entirely. Richer organizations can still invest in coaches, analytics, medicine, training facilities and travel. **Equal player-pay rules do not create equal conditions for performance.** 報酬の上限が効くほど、別の投資先が重要になる可能性もある。

Consider the athlete's side. When clubs compete through higher offers, players gain bargaining power. A cap may protect club finances and competitive balance, yet it may also suppress workers' pay relative to a growing industry. スター選手条項などの例外が公平性を高めるのか、かえって格差を残すのかは検証が必要だ。

Here is a hypothesis, not a finding: when salaries become constrained, clubs may compete more on playing opportunities, development, facilities, contract security and family life. ただし小規模クラブの育成には時間と失敗の余地が必要だ。Competitive balance may depend on **investment beyond the salary line**.

## 6｜破：年俸表の隣にあった、もっと不穏な表

The table that held my attention longest was not the salary table. It was contract duration. Of 350 Bプレミア players, **214 (61.1%) have current contracts scheduled to expire during 2026–27**. Another 81 are contracted through the next season, 45 through the following one, and 10 through 2029–30. 満了予定は移籍確定を意味しない。Still, the official [page 12](https://www.bleague.jp/files/user/2026-27_salary.pdf) puts **when** beside **how much**.

Consider two offers of ¥30 million per year. A one-year contract and a three-year contract do not distribute uncertainty in the same way. Athletes gain or lose security; clubs gain stability but may bear performance and injury risk. 契約書は金額の表であると同時に、**将来のリスク配分表**でもある。

Who holds the right to end or extend a deal also matters. These are **選択権（options）**, and a player-held right differs from a club-held one. The league disclosed individual durations and certain rights, while keeping individual salary amounts private. つまり今回公開されたのは、高給取りの名簿ではなく、次の交渉がいつ起こり得るかを考える材料でもある。Effects on negotiations remain untested.

## 7｜離：スーパーの値札より、求人票の給与欄に近い

Leave sport for a moment and look at a job advertisement. One employer says “¥4–8 million”; another says “negotiable.” Applicants enter those conversations with different information. When one side knows more about market prices, economists call it **情報の非対称性（Information Asymmetry）**. 給与相場の開示は、その差を一部縮める。

Economist ゾーイ・カレン（Zoë Cullen）shows in [her review of pay transparency](https://www.nber.org/papers/w31060) that the effects depend on **who sees whose pay**. Within a firm, transparency may narrow gaps but sometimes dampen average pay; across firms, it may strengthen job search and bargaining. **More transparency does not automatically mean higher wages for everyone.**

We cannot transfer those findings directly to Bリーグ. Here, individual salaries remain private, but aggregate salary bands, club totals and individual contract durations are visible. 相場を知らせ、個人の金額は伏せるという公開の仕方だ。**Some negotiations may benefit from privacy; others may suffer from missing information.** Both are plausible.

At first I saw the report as proof that players earn more. Now I am curious about who designed the disclosure and whose decisions it changes. Numbers may do more than describe a market; they may help reshape the next negotiation. 求人票の給与欄も、そう見れば単なる説明文ではない。

## 8｜どう検証すれば、このニュースが経営の知識になるか

For consulting practice, replace “Are salaries high?” with **“Where does new revenue go: athletes, facilities, or operations?”** Compare club revenue, salary-cap totals, win percentage and attendance, and calculate a carefully defined compensation-to-revenue ratio. ただし、2025–26年の売上と2026–27年開幕時点の報酬を、同じ年度の実績として割ってはいけない。Align the period and business scope.

Then compare clubs with similar spending but different results. Does payroll cause wins, or do successful clubs attract sponsorship? ここでは**相関と因果**を分ける。Track contract expiries, injury replacements and star-player exceptions too. The league's planned September 2027 final disclosure should help show the gap between opening estimates and season-end totals.

Set up falsification tests. If high spend does not predict results, a simple money-wins theory is weak. If disparities remain under the cap, investigate coaching and development. If disclosure does not change bargaining, perhaps aggregate bands are insufficient. 調査とは、最初の印象を正当化する作業ではない。It is a way to find where our explanation breaks.

## 9｜結局、一億円の選手は何を教えてくれたのか

At first, I wondered whether 62 expensive players could possibly fit inside a ¥800 million box. It was a mismatch of units. The deeper puzzle is institutional: a league caps pay, sets a floor, publishes contract durations, but keeps each person's salary private. 競争させたい。しかしクラブの破綻は避けたい。評価したい。しかし個人情報も守りたい。The rules hold those tensions together.

The next time I see a job posting, I may look beyond the number: who knows it, who doesn't, and when can the deal be renegotiated? Pay reflects work, but also information, contract terms and competition. **Sixty-two ¥100 million players turned out to be an invitation to study how prices are made.**
