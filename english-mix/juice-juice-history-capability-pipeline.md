# Juice=Juice史――「歌えるグループ」は、どう世代交代できたのか
## 研修生新ユニット、初期5人、段原瑠々、宮本佳林卒業、井上玲音、合同オーディション、11人体制から読む「能力を再生産する組織」

### 要旨

Juice=Juice acquired a reputation as a **strong vocal group** very early.

宮本佳林、高木紗友希、金澤朋子。Later, 植村あかり developed substantially, and 段原瑠々 joined in 2017.

But that explanation is not enough.

If Juice=Juice's strength had depended only on several unusually gifted original members, the identity should have weakened as 宮本、高木、金澤、植村 left.

Yet in 2026, no original member remains. The official lineup is led by 段原瑠々 with 井上玲音 as subleader, and the group is still described externally as highly skilled vocally.

So what survived?

本稿の答えは、**the singers did not survive; the environment that keeps producing singers did.**

---

## 1. 2013――It began as an adaptive unit, not a finished product

2013年2月3日、つんく♂ announced a new unit centered on Hello! Project Kenshusei.

宮本佳林、金澤朋子、宮崎由加、植村あかり、高木紗友希、大塚愛菜の6人。

The key detail is that this was not presented as a permanently fixed final lineup.

公式コメントでは、適性を再確認し、**additional members or reductions were still possible** before the unit became fully formalized.

So Juice=Juice began as an extension of training and selection, not as a sacred fixed roster.

大塚愛菜 left in July, and the major-debut lineup became five: 宮本、金澤、宮崎、植村、高木。

That flexible origin matters when we later look at repeated additions.

---

## 2. 「ロマンスの途中」――Skill became a requirement embedded in the songs

The 2013 major debut 「ロマンスの途中」 defined the early Juice=Juice image quickly.

It was not simply cute idol pop. The group was asked to handle an adult, funk/R&B-leaning track with detailed vocal distribution despite being mostly teenagers.

初期インタビューでも、高木は声の太さと声量、宮本は歌とステージ表現を自分の武器として語っていた。

The important point is not to separate two causes too neatly:

**Were difficult songs given because the members were strong singers, or did repeated difficult songs create a strong-vocal culture?**

The answer is both.

Hard songs are assigned.

Strong singers become visible.

Others try to catch up.

Then the next song assumes a higher baseline.

That feedback loop became part of the group culture.

---

## 3. The original five were not one standardized voice

It is tempting to explain early Juice=Juice through 宮本佳林 and 高木紗友希 alone.

But the group worked because the voices were different.

宮本 had precision and a sharp idol-pop tone.

高木 brought weight, projection and ad-lib power.

金澤 had a lower, sensual tone.

植村 gradually developed a larger vocal presence.

宮崎 functioned as the group leader and stabilizer.

つまり、early Juice=Juice was not about making everyone sound identical.

It was about **arranging different voices inside one demanding repertoire**.

That later made succession easier.

The group did not need to find “the next Karin Miyamoto.”

It could redistribute musical roles across a new combination of people.

---

## 4. 2017――段原瑠々加入 as the first major capability transfer

2017 brought the first major expansion.

梁川奈々美 joined while continuing Country Girls activities, and 段原瑠々 was promoted from Kenshusei.

段原 had already been known for singing ability and won the Best Performance Award at the 2014 Kenshusei public test.

But this was more than simple reinforcement.

In 「Fiesta! Fiesta!」, her voice was immediately placed at a crucial entry point in the song.

The new member was not hidden at the edge of the formation for years.

Instead, **the existing vocal brand was placed on the newcomer almost immediately**.

That is a powerful training mechanism.

Expectation comes first.

The newcomer has to grow into it.

At the same time, incumbent members must redefine their own roles around a new strong capability.

---

## 5. 2018–2019――More members did not mean weaker identity

稲場愛香 joined in 2018.

工藤由愛 and 松永里愛 joined in 2019.

A small high-skill group often risks dilution when headcount increases.

Juice=Juice instead used expansion as **capability portfolio growth**.

稲場 strengthened the dance side.

松永 developed distinctive rhythm and phrasing, informed by interests in R&B, jazz and 1970s rock.

工藤 accumulated live experience as a younger member.

2019 live reporting described not only 金澤、高木、宮本、段原, but also 稲場 and the newly joined 工藤・松永 as having clearly differentiated strengths.

The architecture changed from “a few vocal aces plus others” toward **a group where multiple specialties participate in the same high-performance environment**.

---

## 6. 宮本佳林卒業――The first major identity test

宮本佳林's graduation was announced in 2020.

As one of the group's symbolic figures from formation, her departure raised an obvious question:

**Can Juice=Juice still feel like Juice=Juice without Karin?**

COVID-19 delayed the timing, and she remained until the December graduation concert.

During this transition, 井上玲音 was announced as a new member after Kobushi Factory ended.

The official announcement explicitly said that before full participation, she would train in dance and vocals together with Juice=Juice members and staff.

This sequence matters.

**Do not wait for the expert to disappear before searching for a replacement. Put the next person inside while the existing standard is still embodied by the group.**

That overlap period is where tacit knowledge moves.

---

## 7. 2021――Unexpected departure tested distributed capability

In February 2021, 高木紗友希 ended her activities with Juice=Juice and Hello! Project.

It was sudden and affected planned releases and events.

If the group's musical identity had been locked to one exceptional voice, this kind of departure could have broken the architecture.

But by then, 段原、井上、松永 and others were already inside the system.

This does not mean an individual loss was small.

It means the group had already reached a point where **capability was distributed enough to survive an unplanned exit**.

A strong organization should value stars without making the organization equal to one star.

---

## 8. 2021 joint audition――The vocal culture connects to recruiting

The 2021 joint audition for Juice=Juice and Tsubaki Factory created another important step.

Juice=Juice added 入江里咲 from the open audition, while 有澤一華 and 江端妃咲 were promoted from Kenshusei.

This shows that the next generation did not have to come through one pipeline.

Open audition.

Kenshusei promotion.

Transfer from another group.

Multiple entrances could feed the same organization.

The common element was what happened **after entry**: every member was connected to the Juice=Juice repertoire, rehearsal expectations and live standard.

So the system could be broad at the entrance and demanding after entry.

---

## 9. 2022–2025――Continuous intake without brand dilution

石山咲良 and 遠藤彩加里 joined in 2022.

川嶋美楓 joined in 2023.

林仁愛 joined in 2025.

By 2026, the group has eleven members.

That is more than twice the five-member major-debut lineup.

At this scale, a fixed-image group might become unrecognizable.

But 植村あかり made an interesting observation in the 2023 tenth-anniversary interview.

Earlier, she felt the members had to fit themselves to the songs. Later, she increasingly felt that **songs were arriving that matched the people now in the group**.

That is a sign of a mature capability pipeline.

At first, people catch up to the repertoire.

Later, new people expand what the repertoire itself can become.

The culture is no longer only preserved. It becomes generative.

---

## 10. 植村あかり卒業――The day the original lineup reached zero

植村あかり graduated on June 14, 2024.

At that point, no original Juice=Juice member remained.

The new leader was 段原瑠々.

She was not a founder. She joined in 2017.

That is precisely why the moment matters.

The organization moved from **original members training newcomers** to **a former newcomer training the next newcomers**.

The succession loop completed one full cycle.

At the graduation concert, 段原 spoke about carrying forward the sense of invincibility she had felt from her seniors.

Culture cannot be transferred by documents alone.

It moves through rehearsals, line distribution, mistakes, corrections, backstage routines and shared stages.

Juice=Juice repeatedly created those **overlap periods** between generations.

---

## 11. A “strong vocal group” does not mean everyone sings the same way

Calling Juice=Juice a vocal group can become misleading if it implies uniformity.

Not everyone has the same range, volume, ad-lib style or tone.

段原 brings stability and control.

井上 brings versatility.

松永 brings rhythmic nuance.

有澤 brings a distinct musical background.

Younger members bring new physicality and new tones.

The strength is not sameness.

The strength is **making difficult material work through differentiated expertise**.

In 2026, the eleven-member group performed 「クラクラ・クライマックス」, written by Kohmi Hirose and described as a highly difficult song. 井上玲音 also explicitly acknowledged that the group is evaluated for its vocal ability.

The proof of continuity is not that the current lineup sounds identical to 2013.

It is that **people still give this group difficult songs because they expect the group to solve them**.

---

## 12. ℃-uteとの違い――Leaving a standard vs reproducing a standard

In the previous ℃-ute history, the survival model was a performance standard that continued after the group ended.

Juice=Juice is different.

℃-ute left a completed benchmark that later members can test themselves against.

Juice=Juice keeps operating and repeatedly produces people who can meet a high benchmark from inside the organization.

So the survival model here is:

**`capability_pipeline_survives`**

It means:

1. Strong seniors and newcomers overlap
2. Difficult repertoire is assigned to newcomers too
3. Multiple recruiting routes remain open
4. Expertise is distributed rather than locked to one ace
5. Former newcomers become leaders
6. New capabilities reshape future songs

The system survives because capability can be reproduced.

---

## 13. 結論――What survived was not one voice

Juice=Juice history contains many symbolic voices.

宮本佳林。

高木紗友希。

金澤朋子。

段原瑠々。

井上玲音。

And new voices keep entering behind them.

But the most important inheritance was not any one person's voice.

> **It was the expectation: “If you enter this group, you are expected to become capable of singing at this level.”**

Lessons, line distribution, senior-junior overlap, live performance and difficult songs carry that expectation from generation to generation.

That is why Juice=Juice can remain recognizable after the original members reach zero.

People possess capabilities.

Strong organizations can **produce capability again**.

Juice=Juice did not merely preserve vocal skill as an outcome.

It built a system that could reproduce it.

---

## Key Moments

1. **2013 Formation** — Adaptive Kenshusei-centered unit
2. **2013 Major debut** — High vocal demand becomes part of the brand
3. **2017 段原瑠々・梁川奈々美加入** — First major generation connection
4. **2018 稲場愛香加入** — Capability portfolio expands toward dance
5. **2019 工藤由愛・松永里愛加入** — Young members enter an already high-standard environment
6. **2020 宮本佳林卒業／井上玲音加入** — Exit of a symbol overlaps with entry of a new vocal resource
7. **2021 高木紗友希活動終了** — Distributed capability is stress-tested
8. **2021 Joint audition** — Open audition plus Kenshusei promotion
9. **2022–2025 Continuous intake** — 石山、遠藤、川嶋、林
10. **2024 植村あかり卒業** — Original members reach zero; former newcomer becomes leader
11. **2026 Eleven-member group** — Difficult songs continue to be assigned to the new-generation lineup
