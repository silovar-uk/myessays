# バフェットはなぜぬいぐるみを売るのか――JazwaresのTwo-way Licensing

## From licensed toys to owned IP: acquisitions, contracts, litigation and profit

Investors gathered to discuss capital allocation. しかし会場で売れていたのは、投資信託でも書籍でもなく、ふわふわのぬいぐるみだった。At Berkshire Hathaway's 2023 annual meeting, 10,000 Squishmallows reportedly sold within hours. バフェットとチャーリー・マンガーを模したものもあった。[Jazwares掲載の報道](https://www.jazwares.com/newsroom/100-million-squishmallows-sold-in-a-year-how-the-toy-sensation-joined-warren-buffetts-conglomerate/)

Why would an investment conglomerate sell plush toys? The answer is not that Buffett personally launched a toy business. 2022年、Berkshire acquired Alleghany, and Alleghany already owned the toy company Jazwares. The more interesting question is what kind of company Jazwares actually is. **It licenses brands from others—and licenses its own brand to others.** この双方向性こそ、本稿の中心である。[Berkshire filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067983/000095017024019719/brka-20231231.htm)

Sources are checked as of 16 October 2026. Facts, interpretations and proposals are kept distinct. 未公表のroyalty rate、brand-level earnings、acquisition returnsは推測で補わない。

## 1. Jazwaresは玩具をつくる会社だが、経営の中心には他社と自社の「権利」がある

Jazwares was founded in 1997 in the United States. Its products include plush toys, action figures and costumes. Its portfolio features Squishmallows, Pokémon, Hello Kitty, Star Wars, Fortnite and AEW. ただし、**a brand in its portfolio is not necessarily a brand it owns.**[Official company overview](https://www.jazwares.com/about-us/our-story/)／[Brands](https://www.jazwares.com/brands/)

Two roles must be separated. **Licensee:** Jazwares receives permission to make products using someone else's character. **Brand owner / licensor:** Jazwares owns a brand business, as with Squishmallows, and can allow outside companies to develop other product categories. 同じ売場でも、cash flows and contractual obligations are different. 誰が権利を持つかで、ビジネスモデルが変わる。

## 2. 創業者は法律家だった――人気キャラクターを扱う商売に権利の知識が役立つ

Founder Judd Zebersky previously practiced law. In 1997, he left his legal career and traveled to China to learn toy design and manufacturing. His wife Laura, also a lawyer, joined in 2005. 「JAZ」が社名の由来と紹介されている。[Company history](https://www.jazwares.com/about-us/history/)／[Founder interview](https://jazwares.com/newsroom/one-of-the-worlds-largest-toy-companies-grows-footprint-in-broward-county/)

A toy needs two blueprints: **a physical blueprint and a contractual blueprint**. 前者は素材・形・安全性・量産。The latter answers which character may be used, in which territory, in which category and for how long. 法律家出身だから成功した、という因果までは分からない。しかし玩具の開発能力とrights managementは、どちらも欠かせない。

## 3. 2014年から2022年までの買収史は、商品ではなく事業の組み合わせを変えた

The ownership timeline is precise. Alleghany acquired 30% of Jazwares in 2014 and raised its stake to 80% in 2016. Berkshire then acquired Alleghany in October 2022. つまり、Berkshire did not buy Squishmallows as a standalone brand in 2022.[Alleghany SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/775368/000119312517052180/d308070d10k.htm)／[Berkshire SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067983/000095017024019719/brka-20231231.htm)

Jazwares also acquired businesses. In 2019, it bought Wicked Cool Toys, which operated popular licensed-toy programs. In 2020, it acquired majority stakes in Kellytoy entities, whose brands included Squishmallows. **Squishmallows was not originally invented by Jazwares.** 買収によって自社保有ブランドを獲得した。[2019 acquisition](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/alleghany-capital-corporation-announces-jazwares-acquisition-of-wicked-cool-toys-300929189.html)／[2020 acquisition](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/alleghany-capital-corporation-announces-jazwares-acquisition-of-kellytoy-301033662.html)

Interpretation: acquisitions changed the mix of licensed products and owned IP. ただし、各買収のfinancial contributionは確認できない。Strategic fit is a hypothesis, not a measured acquisition return.

## 4. スクイッシュマロの正体は「柔らかいぬいぐるみ」だけではない

Squishmallows launched in 2017. Each character has a name and personality; designs include animals, food and imaginary creatures. Softness matters, but **collectibility and character identity** also help explain why consumers might want more than one.[Brand expansion statement](https://jazwares.com/newsroom/jazwares-expands-squishmallows-consumer-products-program-and-grows-preeminent-global-lifestyle-brand/)

In 2023, Jazwares reported more than **200 million units sold cumulatively**, over **2,000 characters**, and **65-plus license partners**. These are historical measures, not 2026 annual results. 累計販売数量を売上高と混同してはいけない。[2023 announcement](https://jazwares.com/newsroom/jazwares-expands-squishmallows-consumer-products-program-and-grows-preeminent-global-lifestyle-brand/)

In 2021, Jazwares launched the global Squishmallows consumer-products licensing program. Other companies would turn the brand into apparel, stationery, books and lifestyle products. **The toy became an IP platform.** 売る側であったメーカーが、別の会社にブランドを使わせる側にもなった。

## 5. ポケモンとスクイッシュマロのコラボは、異なる権利を重ねる実験だ

Consider Pokémon Squishmallows. Pokémon is an external IP; Squishmallows is a brand business inside Jazwares. The collaboration was announced in 2022. 一つの商品に、**licensed IP plus owned brand identity**が重なっている。[Official collaboration](https://jazwares.com/newsroom/two-pop-culture-sensations-squishmallows-and-pokemon-join-forces-epic-plush-collaboration/)

This does not reveal the royalty split. 契約料率は非公開であり、自由に想像して計算すべきではない。The key lesson is that collaboration is an agreement between rights, not merely a combination of pictures.

Territories also matter. Jazwares signed a Sanrio toy licensing deal covering specified American markets and later expanded the Hello Kitty and Friends program into EMEA. **EMEA means Europe, the Middle East and Africa.** 日本で著名なキャラクターでも、Japan rights and EMEA rights need not belong to the same licensee.[Sanrio 2023 agreement](https://www.jazwares.com/newsroom/jazwares-named-master-toy-licensee-sanrio-global-sensation-hello-kitty-and-friends-north-america/)／[2026 expansion](https://jazwares.com/newsroom/jazwares-partners-with-sanrio-to-expand-its-hello-kitty-and-friends-range-to-emea/)

## 6. ちいかわの海外展開にも登場する――日本のキャラクターを誰が届けるのか

In 2026, Spiralcute International and Jazwares announced a multi-year deal for **Chiikawa, Mofusand and Koupen Chan**. Jazwares would act as master toy partner and distributor **outside Asia**. The announcement scheduled initial Chiikawa and Mofusand products for fall 2026 and Koupen Chan for spring 2027. 発表上の予定であって、すべての発売実績の確認ではない。[Official announcement](https://www.jazwares.com/newsroom/spiralcute-international-announces-master-toy-partnership-with-jazwares/)

The IP owner controls the characters; the toy partner brings product development, retail distribution and supply-chain capability. 海外展開の仕事は翻訳だけではない。**Having a beloved character and delivering products at scale are different capabilities.**

## 7. 競争の焦点は「柔らかさ」にまで独占権を認めるかという難問になる

In 2024, Jazwares and related companies sued Build-A-Bear over its Skoosherz plush line, alleging infringement of Squishmallows' distinctive appearance. Build-A-Bear denied the claims and argued that soft, pillow-like plush toys already existed in the market.[AP report](https://apnews.com/article/188b5760d9217cbac25890d06dcf592d)

The legal concept is **trade dress**: a product's overall appearance may function as an indicator of commercial origin under certain conditions. It is not identical to owning a character's name or logo. 丸い形や手触りのどこまでを一社の出所表示として保護できるのかが、争点になった。

In August 2025, the parties agreed to dismiss the dispute with prejudice. Settlement details were not disclosed. **The case ended; it was not a final court ruling that every allegation was true.** この区別を外すと、法律の説明が誤る。[Reuters, 25 August 2025](https://www.reuters.com/business/squishmallows-maker-build-a-bear-call-truce-plush-toy-legal-battle-2025-08-25/)

## 8. 売れた玩具はいつまでも売れるわけではない――2025年減益と2026年の回復

Berkshire's 2025 annual filing identified lower Jazwares sales volumes and supply-chain cost pressure among reasons for weaker consumer-products performance. But a new reporting period tells a different story: its first-half 2026 filing said Jazwares' revenue and pre-tax earnings improved year on year, helped by higher sales, gross-margin improvement and tariff refunds. **Performance must be time-stamped.**[2025 annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067983/000119312526083899/brka-20251231.htm)／[2026 half-year filing](https://filings.tools/brk-b/10-q/2026-06-30)

Neither filing gives a complete brand-by-brand profit statement here. スクイッシュマロの累計ヒットとJazwares全体の直近の利益を同一視しない。One bestseller does not eliminate earnings volatility.

## 9. 批判すべきは製品の存在ではなく、知的財産、流行、素材に伴う負担の説明不足だ

Three business risks deserve attention. **Licensing dependence:** a licensee relies on another IP owner's permissions. **Demand volatility:** collectible products can be vulnerable to shifting tastes and inventory mistakes. **Material impact:** mass-produced plush toys require raw materials, transport and eventual disposal. これらは事業構造から考えられるrisk categoriesであり、具体的な違反や損害の証明ではない。

Jazwares' FAQ states that Squishmallows' fabric and filling use polyester fibers. That raises questions about material use, reuse and end-of-life treatment, but it does not itself quantify environmental damage or prove a comparison with other brands. 評価には生産量、寿命、回収率などが必要だ。[Official material FAQ](https://jazwares-support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13296808174605-What-are-Squishmallows-made-of)

Collectibility also raises consumer-protection questions: limited releases and social-media promotion can encourage repeated purchases. ただし、この一般論からJazwares固有の違法行為を断定しない。Criticism should identify a mechanism and evidence, not simply label a popular toy as harmful.

## 10. ライセンスを理解するための九つの用語

- **IP / 知的財産**: copyrights, trademarks and other protected interests; there is no single universal “character IP right.”
- **Licensor / 権利許諾者**: the party granting defined rights to another company.
- **Licensee / 使用許諾先**: the party that receives permission to commercialize the property.
- **Master Toy Licensee / 主要玩具商品化許諾先**: the main authorized toy partner for defined categories or territories, not necessarily the owner of the IP.
- **Royalty / 使用料**: consideration for using licensed rights, sometimes based on sales.
- **Minimum Guarantee / 最低保証料**: a contractual minimum payment where such a clause exists.
- **Territory / 販売地域**: the geography within which a license applies.
- **Trade Dress / 外観の出所表示**: potentially protectable overall product appearance that indicates commercial origin.
- **Sell-in and Sell-through / 流通向け出荷と小売販売**: shipments into retail versus purchases by end customers. 数字は単位と測定点を確認する。

## 11. スポーツとエンタメの実務では「何の商品を売るか」より「何の権利をどこまで持つか」を確認する

**Proposal.** Imagine merchandising a football club mascot. Before drawing the plush toy, build a **rights and responsibilities sheet**. 誰が商標・著作物を管理し、どの会社が製造し、どの販売店に卸し、誰が在庫や返品を負担するかを明確にする。

Record nine things: rights owner; product category; territory; contract term; exclusivity; royalty or minimum guarantee; quality responsibility; inventory responsibility; approval and termination rules. Then measure retail sales, returns, inventory turnover, repeat purchases and rights-holder revenue. **A good licensing deal is measurable, not just visually attractive.**

Compare three cases: A, license an outside mascot to make products; B, allow another company to commercialize your own mascot; C, combine two properties. All three may produce plush toys, but economics and control differ. 企画会議では、A・B・Cを同じ「グッズ売上」として処理しないことが第一歩になる。

## 12. バフェットのぬいぐるみは、何を売っていたのか

Return to Berkshire's annual meeting. An investor bought a very soft toy. しかしその商品は、買収の歴史とrights managementの仕組みも背負っていた。A toy company had become part of a conglomerate, while a former licensee built a brand it could license to others.

The deeper lesson is not that all companies should own characters. **Creating stories people care about and delivering products that carry those stories are distinct economic capabilities.** 前者を持つ会社と後者を持つ会社は、ときに競争し、ときに契約で結ばれる。Jazwaresが示すのは、その両側に立つ経営の姿である。
