# ザ・ピーナッツは、デビュー盤でピーナッツを売っていた
## Two voices linking jazz, television, Mothra and Cold War culture

**October 18, 2026.** This English Mix follows the canonical Japanese article. 事実と証言、研究者の解釈と筆者の推論を分けている。Source numbers correspond to the list at the end.

## 1. ピーナッツが、ピーナッツを売っていた

There is a joke hiding inside a historical record. A duo called **The Peanuts（ザ・ピーナッツ）** debuted on record in April 1959 with 『可愛い花』. The B-side（裏面）was 『南京豆売り』, meaning *The Peanut Vendor*. ピーナッツという名前の歌手が、自分たちのデビュー盤の裏面でピーナッツを売っている。It's an exceptionally tidy first impression.[1]

But this was not simply a novelty written for the duo. 『南京豆売り』came from the Cuban song 『エル・マニセロ（El Manisero）』, based on the cry of a street vendor. The U.S. Library of Congress documents important recordings in 1927 and 1930. The later recording helped launch a craze for Cuban rhythms in the United States. 日本の新人歌手の小さなレコードには、キューバからニューヨークまで続く音楽史が入っていた。[2][18]

One critical distinction: no original selection memo was found proving that this B-side was deliberately picked as a pun on the group's name. 洒落として成立することと、選曲の理由が洒落だったことは別だ。That limit does not spoil the discovery. It directs us toward a bigger question: how did a Japanese vocal duo become a crossroads for music from so many places?

## 2. そもそもザ・ピーナッツとは誰か

The Peanuts were identical twin sisters Emi Ito（伊藤エミ、本名・伊藤日出代）and Yumi Ito（伊藤ユミ、本名・伊藤月子）. Both were born in 1941. They debuted in 1959, retired in 1975, and died in 2012 and 2016 respectively. 「昭和の双子歌手」という一言より、まずこの具体的な活動時期が必要だ。[3][10]

They sang 『可愛い花』『情熱の花』『ふりむかないで』『恋のバカンス』『ウナ・セラ・ディ東京』and 『恋のフーガ』. But they also performed jazz, Latin numbers, film music and television comedy. 歌謡曲は日本の大衆歌の広い呼称であり、一つの厳密な音楽様式ではない。The duo's importance becomes clearer when we think about their capacity to move between styles instead of assigning them one genre.[12]

Their career coincided with the rapid growth of television in Japan. テレビ、ラジオ、劇場、レコード店が互いの人気を支えるようになっていった。The Peanuts were not simply songs on discs; they were recurring presences in a new system of popular entertainment.

## 3. 名前を付けたのは、テレビ局の人だった

Before becoming The Peanuts, the sisters performed in Nagoya as the Ito Sisters（伊藤シスターズ）. In 1958 they caught the attention of musicians in Shin Watanabe's band, the Six Joes（シックス・ジョーズ）. Who exactly noticed them first is narrated differently by later accounts. 宮川泰や渡辺晋、ジミー竹内らが関係していたが、「最初の発見者は一人」と決めるだけの根拠は揃っていない。[4][13][19]

After moving to Tokyo, they lived with Shin and Misa Watanabe（渡辺晋・美佐）and received intensive training. Even their stage name has an unexpected author: according to the agency's historical account, television producer Takayoshi Ihara（井原高忠）named them **The Peanuts**. 発見する、育てる、名前を付ける、出演の場を用意する――別々の人たちの仕事が一組のスターを作った。[5][6]

Training young performers can offer protection and opportunity while also creating constraints on everyday life. 寄宿と育成の歴史から、当時の本人たちが何をどこまで自由に選べたのかを断定することはできない。The agency history is good evidence for its organizational activities, not a complete record of the two women's private decisions.

## 4. 「双子だから息ぴったり」では、音楽の説明にならない

Twins are not born as a perfectly rehearsed choir. We need three basic terms. **Unison（斉唱）** means singing essentially the same melody at the same pitch. **Harmony（和声）** concerns relationships among simultaneous notes. **Counterpoint（対位法）** organizes independent melodic lines that can move together. どれも「同じ顔だからできる」という仕組みではない。

In a firsthand interview, arranger Hiroshi Miyagawa（宮川泰）said he was impressed by the sisters' pitch, tone and harmony even before their Tokyo training. 彼はレッスンでの歌の覚え書きにも驚き、名古屋ですでに優れた指導を受けていたと考えた。Other recollections describe the sisters developing their ability to read music after arrival. Musical intuition and musical literacy are related, but distinct, skills.[4][13]

A useful listening exercise: play 『恋のバカンス』 once for the lyrics, once for the precise endings of phrases, and once for the timing of breaths. これは音源を分離して計測した実験ではなく、聴くときの観察手順の提案だ。That effortless sense of two voices acting as one can now be understood as the product of detailed coordination, not simply a biological gift.

## 5. 表面は米国のジャズ、裏面はキューバの豆売り

The A-side, 『可愛い花』, was adapted from 『小さな花（Petite Fleur）』 by American jazz musician Sidney Bechet（シドニー・ベシェ）. The original was instrumental. Giving it Japanese lyrics and two singers changed the way the melody worked. 外国曲の再演を「元の曲の複製」と呼ぶと、言葉と声による創作を見落とす。[1][22]

Two key practices made this possible. **Lyric adaptation（訳詞）** reshapes words so a piece can be sung in another language. **Arrangement（編曲）** determines instrumentation, introductions, textures and the interaction of melodic parts. 原曲を一語ずつ訳すことも、楽器をそのまま再現することも必須ではない。The Peanuts' voices became part of the arrangement itself.[4][22]

The group later recorded Japanese originals such as 『ふりむかないで』and 『恋のバカンス』, but foreign songs never disappeared from its repertoire. 海外の音楽を輸入する時期が終わり、国産曲だけになったという単純な話ではない。Adaptation and original composition kept informing each other.[12][13]

## 6. テレビの30分には、何人の仕事が入っていたのか

The television program 『シャボン玉ホリデー』ran from 1961 to 1972. Its entertainment combined songs, dancing, comedy sketches and conversation, with The Peanuts and Crazy Cats among the central performers. 彼女たちはカメラの前で一曲歌うだけでなく、番組という作品を毎週成立させる役割を担った。[7]

The Broadcast Library preserves a very concrete record for April 19, 1964: 『コーラス万歳！ピーナッツ！』, airing from 6:30 to 7:00 p.m. The archive identifies music by Miyagawa, choreography by Hidetaku Koido（小井戸秀宅）, direction by Taro Saito（齋藤太朗）, and production by Chikashi Akimoto（秋元近史）. 一見軽い30分番組の裏側には、複数の専門職の分業があった。[7]

Cow Brand Soap（牛乳石鹸）sponsored the program. The company's own advertising history documents this involvement and its use of television personalities. **Sponsorship（番組提供）** is a system where commercial funding and broadcasting work together. 石鹸を売る企業の目的と、面白い番組を作る目的が、同じ30分に存在した。The Peanuts were central to the new relationship between mass entertainment and advertising.[8]

## 7. 事務所は、歌手の紹介役から何を作る会社へ変わったか

Watanabe Production's timeline records a sequence of institutional moves: starting the agency, developing television production, creating master recordings, and building music publishing. **A master recording（原盤）** refers to the specific recorded performance and related rights; it is not the same as the copyright in a song's words and composition.[5][21]

Think of the chain: scout, train, choose songs, arrange, produce a television appearance, sell records, then reuse the music in other media. 人を紹介するだけの事務所と、番組や音源を作る事務所では、収益構造も交渉力も変わる。The Peanuts were both performers in and important examples of that institutional transformation.

Yet company growth does not automatically prove individual freedom or well-being. 本人たちの契約や日々の意思決定を今回直接確認できたわけではない。An account of successful business integration should not be mistaken for a complete account of what the performers experienced.

## 8. 『モスラ』は、双子の歌手を誘拐して見世物にする物語だった

In the 1961 movie 『モスラ』, The Peanuts played the tiny twin fairies called Shobijin（小美人）. They are abducted from Infant Island, forced into a public show by an exploitative promoter, and sing to summon Mothra for rescue. 怪獣映画の中心には、歌い手を誰が支配し、誰のために歌わせるかという問題がある。[9]

The story does **not** prove that the real-life sisters were treated like the fictional victims. 現実の芸能事務所と映画の悪役を同一視するのは根拠がない。Still, the fact that the film stages entertainment itself as a potential form of exploitation is worth noticing.

Historian Michael Bourdaghs（マイケル・ボーダグス）connects the film to nuclear testing and Cold War tensions, including changes between early story ideas and the finished film. 本多猪四郎監督の公式解説も、核実験の影響を受けた島の設定を記している。Behind the fantasy of a giant moth lies a political world of unequal power.[9][10]

## 9. あの不思議な『モスラの歌』と、異国趣味の問題

The music of 『モスラの歌』was composed by Yuji Koseki（古関裕而）. Studies discuss its links to Indonesian language and to Japanese ideas of a distant island. 歌詞の名義や翻訳過程については資料による記述の違いもあり、制作過程の原文書をすべて照合したわけではない。[9][10]

**Exoticism（異国趣味）** describes a way of making cultural distance seem beautiful or mysterious. 好奇心を持つこと自体への批判ではない。しかし「誰が異文化を説明し、どんな歴史を省略し、誰が利益を得るのか」という問いが必要になる。

The 1955 Bandung Conference（バンドン会議）symbolized movements toward decolonization in Asia and Africa. At the same time, Japan had its own history of imperial rule. 南の島を美しく描くことが、他国との連帯にも、歴史の忘却にもつながりうる。The film can be loved while its representation is examined critically.[10][11]

## 10. 冷戦の三つの世界を、同じ二人が歌っていた

Bourdaghs' 2024 study offers a striking framework: three cultural networks of the Cold War. The first involved the United States and Western Europe; the second, the Soviet-led socialist bloc; the third, decolonization and nonalignment. 「第三世界」は当時の歴史的な用語を示しているのであって、現代の人々を序列化するものではない。[10][11]

The Western story includes imported pop songs, tours, connections with Caterina Valente（カテリーナ・ヴァレンテ）, and an appearance on the American *Ed Sullivan Show* in 1966. 日本の歌手が欧米の曲を歌うだけでなく、現地のテレビへ出演する存在になった。They were not simply passive imitators.[3][14]

The socialist connection was even less expected. 『恋のバカンス』, written by Hiroshi Miyagawa and Tokiko Iwatani（岩谷時子）in Japan in 1963, was recorded in Russian by Nina Panteleeva（ニーナ・パンテレーエワ）in 1965. The historical account traces its circulation through a Soviet broadcaster and the duo's own visit to Moscow in 1967. 政治的な国境と、音楽が移動できる範囲は一致しなかった。[10][15]

Cuban-derived music on the first record and the imagined island of 『モスラ』 add yet another direction. ザ・ピーナッツを日米関係だけで語ると、肝心な地図の大部分が失われる。Their repertoire reveals a more entangled Cold War world.

## 11. 日本で生まれた歌が、別の国の思い出になる

A song can be Japanese in origin and local to Russia in memory. 『恋のバカンス』がロシア語で歌われると、聴き手にとっての故郷は録音の発祥地と同じではなくなる。Repeated performance, language and personal experience all shape the sense of ownership.[10]

However, claims such as 'every Russian knows this song' are not reliable population statistics. 今回確認できるのは、1965年のロシア語版と、その後の文化的な流通に関する歴史資料である。Avoiding an exaggerated sentence can make the genuine cross-border achievement clearer.[10][15]

International expansion has at least two distinct forms: an artist performing abroad, and other people adapting the artist's music in another country. 演者が外国へ行くことと、楽曲が現地語になって暮らしへ入ることを分けると、音楽の広まり方の理解が深くなる。

## 12. 作られたアイドルだったのか、類いまれな表現者だったのか

It is tempting to credit the entire success to their producers. But Miyagawa recalled that the sisters were already excellent singers before their major training. 一方で渡辺プロによる育成と曲目選定の影響も確かにあった。These are complementary facts, not opposing verdicts.[4][10]

Bourdaghs also cites research indicating that the sisters contributed to stage-costume design from around 1961 and later advised on ideas for other performers. お揃いの衣装を着ていたからといって、衣装に本人の意見がなかったとは言えない。[10]

Nor does limited evidence of choice prove unlimited control over their careers. 「才能だけで成功した」と「会社が全部作った」は、どちらも具体的な労働を単純化しすぎる。The more productive question is which decisions were made by whom and what each person contributed.

## 13. やりすぎる検証――4枚の記念盤を、4人の別の歌手だと思って読む

We compared the official track listing of King Records' 2009 four-disc 50th-anniversary set. これは波形測定ではなく、発売元が公開した曲目一覧の照合だ。[12]

Disc 1 includes Japanese originals such as 『恋のバカンス』and 『恋のフーガ』. Disc 2 emphasizes foreign-derived repertoire such as 『可愛い花』and 『コーヒー・ルンバ』. Disc 3 includes standards such as 『ムーン・リヴァー』and 『A列車で行こう』. Disc 4 includes songs better known in other Japanese singers' versions, including 『上を向いて歩こう』and 『また逢う日まで』.[12]

You could imagine four different artists: a domestic hitmaking duo, interpreters of foreign pop, jazz singers and performers reinterpreting Japanese standards. けれど全部同じ二人の声だ。ただし後年の編集盤であって、四つの分野へ活動時間を等しく配分したという統計ではない。

The numbers raise their own question. King Records reports 70 singles and 28 albums, while the academic paper cites 80 singles and 50 LPs from another source. 再発売や海外盤など、集計範囲が一致しない可能性があり、定義を確認せず一つの数字に統合するのは危険だ。Good research asks what was actually counted before making a clean-looking chart.[3][10]

## 14. 引退後に「再び活動する」のは、歌手ではなく記録の方だった

The duo announced retirement in February 1975 and gave a farewell performance that April. 1959年から1974年まで紅白歌合戦にも16回連続で出演した。Their performing careers had a clear endpoint; their recordings did not.[3]

In 2016, 24 women were paired to rerecord 12 songs in a tribute project. 二人で歌うという形式そのものが、後世へ継承されたのだ。King Records has continued archival releases, including a 2026 compilation, and a record-history museum announced an exhibition beginning in October 2026.[15][16][17]

We did not visit that exhibition. だが当時のテレビとレコードを理解する資料として、今も二人が扱われていることは確認できる。Their history continues through the work of curating, licensing and listening.

## 15. 知っておくと曲も産業も分かりやすい用語

- **Unison（斉唱）**: singing the same melody at essentially the same pitch.
- **Harmony（和声）**: relations between simultaneous pitches.
- **Counterpoint（対位法）**: coordinating distinct melodic lines.
- **Arrangement（編曲）**: designing instruments, introductions, accompaniment and musical texture.
- **Lyric adaptation（訳詞）**: making a song's words singable in a new language.
- **Cover recording（再録音）**: recording a pre-existing song again.
- **Master recording（原盤）**: the specific recorded performance and related rights.
- **Sponsorship（番組提供）**: commercial support for broadcasting.
- **Show business（興行）**: presenting performances as a revenue-generating activity.
- **Exoticism（異国趣味）**: making cultural distance an aesthetic attraction.
- **Cold War（冷戦）**: the postwar global rivalry centered on the U.S. and Soviet blocs.
- **Nonalignment（非同盟）**: efforts to stay independent of the major military blocs.
- **Bandung Conference（バンドン会議）**: the 1955 Asian-African Conference.
- **Source criticism（資料批判）**: evaluating who created evidence and what it can actually establish.

## 16. あの「南京豆売り」は、世界への出入り口だった

We began with a funny coincidence: The Peanuts sang *The Peanut Vendor* on their debut record. But the front of that record held a jazz melody, while the back connected Japan to Cuban popular music. やがて欧米のテレビ、ソ連で歌われる日本の曲、インドネシアを思わせる映画へと、彼女たちの仕事は広がる。[1][2][10]

That story also includes commercial management, unequal power and exoticized images of other cultures. 単純な「文化交流の美談」にはしない。それでも、一つの双子の声が別々の世界の歌を受け止め、組み替え、もう一度外へ運んだことは確かだ。

**The fact that they were twins did not automatically make them global; their voices became a way for different worlds to meet.** あの『南京豆売り』は、今では駄洒落のような曲名というより、戦後日本の音楽が歩いた世界地図の入口に見えてくる。
