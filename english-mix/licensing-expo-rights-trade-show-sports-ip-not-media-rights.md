# キャラクターの展示会かと思ったら、売り物は「使う権利」だった――The Business of Licensing Expo

Imagine a huge exhibition hall in Las Vegas. Pokémon, toys, movies, food brands, and sports teams are everywhere. 一見、ファン向けのイベントに見える。でも、the main customers are not fans. The real conversations happen between **IP owners** and companies that want to put those brands on products and services.

The merchandise on display is often a preview of something bigger: **permission to manufacture and sell products using someone else's intellectual property**. 物を並べながら、まだ世に出ていない物の契約を話している。That is the curious part.

This essay starts with [ZenWorks' Licensing Expo page](https://www.zenworks.jp/licensingexpo), then checks event disclosures, industry data, and the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO). 事実（what sources say）、解釈（what it means）、提案（what to test next）を分ける。Research cut-off: October 9, 2026.

## 1. Licensing Expoは「ファン向けイベント」ではなく、権利の商談を進める企業間見本市である

**Licensing Expo is a B2B trade show for brand licensing and intellectual property extensions.** It launched in 1980 and is held at the Mandalay Bay Convention Center in Las Vegas. Informa Markets organizes the event, while Licensing International serves as an industry association sponsor. See the [official overview](https://www.licensingexpo.com/about/about/).

Who comes to the show? **Licensors**, **licensees**, retailers, manufacturers, distributors, agents, and service providers. つまり、権利を持つ人だけの集まりではない。商談予約、商品化企画の発表、業界セッション、networkingが組み合わさっている。

In 2026, the event reported **410 exhibitors**, **over 5,000 represented brands**, **more than 12,500 attendees**, and **more than 1,500 qualified retail attendees**. Brands and organizations featured included Pokémon, SEGA, LEGO, Netflix, Coca-Cola, NASCAR, ATP, and Real Madrid. These are figures and examples from the [organizer's recap](https://licensinginternational.org/news/licensing-expo-concludes-with-record-breaking-show-floor-and-participation-from-cultural-icons-cementing-status-as-global-epicenter-for-brand-collaboration/).

But a critical distinction: **5,000 brands on display does not mean 5,000 unrestricted rights are available for purchase**. 地域、商品カテゴリー、exclusive contractsなどによって、許諾可能な範囲は変わる。「world's largest」は主催者側の表現であり、本稿で全世界の展示会と独自比較したものではない。

2026年開催はMay 19–21ですでに終了。The next Las Vegas event is scheduled for **May 25–27, 2027**, according to the [official site](https://www.licensingexpo.com/).

## 2. ライセンスは「知的財産そのものを売ること」ではなく、使い道を区切って貸す契約である

Start with a basic distinction: **assignment transfers ownership; licensing grants permission while ownership remains with the rights holder**. This is explained by [WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/ja/web/business/assignment-licensing) and [Licensing International](https://licensinginternational.org/education/what-is-licensing/).

Four practical terms:

- **Licensor（権利許諾者）**：The IP owner or authorized representative who grants usage rights.
- **Licensee（権利利用者）**：The company using that IP in a product or service.
- **Royalty（ライセンス料）**：Payment for that permission, sometimes tied to sales and sometimes fixed.
- **Licensing agent（権利仲介・代理店）**：An intermediary helping with partner search, negotiations, and approvals.

Take a fictional stationery company that wants to sell notebooks with a famous anime character. The company does **not** buy the anime. 日本で紙製文具に特定の図柄を使う権利を、一定期間認めてもらう。It handles manufacturing, inventory, and distribution; the IP owner retains control over permitted brand usage and approvals.

This creates a division of labor. The licensor can extend a brand into new categories without operating every factory. The licensee can build on existing brand awareness instead of creating all recognition from scratch. But neither side receives guaranteed profit. 品質管理、保証金、製造原価、売れ残りなど、the costs and risks remain real.

## 3. 展示会の四者を分けると、「誰が何を売っているのか」が見える

For analysis, the ecosystem can be split into four roles. This is **our analytical framework**, not an official expo classification.

- **IP owners / brand managers**：ブランドの管理、usage rights、revenue、reachを担う。
- **Licensees / product companies**：商品企画、manufacturing、販売、inventory riskを引き受ける。
- **Retailers / distributors**：売場やcustomer dataを持ち、what actually sellsを判断する。
- **Agents / specialists**：partner matching、negotiation、approvals、cross-border coordinationを支援する。

A simple flow is **IP owner → manufacturer → retailer → customer**. But business development is not always linear. 小売が先に需要を見つけることも、agentが現地のメーカーを探すこともある。To understand the industry, ask not only who owns the IP, but also **who understands the end customer's demand**.

## 4. リンク先のZenWorksは主催者ではなく、日本企業を世界の商談へつなぐ出展窓口である

Who is ZenWorks? According to its [official event page](https://www.zenworks.jp/licensingexpo), **ZenWorks is the exclusive official Japanese-market exhibiting sales contact** for Informa Markets' Licensing Expo, Licensing Expo China, and Brand Licensing Europe.

That does not make ZenWorks the event organizer or the owner of every IP shown at the event. [会社概要](https://www.zenworks.jp/company)には、国際ライセンスの仲介・consulting、海外市場開拓支援、ブランドエージェント、業界向け英語研修などが記されている。The company's president is Roger M. Berman.

The value of such an intermediary is more than translation. A conversation can fail because territory, product category, distribution practice, approval procedures, or contractual obligations are unclear. 海外で権利を利用する仕事には、language skillsだけでなく、commercial coordinationが必要になる。

## 5. 「キャラクターを海外で売る」以外にも、日本企業の参入方法がある

Japanese anime and games are important, but **brand licensing is bigger than character goods**. ZenWorks lists Japanese-related past exhibitors such as Bandai Namco, Konami, Pokémon, Toho, SEGA, Tsuburaya, and Toei Animation in its [expo introduction](https://www.zenworks.jp/licensingexpo). **These are historical examples**, not proof that all companies exhibited in the same year.

Here comes an unexpected example: soy sauce. In its [Kikkoman case description](https://www.zenworks.jp/consulting_kikkoman), ZenWorks explains its involvement in lifestyle-oriented brand licensing that expanded from the US into Europe and Australia through regional collaborations. これは関係企業自身の事例紹介であり、契約額や利益の独立検証ではない。

Why put a food brand on non-food merchandise? Because the brand carries recognition, associations, and cultural meaning beyond its original product. ここから二つの海外戦略が見えてくる。One is **exporting finished goods**; the other is **licensing a brand to a local business that develops and sells goods**. The second can leverage local distribution but requires brand supervision.

## 6. 世界の「3,898億ドル」はライセンス料ではない。ここが最大の落とし穴である

The [2026 Global Licensing Industry Study summary](https://licensinginternational.org/get-survey/) reports **$389.8 billion in 2025 global retail sales of licensed merchandise and services**, up **5.45%** from $369.6 billion in 2024.

By property type:
- **Entertainment / Characters**: $161.8B, or **41.5%**.
- **Corporate Brands**: $98.0B, or **25.2%**.
- **Sports**: $44.4B, or **11.4%**, growing **8.5% year on year**.

These are **retail sales, not royalty revenue and not licensors' profits**. 小売店の売上、メーカーの出荷額、licenseeの売上、licensorが受け取るroyaltyは別物。The full study is not freely published; these figures come from its public summary, with research conducted by Brandar Consulting.

Suppose a licensed product sells at retail for ¥10,000. How much does the rights owner receive? **You cannot calculate that from the retail price alone.** 契約上の料率、ロイヤルティの算定基準、卸売価格、最低保証などが分からないからだ。

The lesson is simple but important: **a large consumer market does not automatically equal a large addressable royalty market**. Market sizing must follow the money.

## 7. ライセンス契約は、少なくとも七つの条件を切り分けて初めて商売になる

An agreement to use a character is rarely permission to do anything, anywhere, forever. Based on the [Licensing International guide](https://licensinginternational.org/education/what-is-licensing/) and [WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/ja/web/business/assignment-licensing), review at least seven dimensions:

1. **Property / Rights（権利の対象）**：Which name, logo, character, or artwork?
2. **Product Category（用途）**：Apparel, stationery, toys, experiences, or another defined use?
3. **Territory & Channel（地域・販路）**：Which countries, stores, and online channels?
4. **Term（期間）**：When does usage start and end, and what happens to remaining inventory?
5. **Exclusivity（独占性）**：Can other companies receive overlapping permissions?
6. **Financial Terms（金銭条件）**：Royalties, advance, minimum guarantee, reporting, audit.
7. **Approval & Liability（監修・責任）**：Brand guidelines, quality approvals, infringement, safety issues.

**Advance**（前払金）と**minimum guarantee**（最低保証額）の意味や精算方法は契約次第。No universal royalty percentage is assumed here. A signed deal is not the same thing as a profitable product launch.

## 8. スポーツも重要市場だが、「ユニフォームの権利」と「試合映像の権利」は違う

In 2026, the expo featured brands such as NASCAR, ATP, and Real Madrid; an event [Sports Pavilion](https://www.licensingexpo.com/whats-on/events/) also highlights merchandise and collaborations. Sports licensing is therefore a meaningful part of this ecosystem.

But **sports merchandising and sports media rights are different businesses**. [WIPO's merchandising explanation](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/sports/licenses) covers trademarks and official licensed goods. Its separate [broadcasting-rights guide](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/sports/broadcasting) covers live broadcasts, highlights, on-demand use, and geographic rights restrictions.

A practical four-part separation:

- **Merchandising rights（商品化権）**：A team logo on a cap or an official shirt.
- **Media / distribution rights（映像利用・配信権）**：Recording, broadcasting, clips, archives, and resale where permitted by contract.
- **Image / likeness permissions（肖像等の利用許諾）**：An athlete's image or name in commercial campaigns, subject to relevant rules.
- **Production services（制作・運用受託）**：Filming, editing, storage, and delivery as a service. Providing these services does not automatically confer commercial exploitation rights.

ここを混ぜると市場分析が壊れる。**The $44.4B sports figure refers to licensed merchandise and services in that industry study; it is not a sports video rights market estimate.** The right counterparty, revenue model, and contract are different in each case.

## 9. 映像事業者への逆提案は「権利を買う」より「権利を使える状態にする」である

**The following is a business hypothesis, not a verified market opportunity.** A company that records and archives sports video does not necessarily benefit from buying popular characters at Licensing Expo. A different opportunity might be **rights-aware content operations**: helping clients understand when and how archived footage can be reused.

Imagine a league with ten years of match recordings. The files exist. But can a sponsor use a clip? Can an overseas partner redistribute it? Can a player montage be sold? 技術的にファイルを出せても、契約上の許諾がなければ使えない。

One possible service would connect four functions: **searchable assets**, **rights metadata**, **approval workflow**, and **usage / revenue tracking**. 「映像を保管した」から「条件を満たせば使える」への移行を支える仕事だ。

Yet there is no proof here of willingness to pay, margin, or superior competitiveness. Existing rights-management systems, league operations, broadcasters, and platform contracts may already serve parts of the need. **A better first step than buying an expo booth may be interviewing the actual owners of these blocked workflows.**

## 10. 似た展示会を比較すると、都市名より「どの市場に商談したいか」が先に来る

[ZenWorks' Informa Markets overview](https://www.zenworks.jp/informamarkets) points to three major events:

- **Licensing Expo / Las Vegas**: a global event with strong relevance to North American partners; next scheduled for **May 25–27, 2027**.
- **Licensing Expo China / Shanghai**: commercial access to the Chinese licensing market; next scheduled for **July 14–16, 2027**, according to [ZenWorks](https://www.zenworks.jp/licensingexposhanghai).
- **Brand Licensing Europe / London**: European licensing partnerships; the 2026 event ran **October 6–8**, as announced by [ZenWorks](https://www.zenworks.jp/brandlicensingeurope).

Do not choose an event merely because it is the biggest. Which territory? Which buyer? Which product? Which distribution channel? 「どこへ行くか」の前に「誰と何を決めるか」がある。An impressive show floor is not a substitute for partner fit.

## 11. 「出展すべきか」は、会場の規模ではなく案件の段階で決める

**Practical proposal, not the organizer's official playbook:**

First, make the offer contract-ready. 対象IP、地域、商品分野、既存の独占許諾、監修条件を整理する。Second, identify the specific demand: manufacturers, retailers, or local agents? Know price points, channels, launch timing, and competing properties.

Third, design meetings around **qualified leads and next actions**, not the number of business cards. A good KPI might be the number of relevant partners who request a concrete proposal or progress into negotiation.

Fourth, calculate **return on participation**. Travel, booth expenses, agent fees, translation, legal work, and approval overhead belong in the cost model. 成約件数と、その後の商品発売件数も分けて追うべきだ。A signed agreement may never produce shelf-ready merchandise.

For beginners, **attend before exhibiting** may be a sensible alternative. Eligibility, ticket prices, and meeting credits can change, so confirm the current [official show guidance](https://www.licensingexpo.com/).

## 12. 調べてもなお分からないのは、華やかな商談がどれだけ利益になるかだ

Public sources establish dates, exhibitor counts, brand participation, and headline retail sales. What they do **not** reliably establish is a comparable, independent series of **contracts signed, royalty rates, product launch rates, or exhibitors' payback periods**. 個社の契約額や条件は多くが非公開だ。

Popularity alone does not guarantee good economics. A famous brand can attract competitors and potentially raise contractual burdens; a niche property may fit a specific audience and distribution channel better. 「5,000ブランド」は選択肢の豊富さを示すが、「5,000の儲かる契約」を示すわけではない。

## 13. 最後に残るのは、「権利の見本市」が商品を作る前から市場を作っているという発見だ

At first, the question was: **what exactly is being sold in a hall full of characters?** The answer turns out to be permission, commercial relationships, and future product categories. 見本市は既存の商品の売場ではなく、まだ商品が並んでいない未来の売場を交渉している。

A soy sauce brand, a football club, and an anime franchise have more in common than they first appear to. Each carries meaning that another company might translate into new products. **Brand licensing connects existing recognition with another firm's manufacturing and distribution capabilities.**

But permission is conditional, not unlimited. Especially in sports video, possession of a file is not ownership of exploitation rights. だから事業機会は、有名IPの取得だけではない。**Making rights understandable, traceable, and usable** may itself create value—provided customers truly need it and will pay for it. The character exhibition begins to look like a map of how future products and transactions are designed.
