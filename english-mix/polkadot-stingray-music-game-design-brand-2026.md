# ポルカドットスティングレイは、なぜエイの名前で猫を育てるのか｜English Mix

## 要旨――エイの名前で始まる、音楽と設計の話

A rock band named after a spotted stingray uses a **cat** as its mascot. しかも中心人物は元ゲームクリエイターで、楽曲だけでなく映像やグッズ、プロモーションも手掛ける。Music research takes an unexpected detour through fish biology and game design. だが、その寄り道がポルカの本質に近づく入口になる。

The thesis is simple: **“a band good at marketing” is not a complete explanation**. 演奏、雫の制作能力、ファンとの接点、タイアップが連動してきた。そして2024年以降には、needs-driven productionだけでは語れない変化も現れる。以下、**facts／interpretation／hypotheses**を明確に分ける。

## 守1．まず誰なのか――福岡で始まった4人組のギターロック

Polkadot Stingrayは**福岡出身の4人組ギターロックバンド**。公式には2015年活動開始、2017年major debut。雫（vocal/guitar）、エジマハルシ（guitar）、ウエムラユウキ（bass）、ミツヤスカズマ（drums）からなる。雫のcreative directionと、4人のband performanceは別々ではなく一緒に機能する。[Official profile](https://polkadot-stingray.jp/about)。

2017年の音楽評では、crisp guitar sound、変化のあるrhythm section、雫の低音を含む歌声、そして歌謡曲的なメロディーとrockの融合が論じられていた。これはcriticismであって客観的な点数ではない。ただ、映像だけで人を引きつけても、**repeat listeningを支えるのは楽曲と演奏**だという視点を与える。[Music criticism](https://rockinon.com/news/detail/156528)。

The 2016 music video **「テレキャスター・ストライプ」** is a useful starting point. Billboard JAPAN reported **10 million YouTube views by April 25, 2018**. これは当時の達成値であって、2026年時点のcurrent view countではない。Music and visuals spreading together became an early model for the band's public presence.[2018 report](https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/62673)。

## 守2．バンド名は魚、顔は猫――意味より記憶の強度を優先した

The band name comes from a freshwater stingray, often identified as *Potamotrygon leopoldi*, found in the Xingu River basin of Brazil. **Polka dot** means 水玉、**stingray**はエイ。雫はアルバイト先で聞いた名前のimpactを覚えていて、バンド名にしたと伝えられる。No secret code required: **memorability itself was the point**.[Name origin](https://guitar-concierge.jp/voice/origin-band/polkadot-stingray/)／[FishBase](https://www.fishbase.se/Fieldguide/FieldGuideSummary.php?c_code=076&genusname=Potamotrygon&speciesname=leopoldi)。

![A real polka-dot stingray, Potamotrygon leopoldi](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Potamotrygon_leopoldi.jpg?width=960)

*The real freshwater ray. Photo: Michael-David Bradford, public domain. [Wikimedia Commons / license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Potamotrygon_leopoldi.jpg).*

But the official mascot is **ビビ, a cat**, not a stingray. A name can act as a discovery hook; a cat can become a recurring, friendly visual symbol. これはブランド上の役割を読み解いた**interpretation**で、関係者の意図を断定していない。A Brazilian ray turning into a Japanese band represented by a cat is odd biology, but interesting branding.[Official site](https://polkadot-stingray.jp/about)。

## 守3．ゲーム会社出身は肩書ではない――数字から制作を見直した経験がある

雫 worked in game planning, development and live operations. In her 2018 J-WAVE explanation, a director could oversee specifications, team schedules, budgets and decisions based on performance after launch. 企画書ではconceptとtarget audienceを先に示すことを勧めていた。This is experience in **designing, releasing and operating a product**, not merely liking games.[J-WAVE interview](https://news.j-wave.co.jp/2018/04/post-5205.html)。

In a 2021 interview, 雫 described checking fan posts and post-release reactions, then feeding them back into songs, merchandise and promotion. ゲームの運営では利用状況や課金などのnumbersを見て、intuition-only creationを改めたという。Here, **marketing means researching demand and using feedback to make the next decision**, not simply buying ads.[R25](https://r25.jp/articles/928885231148859393)／[Interview](https://start.jword.jp/magazine/detail/16882)。

Yet **listening to demand** is not the same as **outsourcing every creative decision to fans**. The creators still choose the finished work. 期待に応えながら、少し意外なものを出す余地をどう残すのか。That tension matters in both music and product design.

## 守4．活動史は三つの節目で読む――拡散、拡張、再定義

Three periods help. **2016–2018**: the 「テレキャスター・ストライプ」MV, 2017 major debut with 『全知全能』, and the reported 10-million-view milestone in 2018. **2019–2022**: larger shows, tie-ins, 『何者』 in 2020 and 『踊る様に』 in 2022. The official profile says **11 of 14 tracks on 『何者』 involved tie-ins or collaborations**.[Official profile](https://polkadot-stingray.jp/about)／[MV report](https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/62673)。

The third turning point is **July 2024**: the announcement that chapter one had ended, followed by the release of 「JO-DEKI」 on July 31. 『逆鱗』 arrived in December 2025; 「逆様」 for the anime 『氷の城壁』 arrived in April 2026; 「無礼な！」 followed on October 30, 2026. As of November 9, **STILL PINK TOUR** is underway.[Official history](https://polkadot-stingray.jp/about)／[Album](https://polkadot-stingray.jp/news/34791)／[Singles](https://polkadot-stingray.jp/release/releasetax/single)／[Live](https://polkadot-stingray.jp/live)。

This timeline is not a claim of uninterrupted growth. Views, concerts and tie-ins measure different things. **“Chapter two” is the band's own label**, but the size and nature of change must be tested against interviews and works, not assumed from the slogan.

## 破1．売れ方を分解する――曲、動画、共同制作、コミュニティは別の装置

This is **analysis, not a measured customer journey**. A functional model has five parts: **songs/performance → visual discovery → collaborations → ongoing contact → participation**. アニメやゲームとのタイアップは新しい接点、SNSとライブは継続接点になり得る。ただしconversion ratesも因果の強さも公開資料では確認できない。[Official profile](https://polkadot-stingray.jp/about)／[Live information](https://polkadot-stingray.jp/live)。

![A conceptual model of music, visuals, collaborations and fandom](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/polkadot-creative-system-2026.svg)

*Diagram: an **interpretive model**, not verified conversion data. 矢印は因果を証明するものではない。*

The key implication is **multiple entry points into one artist world**. Someone discovers the MV, another comes from anime, someone loves the guitar, and someone follows the cat. 最後の人はstingrayの存在を忘れているかもしれない。Different entry points matter only if listeners can eventually connect them to the same band.

But more tie-ins alone will not reproduce this result. Exposure is not the same as loyalty. **Discovery and retention are different problems**, and the quality of songs, performances and operations affects whether one leads to the other.

## 破2．「ニーズ至上主義」だけでは、第二章を説明しきれない

In 2021, 雫 clearly described a **needs-driven** creative process. In discussing 「JO-DEKI」 in 2024, she also described expression emerging more naturally from within. 外側のdemandと内側のpersonal expressionは、単純な二者択一ではない。Still, the later account complicates the idea that audience research alone explains every song.[2021 interview](https://r25.jp/articles/928885231148859393)／[2024 interview](https://www.billboard-japan.com/special/detail/4494)。

In December 2025, rockin'on.com introduced the band's **first all-member interview** around 『逆鱗』. The writer contrasted earlier heavy dependence on 雫 with a renewed sense of trust among the four. これは記者のinterpretationを含み、formal governance changeの証拠ではない。ただ、「第二章」を単なるrebranding sloganとして片付けるには不十分だ。[2025 article](https://rockinon.com/news/detail/213913)。

A strong creator can accelerate an early venture; later, concentrated decisions can also become a burden. The management analogy is **how to grow from founder-led direction toward team ownership without losing identity**. ただし内部の意思決定権や契約は未確認であり、これはbusiness interpretationに留まる。

## 破3．成功の根拠を厳しく見る――何が実証され、何が未確認か

Verified figures include **10 million views reported in 2018** for one MV, **11 tie-in/collaboration tracks out of 14** on 『何者』, and **13 tracks** on 『逆鱗』. These numbers are informative, but they do **not** directly establish revenue, profitability, fan retention or acquisition cost.[Billboard](https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/62673)／[Official](https://polkadot-stingray.jp/about)／[Album](https://polkadot-stingray.jp/news/34791)。

“The MV caused ticket sales” and “game-industry experience caused success” are **causal claims**, not established findings. Timing is not causation. A stronger test would consider comparable artists, promotion, recommendation algorithms, musical appeal and existing fan bases. 音楽コラムが急にresearch designになるが、この注意は必要だ。

Alternative explanations matter. Maybe the songs and musicianship created attention first, and branding followed. Perhaps tie-ins connected the band to pre-existing audiences. These hypotheses may coexist with marketing effects. **Multiple mechanisms** are more plausible than a one-factor legend.

## 離1．ファンクラブをゲーム設計で見る――ファンは「視聴者」だけではない

The official fan club is **「半泣き黒猫団＋」**, a name that feels like joining a fictional group rather than merely paying a subscription. 雫はかつて、ファン投稿を素材として「半泣き黒猫団のテーマ」を作ったと説明している。This is one documented **participatory example**, not proof that every work is crowdsourced.[Official](https://polkadot-stingray.jp/about)／[Artist account](https://www.tfm.co.jp/lock/staff/index.php%40itemid%3D11311%26catid%3D36.html)。

In game design terms, a fan journey can include **discovery, return visits, participation and shared memories**. Gamification does not simply mean handing out points. ただしファン全員が同じstepsを踏むわけではない。This is an analytical model, not a validated behavioral funnel.

For sports business, a match is analogous to the core work, highlights to a music video, mascots to a world-building entry point, and membership to repeated contact. 勝敗や地域性というdifferenceは大きい。What transfers is not the cat itself, but **coherence across touchpoints**.

## 離2．今後の勝ち筋は何か――三つの仮説と反証条件

**Hypothesis A: fit matters more than the sheer number of tie-ins.** ポケモンや『氷の城壁』との実績から、strong creative fit may remain valuable. ただしmore collaborations are not guaranteed. A counter-test is whether these releases lead to sustained listening and live attendance.[Official discography](https://polkadot-stingray.jp/release)。

**Hypothesis B: scaling collaboration becomes important.** 雫のmulti-skilled directionを生かしつつ、4人と外部制作者で品質を共有できるか。The all-member interview suggests a relevant question, not a proven organizational reform. 反証は、実務上の制作分担が変わっていないと確認された場合。[2025 discussion](https://rockinon.com/news/detail/213913)。

**Hypothesis C: distinctive names and visuals may travel across borders.** ただしtranslation alone does not establish overseas demand. We have not verified foreign audience size, brand awareness or touring economics. 「世界進出確実」は、まだエイにboarding passを渡すようなものだ。

## 離3．次の調査はこう組み立てる――面白さを数字で潰さないために

Four testable questions: **How do new listeners first discover the band?** Do listening habits correlate with live attendance? Do tie-in listeners explore other songs? Has the creative team's public role changed since chapter two? 方法はMV・release・tour chronology、listener interviews、そしてvideo comparisonを組み合わせる。

Compare artists by **functions**, not vague similarity: central creative leadership, visual identity, character/community touchpoints, and media collaborations. YOASOBI, Ado and other guitar bands may serve as candidates, but **their business models are not identical**. 共通軸を定義してから差を検証する。

Limits: we have no private streaming analytics, revenue-share contracts, fan-club membership trends or current sell-through figures. Therefore **profitability is not established**. この資料から読めるのは、music, production and audience contactがどう連動し得るかというdesign logicまでである。

## 結論――エイが猫になる理由は、すべてを説明しないことにある

Back to the ray and the cat. Why does a stingray-named band use ビビ as its visual face? Whatever the exact original intent, the general lesson is that **names, sounds, videos and mascots do not have to repeat the same message**. Different pieces can invite people into one coherent world.

But calling the band a brilliant marketing machine is also incomplete. Earlier criticism focused on sound; the 2024 interview emphasized personal expression; the 2025 discussion examined the team. **The mechanism of discovery is not identical to the reason to keep creating.** That tension makes Polkadot Stingray worth studying beyond the catchy name.

## 再利用できるリサーチプロンプト――音楽家を事業と作品の両面から読む

Use the prompt below to apply this method to another artist. **Separate official facts, artist statements, criticism and hypotheses**, and do not confuse old milestones with current numbers.

```text
Research target: ［artist］
1. Verify official profile, members, chronology and latest releases.
2. Separate name, visual identity, music, video, tie-ins and fandom.
3. Compare three songs and three videos: observation vs judgment.
4. Put a date and denominator on every metric; don't invent causality.
5. 守：basics/history／破：competing hypotheses／離：transferable insights.
6. Each section: evidence → interpretation → counterpoint → next question.
7. Check image rights; label conceptual diagrams as hypotheses.
8. Report missing data, next research steps and primary-source links.
```
