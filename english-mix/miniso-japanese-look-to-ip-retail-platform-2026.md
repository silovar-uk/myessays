# 日本の店っぽかったMINISOは、いつからIP Retailの店になったのか

## 2013年の創業、日本風ブランドへの批判、店舗網と利益、2026年の知的財産戦略まで

A red sign, a short name, and neat shelves. MINISO looks a little like a Japanese variety store. しかし実際には2013年に中国・広州で生まれた企業だ。By 2026, the company called itself an “IP operation platform.” 雑貨屋にしては大きな肩書である。[日本向け公式紹介](https://miniso-jp.com/our-story)／[2026年10月発表](https://ir.miniso.com/2026-10-09-MINISO-Groups-Latest-Business-Update)

When did the change happen? Was MINISO always about characters, or did it pivot away from Japanese-style lifestyle goods? 調べると、店舗デザインだけでなく、store economics、licensing rights、owned characters、revenue recognitionがつながってくる。

This article reviews public information through 16 October 2026. 企業のfilingsとearnings releasesを優先し、criticismには報道と会社のresponsesを分けて記す。Facts, interpretation, and proposals are separated. 未公表のroyalty rateを推計で補わない。

## 1. MINISOは日本企業ではなく、広州で生まれた世界的な小売グループだ

**Fact.** MINISO opened its first store in Guangzhou in 2013. Founder Ye Guofu led its expansion overseas in 2015, the New York listing in 2020, and the Hong Kong listing in 2022. At June 2026, the group operated 8,674 stores: 8,309 MINISO stores and 365 TOP TOY stores. 店舗数の分母に注意したい。[公式沿革](https://miniso-jp.com/our-story)／[Interim results](https://ir.miniso.com/2026-08-28-MINISO-Group-Announces-2026-June-Quarter-and-Interim-Unaudited-Financial-Results)

Its assortment includes stationery, household goods, cosmetics, fragrances, plush toys, and collectibles. 必需品も、趣味のものもある。The 2025 annual filing treats MINISO and TOP TOY as different brands. Saying “8,674 MINISO stores” would therefore confuse group stores with the core brand.[Annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1815846/000110465926048172/mnso-20251231x20f.htm)

**Interpretation.** “A Chinese dollar store” captures affordability but misses pricing diversity, licensed products, and store formats. より正確には、product developmentとretail distributionを組み合わせる企業である。

## 2. 創業から上場までの歴史は、商品の発明より売り方の複製にある

The founder says that specialty stores seen during a trip inspired the business in 2013. これはcompany narrativeであり、独立した動機の検証ではない。The observable milestones are the first shop in 2013, international entry in 2015, the US listing in 2020, and Hong Kong listing in 2022.[Brand origin](https://www.miniso.com/brand/)／[History](https://miniso-jp.com/our-story)

TOP TOY launched in December 2020. It focuses on blind boxes, figures, model toys, and collectibles. 2024年からはMINISO LANDなど、larger IP-focused storesも展開し始めた。[2024 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1815846/000141057825000858/mnso-20241231x20f.htm)／[Store formats](https://www.miniso.com/brand/news_192.html)

**Interpretation.** The story is not only “great products sold well.” 先にrapid product renewalとstore expansionの仕組みを作り、その上にIPの商品を載せたと読むことができる。Distribution came before the current IP strategy.

## 3. 「日本風」という出自の演出は、2022年に会社自身が問題と認めた

For years MINISO presented itself as Japanese-inspired, using Japanese-looking branding and a Japanese chief designer. In 2022 it apologized after criticism in China and said its early positioning and marketing had been mistaken. 日本風の表現を2019年から見直してきたという。[Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/RYOHIN-KEIKAKU-CO-LTD-6492037/news/Chinese-retailer-Miniso-to-ditch-Japanese-styling-after-backlash-41363418/)／[Bloomberg](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/news/2022/08/19/business/miniso-china-japan/)

One reported flashpoint was a doll in a Chinese qipao labeled as a Japanese geisha. ここにはmisleading cultural labelingと、Chinese domestic political reactionという二つの争点がある。Country of origin, a designer's nationality, and customer perception are not interchangeable.[Reuters background](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/asia/news/why-did-100-chinese-miniso-go-japanese-style-branding-3101256)

**Interpretation.** We cannot quantify the sales benefit of its old Japanese-style identity from available data. だが、borrowed cultural codesによるrecognitionは、後でexplainabilityとaccountabilityの問題を生む場合がある。

## 4. 急拡大の仕掛けは、全部の店を自社で経営しないことにある

**Fact.** Of 4,665 MINISO stores in mainland China at June 2026, just 15 were directly operated; 4,624 used the retail partner model and 26 used distributors. Overseas, the mix was 795 direct, 439 partner, and 2,410 distributor stores. 国や地域によってoperating modelsが違う。[2026 store breakdown](https://ir.miniso.com/2026-08-28-MINISO-Group-Announces-2026-June-Quarter-and-Interim-Unaudited-Financial-Results)

Under the retail partner model, partners fund store fit-outs and routine operations while MINISO supplies products, guidance, and systems. 重要なのはinventory ownershipである。According to the company's filing, products generally remain MINISO inventory until sold to the end customer, when product sales revenue is recognized. ただのwholesaleとは異なる。[Retail partner disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1815846/000141057825000858/mnso-20241231x20f.htm)

**Interpretation.** Sharing store capital expenditure can accelerate expansion without financing every shop centrally. ただしinventory riskとsell-through managementがなくなるわけではない。The capital model and the product model must be analyzed separately.

## 5. 「IPの店」には、他社の権利を借りる方法と自社の権利を育てる方法がある

IP means intellectual property, not merely a cute illustration. そこにはcopyright、trademark、design rights、licensing agreementsがある。Selling products with Sanrio or Disney characters means operating within permissions granted by rights holders. 既存のfan awarenessを商品へ取り込む。[MINISO LAND assortment](https://miniso.com/brand/news_174.html)

There is also proprietary IP. MINISO launched merchandise featuring YOYO in June 2025. In October 2026 it reported over RMB1 billion in YOYO-related GMV during the first nine months of 2026, across 53 markets. **GMV is not royalty revenue or net profit.** この数字はpreliminary operational dataである。[October 2026 update](https://ir.miniso.com/2026-10-09-MINISO-Groups-Latest-Business-Update)

**Interpretation.** Licensed IP brings a ready-made audience but also contract dependency. Proprietary IP costs time and carries failure risk, but can offer greater long-term control. ただしYOYO単独のprofit marginは公開資料から算出できない。These are two investment strategies with different risks.

## 6. MINISO LANDは、商品棚をキャラクターに出会う体験へ変える試みだ

MINISO LAND, introduced in 2024, offers larger stores with themed areas, photo opportunities and interactive merchandising. The company says its Shanghai flagship exceeded RMB100 million in revenue over nine months, with IP products making up 79.6% of revenue and a 35–40% repeat purchase rate. **These are store-specific, company-reported figures.** 全店舗平均ではない。[Company announcement](https://miniso.com/brand/news_174.html)

A large store creates visibility but also adds rent, fit-out costs, staffing, and inventory. IP plus large-format stores became two strategic drivers in 2026. ただし派手な売場が高いoperating profitを生むとは限らない。Comparable store-level return on investment remains unavailable.[Formats](https://www.miniso.com/brand/news_192.html)／[Interim results](https://ir.miniso.com/2026-08-28-MINISO-Group-Announces-2026-June-Quarter-and-Interim-Unaudited-Financial-Results)

**Interpretation.** A character encounter, a photo, or a limited item can become a reason to visit. これはexperiential retailとして理解できる。But incremental purchase impact requires a controlled comparison, not just attractive store photography.

## 7. 2026年上半期、売上は増えた。しかし同じ速さで利益が増えたわけではない

Group revenue reached RMB21.4438 billion in 2025, up 26.2%. For H1 2026, revenue was RMB11.4989 billion, up 22.4%. MINISO contributed RMB10.5132 billion and TOP TOY RMB0.9846 billion. These are **renminbi**, not Japanese yen. 店舗数と売上を混同しない。[FY2025 results](https://ir.miniso.com/2026-03-31-MINISO-Group-Announces-December-Quarter-and-Full-Year-of-2025-Unaudited-Financial-Results)／[H1 2026 results](https://ir.miniso.com/2026-08-28-MINISO-Group-Announces-2026-June-Quarter-and-Interim-Unaudited-Financial-Results)

H1 operating profit rose only 6.1% to RMB1.6399 billion. Operating margin fell from 16.5% to 14.3%, while gross margin stayed at 44.3%. Adjusted net margin excluding FX dropped from 13.2% to 10.6%. **Revenue growth and margin growth are different things.**[Financial release](https://ir.miniso.com/2026-08-28-MINISO-Group-Announces-2026-June-Quarter-and-Interim-Unaudited-Financial-Results)

**Interpretation.** More stores, higher revenue, and stronger IP demand should not be counted as the same success. 新店数、same-store performance、gross margin、operating marginを別々に見ることでgrowth qualityを判断できる。

## 8. IP使用料は追加費用だが、利益率低下をすべてIPのせいにはできない

In H1 2026, selling and distribution expenses excluding share-based compensation rose from 23.1% to 25.8% of revenue. The company identified increases of about 1.0 percentage point for direct-store rent and depreciation, 0.5 for advertising, 0.5 for licensing, and about 0.4 for payroll. ライセンスだけが費用増の原因ではない。[Expense breakdown](https://ir.miniso.com/2026-08-28-MINISO-Group-Announces-2026-June-Quarter-and-Interim-Unaudited-Financial-Results)

Licensing expenses grew 44.6% in 2025. Separately, MINISO acquired a 29.4% stake in supermarket chain Yonghui, and its share of Yonghui losses affected group earnings. Operating costs, equity-method investment results, and financing expenses are different lines. これを全部IPの失敗として説明するのは誤り。[Annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1815846/000110465926048172/mnso-20251231x20f.htm)

**Interpretation.** The right question is not only whether licensed goods sell. We need incremental gross profit after royalties, space, advertising, and stock risk. 商品棚には売上が見えるが、cost allocationは見えない。

## 9. 批判は「日本風の表示」と「財務・統治の疑義」を別々に検証する必要がある

The 2022 apology concerned brand origin and cultural presentation. A separate 2022 report by short seller Blue Orca raised allegations about partner-store independence and transactions involving the founder. MINISO rejected the claims and formed an independent committee. 二つの騒動を同じ証拠で論じない。[Company rebuttal](https://ir.miniso.com/2022-07-28-MINISO-Refutes-Blue-Orcas-Short-Seller-Report)

In September 2022 the company said external legal and accounting advisers had helped its investigation, and that the key allegations were not substantiated. これはthe company's disclosure of investigation findingsであり、すべての争点に関する無条件の司法認定ではない。Subsequent US litigation documents must be treated separately.[Investigation update](https://ir.miniso.com/2022-09-15-MINISO-Announces-Substantial-Completion-of-Independent-Investigation)／[US court document](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/new-york/nysdce/1:2022cv09864/589753/109/)

**Interpretation.** A retailer's value depends not only on products, but on ownership, revenue recognition, and related-party governance. 「炎上」という一語では、testable allegationsとestablished factsの違いを失う。

## 10. 無印良品・ダイソー・POP MARTとの比較で、同じ「雑貨店」が別の仕事をしていると分かる

MINISO looks similar to MUJI and Daiso from the outside. MUJI emphasizes own-brand lifestyle goods; Daiso has a broad low-price household assortment. POP MART is more centered on collectible toys and character franchises. MINISO combines many retail categories with licensed IP, proprietary IP, and themed stores. 商品の見た目ではなくbusiness functionsを比較したい。

This is a conceptual business-model comparison, **not** evidence that the customer base, product quality, or profitability is equal. Even among IP-oriented companies, value flows differently to rights holders, manufacturers, distributors, and retailers. MINISOとPOP MARTを単に「IP企業」で括るだけでは粗すぎる。[MINISO overview](https://www.miniso.com/brand/)／[POP MART](https://www.popmart.com/)／[Ryohin Keikaku](https://www.ryohin-keikaku.jp/)

**Interpretation.** Compare functions: Who owns the IP? Who manufactures? Who pays for the store? Why does a customer visit? その問いで、similar-looking stores can reveal very different unit economics.

## 11. 実務では、売場の派手さではなく五つの単位で採算を調べる

**Proposal.** Study five units: product economics (rights, royalty, gross margin), store economics (area, rent, staff, payback), customer economics (retention, repeat visits, membership), regional operating models (direct, partner, distributor), and corporate profit (operations versus investment gains or losses). 店舗数だけで競争力を結論しない。

**Proposal.** Compare ordinary stores and MINISO LAND after adjusting for location, area, customer mix, and store age. Measure conversion, basket size, revisit frequency, gross profit per square meter, and store payback. For licensed products, distinguish genuinely incremental purchases from substitution and subtract royalties. 「人気」ではなくincremental profitを測る。

These measures require internal data. 本稿で確認できたのはpublic financial results and store countsまでであり、customer cohortsやstore-level ROIは未取得だ。A sound business decision needs that next round of data collection.

## 12. 日本らしい店に見えた理由を調べると、今度はIPの「誰が持つか」が気になる

At first, the red logo raises a simple question: Is this Japanese? The company began in China, used Japanese-style branding, later apologized for that positioning, and grew a global retail network. 今ではlicensed charactersとproprietary charactersを同じ流通網で展開している。

The better question becomes: **Whose rights, whose capital, whose store, and whose profit?** MINISO did not become an IP company overnight. It is repurposing the store network it built into a platform connecting characters, products, and consumer desire. その成否はeye-catching salesではなく、cost, repeat visits, and profitで測る必要がある。

## 用語と調査時の注意点

**IP (intellectual property)** is the legal bundle behind commercial characters. A **license** grants usage rights; a **royalty** is payment for those rights. **GMV (gross merchandise value)** measures merchandise transactions, not consolidated revenue or net profit. **SKU (stock keeping unit)** identifies stock by attributes such as size or color. **Blind box** means the specific item is unknown before opening. 日本語ではそれぞれ知的財産、利用許諾、使用料、流通取引総額、在庫管理単位、開封まで種類が分からない販売形式に当たる。

**SSSG (same-store sales growth)** compares existing stores rather than newly opened stores, though exact definitions require checking the issuer's notes. **Operating margin** is operating profit divided by revenue. **Adjusted profit** excludes items defined by management and is not the same as statutory profit. **Equity-method income/loss** reflects results from investees rather than individual store operations. 似た指標を混同しない。
