# 右手で左手を触ると、どっちが「触っている側」なのか
## The body is an object, but not merely an object.

右手の人差し指で左手の甲を触る。

The right hand touches. The left hand is touched.

簡単。

Now shift attention to the left hand. It is an object being touched, but it is also **the side that feels pressure and warmth**.

左右を入れ替えると、roles reverse immediately.

A desk never does this. 机を触っても、机側から「触られた」と感じることはない。

The body is a thing in the world, but it is not just another thing.

Maurice Merleau-Ponty built phenomenology around this awkward fact.

---

## 1. Is the body a vehicle carrying consciousness?

分かりやすいモデルは、mind inside / body outside。

The mind gives commands. The body executes them. Senses collect data and send it inward.

But lived experience is messier.

階段を降りるとき、「right foot down 18cm, shift centre of gravity 3cm」と計算しない。

The body responds before explicit calculation.

Merleau-Ponty's *Phenomenology of Perception* treats perception as fundamentally embodied.

We do not merely **have** bodies. 私たちは身体を通してworldに**いる**。

---

## 2. “Within reach” is not only centimetres

机の端にペンがある。

You know it is reachable without measuring 62cm.

身体はworldを「届く」「一歩必要」「ちょっと遠い」というpossibilities for actionとして把握する。

A chair appears sittable. A narrow gap appears passable or too narrow. スマホには親指が届く場所と届きにくい場所がある。

The body carries a **lived scale** of the world.

It is an object that can be measured, but also a reference point through which “near,” “far,” “graspable,” and “avoidable” appear.

---

## 3. Over-serious experiment: how far can a tool extend the body?

Merleau-Ponty famously discusses the blind person's cane.

At first, a cane can be felt as a stick in the hand. With skilled use, attention shifts toward **what the tip encounters**.

探索の先端が手から杖先へ伸びたようにexperienceされる。

Everyday cousins of this structure:

- 自転車に慣れると、車幅を身体感覚で扱う
- With a mouse, attention lands on the cursor rather than the plastic shell
- 箸に慣れると、先端で食べ物をつまむ感じになる

This does not mean a tool literally becomes biological tissue.

The phenomenological point is narrower and stranger:

**The practical boundary of “where I act from” can stretch.**

---

## 4. My body is visible, but never completely from here

他人の身体は正面から見られる。でも自分の顔は鏡なしでは見えず、背中も直接は見えない。

Still, I do not experience myself as body-less.

第1回のコップにもhidden sideがあった。But the body's invisibility is special.

My body is both **an object that can be seen** and **the position from which seeing happens**.

It cannot become a completely external object while remaining my lived point of view.

---

## 5. The world is already measured by a body

最初の手の実験へ戻る。

The touching hand can become the touched hand. Subject and object do not stay perfectly separate.

Merleau-Ponty's move is not to remove this ambiguity but to take it seriously.

The stairs appear climbable. The door appears wide enough. The phone appears thumb-reachable or awkward.

**The world is already stained with the habits and capacities of a body.**

「客観的に世界を見ている」というイメージが少し怪しくなる。

There is always a body at the point of view—and that body is already involved.
