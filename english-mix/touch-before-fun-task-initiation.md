# 「掃除する」は大きすぎる。ゴミ袋に触れば勝ちにしてみる
## Use fun as a cue, not an enemy

In private time、やるべきことより、楽しそうなことの方へ簡単に流れる。

掃除。Consulting study. やった方がいいのは分かっている。

But when something interesting is right there、だいたいそっちが勝つ。

長いあいだ、「もっとpriorityを守るべきだ」「first finish what I should do」と考えていた。

でも最近、別の疑問が出てきた。

**Maybe “掃除する” and “勉強する” are simply too large as action units.**

掃除を完了しなくていい。

You do not need one full hour of study.

First, touch it.

ゴミ袋を一枚出す。床の物を3個戻す。Open the case book. 設問を一つ読む。

And you may stop there.

この「touch = success」を、本気でruleにしたらどうなるのか。

---

## 1. FACT｜Fun is strong because the reward is now

Behavioral economicsでは、future benefitよりimmediate cost / rewardを重く見る傾向をpresent biasの枠組みで扱う。

O'DonoghueとRabinは1999年、immediate costを伴うtaskがprocrastinationにつながりやすい構造をモデル化した。

[O'Donoghue & Rabin, “Doing It Now or Later,” American Economic Review, 1999](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.89.1.103)

掃除は、cost now。

勉強も、effort now。

Benefit comes later.

一方、楽しそうなことは、clickした瞬間からrewardが始まる。

Steelの2007年meta-analysisでも、procrastinationと一貫して関連した要因としてtask aversiveness、delay、self-efficacy、impulsivenessなどが挙げられている。

[Steel, “The Nature of Procrastination,” Psychological Bulletin, 2007](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.133.1.65)

So I do not need to turn this immediately into a character judgment.

少なくとも問題の一部は、**how the choice is structured**にある。

---

## 2. FACT｜Sometimes procrastination protects the current mood

Procrastination研究には、emotion regulationとして見る流れもある。

PychylとSiroisは、aversive taskを避けることがshort-term mood repairとして働く見方を整理している。

[Pychyl & Sirois, “Procrastination, Emotion Regulation, and Well-Being,” 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-12-802862-9.00008-6)

This feels familiar.

「勉強しない」と決めているわけではない。

I am avoiding the slightly heavy feeling right before starting.

つまり問題は、60 minutes of studyより前。

**The first 30 seconds may be the real battlefield.**

そこで、「completionを小さくする」より一段手前へ行く。

Make initiation smaller.

---

## 3. INTERPRETATION｜Change the unit from completion to contact

ここからはresearch factではなく、自分向けのinterpretation。

“Clean the room” contains too many hidden decisions.

- Where do I start?
- What do I throw away?
- When is it finished?
- How long will it take?
- What if another mess appears?

開始前なのに、already heavy.

だからsuccess conditionを変える。

**掃除する → touch cleaning.**

**Study consulting → touch the material.**

“Touch”はmetaphorだけにしない。

Make it observable.

~~~text
掃除
NG：部屋をきれいにする
OK：床の物を3個だけ戻す
OK：ゴミを3個だけ捨てる

Consulting study
NG：ケース面接対策をする
OK：ケース問題を1問だけ開く
OK：設問を1回読む
OK：論点を1行だけ書く
~~~

Most important:

**Stopping is allowed.**

「始めたなら10分やろう」にすると、future me detects the trap.

Touch means touch.

Not a disguised full task.

---

## 4. FACT｜If-then plans help action initiation

Implementation intentionは、goalだけでなくcueとactionを結びつける。

> If X happens, I will do Y.

GollwitzerとSheeranの2006年meta-analysisは94のindependent testsをまとめ、implementation intentionsがgoal attainmentを改善し、goal strivingのinitiationにも役立つことを報告した。

[Gollwitzer & Sheeran, “Implementation Intentions and Goal Achievement,” 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1)

ここから自分用に変換する。

Not “I will study tonight.”

Instead:

> **If I am about to start something fun, I will touch one should-task first.**

楽しそうなことを禁止しない。

Use the moment before fun as the cue.

**Fun becomes a start button.**

---

## 5. CONNECTION｜Do not fight temptation; reuse it

ここでtemptation bundlingにつながる。

Milkmanらは、tempting audiobookとexerciseをbundleする方法をfield experimentで検証した。Later, Kirgiosらのlarge field experimentでも、enjoyable wantsとexerciseを組み合わせる方法がgym behaviorを押し上げた。

[Milkman, Minson & Volpp, 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25843979/)

[Kirgios et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obhdp.2020.09.003)

But this is not direct proof of my rule.

「funの前に掃除30秒」は、same interventionではない。

So I treat it as a personal design proposal inspired by neighboring evidence.

The idea I borrow is simple:

**Do not delete the want. Attach the should to it.**

楽しみをpunishmentにしない。

Use it as a cue.

---

## 6. EXPERIMENT｜Turn vague duties into touchable actions

まず、自分の2 tasksをcontact unitsへ変える。

### Cleaning

~~~text
Big task:
部屋を掃除する

Touch options:
・床の物を3個だけ戻す
・ゴミを3個だけ捨てる
・テーブルの1区画だけ空ける
・掃除機を出すだけ
~~~

### Consulting study

~~~text
Big task:
コンサルの勉強をする

Touch options:
・ケース問題を1問だけ開く
・設問を1回読む
・論点を1行だけ書く
・前回ノートの最後の3行だけ読む
~~~

Now the metric changes.

Not:

“Did I study properly today?”

Instead:

**“Did I touch the material at least once?”**

Track contact rate, not completion rate.

---

## 7. PROPOSAL｜My JUST TOUCH rule

2週間だけ、こう試す。

~~~text
JUST TOUCH

Targets:
・掃除
・コンサル勉強

Trigger:
1〜2 times per day
right before starting something fun

Action:
touch either task for 30 sec to 2 min

Success:
touch = success

Stop:
you may stop after 2 min

Continue:
optional

Reward:
after touching, enjoy the fun thing as planned

Log:
○ = touched
☆ = continued for 5+ minutes
× = no touch
~~~

The key is not to chase ☆.

☆ is a side effect.

○ is the actual target.

If every two-minute touch ends after two minutes, the rule still worked.

---

## 8. LIMIT｜Small is not magic

Research does not prove that “30 seconds of contact” universally fixes cleaning or study procrastination.

Implementation intentions have a broad evidence base, but this exact JUST TOUCH rule is my synthesis.

Temptation bundling evidence also comes mainly from exercise contexts.

And deadlines alone are not magic. In a field experiment by Bisin and Hyndman, people showed demand for self-imposed deadlines, but deadline conditions did not increase completion rates in that experimental context.

[Bisin & Hyndman, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geb.2019.11.010)

So this is not a universal productivity law.

It is a personal experiment to reduce starting friction.

That is enough.

---

## 9. DISCOVERY｜The value of touching is not only momentum

At first I thought:

“If I start small, maybe momentum will carry me.”

But research changed the idea a little.

Touching has another value.

It makes the task concrete.

「コンサルの勉強をしないと」が、

> このケースの市場規模の切り方が分からない

に変わる。

「掃除しないと」が、

> 床の服を戻せば、まず歩ける

に変わる。

**Untouched tasks are heavy partly because they have no shape.**

Once touched, the task gains edges.

The goal is not to become a person who always finishes everything.

**Reduce the number of things I have never touched.**

楽しそうな方へ行く自分を止めない。

Just take a 30-second detour toward future me.

That feels much more usable.

---

## APPENDIX｜Prompt to turn “shoulds” into touch tasks

~~~text
You are a task-initiation designer.

Goal:
Convert my oversized, procrastinated tasks into
30-second to 2-minute "touch tasks."

Rules:
- success means initiation, not completion
- every action must be observable
- each action must be finishable in 30 sec to 2 min
- stopping after the touch is fully allowed
- do not use vague verbs like "think," "focus," or "try hard"
- never assume I will continue
- if setup is required, setup itself can be the touch task
- give 3 options per task
- finally create one if-then sentence using:
  "right before I start something fun"

Input:
{{my procrastinated tasks}}
~~~
