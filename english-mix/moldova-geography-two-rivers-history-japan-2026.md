# モルドバはなぜ、二つの川の間で国境と進路が揺れるのか
*English Mix — Three rivers, shifting borders and the links with Japan*

## 要旨――モルドバは「どこにあるか」を知ると「なぜそうなったか」が見える

**Moldova is a small, landlocked country between Romania and Ukraine.** The shape of the land does not explain everything, but it gives us a useful starting point. なぜルーマニアと文化を共有しながら独立国家なのか。Why is part of the territory outside the central government's effective control? The key is to read the map through **three rivers**: the Prut, the Nistru and the Danube.

We will separate *facts, interpretations and hypotheses*. 人口統計も、2024年の国勢調査と2026年の推計値を区別する。Open the [Moldova map](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=7/47.05/28.85) as you read.

## 守1．まず位置を確かめる――海のない東ヨーロッパの国

Moldova lies in **Eastern Europe**, with Romania to the west and Ukraine to the north, east and southeast. 国土は**33,843平方キロメートル**、九州より少し小さい。Its capital is **Chișinău（キシナウ）**, its official language is Romanian（ルーマニア語）, and its currency is the Moldovan leu（モルドバ・レイ）.

The Japanese foreign ministry gives a **2026 population estimate of 2.366 million**, excluding Transnistria. The **2024 census counted 2,409,207 usual residents**, but areas outside the central government's control could not be surveyed. These are different reference dates and coverage rules, not two numbers to add together. [Japan MOFA country data](https://www.mofa.go.jp/mofaj/area/moldova/data.html) / [Moldova 2024 census](https://statistica.gov.md/en/final-results-of-the-2024-population-and-housing-census-geographical-distributio-10121_61877.html).

Romania is already an EU member. Ukraine is fighting against Russia's full-scale invasion and also seeks EU membership. 「西のルーマニア、東のロシア側ウクライナ」という古い二分法は、現在の現実に合わない。

## 守2．最重要の三つの川――西の国境、東の分離問題、南の物流

The **Prut River（プルト川）** forms much of the border between Romania and Moldova. It flows south and joins the Danube. This is not just a physical border: it also reminds us of the historical division of the region in 1812.

The **Nistru River（ニストル川）**, also called the **Dniester（ドニエストル川）**, is closely connected to the breakaway region of **Transnistria（沿ドニエストル地域）**. But the river is not a perfect political dividing line. Bender on the western bank is under the breakaway administration's control, while some places on the eastern bank remain under Moldova's authorities. [OSCE conflict background](https://www.osce.org/cio/119489) / [Census coverage](https://statistica.gov.md/en/final-results-of-the-2024-population-and-housing-census-geographical-distributio-10121_61877.html).

The **Danube River（ドナウ川）** gives the country a precious connection to international shipping. At the southern tip stands **Giurgiulești International Free Port（ジュルジュレシュティ国際自由港）**. It handles more than 70% of Moldova's waterborne imports and exports, according to the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development. In 2026, the operator was sold to Romania's Port of Constanța. [EBRD announcement](https://www.ebrd.com/home/news-and-events/news/2026/ebrd-completes-landmark-exit-from-giurgiulesti-international-fre.html).

Remember three different functions: **Prut = international border; Nistru = contested governance; Danube = trade connection.** 川は単なる自然物ではなく、history and infrastructure が重なる場所なのである。

## 守3．地形と気候――山国ではなく、丘陵・黒土・干ばつの国

Moldova is a land of **rolling hills, valleys, farmland and some forests**, not high mountains. 中央のコドリ丘陵（Codrii）には森があり、最高地点は約430メートル。Northern areas are important for agriculture; the south is more open and generally drier.

One useful term is **chernozem（チェルノーゼム）**, a dark, fertile soil rich in organic matter. JICA describes around four-fifths of the country as covered by fertile black soil. But **fertile soil does not guarantee stable income**. Drought, irrigation, machinery, erosion and market access also matter. [JICA agriculture project](https://www.jica.go.jp/oda/project/MDA-P2/).

The climate has continental features: hot summers and cold winters. 黒土という自然資本は大きな利点だが、weather risk をなくしてくれるわけではない。

## 守4．都市の位置を知る――名前の暗記ではなく役割で覚える

Remember the cities by what they do, not by spelling alone.

- **Chișinău（キシナウ）**: the central capital, where government, education and business concentrate.
- **Bălți（バルツィ）**: a northern city linked to agricultural regions.
- **Tiraspol（ティラスポリ）**: the de facto administrative centre of Transnistria.
- **Comrat（コムラト）**: the administrative centre of Gagauzia.
- **Giurgiulești（ジュルジュレシュティ）**: the southern river-port gateway.
- **Orheiul Vechi（オルヘイ・ヴェキ）**: a historic landscape shaped by the Răut River valley and limestone cliffs.

A mental map works better than a list: north = agriculture; centre = capital; east = divided control; south = autonomy and shipping. この順に位置を押さえると、政治ニュースの地名が具体的な場所になる。

## 守5．なぜルーマニアと別の国なのか――三つの地名を分ける

Three names often get confused. **Moldavia（モルダヴィア）** refers to a historical principality and region that extended across today's borders. **Bessarabia（ベッサラビア）** broadly refers to the area between the Prut and Nistru, including territories now in Ukraine. **The Republic of Moldova（モルドバ共和国）** is the independent state founded in 1991.

A short timeline explains why the borders look the way they do. The medieval principality emerged in the 14th century and later came under Ottoman suzerainty（宗主権）. In **1812**, the Russian Empire acquired Bessarabia. In **1918**, representatives in Bessarabia voted for union with Romania, though its political legitimacy has been disputed. In **1940**, the Soviet Union took Bessarabia and combined much of it with territory east of the Nistru into a Soviet republic. Moldova declared independence on **27 August 1991**. [Japan MOFA historical overview](https://www.mofa.go.jp/mofaj/area/moldova/data.html).

The Soviet takeover was also shaped by the secret protocol attached to the 1939 Nazi–Soviet pact. ただし、a border drawn by empires does not fully explain modern identities. Schooling, family memory, administration and individual choice also matter.

## 守6．同じ言語と異なる国民意識――「モルドバ人」とは誰か

Romanian（ルーマニア語）is the official language. The name “Moldovan language”（モルドバ語）was historically used in political and social contexts, but the legal name of the official language was unified as Romanian in 2023.

Language is not the same as nationality or identity. In the 2024 census, **76.7% identified ethnically as Moldovan and 8.0% as Romanian**. These are self-reported categories, not a scientific test of who someone “really” is. Other communities include Ukrainians, Russians, Gagauz and Bulgarians. [National Statistics Bureau](https://statistica.gov.md/ro/rezultatele-finale-ale-recensamantului-populatiei-si-locuintelor-2024-caracteris-10121_62043.html).

A person may speak Romanian and identify as Moldovan. また、ロシア語が日常で使われることと、住民全員が同じ政治的立場を取ることも別問題である。

## 守7．沿ドニエストル――国際的な領土と実際の支配がずれる

Transnistria（沿ドニエストル地域）is internationally regarded as part of Moldova, yet the Moldovan central government does not exercise effective control over much of it. Fighting broke out in **1992**, and a ceasefire was reached in July. A final settlement has not followed. [OSCE settlement overview](https://www.osce.org/cio/119489).

Three concepts help here. **Sovereignty（領土主権）** is a legal right over territory. **Effective control（実効支配）** means the practical exercise of power. **An unrecognized state（未承認国家）** claims statehood and operates institutions without broad international recognition. These descriptions should not be mixed.

The term **frozen conflict（凍結紛争）** does not mean that people's problems are frozen away. Tensions around movement, property, language, rights and security remain. ロシア軍の駐留、旧ソ連期の工業、エネルギー供給なども関係するため、民族対立だけでは説明できない。

## 守8．ガガウズ自治地域――もう一つの異なる解決

Gagauzia（ガガウズ自治地域）lies in southern Moldova. Gagauz people speak a Turkic language and have traditionally been Orthodox Christians. They also faced tensions during the end of the Soviet period.

But the outcome differed from Transnistria: a **1994 autonomy law** established a special self-governing region inside Moldova. Autonomy（自治）means that a region can make certain decisions locally while remaining part of the state. [OSCE on Gagauz autonomy](https://moldova.osce.org/mission-to-moldova/104969).

The region is not one simple, continuous block on the map. 自治はすべての政治的緊張を消さないが、分離独立とは異なる制度的な解決方法を示している。

## 守9．欧州連合加盟と中立――2026年の政治をどう見るか

Moldova became an **EU candidate in 2022** and formally began accession talks in **2024**. New groups of negotiation chapters opened in June, July and October **2026**, covering institutions, external relations, the internal market and competitiveness. Opening negotiations does not equal joining the Union. [Council of the EU](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/moldova/) / [October 2026 accession conference](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/10/13/enlargement-eu-and-moldova-start-negotiations-on-internal-market-competitiveness-and-inclusive-growth/).

The **European Union（欧州連合）** is about political, legal and economic integration. **NATO（北大西洋条約機構）** is a military alliance. Moldova's constitution declares permanent neutrality, and the country is not a NATO member. These choices should not be confused.

The 2024 referendum on constitutional EU integration passed by only **about 50.4%**. The 2025 parliamentary election was judged competitive by OSCE election observers, yet they raised serious concerns about foreign interference, illicit financing and disinformation. They also criticised late decisions excluding two parties, which weakened effective appeal rights. [OSCE election assessment](https://odihr.osce.org/news/odihr/662254). A pro-European position alone does not remove the need for democratic scrutiny.

## 守10．経済と人口――豊かな黒土があっても、豊かさは自動的に生まれない

Moldova is not an agriculture-only economy. In 2025, wholesale and retail trade accounted for 15.2% of sectoral value added; information and communications for 7.5%; agriculture and manufacturing each for 7.4%. Goods exports reached **$3.78 billion**, while imports were **$10.91 billion**. Romania was the largest export destination (29%) and import source (22.7%). [Japan MOFA statistics](https://www.mofa.go.jp/mofaj/area/moldova/data.html).

The World Bank points to ageing, labour and skills shortages, weak competition and limits of a **remittance-led model（海外送金に依存する経済構造）**. [World Bank overview](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/country/moldova). The 2024 census population was **13.6% smaller** than in 2014. [Census comparison](https://statistica.gov.md/en/final-results-of-the-2024-population-and-housing-census-geographical-distributio-10121_61877.html).

The country also hosted **nearly 140,000 Ukrainian refugees** in early 2026, according to UNHCR. [UNHCR 2026 update](https://www.unhcr.org/md/en/news/press-releases/four-years-full-scale-invasion-refugees-ukraine-moldova-still-need-protection). This creates demands on housing, healthcare and education, while some displaced people also contribute to local economies. 数字は対象と時点を確認して比較しなければならない。

## 守11．日本との接点――外交だけではなく、機械・医療・放送

Japan recognized Moldova on **28 December 1991** and established diplomatic relations on **16 March 1992**. The Japanese embassy in Moldova opened in 2016. [MOFA bilateral relations](https://www.mofa.go.jp/mofaj/area/moldova/data.html).

In 2025, Japanese exports to Moldova were **¥860 million**, while imports from Moldova were **¥7.67 billion**. Japan's exports included machinery; imports included clothes, chemicals, food and wine. Wine is a useful cultural entry point, but it is not the whole trade relationship.

Three examples make Japan's role concrete. **Agricultural modernization（農業の近代化）**: a **¥2.059 billion loan agreement** in 2020 supported equipment for small and medium-scale farmers. [JICA agriculture project](https://www.jica.go.jp/oda/project/MDA-P2/). **Healthcare（医療体制の強化）**: a **¥1 billion grant** signed in 2022 supported equipment in hospitals serving Moldovans and Ukrainian refugees. [JICA medical project](https://www.jica.go.jp/oda/project/2260280/). **Public broadcasting（公共放送）**: a grant of up to **¥192 million** in March 2026 supported programme-production equipment for an educational and cultural channel. [JICA broadcasting project](https://www.jica.go.jp/overseas/moldova/information/topics/2025/1579816_58000.html).

The common idea is **capacity building（社会の基盤となる能力づくり）**: helping people produce food, receive care, and access educational content.

## 守12．批判的に読む――三つのわかりやすい説明の限界

“Moldova is a buffer state（緩衝国家）.” This is a tempting geopolitical shortcut, but it risks hiding the choices of Moldovan citizens. A country is not merely a chessboard for its neighbours.

“People speak Romanian, so they should form one state.” A shared language does not automatically create a shared political preference. 文化的な共通性と国家への帰属意思は区別する必要がある。

“EU membership will solve the problems.” Integration may improve trade and institutions, but it can also create adjustment costs and competition. Rule of law, minority protection and local administrative capacity still require work. いずれの説明も、単純すぎると現実の複雑さを失う。

## 破1．仮説：欧州統合は「思想」だけでなく物流の合理性によって進む

**Fact:** Romania is Moldova's largest trade partner; a Romanian port operator acquired the operator of Giurgiulești in 2026. Negotiations on the EU internal market are progressing.

**Hypothesis:** Economic actors may prefer rules that match the transport routes and business partners they already rely on. 物流のつながりが、制度統合を求める現実的な動機になるのではないか。

**Counterargument:** Trade patterns alone cannot explain political identity or referendum results.

**How to test:** Compare border-crossing time, freight costs, number of EU-oriented exporters and investment flows over time. 単なる貿易額ではなく、企業行動の変化を見る。

## 破2．仮説：沿ドニエストルの難問は、国境ではなく制度の再接続にある

**Fact:** Legal sovereignty and effective administration do not fully align. Infrastructure, industry and public institutions have followed different paths.

**Hypothesis:** Even after a political agreement, integrating taxation, policing, courts, pensions, education and energy contracts could be difficult.

**Counterargument:** Institutional efficiency should never override people's security, language rights or freedom.

**How to test:** Map which institution serves which residents, where businesses are registered, and who bears the cost of an eventual transition. 統合後の生活上の変化まで分析対象にする。

## 破3．仮説：長期的な最大制約は人口減少かもしれない

**Fact:** The census shows a 13.6% decline from 2014 to 2024. The World Bank identifies labour shortages and ageing.

**Hypothesis:** An adverse cycle may emerge: outward migration → fewer workers → weaker tax revenues → weaker public services → further migration. これを累積的な悪循環と呼べる。

**Counterargument:** Return migration, remittances, skills gained abroad and new EU-linked investments may produce benefits.

**How to test:** Track migration by age and region, returning workers, productivity and the costs of maintaining schools and hospitals. 人口減少そのものを結論にしない。

## 離1．国境は一本の線ではない――法・行政・経済・文化の重なり

The most useful insight is that **a border has many layers**. A legal border may differ from the boundary of administration, trade, language or information. Moldova and Romania are legally separate states but share language and economic ties. Transnistria belongs legally to Moldova, yet effective governance is divided.

企業でも同じことが起こる。The formal organisation chart may not match where decisions, customer data or daily work actually flow. A map of legal units is not a map of real cooperation.

## 離2．小国の戦略は、すべてを所有することより接続の設計にある

A landlocked country with access to a river port offers a practical lesson: **connections can matter more than size**. Roads, rail, customs, product standards and shipping partners make trade possible.

But connection creates dependence. If one route or one buyer dominates, the cost of disruption rises. Therefore, **growth through connection** and **resilience through diversification** must be designed together. 小国の貿易にも企業の取引先戦略にも通じる。

## 離3．自治をどう設計するか――同じルールと異なるルール

Gagauzia and Transnistria remind us that cultural difference does not predetermine one political outcome. **Autonomy needs clear competences, funding and dispute-resolution rules.** 地方へ権限を渡しても、財源と手続きがなければ制度は動かない。

This logic applies to relations between central and local governments, corporate headquarters and business units, or sports leagues and clubs. 共通化すべきものと現場に任せるものを一緒に設計する必要がある。

## 理解を助ける用語集

- **Landlocked country（内陸国）**：海岸線を持たない国。River ports（河川港）を使える場合もある。
- **River basin（流域）**：水が同じ川へ集まる範囲。国境をまたぐことがある。
- **Chernozem（チェルノーゼム）**：肥沃な黒土。
- **Suzerainty（宗主権）**：上位の国が外交などに権限を及ぼす歴史的関係。
- **Sovereignty（領土主権）**：領土を統治する法的な権利。
- **Effective control（実効支配）**：現実に統治権力を行使すること。
- **Unrecognized state（未承認国家）**：独立を主張するが、広く国家承認を受けていない存在。
- **Frozen conflict（凍結紛争）**：大規模戦闘の停止後も政治的解決がない紛争。
- **Autonomy（自治）**：国の枠内で地域に一定の意思決定権限を与える制度。
- **Permanent neutrality（永世中立）**：恒常的な中立を掲げる原則。
- **Official development assistance（政府開発援助）**：開発や社会基盤のための公的な協力。
- **Yen loan（円借款）**：返済を前提とする円建て開発資金。
- **Grant aid（無償資金協力）**：原則返済を要しない資金協力。

## 結論――地図を覚えることは、歴史の原因を一つに決めることではない

The Prut marks much of an international border. The Nistru sits close to a divided system of governance. The Danube offers a route to international trade. **Geography creates opportunities and constraints, but it does not make political decisions for people.**

だからモルドバを、単なる「大国の間にある小国」ではなく、**どの接続を増やし、どの依存を減らし、どの違いを制度に包み込むかを考える国**として理解したい。Japan's support for agriculture, healthcare and public broadcasting then becomes more than a list of aid projects: it is part of the practical capacity that allows a country to function.
