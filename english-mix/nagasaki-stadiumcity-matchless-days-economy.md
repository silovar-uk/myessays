# スタジアムなのに、主役は「試合のない日」だった
## 2.2億円の図表ズレから、長崎スタジアムシティ378.3億円を読み直す

On October 1, 2026, Funai Consulting Research released an integrated impact report on Nagasaki Stadium City, V-Varen Nagasaki, and Nagasaki Velca. Headlineは強い。The estimated 2026 economic ripple effect for Nagasaki Prefecture is 378.3億円, roughly eleven times the 33.6億円 level in 2023.

普通なら、ここで“huge”で終わる。But on page 8 of the PDF, my calculator stopped.

Figure 10 lists match-day demand of 89.4億円 for V-Varen Nagasaki and 39.3億円 for Nagasaki Velca. 足すと128.7億円。Yet the same figure labels the match-day total as 130.9億円. Add the non-match-day 162.2億円 and the two printed totals become 293.1億円, even though annual final demand is stated as 290.9億円. 128.7＋162.2, by contrast, lands exactly on 290.9.

This does not overturn the report. むしろ公開版の“試合日計130.9億円”にarithmetic inconsistencyがあり、誤記の可能性が高い、と読むのが自然。ただし発行元の訂正確認までは断定しない。The interesting part is not the 2.2億円 gap itself. Once you start from that tiny discomfort, the 378.3億円 headline stops being something to praise or distrust wholesale and becomes something you can disassemble.

So I cross-checked the 16-page report against Funai’s release, Nagasaki Prefecture’s input-output table and impact-analysis tool, Nagasaki Stadium City’s first-anniversary report, and Nagasaki City’s downtown and 2026 budget policies. 結論は、長崎モデルの妙味は“378.3億円のstadium”ではない。**Match days, non-match days, visitors from outside the prefecture, local residents, companies, and government operate on different clocks and wallets—and the model connects them in one place.**

## 1. 378.3億円は「売上」でも「利益」でもなく、県内で誘発された生産の総額である

First, remove the biggest possible misreading. 378.3億円 is not Nagasaki Stadium City’s revenue. ジャパネットグループのprofitでもない。It is an estimate of how much production inside Nagasaki Prefecture is directly and indirectly induced by Nagasaki Stadium City and the two clubs.

The calculation starts from estimated 2026 final demand of 290.9億円. そこから県外へ流出する調達分などをprefectural self-sufficiency ratesで除き、direct effect 240.8億円を置く。Then 87.0億円 of first-round effects from procurement and outsourcing, plus 50.5億円 of second-round effects from increased employee income and consumption, bring the total to 378.3億円. The multiplier is 1.57.

So “they earned 378.3億円” is much too rough. More precisely, **290.9億円 of demand is estimated to induce 378.3億円 of prefectural production after local procurement and income-consumption linkages are traced.** これはwordingの細部ではない。Facility P&L and regional economic circulation are not the same wallet.

![290.9億円の最終需要から378.3億円の経済波及効果へ至る算出構造。](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/nagasaki-stadiumcity-impact-flow.svg)

*図1：2026 estimate reconstructed from the public report. Final demand 290.9億円 becomes direct effect 240.8億円 after out-of-prefecture leakage, then expands through first-round 87.0億円 and second-round 50.5億円 effects. Source: Funai Consulting Research.*

Nagasaki Prefecture itself publishes the 2020 input-output table as a map of annual transactions in goods and services inside the prefecture and provides an economic-impact analysis tool. 今回のreportは、その40-sector tableをものさしにしている。The useful question is not only “how big is 378.3?” but “which demand travels through which industries, and how much stays in Nagasaki?”

Sources: [Nagasaki Prefecture, 2020 Input-Output Table](https://www.pref.nagasaki.jp/doc/page-764344.html) ／ [Economic Impact Analysis Tool](https://www.pref.nagasaki.jp/doc/page-764376.html)

## 2. 面白いのは「施設が囲い込んだ金」ではなく、47％を施設の外へ逃がしていることである

If you manage a commercial facility, you usually want customers to open their wallets inside your property. 食事も買い物も宿泊もinsideで完結すれば、revenue captureは高い。But from a regional-development perspective, the logic flips. If money never escapes the facility, the city around it does not benefit much.

The report splits 378.3億円 into roughly 200億円 created as Stadium City/club business demand, about 40億円 of direct spending in city hotels, restaurants, transport, souvenirs and similar activity, and about 138億円 spreading through local procurement and income-driven demand. 後ろ二つを合わせた約178億円、47％が“施設の外・長崎県内”へ広がる整理。

One caution matters. “178億円 outside” does not mean 178億円 of direct sales at restaurants outside the stadium. 市中でのdirect demandは約40億円で、about 138億円 is ripple demand through suppliers and household consumption. Even so, the structure matters: the venue acts as a magnet, then passes demand to hotels, restaurants, transport, wholesalers, cleaners, producers, and workers.

![378.3億円を施設内、市中の直接需要、県内産業への波及需要に分けた図。](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/nagasaki-stadiumcity-outside-47.svg)

*図2：About 200億円 originates in venue/club business, while roughly 40億円 is direct city spending and about 138億円 is broader prefectural ripple demand. Source: Funai Consulting Research.*

Read this only as “revenue leakage” and you miss the model. 運営会社のcapture最大化と、地域全体のtransaction volume最大化は完全には同じではない。**Nagasaki’s distinctive move is to attract people strongly enough inside the complex that some of the value can deliberately spill into the city.**

Source: [Funai report, Chapter 2](https://asset.funaisoken.ne.jp/official/press-release/nagasaki-stadiumcity.pdf)

## 3. スポーツ施設なのに、需要の半分超は「試合のない日」に生まれる

The stranger fact is that a sports district is supported by time when sports are not being played. 公表文ではannual demand 290.9億円の約55％がnon-match daysに発生するとし、PDF本文では162.2億円、約56％としている。

The non-match-day side is 92.8億円 in hotel, owned events, rent and related “other distribution sales,” 36.3億円 in commercial-tenant sales, 24.2億円 in lodging/transport/food spending by event stakeholders, and 8.9億円 in prefectural spending tied to external venue events. 合計162.2億円。This is a revenue portfolio designed to keep the 300-plus non-home-game days from becoming blank space.

The PDF’s Figure 10, however, contains the arithmetic gap noted above. V-Varen’s 89.4億円 plus Velca’s 39.3億円 equals 128.7億円, while the printed “match-day total” is 130.9億円. 非試合日162.2億円と合わせ、annual 290.9億円に一致するのは128.7億円のほう。The overall claim still points in the same direction, but the exercise shows why it is worth going back to addition rather than copying headline numbers.

![PDF図表10にある試合日需要の算術上の不整合を確認した図。](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/nagasaki-stadiumcity-matchday-check.svg)

*図3：Arithmetic check of the published values. 89.4＋39.3＝128.7, and 128.7＋162.2＝290.9. The printed 130.9億円 match-day total leaves a 2.2億円 inconsistency, so this essay treats it as a possible typo pending correction.*

After that check, the real point is not whether the non-match share rounds to 55 or 56 percent. **The district is optimizing the utilization of time across the whole city block, not merely stadium utilization.** サッカーがない日はbasketball、両方ない日はhotel, office, retail, live events, kabuki, corporate events, and weekday daytime programming. It looks less like a sports venue and more like a town built from overlapping demand clocks.

Sources: [Funai news release](https://www.funaisoken.co.jp/news/29948) ／ [Funai report, Chapter 3](https://asset.funaisoken.ne.jp/official/press-release/nagasaki-stadiumcity.pdf)

## 4. 長崎では「遠い」が弱点ではなく、宿泊を生む条件へ変換される

For demand to spill out of the complex, visitors need to stay in the city. ここでNagasaki’s geography matters. The report estimates an average overnight-stay rate of 48.1％ for V-Varen away supporters, spending of 30,647円 per overnight visitor—about five times a day-tripper—and 16.7億円 of annual prefectural spending from away visitors.

But 48.1％ is not a simple “48.1％ measured in Nagasaki” observation. 起点は、類似構造を持つHiroshima stadiumの休日GPS dataで実測された30.8％。The model then checks the opponent’s home location, kickoff time, weekday/weekend, and the last train or flight that would allow same-day return, match by match, while adding only five percentage points for the adjacent hotel effect.

That method is fascinating because “far away” is normally framed as a weakness for regional sports. でも19時kickoff後に帰れないなら、そのfriction can become one night, dinner, breakfast, next-day sightseeing, and souvenirs. This does not mean bad access is automatically good. **Distance becomes regional spending only when the value of staying exceeds the pain of getting there.**

And again, estimate and observation should stay separate. 48.1％ is model output. 次に見たいのはNagasaki actuals: real overnight rate, nights stayed, next-day circulation, repeat visits. The model becomes more valuable if post-opening data can gradually replace assumptions.

Source: [Funai report, Chapter 3](https://asset.funaisoken.ne.jp/official/press-release/nagasaki-stadiumcity.pdf)

## 5. 「1000億円÷378.3億円＝約2.6年」と計算すると、きれいに間違える

Now for a deliberately bad calculation. Japanet says the total project cost was about 1,000億円. 1,000 divided by 378.3 is roughly 2.6. すると“two and a half years to pay back the investment?”と言いたくなる。

Don’t. Those are different concepts. 1,000億円 is development investment; 378.3億円 is annual induced production across Nagasaki Prefecture. 後者はoperator cash flowではなく、市中消費、仕入れ、other industries’ production, and consumption generated from employee incomeまで含む。A payback calculation would require operating cash flow, maintenance capex, financing, tax, depreciation, cost of capital, and other project-level data.

This forbidden division teaches a useful habit. 同じ“億円”でも、numerator and denominator can describe different worlds. Stadium debates often place construction cost, revenue, profit, tax revenue, economic impact, and social value on one page. The more identical the unit looks, the more aggressively the concepts need to be separated.

So the role of 378.3億円 is not to answer how quickly 1,000億円 returns to Japanet. **It shows which prefectural transactions are activated by the venue and its events.** Business viability and regional-policy value are connected, but they are not the same calculation.

Source: [Japanet Group public statement on approximately 1,000億円 project cost](https://corporate.japanet.co.jp/notice/2026910/)

## 6. 算出はかなり慎重に作られている。それでも「実績そのもの」ではない

At least from the disclosed methodology, the model is not designed only to inflate the number. 運営管理費はrevenueとのdouble countingを避けるため除外し、Nagasaki input-output self-sufficiency ratesで県外流出を落とし、2026未経過月はconfirmed growth ratesを前年同月へ適用する。The report also says KPIs are classified as actuals, estimates from operating data, or externally set statistical values.

Still, 378.3億円 is not a finalized full-year 2026 actual. It includes months that had not yet elapsed at the October release, and values such as the 48.1％ overnight rate are model-based. さらにFunai’s own release describes the work as a third-party study commissioned by Japanet Holdings. Both the analytical author and commissioning relationship belong in the reader’s mental model.

Input-output analysis itself has assumptions. METI’s simple impact calculator explicitly warns that results may differ from actual effects because the method depends on assumptions. 今回のreportも末尾でaccuracy and completenessを保証しない旨を記す。The useful question is not “true or false, 378.3?” but **which assumptions drive it, which inputs are observed, and which are modeled.**

Public documents also show a production-inducement model, not a full counterfactual net-benefit analysis subtracting every yen that might have been spent elsewhere in Nagasaki without Stadium City. だからeconomic impactを“地域への純追加利益”と一語で読むのは強すぎる。It is one important lens, not the whole welfare calculation.

That does not make the report less useful. むしろlarge sports infrastructureを“建設費が高い／人が来た”の二択から、demand, local procurement, overnight stay, employment, tax, and non-match daysへ分解したことに価値がある。The number works best as common language for a more concrete debate, not as a trophy.

Sources: [METI, simple economic-impact calculation tool](https://www.meti.go.jp/meti_lib/report/2022FY/000505.pdf) ／ [Nagasaki Prefecture Economic Impact Analysis Tool](https://www.pref.nagasaki.jp/doc/page-764376.html) ／ [Funai report, Chapter 5](https://asset.funaisoken.ne.jp/official/press-release/nagasaki-stadiumcity.pdf)

## 7. 施設外47％は自然発生ではない。街側にも「受け皿の設計」が要る

The 178億円 outside the complex can look automatic: build a stadium and money spills into town. 実際には、その間にroads, transit, hotels, walking routes, coupons, information, and open businessesというinterfacesがある。A magnet without circuitry does not create useful flow.

In fiscal 2026, Nagasaki City budgeted 1億638万円 for a “city vibrancy creation” program, including support for a second night of lodging for out-of-prefecture sports spectators, electronic coupons usable at city shops, and free shuttle buses into downtown. 市の説明は、Stadium Cityで生まれるにぎわいを市内全域へ広げ、regional economic spilloverを最大化することを目的に置く。

The city’s downtown revitalization plan also defines a 325-hectare area containing Nagasaki Stadium City, the Nagasaki Station area, Matsugae international cruise terminal, and the traditional central district. 別施策ではcirculation improvement and a walkable urban environmentを掲げる。At that point, Stadium City is no longer treated only as a destination; it becomes a node in a multi-center urban network.

**The 47％ outside share is not completed by the building alone. The venue attracts people, the city receives them, and interfaces create one more night, one more shop, one more trip.** スタジアムの外側を行政や既存商業がどうdesignするかが、next growth leverになる。

Sources: [Nagasaki City mayoral briefing on FY2026 priority projects](https://www.city.nagasaki.lg.jp/site/mayor/76125.html) ／ [Nagasaki City Downtown Revitalization Plan](https://www.city.nagasaki.lg.jp/page/77906.html) ／ [Nagasaki City walkable-downtown initiative](https://www.city.nagasaki.lg.jp/page/87478.html)

## 8. 長崎モデルの本体は、施設ではなく「三つの需要時計」を重ねることにある

The report describes three consumption engines: V-Varen’s stay-inducing external demand, Velca’s high-frequency circulation, and Stadium City’s year-round mixed-use demand. この三つはsame day, same mechanismで稼ぐ事業ではない。That difference is exactly why they can complement one another.

Soccer has fewer home games but can bring larger travel-and-stay spending from around the country. Basketball is indoor, high-frequency, and can stack weekend consumption repeatedly. Hotel, retail, office, and external events operate independently of match schedules and fill weekday and seasonal valleys. 時間軸の違うdemandを同じdistrictへ重ねることで、一つのbusinessの閑散期を別のbusinessが埋める。

![V・ファーレン長崎、長崎ヴェルカ、複合施設の三つの需要時計を重ねる概念図。](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/nagasaki-stadiumcity-three-clocks.svg)

*図4：This essay’s interpretation. Soccer contributes travel and overnight demand, basketball contributes frequency, and hotel/retail/office/events contribute everyday use. The three-category foundation comes from Funai Consulting Research.*

For another city, copying Nagasaki does not mean placing a hotel beside a stadium. 人口、transport, tourism assets, sports calendars, existing retail, and corporate demand all differ. What travels is not the shape of the building but **the design habit of identifying weak days, hours, and seasons, then layering a different demand clock over each gap.**

Source: [Funai report, Chapters 3 and 4](https://asset.funaisoken.ne.jp/official/press-release/nagasaki-stadiumcity.pdf)

## 9. 次に見るべきKPIは、378.3億円そのものより「外へ出た比率」と「空白時間の減り方」である

From here, this is my proposal. 次年度以降を追うなら、total economic impactだけでなくstructural KPIsを並べたほうが、managementにもpolicyにも使いやすい。Track the non-match-day demand share, direct spending outside the complex, Nagasaki-observed away-fan overnight rate and nights stayed, city circulation, spending by external-event stakeholders, hotel occupancy, tenant sales, and office occupancy.

Especially, the “47％ outside” should be split. 約178億円を一塊で見るより、市中direct demand約40億円と、input-output ripple約138億円を分ける。Government interventions can more directly influence the first layer—lodging, dining, transport, shopping, and circulation—and a stronger first layer can thicken downstream effects.

At the first anniversary, Stadium City reported 4.85 million cumulative visits and about 500,000 app users. 次はfootfallだけでなく、“how often,” “why on non-match days,” and “how far outside the site people move”へ指標を進めたい。That would show whether the district is becoming everyday infrastructure rather than a one-off destination.

KPIの役割は、big numberをbiggerに見せることではない。It is to identify which connection works and where flow gets stuck, then feed that insight into the next intervention. Can 378.3億円 become a dashboard rather than a podium? That is the interesting question for year two.

Source: [Nagasaki Stadium City first-anniversary report](https://www.nagasakistadiumcity.com/news/20251031-3/)

## 10. 2.2億円のズレを見つけたら、378.3億円がむしろ面白くなった

Before reading deeply, the story looked simple: a privately financed project of about 1,000億円 creates an annual economic impact of 378.3億円. 大きなbuildingと大きなnumber。Easy to remember.

One round of addition changed the view. 2.2億円のlabel gapがあり、378.3億円はrevenueではなく、178億円 outsideも全部direct shop salesではなく、48.1％ overnightもsimple Nagasaki observationではなかった。The more carefully the numbers were questioned, the clearer the operating design became.

What remained was less glamorous and more powerful. Match days import people; non-match days are carried by hotel, retail, office, and events. 遠さをovernight stayへ変え、施設内の熱狂をcityへ流し、行政がcirculation interfacesを足す。Soccer, basketball, and everyday use run on three clocks, shrinking blank time across the year.

So the portable question from Nagasaki is not “should we build a stadium too?” **How many demand streams in our city run on different clocks, and can we connect them into one economic system?** 378.3億円より、その問いのほうが他地域へ持っていける。
