# 毒ガエルは隠れない、川は盗む
## Look one layer outside the object

### 要旨

A poison frog can be conspicuous on purpose. A river can steal another river’s upstream flow. 全員が同じ情報を持っていてもcoordinationは成立しないことがある。A measured value can sit inside a specification and still deserve rejection. そしてa “nice” web animation can make someone dizzy or nauseous.

These sound like five unrelated facts. But 警告シグナル（Aposematism）、河川争奪（Stream Capture）、共通知識（Common Knowledge）、ガードバンド（Guard Band）、利用者操作によるアニメーション（Animation from Interactions）を横に並べると、one repeated failure of observation appears:

**We stop our attention at the boundary of the named object.**

This essay treats the five terms not as vocabulary items to memorize, but as five ways to move the boundary of observation outward by one step.

> **Information date: 2026-10-05**
>
> These concepts come from different domains and do not share one mechanism. The point is not to claim that evolution, geomorphology, game theory, metrology, and accessibility are secretly the same. 比較するのは「どこまでを一つの現象として観察するか」というviewing habitである。

## 1. 毒ガエルは、隠れる気があまりない

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
If an animal is dangerous, hiding seems like the obvious strategy.
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
Yet in 警告シグナル（Aposematism）, defended animals can use conspicuous colors or markings to tell predators that attacking them is a bad deal.
<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
The U.S. National Park Service describes aposematism as coloration or markings that warn or repel predators because the animal may be toxic, venomous, foul-tasting, foul-smelling, spiny, or otherwise costly to attack. Evolutionary research also studies warning signals through predator vision and learning.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
So the meaning of bright yellow or red does not live inside the color itself. It becomes a warning only when a receiver sees it, associates it with a cost, and changes behavior.
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
To understand a signal, you often have to include the receiver’s response inside the thing you are studying.

ここが面白い。Visibility is not always vulnerability. Under the right conditions, **being noticed can become part of defense**.

Of course, “be conspicuous” is not a universal design principle. Aposematism depends on real defense, predator learning, frequency, and ecology. 広告にそのまま移植すると雑になる。But the observation habit survives the move: when something is visually salient, ask not only “how visible is it?” but **“whose behavior is this visibility trying to change?”**

Sources: [U.S. National Park Service, “What is Aposematism?”](https://home.nps.gov/maca/learn/nature/insects.htm) ／ [Stevens & Ruxton, “Linking the evolution and form of warning coloration in nature”](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3234570/)

## 2. 川は、自分の流れだけでは説明できない

The frog pushed observation outward toward the receiver. The river pushes it outward toward a neighboring system.

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
A river looks like a line from upstream to downstream, but its future can depend on a different drainage network beside it.
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
河川争奪（Stream Capture / River Piracy）occurs when erosion and landscape change allow one drainage system to intercept and reroute the headwaters of another.
<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
Geomorphology studies document cases in which headward erosion intersects another stream and reorganizes watershed boundaries. In California’s Livermore Basin, repeated capture events were reconstructed from both landform evidence and sediment provenance.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
The important change is not merely that one channel bends differently. The network’s source of water and sediment changes.
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
To understand a flow, look not only at its current path but also at the upstream sources that feed it—and at neighboring systems that can capture those sources.

河道変遷（River Avulsion）との違いもここにある。Avulsion is primarily a channel switching to a new course; stream capture is a **network-to-network transfer of upstream supply**.

There is no need to say “business is exactly like a river.” Better to keep the geology intact and borrow only the question. 顧客流入、attention、talent、information――どんなflowでも、current routeだけ見ていると、upstream sourceが別系統へ移った瞬間を見逃す。

Sources: [Mikesell et al., “Stream capture and piracy recorded by provenance in fluvial fan strata”](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0169555X09003778) ／ [Ma et al., “Automatic detection of river capture”](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169555X23000077)

## 3. 「全員が知っている」は、共有の完成形ではない

Now move from rivers to a meeting room.

Tomorrow’s meeting starts at 10:00. Five people each receive a private message saying “10:00.” Everyone knows the time.

But one person may still wonder: **Does everyone else know?**

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
Information sharing has a deeper state than “everyone individually possesses the same fact.”
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
共通知識（Common Knowledge）means not only that everyone knows an event, but that everyone knows everyone knows it, everyone knows that everyone knows everyone knows it, and so on.
<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy distinguishes mutual knowledge—everyone knows—from common knowledge, in which higher-order knowledge recursively holds. Game theory uses this distinction because outcomes can depend on what players know about one another’s knowledge and rationality.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
Information therefore has two lives: content inside individual heads, and coordination value created by beliefs about other people’s heads.
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Sharing is sometimes not the act of distributing information; it is the design of confidence that the information is shared.

Real organizations do not create mathematically perfect infinite layers of knowledge. それでも、visible group announcements, a single decision log, public confirmation, or explicit acknowledgement can reduce uncertainty about whether others are operating from the same premise.

“メールしたから共有した” describes transmission. It does not necessarily describe coordination. The frog taught us to include the receiver. 共通知識はさらに進んで、**receivers’ beliefs about other receivers**まで観察へ入れる。

Source: [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “Epistemic Foundations of Game Theory”](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/epistemic-game/)

## 4. 9.99は、10.00未満だから安全――とは限らない

Suppose the upper specification limit is 10.00. The instrument reads 9.99.

Pass. Easy.

Or maybe not.

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
If a conformity decision uses only the measured value and the specification limit, uncertainty in the measurement disappears from the decision.
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
ガードバンド（Guard Band）introduces a decision boundary that can sit inside the specification limit, taking measurement uncertainty and decision risk into account.
<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
The U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology（NIST）defines a decision rule as a rule describing how measurement uncertainty is accounted for when stating conformity. Its work on conformity assessment also emphasizes the economic consequences of accepting nonconforming products and rejecting conforming ones.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
The specification boundary and the acceptance boundary therefore do not have to be identical. Once uncertainty and asymmetric error costs enter the picture, the decision line itself becomes a design choice.
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Uncertainty is not merely a footnote attached to a number; it can move the boundary of action.

More conservative is not automatically better. A narrower acceptance zone may reduce one kind of error while increasing another. だからthe interesting question is no longer “Is 9.99 below 10.00?” It becomes **“Which mistake costs more, and how should uncertainty change our decision rule?”**

We started with a number and ended with governance. 数字の一段外側には、judgmentがある。

Sources: [NIST, “Assessment of Conformity, Decision Rules and Risk Analysis”](https://www.nist.gov/publications/assessment-conformity-decision-rules-and-risk-analysis) ／ [NIST, “Decision Rule”](https://www.nist.gov/glossary-term/21621)

## 5. 画面は、画面の端で終わっていない

Click a button. A card expands. The background slides backward. Scroll and the image moves at a different speed.

If it is polished, we may call it delightful.

But delight is not a property of pixels.

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
Motion on a screen is visual design, but it is also sensory input entering a human body.
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
WCAG 2.2 success criterion 2.3.3, 利用者操作によるアニメーション（Animation from Interactions）, says interaction-triggered motion animation should be disable-able unless the motion is essential to the functionality or information.
<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
W3C explains that unnecessary movement such as parallax scrolling can trigger dizziness, nausea, and headaches in people with vestibular disorders, and lists support for reduced-motion preferences among sufficient techniques.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
That changes the review question. “Does this animation feel smooth?” is not enough. You need to trace the chain from interaction to visual motion to sensory system to physical reaction.
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
The boundary of an interface is not the edge of the screen. The user’s body is part of the system that receives the design.

ここで五つが一気につながる。A warning color needs a predator. A river needs its neighboring drainage network. Shared information needs beliefs about other people’s knowledge. A measurement needs a decision rule. Motion needs a body.

The “outside” is different every time. だからこそ、この比較は一つの万能理論ではなく、observation trainingとして使える。

Sources: [W3C, “Understanding Success Criterion 2.3.3: Animation from Interactions”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions) ／ [W3C, “Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2”](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)

## 6. 五つの専門語は、五種類の「外側」を教えている

Strip away the disciplines for a moment.

| Concept | What first catches the eye | One layer outside |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 警告シグナル | 色・模様 | receiver learning and avoidance |
| 河川争奪 | 一本の流路 | neighboring drainage and upstream supply |
| 共通知識 | information held by each person | knowledge about others’ knowledge |
| ガードバンド | measured value and spec | uncertainty, error cost, decision rule |
| 利用者操作によるアニメーション | movement on screen | physical response in the user |

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
The lesson is not the grand philosophical claim that “everything is external.”
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
These five mechanisms are genuinely different: evolution, erosion, epistemic structure, conformity assessment, and vestibular response should not be collapsed into one causal story.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
What repeats is a failure mode of attention. We see a named object—color, river, number, interface—and let the noun decide where observation stops.
<!-- level:4 role:bridge -->
But causal structure often continues across that noun boundary into receivers, adjacent systems, higher-order beliefs, decision rules, or bodies.
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
The value of technical vocabulary is therefore not only that it gives us more labels; it can teach us where our unit of observation was too small.

This is why lateral thinking works better when the analogy stays imperfect. 「河川争奪＝市場競争」と同一視したら、difference disappears. Better to understand the geological phenomenon precisely, then carry home only the question: **Where did I stop looking too early?**

## 7. 「対象の一段外側」を見るための五問

You do not need to use the technical terms in every meeting. Keep the five questions.

1. **Receiver** — これは何を表すかではなく、受け手の行動をどう変えるか。
2. **Source** — 今どこを流れるかではなく、what feeds the flow, and can that source move elsewhere?
3. **Higher-order knowledge** — 皆が知るだけでなく、do they know that everyone else knows?
4. **Decision boundary** — 値はいくつかではなく、how should uncertainty and error cost move the action threshold?
5. **Body** — 画面や製品がどう動くかではなく、what does that stimulus do to a human body?

A reusable prompt can be even shorter:

> **Expand the boundary of the object you are observing by one layer. Check the receiver, neighboring network, beliefs about others’ knowledge, uncertainty in measurement and decision, and the user’s body. Which missing relation changes the causal explanation, risk, or design decision? Use cross-domain analogy only to widen the unit of observation, not to claim identical mechanisms.**

「システム思考をしよう」まで大きくすると、Monday morningには使いにくい。One layer is enough. Frog → predator. River → neighboring watershed. Meeting → knowledge about knowledge. Measurement → decision boundary. Interface → body.

## 8. 毒ガエルへ戻る

At the beginning, the frog was just funny.

派手な毒ガエルを見ると、「危険なのにそんな目立って大丈夫なんか」と思う。そのsmall weirdnessから調べ始めた。

After walking sideways through river piracy, common knowledge, guard bands, and accessibility, the frog looks different.

A color is not a warning by itself.

A river does not determine its own flow by itself.

Information does not create coordination merely by being possessed.

A number does not determine a decision merely by being measured.

A screen does not contain the full effect of its own motion.

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
We often mistake the boundary of the noun for the boundary of the cause.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
These five concepts, by entirely different routes, show causal structure continuing beyond the object that first catches attention.
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Maybe the best technical terms do more than name unfamiliar things: they move the point where we stop looking.

毒ガエルは隠れない。

To understand why, you have to look past the frog and into the predator.

A surprising amount of the world seems to require the same extra step.
