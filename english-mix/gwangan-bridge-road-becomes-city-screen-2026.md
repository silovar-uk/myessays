# 渡る人には見えない橋――広安大橋はなぜ釜山の巨大なスクリーンになったのか

*English Mix / 2026年10月28日。Japanese is the reading base; English is woven into the same argument. Official documents support the facts; hypotheses are labeled.*

A bridge is supposed to help people cross. それならなぜ釜山の広安大橋は、渡らない人を海辺に集めるのだろう。

夜景写真では a gigantic illuminated bridge が湾を横切る。On the road, drivers keep moving. 海岸では人が立ち止まり、遠くからその全景を見る。The paradox is simple: **the best view of a bridge often belongs to someone who does not use it.** 運転中に橋の全景を眺めようとしたら、まず安全運転をやり直していただきたい。

![Gwangan Bridge illuminated purple in 2019](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fd/Gwangan_Bridge%2C_Busan%2C_lighted_up_in_purple_for_BTS%2C_June_2019.jpg/1280px-Gwangan_Bridge%2C_Busan%2C_lighted_up_in_purple_for_BTS%2C_June_2019.jpg)

*Photo 1：Busan Metropolitan City citizen photojournalist Kwon Ki-hak／[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gwangan_Bridge,_Busan,_lighted_up_in_purple_for_BTS,_June_2019.jpg)／[KOGL Type 1](https://www.kogl.or.kr/info/licenseType1.do). This is a 2019 scene, before the 2025 lighting upgrade.*

## 守｜7.42キロの橋は、最初から夜景のために造られたわけではない

The engineering facts come first. 広安大橋は全長 **7,420 m**、上下二層で合計8車線。The central suspension span is 900 m; the remaining 6,520 m consists of connecting structures. 「7.42キロ全部が吊り橋」ではない。[Busan City](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/the-seven-bridges-of-busan)／[Academy of Korean Studies](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0067772)。

Construction began in 1994, and official opening followed on January 6, 2003, after temporary use for the 2002 Asian Games. 歩行者、自転車、一般の二輪車は原則通行できない。It is a motor-vehicle route, not a seaside promenade. A tourist landmark and a restricted roadway can occupy the same space.

Why build across the sea? 1993年の議会議事録には、新しい海雲台の住宅地をめぐる traffic demand と、1991年の交通影響評価が残る。A 2015 city-planning official also cited port logistics, congestion on Suyeong-ro, and traffic generated by Haeundae's development. [1993 council minutes](https://council.busan.go.kr/assem/user/assem/minute/printReview.busan?minuteSid=16274)／[2015 council minutes](https://council.busan.go.kr/assem/user/assem/minute/printReview.busan?minuteSid=12439)。

A 2012 Busan City retrospective describes the alternatives: road widening, elevated routes and a waterfront road. 陸側には補償費、騒音、排気、住宅地への影響という問題があったという。This is a participant's recollection, not a complete independent comparison, but it exposes the original problem. [Busan City retrospective](https://www.busan.go.kr/news/specnews/view?dataNo=36460&gugun=Prev)。

So the bridge did not begin as a giant light installation. **It began as an attempt to move traffic where the crowded land route could not easily absorb it.** 海は障壁であると同時に、新しい道路を通す空間だった。

*Photo 2：ZENsan／[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Gwangan_Bridge.jpg)／[CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Unmodified.*

## 守｜同じ橋でも、運転する人と海岸で眺める人では「商品」が違う

Now there are two audiences. 運転者が受け取るのは a shorter or more convenient journey。海岸で橋を見る人が受け取るのは a view, a moment, a reason to linger。The same asset delivers different forms of value to different people.

![Two audiences of Gwangan Bridge](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/gwangan-bridge-2026-two-audiences.svg)

*Figure 1：Two audiences, two benefits. Conceptual model, not measured economic effects.*

In 2013, Busan City completed a major LED landscape-lighting project. 同時に、海岸沿いには音楽用スピーカー54基を配置したと発表している。That feature makes little sense as a benefit to drivers moving across the bridge. It clearly speaks to people looking at it from the waterfront. [Busan City (2013)](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/bsnews01/795845)。

The key distinction is not simply day versus night. **One group saves time; another chooses to spend time.** 交通インフラの価値と、滞在空間を形づくる価値が同じ橋に重なっている。

## 破｜LEDを約2倍にしたのは、橋を約2倍明るくするためとは限らない

There is a neat number hidden in the story: 13,465. 2025年10月26日、照明改修工事が完了し、11月から運用が始まった。According to Busan City, the number of lighting fixtures rose from **7,011 to 13,465**, while spacing fell from **1 m to 0.5 m**. Moving lights and richer media content were also added. [Busan City (2025)](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/ai-translated-press-releases/1706013)。

![Lighting fixture comparison in Gwangan Bridge](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/gwangan-bridge-2026-lighting-density.svg)

*Figure 2：7,011 → 13,465 fixtures, approximately +92%; spacing 1 m → 0.5 m. This is not a chart of electricity use, visitor growth or profit.*

More fixtures do not automatically mean more tourism. They increase the possible detail of lighting expression. これは LED screen とまったく同じ構造だという意味ではないが、光で模様やメッセージを描く自由度は高まる。The city specifically highlighted clearer messages, colors and resolution.

This is where a consultant should resist the temptation to invent a conversion rate. 13,465 divided by 7,011 is roughly 1.92. That does **not** make tourism revenue 1.92 times larger. We must separate **Input**（照明器具数）、**Output**（表現できる内容）、**Outcome**（訪問・滞在・支出への影響）。A brighter spreadsheet is not the same thing as a brighter economy.

## 破｜橋の光は「案内板」ではなく、都市の予定表にもなり得る

A permanent structure can become a changing stage. 2025年の釜山花火祭りでは、橋のライトアップが催事の演出に組み込まれ、イベント前に市民からのメッセージをトラス部分に示す計画も発表された。[Busan Fireworks Festival (2025)](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/ai-translated-press-releases/1705569)。

Meanwhile, Visit Busan listed different weekly themes for the October 2026 Gwangalli M Drone Light Show. 注意したいのは、the drone show is a separate event, not the bridge's own LED system。But both share the waterfront as a place where people come to watch. [Visit Busan (October 2026)](https://www.visitbusan.net/schedule/view.do?boardId=BBS_0000010&dataSid=6388&menuCd=DOM_000000304010000000)。

A bridge cannot be rebuilt every weekend. A programme of light and events can change. ここからの仮説は、fixed urban capital can gain an editable layer of experience、というものだ。However, the available evidence does not establish how many additional visitors this causes.

## 破｜「きれいだから成功」では、橋を使わない市民の負担が消えてしまう

A nightscape has operating costs. 照明の電力と保守、イベント時の traffic restrictions、混雑、安全管理。Busan's 2025 fireworks announcement explicitly addressed bridge closures, crowd safety and extra public transport. It also discussed redundancy after a previous lighting-cable damage incident. [Busan City (2025)](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/ai-translated-press-releases/1705569)。

That does not prove every suspected ecological or social harm. 今回の資料では、照明による生態系への定量的影響や電力削減率を確認できなかった。A potential risk is not a measured outcome.

A serious evaluation would track three dimensions: **transport performance**, **waterfront dwell time**, and **full operating costs**. 曜日、季節、天候、イベント、規制の有無を考慮し、対照地点や時期も比較する。The relevant public value is more complicated than either traffic volume or Instagram photos.

## 離｜スタジアムや駅前広場にも、「利用していない人が受け取る価値」がある

Now leave the bridge. スタジアムの観客は試合を見る。Nearby businesses may see foot traffic; residents may experience excitement, noise or congestion. 駅を通り抜ける人は移動するが、駅前広場で待ち合わせる人は stay。In each case, the direct user is only one part of the story.

Economists use **externalities（外部効果）** to discuss positive and negative consequences borne by others. ただし景色を眺める本人の楽しさをすべて外部効果と呼ぶのは雑だ。The more important question is editorial: **Who benefits without using the facility for its primary purpose?**

A stadium, a station, a bridge. 新しい施設を建設する前に、既存の建物を見ている人、近くにいる人、困っている人の存在を調べてみる。Not everything needs more lights. Perhaps some assets need a second reading before they need a second investment.

## 結び｜渡り終えたとき終わる橋と、立ち止まるほど始まる橋

For the driver, the bridge's job ends at the other shore. 海岸で眺める人にとって、その橋は立ち止まる理由になる。**One structure makes time shorter for one person and worth spending for another.**

At first, the story seemed to be about 13,000 lights. 調べると、海雲台の交通、住民への影響、道路の選択、2013年と2025年の照明投資という、時間差で積み重なった仕事が現れた。The bridge has not stopped being a road. People have learned to value it from a different position.

The strange part is not the 13,465 fixtures. **An old asset becomes a different asset when someone discovers a different way to use—or simply look at—it.** 光はその変化を見せている。

## 調査ノート｜確かめたこと、まだ確かめていないこと

**Verified**: 7.42 km overall, 900 m central suspension span, two decks/eight lanes, a 2003 official opening, transport-demand origins, 2013 lighting, 2025 upgrade to 13,465 fixtures, and scheduled Gwangalli drone performances in October 2026.

**Interpretation**: Drivers and spectators receive different benefits from the same bridge. Programmable lighting and recurring events may provide a repeatable experience around a permanent structure. No causal estimate of tourist arrivals or spending was established here.

**Next research**: Conduct matched weekday/weekend waterfront observations before and after illumination, log weather and events, interview drivers, residents, visitors and businesses, and compare actual traffic, crowd and cost data. We should not confuse beautiful photographs with measured economic impact.

**Precision note**: “Second longest bridge in Korea” is sensitive to definitions of bridge versus connected road; the article therefore uses verified dimensions rather than a rank. The 13,465 count concerns lighting *fixtures*, not necessarily individual LED chips.
