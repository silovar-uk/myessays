# おかわりくんは、なぜこんなに三振できたのか
## How can one hitter have 2,166 strikeouts and 482 home runs?

Here is a strange pair of numbers.

2026年9月6日現在、中村剛也はNPBで**2,163 games**に出場している。

His career strikeout total is **2,166**.

三振のほうが3つ多い。

```text
Games played   2,163
Strikeouts     2,166
               ↑
        strikeouts win by 3
```

Of course, this is mathematically normal. A hitter can strike out several times in one game.

でも、見た目はかなり変である。

**He has struck out more times than he has played games.**

そして2166三振は、NPBのall-time recordでもある。

If that were the whole story, we could call him a classic power hitter: big swings, big home runs, lots of strikeouts.

でも、もう一つ数字を置く。

**482 home runs.**

That total ranks 10th in NPB history and, as of September 6, 2026, first among active players.

つまり同じ選手の中に、

- **most career strikeouts in NPB history**
- **10th-most career home runs in NPB history**

が同居している。

Huge failure count. Huge success count.

That combination is much more interesting than either record alone.

---

## 1. “Okawari-kun” sounds almost too casual

中村剛也は1983年生まれ。大阪桐蔭高校から西武に入り、2003年から一軍でプレーしている。

The Lions' 2026 player profile lists six home run titles: 2008, 2009, 2011, 2012, 2014 and 2015.

Six times, he led the Pacific League in home runs.

That is a heavy résumé.

呼び名は「おかわりくん」。

Number Web traces the nickname to a 2005 spring-camp morning speech in which Nakamura spoke of “okawari” — asking for another helping — as a favourite word.

It is an unusually domestic nickname for an all-time slugger.

「おかわり」は、もう一杯、もう一皿、という言葉である。

But after looking at his career totals, the word starts to feel different.

**Another plate appearance. Another season. Another chance.**

この人のキャリアそのものが、ものすごい回数の「もう一度」でできているからだ。

---

## 2. In 2011, one man hit more home runs than one whole team

The clearest way to understand Nakamura's peak is probably 2011.

その年のパ・リーグ本塁打ランキング。

```text
2011 Pacific League HR

Takeya Nakamura   48   ████████████████████████
Nobuhiro Matsuda  25   ████████████▌
3rd place         18   █████████
```

Nakamura hit 48.

Second-place Nobuhiro Matsuda hit 25.

**The gap was 23 home runs.**

松田がもう一度ほぼ同じ本数を打って、ようやく届く距離である。

Then the comparison becomes silly.

The entire Chiba Lotte Marines team hit **46 home runs** in 2011.

Nakamura hit **48** by himself.

```text
Takeya Nakamura alone      48 HR
Chiba Lotte, entire team   46 HR
```

**One player beat one team by two.**

野球は9人でやる競技のはずなのに、比較表だけ急に一対一になる。

And there is an even wider view.

The six Pacific League teams combined for 454 home runs in 2011.

Nakamura's 48 represented about **10.6% of every home run hit in the league**.

西武全体は103本。そのうち中村が48本なので、チーム本塁打の約46.6%を一人で打っている。

Nearly half of Seibu's home runs came from one bat.

---

## 3. Context makes 48 look stranger, not smaller

2011 was also a severe low-offence season in NPB after the introduction of the unified ball.

パ・リーグ6球団の本塁打を合計すると、2010年は742本、2011年は454本。

That is a drop of about **38.8%**.

We should be careful here.

この差をすべてボールだけの因果として説明することはできない。また、中村自身も2010年は85試合出場だったため、彼の2010年25本と2011年48本を単純に比較するのも雑である。

But the league environment clearly changed, and 48 home runs stood out dramatically inside that environment.

パ・リーグ.comも2011年について、中村一人でリーグ本塁打の10.6%を占めたことを紹介している。

**A raw number tells us what happened. Context tells us how weird it was.**

48本は大きい。

でも「454本のうち48本」と見ると、急に形が見える。

---

## 4. So, were the strikeouts the “price” of the home runs?

ここで2166三振に戻る。

It is tempting to tell a simple story:

> He swung for home runs, so strikeouts were the price he paid.

That is too neat.

**Strikeouts and home runs are not a simple trade-off.**

More strikeouts do not automatically produce more home runs.

三振はアウトであり、わざわざ美談にする必要もない。

Career strikeouts mix together several different things:

- how often a hitter strikes out
- how many plate appearances he receives
- how many seasons he remains good enough to keep receiving them

中村は2026年9月6日時点で**8,326 plate appearances**を持つ。

2,166 strikeouts divided by 8,326 plate appearances is about 26.0%.

His 482 home runs work out to roughly one home run every 17.3 plate appearances.

The important point is not that 26.0% is good or bad by itself.

重要なのは、**歴代最多というcounting recordは、三振しやすいだけでは作れない**ことである。

You also need enormous opportunity.

何千回も打席を任され、何年も一軍に残らなければ、2166まで失敗を記録することすらできない。

So the question changes.

Not:

**Why did he strike out so much?**

But:

**Why did he keep earning another plate appearance for so long, despite all those strikeouts?**

この問いのほうが、中村剛也のキャリアに近い。

---

## 5. The strikeout record does not describe his swing

A huge strikeout total can make us imagine a crude, violent swing.

でも、中村の打撃についての専門家評はかなり違う。

In 2023, former catcher Motonobu Tanishige described Nakamura as rotating around a compact axis, almost as if he were turning inside a drum. Akio Saito focused on how little visible effort there seemed to be in the swing despite the distance of the ball.

別の記事では笘篠賢治が、手の伸縮とbody turnの組み合わせを高く評価している。

A 2020 Pacific League analysis also highlighted his ability to hit home runs not only to the pull side but to the opposite field.

つまり、

**“all-time strikeout leader” does not mean “mindless all-or-nothing swinger.”**

記録のラベルと、実際の技術は別物である。

That is one reason the career is interesting: a technically refined, often effortless-looking swing produced both an extraordinary number of home runs and an extraordinary number of strikeouts over a very long time.

---

## 6. A useless calculation that looks suspiciously perfect

ここで、少しだけ役に立たない計算をする。

2,163 career games. 482 home runs.

```text
2,163 games ÷ 482 HR = 4.488 games per HR
```

2,166 career strikeouts. 482 home runs.

```text
2,166 SO ÷ 482 HR = 4.494 strikeouts per HR
```

Almost identical.

```text
Games per HR       4.488
Strikeouts per HR  4.494
                   ^^^^^
              nearly the same
```

This is **not a useful baseball metric**.

試合数と三振数は違う種類のカウントなので、この一致に競技上の因果関係はない。

It is numerical coincidence — nothing more.

でも、妙にできすぎている。

If we compress his whole career into one absurd sentence:

**About every 4.49 games, Nakamura strikes out about 4.49 times and hits one home run.**

圧縮しすぎて、野球選手ではなく周期現象みたいになった。

Still, the coincidence points us back to something real.

All three huge totals come from the same thing: **time and repeated opportunity.**

---

## 7. Not “strikeouts are fine,” but “the whole package was worth another at-bat”

At the beginning, I thought the story might be about tolerance for failure.

「三振を恐れず振り続けたから成功した」という話にすれば、きれいにまとまる。

Too clean, again.

A strikeout still costs an out.

But Nakamura won six home run titles between 2008 and 2015.

In 2011, one player produced 10.6% of all Pacific League home runs.

そして40代まで現役を続け、2026年9月6日時点で482本まで積み上げた。

So the better description is colder and more interesting:

**Even after counting the strikeouts, the total value of his batting kept earning him the next plate appearance.**

その「次」が8326打席まで続いた。

That is how both sides became historic.

The all-time strikeout record is certainly a record of failed plate appearances.

同時に、それだけ長く失敗を記録できる場所に居続けたという、longevity and opportunityの記録でもある。

A career total never measures only the event itself.

It also measures how long you were allowed — and good enough — to keep accumulating events.

---

## 8. “Okawari” sounds different now

Before researching this, “Okawari-kun” sounded like a friendly nickname about food.

調べ終わったあとでは、勝手にもう一つの意味が重なる。

Strike out.

Come back tomorrow.

Get another plate appearance.

Hit a home run.

Strike out again.

Come back again.

**Another at-bat. Another year. Another homer.**

「おかわり」は、もう一杯という意味の言葉である。

But it also sounds strangely right for a player who kept returning to the batter's box for 24 seasons.

2,166 strikeouts are part of that repetition.

482 home runs are part of it too.

片方だけ見ると、中村剛也は「三振の多い打者」か「すごいホームランバッター」になる。

Look at both, and he becomes something more interesting:

**a hitter who returned to the place where both success and failure are recorded, an almost absurd number of times.**

そう考えると、「おかわりくん」という呼び名が、最初より少し格好よく聞こえる。

## Useful English chunks

- **all-time record** — 歴代記録
- **career total** — 通算の合計記録
- **plate appearance** — 打席
- **home run leader** — 本塁打王／本塁打ランキング首位
- **opposite field** — 逆方向
- **low-offence environment** — 得点・打撃が低調な環境
- **trade-off** — 一方を得るために他方を失う関係
- **earn another plate appearance** — 次の打席を任されるだけの結果を出す
- **longevity** — 長期間にわたって現役・高水準を続けること
