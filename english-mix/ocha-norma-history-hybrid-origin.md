# OCHA NORMA史――長い下積みと未経験者は、どう同じスタートラインに立ったのか
## From trainee unit to audition winners, ten-member formation, major debut and an eight-member present: building one team from different entrances

### 要旨

OCHA NORMA did not suddenly begin when ten people were gathered on December 12, 2021.

The earliest line goes back to July 2019, when selected Hello Pro Kenshusei members—Kirara Yonemura, Kanami Ishiguri, Nanami Kubota, Ruru Kanemitsu and Madoka Saito—started working as the provisional “Hello Pro Kenshusei Unit.” The official announcement explicitly said the membership was not yet fixed, replacements were possible, and no permanent unit name would be given yet.

So the prototype of OCHA NORMA was not a finished group. It was a **pre-debut operating environment**.

Later the structure changed. In July 2021, Natsume Nakayama, Ruli Hiromoto, Miku Nishizaki and Momo Kitahara were added to the new-group project, expanding the active unit to eight. Then open-audition winners Sumire Tashiro and Roko Tsutsui joined.

On December 12, 2021, those ten people were finally announced as OCHA NORMA.

The important point is that their previous careers were highly unequal.

Some had spent years inside the trainee system. Some came through the Hokkaido trainee route. Others had only just entered through an open audition and had almost no internal Hello! Project performance history.

Normally, this would be treated as a large seniority gap.

OCHA NORMA did something more interesting.

**December 12, 2021 became the group formation date for all ten.**

Their pasts remained different, but inside OCHA NORMA everyone became a founding member.

This essay calls that design **hybrid origin**: integrating different entry routes into one founding period.

---

## 1. 2019――最初から「未完成」で始まった

On July 11, 2019, Hello! Project announced a new internal trainee unit.

The starting members were Kirara Yonemura, Kanami Ishiguri, Nanami Kubota, Ruru Kanemitsu and Madoka Saito.

The official language is revealing:

- the unit would be used to monitor members more closely on the path to debut,
- they would gain practical experience similar to existing Hello! Project groups,
- the lineup might change,
- and the unit would remain unnamed because the membership was not yet fixed.

This was more than a temporary song unit.

It was an **intermediate organization between training and debut**.

In business terms, it resembles assigning candidates to a real operating team before deciding the final organizational form.

That has a clear benefit: people gain real experience before formal launch.

But it also creates uncertainty. Even after being selected, members still do not know whether the final group will exist in the same shape.

That long in-between period is part of why several future OCHA NORMA members accumulated unusually long pre-debut histories.

---

## 2. 2021――4人から8人へ。Selection becomes integration

By 2021, the active trainee unit consisted of Yonemura, Ishiguri, Kubota and Saito.

On July 6, Hello! Project announced that Nakayama, Hiromoto, Nishizaki and Kitahara would join the new group project, creating an eight-member structure.

Still, there was no final group name.

At the same time, management announced a new open audition and said the final shape of the group would be decided within the year.

That decision matters.

They could have closed the system at eight and moved directly to debut.

Instead, they left the architecture open.

The future group would be built through:

**internal development + open recruitment.**

Not trainee promotion alone.

Not public audition alone.

Two talent pipelines would converge before launch.

That is the central organizational feature of OCHA NORMA.

---

## 3. 2021年12月12日――Turning unequal histories into one founding cohort

On December 12, 2021, at a Kenshusei performance in Zepp Tokyo, the new group name was announced:

OCHA NORMA.

The name combines *ocha-no-ma*—a warm shared living-room space—with the Latin *norma*, meaning a standard or norm. The official intention was to create a new-generation standard that could entertain people in the “living room.”

At the same event, audition winners Sumire Tashiro and Roko Tsutsui joined, completing the ten-member lineup.

Now people with very different levels of Hello! Project experience stood in the same group.

The experience gap did not disappear.

But its organizational meaning changed.

They were no longer simply “long-time trainees plus newcomers.”

They became **the ten founding members of OCHA NORMA**.

This is an important identity mechanism.

If long-serving trainees alone are treated as the “real core,” newcomers can remain permanently secondary.

If the trainee history is ignored, years of development are flattened into nothing.

OCHA NORMA did neither.

Past experience remained an individual asset.

But **the group’s zero point was shared.**

Everyone could be part of the founding generation.

---

## 4. 「新しい標準」という、かなり大きな名前

Calling yourself a “new standard” is ambitious.

But the standard does not need to mean “best at everything.”

The formation history suggests another interpretation:

**A modern standard can have multiple entrances.**

You can come through years of Kenshusei training.

You can come through Hokkaido Kenshusei.

You can come through an open audition.

The point is not to make everyone’s past identical.

The point is to create shared work after entry.

Seen this way, OCHA NORMA reflects a broader shift in Hello! Project: development became increasingly multi-route rather than a single straight line from trainee to debut.

---

## 5. 2022――「クラウチングスタート」は偶然ではない

On July 13, 2022, OCHA NORMA made its major debut with “Koi no Crouching Start / Omatsuri Debut da ze!”

The phrase “crouching start” fits the group unusually well.

They did not all start running on debut day.

Some had already been running for years.

The trainee unit itself had existed since 2019.

Yet the major debut could still function as a shared starting line.

That is the logic of OCHA NORMA:

**individual starting points can differ; the group starting point can still be shared.**

Later that year, the group received a New Artist Award at the 64th Japan Record Awards. The Grand New Artist Award went to Aimi Tanaka, so it is important not to inflate the claim: OCHA NORMA was one of the four New Artist Award recipients.

The meaningful point is that within one year of formation, the group had already reached a major external recognition stage.

---

## 6. 10人固定――Don’t equalize the past; accumulate a shared future

For several years after formation, OCHA NORMA operated as ten members.

This is where the hybrid-origin design starts to work.

The organization did not need to erase pre-debut seniority differences. Instead, it could simply accumulate **shared post-formation history**.

Singles.

Tours.

Events.

Theater.

Regional projects.

The more work ten people did together, the less important the question “who had been a trainee for how many years?” became relative to “what have we done together as OCHA NORMA?”

Organizations cannot make everyone’s past identical.

But they can make the future increasingly shared.

> Don’t standardize the past. Build a common future.

That is the strength of hybrid origin.

---

## 7. 2024〜25年――The founding shape breaks

A founding-cohort identity also has a weakness.

When someone leaves, the original story—“these ten are OCHA NORMA”—is disrupted.

That happened in 2024–25.

Sumire Tashiro entered a period of inactivity for health reasons and graduated on March 11, 2025.

Kanami Ishiguri had also limited activities for health reasons and graduated on March 21, 2025.

The official announcements describe both decisions in relation to their health and their own wishes. There is no need to turn diagnoses or private difficulty into dramatic storytelling.

The organizational fact is enough:

**OCHA NORMA became an eight-member group for the first time.**

There is an additional symbolic layer.

Ishiguri came from one of the longest trainee histories in the group.

Tashiro entered through the open audition at the final stage of formation.

In other words, in March 2025 the group lost one member from each of its two contrasting entry paths.

Yet the remaining eight continued.

That suggests the legitimacy of OCHA NORMA did not belong exclusively to the trainee side or the audition side.

**After the two pipelines had been integrated, the shared OCHA NORMA history itself became the core asset.**

---

## 8. 2026――Eight members, same name, new work

As of 2026, the official profile lists eight members:

- Madoka Saito, leader
- Ruli Hiromoto, subleader
- Kirara Yonemura
- Nanami Kubota
- Natsume Nakayama
- Miku Nishizaki
- Momo Kitahara
- Roko Tsutsui

On July 8, 2026, the group released its sixth single, “Dantotsu de Aishite / Mata, Anata ni Koikogareteirunda,” while continuing spring and autumn live tours.

The striking point is that the group did not immediately refill the lineup to ten.

At least as of August 2026, OCHA NORMA is operating as eight.

So continuity does not mean “restore the original headcount.”

It means **keep operating the shared culture created by the hybrid founding process even after the founding shape changes.**

---

## 9. Juice=Juiceとの違い――Capability reproduction vs entry-route integration

Juice=Juice also uses multiple talent sources: trainees, auditions and transfers.

But its central organizational logic is different.

Juice=Juice repeatedly turns new members into people who can meet a demanding vocal standard. That is why the previous essay used `capability_pipeline_survives`.

OCHA NORMA’s defining feature appears earlier in the lifecycle.

It integrated different entry routes **before debut**, converting unequal prehistories into one founding cohort.

Juice=Juice:

> Bring people in and reproduce a shared capability standard.

OCHA NORMA:

> Bring different histories together and create a shared starting line.

Similar tools, different organizational focus.

---

## 10. hybrid originの弱点――A shared founding date does not make the past equal

This model should not be romanticized.

Someone who spent years as a trainee and someone who debuted shortly after an open audition did not pay the same time cost or live with the same uncertainty.

Saying “we all became founding members on the same day” does not erase that asymmetry.

Hybrid origin retains differences in:

- experience,
- age,
- preexisting fan recognition,
- and narratives of “who waited longer.”

The goal therefore cannot be to pretend the past was equal.

The real test is whether roles remain open to revision after formation.

A long-serving trainee does not need to remain permanently central.

A late entrant does not need to remain permanently “the newcomer.”

Shared work must be able to rewrite the relationship.

---

## 11. OCHA NORMAが示した「新しい標準」

The “new standard” in the group name may not describe one perfect idol template.

It may describe a production system:

**people developed through different routes can still be integrated into one functioning team.**

2019 provisional unit.

2021 expansion to eight.

Open audition.

Ten-member formation.

2022 major debut.

Two graduations in 2025.

Eight-member activity in 2026.

At no point did everyone have the same background.

Yet OCHA NORMA continued to exist as one organization.

---

## 結論――同じ過去を持たなくても、同じ歴史を作り始められる

The OCHA NORMA story can be reduced to one idea:

> **People do not need the same past to become one founding team.  
> They need a point from which they can begin making the same history.**

This essay names that survival model **`hybrid_origin_survives`**.

Long-term trainees.

Later-selected trainees.

Open-audition winners.

Their prehistories were different.

But after December 12, 2021, they began accumulating “OCHA NORMA history” together.

Even after the original ten became eight, that shared history remained usable.

**Organizational unity does not require everyone to come from the same place. It requires people with different pasts to become capable of moving in the same direction.**

That may be the most interesting meaning of OCHA NORMA’s promise to become a “new standard.”

### Survival Model

**`hybrid_origin_survives`**

> Multiple talent-development and recruitment routes are integrated into one founding period before debut, making shared post-formation history—not identical prehistory—the source of organizational legitimacy.
