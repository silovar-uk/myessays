# マスターへの基礎力は、技を増やすより「読めなくても返せる」を増やす
## From reads to reliable answers――モダン・マリーザの15分・30分training plan

I wanted to practice cancel Drive Rush. 最大溜め立ち強Pでplus framesを取り、その後に中アシストコンボを使いたい。Raw Drive Rushから弱アシストコンボにも行きたい。ハーフハートもanti-airとして使いたい。DiamondからMasterを目指すなら、add one more tool at a time――最初はそう考えていた。

But the 10-question check changed the order. 対空はpredicted Gladiusが中心で、reaction anti-airはまだ安定していない。Punish knowledgeも薄い。Drive Impactもread-based Gladiusで処理することが多く、Drive Gaugeはburnoutしてから使いすぎに気づく。Knockdown後は前へ出るが、okizeme patternは固定されていない。

> Cancel Drive Rush was the first thing I wanted to train. 診断後、そのpriorityは下がった。Not because cancel Drive Rush is weak. その前に、毎試合払っているlossが見つかったからだ。

At the same time, opponent habits are a strength. 相手の飛び、暴れ、throw techなどを意識して覚えられる。投げ抜けをむやみに押さず、take the throw to avoid bigger damageという方針もある。最大溜め立ち強Pなどで相手のDrive Gaugeを削り、burnoutを狙うgame planも持っている。Problem is the opponent with no obvious weakness, especially one who touches first with fast buttons.

Here, “Master fundamentals” is not a scientific universal checklist. 今回の10問から、current playで高頻度に起こりそうな取りこぼしを減らすためのoperational checklistとして使う。Game data is based on the state as of 2026-09-26, after the Year4 adjustment on 2026-08-03.

## 1. 10問の答えは「good reads, weak default answers」という形を示した

First, separate the answers as facts. 相手の癖を見て記憶できる。投げ抜けを意識的に減らし、guard、weak mash、Drive Reversalをdefensive optionsとして練習している。Hit confirmも一部ではできる。相手の弱点が見つかれば複数のwin conditionsを作れ、相手のDrive Gaugeを削ってburnoutを狙う発想もある。

- Confirmed strengths：相手の反復行動の観察、read-based Gladius、投げを必要経費として扱う守備方針、一部のhit confirm、相手のDrive Gaugeを狙う攻め。
- Gaps：reaction anti-air、move-by-move punish knowledge、reaction Drive Impact、own gauge awareness、repeatable okizeme after common knockdowns。
- In training now：weak mash、Drive Reversal、raw Drive Rush、cancel Drive Rush、Half Heart。
- Known loss pattern：相手の弱点を見つけられず、fast-startup buttonsで先に触られる試合。

The next part is interpretation. 現在の強みは「相手を見て、conditional answerを変える力」にある。一方、anti-airやpunishのように、相手のpersonalityを読む前から用意できるanswersがまだautomaticではない。When the opponent shows a clear habit, reads can cover the gap. 癖が見えない相手ほどfoundation gapが出る、というhypothesisが立つ。

> Reads are not the weakness. 読みが当たらない時間を支えるminimum guaranteeが薄い。

This is not mechanically proven by rank or win rate. だから「Masterにはanti-air success rate X%が必須」とは言わない。Goal is not to remove reads. その下へ「jumpを見たら落とす」「big recoveryならpunish」「knockdownならsame setup」「use gauge only after checking it」というfloorを敷く。

## 2. Practice should move “fixed → mixed → match” so execution does not become the only skill

The official SF6 manual says Training Mode can record up to eight opponent actions, turn slots on or off, and replay them repeatedly. つまり、同じjumpを100回落とすだけでなく、“jump, Drive Impact, ground button, or nothing?”を混ぜて、screenを見てanswerを選ぶdrillがgame内だけで作れる。

Motor-learning research compares blocked practice, where one task is repeated in a block, with random practice, where several tasks are interleaved. 2024年のsystematic review and meta-analysisでは、34 studies・1421 participantsのtransfer tasksでrandom practice側にmedium effectが出た。ただしapplied settingsだけではeffectはsmall and not statistically significantで、heterogeneityも大きい。Another 2024 meta-analysis found that retention benefits in applied settings were almost negligible.

So this is not “random practice is always better.” SF6-specific researchでもない。The useful principle is narrower: **separate learning the input from learning when to choose it**.

1. Fixed practice：one situation only. まずinputを迷わなくする。
2. Mixed practice：two, three, then four opponent actions. 正しいanswerを選ぶ。
3. Match check：take one observation theme into ranked, then recreate one failure from replay in Training Mode.

This three-step loop fits the current problem better than adding volume. It tells us whether the failure was input, perception, selection, or knowledge.

## 3. The 15-minute version is fixed at “reaction 4, punish 3, okizeme 3, rush 3, spacing 2”

Do not assume long daily sessions. 15分しかない日は、maximum comboを伸ばすよりhigh-frequency lossesへ毎回触る。The allocation below is a proposal from this diagnosis, not a scientifically optimal schedule.

1. 4 min: reaction gate. Record forward jump attack, Drive Impact, fast ground button, and do nothing. Start with one action, then two, then mix all four.
2. 3 min: punish. Pick only one matchup for the day. 直近のloss replayから“close punish”と“big recovery”を一つずつrecordして反復する。
3. 3 min: okizeme. Start with one setup after medium Dimachaerus. 安定したらstrong Dimachaerus with no follow-upを一つ追加する。
4. 3 min: Drive Rush. Practice crouching MP → cancel Drive Rush → standing MP on both sides. 最後の1分だけhit/blockをrandomにする。Say your Drive Gauge aloud before starting.
5. 2 min: fast-button spacing. Record the ground button that caused trouble. 最初はattackせずrangeだけ見て、その後just outsideに立ってwhiff後にcrouching MPなどを返す。

Success is not “the combo came out.” It is “the right answer was chosen for the right event.” Anti-air drillでDrive ImpactへHalf Heartを出したらfailであり、do-nothing slotで焦ってbuttonを振ってもfailにする。

> Not “could I do it?” but “did I do it only when I should?”

This 15-minute menu is a minimum line, not a plan to improve everything every day. すでにstableな項目があれば、そのminutesをmost failed itemへ移してよい。

## 4. The 30-minute version adds selection and correction, not more copies of the same input

If you have 30 minutes, do not simply run the 15-minute menu twice. 増やすのはsame inputのrepetitionsではなく、mixed decisionsとrecent-match reconstructionの時間にする。

1. 5 min: fixed execution. Check Half Heart anti-air, Drive Impact counter, and crouching MP cancel Drive Rush on both sides.
2. 8 min: mixed reaction. Replay jump, Drive Impact, fast ground button, and wait. Choose anti-air, counter, guard, or no action.
3. 5 min: punish. 直近のloss replay一試合から、実際に見逃したtwo movesだけ抜き出す。
4. 5 min: okizeme. Repeat the medium Dimachaerus setup, then mix wake-up 4F, invincible reversal, and guard.
5. 4 min: spacing versus fast ground buttons. Move across the hit/whiff boundary and answer only after a whiff.
6. 3 min: replay note. Pick one ranked observation theme. 終了後にfailureを“input / perception / selection / knowledge”のどこかへ分類する。

The goal is not to become good at Training Mode. 実戦のfailureをTraining Modeへ持ち帰れることがgoalになる。Menu can stay stable, but the raw material should come from your own losses.

## 5. Move anti-air and Drive Impact one step from “read” to “see and answer”

Half Heart is currently a 9-frame crouching heavy punch; the normal version is -6 on block. Holding it changes the move and makes startup 21 frames. だから最初のroleは“地上でなんとなく置く”ではなく、正面jumpを見たときのnormal anti-airに限定する。Keep predicted Gladius as a weapon, but add one route for jumps you did not predict.

- Slot 1: forward jump attack. Answer with normal Half Heart. Do not hold it.
- Slot 2: Drive Impact. Answer with Drive Impact counter.
- Slot 3: step forward into a fast ground button. First answer is guard. 確定反撃を知っている場合だけpunishへ進む。
- Slot 4: do nothing or guard. Do not force an attack. “Wait” is also a correct answer.

First 2 minutes: Slot 1 only, ten Half Hearts. 次の1分はSlots 1 and 2. 最後の1分でfour-way mixへ増やす。If execution falls apart, the mix came too early.

Do not record only anti-air hit rate. Count how many of 20 replays had the correct classification and response. 提案上のgraduation lineは16/20以上をtwo sets連続とするが、これはresearch-derived thresholdではなく、difficultyを上げるためのoperational ruleである。

Reference: [SF6 Official Web Manual: Training Mode Record Settings Tips](https://game.capcom.com/manual/SF6/ja/switch2/page/8/6) ／ [Current Marisa frame data](https://frame-search.com/?character_name=%E3%83%9E%E3%83%AA%E3%82%B6&lang=ja-jp)

## 6. Learn punishes by harvesting two moves from a loss, not by memorizing every character at once

The current punish problem comes before combo damage: “I do not know what is punishable.” しかもreal matchではframesだけでなくdistanceも必要になる。A move can be unsafe and still not be punishable by your chosen button if it does not reach. “距離による”という感覚は正しい。

- Small gap: current crouching LP starts in 4 frames. 近距離のfast punishを確認する入口にする。Current Modern Marisa guides also use crouching LP routes for 4F punish situations such as blocked Drive Reversal.
- More distance: do not force one universal answer. If crouching LP does not reach, test an actually reaching option such as medium assist sequence in Training Mode.
- Big recovery: against invincible reversals with enough recovery, keep a charged standing heavy punch punish-counter route as one “big punish” pattern. 最大routeへ伸ばすのはlaterでよい。

Pick only one character per day. 直近のloss replayから“blocked it but did nothing” movesをtwo choicesだけ取る。Check with frame data and Training Mode, then repeat ten times on both sides. Next day, harvest two more.

The important part is to separate types of failure. “Did not know it was punishable,” “knew but misjudged distance,” and “chose the right starter but dropped execution” are different problems and need different practice.

Reference: [Modern Marisa neutral, combos, and okizeme, Year4](https://www.sukoreru.com/sf6-modern-marisa) ／ [Current Marisa frame data](https://frame-search.com/?character_name=%E3%83%9E%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%82%B6&lang=ja-jp)

## 7. Do not learn every okizeme setup; lock down only “medium Dimachaerus” and “heavy Dimachaerus, no follow-up”

Right now the post-knockdown plan is “go forward.” If every knockdown route is added at once, search cost rises. まず実戦で使いやすいtwo situationsへ限定する。

- First setup: medium Dimachaerus ender → raw Drive Rush → standing MP. Current Modern guide lists Drive Rush standing MP after medium Dimachaerus. まずstrikeを固定し、stableになったらcommand throw and guardを追加する。
- Second setup: heavy Dimachaerus with no follow-up → forward jump → standing MP immediately on landing. Current guide presents standing MP, command throw, and guard from this timing. ドライブゲージが少ない時のlow-cost planにもなる。

Start with wake-up guard fixed and reproduce the timing ten times. 次にwake-up 4Fをmixして、strikeがmeatyになっているかを見る。Then add invincible reversal and learn to choose guard instead of auto-attacking. Okizeme also follows fixed → mixed.

The goal is not “best okizeme.” It is to remove hesitation at the moment of knockdown. Two automatic patterns come first; corner-only setups, safe jumps, and more charged options can come later.

Reference: [Modern Marisa neutral, combos, and okizeme, Year4](https://www.sukoreru.com/sf6-modern-marisa)

## 8. Do not postpone cancel Drive Rush; attach it to the habit of checking your gauge

Cancel Drive Rush still belongs in the plan. Current crouching MP is 8F, +3 on hit, -2 on block, and cancelable. The current Year4 Modern guide uses crouching MP → cancel Drive Rush → standing MP as a core sequence on both hit and block; on block it creates close advantage and branches into weak assist, throw, or shimmy.

Charged standing heavy punch is also +5 on block in current frame data. The current Modern guide shows a Drive Gauge pressure sequence using charged standing heavy punch into its heavy follow-up, then medium assist. だから“大Pで有利を取った後に中アシストコンボ”というideaは、future offense patternとして残せる。

- Execution drill: crouching MP → cancel Drive Rush → standing MP, ten times on each side. Start with dummy on guard.
- Decision drill: random hit/block. On hit, continue the combo; on block, choose weak assist or throw.
- Raw Rush drill: raw Drive Rush → weak assist combo, five times on each side. Keep it as one entry route, not the only entry route.
- Plus-frame drill: test charged standing heavy punch pressure against 4F mash, Drive Impact, invincible reversal, and guard. Learn where autopilot becomes dangerous.
- Gauge-check drill: say your current Drive Gauge aloud before every cancel Drive Rush. 練習中だけ“3 bars or less = no cancel Drive Rush”というartificial constraintを置いてもよい。

That last rule is not optimal match strategy. It is a training constraint for noticing the gauge before spending it. 実戦ではlethal, corner, opponent gauge, and round state change the answer. For now, “look before spend” matters more than optimization because the current habit notices overspending after burnout.

## 9. When fast buttons beat you, reproduce the distance before trying to press faster

“I lose when the opponent touches me with fast-startup buttons” is useful self-observation, but the cause is not yet known. Maybe the button is simply fast. Maybe you entered its range first. Maybe you pressed while minus. Maybe Gladius was interrupted before startup. Different causes need different answers.

1. Take one “fast button stopped me” moment from a loss replay and recreate the exact opponent move and starting distance.
2. For the first ten trials, press nothing. Move slightly forward and back and learn the hit/whiff boundary.
3. For the next ten, stand just outside that boundary. When it whiffs, answer with a button that actually reaches, such as crouching MP or standing heavy punch. If nothing reaches, taking space is also success.
4. Finally mix fast ground button, forward jump, Drive Impact, and wait. Move from “waiting for one poke” back toward real-match attention.

The target is not raw reaction speed. 調べたいのは“自分からstrong rangeへ入っていないか”“whiff後に何が届くか”“groundを見た瞬間にjumpを通していないか”である。

Do not automatically race startup with startup. First learn where the opponent fast button works and where it stops working. その後でもclose-range pressureに負けるなら、4F mashやDrive Reversalなどdefensive answersを検討できる。

## 10. Graduation should be based on choosing correctly, not on rank points

Rank points are noisy as a practice pass/fail signal. Opponent character, matchmaking, and short-term variance all affect the result. だからeach drillにsmall graduation ruleを置く。The numbers below are operational proposals, not scientific standards.

- Reaction gate: at least 16 correct choices out of 20 in the four-way mix, twice in a row.
- Punish: for the two selected moves, complete the reaching punish at least 8/10 on both sides.
- Okizeme: first reproduce the same timing 10/10 against guard, then change your answer correctly when 4F, invincible reversal, and guard are mixed.
- Cancel Drive Rush: 10/10 on both sides, then at least 16/20 correct follow-ups in random hit/block.
- Gauge management: use a ten-ranked-match block and count “unplanned burnout caused by not looking at gauge.” First target: make one block with zero.
- Fast-button response: after replay review, explain the loss in one sentence as distance, minus frames, startup interruption, or whiff-punish problem instead of only “it was fast.”

In ranked, do not consciously track everything at once. Pick one observation theme for each ten-match block. 勝敗とは別に、foundationがmatchへtransferしたかを見る。

- Number of clear forward jumps where you attempted reaction anti-air.
- Number of Drive Impacts where you tried to react with Drive Impact.
- Number of blocked moves you recognized as punishable, and how many you actually punished.
- Number of unplanned burnouts.
- Number of knockdowns where you entered the chosen okizeme pattern.

With this record, a loss does not end as “their buttons were fast.” It produces one scene to bring back into Training Mode.

## 11. Master fundamentals add “I do not need the read” underneath “I can read”

This started as a cancel Drive Rush problem. After research, cancel Drive Rush still remains in the plan, but its meaning changes. It is not only a new damage tool. しゃがみ中Pでtouchした後にoffenseを続け、同時にown gaugeを見る教材になる。

Half Heart changes in the same way. It is not another anti-air to collect. “I predicted jump, so Gladius” gains a second path: “I did not predict it, but I saw the jump, so Half Heart.” Punish learning becomes two moves harvested from losses, not a giant frame-data exam. Okizeme becomes two automatic knockdowns before adding more options.

> Master practice is not mainly about adding moves. 「見えたら返る」「倒したらsame setup」「ゲージを見てから使う」を増やすworkである。

Keep the ability to read the opponent. If a weakness appears, exploit it as before. When nothing is obvious, fall back to the floor. 調べる前は“more offense tools”がnext stepに見えた。After research, the next thing to increase was not the number of moves, but the number of **reliable answers that still work before you know who the opponent is**.
