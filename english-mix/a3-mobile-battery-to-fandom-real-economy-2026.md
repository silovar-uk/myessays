# From Power Banks to Fan Experiences――A3は「推しの熱」をどう事業にしたのか

*Research date: 2026年10月27日。Primary sources first. 事実、当事者の主張、分析、提案を区別する。*

## 守｜会社の起点は「アニメ愛」より先に、売り場にあった

In October 2026, A3's news includes anime-themed drinks and pop-up shops across Chinese cities. ところが創業期の商品は、まさかのモバイルバッテリー。From charging phones to selling fan experiences. 語呂は美しいが、事業転換はもっと地道な現場観察から始まった。[公式ニュース](https://athree3.com/)／[代表インタビュー](https://note.com/athree3/n/na1a76b1beb0c)。

Founder Takashi Ozawa recalls selling mobile chargers to electronics retailers, learning packaging and pricing by asking buyers directly. しかし競争が増えて値下がりした。At a retail shelf, he noticed a popular character product and began exploring anime merchandise stores. 当時の客層や行列への驚きは本人の回想であり、市場全体の統計調査ではない。[A3 note](https://note.com/athree3/n/na1a76b1beb0c)／[社長名鑑](https://shachomeikan.jp/industry_article/6202)。

The discovery was not simply “put a famous character on a battery.” **The same physical product can compete under different buying motives.** 電池なら性能と価格、キャラクターグッズなら作品とのつながりが購買理由になる。This is a shift in the definition of demand, not merely product decoration.

## 守｜版権を取って商品を作っても、初めは売れなかった

The early launch was not an instant success. 小澤氏は、権利元への交渉の末に商品化したものの、期待どおりに売れなかったと語る。Retailers were asked to stock the items, but promotion to fans after release was insufficient. **Getting the rights is not the same as creating demand.**[A3 note](https://note.com/athree3/n/na1a76b1beb0c)。

Later, an upcoming game title was suggested by a rights holder, and A3 reportedly released related products early and achieved a hit. これは創業者の証言である。The title and exact sales value are not verified in that passage. 成功の規模や因果を誇張する必要はない。[取材原文](https://note.com/athree3/n/na1a76b1beb0c)。

A license is a necessary input, not a finished business. 商品化をゴールにすると、告知、需要予測、店頭展開、配送、在庫という本番が抜け落ちる。A3's later one-stop service model can be read as an answer to this early operational gap. ただし会社が明示した唯一の成長原因という意味ではない。

## 守｜「IPリアルプラットフォーム」は四者をつなぐ仕事の束である

A3 calls its business an “IP real-world platform.” **IP means intellectual property**, here mainly the rights around works, characters and designs. 「リアル」は画面の外のグッズ、カフェ、舞台、店舗、イベント。The company turns content into physical products and experiences, not just into more screen time.[事業紹介](https://athree3.com/business/)。

Four parties matter: **rights holders**, **creators and manufacturers**, **sales channels**, and **fans**. 一つのアクリルスタンドでも、許諾、デザイン監修、製造、在庫、告知、注文、発送、CS対応がある。A tiny product may sit on top of a long chain of contracts and operational decisions.

But a company calling itself a “platform” does not automatically prove a two-sided digital marketplace or a network effect. まずは企画・製造・小売・権利者向け支援が組み合わさった事業体として捉えたい。The operating model matters more than the label.

## 守｜2012年の一人会社は、制作・店舗・製作委員会・海外へと段階的に拡張した

The official timeline begins with incorporation in 2012, online commerce and content in 2014, the GraffArt design series in 2016, and an Ikebukuro retail store in 2018. 2020年にはアニメ製作委員会へ参加し、2021年にはMomentumBaseとJoy Creationを開始。海外事業は2023年に本格化し、2025年に中国・無錫、2026年7月に上海の店舗へ進んだ。[公式沿革](https://athree3.com/company/information/)。

As of August 2026, A3 reported **436 group employees and 339 at the parent company**, both including part-time staff. 資本金は1,241万円。These counts say something about operating scale, but not about margin, profitability or headcount growth by employment category. 今回確認した資料から事業別損益は復元できない。[会社概要](https://athree3.com/company/information/)。

## 守｜A3の各サービスは「誰の仕事を引き受けるか」で分けると見通しがよい

Forget the brand list for a moment; ask **whose job is being done**. Joy Creation covers planning and selling goods, events, theatrical projects and more. MomentumBase helps businesses handle production and sales of original merchandise. 公式は「在庫リスクゼロ」を訴求するが、実際のリスク配分は契約確認が必要だ。[Joy Creation](https://athree3.com/business/joycreation/)／[MomentumBase](https://athree3.com/business/momentumbase/)。

eeo is the customer-facing ecosystem of stores, online shopping and cafés. In January 2026, A3 announced more than **400,000 registered members**, and in July launched a smartphone app. モバイルバッテリーの会社が、今度は自社アプリでファンとの継続接点を作る。The product has changed; so has the relationship with the buyer.[会員数発表](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001061.000012048.html)／[公式ニュース](https://athree3.com/)。

Shikakeru EC proposes a revenue-sharing approach to joint IP commerce: planning, online shop operations, inventory and delivery. OSHI+ sells fan-activity goods, while the B2B advisory service “A3推し活部” started in April 2026. 権利元、販売企業、ファンのどれを顧客にするかで、同じ「IP事業」でもビジネスモデルは違う。[シカケルEC](https://athree3.com/shikakeru-ec/)／[推し活部](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001112.000012048.html)。

## 破｜「40万人」「550社」「2,547件」は強い数字だが、同じ種類の数字ではない

A3 mentions over 400,000 eeo members, 550 rights-holder companies with past transactions, 2,547 cumulative IP properties and more than 5,000 IP projects. **These are four different denominators.** 会員数、取引企業数、作品数、企画件数を全部「顧客数」にしてはいけない。[会員数](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001061.000012048.html)／[シカケルEC](https://athree3.com/shikakeru-ec/)／[推し活部](https://athree3.com/oshikatsu/)。

Distribution numbers also vary by source: Joy Creation says more than 2,000 outlets, a 2026 announcement says over 8,000 domestic outlets, and a 2024 announcement says over 12,000. 同じ定義で数えた増減とは限らない。They may refer to different networks, time points or eligibility rules, but public documents do not resolve the difference.[Joy Creation](https://athree3.com/business/joycreation/)／[2026資料](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001112.000012048.html)／[2024資料](https://athree3.com/2024/09/27/20240927/)。

Registered users are not monthly buyers. A cumulative IP catalogue is not an active catalogue. 取引可能店舗も常時取扱店舗とは違う。To assess economics, we would need purchase frequency, order value, repeat purchases, active buyers and inventory turns. **A number becomes useful only when its measurement unit is clear.**

## 破｜「推し活3.9兆円」は実売上ではない。算式まで戻ると意味が変わる

In October 2025, A3 estimated the Japanese fandom-spending market at about **¥3.9 trillion a year**, with **¥2.9 trillion** attributed to physical products, events, travel and related spending. 調査は15～69歳の23,445人へのインターネット調査を基礎に、性年代を補正。推しがいる割合34.8％から2,828万人、支出する人を約1,997万人と推定した。支出者から抽出した1,000人の選択式回答を使い、平均月額16,356円を算出している。[A3調査方法](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000993.000012048.html)。

The arithmetic is approximately **19.97 million spenders × ¥16,356 per month × 12 months = ¥3.9 trillion**. This is a survey-based estimate, not audited transaction data. 2.9兆円も費目比率を適用した計算値である。Using category midpoints, treating the top “¥300,000 or more” bracket as ¥300,000, self-reporting and online-panel selection all affect precision. 公開された方法だからこそ検証可能だ。[原調査](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000993.000012048.html)。

Japan's Association of Japanese Animations separately reported **¥3.8407 trillion for the broad 2024 anime market**. Nearly the same headline number, very different definitions. A3の推し活調査には芸能・スポーツ等も含まれ、対象年も異なる。Do not add these markets together or divide company sales by either figure without defining the actual addressable market.[日本動画協会](https://ajaweb.xsrv.jp/info/2579)／[A3調査](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000993.000012048.html)。

## 破｜「売れる商品」を作るには、権利・在庫・ファン体験の三つを同時に設計する

“Licensing” is often casually called “getting the rights,” but legal permissions depend on the work, design, use and contract. キャラクターの使用、別の衣装を描く行為、原作とアニメの絵柄などで権利範囲は違い得る。Japan's Agency for Cultural Affairs explains the importance of specifying permitted uses and alterations. 「商品化権」という一語ですべてを説明するのは危うい。[文化庁FAQ](https://www.bunka.go.jp/seisaku/bunka_gyosei/kibankyoka/faq/index.html)。

Operational questions follow: who pays for design, who carries minimum guarantees, how royalties work, what the production lot is, and who absorbs unsold stock. **Revenue share** means dividing revenue under a contract; it does not tell us the actual percentage or whether the base is gross sales or profit. A3の全案件の料率・契約条件は公開されていない。[シカケルEC](https://athree3.com/shikakeru-ec/)。

My hypothesis is that A3's advantage, if proven, is a repeatable operating system across rights, product planning, channels and fan feedback—not just an eye for popular IP. 小澤氏は感覚を「数値化」し、仕組み化することを強みと説明する。ただし案件別粗利、在庫回転、顧客維持率がなければ優位性の程度は未検証だ。[社長名鑑](https://shachomeikan.jp/industry_article/6202)。

## 破｜「推しのためなら買う」を当然視すると、消費者保護と作品価値を見失う

A fandom business can create joy and spending pressure at the same time. A3 has blind-packed merchandise and also lists online lottery products among its offerings. ランダム形式を直ちに違法とはいえない。一方、重複購入、コンプリート欲求、抽選条件、返品可能性は、消費者の観点で確認すべきだ。[商品例](https://gamehack.jp/385557/)／[Joy Creation](https://athree3.com/business/joycreation/)。

A random item for sale is not legally identical to a promotional prize. 日本の景品表示法は、顧客誘引、取引付随性などの条件に基づいて「景品」を定義する。Different mechanics can have different legal consequences. 本稿はA3に違反があると主張するものではない。[消費者庁FAQ](https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/representation/fair_labeling/faq/card)／[景品表示法](https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/representation/fair_labeling)。

Short-term transaction volume and long-term fan trust may diverge. 何度も買わせることと、気持ちよく応援を続けてもらうことは同義ではない。A stronger customer-value model would track choice, satisfaction, repeat engagement and the burden of unwanted duplicates. これは業界への評価軸の提案である。

## 破｜上海の店舗は単なる「アニメの輸出」ではなく、現地で販売を成立させる実験である

The timeline matters: overseas expansion from 2023, the first China store in Wuxi in January 2025, a Shanghai subsidiary in July 2025, and the Shanghai eeo Store in July 2026. 中国複数都市でのポップアップや飲料コラボも公式に発表されている。Shipping merchandise abroad is different from running retail, food collaborations and local distribution. [沿革](https://athree3.com/company/information/)／[海外事業](https://athree3.com/global/)。

Streaming can cross borders faster than physical goods. しかし商品には輸入、現地物流、権利の地域制限、商業施設との交渉、食品規制などが伴う。These are general operating constraints, not claims of wrongdoing by A3. The company frames the shortage of real-world touchpoints for global fans as an opportunity. [A3海外事業](https://athree3.com/global/)。

To test commercial success, ask about geographic licensing, local partners, FX exposure, acquisition costs, repeat purchases and inventory. 店舗を開いた事実と、利益を出した事実は違う。We did not verify country-level revenues or contribution margins. **Expansion is observable; profitability is not.**

## 離｜A3が売っているものは「アニメグッズ」だけではなく、ファンが参加できる場面である

By product category, A3 looks scattered: acrylic stands, cafés, events, stage shows and lotteries. By **customer journey**, it looks more coherent. 作品を知る、友人と語る、商品を探す、買う、飾る、出かける。Different products support different moments in the same relationship with a beloved work. A drink and a badge need not be substitutes to belong in one experience system.

The hypothesis: A3's central capability is to turn fandom into **purchasing, visiting and participating**, not merely to predict a hit. もちろん人気作品へのアクセスは重要だ。しかし創業初期には許諾を得ただけでは売れなかった。The later business portfolio can be interpreted as gradually building the missing commercialization steps. この読みは筆者の解釈であり、唯一の経営意図として断定しない。

## 離｜スポーツクラブに置き換えると「グッズ売上」より広い問いが見えてくる

A football club may run ticketing, merchandise, food, local sponsorship activation and away-game experiences in separate departments. The useful question is not “Should we outsource everything to A3?” むしろ、ファンにとって試合前の期待から帰宅後の余韻までが一続きになっているか。This is a customer-experience design question, beyond merchandise alone.

A practical pilot could record five things: image and likeness permissions, expected demand and first production volume, promotion-to-purchase conversion, unsold-stock responsibility, and satisfaction plus repeat purchases. これはA3の内部運用を説明したものではなく、応用提案である。Measure what the fan does next, not only how much the event sold. 商品は単発売上から長期的な関係づくりの手段へ変わり得る。

## 再調査｜最大の未解明は、成功した企画ではなく失敗した企画の比率である

The unanswered question is not how many IP titles A3 has handled, but **which projects it rejects or stops**. 累計2,547件のIPや5,000件以上の企画は活動量であり、案件成功率ではない。We do not know cancellations, overstock rates or loss-making projects. Good merchandising also means knowing when not to commit inventory.

For deeper diligence, ask for royalty and minimum-guarantee terms where disclosable, production lots, pre-order ratios, retail sell-through, returns, customer acquisition cost, gross profit, markdowns and repeat rate. 国内案件と海外案件を同じ定義で比較したい。Only then can a phrase like “maximizing IP value” become a falsifiable business hypothesis.

## 検証プロンプト｜IP企業を調べるときは、人気より取引構造を先に確かめる

**Reusable research prompt（提案）**：『For a company monetizing intellectual property, map (1) rights holder and license scope, (2) planning and manufacturing roles, (3) channels and inventory ownership, (4) buyers, frequency and order value, (5) wholesale versus service versus joint venture versus direct retail, (6) country-specific rights, (7) metric definitions and dates, (8) consumer-protection concerns, and (9) activities competitors cannot readily copy. Label every statement as fact, attributed company claim, hypothesis or proposal. 市場規模の算式・調査対象・費目を復元し、企業実売上と混同しない。Unknown means unknown; specify what evidence could resolve it.』

We began with a strange question: Why do power banks and Shanghai anime drinks belong to the same company? 製品だけを並べれば、やっぱり変だ。しかし「売り場で需要を見つける→権利を得る→商品や体験を作る→ファンへ届ける」と並べると、一つの仕事の延長に見える。**A3 may be better understood as a company expanding real-world moments of fandom, rather than simply expanding its merchandise catalogue.** これが調べた後に残る見方だ。

## 資料と用語｜数字には必ず母数と出所を付ける

**Glossary**：IP＝intellectual property／知的財産。Licensing＝使用許諾。Rights holder＝権利元。EC＝electronic commerce／電子商取引。Revenue share＝収入の分配。Royalty＝権利使用料。Minimum guarantee＝契約上の最低保証金（必須とは限らない）。LTV＝顧客関係から長期的に得る価値。Blind-pack merchandise＝購入時に種類を選べない商品。
