# Duck Hunt vs Ridley: do not shoot the big target — remove its destinations

## Ridley runs at 2.2. The real plan is to stop the approach, then erase landing and recovery routes

First observation:

Ridley is enormous.

Second observation:

Surely this means every projectile hits him for free.

Not quite.

Ultimate Frame Data lists Ridley's run speed at **2.2** and air speed at 1.05.

He weighs 107.

[Ultimate Frame Data: Ridley](https://ultimateframedata.com/ridley)

This is not a slow moving wall.

It is a giant monster with surprisingly serious ground speed.

見た目は怪獣。

Movement is closer to a sports car.

That changes the matchup immediately.

The goal is not “hit the large target more.”

The goal is:

**stop Ridley from turning one approach into a long advantage state, then exploit the large hurtbox once you are the one in control.**

---

## 1. Ridley deletes distance faster than his model suggests

Useful numbers:

- Run speed: 2.2
- Air speed: 1.05
- Fall speed: 1.78
- Fast fall: 2.848
- Nair: frame 8
- Bair: frame 10
- Space Pirate Rush: frame 22

[Ultimate Frame Data: Ridley](https://ultimateframedata.com/ridley)

SmashWiki describes Ridley as powerful in advantage but unusually vulnerable in disadvantage because of his huge hurtbox and limited defensive escape tools.

[SmashWiki: Ridley (SSBU)](https://www.ssbwiki.com/Ridley_(SSBU))

So Duck Hunt should not use neutral as a relaxed projectile exhibition.

Before touching B, ask:

**If Ridley starts running now, what is between us?**

Can?

Clay?

Gunman?

Dtilt?

If the answer is “nothing,” your setup time is already over.

---

## 2. Can should behave like a motion sensor

Because Ridley is large, it is tempting to send Can all the way into him.

But if Can passes behind Ridley, the lane between you becomes empty.

That is exactly when run speed 2.2 matters.

In this matchup, Can often has more value **between** the characters than inside Ridley's body.

Leave it in midfield.

Ridley dashes.

Ping.

Ridley jumps over.

Prepare Bair.

Ridley stops.

Add Gunman.

Ridley shields.

Take space.

Can is no longer just damage.

**It is an intrusion detector.**

The question becomes not “Did Can hit?”

but:

“Did Ridley get to cross this part of the stage without making a decision?”

---

## 3. Space Pirate Rush means shield cannot be your only answer

Ridley's Side-B, Space Pirate Rush, is a command grab.

Its grab begins on frame 22.

[Ultimate Frame Data: Ridley](https://ultimateframedata.com/ridley)

So the obvious zoning habit—

projectile,

projectile,

shield when they finally arrive—

creates a problem.

Ridley can grab the shield.

The answer is not to react perfectly to every Side-B.

The better answer is:

**do not build a defensive habit that makes Side-B obvious.**

Dash back.

Jump.

Move behind Can.

Dtilt.

Clay.

Gunman.

Shield is still useful.

It just cannot become the final state of every projectile sequence.

---

## 4. Plasma Breath gives Duck Hunt something unusual: a ranged target on Ridley's face

Plasma Breath can fire up to five fireballs.

More importantly, Ridley has a special vulnerable mouth during the charge.

Ultimate Frame Data says hitting the mouth during frames 16–45 causes Ridley to recoil.

[Ultimate Frame Data: Ridley](https://ultimateframedata.com/ridley)

SmashWiki likewise notes that being hit during charge causes self-damage and a long stun.

[SmashWiki: Plasma Breath](https://www.ssbwiki.com/Plasma_Breath)

For many characters, Ridley can charge at a distance where they cannot immediately threaten the mouth.

Duck Hunt has Can.

Clay.

Gunman.

So when Ridley begins charging, do not only think:

“avoid the fireballs.”

Also ask:

**Can I make charging itself unsafe?**

Not every situation allows it.

But Ridley should not receive a completely free charge simply because he is far away.

His projectile preparation can become your setup window.

---

## 5. Clay is strongest when it removes the fastest ground route

Ridley's size makes Clay easier to hit.

That is useful.

But the more important function is to make full-speed ground entry uncomfortable.

Clay is present.

Running straight through becomes risky.

So Ridley jumps.

Now Bair and Uair become relevant.

Ridley waits.

Now Gunman arrives.

リドリーを「大きいから当てる」のではなく、

**make the fastest route expensive.**

Clay is not only a combo starter.

It is a road closure.

---

## 6. Once Ridley is airborne, do not think “combo.” Think “no landing”

Ridley's large hurtbox and fast falling speed make him vulnerable to juggling and extended disadvantage.

[SmashWiki: Ridley (SSBU)](https://www.ssbwiki.com/Ridley_(SSBU))

Duck Hunt has excellent tools for creating layered vertical pressure:

Utilt.

Uair.

Can.

Delayed Gunman.

So after the first hit, change the goal.

Do not ask:

“How many true hits can I add?”

Ask:

**“Where can Ridley land that is not already occupied by something?”**

Can below.

You slightly to the side.

Uair above.

Clay toward the escape.

If Ridley air dodges once and still cannot reach neutral, that is often more valuable than one extra combo hit.

Duck Hunt damage is often the result of **failed landings accumulating**.

---

## 7. Respect Ridley Nair during landing pressure

Ridley Nair begins frame 8 and covers a large area.

[Ultimate Frame Data: Ridley](https://ultimateframedata.com/ridley)

So standing directly under him with no setup can turn a good juggle into a direct hitbox contest.

Do not make your body responsible for every landing.

Put Can where you want Ridley to land.

Stand offset.

Let Gunman fire later.

Make Nair interact with an object first.

The goal is not to beat Nair with a faster aerial.

**The goal is to make Nair fail to end disadvantage.**

That is a much more Duck Hunt-like problem.

---

## 8. Wing Blitz has power, but limited route choice

SmashWiki identifies Ridley's recovery as exploitable because Wing Blitz has limited angles.

[SmashWiki: Ridley (SSBU)](https://www.ssbwiki.com/Ridley_(SSBU))

Ultimate Frame Data lists startup around frames 34–37 depending on direction.

[Ultimate Frame Data: Ridley](https://ultimateframedata.com/ridley)

The mistake is to jump directly into Wing Blitz's active hitbox.

It is strong and has directional intangibility properties.

Duck Hunt does not need to win that collision.

Instead:

Can at a low destination.

Gunman across a horizontal line.

Bair above.

Clay where Space Pirate Rush might return.

**Attack the route before or after Wing Blitz, not necessarily Wing Blitz itself.**

Recovery becomes geometry, not bravery.

---

## 9. The matchup reverses brutally when Duck Hunt is offstage

Ridley is also excellent at edgeguarding.

Plasma Breath can cover low space.

Fair has multiple hits.

Nair is broad.

Downward Wing Blitz can meteor.

[SmashWiki: Ridley (SSBU)](https://www.ssbwiki.com/Ridley_(SSBU))

Duck Hunt's initial Up-B rise does not attack.

So do not repeat one low recovery timing.

Save jump.

Move Can before recovery.

Control Can during Duck Jump.

Use the attack / air-dodge cancel from frame 51 onward when appropriate.

[Ultimate Frame Data: Duck Hunt](https://ultimateframedata.com/duck_hunt)

対リドリーは、edgeguardする側とされる側で景色が極端に変わる。

Winning neutral is not enough if every lost neutral becomes a full offstage sequence.

---

## 10. Gunman is a delayed toll booth for a fast runner

Wild Gunman fires after different delays, roughly frame 55 to 91 depending on the Gunman.

[Ultimate Frame Data: Duck Hunt](https://ultimateframedata.com/duck_hunt)

Against a fast ground character, that delay can be useful.

Place Gunman.

Stand ahead of him.

Ridley runs now.

You retreat.

Gunman fires later.

Add Can.

Ridley has speed, but speed does not erase future bullets already scheduled.

So Gunman becomes:

**a delayed toll booth on the approach lane.**

Ridley may shield.

Jump.

Stop.

Take the shot.

Any of those decisions give Duck Hunt another timing layer.

---

## 11. At kill percent, resist the urge to turn into a worse powerhouse

Ridley weighs 107.

He is not light, but he is also not as durable as his size visually suggests.

[Ultimate Frame Data: Ridley](https://ultimateframedata.com/ridley)

This tempts Duck Hunt players to start fishing for Smash attacks.

Whiff.

Ridley runs.

Now the monster gets a turn.

Instead, at kill percent, keep using the matchup's actual weakness:

another juggle.

another ledge trap.

Bair.

Uair.

Can explosion.

Make Ridley solve one more recovery or landing problem.

**Do not fight Ridley in the category Ridley is built to win: large single-hit reward.**

## 13. A 10-minute Ridley drill

### 3 min — stop the entry

Keep Can in the lane instead of sending it through the opponent.

Rotate Clay, Dtilt and Gunman.

### 3 min — hunt the second landing

Hit one Uair.

Then stop thinking combo.

Place Can for the next landing.

### 2 min — cover Wing Blitz destinations

Practice covering the end points instead of colliding with the move.

### 2 min — survive offstage

Vary recovery height.

Control Can during Up-B.

Use the frame-51 cancel options.

The skill is not hitting Ridley.

It is:

**reducing the number of moments where Ridley is free to choose a route.**

---

## 14. Before the research, Ridley looked easy because he was large. After it, he looks vulnerable because he cannot hide from layered futures

Yes, Ridley is big.

Yes, your projectiles hit him.

But that is not the deepest advantage.

Ridley is also fast.

Dangerous up close.

Very capable of turning one mistake into edgeguard pressure.

So the matchup cannot be “shoot the giant.”

The better idea is:

Can before the dash.

Bair after the jump.

Can under the landing.

Gunman at the recovery destination.

One problem after another.

**Convert Ridley's size from hitability into escapability.**

Do not just make him easy to hit.

Make every safe destination disappear.

That is where Duck Hunt becomes Duck Hunt.

---

## Match checklist

- Big does not mean slow: run speed 2.2
- Keep Can between you and Ridley
- Do not let shield become the automatic end of every zoning sequence
- Plasma Breath charge creates a mouth target
- After launching Ridley, prioritise the second landing trap
- Cover Wing Blitz destinations rather than contesting its hitbox
- Vary Duck Hunt recovery against fireball pressure
- At kill percent, choose another advantage state before a desperate Smash attack
