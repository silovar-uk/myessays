# Pokémonはなぜ倒すだけでは勝てないのか――LoLから読み解く『ユナイト』

*Reference date: October 27, 2026. Japanese article is the canonical version. Original schematic illustrations are included.*

## 入口｜このゲームでは、何体倒しても勝利が決まらない

Pokémonたちが10分間戦っても、knockouts alone do not win the game. 相手を倒しても復活し、試合は続く。The real job is to deliver points into the opponent's goal zones. 激しい戦闘の見た目とは裏腹に、勝敗の判定は「どれだけ得点を届けたか」である。

「Pokémon UNITE is Pokémon's LoL」という説明は、LoL未経験者には何の説明にもならない。This essay starts from first principles: what is a match, why do players fight, and what actually counts as winning? そのうえで「確認事実」「解釈」「提案」を分け、同じジャンルで勝利条件を変える意味を考える。

## 守｜『ユナイト』は5人で協力して点を入れる10分間の対戦ゲームである

【確認事実】Pokémon UNITE is a free-to-start team strategy game, jointly developed by The Pokémon Company and Tencent's TiMi Studios. Nintendo Switchとスマートフォンに対応する。The main format is five versus five over ten minutes; the team with more total points wins. 「エオスエナジー」を相手ゴールへ入れると得点となる。[公式製品紹介](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/ja/)／[公式遊び方](https://unite.pokemon.com/overview/)／[公式10分ガイド](https://www.pokemon.com/us/strategy/a-minute-by-minute-guide-to-pokemon-unite-battles)。

One match has a simple loop: choose a Pokémon, defeat wild Pokémon, earn experience and Aeos energy, learn stronger moves, then score while defending your own side. 経験値（experience points）は自分を育てる資源。エオスエナジー（Aeos energy）はゴールへ運ぶ得点材料。A player can become stronger without having scored yet. この区別が、試合を見る最初の鍵になる。

One surprise: the familiar elemental type-advantage chart does not apply in Unite Battles. つまり本編の炎・水・草などの相性では勝負が決まらない。Same creatures, different sport. [任天堂の開始案内](https://www.nintendo.com/jp/topics/article/9ce18882-75f8-4875-b2af-1a5308f5177a)。

## 守｜LoLは『敵の本陣を壊した側が勝つ』別の5対5競技である

【確認事実】LoL stands for League of Legends, released by Riot Games in 2009. In its familiar five-on-five mode, players control champions, move along lanes, break defensive structures, and finally destroy the enemy Nexus—the heart of the base. **Winning means destroying the enemy base, not collecting the highest score before a fixed clock expires.** これがユナイトとの第一の相違点。[LoL公式入門](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/how-to-play/)／[ライアット社史](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/riot-games-expands-publishing-business-into-asia-pacific)。

Both games share a grammar: level up, control lanes, win teamfights, take valuable neutral objectives. LoL has three lanes, automatic allied minions, turrets, and a base to destroy. ユナイトは上・下の主な2ルートと中央エリアを使い、獲得したエナジーを相手ゴールへ届ける。The verbs look similar; the final objective is different.

![Original schematic comparing the win conditions of LoL and Pokémon UNITE](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/pokemon-unite-vs-lol-original-2026.svg)

## 守｜MOBAとは『全軍を操る戦争ゲーム』から一人の英雄を切り出した形式である

The genre is called MOBA, short for Multiplayer Online Battle Arena. 直訳より実態が重要で、each player controls one powerful character rather than commanding an entire army. チームの役割分担と、地図上の目的達成が勝敗を分ける。

【歴史の整理】MOBA did not suddenly appear with LoL. Its lineage runs through user-made StarCraft maps such as Aeon of Strife and Warcraft III modifications such as Defense of the Ancients (DotA). そこから一人の強いキャラクターをチームで操作する形式が育ち、2009年のLoLへつながった。ただし「唯一の発明者」を決めるほど単純な系譜ではない。[ジャンル史の研究](https://dspace02.jaist.ac.jp/dspace/bitstream/10119/15211/5/paper.pdf)／[ライアット社史](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/riot-games-expands-publishing-business-into-asia-pacific)。

## 守｜自分の仕事は『5種類の役割』と『地図の3領域』で理解できる

【確認事実】Pokémon generally fall into five battle roles: Attacker, Speedster, All-Rounder, Defender, and Supporter. アタック型は攻撃、スピード型は急襲、バランス型は攻守、ディフェンス型は前線維持、サポート型は味方の補助を担う。ただしrole names are broad descriptions, not hard limits on what a Pokémon may do.[公式ポケモン一覧](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/ja/pokemon/)。

The battlefield has top path, bottom path, and central area. In the opening minutes, teammates should avoid crowding the same resource without a plan. 中央担当はそこで育ち、味方のルートを手伝う。Other MOBA players call central-area play jungling and a surprise assist a gank. しかし用語の暗記より、「who collects resources, who provides backup, and when」が大事。[公式10分ガイド](https://www.pokemon.com/us/strategy/a-minute-by-minute-guide-to-pokemon-unite-battles)。

Useful terms: a Unite Move is a powerful special move; a teamfight is a clash involving several players; crowd control limits enemy actions. 「もちもの」（held items）は事前にセットする装備、「バトルアイテム」（battle item）は試合中に使う道具。Character strength, item strength, and team positioning are not the same thing. [公式アイテム解説](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/en/item/)。

## 守｜10分を4つに分けると、戦っている理由が読める

A practical four-part view of the ten-minute match: opening—farm wild Pokémon and find safe scores; middle—contest objectives that give experience or powerful effects; preparation—regroup and secure position; final two minutes—every successful score counts double. 序盤の一撃よりも、終盤に5人がどこへ集まるかが重要になる。

A central boss monster is often called an objective—not because it is the ultimate win condition, but because controlling it helps reach that condition. 日本語では「重要目標」と理解すれば十分。Defeating it is a means; scoring or destroying the base is the end. この区別はLoLにも共通する。[LoL公式入門](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/how-to-play/)。

## 守｜残り2分だけ同じエナジーが2倍の点数になる

【確認事実】The final two minutes are the Final Stretch: both teams score double points. Deposit 50 Aeos energy with three minutes left and it is worth 50 points; deposit the same amount with one minute left and it is worth 100. **The double score depends on the clock, not on which team defeats the boss.** [公式10分ガイド](https://www.pokemon.com/us/strategy/a-minute-by-minute-guide-to-pokemon-unite-battles)／[対戦戦術の現行解説](https://wikiwiki.jp/poke-unite/%E6%88%A6%E7%95%A5%E3%83%BB%E6%88%A6%E8%A1%93%28%E3%83%86%E3%82%A4%E3%82%A2%29)。

![Original illustration of how the same 50 energy becomes 100 points during the Final Stretch](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/pokemon-unite-final-two-minutes-original-2026.svg)

【思考実験】Two successful 50-energy scores before the Final Stretch equal 100 points; two identical scores during the Final Stretch equal 200. But waiting is risky. You might be knocked out or have your scoring interrupted. The strategic question is not simply 'score late'; it is 'is the extra reward worth the risk?' この比較はルールの算術であって、実試合の勝率データではない。

## 守｜サンダーだけを覚えると、2026年のユナイトを見誤る

【歴史】At launch in 2021, Zapdos shaped the endgame. In September 2022, Theia Sky Ruins introduced Rayquaza, whose defeat granted scoring-support effects including a shield. December 2025 added a Kyogre version of the map, with effects including easier scoring and gradual HP recovery. つまり'the final boss is always Zapdos'も'always Rayquaza'も間違い。Boss effects vary by map and era.[2022年公式案内](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/archive/ja/news/78/)／[2025年更新予告](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/archive/ja/news/380/)／[2026年9月の対戦地図](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/ja/topics/news/20260831/)。

The boss fight resembles a decisive set piece in football, but it is not automatically a goal. Securing the boss creates an advantage that must still be converted into scoring. A leading team can create unnecessary risk by starting a fight the opponent may steal. これは事実の説明ではなく、the incentive structureからの戦略的解釈である。

## 守｜5年間の歴史は『発売して終わり』ではなく、ルールを変え続ける運営史だった

【確認事実】Announced in June 2020 by The Pokémon Company and Tencent; launched on Nintendo Switch on July 21, 2021, then on mobile on September 22, 2021. A major map arrived in 2022. December 2025 changed license acquisition and ranked play. The game marked five years on July 21, 2026, with announcements of new playable Pokémon. 単発の製品でなく、更新され続けるサービスだ。[2020年発表](https://www.tencent.com/en-us/articles/2201059.html)／[配信日](https://press.pokemon.com/en-CA/POKEMON-UNITE-TO-RELEASE-FOR-MOBILE-DEVICES-TOMORROW)／[5周年発表](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/ja/topics/news/20260626-1/)。

This is a live-service game. Research must be date-stamped. On October 13, 2026, official balance changes adjusted moves and stats for Pokémon such as Solgaleo, Slowbro, and Morpeko. キャラクターの強さは永久不変ではなく、patches change the environment. [2026年10月13日公式更新](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/ja/topics/battle/20261013-2/)。

## 破｜『基本プレイ無料』と『同じ条件で勝負できる』は別の約束である

【確認事実】Free-to-start is not the same as an equal starting position. Pokémon licenses, cosmetic Holowear, battle passes, and paid Aeos gems are different kinds of products. A costume changes appearance; upgraded held items affect in-game statistics. In July 2021, players criticized how much grinding or spending item upgrades required. ただし2021年の批判から「2026年も支払額だけで勝敗が決まる」とは推論できない。[公式アイテム案内](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/en/item/)／[2021年の批判報道](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/pokemon-unite-players-cry-foul-over-expensive-power-boosting-held-items/1100-6494272/)。

On December 4, 2025, Aeos coins stopped being issued and stopped buying licenses in the shop. A progression system called License Journeys began, using license points earned through play; paid-gem purchases remained available. Held-item level caps also rose from 30 to 40. These are factual economic changes. **Whether the changes increased the paid-versus-free win-rate gap is an empirical question, not something the patch note proves.** [ライセンス制度変更](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/archive/ja/news/373/)／[もちもの40への変更](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/archive/ja/news/381/)。

【論点】To evaluate monetization, separate four questions: Does payment grant exclusive power? How long does an unpaid player take to reach comparable strength? How freely can a player choose a preferred Pokémon? How much revenue comes from cosmetics? 公式資料からは支払額分布や、有料・無料の実力差を調整した勝率差を確認できない。

## 破｜得点を隠す設計は『最後まで諦めない』を生み、同時に判断材料を減らす

【歴史的事実】In 2021, the game did not show an exact live team scoreboard. Producer Masaaki Hoshino explained the desire to keep players trying until the end, because comebacks remained possible. That's a stated design intention, not proof that hiding scores statistically prevents quitting. 本稿では2021年時点の設計思想として扱い、2026年の表示仕様までは無確認のまま断定しない。[開発者発言の報道](https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/20211027-180328/)／[補足報道](https://nintendoeverything.com/pokemon-unite-dev-explains-lack-of-scoreboard/)。

【解釈】Information design has a trade-off. Hiding the exact score may keep hope alive, but competitive players lose information for making precise decisions. 終盤に逆転しやすいルールと点差の不透明さが重なる。It may reduce surrender motivation; it may also reduce strategic clarity. 両方の効果は実測しなければ分からない。

## 破｜『味方ガチャ』への怒りは、5人の共同生産だから起こる

【確認できる批判】Player reviews raise concerns about matchmaking, uneven Pokémon balance, weak coordination, and surrender behavior. Reviews are useful first-person accounts but do not represent a statistically random sample of players. 公開レビューだけで「不公平なマッチング率」を測定することはできない。[App Storeの利用者レビュー](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/pok%C3%A9mon-unite/id1512321575?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews)／[降参をめぐる報道](https://kotaku.com/pokemon-unite-players-sure-dont-like-surrendering-1847683370)。

【解釈】Team output is not merely the sum of five individual skill levels. Timing, protection, and coordination interact. When a team loses, it becomes easy to credit oneself and blame others. That is a hypothesis about attribution in team performance, not a measured psychological result from this game. 対戦を「個人の総和」ではなく「共同生産」と見ると、不満の種類も理解しやすくなる。

## 破｜2026年の世界王者と2027年の独立大会が、競技としてのもう一つの顔を示す

【確認事実】Japan's ZETA DIVISION won the Pokémon UNITE world title in San Francisco in August 2026, defeating Europe's KOMAÏ in the final. For 2027, the title moves to its own international event: Aeos Crown 2027. The announced Tokyo finals are March 20–21, with 32 teams and a total prize pool of US$1 million including supplementary awards. これは開催予定であり、すでに終了した実績ではない。[2026年大会結果](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/en/topics/news/20260831/)／[2027年大会公式概要](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/ja/topics/news/aeos-crown-jp/)。

The 2027 event also announces an All-Star Draft: once a Pokémon is selected within the same match series, it cannot be selected again later in that series. This adds strategic choice across games, beyond mechanical skill in a single game. 選手の技量に加え、チームの準備と選択の広さを競う形式になる。[大会規定の発表](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/ja/topics/news/aeos-crown-jp/)。

## 離｜ポケモンは難しいジャンルの『説明の手間』を引き受ける入口になった

【仮説】Familiar characters may lower the learning barrier to an unfamiliar genre. Pikachu or Snorlax comes with an image in the player's mind, even if the battle rules are new. ただし本編のタイプ相性はなく、知名度から正しい操作方法まで分かるわけではない。The intellectual-property advantage is an entry hypothesis, not proof of a higher retention rate.

A shorter control scheme does not eliminate strategic complexity. 選択肢が少なく見えても、where to rotate, whether to fight, and when to score remain difficult choices. Accessibility of controls and mastery of decisions are separate dimensions. ポケモンという入口と、競技としての深さは矛盾しない。

## 離｜スポーツ事業として見ると、『10分の密度』と『見せる情報』が商品設計になる

【解釈】A fixed ten-minute match is easy to schedule in a broadcast, and a decisive Final Stretch creates an expected climax. But comeback mechanics have a cost: early efforts may feel discounted, and spectators may struggle to read who is winning. スポーツ興行の観点では、excitementとcompetitive legibilityを同時に設計する必要がある。

【提案】Test information design in esports broadcasts. Vary whether viewers see a precise team score, objective timers, or short explanations of why teams rotate. Compare audience retention, comprehension, and intention to watch again. このテストは提案であり、実際の大会で検証済みの結果ではない。

## 再調査｜ルールから言えることと、試合データがないと言えないことを分ける

After checking official rules, one conclusion is solid: the games share a genre but reward different end objectives. Three empirical questions remain open: how often the Final Stretch reverses a match, how strongly taking the central boss predicts victory, and how monetization relates to competitive fairness. 調べられることと、現状の資料だけでは言えないことを分離する。

【追加調査計画】Sample official 2026 Worlds match videos. Record the team that secured the central boss, the winner, late scores where visible, and indicators of team strength. If exact point differences cannot be read, mark them missing—do not invent values. Correlation does not prove causation: stronger teams may both win boss fights and win the match. [2026年世界大会公式記録](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/en/topics/news/20260831/)。

【限界】No hands-on controlled test, viewer experiment, or full match-level statistical analysis was conducted for this article. The reference date is October 27, 2026. Historical rules, current announcements, and future plans are explicitly separated. The numeric scoring example is hypothetical.

## 結び｜『ポケモン版LoL』とは、同じ戦場の形で別の勝ち方を学ばせるゲームだった

Return to the odd fact at the beginning: everyone is fighting, but fighting is not the scoring rule. Leveling up, winning teamfights, and taking major objectives are means to an end. In Pokémon UNITE, the end is the points delivered to goal zones. だから戦闘で最強の選手と、勝利へ最も貢献する選手が常に一致するとは限らない。

“Pokémon's version of LoL” is a convenient shortcut only if you already know LoL. A better beginner explanation is: five players work together for ten minutes, grow by fighting, and deliver collected energy into the opponent's goals; scoring doubles in the final two minutes. それだけで画面の見え方が変わる。It is a team sport about where to spend time and people, not merely a contest of knockouts.
