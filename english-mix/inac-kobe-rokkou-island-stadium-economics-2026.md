# 女子サッカーの新しいホームは、誰の財布で動くのか
## 年8,272万円の土地賃料、債務超過、平均2,848人から六甲アイランドの新スタジアムを読み直す

The first thing that caught my eye was not the roof or the seats. **Annual land rent: 8,271万9,120円.** そしてlease termは20年。How many women's football matches would it take to cover that bill? この小さな疑問から、新しい施設のeconomicsを調べてみる。

On 2026年10月9日、神戸市は六甲アイランドの市有地を使うconsortiumをpreferred negotiating partyに選んだ。Planは8,000〜10,000席、温浴施設・カフェなどを含み、2029年8月頃の開業を目指す。The essential distinction: **the city owns the land; the consortium plans to lease, develop and operate facilities.** これは単独のclub-owned stadiumとは違う。[神戸市](https://www.city.kobe.lg.jp/a26136/196404855699.html)

This article cross-checks municipal tender documents, three years of club financial statements, parent-company results, WEリーグattendance data and local urban policy. Facts, calculations and hypotheses are separate. 建設費、funding、持分、施設ごとのP&Lは公開情報で未確認。Rather than filling gaps with guesses, we will treat them as the questions that matter.

![Proposed INAC Kobe stadium rendering released by Kobe City](https://www.city.kobe.lg.jp/images/86787/20261007105555.jpg)

*Figure 1: 神戸市が掲載したconcept rendering. It is not the final approved design. Source: 神戸市／アイナックフットボールクラブ。[原典](https://www.city.kobe.lg.jp/a26136/196404855699.html)*

## 1. 競争入札の「勝者」より、1者しか応募しなかった事実が出発点になる

Only one consortium applied. The city awarded its proposal 83.6/100 and selected it for priority negotiations. 「競合を圧倒した」わけではない。There was no competing bid to outrank. The score assesses a proposal; it does not certify that finance, construction and long-term operation will succeed.[選考結果](https://www.city.kobe.lg.jp/a26136/196404855699.html)

The initial call described about 43,739.77㎡, while the selected proposal lists 31,333㎡. Difference: roughly 12,407㎡. ただしscopeや境界を確認せずに「計画縮小」とは言えない。Land area is not decorative metadata. It sets the physical limits of every revenue idea.[公募と選定](https://www.city.kobe.lg.jp/a26136/business/recruit/rokkoisland_jigyoteian.html)

## 2. 三つの会社の仕事は見えても、三つの財布の負担割合は見えない

The consortium has three members: lead company ウイングトワ、アイナックフットボールクラブ、村上工務店. The latter operates in construction, development planning and property management. Yet **membership is not a financing cap table**. 誰が工事を発注し、土地賃料を払い、施設収入を得るかは未公表。[神戸市](https://www.city.kobe.lg.jp/a26136/196404855699.html)／[村上工務店](https://www.murakami-gc.co.jp/business/)

Multiplying the stated annual rent by 20 gives 16億5,438万2,400円, **only under a constant-rent assumption**. 契約条項、賃料改定、引渡日で変わり得る。And that total excludes the stadium construction, maintenance, security and renewal costs. Avoiding land purchase does not remove the cost of owning and operating buildings.

## 3. INACは2001年創設の強豪だが、いまは一つの企業グループの事業でもある

INAC Kobe was founded in 2001, became closely associated with Japan's 2011 World Cup-winning era, and won the 2025/26 WEリーグ. Its operating company, however, was established in 2006. The team's sporting history and the legal entity's financial history are different timelines.[会社概要](https://inac-kobe.com/company)／[優勝への言及](https://ssl.nihon-data.jp/new/news-13075/)

The strategic turning point came in March 2024: listed environmental-services company 大栄環境 acquired the operator. It defines football as part of regional sports promotion. In June 2026, former Japan international 阿部浩之 became company president. A club can serve community relationships as well as sporting results. But an acquisition does not imply unlimited funding for a future stadium.[沿革](https://www.dinsgr.co.jp/about/history/2023/)／[人事報道](https://www.nikkansports.com/soccer/news/202606080001050.html)

## 4. 決算公告を開くと、競技成績と財務の方向は一致していなかった

The operator publishes balance sheets through the parent company. Net losses were about 4,862万円 in FY2024, 2億5,692万円 in FY2025, and 1億1,594万円 in FY2026. All three years were in the red. But the public statements are not full income statements: **revenue and operating profit cannot be reconstructed from them**.[FY2026](https://www.dinsgr.co.jp/about/koukoku/pdf/inac/22th_koukoku.pdf)／[FY2025](https://www.dinsgr.co.jp/about/koukoku/pdf/inac/21th_koukoku.pdf)／[FY2024](https://www.dinsgr.co.jp/about/koukoku/pdf/inac/20th_koukoku.pdf)

- FY2024: net assets **−3,993万円**, net loss **−4,862万円**
- FY2025: net assets **−1億9,685万円**, net loss **−2億5,692万円**
- FY2026: net assets **−3億1,279万円**, net loss **−1億1,594万円**
- FY2026: long-term borrowing **4億5,500万円**; cash **約1億9,105万円**

This is not proof that women's football can never make money. Net income includes non-operating effects, and negative equity is not the same as today's cash running out. 2025年の損失理由も公開資料だけでは断定できない。Still, the figures rule out any casual assumption that the club alone can comfortably shoulder a major construction project. Funding allocation is the next essential question.

## 5. 親会社は大きく黒字。それでも「親が払うから大丈夫」では説明不足になる

The parent's FY2026 consolidated revenue reached 878億5,500万円, with operating profit of 221億8,900万円. That is a radically different financial scale from the football subsidiary. 親会社による保有はcapital accessの可能性を広げる。[大栄環境IR](https://www.dinsgr.co.jp/ir/midtermplan/)

But 221億円 of group operating profit is not 221億円 of free money for this development. Existing capital expenditure, debt and shareholder obligations all compete for funds. The project lead is ウイングトワ, not the football company itself. Its current ownership and funding commitments must be verified independently. **Parent strength supports financing credibility; it is not a disclosed project financing plan.**

## 6. 新スタジアムは大きすぎるのか。観客2,848人から座席を数え直す

INAC averaged 2,848 spectators across 11 home league fixtures in 2025/26, a total of 31,332. The league-wide mean was 2,392. If you compare today's crowd with 8,000–10,000 planned seats, it equals just 28.5–35.6% of capacity. This is **a hypothetical new-venue fill rate**, not an observed occupancy rate.[公式統計](https://data.weleague.jp/SFTD14/search?competition_frame=70&competition_year=2025&endDayNum=8)

![INAC Kobe current crowd compared with proposed stadium capacity](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/inac-kobe-stadium-attendance-2026.svg)

*Figure 2: Current 2025/26 average attendance 2,848 versus proposed 8,000–10,000 capacity. This is a comparison, **not an attendance forecast**. Data: WEリーグ and 神戸市.*

The current ノエビアスタジアム神戸 has an official capacity of 30,132, dedicated ball-sport sightlines and a retractable roof. A smaller venue is not automatically a better one. What changes is potentially **the intimacy, control of usage rights, commercial permissions and local everyday demand**—not simply the number of seats.[ノエスタ公式案内](https://www.vissel-kobe.co.jp/stadium/)

## 7. やってはいけない計算をあえてやる。入場料だけでは土地代を説明しにくい

A deliberately rough thought experiment: 31,332 attendees × an assumed 2,500円 each = 7,833万円 gross ticket face value. That is less than 8,271万9,120円 in annual land rent. This is **not actual box-office revenue**. Complimentary seats, tax, commissions, season packages and revenue-sharing all matter. しかもclubが賃料の全額を負担するとは限らない。The point is not a viability verdict; it is to expose why match tickets alone cannot explain the scheme.

Raise average crowds to 5,000 across 11 games at the same hypothetical 2,500円 ticket price: gross face value becomes 1億3,750万円. The increase is only about 5,917万円 versus the current-crowd scenario. Stadium depreciation, maintenance, utilities, staffing, security and finance costs are still outside this arithmetic. Crowd growth helps, but it cannot be the entire business case.

## 8. 温浴施設とカフェは付録ではない。別々の来訪理由をつくる本体である

The scheme includes baths, sauna, running facilities, fitness, restaurants and sports-play rooms. These are not merely extras for matchday visitors. Football occupies limited dates; people exercise, bathe, eat and bring children on different days and hours. They need not be committed football fans. **Separate reasons to visit produce separate clocks of demand.**[公表計画](https://www.city.kobe.lg.jp/a26136/196404855699.html)

A bathhouse is no guaranteed profit engine: energy, water, hygiene and staff costs are considerable. Residents might love a weekday visit and avoid a congested matchday. The challenge is not only cross-selling fans. It is **designing coexistence between user groups with different schedules and tolerance for crowds**.

## 9. 六甲アイランドの側にも、毎日人が来る理由を探す事情がある

Kobe City has pursued a renewed vision for 六甲アイランド since 2021. A 2026 city-council exchange specifically emphasized attracting people from outside the island while giving residents more everyday commercial options. This land deal is therefore not simply a gift to a football club. It is where **municipal footfall policy intersects with the developer's year-round revenue ambition**.[都市方針](https://www.city.kobe.lg.jp/a26136/21030301.html)

An island location adds a transport test: 六甲ライナーcapacity, walkability, cycling, cars, crowd dispersal and neighborhood impacts. 公表情報だけで交通処理能力は評価できない。A stadium that can seat 10,000 people is not automatically a district that can move 10,000 people home comfortably.

## 10. 事業を四つの財布に分解すると、黒字への道筋と落とし穴が見えてくる

The project has at least four possible revenue pools: (1) club—tickets, sponsors, membership and merchandise; (2) venue—rentals, naming rights, advertising and tenant rent; (3) everyday consumer spending—fitness, baths, food and play; (4) business/community programs—classes, wellness and corporate use. **These are analytical categories, not disclosed forecasts.** Before adding them, identify which entity actually books each revenue stream.

Even if a restaurant sells 1億円 of food, the property owner may receive only base rent and a percentage of sales. Sponsors require servicing, and bath usage consumes water and energy. A useful business model must read **gross contribution, fixed costs, investment obligations and the legal destination of profit**—not just stack headline sales.

## 11. 長崎や広島をまねれば解けるのか。比較するなら条件の違いから

Nagasaki Stadium City combines hotel, retail, events, football and basketball, keeping the district busy outside matchdays. Hiroshima's women's club averaged 5,778 home fans in 2025/26 versus INAC's 2,848. But ownership, funding, urban catchment, building mix and number of anchor teams differ. **Benchmark mechanisms, not borrowed profitability ratios.**[WEデータ](https://data.weleague.jp/SFTD14/search?competition_frame=70&competition_year=2025&endDayNum=8)／[長崎分析](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/#/essay/nagasaki-stadiumcity-matchless-days-economy)

Perhaps the bigger change is combining appropriately sized seating with more control over the visitor journey. In a large borrowed venue, a club has limited freedom to shape restaurants, sponsorship inventory and recurring activities. But the planned rights have not been published, so this is a hypothesis. The real opportunity is who designs the relationship with visitors between matches.

## 12. 実現性を判定するなら、次の数字を入手するまで結論を急がない

The project may work. But profitability cannot yet be assessed. We lack construction cost, contributions, loans, allocation of land rent, asset ownership, operating rights, sport-competition compliance and transport/disaster arrangements. Calling it an established success model now would confuse a promising proposal with a tested operation.

- Construction capex and financing: who builds, borrows and depreciates?
- Rights to buildings, advertising, naming and shops: whose assets and revenue?
- Annual event dates, weekday footfall and customer spend: what validates non-matchday demand?
- Paid admissions, ticket yield and repeat purchase: how does higher attendance become revenue?
- Rent revisions, year-20 arrangements, renewal reserves and transport mitigation: who absorbs downside?

A practical proposal would sequence validation. First: weekday/weekend catchment research and small pilot events. Second: separate club, retail, bath and venue P&Ls, with explicit rights. Third: investment-return scenarios and downside allocation. This is **our recommendation, not the consortium's published plan**. The key discipline is making uncertainty measurable before construction.

## 13. スタジアムは「女子サッカーの家」になる。その家計は街全体で考える必要がある

Return to the opening rent. Dividing 8,271万9,120円 by 11 home league fixtures gives around 752万円 per match. A daunting allocation—except the payer and the economic activity are not necessarily restricted to 11 games. That's why the project involves a consortium, wellness businesses and an urban-development agenda.

A civic mission alone cannot maintain concrete and steel. Yet judging viability solely from today's 2,848 spectators makes the business boundary too narrow. The real question shifts from **“How many fans can INAC attract?” to “Who will come on which days, for what purpose, and who carries the cost?”** Building a home for a women's football club may ultimately mean designing a new weekly timetable for an entire district.
