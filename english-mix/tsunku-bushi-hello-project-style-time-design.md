# 「幸せ」は四音で足りない――“Tsunku-bushi”と“Hello! Project style”をtime designとして読む
## 16-beat feel, sticky pronunciation, ear-catching words, vocal assignment, and cultural transmissionまで分解する

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
In one song, an ordinary Japanese word such as「幸せ」somehow needs more than its ordinary four mora-like beats. つんく♂は自作曲の歌唱を振り返り、young singersが拍の前へ走らないよう、一語の内部にtiny phonetic movementsを加え、voiceを少し後ろへ置く歌わせ方をしたと説明している。The lyric sheet gains no extra letters. Yet the inside of the mouth suddenly feels overcrowded. まず、この妙な現象から始めたい。

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
According to Tsunku’s own explanation, the melody had a slight swung feel, and younger singers tended to rush forward. そこで発音の内部を細かくし、少し“sticky”に歌わせた。He also notes that this kind of detailed delivery could be useful during vocal editing, and that singers learned the habit by following his demo vocals. 奇妙な発音は、ornament firstではなく、timing control firstだったのである。

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
When people say「つんく節」, it is tempting to collect visible quirks: 語尾、nasal color、独特の英語、癖の強い歌詞。しかし本人の説明を追うと、those surface features often serve another purpose. 発音を崩すのは、simply to sound strangeではない。Where does the voice land? How long does it cling to the beat? Where does the listener’s ear get caught? 言葉の形は、そのために動かされる。

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
So this essay treats「つんく節」not as a cabinet of quirks but as a **system connecting time, pronunciation, words, casting, and production**. そして「ハロプロ節」は、その一部がlessons, demo vocals, vocal assignment, and generational turnoverを通じてcollective bodily knowledgeへ変わったもの、と仮に分ける。Once separated, a better question appears: why can a song feel “Hello! Project-like” even when Tsunku did not write it?

<!-- level:2 role:evidence -->
**Key sources**：つんく♂「[『そうだ！We're ALIVE』セルフライナーノーツ](https://note.tsunku.net/n/nff15e1bc0c9b)」／WEBザテレビジョン「[田中れいなインタビュー](https://thetv.jp/news/detail/227178/p3/)」

## 1. 「節」を五つに割ると、the actual mechanism starts to appear

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
「つんく節」は便利な言葉だが、almost too convenient. 「なんか、つんくっぽい」でconversation can end before analysis begins. そこで、少々野暮を承知でdecomposeしてみる。The evidence lines up surprisingly well when we separate at least five layers.

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
Layer one is **time**: 16-beat subdivision and where the body sits between beats. Layer two is **pronunciation**: 子音、母音の滑り、nasal detail、語尾の処理でvoice placementを変える。Layer three is **words**: an「耳キャッチ」or a decisive line that stays after one listen. Layer four is **casting**: who sings, whether it is solo, small group, or unison. Layer five is **culture**: demo vocals, lessons, recording direction, and observation through which the method travels from person to person.

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
The important point is that these are not five unrelated tricks. 16ビートを細かく感じるから、pronunciationも細かくなる。A memorable line gains force because a particular voice receives it. 仮歌を忠実に再現するcultureがあるから、composer’s time feel can enter a singer’s body. This is why copying one visible feature rarely recreates the whole impression.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
It may be better to say that「つんく節」lives in **the connections among the five layers**. 料理なら、しょうゆの銘柄よりheat and sequenceに近い。Buying the right soy sauce does not recreate a restaurant. Likewise, saying「うぃ」once does not turn a song into Hello! Project. そんな簡単なら、歌唱指導は調味料売り場で終わる。

## 2. 第一層は16ビート――the space between beats is busy

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
One foundational term Tsunku repeatedly uses is「16ビート」. ただし、これをgenre labelのように「16ビートの曲」とだけ理解すると少し外す。His own explanations point more toward **continuously feeling subdivisions inside the beat**, not merely identifying tempo or genre.

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
In his 2026 liner notes for Morning Musume。'26「Lonely...But not Alone」, Tsunku looks back on his love of funky and soulful music since Sharam Q and says that「16ビート」has repeatedly been a compositional keyword. さらに、細かなrhythmを絶えず感じながら数え、同時にlarger beatsも数える必要があると説明する。The song even contains sections that resist conventional phrase lengths, giving the members a fresh rhythmic task.

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
This is different from only stepping「one, two, three, four」. 四つの拍それぞれの内部に、smaller gridがある。そのgridを感じていると、a vocal can enter slightly early, exactly on the beat, or just behind it, and those differences become meaningful. 歌手はmelodyを当てるだけでなく、time meshのどこへ音を差し込むかを選ぶことになる。

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
That is why the fine pronunciation often associated with「つんく♂歌唱」makes more sense when connected to 16-beat feel. 音節の内部にtiny movementを作れば、それ自体がrhythmic materialになる。If a singer follows only the surface of the beat, the same pitch can still rush forward. Pronunciation becomes a small weight that pulls the voice back into position.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Practically, 16-beat feel is useful to think of as **the resolution of the singer’s attention**, not merely an accompaniment label. 「この曲は16ビートか」を判定するより、“Where does the voice sit between the four beats? Where does it cling, and where does it suddenly accelerate?” と聴く。Zoom in on time, and the pronunciation layer begins to appear.

## 3. 第二層は発音――a “strange mouth” changes where the voice lands

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
The famous「い」が「うぃ」のように聞こえるdelivery, stronger consonants, tiny movements around a note, and distinctive phrase endings can certainly function as markers of「つんく♂歌唱」. ただし、the symbols are dangerous if memorized without purpose. The testimonies point to a deeper idea: **phonetic deformation and timing are coupled**.

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
田中れいなは2020年のインタビューで、Tsunku’s demo vocal was her best model and that reproducing it helped the detailed rhythm, falsetto, consonant strength, and phrase-ending habits become natural. In 2026, 前島亜美 also said she practiced a Tsunku composition with a 16-beat click and consciously studied a Hello! Project-like way of shifting「い」toward「うぃ」while watching past performances.

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
What is fascinating is that moving slightly away from neutral spoken Japanese can make musical placement clearer. 母音へ入る前にsmall glideを置く。Strengthen a consonant. 語尾を捨てず少し持ち上げる。These are linguistically different phenomena, but musically each can sharpen when a sound starts, how it occupies a subdivision, and how it ends.

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
Tsunku’s own explanation of subdividing the pronunciation of「幸せ」to keep younger singers from rushing is especially revealing. 発音はmeaning carrierだけではない。**It can function as a timing regulator.** Once you see that, a nasal insertion or vowel glide can be judged by purpose: is it actually changing placement, or is it merely surface imitation?

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
In practice, when you hear an unusual pronunciation, ask not「what quirk is this?」but「how does this change onset, ending, or center of gravity?」. 何も変わらないなら、その発音はcore mechanismではないかもしれない。Tsunku-style pronunciation becomes much more three-dimensional when heard as **a way of editing time with the mouth**.

## 4. 第三層は言葉――catch the ear before explaining the meaning

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
Calling Tsunku’s lyrics merely「独特な言い回し」also misses something. 本人は作詞を語る際、一聴でlistener’s earをつかむ言葉を重視し、それを「耳キャッチ」と呼んでいる。He also says an entire song can grow from something as tiny as “threading the eye of a needle”: not a huge event, but one small mental point explored seriously.

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
In his notes on「そうだ！We're ALIVE」, Tsunku explains that sometimes an English-like sound can be inserted because rhythmic pleasure matters more than strict semantic logic. 別の作詞論では、listeners do not always absorb lyrics on first contact; a phrase riding the melody can hit the ear first and become the entrance to the rest of the song.

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
That goal differs slightly from writing beautiful prose on a page. 歌の言葉にはmeaningだけでなく、sound shape, length, consonant attack, and repeatabilityがある。A short exclamation, a hyper-specific noun, or a suddenly foreign-sounding fragment may look context-poor in print but become a signpost in music. Sometimes meaning arrives half a second late, and that delay is exactly why the ear has already been caught.

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
So if you want to study “Tsunku-like lyrics,” scattering strange words is not enough. The better question is where the song drives **a sonic stake that survives one listen**. その杭がsong’s themeへ戻る入口になっているか。If the ear-catch and the theme have no relation, strangeness becomes smoke. If they connect, strangeness becomes signage.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Tsunku’s word design becomes easier to understand when we see it not as “meaning versus nonsense,” but as **the order in which meaning reaches the listener**. まずsoundが残り、meaningがあとから追いつく。そのdelay itself can be part of songwriting. A lyric is not only text to be read; it is text passing through time.

## 5. 第四層は尺度――from a 5 p.m. chime to the world

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
Fans and discussion participants often point to the coexistence of everyday detail and grand scale in Hello! Project lyrics. でも、これは「宇宙という単語が多い」といったword-count storyではない。The more interesting feature is **how quickly the camera moves from tiny daily detail to life, society, or the world**.

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
In the 2026 notes for「Lonely...But not Alone」, Tsunku places thoughts about society and the world beside concrete details such as garbage, phone reception, the evening chime, and taking a bath. 別の対談では、participants describe the mix of “生活感と壮大さ” and the speed of moving between large and small themes as something they hear in Tsunku and Hello! Project works.

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
Sing “world peace” only at the scale of world peace, and the phrase can lose texture. 反対に、冷蔵庫のプリンだけならconcreteだが、そこで閉じる。A song can begin with one small object, jump to loneliness or human desire, then return to an ordinary room. That vertical movement creates unusual perspective.

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
This movement resembles what Eric Hayot calls an **Uneven U**: moving from a problem to concrete detail and returning to a larger level with more meaning than before. もちろん、Tsunku’s songs are not “explained” by Hayot’s theory. The useful resemblance is simpler: **the gap between concrete and abstract can itself generate momentum**.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Practically, do not count “grand words.” Ask **how many meters the camera travels inside the song**. 手元からstreetへ、streetからworldへ、そしてroomへ戻る。The faster the zoom, the more an ordinary sentence can suddenly feel philosophical. Sometimes the strangeness of Tsunku-bushi lives less in vocabulary than in zoom speed.

## 6. 第五層は配役――the singer’s identity becomes arrangement material

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
By this point, describing「つんく節」only as lyrics and composition feels too narrow. ハロー！プロジェクトでは、who sings which line, how many people sing it, and whether a phrase is solo or unison can radically change its meaning. The personality of the voice becomes part of the arrangement.

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
田中れいなの証言では、reproducing Tsunku’s demo in detail helped internalize the singing method. Tsunku himself wrote in 2021 that large-group unison can blur individuality, so he used solos and smaller combinations, while keeping lyrics from becoming overly complex against finely rhythmic melodies.

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
Conversations with arranger 大久保薫 also show that the finished sound does not simply fall intact from the composer’s head. つんく♂ gives direction, sounds and sections are removed or added, and space is created for the vocal. The singer, too, is not a photocopier of the demo; the design becomes real only through a specific voice and body. The score is still only a partial product.

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
This makes the “owner” of「つんく節」slightly blurry. Is it the lyricist, composer, arranger, singer, or recording direction? 一つに決めにくい。しかし、その曖昧さこそ重要である。If an authorial intention becomes sound only by passing through other people’s bodies and techniques, **style can be embedded in a production process, not only in one person’s fingerprint**.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Then the next question follows naturally. If a singer has internalized the same timing, pronunciation, and role awareness, could a non-Tsunku song still feel “Hello! Project-like”? ここで、つんく節からハロプロ節へ話が移る。

## 7. 「ハロプロ節」は、authorial habit becoming an institution

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
For analysis, it is useful not to treat「つんく節」and「ハロプロ節」as synonyms. 前者はTsunku’s methods across lyrics, composition, demo vocals, and recording direction. The latter is what happens when part of that method is repeated inside Hello! Project until it becomes **a shared performance grammar among singers and staff**.

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
Official Hello! Project credits show that, especially from the mid-2010s onward, songs by many non-Tsunku lyricists and composers coexist within the same groups. それでも2026年、前島亜美がTsunku compositionへ取り組む際、16-beat feelや「い→うぃ」に近い発音を、past Hello! Project performancesを研究しながら学んだ。Even an outsider can already perceive a shared repertoire of techniques.

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
Former members point in the same direction. 田中れいな says detailed demo imitation made the technique natural; 市井紗耶香 also remembers learning parts from Tsunku’s demo and receiving fine pronunciation guidance to avoid rushing. A written textbook is not necessary if the same instruction and imitation recur for years.

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
Seen as cultural transmission, this is the fascinating part. An individual quirk normally fades when the individual leaves. しかし、その癖が「こう歌うと合う」「ここは細かく数える」「この声をここへ置く」というoperational judgmentへ翻訳されると、the next person can reuse it. **When aesthetics become procedure, individual craft becomes institution.**

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
So「ハロプロ節」may be closer to **a bodily dialect of a community** than a copy of Tsunku. 作家が変わればvocabularyやharmonyは変わる。それでもsingers’ timing and placement can preserve continuity, making the ear feel “this belongs to the same land.” That “likeness” cannot be explained by songwriter credit alone.

## 8. 15分で一曲を分解する「五層聴取法」

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
This should not remain only appreciation trivia. そこで、一曲を15分でdecomposeする方法にする。You do not need an instrument. 必要なのはofficial credits、音源、歌詞、そして机を指で叩ける程度のrhythm senseだけ。The desk requires no member registration and is always available.

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
1. **0〜3分｜時間を見る**：Tap four beats, then feel four subdivisions inside each beat. 声がbeat head、middle、slightly behindのどこへ入るかを三か所だけ印にする。  
2. **3〜6分｜発音を見る**：Find strong consonants, vowel glides, tiny nasal-like elements, and phrase endings. 「変だから」ではなく、how timing changesを書く。  
3. **6〜9分｜言葉を見る**：Pick three words or phrases that survive one listen. 感嘆、repetition、固有名詞、foreign-sounding fragmentsがmeaningとrhythmのどちらへ効くか分ける。  
4. **9〜12分｜尺度を見る**：Find the smallest concrete object and the largest abstract idea, then count how quickly the lyric moves between them. カメラのzoom speedを見る。  
5. **12〜15分｜配役を見る**：Mark solo, small group, unison, and call-and-response. 「なぜこのvoiceなのか」を一つ仮説にし、最後にofficial creditsで作詞・作曲・編曲を分ける。

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
The key is not to decide “this sounds like Tsunku” before listening closely. 五層をseparately observeしてから、where they connectを見る。A striking pronunciation may not be central if it has no relationship to timing or casting. Conversely, a song with no flashy phonetic trick may still carry strong continuity through fine timing and the leap from tiny detail to large theme.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
After fifteen minutes, do not leave a score like「つんく節度80点」. Leave one sentence explaining **which layers connect to create the song’s particular sense of “likeness.”** Style is more interesting as wiring than as grading.

## 9. 作る側の実践――add a constraint, not a costume

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
This structure can also help anyone making lyrics, copy, plans, or creative work. ただし、「つんく♂風」を再現するのではなく、methodを抽象化したほうがよい。Instead of borrowing surface quirks, **place one small constraint in the work and let it propagate across several layers**.

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
> **15-minute making experiment**  
> ①題材は「今日ちょっとだけ気になったこと」を一つに絞る。  
> ②Create one short line that can survive a single listen.  
> ③四拍を細かく数え、そのlineをwhere to place itという観点で三通り試す。  
> ④If you alter pronunciation, do it once and explain what it changes in timing.  
> ⑤最小の具体から、一段だけlarger questionへ飛ぶ。  
> ⑥複数人で表現するなら、“who changes the meaning by saying it?”で役割を決める。

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
The crucial thing is not to turn every「い」into「うぃ」and declare victory. 表面の発音だけを足すと、すぐcostumeになる。Even without any special pronunciation, the method can still teach us plenty if fine timing, one ear-catching point, a jump from small detail to larger meaning, and role assignment work together. If adding three「うぃ」finished the job, music production would be easier than setting up wireless LAN. Civilization is not that generous.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
What is worth borrowing is not the quirk but **the habit of making one decision cause another**. リズムを決めたらpronunciationが変わる。Words change casting. 題材 changes the distance of the viewpoint. When parts of a work begin to have reasons for one another, an accidental pile of tricks starts to feel like a coherent style.

## 10. 反証も必要――not everything is 16-beat, and not everything uses one vocal style

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
Once a model becomes tidy, it starts trying to explain everything. That is the danger. 「つんく節」「ハロプロ節」はformal musicological categoriesではなく、本人、歌手、media、fansが使うvernacular labelsである。So the five-layer model here is an observation tool, not a theorem that forces every song into one box.

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
Tsunku’s own catalogue varies widely by era, group, rhythm, register, harmony, and vocal demand. ハロー！プロジェクトにもmany lyricists, composers, and arrangersが参加し、それぞれのauthorial voiceがある。When musician 小林郁也 analyzed a particular period of Morning Musume。in 2014, he pointed to several interacting methods—relative harmonic stasis, moving bass and melody, clear sections, rhythmic acceleration—rather than reducing the sound to one formula.

<!-- level:2 role:qualification -->
Likewise, terms such as「16ビート」「い→うぃ」「子音を強く」are useful working language for singers, but they are not complete acoustic descriptions. 波形やphonetic transcriptionで測れば、different songs and singers may involve different physical phenomena. This essay respects the practical vocabulary used by creators and performers without treating it as universal phonetic law.

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
Exceptions actually make “style” easier to see. If every song behaved identically, it would be a specification, not a style. ある曲ではpronunciation、別の曲ではcasting、また別の曲ではeveryday-to-cosmic scale shiftが強い。Listeners bundle several partial cues and still say「なんかハロプロだ」. Recognition may work more like a family resemblance than a single matching test.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
The goal, then, is not to police “authentic Tsunku-bushi.” **Ask what continues, what changes, in which era, song, and production stage.** 「節」という雑な言葉を雑なまま愛しつつ、observationだけは雑にしない。そのくらいがちょうどよい。

## 11. 調べる前と後で、「節」の聞こえ方が変わった

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
I began by wanting to know why the mouth becomes strange at certain moments. 「なぜそこで、その口になるのか」。But after following the evidence, the mouth turned out to be almost late in the chain. Before pronunciation comes rhythm; beside it sit melody and lyrics; around it are casting, demo vocals, and lessons.

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
A seemingly eccentric way of subdividing a word like「幸せ」suddenly looks rational once its timing purpose becomes visible. そして、そのrational choiceを何年もmany singersが身体で反復すると、what began as one producer’s method can become the group’s “normal.” That is the moment an individual habit starts becoming culture.

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
So「つんく節」and「ハロプロ節」are not two labels for exactly the same outline. 前者にはauthorial decision-makingがあり、後者にはthose decisions after they have passed into other bodies. They overlap, but not perfectly. Once we accept the gap, non-Tsunku songs and later generations can be heard not as “mainline versus non-mainline,” but as transformations of a living culture.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
In the end, what I had called「節」looked less like a sonic fingerprint and more like **a wiring diagram for time**. 拍の間へvoiceを置き、口の動きで位置を調整し、ear-catching wordsを選び、whose voice will carry themを決め、そのmethodを次のbodyへ渡す。Next time one strange syllable makes you laugh, laugh. Then ask, “What did that tiny sound move inside time?” The song may stay interesting for a little longer.

## 12. 再リサーチ用プロンプト

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
Neither creator statements alone nor fan impressions alone can fully explain「つんく節」「ハロプロ節」. 作詞、composition、recording、singing、choreography、arrangement、training、era differencesをいったん分け、最後にもう一度つなぐ必要がある。

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
The prompt below is designed not to reproduce this essay’s conclusion but to challenge it. 新しいinterviewsやsongsが増えたとき、which parts of the five-layer model survive and which collapseを検証できるようにする。

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
If style is a living culture, the conclusion should remain revisable. 固定すべきなのはanswerではなく、**the method of inquiry**である。

~~~text
あなたは、日本のポピュラー音楽、アイドル史、歌唱研究、作詞作曲、録音制作を横断する研究者です。
テーマ：「つんく節」「ハロプロ節」と呼ばれるものは、実際には何の組み合わせなのか。

1. 「つんく節」「つんく♂歌唱」「ハロプロ節」を最初から同義にしない。
2. つんく♂本人のセルフライナーノーツ、公式インタビュー、ハロー！プロジェクト公式クレジットを一次資料として優先する。
3. 元・現メンバー、他歌手、編曲家の証言を別レイヤーとして集める。
4. 各主張を「本人の明示」「歌手の証言」「批評家の分析」「ファン／対談参加者の解釈」「筆者の仮説」に分類する。
5. 次の五層を別々に観察する。
   - 時間：16ビート、細分、前ノリ／後ろノリ、変則的な区切り
   - 発音：子音、母音の滑り、鼻音、語尾、細かな音の追加
   - 言葉：耳キャッチ、パンチライン、小さな題材、反復、外国語風の音
   - 配役：仮歌、歌割り、ソロ、少人数、ユニゾン
   - 文化：レッスン、録音指示、世代継承、非つんく♂曲への残存
6. 「16ビートだからハロプロ」「い→うぃだからつんく節」のような単一要因の断定を反証する例を探す。
7. 1990年代、2000年代、2010年代、2020年代で同じ特徴が続くかを比較する。
8. つんく♂作詞作曲曲と、他作家曲を同一グループ内で比較し、歌唱文化の連続性と作家性の差を分ける。
9. 可能なら同一曲の仮歌、録音証言、ライブ映像、公式歌詞、編曲クレジットを照合する。
10. 音響的に検証できる主張は、波形、拍位置、音価、発音記号などで測る方法を提案する。
11. 著作権上、歌詞は必要最小限の短い引用にとどめ、原文全文を再掲しない。
12. 最後に「何がつんく♂個人の方法で、何がハロー！プロジェクトの共有文化になったか」を分けて結論する。
~~~
