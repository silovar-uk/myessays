# B&ZAIは、武道館の1曲目でバンドをやらなかった
## Why a band opened Budokan like an idol｜8-piece setup, 100 stages, SUMMER SONIC, and ROCK'N'DOL

May 9, 2026. Nippon Budokan.

B&ZAI opened their solo show with **「なつ♡あい」**.

Not with a wall of guitars.

Not with a song designed to prove that eight idols can play as a band.

They started with a bright, straight-ahead idol original.

少し妙だ。

B&ZAI had been formed in 2025 as an eight-member group where every member carries an instrumental role. 結成直後から「バンドもやるアイドル」としてBudokanを目標に掲げ、roughly a year later, they reached it.

And at Budokan, they did not begin by “proving the band.”

[ORICON NEWS「B&ZAI、日本武道館公演セットリスト」](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2453359/full/)

That raises the obvious question.

Are B&ZAI a band, or are they idols?

調べる前は、そのtwo-way classificationをすれば正体が分かると思っていた。

After looking through the formation, live reports, festival appearance and setlists, the question itself started to look wrong.

**B&ZAI are not trying to choose band or idol. Their product is the ability to switch between the two with the same eight people.**

---

## 1. FACT｜“B&ZAI” is much more literally BAND than it looks

Start with the name.

B&ZAI is pronounced “Banzai.”

日刊スポーツの取材では、「B&」がband、「ZAI」が「逸材」「財産」に由来し、バンドで“財”を成すという意味が説明されている。

So the ampersand is not only visual styling.

**The idea of a band is embedded in the name itself.**

[日刊スポーツ「B&ZAI、初単独ライブで語ったグループ名」](https://www.nikkansports.com/m/entertainment/news/amp/202506260001025.html)

Then came the 2026 tour title:

**B&ZAI LIVE TOUR 2026 - ROCK'N'DOL -**

Hashimoto explained that the title joined rock spirit with their love of being idols and of STARTO culture.

これは外から付けたgenre labelではない。

The group itself created a word for the overlap.

**ROCK + IDOL = ROCK'N'DOL.**

[日刊スポーツ「B&ZAI『ROCK'N'DOL』に込めた思い」](https://www.nikkansports.com/entertainment/news/202602270001640.html)

---

## 2. FACT｜An eight-piece setup already breaks the normal band seating chart

At the first solo concert in June 2025, ORICON described B&ZAI as STARTO ENTERTAINMENT's first eight-member band lineup.

The reported roles were:

- 橋本涼：vocal / guitar
- 矢花黎：bass
- 今野大輝：guitar
- 菅田琳寧：guitar / saxophone
- 本髙克樹：keyboard
- 鈴木悠仁：vocal / guitar
- 川﨑星輝：drums
- 稲葉通陽：violin / trumpet

[ORICON NEWS「B&ZAI、初単独ライブ『First Beat』」](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2392771/full/)

Guitars, bass, drums, keyboard.

Already large.

Then add saxophone, violin and trumpet.

But the stranger part is that those eight people do not stay in those positions.

踊る。歌う。ローラースケートを使う。アクロバットも入る。

In the first solo report, 「truth」 used Inaba's violin in the arrangement. 「Cry out」 combined dance, acrobatics and band performance in the same show. Hashimoto also performed on roller skates.

A conventional band becomes stable when people enter their instrumental positions.

B&ZAI remain B&ZAI even when they **leave** those positions.

That is a different system.

---

## 3. FACT｜They were not eight rookie band members assembled from zero

B&ZAI were formed on February 16, 2025.

STARTO ENTERTAINMENT announced a Junior reorganization creating ACEes, KEY TO LIT and B&ZAI.

The eight members came from existing groups:

- HiHi Jets: 橋本涼
- 7 MEN 侍: 矢花黎、今野大輝、菅田琳寧、本髙克樹
- 少年忍者: 鈴木悠仁、川﨑星輝、稲葉通陽

[ORICON NEWS「ジュニア再編、新グループ3組結成」](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2369427/full/)

[ジュニア Official Website「B&ZAI」](https://jr-official.starto.jp/s/jr/groups/23)

This matters.

They were not eight beginners told, “Today you become a band.”

Each member already carried experience from a different performance context.

再編は、existing experienceをいったん解体し、another eight-person architectureへ載せ直す作業だった。

So the early challenge was not only learning instruments or songs.

It was connecting different kinds of **idol bodies**—singing, dancing, playing, moving, talking—inside one group.

これはinterpretationだが、later stage design keeps returning to exactly that connection.

---

## 4. CASE｜Their first solo show was already more than “the band part”

About four months after formation, B&ZAI held their first solo concert, “B&ZAI LIVE 2025 First Beat,” at Pia Arena MM on June 25–26, 2025.

Three performances, 30,000 people in total.

The interesting part is the design.

They did not spend the whole first solo concert trying to establish credibility as “a real band.”

先輩曲をband arrangementで演奏する。

Then they dance with handheld microphones.

They use roller skates.

They use acrobatics.

They move back into band performance.

[WEBザテレビジョン「B&ZAI初単独ライブ『First Beat』セットリスト」](https://thetv.jp/news/detail/1274461/p4/)

At that stage, their original catalog was still thin. Reports introduced new songs such as “BML” and “First Beat,” while much of the show used songs inherited from senior groups.

That could be described simply as “not enough original songs yet.”

But there is another reading.

When ownership of songs is limited, identity has to come from:

**selection**
**arrangement**
**who holds what**
**when instruments disappear**

In other words, B&ZAI initially had to build identity through **editorial control of performance**, not just through ownership of repertoire.

---

## 5. OVERKILL RESEARCH｜I tried to label the Budokan set “band” or “idol.” The categories broke.

So I did the slightly unnecessary thing.

I tried to classify the 2026 Budokan setlist into two buckets:

**Band songs**
and
**Idol songs**

Surely that would reveal where the group really sits.

It did not work.

The opener is 「なつ♡あい」, a B&ZAI original presented from the idol side.

Then come senior-group songs such as “SHAKE,” “パリピポアンセム” and “weeeek,” mixed with B&ZAI originals including “Ready for B&ZAI,” “KISS'N'DOL” and “Ain't No Dream Over.”

Later, the show moves into a stronger band block: “First Beat,” “LOVE YOU ONLY,” “無責任ヒーロー,” and toward the end “Braver.” The encore includes “衝動 Never end.”

[ORICON NEWS「B&ZAI、日本武道館公演セットリスト」](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2453359/full/)

Counting only unique tracks explicitly credited to B&ZAI in ORICON's list, there are at least seven originals across the show.

Their own repertoire had clearly grown from the first-solo period.

Yet the classification became harder, not easier.

Because:

**song origin ≠ performance mode.**

A senior idol song can become a band performance.

An original B&ZAI song can be performed in full idol mode.

The same eight people can put instruments down and then pick them up again a few songs later.

So genre labels are not enough.

The useful unit is not “what kind of song is this?”

It is:

**How are these eight people switching modes right now?**

---

## 6. FACT｜The group named that switching system “ROCK'N'DOL”

Back to the 2026 tour title.

ROCK'N'DOL.

The word is useful because it does not make one side subordinate.

It does not say rock is real and idol is decoration.

It does not say idol is real and band is a special skill.

二つを横に置いて、one wordにしている。

[日刊スポーツ「B&ZAI『ROCK'N'DOL』に込めた思い」](https://www.nikkansports.com/entertainment/news/202602270001640.html)

Now the Budokan opener looks different.

If the primary goal had been “prove we are a serious band,” opening with instrumental force would have been an obvious design choice.

Instead, they opened with 「なつ♡あい」.

They could go to the band later.

Then back to dance.

Then back again.

**They did not need the first song to settle the category.**

That may be the most ROCK'N'DOL thing in the entire show.

---

## 7. CASE｜They built the group through repetitions, not by waiting for a finished form

At the first solo show, Hashimoto spoke about wanting to perform many shows regardless of venue size, while also naming Budokan as a goal.

[ORICON NEWS「B&ZAI、初単独で今後の目標を語る」](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2392705/full/)

Then the pace accelerated.

August 2025: SUMMER SONIC.

March 2026: ORICON reports the group reached its 100th performance.

May 2026: Nippon Budokan.

[SUMMER SONIC 2025「TOKYO DAY2 LINEUP」](https://www.summersonic.com/2025/lineup/tokyo-day2/)

[ORICON NEWS「B&ZAI、日本武道館で約9000人魅了」](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2453359/full/)

The interesting number is not simply “100 = success.”

For a group with constant mode changes, every performance is also practice in transition.

Pick up instruments.

Put them down.

Change positions.

Sing.

Dance.

Return to playing.

That is not only song rehearsal. It is **system rehearsal**.

「公演数を重ねたい」という初期の発言と、翌年の100公演を並べると、B&ZAI looks less like a finished concept delivered to large venues and more like a concept refined through live repetition.

That last sentence is interpretation, not a reported fact.

---

## 8. CASE｜SUMMER SONIC does not “prove they were accepted as a band.” That restraint matters.

On August 17, 2025, B&ZAI appeared on the PACIFIC STAGE at SUMMER SONIC 2025 Tokyo.

They are on the official lineup.

[SUMMER SONIC 2025「TOKYO DAY2 LINEUP」](https://www.summersonic.com/2025/lineup/tokyo-day2/)

It is tempting to write:

“An idol band entered a rock festival and was recognized as a real band.”

That sentence is neat.

It also goes beyond the evidence.

The lineup proves the appearance.

It does not automatically prove what the appearance “certified.”

むしろ、その後もB&ZAIがidol sideを捨てなかったことの方が重要だ。

At Budokan in 2026, band performance still coexisted with senior-group songs, dance, staging and the broader STARTO live grammar.

So SUMMER SONIC looks less like graduation from idol culture and more like an expansion of where that hybrid format could operate.

**They did not have to stop being idols to enter another kind of venue.**

---

## 9. DISCOVERY｜The real weapon is not the instruments. It is the switch.

Before researching B&ZAI, the easy description was:

“An idol group where all eight members can play instruments.”

True.

But incomplete.

The first solo was already not band-only.

The Budokan opener was 「なつ♡あい」.

The group itself uses the name ROCK'N'DOL.

As the original catalog grows, they still reinterpret songs from senior groups.

They can move into band mode and then leave it without changing the group.

So the core advantage is probably not simply:

**Eight people can play instruments.**

It is:

**All eight can enter band mode, leave band mode, and remain the same group.**

楽器は重要。

But being unfixed by the instruments matters too.

Compared with a conventional band, that is strange.

Compared with a conventional idol group, it is also strange.

At the start, I asked whether B&ZAI were a band or idols.

After the research, the question feels slightly clumsy.

They are not trying to give one answer.

**They are building a stage where the same eight people can cross the border repeatedly.**

That is why opening Budokan without immediately “doing the band” does not weaken the concept.

They can enter that mode later.

And leave again.

At first B&ZAI looked like **idols who can play in a band**.

Now they look closer to **eight performers who turned the switch between band and idol into the expression itself**.

ROCK'N'DOL is a strange word.

It is also unexpectedly precise.
