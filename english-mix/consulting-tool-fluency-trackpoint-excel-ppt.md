# コンサルが赤いポチに慣れる前に、何に慣れるべきか

*TrackPoint, Excel, PowerPoint――操作の先にあるdecision-ready work*

A ThinkPad has a tiny red dot in the keyboard. その操作が上達しても、売上の計算が間違っていたら意味がない。A fast cursor cannot fix a wrong number. 大事なのは指の動きではなく、正しい判断へたどり着けるかだ。

I want to get better at Excel and PowerPoint for consulting work. 基本操作は分かっているつもりだ。でもknowing the buttons（ボタンを知ること）とdelivering decision-ready work（判断できる成果物を作ること）は違う。

The linked note article from 2019 is about the TrackPoint, not the trackpad. 赤い突起と平らな面は別の道具。A small naming mistake reveals a bigger question: are we learning a device, or learning a better workflow?

**As of October 9, 2026.** Windows版Microsoft 365を中心に確認。端末とWeb版で動作が異なる場合がある。

関連：[PowerPointは、マウスを速くするより触る回数を減らせ](#/essay/thinkpad-powerpoint-reduce-mouse-switching)

## 1. The red dot is about switching――赤い点が減らす切り替え

The note says TrackPoint reduces movement between typing and pointing. Lenovo's official guide confirms finger-pressure pointing and center-button scrolling. これは製品の仕様として確認できる。

The “200 round trips” and “40 meters” in the article are not independently verified here as general averages. 数値はそのまま一般化しない。The fingernail advice also differs from Lenovo's fingertip guidance. 手に痛みが出るならやめる。

The bigger idea is switching cost（切り替えによる負担）. We switch from keyboard to mouse, from data to slides, and from analysis to explanation. すべての切り替えをなくすのではなく、無駄な切り替えを見つけたい。

## 2. Match the device to the task――道具は用途で使い分ける

TrackPoint is a pointing stick; trackpad is a touch surface; mouse is a separate pointer. Windows supports scrolling and some multi-finger gestures on compatible trackpads. 機種・設定差がある。

A hypothesis: TrackPoint for short typing-to-pointer moves, trackpad for scrolling and gestures, mouse for detailed dragging. ただしbest device（最適な機器）は人と作業で変わる。

Compare the same task using all three devices. Measure time, mistakes, and comfort. The winner is the workflow that works, not the gadget with the strongest fan club.

- Typing plus short moves: try TrackPoint.
- Long scrolling and window switching: try trackpad gestures.
- Fine placement and long dragging: compare a mouse.

## 3. Excel must be auditable――Excelは検証できて初めて役立つ

Knowing SUM or XLOOKUP is useful. But a consulting model needs an audit trail（根拠の追跡経路）. A plausible number is not necessarily a reliable number.

Consider club revenue. Fiscal years, consolidated versus standalone figures, disclosed versus estimated values may differ. Fast formulas cannot fix inconsistent definitions. First define what is comparable.

Microsoft documents Excel Tables, error checking, and Power Query for importing, transforming, and refreshing data. これらは単発のtipsではなく、reproducible analysis（再現可能な分析）を作る部品だ。

Practice a workbook with distinct stages: raw data, transformation, analysis, validation. 元データを残し、答えがどう変わったか説明できることが目標になる。

- Input: keep source, date, definition, unit, and period.
- Transform: use Tables; check missing values, duplicates, and joins.
- Calculate: state scope and denominators.
- Validate: reconcile totals, outliers, formula references, and refresh behavior.

## 4. PowerPoint is an argument tool――図形より先に主張を決める

PowerPoint has duplication, formatting, alignment, and distribution tools. They can reduce repetitive editing. But perfectly aligned rectangles cannot make a decision.

“Market size” is a topic, not an argument. A claim like “The total market is large, but our serviceable workflow is narrow” gives the slide a testable direction. ただしclaim（主張）はevidence（根拠）で裏づける必要がある。

First write one message sentence. Then choose evidence, comparison, and counterexamples. Only then use formatting shortcuts. Learn both argument design（論証設計）and tool fluency（操作の熟達）.

- Question: what does the slide answer?
- Claim: what does the evidence support?
- Evidence: are sources, units, periods, and comparisons visible?
- Decision: what should the reader decide or investigate?

## 5. Practice one end-to-end case――一つの案件を通しで解く

Build one fictional case: select ten sports clubs for further research on a video-management service. Use mock revenue, match counts, video-use frequency, responsible departments, and rights constraints. These are not real club facts.

Define each field and its assumptions. Compare actual workflow needs, not revenue alone. A big budget is not necessarily a buying reason. Excel makes the distinctions explicit.

Create only two slides: how the market is segmented, and which prospects warrant further research. Keep the mock-data label and uncertain assumptions. Do not turn a hypothesis into a fact during transfer.

Now the exercise is not “use Excel and then PowerPoint.” It is transforming evidence into a decision-ready explanation while preserving uncertainty. That is the workflow worth repeating.

## 6. Run a ten-day improvement loop――10営業日の改善実験

Proposed plan: 15–20 minutes daily. Keep the case comparable and change only one or two habits at a time. This is a practical experiment, not a scientifically validated optimal schedule.

Start with devices and Excel, then PowerPoint and the full workflow. If Alt+Tab beats a gesture, use it. Record desktop versus web Excel, because shortcuts may differ.

On day ten, repeat a comparable task. A faster result with more errors is not an improvement. Keep three habits at most; discard the rest.

- Days 1–2: baseline the model and two-slide deliverable.
- Days 3–4: compare TrackPoint, trackpad, and mouse.
- Days 5–6: Excel Tables, navigation, references, and error checks.
- Days 7–8: duplicate, align, and revise claims and evidence.
- Day 9: update inputs, calculations, and slides.
- Day 10: retest and keep three effective habits.

## 7. Fast is not the same as finished――速さには品質条件がある

A 20-minute slide deck that needs 30 minutes of rework takes 50 minutes. A fast spreadsheet with the wrong source is not useful. Speed-only metrics may reward skipped checks.

Track four measures: production time, errors, end-to-end time including rework, and evidence traceability. Accuracy, confidentiality, and honest sourcing are non-negotiable. These KPIs are proposals, not industry benchmarks.

Practice on public or mock data. Do not send confidential client files to unapproved AI or personal tools. A workflow is not effective if it breaks required safeguards.

## 8. Keep the workflow, not the trick――残すべきは仕事の型

Excel should give traceable numbers. PowerPoint should make a decision discussable. Input devices should remove friction on the way. この順に考えると、学ぶべきskill（技能）の優先順位が変わる。

The red dot points to switching costs. But a larger cost may be going back from a slide to Excel to find missing evidence, then forgetting to update the slide. That rework matters more than the distance your hand travels.

Tool fluency means explaining the path from data to claim, updating it when inputs change, and marking uncertainty honestly. It means repeating the work with comparable quality. The red dot may save a hand movement; a good workflow preserves the meaning of the work.

## Verification notes――事実・解釈・提案

- Verified: the note covers TrackPoint, Lenovo explains how it works, and Microsoft documents the listed Excel and PowerPoint functions.
- Interpretation: tool fluency can be judged by reduced rework and better repeatability.
- Proposal: a ten-day experiment using time, errors, total rework, and traceability. Its effectiveness has not been measured.
