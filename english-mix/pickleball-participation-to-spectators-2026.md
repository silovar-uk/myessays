# ピックルボールはなぜ急に流行したのか — From Playing to Watching
### What 24.3 million players do, and what they do not prove

**Research date: November 11, 2026.** このEnglish Mix版は日本語を理解の足場に、自然な英語を文脈の中へ織り込む。Facts, interpretation, and forecasts are kept separate.

### 要旨｜Participation is not the same as profitability

A booming sport sounds like a booming business. でもその二つは別だ。In 2025, 24.3 million people in the United States played pickleball. Yet the first half of 2026 brought signs of slower growth, and one indoor-court operator entered restructuring. Meanwhile, TV audiences and attendance at professional events were growing. 「する」「観る」「施設を運営する」を同じ市場として扱うと、重要な違いを見落とす。

## 1｜守：What exactly is pickleball?

ピックルボールは穴の開いたプラスチックボールと、板状のパドルを使うラケット競技。You can play singles or doubles, although doubles is particularly common. The court measures 6.10 by 13.41 metres, much smaller than a doubles tennis court. ネット中央は約86センチメートル。

The small playing area makes the first rally relatively accessible. But the same short distance creates fast exchanges, precise placement and difficult teamwork at higher levels. **Easy to learn does not mean easy to master.**

The key rule involves the **non-volley zone**, nicknamed the kitchen. ネットから両側約2.13メートルの区域では、空中の球を直接打つボレーができない。You may enter the kitchen, and you may hit a ball there after it bounces. If your momentum carries you into the kitchen after a volley, that can also be a fault.

The **two-bounce rule** requires the receiver to let the serve bounce and the serving side to let the return bounce. その後は、キッチンの規制を守ればボレーしてもよい。This prevents an immediate net attack and helps create longer rallies.

通常は11点先取、2点差が必要。Traditional side-out scoring awards points only to the serving side; some professional formats use rally scoring. The format should always be checked for the specific event. [Official rules, 2026](https://usapickleball.org/rules/).

## 2｜守：A family game from 1965

Pickleball was invented in 1965 on Bainbridge Island, Washington. ジョエル・プリチャードらが家族の遊びとして考案した。Existing badminton space and simple equipment became a new game. Competitive structures developed later, with major milestones in the 1970s and organized rules in 1984.

The name has competing origin stories: a dog named Pickles, and a rowing term, pickle boat. 家族関係者の説明には後者を支持するものもあり、犬説だけを確定した由来と扱うのは避けたい。[USA Pickleball history](https://usapickleball.org/about/).

Why did it explode decades later? Low entry barriers mattered, as did small-group play and social connection. コロナ禍は追い風だったと考えられるが、それだけでは説明できない。The sport later acquired commercial courts, brands, governing bodies, and professional leagues. **The game came first; the media product came later.**

## 3｜守：「する」スポーツとしての強さ

For a first-time player, the attraction is immediate: a rally is possible, four people can share one court, and the equipment is relatively simple. 友人同士、親子、同僚、地域の交流にも使いやすい。ただし、年齢や経験の違いが完全に消えるわけではない。

At higher levels, the challenge becomes strategic. You must decide not only where to hit, but what reply you want to force. A good shot creates a difficult next shot for the opponent. ダブルスでは相棒との距離、守備範囲、球を打つ順番も重要だ。

For beginners, a rental-paddle session is a safer starting point than an expensive purchase. キッチンと得点ルールを学び、ラリーを経験する。Then practise short, controlled shots. Footwear, warm-up and safe movement still matter: accessibility does not eliminate injuries. [Pickleball Japan](https://japanpickleball.org/).

## 4｜守：「観る」ときは the battle for the net を見る

Professional doubles can look strangely slow. Players exchange gentle balls near the net. なぜ強打しないのか。Because a high, attackable reply is exactly what the other team is waiting for. What looks like a quiet exchange is often positional pressure.

Five useful terms make the game easier to watch.

- **Dink（ディンク）** — a soft shot into the opponent's kitchen. 相手に高い球を打たせようとする繊細な駆け引き。
- **Third-shot drop（サードショットドロップ）** — a gentle third shot that helps the serving team move forward.
- **Drive（ドライブ）** — a fast and low offensive shot. 打つ速さの選択そのものが戦術。
- **Speed-up（スピードアップ）** — a sudden acceleration from a slow exchange.
- **Poach / Around the post（ポーチ／ATP）** — an interception by a partner, or a spectacular reply around the net post.

Watch the players, not just the ball. レシーブ側は先にネット付近へ進みやすい。The serving team tries to neutralize that advantage with the third shot. Once both teams are at the line, the question becomes: who will offer the first attackable ball? [USA Pickleball](https://usapickleball.org/what-is-pickleball/).

## 5｜守：Two different professional products

**PPA Tour** is a player-focused tournament circuit, with singles and doubles. テニスのツアーに近く、「誰が強いか」を中心にスターが生まれる。Ben Johns and Anna Leigh Waters are examples of internationally recognized players. [PPA Tour](https://www.ppatour.com/).

**Major League Pickleball (MLP)** emphasizes teams and collective results. 2026年は20チームが参加し、男子・女子・混合ダブルスで競った。A tied match can be settled by the DreamBreaker, a rotating singles tiebreaker. This structure creates a different reason to care: loyalty to a team, not only admiration for an athlete. [MLP](https://majorleaguepickleball.co/).

These models build different fan relationships, but neither guarantees a profitable league. **Viewership is a demand indicator, not a profit statement.** 放映権、協賛、チケット収入と、会場・中継・選手報酬の費用は別に見る必要がある。

## 6｜最新動向：24.3 million players, but slower growth

According to SFIA, 24.3 million Americans played pickleball in 2025, up from 4.2 million in 2020. 2025年の前年比増加率は22.8%。Of those players, 7.5 million played at least eight times in the year. 若者の参加率や女性の参加比率も拡大した。[SFIA annual research](https://www.sfia.org/sports-fitness-industry-association/resources/sfia-releases-2026-pickleball-single-sport-report-team-sports-reports-to-follow).

But SFIA's 2026 mid-year report found **1.3% growth** for pickleball in the first half, versus 14.5% for padel. これは成熟の兆候とも読めるが、2025年の通年前年比と2026年の中間集計は対象期間が違う。Different base sizes also make the two sports' growth rates difficult to compare directly. [SFIA mid-year report](https://www.sfia.org/sports-fitness-industry-association/resources/u-s-sports-and-fitness-participation-reaches-record-high-new-sfia-mid-year-data-shows).

The commercial lesson is simple: do not build a ten-year financial forecast on the assumption of 20% annual player growth. 市場が拡大していても、伸び方は変わりうる。

## 7｜最新動向：Watching is growing on its own timeline

The 2026 MLP championship broadcast on CBS drew an average of **773,000 viewers**. The league also reported **69% growth in on-site attendance** and **87% growth in ticket revenue** compared with 13 events in 2025. [MLP 2026 season review](https://majorleaguepickleball.co/news/mlp-produces-record-setting-2026-season/).

Participation growth moderated, while professional viewership grew. That is not necessarily a contradiction. プレーヤーが競技を理解し、有名選手を覚え、後から観戦を始める時間差がありうる。But that pathway remains a hypothesis until player and viewer behavior is studied together.

More viewers are good news. They do not establish that ticketing, sponsorship and media rights cover all league costs. **An audience is not the same thing as operating profit.**

## 8｜最新動向：Japan's 2026 institutional and event shift

In April 2026, the former JPA and PJF organizations were combined into the new Pickleball Japan framework. 日本の公式統括団体の会員数は、2026年9月26日時点で5,955人。That figure measures members, not everyone who plays. [Pickleball Japan](https://japanpickleball.org/pj/).

A major international event, **PPA Asia 500 Sansan Tokyo Open**, took place in Tachikawa on July 1–4, hosted by TBS Holdings with Sansan as special sponsor. TBS presented pickleball as both a sporting event and a way to create shared experiences and business connections. [TBS event statement](https://www.tbs.co.jp/pickleball-tokyo/about.html).

A domestic industry survey estimated **about 330,000 current players** from a 30,000-person consumer sample. ただしこれは民間調査による推計であり、米国SFIAの年間参加人口とは定義が違う。It should not be compared mechanically with the US figure. [Pickleball One research](https://pickle-one.com/research/2026-competitor-population/).

The **PPA Asia 125 PJ Tokyo Challenger** is scheduled for December 7–9 at Ariake. 日本で競技の普及と観戦習慣がどう結びつくか、実際の運営と来場実績を追いたい。[Official event details](https://japanpickleball.org/ppa-asia-125/).

## 9｜破：The Picklr problem — growth without a safe balance sheet

In October 2026, The Picklr's parent companies entered Chapter 11 restructuring in the United States. 申立日は10月9日で、全店舗の一斉閉鎖を意味しない。[Official case site](https://cases.omniagentsolutions.com/?clientId=3812).

News reporting cited about **$10.3 million in 2025 revenue** and a **$4.4 million net loss**, with early franchise lease guarantees placing pressure on the business. [Quartz coverage](https://qz.com/picklr-pickleball-franchisor-chapter-11-bankruptcy-101326).

This is a lesson about business-model design, not proof that the sport is failing. A court has rent, staff, utilities, maintenance, marketing and financial obligations. 週末が満員でも平日昼間に空きがあれば、年間収支は厳しくなる。Increasing occupancy through discounts may reduce revenue per hour.

A serious feasibility study needs **utilization by time slot, average revenue per court-hour, membership retention, and contribution margin**. 参加人口の予測だけでは出店判断に足りない。

## 10｜破：Noise, injuries, equipment and public space

A plastic ball struck with a hard paddle makes a distinctive sound. 住宅地に近い屋外コートでは騒音が問題になることがある。Converting tennis courts also changes how public space is allocated among sports. Popularity alone does not settle that conflict.

Injury is another limitation. Quick turns and falls matter, especially when older adults join. 救急外来の負傷事例を見ても、参加者全体の発生率やテニスとの安全性比較は直ちには分からない。A low barrier to entry still requires sensible safety guidance.

Equipment technology brings a third issue: faster, higher-spin paddles can reshape the sport. 競技用具をどこまで認めるかは、公平性と安全性の両方にかかわる。[USA Pickleball equipment and rules](https://usapickleball.org/rules/).

## 11｜破：Three hypotheses for the next three years

**Hypothesis A — indoor courts will face consolidation.** 競技人口が伸びても、家賃と固定費は高い。Operators with lessons, events, food service and corporate contracts may be more resilient than those relying only on hourly bookings. Test this with same-store revenue, closures, daytime occupancy and lease costs.

**Hypothesis B — Japan's participation market will lead its spectator market.** 体験会は小さな規模でも成立するが、プロ興行にはスター、試合の物語、継続的な視聴が必要。Measure repeat participation, paid attendance, and recurring viewership before assuming an audience exists.

**Hypothesis C — the core product may become community.** Four players need a court, but what keeps them coming back may be friendship, routine and belonging. スポーツ施設を「コートを貸す場所」から「人が定期的に会う場所」へ変えられるかが鍵になる。

These are hypotheses, not forecasts supported by a predictive model. 検証する指標を明示して初めて、経営判断に使える。

## 12｜離：Your real competitor may not play another sport

A pickleball venue competes directly with tennis and padel. But consider what customers actually buy: exercise, competition, company, or an enjoyable hour after work. そう考えるとフィットネス、地域サークル、交流イベント、さらにはボードゲームカフェも余暇時間の競合だ。

The spectator market competes for a different scarce resource: attention. プロ選手の技術が高いだけでは、次の試合を見る理由にはならない。Fans need recognizable athletes, rivalry, team identity, and an easy-to-follow narrative.

It helps to separate four markets: **participation** (courts, coaching, memberships), **equipment** (paddles, balls), **professional entertainment** (tickets, sponsorship, media rights), and **related services** (corporate events, communities, food). They may reinforce one another, but their unit economics are different.

Like golf, a sport can have a huge playing market and a separate professional spectator market. **Player numbers show how widely a sport is experienced; they do not tell us who captures the value.**

## 13｜実践：What to do, watch and measure first

**To play:** start with a rental-paddle session. Learn the kitchen and two-bounce rules. Enjoy a rally before worrying about winning, then practise the dink and third-shot drop. 日本の体験会は[Pickleball Japan](https://japanpickleball.org/)で探せる。

**To watch:** compare a [PPA Tour](https://www.ppatour.com/) doubles highlight with an [MLP](https://majorleaguepickleball.co/) team match. Focus on court positioning, not only the finishing shot. 「どちらが先にネット前を取ったか」を追うだけで見方が変わる。

**To evaluate the market:** do not add members, survey-based players, event attendances and TV viewers into one fictional number called fans. Ask who pays, for what, how often, and who bears the fixed cost. 事業性は、人気の総量ではなく顧客行動と収益構造で確かめる。

## 14｜結論：What lasts after the boom?

Pickleball lowered the social and technical cost of starting a game. それは強い価値だ。しかし、続くコミュニティ、採算の合う施設、何度も観たくなるプロ興行は、それぞれ別の設計が必要になる。

24.3 million US players, roughly 330,000 estimated Japanese players, and 773,000 average CBS viewers represent different populations, methods and markets. The important question is not which number is biggest. **It is what each number actually measures — and whose business can create lasting value from it.**
