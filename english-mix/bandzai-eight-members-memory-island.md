# B&ZAIの8人を覚えたい。だから無人島を作った

## Don’t memorize eight names. Build eight routes back to them.

前回、I thought I had understood B&ZAI.

[前編「B&ZAIは、武道館の1曲目でバンドをやらなかった」](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/#/essay/bandzai-rockndol-band-idol-switching?lang=en)では、formation, First Beat, SUMMER SONIC, Budokanを追いながら、B&ZAIをbandかidolかに分類しようとした。

The categories broke.

Eight people can pick up instruments, put them down, dance, sing, and return to the band setup.

So the real weapon looked like **the switch**.

かなり分かった気になった。

Then I looked at a group photo the next day.

**Who is who?**

I had understood the operating system and forgotten the humans running it.

This sequel fixes that problem.

---

## The members themselves were already doing memory training on FUJIYAMA

On August 28, 2026, the official Junior site uploaded a video titled:

**「FUJIYAMAに絶叫しながら9ワード暗記せよ」**

They tried to memorize nine words while riding FUJIYAMA.

[ジュニア Official Website](https://jr-official.starto.jp/s/jr/video/108646?ima=4030)

This is an aggressively bad-looking study environment.

I have never opened an English vocabulary book and thought:

“Retention feels weak today. Maybe I need more roller-coaster.”

Still, the members themselves had turned memory into content.

So I decided not to make another ordinary profile guide.

The goal is practical:

**By the end, can I actually retrieve eight names from eight faces and roles?**

---

## Step 1: stop treating eight people as one flat list

The current official B&ZAI lineup is:

橋本涼、矢花黎、今野大輝、菅田琳寧、本髙克樹、鈴木悠仁、川﨑星輝、稲葉通陽。

[Official B&ZAI profile](https://jr-official.starto.jp/s/jr/groups/23)

A flat list is technically correct and mentally useless.

So first, chunk the eight by their groups before the February 2025 Junior reorganization.

- HiHi Jets: 橋本涼
- 7 MEN 侍: 矢花黎、今野大輝、菅田琳寧、本髙克樹
- 少年忍者: 鈴木悠仁、川﨑星輝、稲葉通陽

[ORICON NEWS](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2369427/full/)

That gives one clean number pattern:

**1 – 4 – 3.**

One Hashimoto.

Four former 7 MEN 侍 members.

Three former 少年忍者 members.

Eight names have become three boxes.

At B&ZAI LIVE 2025 First Beat, ORICON introduced the main instrumental setup as:

- 橋本涼：vocal / guitar
- 矢花黎：bass
- 今野大輝：guitar
- 菅田琳寧：guitar / saxophone
- 本髙克樹：keyboard
- 鈴木悠仁：vocal / guitar
- 川﨑星輝：drums
- 稲葉通陽：violin / trumpet

[ORICON NEWS](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2392713/full/)

These are memory anchors, not permanent job titles. B&ZAI moves between instruments, singing and dance.

But now each person has a place in the stage picture.

Hashimoto: voice and guitar.

Yabana: bass.

Konno: guitar and strong vocal skill.

Sugeta: saxophone in the mix.

Motodaka: keyboard.

Suzuki: voice and guitar.

Kawasaki: drums.

Still, Hashimoto and Suzuki overlap.

We need one more layer.

The official profiles accidentally provide a perfect one.

## Step 3: ask what each person brings to a deserted island

The Junior profiles include the question:

**「無人島にひとつ持っていくなら何？」**

The answers are:

橋本涼：ナタ.

矢花黎：a storage medium to record the life and thoughts on the island so someone may discover them later.

今野大輝：マッチ.

菅田琳寧：日傘.

本髙克樹：眼鏡.

鈴木悠仁：ライター.

川﨑星輝：初対面の人.

稲葉通陽：田植えセット.

Suddenly this is no longer a profile list.

It is a settlement simulation.

Hashimoto wants a machete-like nata.

Konno has matches.

Suzuki has a lighter.

Sugeta protects himself from the sun.

Motodaka needs glasses.

Then the logic starts drifting.

Yabana wants to archive the island.

Kawasaki brings **a person he has never met before**.

Inaba begins rice farming.

B&ZAI could be stranded at noon and have local government by Thursday.

More importantly: these cues are difficult to confuse.

## Why strange cues can help—but not magically

Memory research has a long history of studying distinctiveness.

Unusual items can be easier to retrieve when they stand out from the material around them.

But “weird = automatically memorable” is too simple.

The distinctiveness effect depends on encoding and retrieval conditions.

[Waddill & McDaniel, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9519701/)

So the method here is not “collect eight weird facts.”

It is three layers:

**chunk the group.**

**put each person somewhere.**

**retrieve without looking.**

The deserted island becomes a shared spatial map for all eight.

He is the only former HiHi Jets member in B&ZAI.

And for the island, he chooses a nata.

So build a bridge at the entrance of the island.

On the bridge stands **橋本**.

He has a guitar and a nata.

It is slightly threatening.

It is also impossible to misplace.

Bridge → Hashimoto.

HiHi Jets → only one → Hashimoto.

Nata → Hashimoto.

Three retrieval routes already.

## Build one memory island

The Method of Loci links information to imagined or familiar locations and retrieves it by mentally travelling through them.

A 2025 systematic review and meta-analysis found evidence that it can improve immediate serial recall in adults compared with rehearsal, while also rating the overall evidence quality low to very low because of bias and other limitations.

So: useful technique, not sorcery.

[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40457944/)

My island looks like this.

### Entrance

A bridge.

Hashimoto with guitar and nata.

### Center: former 7 MEN 侍 zone

Yabana on bass, archiving island life.

Konno lighting a fire with matches and singing.

Sugeta doing acrobatics under a parasol, then grabbing a saxophone.

Motodaka in glasses at a keyboard, counting fish.

### Beyond the forest: former 少年忍者 zone

Suzuki with guitar and lighter.

Kawasaki at the drums, with a complete stranger beside him.

### Deepest part of the island

A rice field.

Inaba planting rice while playing violin.

This island is a disaster.

It is also a working index.

## Close the page. Retrieval is the test.

Reading the list again feels productive.

But feeling familiar with the list is not the same as being able to retrieve it.

In a classic 2006 study, Roediger and Karpicke compared restudying with repeated recall tests.

Restudying helped on a test five minutes later, but testing produced better retention after two days and one week.

[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16507066/)

So now: don’t scroll up.

Answer.

Former HiHi Jets. Low voice. Guitar. Nata.

**橋本涼.**

Bass. Bandmaster. Archive.

**矢花黎.**

**今野大輝.**

**菅田琳寧.**

Former 少年忍者. Guitar. Lighter.

## Learning the members changed the first essay

In the first essay, I wrote that B&ZAI’s strength was the switch between band mode and idol mode.

After learning the eight people, that claim looks more specific.

Yabana gives the group a bass-and-bandmaster axis.

Konno brings vocal strength and the leader role.

Sugeta adds physical performance and saxophone.

Motodaka anchors the keyboard side and brings a very different cognitive profile.

Hashimoto and Suzuki both connect voice and guitar, but arrive from different histories.

Kawasaki is the drummer.

Inaba brings violin and other instruments.

This is not eight copies of one skill set.

It is eight different capability bundles that can temporarily become one band.

Then separate.

Then dance.

Then sing.

Then assemble again.

So memorizing the people was not a fan-trivia side quest.

**Once the people became distinct, the group architecture became distinct too.**

---

## Conclusion｜Don’t memorize eight names. Build eight routes back.

The easiest method for me was:

**1 – 4 – 3.**

Then instruments.

Then one distinctive official-profile cue.

Then spatial placement.

Then retrieval.

橋本：bridge / HiHi / nata.

矢花：bass / archive.

今野：leader / matches.

菅田：sax / parasol.

本髙：keyboard / fish / glasses.

鈴木：guitar / lighter.

川﨑：drums / stranger.

稲葉：violin / rice planting.

The goal is not to store eight isolated names.

It is to build **eight retrieval routes**.

And if everything collapses tomorrow, one member should survive memory intact.

Deep inside the island,

Inaba has already started planting rice.
