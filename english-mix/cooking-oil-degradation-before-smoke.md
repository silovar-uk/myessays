# Oil Starts Changing Before It Smokes
## 「中火で温める」を分子まで追う

レシピによく出てくる。

**「フライパンに油を入れ、中火で温める。」**

We read it and move on.

But what exactly are we waiting for?

水ならboilingがある。バターならmeltingがある。

Oil does not give us such a clean signal.

そして、なぜ強火ではなく中火なのか。

At first, my answer was simple.

**Because you do not want to cross the smoke point.**

That is not wrong.

But it is too simple.

調べると、油は煙が見える前から変化していた。

---

## 1. Smoke point is a warning, not the true starting line

USDA explains that oils have different smoke points and that, at the smoke point, oil begins to break down and can develop unpleasant smell or taste.

That makes smoke a very useful kitchen signal.

Visible. Smellable. Hard to miss.

でも、油の劣化は「発煙点を超えた瞬間にだけ始まる」わけではない。

In frying research, hot oil is exposed to heat, oxygen and moisture. Multiple reactions progress over time.

主なものは**thermo-oxidation, polymerization, and hydrolysis**。

So the smoke point is better understood as one obvious alarm in a longer process.

さらに、加水分解などで遊離脂肪酸が増えると、smoke point itself can fall.

The oil changes, and then it becomes easier for the oil to smoke.

A small chemical feedback loop.

[USDA FSIS, “Deep Fat Frying and Food Safety”](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/deep-fat-frying)

[Goicoechea-Oses et al., “Vegetable Oils and Their Use for Frying,” Foods, 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11675685/)

---

## 2. “Oil degradation” is actually several stories happening at once

「油が劣化する」と言うと、新品→古い油という一本線を想像しやすい。

The chemistry is much messier.

### Oxidation / 酸化

Unsaturated fatty acids can react with oxygen.

Heat speeds this up. Early oxidation products can break down further into aldehydes, ketones, acids and many other compounds.

つまり、oil is not simply “getting older.”

**New molecules are being created from the original ones.**

A 2025 review on aldehydes from heated vegetable oils shows that the amount and type of products depend on oil composition, temperature and heating time.

### Hydrolysis / 加水分解

Then food enters the story.

Food contains water.

高温の油に食材を入れると、水分は蒸気になる。同時に、水は油のtriglyceridesを分解する反応にも関わる。

This can increase free fatty acids, monoacylglycerols and diacylglycerols.

I thought I was researching oil.

Suddenly, water became a co-author.

### Polymerization / 重合

This one sounds the strangest.

**Oil molecules can link together and form larger molecules.**

Dimers. Oligomers. Bigger structures.

重合が進むと、粘度上昇や色の変化にもつながる。

Old frying oil can literally contain more large-molecular-weight products than fresh oil.

That “heavy” feeling is not only a metaphor.

[Chemical Changes in Deep-Fat Frying: Reaction Mechanisms, Oil Degradation, and Health Implications, 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12516161/)

[Toxic aldehydes in cooking vegetable oils: Generation, toxicity and disposal methods, 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12281009/)

## 3. So why medium heat?

ここまで読んで、「中火ならこれらの反応が止まるのか」と思う。

No.

**Medium heat is not a chemical boundary.**

コンロの「中火」はoil temperatureそのものではない。鍋、油量、加熱時間、機器で温度は変わる。

So why start there?

Because it gives you more control.

強火より温度上昇を読みやすく、食材を入れる前に必要以上の高温へovershootしにくい。

That changed the meaning of the recipe instruction for me.

「中火」は、油に優しい魔法の火力ではない。

It is a control setting for the cook.

**Heat the oil at a speed you can still manage.**

That is a much more useful interpretation.

---

## 4. Another correction: “degradation” does not mean “instantly dangerous”

Once you start reading about oxidation products and aldehydes, it is tempting to swing too far.

“Then heated oil is bad, right?”

That is also too simple.

USDA Agricultural Research Service notes that oxidation, hydrolysis and polymerization change frying oil. Further breakdown can create off-flavors and potentially harmful compounds at high concentrations.

But the same source also notes something awkward for a simple warning story:

**A small amount of oxidation helps create the characteristic flavor of fried food.**

食品科学、急に白黒を拒否してくる。

The goal is not “zero chemical change.”

Cooking itself is controlled change.

香りも色も食感も、加熱で変化するから生まれる。

The better question is: how hot, how long, how many times, and with what oil?

また、ここで扱った研究の多くは160〜190℃程度のdeep fryingやrepeated heatingが中心。家庭で短時間炒める少量の油と、業務用フライヤーを同じ条件として語るのは避けたい。

[USDA ARS, “Frying Oil Deterioration”](https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/publications/publication/?seqNo115=212286)

[USDA ARS, 2026 edible-oil oxidation research summary](https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/publications/publications-at-this-location/publication/?seqNo115=427405)

## 5. At home, we do not measure molecules. We watch signals.

研究現場や業務用管理では、total polar compounds, acid value, peroxide valueなどで油の状態を測る。

Some countries use limits on total polar compounds for frying-oil control.

家で毎回これを測るのは、a little excessive.

USDA gives much simpler household signals for reused frying oil: cloudiness, foaming, bad smell or taste. Other guidance also mentions darkening, excessive smoking and rancid odor.

見た目。

におい。

泡。

煙。

The ordinary kitchen senses were doing more chemistry than I gave them credit for.

[USDA FSIS, “Can oil be reused safely?”](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/Can-oil-be-reused-safely)

[USDA FSIS, “How long can I keep oil after frying?”](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/How-long-can-I-keep-oil-after-frying)

## 6. “The oil gets tired” was not a terrible metaphor after all

最初、自分は油の劣化を「油が疲れる感じ」と説明した。

Scientifically, oil does not get tired.

But after researching it, I like the metaphor a little more.

Heat leaves a history.

Oxygen leaves a history.

Water from food leaves a history.

Time leaves a history.

元の油は、一瞬で別物になるのではなく、加熱されるたびに少しずつ構成が変わっていく。

Smoke is not the whole story.

**The oil is changing before we can see the change.**

だから、「中火で温める」は油を無傷に保つ指示ではない。

It means: do not rush the oil past the point you actually need.

Now, when I see that boring recipe line, it looks slightly different.

「中火で温める」は火力指定でありながら、同時にこう言っているようにも見える。

**Keep the change slow enough that you are still the one controlling it.**
