# 58% Possession, Zero Goals――マリノスの攻撃はどこで止まるのか

## Keeping the ball is not the same as reaching the goal

A team has **58% possession**, takes **ten shots**, concedes nothing, and still does not win. ここまではよくある0–0だ。ところが、その対戦相手が**17 shots**を放っていたら、keeping the ball and creating danger can no longer be treated as the same thing.

That was Yokohama F. Marinos against JEF United Chiba on **October 17, 2026**. マリノスは10 shots, two on target。千葉は17 shots, five on target。The official J.League data also records 58%–42% possession in Marinos' favour. [Official match statistics](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/101707/stats/)

The question is not whether possession is good or bad. **Where was the ball, and what options did that possession create?** パスを回せたことと、相手の守備者に難しい判断を迫れたことは違う。The preview of the October 21 Marinos–Verdy match becomes more useful when we examine the processes before a goal.

This research is limited to sources available on **October 19, 2026**. It uses official event totals and interviews, not independent full-match video coding. 事実と解釈、観戦時の提案は分けて扱う。

## 1. Twenty-one shots and three on target describe an output, not its cause

Start with two matches. **October 10: Cerezo Osaka 0–0 Marinos.** Marinos had **52% possession, 11 shots and one on target**. Cerezo registered eight shots, two on target. [Official statistics](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/101008/stats/)

**October 17: Chiba 0–0 Marinos.** The corresponding figures for Marinos were **58%, ten shots and two on target**; Chiba took 17 shots, five on target. [Official statistics](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/101707/stats/)

Across those two fixtures, Marinos therefore recorded **21 shots, three on target and zero goals**. The two opposing teams together had **25 shots, seven on target and zero goals**. これはＪリーグの試合記録から行った単純な再集計で、同じ相手との反復実験ではない。

The on-target share was **3/21 ≈ 14.3%** for Marinos and **7/25 = 28.0%** for their two opponents combined. Does that prove Marinos lack shooting technique? **No.** Shot distance, angle, defenders, blocked attempts and the different kinds of opportunities matter. A two-match ratio is not a stable estimate of player ability.

それでも「決定力がない」の一語より、問いは具体的になる。What kinds of shots were taken, from where, under what pressure, and by whom? The numbers identify the next research question rather than provide the final explanation.

## 2. The phrase "poor finishing" hides four different problems

A goal is the final outcome of several interacting actions. For analysis, we can separate **progression**（前進）, **penetration**（相手守備の間や背後への侵入）, **shot creation**（打てる状況を作ること）, and **shot outcome**（枠内、ＧＫや守備者を破る結果）. This is a conceptual map, not a claim that every attack must pass through four identical steps.

For example, a long shot may bypass the usual penetration phase. A direct counterattack may involve very few passes. 一方、長いボール保持でも相手守備を動かせなければシュートまで進まない。A possession figure alone cannot tell us which phase was effective.

The Chiba match report includes **passing accuracy** and an official **chance-creation** category, but neither establishes the probability of an individual shot becoming a goal. [Official J.League stats](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/101707/stats/)

Expected goals, commonly shortened to **xG**, is an estimate based on shot situations. We have **not verified an xG total for these two fixtures** for this analysis, so we cannot supply one. 似た名前の指標を恣意的に「決定機」とまとめるのも避ける。Without the location and context of the attempts, a neat causal story would be invention.

The more precise question is: **did Marinos fail to move forward, enter dangerous spaces, create clean attempts, or beat the keeper?** Only a separation of these possibilities allows a credible diagnosis.

## 3. Corica named the spaces that were difficult to use

After the Chiba match, head coach **Steve Corica** described a first half in which Chiba were more effective in their press and in winning second balls. He said Marinos improved in the second half. More unusually, he identified an actual spacing issue: the **wingers were too wide**, while Chiba's midfielder stepped toward Marinos' midfielder and left space behind. Marinos had planned to exploit that space but struggled to do so. [Corica's official press conference](https://www.f-marinos.com/tricolore_plus/article/1562)

Here the abstract football vocabulary becomes practical. If an opponent moves out to pressure the receiving midfielder, the space behind that defender may become available. But a teammate must arrive there at the right moment, at the right distance, and the pass must be feasible. ただ「幅を取る」だけでは足りない。その幅を、いつ内側へつなぐかが問題になる。

Corica also referred to the **poor pitch condition**. This matters as a qualification: performance in that away game cannot be reduced to tactical choices alone, or automatically projected onto the October 21 National Stadium match. 監督の説明は重要な当事者証言だが、独立した映像分析による因果の証明ではない。

What we gain is a testable candidate: **can Marinos exploit space created by the opponent's first movement?** A useful question is far more valuable than a vague demand to be more clinical.

## 4. Verdy's ten-man derby is not a normal tactical baseline

Tokyo Verdy lost **0–4 to FC Tokyo on October 17**, after **Itsuki Someno's 18th-minute dismissal**. Manager **Naoki Soma** said his initial intention was to endure some opposition possession, then enter the attacking half quickly after recovering the ball. [Verdy's official post-match report](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/info/2026101708/report)

The red card changed the situation. Soma introduced **Kazunari Ichimi** in an attempt to establish an attacking outlet after regaining the ball. He later shifted to a **5-3-1**, partly to avoid further concessions while playing a man short. When the team tried to push forward, it conceded again. These are the manager's explanations of his decisions, not assumptions drawn from a formation chart.

Therefore, it would be misleading to say Verdy will necessarily defend that way against Marinos. 試合開始時は11対11が想定され、染野は次戦に出場できないと監督が明言している。The October 21 starting lineup, however, was not confirmed on October 19.

Two different problems are possible. A high Verdy press would test Marinos' ability to escape pressure. A deeper defensive block would test their ability to draw defenders out and enter the space left behind. **The opponent's choices define the next tactical puzzle.**

## 5. "Play quickly" and "keep the ball" need not be opposites

Verdy midfielder **Kai Shibato** explained that playing long balls behind the opposition had been an intentional, shared approach. Captain **Koki Morita** spoke about the need to settle possession rather than clear the ball straight back to the other team. Soma stressed fast entry into the opponent's half after recovering possession. [Official player and coach interviews](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/info/2026101708/report)

At first these statements seem to contradict one another: move forward immediately, but slow down enough to retain the ball. Yet **speed and sustainability are different dimensions**. If a forward cannot secure a direct pass and no teammate supports the second action, the team may soon be defending again.

この点では両チームの問題が意外につながる。Marinos must turn possession into useful attacking decisions; Verdy must turn ball recovery into an attack that lasts longer than one clearance. The shared concept is **continuity of options**, not the assertion that both sides play the same football.

## 6. A deliberately small experiment: the first five possessions

**Proposal.** During the October 21 match, record the first **five identifiable possession sequences for each team**. Start a sequence when a team clearly secures the ball. End it when possession changes or play stops. Ambiguous loose-ball situations should be marked as such, rather than silently assigned.

For each sequence, record **starting area; where pressure first arrives; whether the ball progresses to an advanced area; whether a shot is created; and how the sequence ends**. A possession lost in one's own half is not the same as an attack that reaches the box before breaking down. Yet starting positions vary too, so raw counts cannot establish which team is better.

Five sequences are much too few for a statistical performance rating. 放送映像だけでは画面外の選手配置も分からない。But this small exercise teaches something important: it exposes where observation ends and interpretation begins. It is a field guide, not a substitute for professional event data.

## 7. Write the disproof before the match starts

**Hypothesis A:** Marinos may still struggle to create high-quality attempts if they cannot exploit spaces between or behind Verdy's defensive lines. **Hypothesis B:** Verdy may spend long periods defending if their first recovery action cannot lead to a second useful action. どちらも2026年10月19日時点の仮説であり、公式分析の結論ではない。

We should define potential counterexamples in advance. If Marinos create repeated good chances without entering the expected spaces, Hypothesis A requires revision. If Verdy repeatedly create danger through direct balls or set pieces without long spells of retention, Hypothesis B is too narrow. A goal does not automatically disprove a preceding structural weakness, and a missed chance does not automatically confirm one.

Changes in personnel, pitch, red cards and scoreline should be logged as context. そうしないと「試合前の見立てが当たった」という物語を後から好きなように作れてしまう。A hypothesis earns value by risking being wrong.

## 8. Three roles keep the analysis honest

**Skeptic:** Challenge four shortcuts—more possession means domination; low shot accuracy proves an individual weakness; two goalless games establish a chronic problem; Verdy's ten-man shape predicts the next game.

**Researcher:** Recheck J.League's match data and the actual statements by Corica, Soma, Shibato and Morita. A coach's explanation is evidence of what the coach said and believed; it is not independent proof of the cause. シュート地点やxGが未確認なら、数字を補わない。

**Process designer:** Use one observation sheet for recoveries, starting locations, pressure, progression, penetration, shots and contextual changes. After the match, keep observed actions separate from proposed causes. Repeating the same definitions in another fixture would help test whether the categories are usable.

## 9. A scoreless match contains many different stories

Return to the opening puzzle. Marinos had 58% possession against Chiba but took fewer shots and fewer on-target shots. Across two goalless draws, they registered 21 attempts, just three on target. コリカ監督の話を読むと、広がりすぎたサイド、前に出てくる相手中盤、その背後を使うタイミング、悪いピッチが見えてきた。

The goal is the last event in a chain of choices. A 0–0 scoreboard conceals those choices. On October 21, I would watch **who arrives behind the first defender drawn toward the ball**, and **who supports Verdy's first pass after a recovery**.

ゴールが生まれなくても、戦術の問いに答えは出せるかもしれない。The question is not simply who failed to finish. It is **where the next useful option appeared—and whether anyone could take it**.
