# ウォーキング人口5,641万人。では、階段を上った人は別人なのか

## スポーツ統計は、数字より先に「数え方」を読む

“About **56.41 million people walk**.” Impressive. Then the spreadsheet lists another **20.11 million** for stair climbing and **11.51 million** for training. Are we creating extra people whenever someone takes the stairs? Of course not. So **what exactly is this table counting?**

The workbook contains **15 market domains** and **61 activity categories**, plus selected market-size and consumer-friction evidence. It is rich, but its numbers are not always comparable. 本稿ではsource definitionsを確認し、measurementを分解した後、統計の収集をdecision-ready evidenceの管理へ進める。

## 1. 約5,641万人は「数えた人数」ではなく、割合を人口に掛けた概算である

Start with the denominator. スポーツ庁の2025年度調査は、18～79歳の登録モニターを対象とするweb surveyで、有効回答は40,000件。報告書にあるウォーキングのannual participation rate（過去1年間の実施経験率）は**60.3％**だ。[スポーツ庁・一次資料](https://www.mext.go.jp/sports/content/260310-spt_kensport01-000048090_2.pdf)

The spreadsheet translates 60.3% into **約5,641万人** by applying it to a population base of roughly 93.55 million. The arithmetic is coherent, but the figure is an *extrapolation*, not a headcount obtained by interviewing 56 million individuals. 分母の人口原典と年齢範囲は、まだverification required（再検証が必要）である。

A precise-looking number is not necessarily precise evidence. 「5,641万人」という表示の信頼性を決めるのはdigitsではなく、question wording、sample design、population alignmentだ。市場を見る前に、まずdenominatorを確認する。

## 2. 週1回以上の51.7％と、年間ウォーキング60.3％は矛盾しない

The same agency reports **51.7%** for adults aged 20+ who play sports at least once a week. これはweekly frequency of any sport。一方の**60.3%**は18～79歳のannual experience of walking。Age coverage、activity、time windowが異なるため、数字は矛盾しない。[スポーツ庁・報道発表](https://www.mext.go.jp/sports/b_menu/houdou/jsa_00234.html)

If we conclude that walking is more common *every week* because 60.3 exceeds 51.7, we silently replace “once in a year” with “at least weekly.” さらにage groupsまでずれている。Comparable statistics need the same activity definition, reference period, frequency threshold, and population. 

## 3. 61種目の人数を合計しても、61通りの「人間」が現れるわけではない

The spreadsheet lists **61 activity categories**. But one person can walk, climb stairs, and run. Multi-response data means the same participant may appear in several rows. **Adding all 61 population estimates cannot produce a count of unique people.** 一覧は活動の分布であって、61種類の別々の人間の名簿ではない。

Definitions matter. ウォーキングにはcasual walkingや一駅歩きも含まれ、階段昇降には日常行動も含まれる。This is valid for a population survey, but it is not equivalent to paid event attendance or registered athletes. 種目の名前が同じでも、business relevance（事業上の意味）は異なる。

Store the activity definition, multiple-response rule, and estimation method next to every number. 面倒なmetadataに見えるが、これがない表は再利用時に意味が漂流する。Statistics need not just values, but a memory of what was counted.

## 4. 人数ランキングを時間ランキングへ変えると、別の景色になる

The agency also provides frequency and duration for respondents’ top activities. Walking averages **2.6 days/week** and **45.5 minutes/day**, whereas stair climbing averages **2.7 days/week** but **12.0 minutes/day** in those tabulations. [スポーツ庁・種目別時間](https://www.mext.go.jp/sports/content/260310-spt_kensport01-000048090_2.pdf)。The unit changes the story.

A round of golf is listed at **262.5 minutes per day** in the same source. Yet the duration data cover respondents’ *top five activities*, not necessarily every participant included in each annual-experience percentage. Therefore, multiplying population × frequency × duration would mix denominators. この一歩を急ぐと、もっともらしい全国総時間を捏造してしまう。

Two dimensions emerge: reach（参加の広さ）and intensity（活動の深さ）. They may inform awareness and usage opportunities, but neither directly measures willingness to pay. 次の調査に必要なのは、時間を売上と同一視することではなく、その間をつなぐ購買行動の観測だ。

## 5. スポーツ用品1兆7,313億円は、スポーツに使った財布の合計ではない

A commercial report estimates Japan’s **2025 sporting goods market at ¥1,731.31 billion**, up 3.1% year on year. But the metric is *domestic shipment value*, across 17 product categories. It is not total consumer spending on everything sports-related. [矢野経済研究所・原発表](https://www.yano.co.jp/press-release/show/press_id/4078)

The research firm explicitly notes categories where volume declined but value rose due to higher prices. Lifestyle purchases also contribute to outdoor goods and shoes. So **+3.1% in shipment value does not mean +3.1% in participants or in units sold**. Price, quantity, and use case need separate evidence.

The workbook also contains a ¥457.717 billion entry for sports/health instruction activities and private estimates for sports medicine and tourism. These are **different measurement bases**—business revenue, shipment value, and modeled market size. そのまま足しても「スポーツ産業全体」にはならない。なお、個別金額の原表との完全照合は未完了である。

## 6. 「スポーツをしない理由」は、別の調査からしか見えてこない

Population and market-size tables omit friction（参加の障壁）. In a separate NRI survey, respondents named low stamina/fatigue (**34.5%**), cost (**30.4%**), lack of time (**29.6%**), and lack of companions (**23.5%**) as obstacles. [NRI・2026年の調査](https://www.nri.com/en/knowledge/report/20260728_1.html)

But this sample is **3,018 people aged 15–69 in Tokyo and six nearby prefectures**, not a nationwide representative count of all ages. An obstacle rate is not a purchase probability. Multiplying it by the walking-population estimate would mix populations and create an unsupported sales forecast.

The useful question shifts: must a sports business sell more equipment or facilities, or could it reduce scheduling friction, beginner anxiety, and social isolation? これは**事業仮説**であり、survey evidence of willingness to payではない。次に顧客の行動で試すべき問いだ。

## 7. 15市場と61種目は、そもそも同じ分類表ではない

The original dataset has **15 market domains** and **61 participation categories**. They are not competing taxonomies. Markets approximately describe *who sells what*; activities describe *what people did*. 同じExcel上で近くに見えても、分類の目的が違う。

Running crosses apparel, events, tourism, insurance, measurement services, and public infrastructure. One runner is not one market. To map revenue without double counting, record buyer, payer, provider, and transaction layer separately. 競技を起点にした分類は便利だが、財務上のmarket boundaryではない。

The research portal deliberately separates market records, activity estimates, and a source ledger. Its UX goal is not decoration but preventing false equivalence. 次期設計では課題（friction）とpayer（支払者）をbridge dimensionsとして接続し、統計から仮説までの経路を見えるようにする。

## 8. ゼロベースで問い直すと、「大きい市場探し」より先に「誰の困りごとか」が来る

“Capture 1% of a ¥1 trillion market” sounds persuasive. The math works; the customer may not exist. More decimal places cannot repair the missing buyer. Before modeling revenue, identify the user, the payer, and the decision-maker—who may be three different people.

Consider a hypothetical service reducing first-time race anxiety. This is **a proposed experiment, not a validated business**. Investigate the specific anxious moment, current workaround, current spending, willingness to pay, and event-side operating burden. Only then consider scalable revenue. Starting with national runner counts would invert the research sequence.

Statistics remain essential—but later in the causal chain. Once a real problem is observed, quantify how widely it occurs, the alternatives, and customer-acquisition economics. A market model becomes a map for a tested hypothesis rather than a substitute for finding one.

## 9. 次に集めるのは数字そのものではなく、数字の来歴である

The next research cycle has three stages. **Definition audit:** verify denominators, boundaries, transaction layers, and whether figures are observed or forecast. **Usage intensity:** append frequency and duration, keeping the top-five-response limitation. **Commercial linkage:** record user, payer, decision-maker, current alternative, and falsification test. 数字を増やす前に、数字のprovenance（来歴）を固定する。

A useful data record needs id, value, unit, geography, age scope, survey period, sample size, question/market definition, method, source URL, source location, verification date, and status. Separate source fact, derived calculation, author interpretation, and untested proposal. That distinction creates an audit trail, not merely a prettier spreadsheet.

Each research cycle should end with a decision-ready question. Example: can reported activity duration rank total commercial opportunities? **Not yet**—the top-five-activities response rule limits direct comparison. A negative result is useful if it prevents false precision in an investment decision.

## 10. 前提を疑う担当・リサーチ担当・手順担当を分けると、見落としが残りにくい

We reviewed the work through three analytical roles. The **Assumption Challenger** questions whether a large market implies customers and whether multiple responses can be summed. The **Researcher** checks original question wording, market definitions, and sample scope. The **Method Designer** turns those findings into data requirements and decision gates. These are analytical perspectives, **not claims of an independent third-party audit**.

Research → structure → re-research → reusable specification. In the shuhari analogy, **守 (follow)** means respecting sources and definitions, **破 (challenge)** means questioning inherited market categories, and **離 (depart)** means building a new evidence-to-decision system. 守破離は気分の問題ではなく、検証の順番として使う。

## 11. ウォーキング約5,641万人を見て、ようやく「誰が歩いたのか」と聞ける

Return to **約5,641万人**. The estimated reach of walking is notable. But that single figure cannot tell us weekly habits, motivations, frictions, or purchasing behavior. The lesson is not to reject statistics. It is to stop asking a number to answer questions it was never designed to answer.

At first, a completed spreadsheet looks like progress. After checking the sources, the more important question is what each filled cell *actually permits us to claim*. A good evidence repository preserves both findings and limits, so the next researcher can continue without inheriting false certainty.
