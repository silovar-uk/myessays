# 鞘師里保史――「エース」という型を壊し、自分で選ぶ人になる
## English Mix｜Being Chosen → Mission → Identity → Return → Choosing

### Abstract

**Riho Sayashi was chosen before she learned how to choose.**

鞘師里保は12歳でモーニング娘。9期メンバーになった。

She was soon called an **ace** — a key performer who carried strong expectations from fans, media, and the group itself.

しかし、この人物史を「若いエースがプレッシャーを受けて辞めた話」にすると単純すぎる。

She also wanted to meet those expectations.

期待は外から押し付けられただけではない。本人自身も「エース」であろうとした。

Later, she described that feeling as a strong **sense of mission**.

そして卒業後、彼女は自分で「鞘師里保として」の型を作っていた、と振り返った。

This essay follows that change:

**Being Chosen → Expectation → Mission → Identity → Exit → Return → Choosing.**

---

## Prologue｜2019 — Returning did not mean going back

![Riho Sayashi performing again on a Hello Project stage in 2019](https://aramajapan.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/aramajapan.com-former-morning-musume-member-riho-sayashi-performs-for-the-first-time-in-over-3-years-former-morning-musume-member-riho-sayashi-performs-for-the-first-time-in-over-3-years.jpg)

*2019 Hina Fes appearance. Image source: [ARAMA! JAPAN](https://aramajapan.com/news/concert/former-morning-musume-member-riho-sayashi-performs-for-the-first-time-in-over-3-years/96912/). The official set list is also available from [Hello! Project](https://helloproject.com/release/6074/).*

In March 2019, Sayashi appeared at **Hello! Project 20th Anniversary!! Hina Fes 2019**.

モーニング娘。卒業から3年以上が経っていた。

She performed “Only you～One・Two・Three(updated)” with current and former teammates, and also joined “Fantasyが始まる.”

But she did not rejoin Morning Musume.

ハロー！プロジェクトへ復帰したわけでもない。

**Return ≠ Regression.**

戻ることは、元に戻ることではない。

That difference is the key to her story.

---

## 1｜Dance came first

Riho Sayashi was born on May 28, 1998, in Hiroshima.

公式プロフィールによれば、幼少期からアクターズスクール広島でダンスを学んだ。

Hello! Project’s book description says she discovered dance at age six.

つまり、モーニング娘。加入前から身体表現の訓練を積んでいた。

She was not created from zero by the audition.

The audition connected an already developing performer to a much larger system.

[Riho Sayashi Official Site](https://rihosayashi.jp/)

[Hello! Project “17歳の決断”](https://helloproject.com/release/4602/)

---

## 2｜Age 12 — Being Chosen

On January 2, 2011, Sayashi joined Morning Musume as a 9th-generation member.

譜久村聖、生田衣梨奈、鈴木香音と同じ世代だった。

Her first single with the group was “まじですかスカ！”.

She was only twelve.

At the beginning of her career, one verb appears again and again:

**chosen**.

オーディションで選ばれる。

Chosen for important singing parts.

前のポジションに選ばれる。

Eventually, people started calling her an **ace**.

But “ace” was not an official job title.

リーダーのように明文化された役割ではない。

It formed because expectations gathered around one person.

---

## 3｜Ace = concentration of expectation

Official Hello! Project material later described Sayashi as the ace of Morning Musume.'15.

「エース」という呼称そのものは、ファンだけの言葉ではなかった。

Still, being an ace is more than being “the best dancer” or “the best singer.”

It can also mean becoming the place where expectations concentrate.

歌割り。

Center positions.

Dance performance.

The image of a new Morning Musume.

Media attention.

Fan expectations.

When all these things gather around one member, a name can start to feel like a role.

**“Riho Sayashi” became both a person and an expectation.**

[Hello! Project “モーニング娘。鞘師里保全集2011-2015”](https://helloproject.com/release/4547/)

---

## 4｜2012 — One・Two・Three

Morning Musume released “One・Two・Three／The 摩天楼ショー” on July 4, 2012.

この曲は、EDM色の強い楽曲とフォーメーションを前面に出す時代を語る重要な節目になった。

[Hello! Project “One・Two・Three／The 摩天楼ショー”](https://helloproject.com/release/909/)

Sayashi became one visible symbol of this new performance style.

ただし、「鞘師一人がモーニング娘。を復活させた」と書くのは違う。

There were many factors:

道重さゆみのリーダーシップ、田中れいなの歌、若いメンバー、新しいサウンド、フォーメーションダンス、つんく♂の楽曲、メディアでの再評価。

The group changed together.

Sayashi was one of the people who made that change visible through performance.

---

## 5｜Leader and Ace are different roles

Sayumi Michishige and Riho Sayashi were both central, but in different ways.

道重はLeaderであり、Public Faceだった。

She could explain the group, represent it in media, and connect its past to its present.

鞘師はPerformance Centerとして期待された。

**Leadership ≠ Ace.**

The group had more than one kind of center.

だから2014年に道重が卒業すると、構造そのものが変わった。

Sayashi did not simply “replace” Michishige.

They had been doing different jobs.

---

## 6｜Expectation was not only pressure

It is easy to tell a sad story:

**A talented girl was given too much pressure.**

でも、それだけでは本人の能動性が消えてしまう。

In a 2014 Billboard JAPAN interview, Sayashi talked about wanting to move beyond copying admired seniors and finding something that was truly her own.

センター位置そのものより、どこにいてもエースだと思われる存在を目指していた。

She wanted to meet the expectation.

This is why **mission** may be a better word than pressure alone.

Pressure comes from outside.

**Mission can grow inside you.**

[Billboard JAPAN 2014 interview](https://www.billboard-japan.com/special/detail/1036)

---

## 7｜Expectation became mission

In 2020, Sayashi looked back on her years after graduation.

卒業直後にも「何かやらなきゃいけない」という使命感があったと語っている。

She felt that leaving a major place like Morning Musume meant she had to keep proving herself.

Even more importantly, she said she had created her own mold of what **“Riho Sayashi”** should be.

ファンの期待に応えたい気持ちから、自分自身をその型の中に閉じ込めた部分があったという。

This is retrospective testimony.

2015年当時の心理を、そのまま2020年の言葉で確定してはいけない。

But it tells us how she later understood that period.

**Expectation → Mission → Identity.**

期待される自分が、少しずつ「自分とはこういう人間だ」に変わっていく。

[Billboard JAPAN 2020 interview](https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/94094/2)

---

## 8｜2015 — There was not one single reason

On October 29, 2015, Sayashi announced that she would graduate from Morning Musume on December 31.

公式発表では、「自分のダンスの更なるスキルアップに専念したい」という本人の希望が示された。

[Hello! Project official announcement](https://helloproject.com/news/3725/)

But her December interview contained more than one reason.

She talked about wanting to be **more herself**.

小学生から芸能活動をしていたため、「社会勉強しなきゃ」という不安も語った。

She wanted to experience a world outside the group.

表現の幅も広げたかった。

She also said that making the decision was extremely painful.

So there is no need to choose one “true reason.”

**Several reasons can be true at the same time.**

[Billboard JAPAN graduation interview](https://www.billboard-japan.com/special/detail/1443)

---

## 9｜December 31 — Leaving the role

Sayashi joined on January 2, 2011 and graduated on December 31, 2015.

本人は「丸5年」という区切りを意識していた。

At the countdown concert, the official set list places her graduation ceremony after “One･Two･Three(updated)” and before “ENDLESS SKY.”

[Hello! Project COUNTDOWN PARTY 2015](https://helloproject.com/release/4611/)

She was seventeen.

It would be easy to call this escape.

でも本人は、モーニング娘。での5年間には満足しているとも話していた。

She was leaving a place where she had succeeded.

**Choosing to leave a successful role is also a form of choice.**

---

## 10｜Leaving the group did not remove the role from her mind

After graduation, Sayashi studied dance and language, mainly in New York.

2018年11月末にはアップフロントとの専属契約を終了し、ハロー！プロジェクトも卒業した。

At that time, she wrote that she still wanted to sing, dance, and act.

[Hello! Project 2018 announcement](https://helloproject.com/news/9630/)

But freedom did not arrive instantly.

後年の本人によれば、卒業後もしばらく使命感は残っていた。

The official role had ended.

The internal role had not.

**Leaving a position and leaving an identity are different processes.**

---

## 11｜Breaking the mold

Sayashi later said that her self-made mold had also become her style.

つまり、その型は単純な悪者ではない。

It helped her succeed.

But a successful pattern can become too powerful.

周囲も同じものを期待する。

You learn to reproduce it.

Eventually, one successful identity may occupy too much space.

**Sometimes you need to break a mold not because it failed, but because it worked too well.**

---

## 12｜Return ≠ Regression

Now we return to 2019.

もう一度「One・Two・Three」を踊る。

She stood on a Hello! Project stage again.

But she did not become the 17-year-old ace again.

過去を消すのではなく、過去との距離を変えた。

This is what return can mean.

You can revisit a former identity without living inside it again.

---

## 13｜From schedule to choice

Sayashi fully restarted entertainment activities in September 2020.

その後、舞台、ドラマ、ミュージカル、音楽へ活動を広げた。

![Riho Sayashi during the beginning of her solo music chapter](https://www.crank-in.net/img/db/1444071_1200.jpg)

*2021 interview portrait. Source: [Crank-in!](https://www.crank-in.net/interview/84944/1).*

In a 2023 Billboard JAPAN interview, she described an important difference.

グループ時代は、決められたスケジュールへがむしゃらについていくスタンスだった。

During her years away, she had to decide and act for herself.

**The question changed.**

10代：What am I expected to do?

私は何を期待されているのか。

20代：What do I want to choose?

私は何を選びたいのか。

[Billboard JAPAN 2023 interview](https://billboard-japan.com/special/detail/3984)

---

## 14｜She did not abandon dance

![Riho Sayashi performing on her 2022 solo tour](https://ogre.natalie.mu/media/news/music/2022/1231/USQ_8098.jpg?imdensity=1&imwidth=468)

*2022 “UNISON” tour. Source: [Music Natalie](https://natalie.mu/music/news/507388).*

Sayashi did not solve the problem by rejecting her strongest skill.

ダンスを捨てたわけではない。

She still sings and dances at a high level.

But she also acts, writes, and explores different forms of performance.

**Freedom did not mean becoming the opposite of her old self.**

It meant not allowing one strength to define the whole person.

In 2024, she told ORICON that she once felt she had to fix a clear image of herself.

今はその考えを外し、「選択肢は自由にある」と思えるようになったという。

[ORICON NEWS 2024 interview](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2351098/full/)

---

## 15｜OWN — choosing again

Sayashi made her major debut from avex trax in 2025.

2026年にはコンセプトEP『OWN』を発表した。

Avex describes the work through the idea **“Own your mistakes.”**

迷い、衝動、葛藤、不完全さを抱えながら、「自分自身を選び直していく過程」を描く作品だという。

[avex “OWN” announcement](https://avexnet.jp/news/1033898)

![Riho Sayashi in her 2026 solo-activity era](https://e.usen.com/image/sayashiriho_2602.jpg)

*2026 artist image. Source: [USEN encore](https://e.usen.com/news/news-event/5-11270.html).*

We should not pretend that this was her plan from age twelve.

2011年からずっと同じテーマを考えていた、と遡及してはいけない。

But from today’s point of view, the contrast is striking.

At twelve, her story began with being chosen.

At twenty-eight, she is making art about what to choose as her own.

---

## People × Music × System × Society

### People — Mission becomes identity

鞘師は成長期を「エース」という強い期待の中で過ごした。

Later, she understood that expectation as something she had also internalized.

### Music — A new Morning Musume became visible through the body

「One・Two・Three」以降、ダンスとフォーメーションはグループ変化の重要な視覚言語になった。

Sayashi became one important performer inside that change, not its single cause.

### System — Ace is an unofficial role

「エース」は正式な役職ではない。

But repeated selection, center positions, media language, and fan expectation can create a very real role.

### Society — Growing up in public

Twelve to seventeen is a period when identity normally changes quickly.

アイドルの場合、その更新が常に他者の視線の中で進む。

Changing yourself can become public work.

---

## Epilogue｜From being chosen to choosing

Riho Sayashi was not simply a victim of pressure.

そして、「全部乗り越えた成功者」とまとめる必要もない。

She was expected to perform.

期待へ応えた。

Expectation became mission.

使命感は自己像になった。

She left the role.

でも役割はすぐには心から消えなかった。

She took time.

戻ってきた。

But she did not go backward.

**She was chosen before she learned how to choose.**

まず、選ばれる側として始まった。

**Expectation became mission. Mission became identity.**

期待は使命感になり、使命感は自己像になった。

**Returning did not mean going back.**

戻ることは、元に戻ることではなかった。

**She moved from being chosen to choosing.**

そして、自分で選ぶ人へ移っていった。

The most interesting question is not only, “How great was Riho Sayashi as an ace?”

もっと面白い問いは、こちらだ。

**What does an ace role give a person — and what does it demand from them?**

エースという役割は、人に何を与え、何を要求するのか。

- **break the mold** — 型を壊す

- **return without going backward** — 元に戻らず帰る
- **make your own choice** — 自分で選ぶ
- **redefine yourself** — 自分自身を再定義する
