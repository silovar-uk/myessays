# 草加は、せんべいだけじゃない――なぜレザーの街になったのか
## Water, Tokyo, and the network behind SOKA LEATHER

草加と聞いて、the first thing that comes to mind is probably Soka senbei.

自分もそうだった。だから「草加はレザーでも有名らしい」と聞いたとき、it felt surprisingly unfamiliar.

But once I looked into it, this was not simply a story about a few leather workshops. In 2024, Soka was recognized by the Tanners’ Council of Japan as one of Japan’s four major leather-producing areas, alongside Himeji/Tatsuno, Tokyo/Sumida, and Wakayama.

さらに面白いのは、草加には「これ一種類だけが圧倒的に強い」というより、**many kinds of leather and many stages of production are concentrated in the same area**という特徴があることだった。

So why did Soka become a leather town?

## 1. 始まりは「革に向いた街」だったこと

草加の皮革産業の起点は1935年（昭和10年）とされる。According to Soka City, Nitto Hikaku opened a factory there, and leather-related companies began moving in from areas such as Mikawashima in Tokyo.

Two conditions mattered: **abundant groundwater** and **proximity to Tokyo**.

皮を革に変える「なめし」では大量の水を使う。That makes stable access to water a basic industrial advantage.

At the same time, Soka was close enough to Tokyo to stay connected to suppliers, craftspeople, clients, and markets, while offering more room for production facilities.

つまり草加には、

- plenty of water
- access to Tokyo
- space for factories

という条件が重なっていた。

Industrial clusters do not always begin with a branding strategy. **Companies gather where work is easier to sustain, and the cluster itself later becomes a regional advantage.** 草加レザーは、その流れをかなり分かりやすく見せてくれる。

## 2. 戦後、草加は一気に産地になった

After wartime controls were lifted, Soka’s leather industry expanded as imports of raw hides increased.

草加市の年表によると、1953年には国内皮革産業の約10％のシェアを占め、1959年には生産量が全国4位。By the Showa 40s, the number of tanning businesses had reached roughly 60.

ここで大事なのは、Soka did not suddenly become important in 2024.

The production base had existed for decades. What happened more recently was a **reframing and re-articulation of the region’s value** after years of structural change and downturns.

## 3. 草加の強みは「何の革か」より「何でもいる」

Other major leather regions are easier to explain by specialty.

- Himeji / Tatsuno: especially strong in cow leather and Japan’s largest tanning concentration
- Tokyo / Sumida: well known for pigskin
- Wakayama: known for sheep leather

草加は少し違う。Its tanners handle cow, pig, sheep, goat, deer, kangaroo, reptiles and other materials.

さらに、raw hide, tanning, dyeing, processing, cutting, sewing, and finished goods can be connected within Soka and its surrounding area.

SOKA LEATHER explains that leather production can involve more than 100 detailed processes. 草加では、その多くを地域の事業者同士でつなげられる。

In other words, the competitive advantage is not one superstar craftsperson. It is **the network of specialists itself**.

産業クラスターとして見ると分かりやすい。If one project needs an unusual finish, a small production lot, or a rare material, another nearby specialist may be able to help.

**Diversity becomes development capacity.**

## 4. ずっと順風満帆だったわけではない

Soka’s leather industry grew strongly in the 1960s and 1970s, but later faced environmental regulation, the bursting of Japan’s bubble economy, and shifts in demand.

草加市の年表では、1997年の出荷額は前年比で約3分の1まで落ち込んだとされる。

This matters when we think about traditional local industries.

「伝統産業」と聞くと、we often imagine a craft surviving because it never changed. In reality, many industries survive **because they keep changing**.

草加もそうだった。

In 2002, the Soka Leather Craftsmen Association was formed. In 2018, the region began promoting its leather industry nationally under the name **SOKA LEATHER**.

ここで産地は、ただ革を作る場所から、**a place that also explains what makes its system valuable**へ変わっていく。

## 5. 「廃棄しない」まで含めてレザーを再編集する

What stood out in recent Soka projects was that the story is not only about luxury leather. The region is also rethinking what counts as usable material.

代表例が **U-TaaaN PROJECT by SOKA LEATHER**。

The project receives hides from deer and other wild animals captured in different parts of Japan, tans them in Soka, turns them into leather or finished goods, and sends the added value back to the original region.

捨てられやすかった原皮を、regional resourcesとして使い直す発想や。

Soka has also developed a form of leather using rice-bran-derived oil as a major tanning agent, known as **米ぬかなめし**.

And in 2022, **THE LEATHER SCRAP KIMONO**, designed by Tomoe Shinohara and produced with the participation of Soka leather craftspeople, won two categories at the 101st New York ADC Awards. The piece used edge portions of Ezo deer leather that would normally be difficult to use, with several Soka companies passing the material from one process to the next.

ここに草加らしさが凝縮されている。

**Many materials. Many processes. That makes it easier to combine craft with design, sustainability, and regional problem-solving.**

## 6. 2024年の「4大産地」認定は、ゴールというより再定義

In 2024, Soka was officially recognized by the Tanners’ Council of Japan as one of the country’s four major leather-producing areas.

草加市が挙げる理由は、**the wide variety of leathers handled in the region** and **the integrated local production system**.

So the recognition was not simply a ranking of production volume.

草加は、Himeji/Tatsunoのような古い産地と比べれば歴史は短い。But being a later-developed cluster may have helped it become a place where different materials, techniques, and specialists coexist.

地域ブランドとして考えると、これはかなり面白い。

Instead of saying, “We are the town of one specific leather,” Soka can almost say:

**“If it is leather-related, someone around here can probably do it.”**

That is a network-based advantage.

## 7. 草加せんべいの裏に、もう一つの草加がある

Because the image of “Soka = senbei” is so strong, the leather story feels unexpected at first.

でも調べ終わるころには、it no longer felt like a random hidden specialty.

There was water.

東京に近かった。

Companies and craftspeople gathered.

一種類に特化しすぎず、多様な革と工程が残った。

And when the industry became harder to sustain, the region rebuilt its meaning through craft networks, branding, design, and environmental initiatives.

街の名物は、tourism posters are not the only place where local identity lives.

**If you ask why an industry exists in a particular town, you start seeing geography, metropolitan connections, regulation, economic cycles, technology, and networks.**

草加レザーは、革そのものだけでなく、how a local industry forms, survives, and reinvents itselfを学ぶ題材として面白かった。
