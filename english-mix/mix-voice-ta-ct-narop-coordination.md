# There Is No “Falsetto Muscle.” でも、筋肉だけ見てもミックスボイスは分からない

“Chest voice is TA, falsetto is CT.” ボイトレを調べると、この整理は驚くほどよく出てくる。Ryoji Tanaka’s video, “ナロップと声帯の筋肉〜ミックスボイスの正体〜,” attacks that shortcut with a strong claim: there is no such thing as a dedicated “falsetto muscle.” As an opening move, this is useful. 少なくともCT（cricothyroid）をfalsetto-only switchとして理解するのは正確ではない。

But the interesting part begins after that simplification is broken. 「ではTAとCTだけを正しく理解すればmix voiceは説明できるのか」と問うと、the answer becomes more complicated. Pitch, register, glottal closure, airflow pressure, and vocal-tract shape interact at the same time. ミックスボイスはtwo-muscle tug-of-warというより、multiple variablesを同時に安定させる問題へ移っていく。

## “Inside / outside” is useful, but it is not the anatomical intrinsic / extrinsic distinction

The video describes TA as “the muscle inside the vocal fold” and CT as “the muscle outside.” 位置関係のイメージとしては便利だが、anatomical classificationでは注意が要る。Both CT and TA are generally classified as intrinsic laryngeal muscles. CT is “outside” only in the practical sense that it is not inside the vocal-fold body; it sits on the anterolateral larynx between the cricoid and thyroid cartilages.

When CT contracts, it changes the relationship between those cartilages and tends to lengthen and tense the vocal folds. その結果、fundamental frequency F0――知覚されるpitch――を上げる方向に働く。TA, meanwhile, affects vocal-fold thickness, length, stiffness, and adduction, changing vocal weight and contact behavior. ここまでは動画の「CTは音程、TAは声の強さ」にかなり近い。

But one level deeper, TA is not simply a “volume knob.” 条件によってTA activationはF0を上げも下げもでき、CT、other adductors、subglottal pressureとの組み合わせで結果が変わる。A vocal fold is not a passive guitar string; it is living tissue whose own muscle can change thickness and stiffness. 二つの筋肉にはdifferent jobsがあるが、one muscle = one acoustic resultではない。

[NCBI Bookshelf: Anatomy, Head and Neck: Larynx Muscles](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK545231/) / [Titze, 1993: Current topics in voice production mechanisms](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8517148/)

Once the “chest muscle vs falsetto muscle” picture is dropped, a different model appears: multiple actuators modifying the same vibrating tissue from different directions. ここで初めて、registerをmuscle nameから切り離して考えられる。

---

## Pitch and register live on different coordinates

The same pitch can be produced in chest-like and falsetto-like phonation. 感覚的にも分かるし、research上も重要な事実である。Roubeau and colleagues organized vocal-fold vibration into laryngeal mechanisms M0–M3 and showed a frequency overlap between the two major mechanisms, M1 and M2. Roughly speaking, M1 strongly corresponds to chest/modal production and M2 to head/falsetto production. つまりsame pitch does not guarantee the same vibratory mechanism.

This is why the video’s rejection of “CT = falsetto muscle” matters. CT is important for creating higher F0, but CT activation does not automatically flip the voice into falsetto. 声区のtransitionでは、TA activity, fold contact, vibrating mass, glottal geometry, and pressure can change together.

Still, we should be careful with a stronger rule such as “at the same pitch CT always works harder in chest voice.” A 2014 preliminary EMG study of seven singers found that pitch level had a larger effect than register label, and above 300 Hz the productions were CT-dominant or near-equal in CT/TA activity regardless of register. サンプルは小さいのでこれもabsolute lawではないが、少なくともregister labelだけからmuscle activityを一意に逆算できない。

[Roubeau, Henrich & Castellengo, 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18538982/) / [Kochis-Jennings et al., 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24856144/)

The value of learning muscle names is therefore not to build a direct control chart: “activate this and get that sound.” むしろ、same note has multiple possible coordinations, and those coordinations can shift discontinuously. Pitch is one coordinate; register is another.

## “Strong voice = hard TA” is also incomplete; intensity includes pressure, closure, and the tract

The video explains that when TA becomes “muki-muki,” the folds get firmer and the voice becomes stronger. 教育モデルとしては方向をつかみやすい。But voice science does not hand loudness to TA alone. Titze’s review treats F0 as coordinated among CT, TA, and lung pressure, while vocal intensity depends strongly on pressure. Animal-model work also demonstrates motor equivalence: the same F0 and SPL can be achieved through more than one pattern of intrinsic-laryngeal-muscle activation.

It helps to separate phonation into four controllable dimensions.

- **Pitch**: CT is central, with TA and pressure also affecting F0.
- **Contact / vocal weight**: TA plus adductors such as LCA/IA shape closure and contact.
- **Air**: subglottal pressure and airflow affect onset, intensity, and stability.
- **Vocal tract**: vowels, tongue, pharynx, and epilaryngeal geometry alter resonance and acoustic loading on the folds.

[Chhetri et al., 2016: Interactions of subglottal pressure and neuromuscular activation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26971707/)

Once you separate these knobs, shortcuts such as “high notes fail because CT is weak” or “soft voice means TA needs strengthening” become less convincing. 問題はtoo much closure, too much flow, an unfavorable vowel, or an abrupt register transitionかもしれない。Voice becomes easier to diagnose as a problem of conditions, not a raw muscle-capacity test.

## Mix is not a “third muscle,” but it is not imaginary either

So what is mixed voice? ここはresearch languageとstudio languageが最も滑りやすい場所である。If we say “50% chest + 50% head,” it sounds like two separate voices are being blended on a mixer. But there is no dedicated “M1.5 muscle.” 一方で、だからmixは存在しない、と片づけるのも早い。

A 2023 study from Kyushu University researchers compared chest, mix, and falsetto in 12 healthy singers using aerodynamic, acoustic, and EGG measures, with high-speed imaging in a subset. Mix showed features that often sat between chest and falsetto but were not reducible to a trivial average. Another line of work argues that stable mixed registration depends not only on muscle activation, but also on glottal geometry, transglottal pressure, tissue stiffness, and vocal-tract conditions.

[Lee et al., 2023: Differences Among Mixed, Chest, and Falsetto Registers](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33518476/) / [Titze, 2014: Bi-stable vocal fold adduction](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25235006/)

So instead of defining mix as one organ or one fixed TA/CT ratio, it is more useful to treat it as a coordination that keeps timbre, contact, pressure, and resonance from collapsing around a register boundary. ミックスはplaceというより、multiple constraintsの間を安定して通り抜けるskillに近い。

## Narop connects to existing science in places, but that is not the same as validating “Narop itself”

Tanaka defines Narop as a term built from Narrow + Pointing, describing a state where the space just above the vocal folds, especially around the AES, is narrowed while the oral cavity remains comparatively open. 動画では「うがいで水をためる口」をcueにして、closure and vocal strengthを調整する。His own explanation also places supraglottal resistance and the pressure difference across the glottis at the center of the model.

There is established voice science nearby. Titze’s work on semi-occluded vocal tract exercises（SOVT）, including straw phonation, explains how increased supraglottal pressure and source–tract interaction can improve impedance matching and potentially improve vocal economy. The paper also discusses how vocal-fold adduction plus epilarynx-tube narrowing may produce related benefits. ASHA similarly explains that straw phonation raises pressure above the folds and can reduce collision forces.

[Titze, 2006: Voice Training and Therapy With a Semi-Occluded Vocal Tract](https://pubs.asha.org/doi/10.1044/1092-4388%282006/035%29) / [ASHA: Voice Disorders — SOVT Exercises](https://www.asha.org/practice-portal/clinical-topics/voice-disorders/) / [田中りょうじ先生「ナロップ理論」](https://note.com/tanakaryoji/n/n35599ea22ec1)

But a line still matters here. The existence of SOVT and epilaryngeal-narrowing research does not directly prove that “the gargle posture activates TA by this amount,” that “epiglottis manipulation creates ideal closure,” or that Narop is the decisive mechanism of mixed voice. ナロップは現時点ではTanaka’s pedagogical/practical modelであり、how closely it maps onto measured physiology needs claim-by-claim verification.

That distinction does not make Narop useless. A vocal cue is not an anatomical description; it is an interface that helps a body reproduce a movement. 科学っぽい言葉を多く使うことより、what is manipulated, what changes, and what remains unverifiedを分けられるほうが強い。

---

## Read “swallowing means no strength training” as “singing is not only about strength”

The video argues that swallowing closes the larynx with very large force, so singers do not need special “muscle training”; what matters is fine control. この方向性――singingを単純なhypertrophy contestにしない――は妥当である。But swallowing and singing are different tasks with different goals and neural control, so “strong closure during swallowing = all singing capacity is already sufficient” is a larger claim than the evidence supports.

Clinical voice practice uses Vocal Function Exercises and SOVT to improve efficiency, endurance, and coordination without simply pushing harder. In a randomized controlled trial, both flow-resistant-tube SOVT and VFE improved Voice Handicap Index outcomes relative to a no-treatment group in people with dysphonia. ここで起きるadaptationをmuscle hypertrophyと呼ぶ必要はない。Task-specific coordination, sensory calibration, economy, and endurance are the more useful frame.

[Kapsner-Smith et al., 2015](https://pubs.asha.org/doi/10.1044/2015_JSLHR-S-13-0231)

“Strength or relaxation” is another false binary. 歌唱で欲しいのは、necessary force at the necessary time, without extra force, reproduced on demand. If the axis shifts from strength to controllability, the video’s emphasis on “micro-adjustment” becomes stronger, not weaker.

## Proposal: in practice, log the variable you changed and the result that changed

To bring this into practice, it is more useful to change one observable variable at a time than to try to contract TA or CT directly. これは動画のmethodを置き換える提案ではなく、cause and effectを混同しないための補助線である。

- At the same pitch, move from soft → medium → moderately strong and record where breathiness, flipping, compression, or timbre changes.
- Glide through the transition at an easy volume. Treat the task as continuous pitch tracking, not “activate CT.”
- If the boundary is unstable, pass through it once with straw phonation or lip trill, then return to a vowel.
- Change vowels and see whether the same pitch and similar loudness become easier or harder. If they do, the vocal tract is part of the problem.
- Do not interpret pain, strong discomfort, or persistent hoarseness as proof that training is working. If symptoms persist, consult an ENT or qualified voice professional.

The point of this log is to turn “I hit the note once” into reproducible conditions. ボイトレのprogressは、mysterious correct muscleを発見することより、the number of conditions under which you can recreate the resultを増やすことなのかもしれない。

---

## A good voice theory is not a photograph of the throat; it is a control panel

Return to the opening question: is there a “falsetto muscle”? Calling CT that is too crude. CT is crucial for pitch, and TA strongly affects thickness, stiffness, and contact. しかしregisterはtwo muscles aloneでは決まらず、closure, pressure, vocal tract, vowel, and loudnessを含むsystem stateとして現れる。

So the biggest thing to carry away from the video is not the muscle names themselves. 「地声＝TA、裏声＝CT」というone-to-one mappingを疑い、voiceをcoordinationとして見る姿勢である。From there, Narop no longer has to be judged only as “correct anatomy” or “incorrect anatomy.” We can separate what connects to established physics, what works as a teaching cue, and what remains unverified.

If mixed voice needs one word, “coordination” is better than “third muscle.” ただしそのcoordinationはTAとCTだけのtwo-party meetingでもない。It is a learned path through constraints involving muscle, pressure, vibration, and resonance. Once you see it that way, voice training changes from a quiz about naming the right muscle into an experiment about finding reproducible conditions.
