# プロレスは、相手を壊さないからfightできる――Shared Frameの成立条件

Trust in the ring、スタジアムのbanter、日常のjokesはどうつながるか

Imagine a stranger suddenly rushing toward you and striking your neck with an outstretched arm. 普通なら事件である。But inside a wrestling ring, it becomes a lariat, and the crowd may roar. The physical action seems similar; its meaning is radically different.

The difference is not just the location. Wrestlers share technical knowledge, expectations, roles, and a responsibility to protect one another's bodies. だから試合は成立する。But the risk is real precisely because a performer entrusts their body to another person.

This strange mechanism exists outside the ring, too. A sharp joke between close friends. Rival supporters teasing one another. 強い言葉が、時には関係を壊すのでなく、relationshipそのものを作る。What turns an apparent attack into a shared performance?

**Information date: October 8, 2026.** 本稿はinterviews、wrestling scholarship、communication theory、football fan studies、Jリーグのofficial rulesを参照した。Everyday applications are the author's interpretation, not universal empirical findings.

## 1. プロレスがdangerousなのは、本当に身体を預けるからだ

Calling wrestling "scripted, therefore safe" gets the problem backward. WWE explicitly warns people not to imitate its performers. They train for years, yet injury is a real possibility. If the danger were entirely fictional, the warning would make little sense.

Former champion Bret Hart has described how much a performer depends on an opponent's trust when executing high-risk moves. Sociological research likewise describes wrestling as coordinated labor: precise movements, timing, and care for the person across the ring. 観客には敵対が見えるが、inside the performance there is cooperation.

**The power to defeat someone on stage depends on the ability to bring them through the performance.** 敵対の表現がcollaborationによって成立する。この逆説が出発点になる。

Sources: [WWE・Do Not Try This at Home](https://www.wwe.com/inside/overtheropes/pleasedonottrythisathome/announcement) ／ [Bret Hart interview](https://www.wrestlezone.com/news/1271451-bret-hart-on-never-injuring-an-opponent-i-take-a-lot-of-pride) ／ [Smith, *Passion Work*](https://www.asanet.org/wp-content/uploads/savvy/documents/spq/Smith_2008_SPQ_Snap.pdf)

## 2. A playful nip is not an attack――動作ではなくmeaningが違う

Gregory Bateson explored a deceptively simple problem in *A Theory of Play and Fantasy* (1955). When animals play-fight, a playful nip resembles a bite. しかし、本気で傷つけようとするbiteと同じ敵意を意味するわけではない。The animals exchange an additional message: **"This is play."**

This is metacommunication: a message about how to interpret other messages. 「やるなあ」という一言も、grinning with a friendとshouting at a strangerでは違う。The dictionary meaning of a sentence cannot tell us what kind of interaction is taking place.

Crucially, a play frame cannot exist only inside the speaker's mind. 受け手もそのframeを認識していなければ、a joke may arrive as a threat. Meaning is not the sole property of the person who speaks.

Sources: [Bateson text](https://sites.google.com/view/self-society/home/thinkers/gregory-bateson/play-and-fantasy) ／ [Wiley, Metacommunication](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/9781118766804.wbiect232)

## 3. Shared trust is not the same as freedom to refuse

It is tempting to conclude: "If both people understand, anything goes." けれど、wrestling research complicates that story. A 2023 study of concussion and professional wrestling reported that some performers felt pressure when declining risky moves: refusal could be interpreted as questioning another performer's competence or trustworthiness.

Trust and equal bargaining power are different things. 相手を尊重する文化が、逆に「断りにくさ」を生むこともある。Even careful coordination cannot erase the risk of injury.

The lesson to take outside the ring is not "strong aggression is fine when people know the rules." It is that **the stronger the performance of conflict, the more carefully we must protect boundaries and the right to refuse.**

Source: [*Concussion in professional wrestling*](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/2159676X.2023.2175898)

## 4. One sentence, three scenes――words alone cannot carry context

A thought experiment. Do not actually provoke anyone. Use the same sentence in three settings: **「次は絶対に負けへんからな」** — "You won't beat me next time."

Say it to a longtime gaming friend with a smile. Say it across a football rivalry after the final whistle. Say it to a colleague you barely know, immediately after they make a mistake at work. 同じwordsでも、challenge、banter、hostilityのように意味が変わる。

What changed? Relationship, setting, purpose, timing, tone, and whether the other person can respond. The meaning may even shift midway through the exchange. 一度"it's a joke"と宣言しても、frameは永久に固定されない。

Communication skill is not merely choosing better words. It also means checking **whether both people are still participating in the same kind of interaction.**

## 5. Stadium banter creates rivals, but not everyone agreed to the role

Football terraces have their own rituals: chanting, teasing, provocation, and triumphant celebration. A 2026 ethnographic account of English fans describes banter and chants as ways of performing group identity. スタジアムは単なるordinary conversationの場所ではなく、a space shaped by competition.

But the ring and the stands are not identical. Wrestlers are direct participants in a performance. Among spectators are strangers, children, first-time visitors, and people who do not wish to trade insults. 試合に参加するclubと、すべての煽りを受け入れるindividual fanは同義ではない。

J.League rules prohibit discriminatory or insulting conduct and violence. That limit matters because "everyone knows how this works" is not always true. **A rival is not a person whose dignity becomes optional.** 熱い対立は、その境界を守ってこそ持続する。

Sources: [Pearson, *An Ethnography of English Football Fans*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/an-ethnography-of-english-football-fans/identity-and-expression/BFCB30D7187B90DE041F10733B47A091) ／ [J.League match rules](https://www.jleague.jp/manners_management/)

## 6. "I was only joking" does not settle what happened

In a 2022 study, researchers interviewed 18 professional footballers about the line between banter and bullying. Their responses revealed differences in perception and the powerful influence of team culture. 冗談が当たり前の環境では、harmful behaviorもnormalに見えやすい。

Even laughter does not provide complete proof of consent. Someone may laugh to avoid disrupting the group, losing status, or embarrassing another person. 別のqualitative study found that legitimized banter could also be associated with anxiety and stress among players.

"I meant it as a joke" may describe an intention. It does not invalidate the other person's experience. **A successful joke is not something the speaker can certify alone.**

Sources: [Frontiers study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.838053/full) ／ [Hickey & Roderick](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/16184742.2022.2124299)

## 7. Instead of "read the room," examine four conditions

Should we eliminate teasing, rivalry, and all strong expressions? そうは思わない。The more useful question is how a shared interaction is designed and maintained. 以下はresearchを踏まえた筆者のproposalであり、official validated frameworkではない。

- **Frame / 文脈**：Is this play, a match, a debate, or a real argument? Do participants agree?
- **Relationship / 関係**：Can both sides respond? Are power, numbers, or status unequal?
- **Boundary / 境界**：Is the challenge about performance, or does it target identity, dignity, or safety?
- **Repair / 修復**：Can someone say stop, rephrase, apologize, or walk away without punishment?

For stadium operations, this suggests communicating not only prohibited conduct, but also the difference between passionate support and personal attack. 日常会話では、相手が笑わなくなったらやめる、というsmall responseから始められる。The goal is not weaker language; it is safer participation.

## 8. The ring's hardest lesson: leave your rival able to return

Return to the lariat. From the outside, it is an image of one person knocking down another. But after looking at the training, trust, and risk, a second scene becomes visible: a performer must protect the conditions that let the opponent continue. そしてそのconditionsは、pressureやinjuryによって崩れうる。

Football provocation and personal jokes are not the same as physical wrestling. Their risk, consent, and escape routes differ. ただし、one question can travel across them: **After this exchange, can the other person still choose to face me again?**

We can play enemies. We can shout that we want to win. We can enjoy sharp and funny rivalry. でも相手そのものを壊してしまえば、there will be nobody left to compete with.

The ring does not simply teach us to be nice. It suggests a tougher ethic: **if you want to fight fiercely, you must preserve the other person's ability to fight back.** だからこそ、プロレスはただの「仲良しの演技」では終わらない。

### Evidence, interpretation, and proposal

- **Evidence**: Wrestling requires training, involves injury risk, and depends on cooperation; research also documents pressure around risky moves. Bateson theorized the "this is play" meta-message. Banter can blur into bullying; J.League prohibits discriminatory or insulting conduct.
- **Interpretation**: Use the paradox of performed conflict and underlying cooperation as a lens for everyday jokes and fan rivalry.
- **Proposal**: Check Frame, Relationship, Boundary, and Repair. This four-part scheme is an editorial synthesis, not an established empirical instrument.
