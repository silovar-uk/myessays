# BEYOOOOONDS史――複雑だからこそ強い。組み合わせは、どう作品になったのか
## How complexity became a production engine

### 要旨

BEYOOOOONDS is one of the harder Hello! Project groups to explain in one sentence.

一つのグループの中にCHICA#TETSUと雨ノ森 川海があり、後にはSeasoningSもある。Songs can begin with dialogue. Members may act, play piano, beatbox, dance as specialists, or become characters inside a story.

Normally, this much structure creates a **clarity problem**.

BEYOOOOONDS did not solve that problem by simplifying itself.

Instead, it turned complexity into optionality.

> **The identity of BEYOOOOONDS is not one fixed character. It is the ability to recombine people, units, roles and skills into a new work.**

I call this survival model **`combinatorial_identity_survives`**.

---

## 1. 2018――the whole was built from smaller modules

2018年10月19日、公式はCHICA#TETSUと雨ノ森 川海という二つのユニットを発表した。At the same time, it announced that audition winners would be added to create a larger group called BEYOOOOONDS.

Official: https://helloproject.com/news/9386/

This is important structurally.

BEYOOOOONDS was not simply “twelve girls, one group name.”

It was closer to:

**subunit → subunit → additional specialists → combined group**

という architecture で始まった。

In December 2018, Miyo Hirai, Honoka Kobayashi and Utano Satoyoshi joined through the “ONLY YOU” audition. Their official profiles already highlighted specific capabilities: dance, piano and choreography/dance.

Official: https://helloproject.com/news/9616/

At first, the three did not even have a subunit name.

The 1st album later turned that awkward organizational fact into a song title: **“We Need a Name!”**

Official: https://helloproject.com/beyooooonds/release/6157/

普通なら「未完成な組織設計」と見える部分まで、BEYOOOOONDSではコンテンツになる。

---

## 2. 「眼鏡の男の子」――a song that starts as theater

BEYOOOOONDS made its major debut on August 7, 2019 with “眼鏡の男の子 / ニッポンノD・N・A！ / Go Waist.”

Official: https://helloproject.com/beyooooonds/release/6039/

“眼鏡の男の子” is the clearest example of the group’s grammar.

The song does not simply begin with an intro.

It begins with a skit.

Members become characters. Dialogue establishes relationships. A member can be a narrator, a friend, or the “boy with glasses,” and the scene flows into the song.

A Real Sound report on their first one-man live specifically described the heavy theatrical element and the way “眼鏡の男の子” emerged from a skit.

Real Sound: https://realsound.jp/2019/12/post-458194.html

For a large idol group, the classic design question is how to distribute singing lines.

BEYOOOOONDS expanded the question:

> **Don’t just distribute lines. Distribute roles.**

人数が多いことが burden ではなく、casting options になる。

---

## 3. More members did not just mean more faces――it meant more functions

BEYOOOOONDS is especially interesting because a “special skill” often becomes an actual production resource.

Momohime Kiyono’s human beatbox was used in the recording of “眼鏡の男の子.” Honoka Kobayashi’s piano appears in recordings and live performances.

Real Sound on Kiyono: https://realsound.jp/2020/05/post-546959_2.html

Real Sound on Kobayashi: https://realsound.jp/2020/05/post-552300_2.html

In a 2021 two-man live, Kobayashi’s piano was paired with dance by Hirai and Satoyoshi before the group moved into “アツイ！”.

Natalie: https://natalie.mu/music/news/445114

So the group’s resource pool is not just:

- singer A
- singer B
- singer C

It also includes:

- acting
- piano
- human beatbox
- choreography
- classical / contemporary dance
- character performance

つまり、special skills are not decorative profile data. They are **modules that can be called by a song or live concept**.

That is why asking only “Who is the ace?” misses a large part of BEYOOOOONDS.

Different works activate different strengths.

---

## 4. 『BEYOOOOOND1St』――an instruction manual for complexity

The 2019 album 『BEYOOOOOND1St』 almost functions like a map of the organization.

There are full-group songs. CHICA#TETSU has “高輪ゲートウェイ駅ができる頃には.” 雨ノ森 川海 has “そこらのやつとは同じにされたくない.” Hirai, Kobayashi and Satoyoshi have “We Need a Name!”

Official: https://helloproject.com/beyooooonds/release/6157/

Members themselves also explained that the album incorporated strengths such as piano and beatbox.

ORICON: https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2149391/full/

Most brands create coherence by reducing difference.

BEYOOOOONDS did the opposite.

> **Difference itself became the coherence.**

This is **heterogeneity as identity**.

The group then won the Best New Artist Award at the 61st Japan Record Awards in December 2019.

Natalie: https://natalie.mu/music/news/361678

That matters because the strange structure was not merely a niche joke. It achieved mainstream institutional recognition too.

---

## 5. 2020–21――modularity became useful under stress

During the COVID-19 period, many groups had to strip performances down.

BEYOOOOONDS already had usable modules.

For home / remote versions of songs such as “眼鏡の男の子” and “都営大江戸線の六本木駅で抱きしめて,” Kobayashi’s piano, Kiyono’s beatbox and member vocals could form a new arrangement without simply reproducing the standard backing track.

Real Sound: https://realsound.jp/2020/05/post-546959_3.html

これは偶然ではない。

The group had spent years connecting individual abilities to actual works.

> **Complex organizations are not necessarily fragile. If the parts are modular, complexity can create resilience.**

---

## 6. SeasoningS――the “unassigned three” became a named function

Hirai, Kobayashi and Satoyoshi were later organized as SeasoningS.

The current official profile describes BEYOOOOONDS as the combined group of CHICA#TETSU, 雨ノ森 川海 and SeasoningS.

Official: https://helloproject.com/beyooooonds/

The name is revealing.

“Seasoning” means something that adds flavor, and the official explanation says the members’ skills should add color and flavor to performances.

つまり、最初は「どちらのユニットにも入っていない3人」だった存在が、最終的には**特技で全体へ味を加える機能**そのものになった。

This is a recurring BEYOOOOONDS move:

**organizational ambiguity → artistic function**

---

## 7. 2022――complexity scaled to Budokan

BEYOOOOONDS held its first Nippon Budokan concert on April 25, 2022.

Official: https://helloproject.com/news/14358/

As an idol group becomes bigger, there is often pressure to simplify the live product into large formations and clean spectacle.

BEYOOOOONDS did not abandon the layers.

Subunit songs, theatrical elements, full-group songs and special skills remained part of the live identity.

This shows something important:

> **Complexity was not a temporary debut gimmick. It survived scale.**

成功すると単純化したくなる。でもBEYOOOOONDSは、成功後も「多すぎる要素」を production assets として持ち続けた。

---

## 8. 2024――when core pieces leave

2024 tested whether the architecture depended too heavily on specific founders.

Reina Ichioka graduated on March 1 after a period of activity suspension due to health issues and discussion with management.

Official: https://helloproject.com/news/17058/

Ichioka mattered structurally. She had been the leader of CHICA#TETSU and one of the original centers of the project design.

Then Yuhane Yamazaki graduated on June 27, seeking a new environment to study music and explore more genres of singing.

Official: https://helloproject.com/news/17190/

Her graduation concert explicitly presented the last live of that 11-member configuration, including “眼鏡の男の子.”

ORICON: https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2333858/full/

The May 2024 single “灰toダイヤモンド / Go City Go / フックの法則” sits directly inside this transition period.

Official: https://helloproject.com/release/7208/

If BEYOOOOONDS had been defined only by its original casting, these departures would have broken the format.

But the deeper identity was not “these exact roles forever.”

It was **the capacity to recast**.

---

## 9. 2025–26――the architecture rewrites itself

Rika Shimakura graduated in June 2025, with plans including overseas study and future music activity around Showa-era songs and pop.

Official: https://helloproject.com/news/18424/

Kurumi Takase then announced graduation, and completed her BEYOOOOONDS / Hello! Project activity at the June 16, 2026 Yokohama Arena concert.

Official announcement: https://helloproject.com/beyooooonds/news/20009/

Concert: https://helloproject.com/event/041ca66c36d46327c352c9413cad2f23c0ddb832/

By this point, several people central to the original 2018 structure had left.

The internal units could have become historical labels only.

Instead, BEYOOOOONDS added three members in 2026: Hana Kojima, Mano Otsubo and Yuna Sugiyama.

Official: https://helloproject.com/news/20517/

And crucially, they were not left as generic “new BEYOOOOONDS members.”

The current structure places:

- Otsubo and Sugiyama in CHICA#TETSU
- Kojima in 雨ノ森 川海

while SeasoningS remains Hirai, Kobayashi and Satoyoshi.

Current profile: https://helloproject.com/beyooooonds/

So the organization did not preserve the old lineup.

It preserved the **logic of placement**.

> The inheritance is not “keep every original piece.” It is “keep asking which combination can create the next work.”

---

## 10. Complexity has a real cost

There is no reason to romanticize complexity.

BEYOOOOONDS has a higher explanation cost than a simpler group.

A newcomer asks “What is BEYOOOOONDS?” and immediately receives three subunit names, multiple histories and many different performance formats.

Graduations also create more redesign work because both the full group and internal units can be affected.

And if one work relies too heavily on one person’s specialty, that format may become difficult after her departure.

So complexity alone is not the advantage.

The key is **modularity**.

Parts must be separable and recombinable.

固定配線された複雑さは brittle になる。組み替えられる複雑さは creative capacity になる。

---

## 11. Survival model――`combinatorial_identity_survives`

Previous group histories in this series showed different kinds of continuity.

Morning Musume keeps a system running.

Juice=Juice reproduces capabilities across generations.

Tsubaki Factory layers cohorts before older cohorts leave.

BEYOOOOONDS is different again.

> **What survives is the right to recombine.**

This is **`combinatorial_identity_survives`**.

The fixed rule is not a final form.

The fixed rule is:

**you may change the form.**

Members can change. Internal-unit membership can change. The skill portfolio can change. Roles in songs can change.

And still, if the group keeps turning combinations into works, it remains legibly BEYOOOOONDS.

---

## 12. What BEYOOOOONDS added to Hello! Project

Organizations usually simplify in order to become understandable.

Reduce roles. Standardize products. Remove overlap.

BEYOOOOONDS chose another route.

It added roles, skills, theater, instruments and subunits.

Then each work answered the question: **which combination matters this time?**

That is why complexity did not remain noise.

It became production capacity.

> **Complexity is not always something to eliminate. If it can be recombined, complexity itself can become creativity.**

That may be BEYOOOOONDS’ most distinctive organizational contribution to Hello! Project.
