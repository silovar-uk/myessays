# 屋根を浮かせて建て直す：When an arena becomes a business platform

*Oak View Group（オーク・ビュー・グループ、OVG）を守破離で読む。情報基準日：2026年11月8日。*

> **Abstract**　Founded in 2015, OVG developed from an arena alliance concept into venue development, management, booking and commercial services. Seattle's Climate Pledge Arena is a vivid example. But complex venues bring construction, safety, regulatory and public-value risks. Examining Co-op Live's delayed 2024 opening and a 2025 U.S. Department of Justice matter, this article asks whether an arena can be both an attractive business and a good deal for the city.

## 1. 守｜Why would anyone suspend a roof to build an arena?

In Seattle, developers preserved a historic roof while rebuilding the arena underneath. The old structure traced back to the 1962 World's Fair. Builders supported the roof and excavated about **50 feet (roughly 15 metres)** beneath it. そんな工法を選ぶのは、単に客席を増やすためではない。The project had to reconcile heritage, entertainment demand, urban design, financing and public accountability. [NHL report on the redevelopment](https://www.nhl.com/news/seattle-kraken-arena-ribbon-cutting-ceremony-327130118).

The resulting **Climate Pledge Arena（気候誓約アリーナ）** opened in 2021, hosting NHL hockey, WNBA basketball, concerts and other events. The official project cost was around **$1.15 billion**. Seattle explains that OVG and partners financed the redevelopment under a development and lease arrangement with the city. The operator is OVG, but **owner, investor and operator are not interchangeable labels**. [City of Seattle](https://seattlecenter.com/about/plans-and-projects/arena) / [Arena facts](https://climatepledgearena.com/frequently-asked-questions/).

Arenas are more than buildings. They are systems for coordinating real estate, event rights, schedules and customers. ここがOVGを理解する入口になる。

## 2. 守｜The company began as an alliance, not a construction company

OVG was launched in **November 2015** by Tim Leiweke and partners connected with Irving Azoff and the live entertainment industry. Its early **Arena/Stadium Alliance（アリーナ・スタジアム連合）** aimed to help existing venue owners combine commercial resources, attract content and optimize sponsorship. Members did not necessarily need to outsource all venue management. [2015 founding release](https://investor.msgsports.com/press-releases/news-details/2015/Tim-Leiweke-and-Azoff-MSG-Entertainment-Join-Forces-to-Launch-the-Oak-View-Group-OVG/default.aspx).

その後OVGは施設開発、運営、興行誘致、飲食、スポンサー営業へと拡大した。Climate Pledge Arena opened in **2021**, Moody Center at the University of Texas in **2022**, and Manchester's Co-op Live finally opened in **May 2024**. [Seattle city](https://seattlecenter.com/about/plans-and-projects/arena) / [Co-op Live reporting](https://news.sky.com/story/manchesters-co-op-live-arena-finally-opens-after-weeks-of-cancellations-and-setbacks-13135966).

The CEO changed after the events of 2025. Chris Granger became interim chief executive in July, then was formally appointed in **December 2025**. [Company statement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/oak-view-group-names-chris-granger-chief-executive-officer-oak-view-group-302630717.html).

An important change appears across this timeline: a firm initially proposing shared commercial services became involved in the infrastructure itself. **The business moved from helping venues earn money to helping create the conditions under which venues can earn money.**

## 3. 守｜Four different businesses live inside one arena

![OVG value chain from development to venue revenues.](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/sports-business/ovg-model.svg)

A venue project includes at least four distinct roles.

**Development（施設開発）**: Estimate demand, plan the building, obtain approvals and secure funding. Construction-cost escalation and interest rates matter.

**Venue management（施設管理運営）**: Manage staffing, security, maintenance, cleaning and the day-to-day operation. The operator may be paid a management fee or participate in selected revenues, depending on contracts.

**Event booking（興行誘致）**: Negotiate with concerts, sporting events and promoters to fill the calendar. A full schedule is not automatically a profitable one.

**Commercial sales（協賛・特別席の販売）**: Sell naming rights, sponsorship inventory and premium seating. **Naming rights（命名権）** allow sponsors to use their brand in a facility's name under an agreed contract.

What should an operator measure? **Contribution margin（貢献利益）** means revenue less variable costs. Event count is useful, but a low-margin concert can add less value than a smaller, profitable corporate event. 固定費、修繕費、資本コストも別に考える必要がある。

**PPP（公民連携、Public–Private Partnership）** describes arrangements in which public and private entities share parts of a project. It does not mean that a private company automatically owns all facilities or that every benefit belongs to the public. The allocation of land rights, ownership, operations and risk depends on the specific agreement.

## 4. 守｜Opening a building and opening a business are different

Manchester's **Co-op Live** illustrates an uncomfortable truth. Its scheduled April 2024 opening faced postponements and cancellations amid safety and technical concerns. Reports included an air-conditioning component falling before a scheduled event. The venue ultimately opened on **May 14, 2024**. [Sky News](https://news.sky.com/story/manchesters-co-op-live-arena-finally-opens-after-weeks-of-cancellations-and-setbacks-13135966) / [ITV account](https://www.itv.com/news/granada/2024-05-02/co-op-live-arena-construction-to-cancellations).

A project can be nearly finished and still be unfit to welcome an audience. Emergency routes, power systems, testing, staff training and traffic arrangements are not decorative details. 施設建設の「進捗率」と営業開始の「準備完了」は別の指標だ。

This distinction reveals why a company that integrates building and operation does not eliminate execution risk. Integration increases the range of problems the company can address—but may also concentrate responsibility when something goes wrong.

## 5. 破｜What did the 2025 antitrust matter reveal?

In July 2025 the **U.S. Department of Justice** announced charges against OVG co-founder and then-CEO Tim Leiweke, concerning allegations of bid-rigging related to a University of Texas arena project. OVG agreed to a **non-prosecution agreement with a $15 million payment**; Legends Hospitality separately agreed to pay **$1.5 million**. [DOJ announcement](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/live-entertainment-ceo-indicted-orchestrating-conspiracy-rig-bidding-process-public) / [OVG case record](https://www.justice.gov/atr/case/us-v-oak-view-group-llc).

The DOJ later recorded that Leiweke received a **presidential pardon in December 2025**. The indictment, company agreements and pardon are separate legal facts. **An allegation is not a criminal conviction**, and a pardon does not itself prove every business process was sound.

**Bid rigging（入札談合）** involves improper coordination among parties that should compete independently. Why is that relevant to venue economics? Large arena projects require unusual expertise and financing, limiting the number of potential bidders. Firms may legitimately cooperate in some settings, but competition among prospective providers must not be unlawfully suppressed.

**Abduction:** The same network and relationships that make a firm capable of delivering complex projects may also create competition-policy challenges. This is a hypothesis about market structure, not a finding that all OVG projects involved wrongdoing.

**Forecast:** Procurement transparency, conflict-of-interest management and independent audits may become increasingly important competitive differentiators. We would test this by comparing bid participation, contract terms and independent oversight—not by assuming bigger networks necessarily improve outcomes.

## 6. 破｜A crowded arena is not proof of public value

A venue can host many events and still offer limited net economic gains to its city. Picture a full concert calendar. Ticket buyers spend money inside the arena, but what if they would otherwise spend it at nearby venues or restaurants? A transfer of spending is not the same as a new economic benefit.

This creates two questions:

- **Private returns（民間の投資収益）**: What cash flows remain with the operator, owner or investors after costs and capital commitments?
- **Social returns（社会的便益）**: What additional benefits and costs arise for residents, other businesses, traffic and public budgets?

The distinction matters most when public land, public services or tax support are involved. Research on stadium subsidies has repeatedly questioned inflated estimates of local economic benefits. [Bradbury and colleagues, 2024](https://ideas.repec.org/a/wly/jpamgt/v43y2024i3p899-937.html).

The method needed here is a **counterfactual（反実仮想）**: what would have happened without this arena? The gross number of attendees cannot answer that question.

## 7. 離｜Rail stations, hotels and urban economies

**Rail stations** convert passenger flow into retail, advertising and surrounding activity. Arenas similarly convert crowds into several kinds of spending. However, stations benefit from daily commuting while arena demand is concentrated on event days. 「人が集まる場所」という共通点だけで、同じ収益構造だとは言えない。

**Real estate** separates capital investment from daily operations. Developers worry about construction costs and long-term asset values; managers worry about bookings, staffing and service. Both matter, but their time horizons differ. 建物の価値と運営利益を、同一のKPIで評価しないことが重要だ。

**Public economics** examines **externalities（外部効果）**—benefits and costs not captured by the venue's accounts. A lively district may benefit from visitors while nearby residents face noise or traffic. To evaluate a publicly significant arena, we need both a business case and a social cost-benefit analysis.

## 8. やりすぎ検証｜What if the arena were full 200 nights a year?

Imagine a sold-out arena on 200 nights every year. Sounds like success. But suppose most visitors are local residents redirecting entertainment spending from other venues, while public traffic costs rise. The local net benefit could be surprisingly small.

Now change one variable: visitors come from other regions, stay overnight, use local restaurants and return later. The regional effect could be different. It is not the crowd itself that answers the question. It is **how much activity would not have occurred otherwise**.

*Practical proposal:* Measure event-level contribution margins and capital returns for the facility. Separately measure net new visitors, hotel stays, displacement from existing venues, traffic and public spending for the city. These are proposed metrics, not OVG's published project results.

## 9. 結論｜An arena is a long-term contract with its city

The floating roof in Seattle initially looked like a strange piece of engineering. But it reveals something broader: the need to connect architecture, financing, heritage, entertainment and local policy.

OVG's business does not simply sell seats or build arenas. It organizes long-lived commitments among many parties. That creates value, but also execution, governance and public-accountability risks. **The most useful final question is not how many people entered the building, but what lasting value the venue created—and for whom.**
