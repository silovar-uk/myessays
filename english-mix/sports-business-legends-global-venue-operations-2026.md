# ホットドッグから450施設へ：Who really runs the stadium?

*Legends Global—守破離で考える sports-business infrastructure. 情報基準日：2026年11月8日。*

> **Abstract**　Legends was created in 2008 by the New York Yankees, Dallas Cowboys and investors, initially focusing on food, beverage and merchandise. After acquiring ASM Global in 2024, it relaunched as Legends Global in 2025. Its story is not simply one of growth. It raises a more interesting question: **When a team outsources operations, who captures the know-how—and who controls the business?** The company's reported network is large, but scale is not proof of profitability.

## 1. 守｜A hot dog is a contract, not just food

A fan buys a hot dog at a baseball game. Simple enough. でも、その売上は球団、施設所有者、販売業者の誰に帰属するのか。The answer depends on contracts. One company may own the venue, another may operate food service, while the team owns the sports brand.

**Concessions** は施設内の飲食売店事業。It may involve food purchasing, staffing, inventory and service operations. A contract could specify fixed fees, revenue sharing or responsibility for unsold stock. Consequently, **customer spending, operator revenue and profit are three different numbers**.

The firm started on October 20, 2008, when the Yankees, Cowboys, CIC Partners and a Goldman Sachs investment arm announced Legends Hospitality Management. Its initial focus was food, catering and merchandise in the teams' new stadiums. [Original announcement](https://cicpartners.com/news/cic-partners-teams-with-dallas-cowboys-new-york-yankees-and-goldman-sachs-to-form-legends-hospitality-management/).

その後、premium seating sales（特別席の販売）、sponsorship（協賛営業）、feasibility studies（事業可能性調査）へ進出した。Sixth Street became its majority investor in 2021. Legends announced an ASM Global acquisition agreement in 2023 and completed the deal in August 2024. The integrated brand, Legends Global, was announced in September 2025. [Acquisition announcement](https://legendsglobal.com/legends-announces-acquisition-of-asm-global/) / [2025 integration](https://legendsglobal.com/press-release-legends-global-charts-bold-path-for-the-future-of-live-sports-and-entertainment/).

The company reports a network of **450+ venues, 20,000 annual events and more than 165 million guests**. These are company-described network figures—not the number of buildings it owns or proof of earnings.

![An editorial diagram of the Legends Global business model.](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/sports-business/legends-model.svg)

Why expand across so many functions? Consider the venue lifecycle: plan the building, sell seats and sponsorship, recruit events, then operate the venue. 分かれている仕事を一社が扱えば引継ぎの負担を減らせる可能性がある。This is similar to **vertical integration**, though the company typically integrates services rather than owning every underlying asset.

## 2. 守｜Outsourcing can save work but transfer knowledge

**Transaction costs** are the costs of finding, contracting with and monitoring business partners. Outsourcing（外部委託）は社内人材を節約し、外部の専門性を使える。しかし、管理や交渉にも費用がかかる。

The integrated model could create **economies of scope**: offering several services together may cost less than offering them separately. But that must be tested against actual project expenses. More services do not automatically mean better margins.

Financial reporting makes the warning concrete. In 2024, Sports Business Journal reported about **$1.7 billion in revenue for pre-acquisition Legends**, around **67% from food and beverage**, and referenced a forecast of negative free cash flow due partly to capital investment. [Source](https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2024/08/23/legends-acquires-asm-global/). These figures do not describe the entire current merged company.

**Free cash flow** は事業活動で得た現金から設備投資などを考慮した資金余力。A company can grow revenues while spending heavily to fulfill contracts. Calling it an 'asset-light software company' would obscure kitchen equipment, staffing and venue obligations.

Outsourcing also creates a **principal–agent problem**: the operator and the owner may pursue different goals. 売上連動報酬は増収を促すが、施設の保守やファン体験を軽視する誘因も生みうる。Contracts need balanced indicators: operating profit, satisfaction, upkeep and compliance.

## 3. 破｜Why buy an entire venue management company?

**Observation:** Legends acquired ASM Global in 2024 in a deal widely reported at about **$2.3 billion**. [Deal reporting](https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2024/08/23/legends-acquires-asm-global/).

**Abduction:** Perhaps the strategic prize was not size alone. It was a connected value chain—from feasibility advice and premium-sales planning to venue management and event booking. By linking these stages, an operator might make earlier decisions with better information about future customers and costs.

**Alternative hypothesis:** The company may have sought market access, cross-selling or scale for financial reasons. The merger announcement cannot establish post-merger profit improvement. We would need comparable data on contract renewals, revenue per venue, contribution margins and integration expenses.

**Forecast:** More bundled venue-service contracts may appear during 2027–2030. At the same time, sophisticated clients may deliberately split contracts to preserve competition. Neither result is guaranteed.

## 4. 破｜Growth cannot be separated from competition policy

In July 2025 the U.S. Department of Justice announced allegations about a University of Texas arena bidding process. Related to the matter, Legends Hospitality entered a **non-prosecution agreement** involving a **$1.5 million payment**. Oak View Group separately agreed to pay $15 million. [DOJ announcement](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/live-entertainment-ceo-indicted-orchestrating-conspiracy-rig-bidding-process-public).

**Bid rigging（入札談合）**とは競争すべき事業者が応札について不当に調整する行為。A non-prosecution agreement is not the same as an individual criminal conviction. The charged OVG founder subsequently received a presidential pardon; these legal events must remain distinct.

The practical lesson is not 'big firms are bad.' Rather, efficiency and competition are separate evaluation criteria. **A bundled service can be convenient and still weaken the buyer's alternatives.** 大規模な外注を評価するなら、価格だけでなく入札の競争性も確認する必要がある。

## 5. 離｜Hotels, trading houses and business-process outsourcing

Hotels separate **property ownership** from brand management and daily operations. Legends similarly creates value without necessarily owning each stadium. ただし、イベント開催日は限定され、ホテルと需要変動の形は異なる。

A Japanese trading house connects producers, financing, information and customers. Legends connects venue owners, sponsors, hospitality teams and event promoters. This is **orchestration（複数事業の統合調整）**, not identical to the trading-house balance sheet.

Business-process outsourcing, or **BPO（業務受託）**, offers another analogy. A company can reduce its own workload while transferring practical know-how to the contractor. If a club outsources sales for years, will it still know what the right sponsorship price is? The economic question becomes an organizational question: **Where does learning accumulate?**

## 6. 結論｜The boundary of the firm is the real story

The hot dog was never just a hot dog. It was a clue to **the boundary of the firm**—the decision about which activities to own and which to entrust to others.

For a Japanese sports club, a practical audit would compare four factors for each task: cost savings, incremental revenue, switching costs and the knowledge retained internally. That is a recommendation, not an observed result at Legends.

The deeper point: **Outsourcing is successful not when a club stops doing work, but when it gains specialist capacity without losing strategic control.**
