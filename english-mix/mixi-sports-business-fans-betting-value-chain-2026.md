# ゲーム会社が競輪場を運営する理由――MIXI Sports Businessを読み解く
## From fan experiences to betting platforms: ownership, value chains, financials and responsibility

One company runs a social network, a hit mobile game, professional sports teams, a betting platform and part of an arena business. しかもMIXIは、豪州のsports betting companyまで傘下に収めた。At first glance, this portfolio looks strange. 「試合を観ること」と「車券を買うこと」を同じsports segmentに入れると、経営の地図としては整っても、スポーツ文化としては妙な違和感が残る。

To understand the logic, move one level up: MIXI speaks about shared experiences, or **We-Time**. 人が同じものを見て、話し、同じ時間を楽しむ。That is the company's strategic narrative, not proof that every business creates equal social value. 観戦の歓声とbetting transactionsは、似た「同時性」を持っても、利益とリスクの配分が違う。[MIXIの中期ビジョン](https://mixi.co.jp/ir/mid-term-vision/)

This is a source-based analysis as of 15 October 2026. **Fact, interpretation and proposal are separate layers.** 公表されていないbusiness-unit profit、顧客データの統合状況、M&Aの正確な寄与額は推測で補完しない。This boundary matters, because a coherent strategy story can sound more conclusive than the evidence actually is.

## 1. MIXIのスポーツは「クラブ経営」と「投票関連事業」の二つでできている

MIXI divides its sports segment into **spectator businesses** and **betting businesses**. 観戦側はFC東京、千葉ジェッツ、Fansta、アリーナ運営への参画。Betting側はTIPSTAR、チャリロト、netkeirin、netkeiba、競輪場の運営受託、海外のPointsBetを含む。The common label is sports; the revenue mechanics are very different.[公式事業紹介](https://mixi.co.jp/business/sports/)／[投資家向け説明](https://mixi.co.jp/ir/individual/plan/)

For clubs, the customer may buy a ticket, merchandise or a sponsorship package. For a betting platform, the user places a wager; for venue operations, a public client may pay a management fee. Media builds an information relationship with users. **Shared attention is not a shared business model.** 観戦・投票・情報閲覧をまとめて「スポーツ需要」と呼ぶだけでは、お金の流れが見えなくなる。

One important distinction: **netkeiba is a horse-racing information service**, not evidence that MIXI operates Japan Racing Association betting. TIPSTAR and Chariloto focus on keirin and auto racing. 「競馬に関わる」と「中央競馬の投票を運営する」は違う。The precise value-chain position matters.[MIXI公式](https://mixi.co.jp/business/sports/)

## 2. スポーツ参入は思いつきではなく、ゲームの次の成長市場を探す判断だった

The company began mixi in 2004 and launched Monster Strike in 2013. The latter made cooperative play a core experience. In a 2022 interview, CEO Koki Kimura discussed the need to find growth beyond Japan's mature smartphone-game market. He identified **event-production capabilities** and **social product know-how** as reasons to enter sports. これは後からつけた単なる「スポーツ好き」の説明ではなく、事業能力の転用を狙う発想だった。[沿革](https://mixi.co.jp/company/history/)／[当時の取材](https://mixil.mixi.co.jp/product/14020/)

The sequence is unusually revealing. MIXI partnered with the Chiba Jets in 2017, expanded into Chariloto, club ownership and Net Dreamers in 2019, launched TIPSTAR in 2020, acquired control of FC Tokyo in 2022, joined the LaLa arena project in 2024 and acquired PointsBet in 2025. **From sponsorship to ownership, from media to transaction infrastructure.** スポーツ参入は単発の広告施策から、複数の収益源を持つportfolioへ移った。[沿革](https://mixi.co.jp/company/history/)／[2019年提携](https://mixi.co.jp/news/2019/0415/460/)

This timeline does **not** prove that a perfect master plan existed from the start. It only shows a pattern compatible with adjacent expansion. Acquisitions can be a form of organizational learning: buy an operating asset, discover its constraints, and decide where to move next. 事後的な戦略物語と、当時の意思決定の証拠は分けて読む必要がある。

## 3. 「We-Time」は事業をつなぐ仮説だが、利益の源泉まで同じではない

MIXI calls this market for time spent together the **We-Time economy**. Its thesis is that friends and families spread experiences through word of mouth, keep using them together and become more engaged. A game session, a shared sports-bar viewing and a live arena crowd fit the narrative. This is a **strategic hypothesis**, not a demonstrated effect for every product.[MIXI中期ビジョン](https://mixi.co.jp/ir/mid-term-vision/)

Betting also involves shared predictions and simultaneous outcomes. But **shared timing does not mean equivalent welfare effects**. 金銭的損失、依存症の危険、規制は観戦と異なる。The We-Time idea explains why MIXI sees a connection; it cannot by itself justify the ethical or economic outcomes of that connection.

To test the hypothesis, compare **retention**, **repeat visits** and **word-of-mouth acquisition** for social versus solo participation. But the necessary data must be collected and used lawfully; we cannot assume free sharing of fan and betting-user profiles across group companies. A good strategic story must be turned into falsifiable metrics.

## 4. 658億円は何の売上なのか――規模と儲けを分けて読む

In FY2026, sports-segment revenue reached **¥65.848 billion**, up **63.8%** from ¥40.206 billion. Segment profit increased from ¥1.999 billion to **¥5.088 billion**. That implies a roughly **7.7% segment-profit margin** and around **38% of group revenue**. This is no longer a branding side project. 数字は会社のsegment reportingの値であり、個別サービスの採算を示すものではない。[有価証券報告書](https://edinetdb.jp/company/E05617/text?fy=2026)／[経営メッセージ](https://mixi.co.jp/ir/message/)

Yet the **63.8% growth is not purely organic growth**. PointsBet entered the group in September 2025, with its results consolidated from October. Domestic betting services also grew. Without a complete disclosed bridge by business, we should not claim a precise split between acquisition-driven and existing-business growth.[FY2026開示](https://edinetdb.jp/company/E05617/text?fy=2026)

**Betting turnover is not necessarily accounting revenue.** 車券の購入総額、的中者への払戻し、主催者・事業者の取り分は別の概念だ。Do not treat the ¥65.848 billion segment revenue as the total amount of tickets sold. The recognition boundary matters as much as the transaction volume.

The digital-entertainment segment still generated about **¥43.05 billion in segment profit** in FY2026, far above sports. Sales scale and earnings power are two different questions. 新規投資フェーズと成熟ゲーム事業の利益率をそのまま競わせる必要はないが、acquisition cost and capital efficiencyを外して成功を判断するのも危険だ。[決算データ](https://edinetdb.jp/company/E05617/text?fy=2026)

## 5. 千葉ジェッツの成長は、良いクラブだけでなく「会場の容量」を変えた効果でもある

MIXI became a Chiba Jets partner in 2017 and acquired the club operator in 2019. In 2024, **LaLa arena TOKYO-BAY**, with about 10,000 seats, opened in Funabashi and became the club's home. It supports concerts as well as basketball. The project matters because venue capacity and live production affect the economics of a sports event.[共同発表](https://mixi.co.jp/news/2024/0408/30483/)

A bigger venue raises the **revenue ceiling** per home game, even if the season has the same number of matches. Pricing, premium seating and sponsorship inventory may also change. MIXI reports growth in Chiba Jets ticket and sponsor revenue after the arena opening. But published totals do not tell us exactly how much came from attendance, price or product mix.[決算説明](https://edinetdb.jp/company/E05617/text?fy=2026)

An essential ownership nuance: this is **not simply a wholly MIXI-owned basketball-only arena**. The project involves Mitsui Fudosan; arena management is handled by TOKYO-BAY Arena Management, a jointly invested company. Club operation and venue operation sit in different legal entities. だれが保有し、だれが運営し、だれが利用料を払い、どの収益を得るかは同一ではない。[共同発表](https://www.mitsuifudosan.co.jp/corporate/news/2024/0408/)

The real unit of analysis is **the visitor's day**, not only the match. The club sells sporting content; the arena operator supplies space and operations; local businesses capture food and shopping demand. Whether the combined project creates value depends on how these returns and costs are allocated, not merely on the number of seats.

## 6. FC東京は売上を増やしたが、クラブの成長には競技成績という制約が残る

MIXI acquired control of FC Tokyo's operator in February 2022. For fiscal 2025, the club reported record revenue of **¥7.21 billion**. Its former president described expenses of roughly ¥7.1 billion and profit below ¥100 million, with two consecutive profitable years. **Record sales do not automatically mean a high-margin club.**[買収発表](https://mixi.co.jp/news/2021/1122/2553/)／[FC東京インタビュー](https://www.fctokyo.co.jp/fan/fanzone/detail/336821/)

Former president Shigeya Kawagishi also warned that business growth had begun to slow and future expansion would require stronger on-field performance. A technology owner can improve CRM and commercial operations, but **sporting results remain an uncertain input**. Eisuke Shirai became the club's president in July 2026.[取材](https://www.fctokyo.co.jp/fan/fanzone/detail/336821/)／[人事発表](https://www.fctokyo.co.jp/news/details/337516/)

Club ownership creates a **governance tension**. A listed parent company evaluates capital allocation; a local football club carries long-standing relationships with municipalities, partners and supporters. The former president stressed preserving those relationships. Parent-company marketing logic alone cannot explain a club's public and historical obligations.[FC東京総括](https://www.fctokyo.co.jp/fan/fanzone/detail/336857/)

## 7. 競輪は「レースをつくる、調べる、買う」をまたぐ垂直的な事業になっている

In keirin, MIXI spans several steps: **venue operations, information media and digital betting interfaces**. Chariloto sells tickets and undertakes venue operations; netkeirin supplies race information; TIPSTAR blends live coverage, prediction and betting. This is closer to **vertical integration** than ordinary sports sponsorship.[IR説明](https://mixi.co.jp/ir/individual/plan/)／[事業一覧](https://mixi.co.jp/business/sports/)

Imagine a new user searching for a rider, studying past results, making a prediction and placing a ticket. Each step contains friction. A group active across these steps has opportunities to understand the **customer journey**, but actual data integration and measurable cross-service conversion are not established merely by corporate ownership.

Keirin is a **publicly authorized competition**, not just a private event. The public organizer, venue operator, racing operations and ticket-sales platform may be distinct actors. So “operating a keirin venue” does **not** mean holding unrestricted rights to organize public racing. 事業参入可能性を論じるなら、法令と委託契約の境界を確認しなければならない。[MIXI事業説明](https://mixi.co.jp/ir/individual/plan/)

## 8. スポーツ観戦を「どこで見るか」に拡張するFanstaは小さいが重要な補助線だ

Fansta helps people find restaurants showing particular sports broadcasts. It is neither the league's media-rights holder nor simply a streaming service. It solves **where to watch together**. スタジアムへ行けない日も共同観戦の場所を探せるという点で、We-Timeの説明に分かりやすく合う。[公式説明](https://mixi.co.jp/business/sports/)

The point is not that Fansta is proven to be a major profit center; standalone results are not disclosed here. It illustrates a business around the match without owning the match: **search, discovery, gathering and shared viewing**. 競技権利を保有しなくても、観戦の周辺体験には商機があり得る。

## 9. 海外ベッティング企業の買収は、国内の制約を越える代わりに別のリスクを引き受けた

In September 2025, MIXI acquired control of **PointsBet**, which operates betting businesses in Australia and Canada. The announced logic: combine an established local betting platform with MIXI's social-product experience. This was **not** an announcement that the foreign betting service became accessible to users in Japan.[買収方針](https://mixi.co.jp/news/2025/0226/38971/)／[取得結果](https://mixi.co.jp/en/news/2025/0916/53061/)

An acquisition is a decision, not a result. On **13 October 2026**, PointsBet forecast approximately **A$113 million** in H1 FY2027 revenue, about **16% below** the previous year, and **negative A$6–8 million** in its own **Normalised EBITDA**. MIXI stated that its consolidated full-year forecast was unchanged. The PointsBet EBITDA definition differs from MIXI's, so the figures are not directly interchangeable.[MIXI開示](https://www.release.tdnet.info/inbs/140120261013549774.pdf)

Domestic product know-how may travel, but **customer acquisition cost, regulation, competition and social habits** do not automatically travel with it. The hard question is whether the acquisition creates defensible capabilities and returns above capital costs. A strategic rationale must survive a disappointing operating forecast.

## 10. ソーシャルベッティングには、共体験の良さだけでは消せない倫理的な緊張がある

A social loop can lower the barrier to joining. In a game, that may be a welcome benefit. In betting, the same design could increase pressure, frequency and financial exposure. **Engagement is not automatically well-being.** 友達と過ごす時間を増やすという目的が、無制限の投票促進を正当化するわけではない。

MIXI publishes **responsible-gambling measures**: warnings, self-check tools, consultation information, exclusion requests by users or families, and purchase limits. TIPSTAR's terms bar users under 20. These measures exist, but their existence alone does not prove sufficient real-world effectiveness.[対策概要](https://mixi.co.jp/sustainability/materiality/itservice/soundness/)／[TIPSTAR規約](https://about.tipstar.com/terms-tipstar/)

As a **proposal**, pair growth KPIs with harm-reduction KPIs: the use of betting limits, time to process exclusion requests, intervention outcomes and access to support. These are proposed measures, **not claims about MIXI's internal dashboard**. A social betting business needs two scoreboards: commercial performance and consumer protection.

## 11. デジタル化によって試合は資産になるが、すべての実験が残るわけではない

In 2022, MIXI and DAZN announced **DAZN MOMENTS**, an NFT-based marketplace for memorable sports clips. The idea was to turn a fleeting highlight into a collectible digital asset. But a launch announcement is **not proof of a durable product**; a contemporary user report documents a notice that the service would close in March 2024.[MIXI発表](https://mixi.co.jp/news/2022/0204/2554/)／[終了通知を伝える記録](https://lateral.blog/2023/12/05/post-1186/)

A technical certificate of ownership does not itself create demand. Sports video value depends on **rights, reuse permission, discovery, editing, distribution and post-purchase utility**. NFT was one possible product form, not the business model itself. 競技映像の二次活用は、技術より先に「誰に、どの権利で、何を届けるか」を決める必要がある。

A company biography built only from surviving services becomes survivorship bias. Failed or discontinued experiments can reveal constraints, but **the cause of closure must not be invented**. Without internal evidence, we can say a service ended; we cannot assert exactly why it ended.

## 12. 2030年代初頭の1,200億円目標は、売上の倍増よりも収益の質を問う

MIXI targets **at least ¥120 billion in sports revenue** by the early 2030s—roughly **1.8 times** FY2026 revenue. At group level, it targets ¥300 billion in revenue, a 20% EBITDA margin and 15% ROE. Its portfolio framework combines growth and returns relative to capital costs. **The target is not simply bigger sales.**[中期ビジョン](https://mixi.co.jp/ir/mid-term-vision/)

An analyst needs **separate scorecards**. For spectators: attendance, yield, sponsorship retention and club profitability. For betting: wagering volume versus recognized revenue, acquisition cost, retention, operating margin and consumer protection. For acquisitions: foreign exchange, consolidation effects and return on invested capital. These are **proposed analytical dimensions**, not an assertion about MIXI's unpublished metrics.

Consider a thought experiment: if foreign betting revenue alone carried the sports segment to ¥120 billion, the financial target might be met without any necessary improvement in local sports participation or fan experience. Conversely, youth development spending may lower short-term margins while serving a legitimate community purpose. **One segment, multiple kinds of value.** Do not use a single revenue total to judge all of them.

## 13. スポーツ企業を調べるなら「誰の時間」と「誰の財布」を同時に追う

Start research not with a list of technologies but with a **value chain**: who creates the content, who discovers it, who experiences it, who pays, and who carries operating responsibility. MIXI provides a practical case because clubs, venues, media and betting platforms occupy different steps.

Five reusable questions: **What exactly is purchased? Which number is turnover and which is revenue? Who owns rights and assets? How much growth is organic versus acquired? What harms must be tracked?** これはMIXIの内部管理表ではなく、他社にも使えるresearch checklistとしての提案だ。

Return to the opening question: why did a social-network and game company arrive at keirin venues and sports betting? Because it sees **shared time** as something that can be designed across venues, media and transactions. Yet the same moment can generate joy for one person, revenue for another and financial risk for someone else. MIXI's case does not prove that We-Time is one unified form of value. It shows why we must ask **whose time, whose money and whose risk**.

## 資料と用語――理解するために最低限分けておきたいもの

**Segment** is a financial-reporting business unit, not a single club. **Segment profit** follows the company's reporting definition. **EBITDA** is an earnings measure before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, but practical definitions can differ. Do not confuse one company's adjusted EBITDA with another company's operating profit.

**Inorganic growth** comes from acquisitions, not solely from expanding existing operations. **Vertical integration** covers multiple stages of an industry. A **value chain** maps the steps through which value reaches the customer. **ROIC** asks whether operating returns justify the capital invested.

**Social betting** is MIXI's product concept for combining prediction or betting with interaction. It is not a universal legal category. **Comprehensive venue management** means contracted operation of multiple functions, not necessarily ownership of the public competition. **Rights clearance** confirms permission to use footage, names, images and other assets.
