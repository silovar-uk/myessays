# この制度は、これからどう変わっていくのか — Where These Institutions Go From Here
## Rule Lifecycle Auditとシリーズ全体のtoolkitで、制度の動態を自分で読み続ける

第1回から第17回まで、NPBという制度を、内側から読み、外部とも比較してきた。

しかし、これらの制度は静止した完成品ではない。今も交渉・提案・改正が続いている。

第17回の最後に、こう問いを置いた。

**What's currently under discussion, or likely to change in the near future — and how should a reader keep tracking that change on their own?**

今回はここから始める。ただし今回は、新しい制度を大量に紹介する回ではない。**this is the episode where the toolkit built over 18 installments gets handed to the reader**である。

> **Information basis: 2026年9月4日**
>
> The three cases discussed below are all as reported in July–August 2026, and none has reached a conclusion — some are still under negotiation. Things will likely move on from here, but tracking what comes next is left to the reader.

---

## 1. SHORT ANSWER — read institutions by "what stage they're at"

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
制度の動態を読むための出発点は、第8回で導入した**Rule Lifecycle Audit**を思い出すことである。
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
Distinguish four stages: CURRENT, PROPOSED, AGREED (but not yet in effect), and EFFECTIVE.
<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
第9回では、この4段階を使って「最新公開協約の条文テキスト」と「2026年の現在運用」を区別した。
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
制度のニュースを見たとき、まず確認すべきは「この話は今、CURRENT・PROPOSED・AGREED・EFFECTIVEのどの段階にあるか」である。
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
段階を区別せずに読むと、a proposal gets mistaken for a done deal, or a settled decision gets underrated as still just rumor.

## 2. WHAT'S IN MOTION — three live cases as of 2026

Rule Lifecycle Auditを、実際に今議論されている3つの事案へ当てはめてみる。

### Case 1: abolishing FA "personnel compensation" (PROPOSED, no agreement reached)

On July 3, 2026, the players' union met with NPB and discussed abolishing the "personnel compensation" system tied to exercising FA rights, but the talks ended without a conclusion. NPB countered with an alternative: the signing club would hand its draft-selection rights over to the player's former club.

At an August 12, 2026 meeting, the union reportedly rejected NPB's draft-rights-transfer proposal, judging it wouldn't actually help activate player movement.

**Lifecycle verdict: PROPOSED. Multiple proposals are on the table, but the two sides haven't reached agreement.**

これは第8回で読んだFAの制度そのものに関わる話であり、Mobility Mechanism Auditの「COST / CONSIDERATION」（人的補償という代償）を、制度側が見直そうとしている動きとして読める。

### Case 2: introducing the pitch clock (AGREED, operational details still pending)

At its July 28, 2026 extraordinary convention, the players' union unanimously resolved to request NPB introduce a pitch clock, possibly starting in the 2027 season. Reporting from August 3, 2026 says NPB has decided to introduce it, but the timing and detailed rules remain under consideration.

**Lifecycle verdict: AGREED (on the direction of introducing it), but the operational side — timing and detailed rules — hasn't reached EFFECTIVE yet.**

「導入が決まった」という見出しだけを見ると、あたかも詳細まで確定したように読めてしまう。しかし、**separate what was actually agreed to from whether the operational details are finalized**必要がある。

### Case 3: a unified heat-safety standard (PROPOSED)

選手会は、気温・湿度などに基づく試合開催可否の統一基準を設けるよう要望しているとされる。

**Lifecycle verdict: PROPOSED. It's at the request stage; no NPB agreement or implementation is confirmable.**

## 3. WHERE TO LOOK — getting closer to primary sources

制度の動きを追うとき、次のような情報源のレイヤーを意識する。

- **NPB's own announcements** — the closest thing to a primary source: rule changes, official notices.
- **The players' union's announcements** — one party's side of a negotiation, to be checked against NPB's own position separately.
- **News coverage** — useful summaries of both sides, but don't judge by the headline alone; read the body carefully for words like "under discussion," "decided," or "under consideration."

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
特に重要なのは、**not conflating a headline saying "NPB decided" with one saying "the union is requesting"** — that distinction matters.
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
どちらの当事者が発信した情報かによって、それが交渉のどの段階にあるかの意味が変わる。

## 5. Applying the toolkit to the three cases

先ほどの3事案に、表のtoolを当てはめてみる。

- **Abolishing FA personnel compensation**: Rule Lifecycle Audit (PROPOSED) + Mobility Mechanism Audit (a proposed revision to the COST/CONSIDERATION variable).
- **The pitch clock**: Rule Lifecycle Audit (AGREED on direction, details still pending). This isn't about player movement or business structure — it's a change to how the game itself is officiated, and doesn't map cleanly onto any existing Audit. It may need **a new kind of tool for in-game operational rules**, something this series hasn't built yet.
- **The heat-safety standard**: Rule Lifecycle Audit (PROPOSED). This is a safety/operations matter that doesn't map onto the revenue or roster axis either.

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
この当てはめ作業自体が、**shows the toolkit isn't universal**ことを示している。
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
ピッチクロックや暑さ対策のような競技運営・安全面の制度は、これまでシリーズが中心に扱ってきたPLAYER SYSTEM・BUSINESS SYSTEMのどちらとも異なる、**a possible third domain**かもしれない。この領域については、今回は名前を付けず、今後の課題として残す。

## 6. WHAT THIS EPISODE DOES NOT MEAN

### This episode ≠ the series finale

The series doesn't end here. This episode exists to let readers keep tracking institutional change on their own from here on.

### PROPOSED ≠ a prediction of whether it will happen

Being at the proposal stage says nothing about whether something will eventually be realized.

### Handing over the toolkit ≠ a finished, universal analytical instrument

Operational and safety-related changes like the pitch clock or heat standards may not be fully explainable with the existing toolkit.

---

## 7. TAKEAWAYS

- Read institutional news through the Rule Lifecycle Audit (CURRENT / PROPOSED / AGREED / EFFECTIVE) to check what stage it's at.
- As of September 2026: abolishing FA personnel compensation is PROPOSED, the pitch clock is AGREED (details pending), and the heat-safety standard is PROPOSED.
- Whether NPB or the union is the source changes what negotiating position the news is coming from.
- Apply whichever Audit built across this series fits the theme at hand.
- Operational and safety-related institutions like the pitch clock or heat standards aren't fully covered by the existing toolkit — that's an open task for later.
