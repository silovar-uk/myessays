# 骨董通りは、名前より先に未来が決まっていた — Antique Street and Its Unfinished Future
### Aoyama’s antiques, a 1964 road plan, and a name that outlived its original market

“Antique Street” sounds ancient. 桐箱入りの茶碗が並ぶ古い商店街を想像する。But in official documents, the same street appears as **Auxiliary Urban Planning Road No. 23**. 風情を一瞬で失う行政名である。Its 25-metre road plan dates to 1964, while the nickname “骨董通り” became widely known in the 1980s. **The road had a planned future before it acquired its famous name.**

This is not a fictional walking diary. We compare three kinds of evidence: museum and shop records, interviews with people who worked here, and Minato City planning documents. 数字の不一致も隠さない。The research question becomes more than “Where is this street?” It is **what we count when we say a neighbourhood has character—and what disappears from the count.**

*写真：2011年4月の骨董通り。撮影・Harani0403／[ウィキメディア・コモンズ](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kotto_Dori_street_Tokyo.JPG)、CC0。2026年の現況写真ではない。*

## 1. そもそも、どこからどこまでが骨董通りなのか

Kottō-dōri is a **nickname**, not a postal district. 表参道駅近くの青山通り・南青山五丁目交差点から、六本木通り・高樹町交差点へ続く道を指す。The postal addresses are mainly 南青山五・六丁目. A street's popular name and its official address system serve different purposes（[港区観光案内](https://www.city.minato.tokyo.jp/kokusaika/documents/guidebook4.pdf)）。

Older names include 高樹町通り and 南町通り. 「高樹町」という住居表示は今は使われないが、交差点名や高速道路の出入口に残る。A name can vanish from addresses while surviving in transport infrastructure. **Urban names do not all change at the same speed**（[2002年の現地取材](https://www.shibukei.com/special/144/)）。

## 2. 骨董店は、なぜここに集まったのか

**Antiques** are old objects collected, used or traded; **fine antiques** emphasize artistic and historical significance. 陶磁器、書画、茶道具、漆器などを扱うが、古ければ自動的に高価になるわけではない。Provenance（来歴）、condition（保存状態）、authenticity（真贋）が重要になる。A specialist district works because knowledgeable sellers and interested buyers can find one another.

To see why dealers gathered here, begin with the **Nezu Museum**. 根津嘉一郎の日本・東洋古美術の収集を公開するため、1941年に開館。Its main building was rebuilt in 1954 after wartime destruction. As of March 2025 it held **7,630 works**, including seven National Treasures. 茶の湯道具を含む専門的な収集が、この地域に目利きと愛好家を引き寄せる土台となった（[美術館公式](https://www.nezu-muse.or.jp/jp/about/index.html)）。

According to a 2002 local report, the museum's former entrance faced this street, and dealers gathered around it from the mid-1950s to serve collectors and tea practitioners. ただし「美術館がある＝骨董街が自動的に生まれる」ではない。Visitors, specialist shops and personal introductions reinforced one another. In economics this resembles **agglomeration**（集積）—proximity makes a specialized market easier to use（[2002年の取材](https://www.shibukei.com/special/144/)）。

## 3. 「骨董通り」の名付けは、古代ではなく宣伝の歴史だった

Here is the surprise: this old-sounding place name has a modern **promotion history**. 中島誠之助は1976年に古伊万里の店「からくさ」を開き、1980年には「南青山骨董通り」を作詞した。TV Tokyo identifies him as the person behind the nickname; a 2002 report describes its spread through songs and essays in the early 1980s. ただし命名の「正確な初日」を示す原資料までは確認できない（[テレビ東京](https://www.tv-tokyo.co.jp/kantei/kaiun_db/kanteishi/kanteishi_1.html)、[2002年取材](https://www.shibukei.com/special/144/)）。

Calling it promotion does **not** make it fake. 骨董店の集積という実態があり、覚えやすい名前がつき、雑誌と口コミがそれを共有した。This is a useful example of **place branding**（場所のブランド化）—a label that makes the characteristics of a place recognizable. これは現代からの分析用語であり、当事者がそう呼んでいたという意味ではない。

A second witness changes the story. 黒崎輝男（イデー創業者）は、近隣でアンティークを売る仲間が集まり、「骨董通り」と自称して盛り上げたと回想している。A single inventor is an attractive story, but a name survives only when many people use it. **There is a difference between naming a street and keeping its name alive**（[当事者インタビュー](https://freedom-univ.com/blog/kuroteru_voice2/)）。

## 4. 骨董店は本当に消えたのか。数え方を変える実験

One source says **about 80 antique dealers** at the 1980s peak; another reports **forty-something around 1989**. 数字が衝突する。ただし、対象エリア、店舗の定義、調査年が一致していない。We cannot convert these anecdotes into a valid decline rate. **A number without a counting rule is not a time series**（[玉川堂](https://www.gyokusendo.com/column01_dento/3463)、[地域記事](https://m-aoyama.net/2023/09/18/%E9%AA%A8%E8%91%A3%E9%80%9A%E3%82%8A-2/)）。

Instead of trusting a nostalgic count, I examined a **2026 art-dealer map** published by the specialist magazine *Me no Me*. It lists shops across the surrounding area, including some not directly facing the street and a temporary office. 大塚美術は南青山五丁目、利菴は小原流会館の地下に店を構える。The market has **not vanished**. But storefront counting misses businesses above or below street level（[2026年地図](https://menomeonline.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/web_aoyamamap.pdf)、[大塚美術](https://www.otsuka-art.com/guide)、[利菴](https://lyanarts.com/shop.html)）。

To measure change properly, we would count three different populations: **ground-floor storefronts on the street; all-floor businesses within 200 metres; and licensed art dealers including offices**. 定義を変えれば結果も変わる。The available public sources do not support one comparable historical series, so this article does not claim a precise percentage decline. **Defining the unit is part of the research, not administrative housekeeping.**

## 5. さらに妙な話。昔は電車が走っていた

The street used to carry a **tram**（都電）. 1960年代の都電6系統は、渋谷方面から青山通りを進み、現在の骨董通りを通って高樹町・六本木通りへ向かった。What now looks like a shopping street was also part of a citywide transit route. **Commercial identity sits on top of transport history**（[路面電車の調査](https://bunshun.jp/articles/-/38772)）。

Trams, art dealers and fashion boutiques belong to different layers of the same place. しかし「都電廃止が骨董店減少を起こした」「家賃だけが原因だ」と断定するデータはない。Transport, customers, property costs, succession and online trading are **competing explanations**. A historical sequence is not yet a causal test.

## 6. 道路計画の数字が、780・810・830メートルと食い違う理由

Three lengths appear in the records: **810 metres** in Minato's urban-planning list, **roughly 830 metres** in a local street guide, and **roughly 780 metres** in the 2026 approved project. 一見すると誤差に見える。But these figures describe different things—planned route, popularly defined street, and approved project section. 同じ数字を測るための資料ではない（[計画一覧](https://www.sonicweb-asp.jp/minato/files/dorokei.html)、[地域案内](https://m-aoyama.net/2023/09/18/%E9%AA%A8%E8%91%A3%E9%80%9A%E3%82%8A-2/)、[認可資料](https://gikai2.city.minato.tokyo.jp/voices/GikaiDoc/attach/Nittei/Nt5296_11.pdf)）。

These are not necessarily conflicting measurements of one identical boundary. ただし筆者が測量して20〜50メートルの差の内訳を確定したわけではない。It is more accurate to keep the official scope attached to each figure than to pick one number as “the true length.” **Numbers become meaningful only with their definitions.**

## 7. 2026年、名前だけでなく道路そのものが変わろうとしている

In July 2026, Minato City obtained **project authorization** for the road improvement. The approved section is about **780 metres**, generally **25 metres wide** (partly 27 metres), with an authorized project period from **1 July 2026 to 31 March 2038**. 1964年の「都市計画決定」と2026年の「事業認可」は別段階。Authorization is not proof that construction is already complete（[区議会一次資料](https://gikai2.city.minato.tokyo.jp/voices/GikaiDoc/attach/Nittei/Nt5296_11.pdf)）。

Minato City's stated problems are concrete: sidewalks around **2 metres wide**, utility poles, insufficient shade, and no dedicated cycling space. The standard 25-metre cross-section in the official drawing allocates **7.75 metres on each side** to pedestrian areas and related uses, with a **9.5-metre central roadway section**. つまり車だけのための拡幅ではない。The plan redesigns walking, cycling, shade and emergency access（[公式断面図](https://gikai2.city.minato.tokyo.jp/voices/GikaiDoc/attach/Nittei/Nt5296_11.pdf)）。

![港区の2026年資料をもとに描いた幅25メートルの計画道路断面](assets/kotto-dori-road-section-2026.svg)

*図：区議会の計画断面図「一般部」を参考に筆者作成。細部を単純化した模式図で、停車帯部などでは構成が異なる。*

The plan moved from a long-standing concept through a **2025 public briefing** to formal project authorization in 2026. ただし2038年3月は認可上の期間で、完成を保証する日付ではない。Land acquisition and construction coordination still matter. The relevant question now is **when and for whom the promised improvements actually arrive**（[地域の説明会記録](https://m-aoyama.net/2025/08/30/%E9%AA%A8%E8%91%A3%E9%80%9A%E3%82%8A%E3%80%80%E3%81%A4%E3%81%84%E3%81%AB%E6%8B%A1%E5%B9%85%E5%B7%A5%E4%BA%8B%E8%A8%88%E7%94%BB%E3%81%8C%E6%9C%AC%E6%A0%BC%E5%A7%8B%E5%8B%95%EF%BC%81%EF%BC%81/)）。

## 8. 批判的に考える。きれいな通りは、誰にとってよい通りなのか

Wider sidewalks, fewer poles and more trees sound positive. しかし**who benefits and who bears the costs?** Pedestrians may gain safety; businesses may face construction disruption; property owners may have land-acquisition concerns. 特定の店に立ち退きが決まった、と確認したわけではない。These are stakeholder questions, **not verified damage claims**.

Rising attractiveness is not automatically success for everyone. 地価や賃料、業種構成の変化により、従来の専門店が続けにくくなる可能性がある。**Gentrification**（ジェントリフィケーション）はそのような地域変化や排除を論じる概念だ。But proving that mechanism on this street requires actual rent, land-price, shop-turnover and resident data. 可能性と実証を混同してはいけない。

A useful fact in the city's 2026 newsletter: there is a registered local group named **青山高樹町通り（骨董通り）を良くする会**. これは通りが、行政と店だけのものではない証拠だ。Residents have an organized role in discussing its future. But we should not claim to speak for them without interviews（[港区広報](https://www.city.minato.tokyo.jp/kouhou/kuse/koho/minato2026/202607/20260715top/05.html)）。

## 9. 「骨董」の仕事はどう成り立つのか。実用的な用語を四つ

**古物商** refers to a legal category of business dealing in second-hand goods; certain operations require a public-safety-commission permit. **古美術商** describes specialist art dealing. 許可は作品の真贋や審美性を国家が保証する証明ではない。Legal permission and scholarly judgement are different things（[警視庁](https://www.keishicho.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/tetsuzuki/kobutsu/tetsuzuki/kyoka.html)）。

**Provenance**（来歴）means the history of an object's origin and ownership. **Authenticity**（真贋）asks whether the claimed maker, period or place of origin is correct. 輸出の際には文化財保護の規制も関わる。Japan's Agency for Cultural Affairs explains its certificate for confirming that an object is not an export-restricted designated cultural property. 美しい器の裏側には記録と手続きがある（[文化庁](https://www.bunka.go.jp/seisaku/bunkazai/kokusai/kobijutsuhin/index.html)）。

**Setback**（建物の後退）は一つの見た目だが、建築基準法上の道路後退と都市計画道路の用地確保は別の制度である。**Planning decision**（都市計画決定）、**project authorization**（事業認可）、**land acquisition**（用地取得）、**construction**（工事）を分ければ、「道が広がる」という一文を具体的な進捗に直せる。

## 10. 机上調査を越えるなら、現地で何を調べるべきか

The extra research here had three parts: compare **780/810/830 metres**, define **three dealer-counting boundaries**, and cross-check a **2026 specialist map against shop websites**. 次の段階は現地実測だ。Record each frontage, floor level, business type, vacant unit, utility pole and walking width; repeat on the same day and time. An openly documented survey is more useful than “the street felt fashionable.”

To test causality, add historical address maps, old phone directories, public land prices, approved land-acquisition drawings and interviews with shop owners. 今回、それらの全年度照合や現地聞き取りは行っていない。Without them, we can identify possible mechanisms but cannot prove which one drove particular departures. **Stating this limit is part of respecting the place.**

## 11. それでも「骨董通り」と呼び続ける意味

At first, “Antique Street” seemed like a misleading label if fewer antiques were visible. But a person helped name it, traders and customers repeated it, magazines spread it, and underneath it lie a tram route and a 1964 road plan. 街の名は今日の店舗名鑑ではない。**It is a compact memory of what the place has been.**

In 2026, the street is preparing for a physical redesign. The 25-metre plan affects accessibility, shops and the old streetscape. 次にここを歩くなら、古い器だけでなく、道幅と建物の境界を見るだろう。The future has already been drawn, though not yet built. **Perhaps the most interesting antique on Antique Street is the long life of its name—and of an unfinished plan.**
