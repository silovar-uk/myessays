# ポスティング制度は、何を国境の外へ広げる制度なのか — What Does the Posting System Extend Across the Border?
## Mobility Mechanism Auditで、FA・トレードとの異同を読む

第18回で、シリーズ全体のtoolkitを読者自身の手に渡した。

Today's episode points that toolkit at an institution the series hasn't touched yet.

**How does the posting system compare to FA and trade as a mobility mechanism — what's the same, and what's different?**

---

## 1. SHORT ANSWER — it isn't just "the same move, but overseas"

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
ポスティング制度は、選手がMLBなど海外リーグへ移籍するための手段である。
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
But it isn't simply a domestic move relabeled as international.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
第8回のFA、第9回のトレードと同じく、ポスティングも**the same seven variables — INITIATOR, RIGHT, CONSENT, DESTINATION, COST, TIMING, AFTER**という同じ変数で読み解ける、player mobilityの一種である。
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
The difference isn't "domestic vs. overseas" — it's **which variable takes a different value than it does under FA or trade**.

---

## 2. WHO CAN USE IT — the boundary with international FA rights

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
第8回で、選手が通算一定年数(9年)のNPB在籍を経ると、**international FA rights**という別の権利を得ることを見た。
<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
ポスティング制度は、**a player still under NPB contract who has not yet earned international FA rights**を対象とする制度である。
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
つまり、海外FA権を得た選手は、ポスティングを使わず海外FA権を行使して自由に交渉できる。Posting exists as **a separate exit to an overseas league**, available to players one step short of that threshold.
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Both paths end in an overseas move, but **exercising international FA rights and going through posting are separate institutions**, and which one applies depends entirely on which side of the nine-year line a player stands on.

---

## 3. HOW IT STARTS — the player asks, the club can say no

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
制度の出発点は、選手が所属球団に対して「ポスティングでの移籍を認めてほしい」と申し出ることである。
<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
So far this resembles FA's structure — a player who meets a condition exercises a right.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
しかし、決定的に違うのは、**the player's own club can reject that request**という点である。球団が容認しなければ、ポスティングは行われない。
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Under FA, meeting the eligibility bar lets a player act without the club's approval. Under posting, **even a willing player cannot start the process until the club's consent clears the gate first** — which puts posting closer to trade's "club-side agreement as a precondition" than to FA.

球団が容認すると、NPBコミッショナーを通じてMLB機構へポスティングが申請され、MLB機構が受理した後、全30球団に対して当該選手が交渉可能であることが公示される。

---

## 4. WHO CAN NEGOTIATE — a market that isn't shaped like FA's or trade's

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
公示後の一定期間、交渉を希望するどのMLB球団とも選手が直接交渉できる。
<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
This "every interested club negotiates at once" design is a feature of the current (post-2018) system.
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
これ以前(2013年以前の一時期)は、最高額を提示した一球団のみが独占交渉権を得る入札方式が採られていた時期もあった。
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
DESTINATION / MARKETという軸で見ると、FAでは交渉相手はNPB内の他球団(元球団との再契約も可能)、トレードでは球団間の合意で決まる一つの移籍先、ポスティングでは**the entire MLB, a market outside NPB altogether**が交渉相手になる。
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
It looks like FA's "the market widens" pattern, but widening within one league and switching to an entirely separate league's market are not the same kind of change — the latter should be read as **a jump to a different market**, not a continuous extension of the first.

---

## 5. WHAT MOVES — who owes what to whom

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
移籍が成立すると、選手を送り出したNPB球団に対して、譲渡金(release fee)が支払われる仕組みが現行制度にはある。
<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
That fee scales with the total value of the contract the player signs with his new MLB club (under the post-2018 formula).
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
This piece skips the specific rates and past dollar figures. What matters here isn't the number — it's **that some consideration flows to the club giving up the player**.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
COST / CONSIDERATIONという軸で比べると、トレードでは球団間で選手・金銭・指名権などが交換されるのに対し、FA(国内)では移籍先球団から旧球団への人的補償や金銭補償という形を取る。The posting fee resembles FA's compensation in that sense — payment to the club being left — but **the payer here is an MLB club, not another NPB club**.
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Noticing that "consideration exists" in all three institutions isn't enough — **losing track of who pays whom** collapses distinctions that actually matter.

---

## 6. WHEN IT CAN HAPPEN — two stages of TIMING

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
ポスティングには、選手が申請できる期間と、公示後に交渉できる期間という、二段階のTIMINGがある。
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
申請期間は例年秋に始まり、公示後は協定で定められた日数の間、交渉が行われる。
<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
If no deal is reached within the negotiation window, the player's contractual status reverts to his original NPB club.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
The exact calendar deadline shifts by agreement year to year and is reported inconsistently across sources, so this piece won't pin down a specific date. What matters structurally is that **an application period and a negotiation period are distinct, and a failed negotiation reverts the player rather than voiding his contract**.
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
This is the same pattern seen in FA's declaration window (Episode 8) and trade's Article 108 transfer window (Episode 9): **player mobility always runs on a TIMING constraint**.

---

## 7. Mobility Mechanism Audit — lining up all three

第8〜9回で作ったMobility Mechanism Auditに、ポスティングを追加する。

| Axis | FA | TRADE | POSTING |
|---|---|---|---|
| INITIATOR | Player exercises a right once eligible | A club-to-club transfer mechanism | Player requests it; the club decides whether to allow it |
| RIGHT / RELATIONSHIP | The pool of clubs he can sign with widens | The existing contract and club-side obligations transfer | The existing contract relationship holds while negotiating rights extend to MLB clubs |
| CONSENT | The player decides whether to exercise FA rights | Pre-consented to transfer via the uniform contract | Club consent is a mandatory gate before anything starts |
| DESTINATION / MARKET | Player negotiates and chooses; re-signing with his old club is possible | Determined by the transfer arrangement between clubs | All of MLB can negotiate simultaneously (current system) |
| COST / CONSIDERATION | Compensation (player or cash) from the signing club to the old one | Players, cash, or draft rights exchanged between clubs | A release fee from the new MLB club to the old NPB club |
| TIMING | The FA eligibility/declaration calendar | The transfer-eligible window | Two stages: an application period, then a post-notice negotiation period |
| AFTER | Negotiation proceeds to a signed agreement | The contractual counterparty changes | Reverts to the original NPB club if no deal is reached |

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
このtableで分かるのは、ポスティングが**a hybrid mechanism — FA's player-initiated exercise of a right combined with trade's club-side consent gate**だということである。
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
"Posting is just FA's overseas version" is only half right. **Its consent structure sits closer to trade, while its destination market is wider than FA's** — it doesn't collapse into either category cleanly.

---

## 8. WHAT THIS EPISODE DOES NOT MEAN

### Posting ≠ exercising international FA rights

A player who has earned international FA rights negotiates with far more freedom and skips the posting procedure entirely. These are separate institutions for players in different positions.

### A release fee existing ≠ the same thing as FA's compensation

Both involve some consideration changing hands, but who pays and how the amount is set are different mechanisms.

### Being able to negotiate with many clubs ≠ the destination being decided by the player alone

A wide negotiating field doesn't guarantee a deal. If no agreement is reached, the player reverts to his original club.

---

## 9. TAKEAWAYS

- The posting system is a club-approval-gated overseas-move mechanism for players who haven't yet earned international FA rights.
- The player initiates it, but the mandatory club-consent gate makes it closer to trade than to FA on that axis.
- Its DESTINATION/MARKET reaches into MLB as a whole after notice — a different scale of market than either FA or trade offers.
- A release fee functions as COST/CONSIDERATION, but this piece doesn't cover specific rates or dollar figures.
- TIMING runs in two stages — application, then negotiation — and a failed negotiation reverts the player to his original club.
