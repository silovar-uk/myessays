# 二軍・育成選手・ファームは何のためにある？
## Getting a player is only the beginning.

Episode 5では、ドラフトを

**PICK → NEGOTIATION RIGHT → CONTRACT**

に分けた。

では、契約が成立したらteam buildingは完成するのか。

No.

A club still has to turn acquired talent into first-team performance.

選手を獲得したあとには、

**development / game reps / evaluation / registration / promotion**

という別の仕事が残っている。

> **Information basis: 2026-08-31**
>
> 2026年からNPBのfarm leagueはEast/Westの2リーグ制ではなく、1 league / 3 regionsになった。Current structureと昔の「二軍」イメージを混ぜずに読む。

## 1. SHORT ANSWER — Acquisition is the start, not the finish

この記事ではplayer developmentを、次のflowで読む。

**ACQUIRE**  
獲得する

↓

**DEVELOP**  
技術・身体・roleを伸ばす

↓

**PLAY**  
game repsを得る

↓

**EVALUATE**  
performanceと状態を見る

↓

**REGISTER**  
一軍で使えるregistration stateへ移す

↓

**PROMOTE / USE**  
一軍で役割を与える

これはNPB公式の制度フローそのものではない。

It is a learning model for reading a club's development system.

## 2. The key distinction — three different axes

ここが今回いちばん重要。

**支配下 / 育成**と、**一軍 / ファーム**は同じ分類ではない。

さらに、**出場選手登録 / 非登録**という別軸もある。

| Axis | Categories | What does it tell us? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract / registration status | 支配下 / 育成 | 選手登録上のstatus |
| Competition environment | 一軍 / farm | どのlevelでプレーするか |
| First-team eligibility now | 出場選手登録 / 非登録 | 今、一軍公式戦に出られる状態か |

This matters because:

**controlled player ≠ always first-team player**

and

**development player ≠ simply a second-team player.**

支配下選手もfarmでプレーする。

育成選手もfarm公式戦へ出場する。

ただし育成選手が一軍公式戦へ進むには、支配下へのtransitionが必要になる。

[NPB 2026 新規支配下選手登録](https://npb.jp/announcement/2026/pn_registered.html)

## 3. 支配下 is not the same as first team

2026年7月31日時点で、NPBは12球団合計836名の支配下登録選手を公示している。

[NPB 2026年度公示](https://npb.jp/announcement/2026/)

でもNPBは、それとは別に毎日、

**出場選手登録および登録抹消**

を公示する。

[NPB 出場選手登録・登録抹消](https://npb.jp/announcement/roster/)

つまり、

**belonging to the controlled roster**

と

**being active for the first team today**

は別である。

This is roster management.

球団は「誰を持っているか」だけでなく、

**who is usable now?**

をシーズン中ずっと選び続けている。

## 4. Active registration is another filter

2026年8月26日のNPB公示では、埼玉西武の出場選手一覧は31名だった。

[NPB 2026-08-26 roster](https://npb.jp/announcement/roster/roster_0826.html)

さらに登録抹消後にはre-registration restrictionがある。

7月20日に抹消された選手について、NPBは7月30日以後でなければ再登録できないと公示している。

[NPB 2026-07-20 roster moves](https://npb.jp/announcement/roster/roster_0720.html)

So the funnel gets narrower.

Club roster

↓

Current active registration

↓

Actual game usage

同じ「一軍」という言葉でも、どのlayerを話しているのか確認した方がいい。

## 5. 育成 is a registration status, not a place

2026年8月27日時点で、NPBの育成選手登録は230名。

[NPB 2026年度公示](https://npb.jp/announcement/2026/)

The word 育成 sounds like a location below the first team.

But it is better understood as a player-registration category.

NPBのファーム日本選手権要項でも、支配下選手と育成選手の両方が出場資格の対象に含まれている。

[NPB Farm Championship 2026](https://npb.jp/farmchamp/2026/information.html)

2026年シーズン中にも、多数の育成選手が支配下へ移行した。

So one possible transition is:

**DEVELOPMENT PLAYER → CONTROLLED PLAYER**

しかし、それでも自動的にfirst-team active rosterへ入るわけではない。

There is another decision after that.

## 6. The 2026 farm is no longer East vs West

2026年の大きなchange。

NPB farm baseball moved from:

**Eastern League / Western League**

into:

**one league / three regions.**

### East — 5

楽天 / オイシックス / ヤクルト / ロッテ / 日本ハム

### Central — 5

西武 / 巨人 / DeNA / ハヤテ / 中日

### West — 4

オリックス / 阪神 / 広島 / ソフトバンク

Total: **14 clubs**.

[NPB 2026 Farm schedule announcement](https://npb.jp/news/detail/20260122_01.html)

First-team NPB has 12 clubs.

Farm baseball has 14 participants.

That gap matters.

オイシックス新潟とハヤテ静岡がfarm competitionへ参加しているため、

**farm ≠ simply the reserve teams of the 12 top-level clubs.**

## 7. Even the number of farm games is different by club

2026 farm scheduling is also unusual if you expect the first-team model.

基本構造は、

**6 intra-region series → 2 inter-region series**

の反復。

しかし予定試合数はuniformではない。

Range: **135–146 games**.

Examples:

- Yakult: 135
- Seibu: 143
- Giants: 144
- Hanshin: 146

[NPB 2026 Farm schedule notes](https://npb.jp/farm/2026/schedule_note.html)

Episode 4で学んだ、

**schedule = architecture of competition**

をもう一度使える。

In the farm context, schedule is also an allocation of **game reps**.

誰と、どれくらい、何試合実戦を持てるか。

That becomes part of the development environment.

## 8. Farm does not mean rookies only

The farm is often described as a place for young players.

That is only part of the picture.

NPB's 2026 Farm Championship eligibility includes:

- rookies
- players who were never on the first-team active list that year
- some players who had first-team registration depending on usage criteria

[NPB Farm Championship 2026](https://npb.jp/farmchamp/2026/information.html)

Experienced players also appear in farm games.

Past NPB official results include players such as 宮城大弥, 西勇輝, 田口麗斗, 髙橋光成 and 大貫晋一 in farm competition.

[NPB 2024 March farm results](https://npb.jp/farm/2024/schedule_03_detail.html)  
[NPB 2024 July farm results](https://npb.jp/farm/2024/schedule_07_detail.html)

Current reporting also shows first-team players using farm games for adjustment and return to play.

So a better mental model is:

**development + adjustment + evaluation environment**

rather than

**a waiting room for young players.**

## 9. DEVELOPMENT PIPELINE — talent has to move through a system

Now connect the pieces.

### ACQUIRE

Draft, development draft, transfer, foreign-player signing, etc.

### DEVELOP

Improve skills, body, role understanding and tactical fit.

### PLAY

Get at-bats, innings and defensive reps.

### EVALUATE

Use performance and context to decide what comes next.

Episode 3 returns here:

**Metric = lens.**

### REGISTER

Some transitions are制度上のtransition.

育成 → 支配下。

支配下 → active first-team registration.

### PROMOTE / USE

Then the club still has to decide how to use the player.

Getting talent and converting talent are different capabilities.

## 10. BOTTLENECK — where does the system get stuck?

A development system should not be judged only by its input.

Ask where the pipeline gets stuck.

Possible bottlenecks:

- too little playing time
- too many players at the same position
- no available roster spot
- injuries
- mismatch between farm success and first-team role
- difficulty crossing the level gap

The point is not to declare one universal NPB problem.

The useful question is:

**Where is this player's transition being blocked?**

That is a much stronger question than:

“Was this a good draft pick?”

## 11. The farm itself is infrastructure

When NPB reviewed new farm-league applicants for 2024 participation, the criteria included:

- stadium and practice facilities
- dormitory / welfare facilities
- enough players to complete games
- managers, coaches and development staff
- financial sustainability
- governance

[NPB Farm League new-entry review](https://npb.jp/news/detail/20230929_03.html)

This changes the business lens.

Player development is not only what a coach says during practice.

It requires facilities, staff, games, operations, medical support, data and time.

**Player development is organizational capability.**

That capability can become part of competitive advantage.

## 12. Do not build a simple “best development team” ranking yet

Could we rank clubs by how many development players became controlled players?

Not safely.

Different clubs have different:

- numbers of development players
- draft inputs
- injury situations
- first-team depth
- positional needs
- available roster spots
- promotion opportunities

So one number is only one lens.

Episode 3 again:

**A metric shows something. It also misses something.**

A future analysis of development quality needs a proper metric design first.

## 13. Six questions for reading a Development System

1. **INPUT — Who enters?**  
   What talent did the club acquire?

2. **OPPORTUNITY — Where do they get game reps?**  
   Is there enough real competition?

3. **DEVELOPMENT — What is being improved?**  
   Skill, body, role, decision-making?

4. **EVALUATION — What counts as progress?**  
   Results only, or underlying quality too?

5. **TRANSITION — How do they move up?**  
   What registration or roster change is required?

6. **BOTTLENECK — Where does it get stuck?**  
   Playing time, role, injury, roster spot or level gap?

Now the question becomes:

**How does this club convert acquired talent into first-team capability?**

## 14. Takeaways

1. **支配下 / 育成 is not the same axis as 一軍 / farm.**
2. **Active first-team registration is another layer.**
3. **The 2026 farm is 14 clubs in one league and three regions.**
4. **Farm games are not only for rookies.** They also provide game reps, adjustment and evaluation opportunities.
5. **Acquisition and development are different team-building capabilities.**
6. **Look for bottlenecks, not only inputs.**
