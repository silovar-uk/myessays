# モーニング娘。史――なぜ同じ名前で30年近く続けられたのか
## 加入、卒業、リーダー、楽曲継承から読む「変わり続けること」の制度

### 要旨

Morning Musume began with five members in 1997. By 2026, none of those founders remain.

それどころか、「LOVEマシーン」が発売された1999年にまだ生まれていなかったメンバーが、現在ではその曲を歌っている。

Ordinarily, if every member of a group has been replaced, we start asking whether it is still the same group.

それでもモーニング娘。という名前は続いてきた。

The key is that Morning Musume became more than a collection of particular people. **It became a system for updating one continuing story by changing the people inside it.**

加入、卒業、リーダー交代、先輩から後輩への技術移転、過去曲の再演、そして2014年からの年号表記。

It did not preserve a finished group.

**It designed itself never to be finished.**

---

## 1. そもそも最初から「固定メンバー」ではなかった

モーニング娘。は1997年、『ASAYAN』のオーディションに落選した中澤裕子、石黒彩、飯田圭織、安倍なつみ、福田明日香の5人から始まった。

They sold 50,000 copies of the indie single “Ai no Tane” in five days and made their major debut with “Morning Coffee” on January 28, 1998.

At that point, they looked like an ordinary five-member newcomer group.

ところが、デビューからわずか数か月後に保田圭、矢口真里、市井紗耶香の3人が加入する。

Tsunku later described this second-generation addition and “Summer Night Town” as the first major turning point, noting how strange it seemed at the time to add three people to a five-member group.

ここでモーニング娘。は、一般的なグループとは違う道へ進んだ。

**Change the members, keep the name.**

That simple rule became one of the operating principles of the next three decades.

## 2. 「LOVEマシーン」はヒット曲である前に、世代交代の成功例だった

1999年、3期として13歳の後藤真希が加入する。

Soon after, “LOVE Machine” became the group's first million seller and pushed Morning Musume into the center of mass culture.

The important point is that this peak did not arrive after the original five had matured into a perfect fixed unit.

むしろ、人が増え、卒業が始まり、編成が揺れている最中に最大のヒットが出た。

The group learned early that **member change could create a new peak instead of merely weakening the brand.**

その後も4期、5期、6期と加入は続く。

辻希美、加護亜依、石川梨華、吉澤ひとみ。高橋愛、新垣里沙。藤本美貴、亀井絵里、道重さゆみ、田中れいな。

Because the name remained, each generation did not need to defeat the previous Morning Musume and start from zero.

**They could add a new chapter to an existing narrative.**

---

## 3. 卒業は「終わり」ではなく、次の編成を始める装置になった

The graduation system is one of Morning Musume's defining mechanisms.

本来、人気メンバーの離脱はグループにとって損失である。

安倍なつみ、後藤真希、辻希美、加護亜依、石川梨華、高橋愛、田中れいな、道重さゆみ、鞘師里保。

Again and again, a member left who seemed impossible to replace.

それでもグループは続いた。

One reason is that graduation became not an exceptional accident but an **officially staged transition with announcement, preparation and handover**.

卒業発表から最終公演までの期間には、本人の物語を締めると同時に、残るメンバーが次の役割を引き受ける時間が生まれる。

A graduation concert is therefore both a farewell ritual and an organizational handover ceremony.

ただし、この仕組みには痛みもある。

Fans repeatedly lose the person they came to support. Younger members inherit expectations, singing lines and leadership gaps.

**Morning Musume's continuity is built on repeated, institutionalized loss.**

---

## 4. リーダーは「一番人気」ではなく、記憶を次代へ渡す中継点

Morning Musume's leadership system also functions as a mechanism of continuity.

中澤裕子から始まり、飯田圭織、矢口真里、吉澤ひとみ、藤本美貴、高橋愛、新垣里沙、道重さゆみ、譜久村聖、生田衣梨奈へと引き継がれてきた。

After Erina Ikuta graduated in 2025, Miki Nonaka became leader and Sakura Oda became subleader.

What matters is that the leader does not have to be the biggest star of the generation.

リーダーの役割には、リハーサルで何を重視するか、過去曲をどう扱うか、後輩へどう伝えるか、MCで誰を前へ出すか、といった暗黙知の継承もある。

In organizational terms, the leader acts as a **culture-transfer node**.

2026年現在、野中美希は2014年加入の12期、小田さくらは2012年加入の11期。一方で2026年には18期が加入している。

Members separated by more than a decade of joining dates coexist inside the same group.

That overlap prevents a total break in institutional memory.

## 5. プラチナ期――売上が落ちても、技術は蓄積できる

The period around 2007–2010 later became known among fans as the “Platinum Era.”

黄金期ほどのテレビ露出やCD売上はなかった。

Yet a relatively stable lineup accumulated huge amounts of live experience, singing practice and group performance knowledge.

ここでモーニング娘。は一つ重要なことを証明した。

**A group can accumulate internal capability even when its mass popularity is no longer at its peak.**

「リゾナント ブルー」のような緊張感の強い楽曲は、黄金期の明るい大衆性とは違うモーニング娘。像を作った。

This does not mean the Platinum Era directly caused the later formation-dance era. But it did help move the evaluation axis from television personality toward collective stage performance.

---

## 6. 2011〜2014――「古い名前」を、新しい価値へ翻訳する

In 2011, the ninth and tenth generations entered in rapid succession and dramatically lowered the average age of the group.

鞘師里保というダンスの軸が生まれ、2012年「One・Two・Three」でEDMとフォーメーションダンスが前面に出る。

Tsunku later named three major turning points: “Summer Night Town,” “LOVE Machine” and “One・Two・Three.”

The interesting thing is that none of them simply reproduced an older successful formula.

**Each retranslated the name “Morning Musume” for a different lineup and a different era.**

2013年には3作連続週間1位、2014年には5作連続週間1位。

That was not a full return to the social scale of 1999. But it successfully gave outsiders a new reason to look at an old name.

---

## 7. 「'14」という年号は、変化をブランド名に埋め込んだ

From January 1, 2014, the group began adding the current year to its name: Morning Musume '14, '15, '16 and so on.

これは意外に大きな発明である。

The lineup, music and performance style of Morning Musume in 1999 were obviously different from those of 2014.

By adding the year, difference itself could become a feature rather than a defect.

**The name stays fixed, but this year's Morning Musume exists only this year.**

年号は、長寿グループ特有の「結局どの時代の話なのか分からない」という問題への実務的なインターフェースでもあった。

## 8. 過去曲は「遺産」ではなく、現役メンバーが使う共通言語

People alone cannot carry a thirty-year identity. Songs do part of the work.

「LOVEマシーン」「恋愛レボリューション21」「ザ☆ピ～ス！」「ここにいるぜぇ！」「みかん」「One・Two・Three」「わがまま 気のまま 愛のジョーク」。

Songs associated with departed members continue to be performed by later generations.

This is slightly different from an ordinary cover.

同じグループ名を持つ現役メンバーが歌うため、楽曲は「昔の人の曲」であると同時に「今のグループの曲」でもある。

The 2023 25th-anniversary best album even rerecorded songs such as “LOVE Machine (updated 23 Ver.),” “Renai Revolution 21 (updated 23 Ver.)” and “One・Two・Three (23 Ver.)” with the then-current lineup.

That turns repertoire into more than nostalgia.

**Old songs become training material, a live-performance protocol and an interface connecting generations of fans.**

## 9. 長期在籍は継承を強くする。しかし、新陳代謝を遅らせる

The system is not automatically healthy simply because it allows graduation and recruitment.

長期在籍メンバーが増えるほど、技術や文化の継承は安定する。一方で、後輩が中心へ移るまでの時間は長くなる。

Too much turnover weakens narrative continuity. Too little turnover slows renewal.

モーニング娘。は約30年、この振り子をずっと調整してきた。

So the real skill is not merely having a graduation system.

**It is continuously adjusting the speed of renewal.**

---

## 10. 2026年――18期が入り、また「未完成」に戻る

In 2025, Erina Ikuta graduated and Miki Nonaka became leader, with Sakura Oda as subleader.

2026年3月にはハロプロ研修生の杉原明紗が加入。

In June, Kenshusei members Hanano Ishikawa and Moa Suzuki joined together with 2025 audition winner Miyu Yasuda. The official site treats the 2026 newcomers as the 18th generation.

同じ6月24日には牧野真莉愛が卒業した。

As of August 2026, the official profile lists eleven members: Miki Nonaka, Sakura Oda, Homare Okamura, Mei Yamazaki, Rio Sakurai, Haruka Inoue, Ako Yumigeta, Meisa Sugihara, Miyu Yasuda, Moa Suzuki and Hanano Ishikawa.

1997年の5人とは、一人も重ならない。

Yet it is still Morning Musume.

That is possible because the organization does not replace everyone at once. Senior and junior tenures overlap; leadership is handed down; old songs are sung by new voices; the stable name is paired with a changing year.

**There are no shared original members, but there are overlapping transfer mechanisms.**

## 11. モーニング娘。は「テセウスの船」なのか

The Ship of Theseus asks whether a ship remains the same ship after every plank has gradually been replaced.

モーニング娘。は、ほとんどその問いをそのまま実演している。

But Morning Musume adds one twist.

**It institutionalized being the same and different at the same time.**

名前は残す。メンバーは変える。

Songs remain. Voices change.

歴史は残す。年号は毎年変える。

The long-term design separates **fixed parts from variable parts**.

That separation is one of the deepest reasons the group can survive replacement.

---

## 12. 結論――継承とは、同じものを守ることではない

If we compress nearly thirty years of Morning Musume into one principle, it is this:

**継承とは、同じ人を残すことではなく、次の人が同じ名前を引き受けられる仕組みを残すこと。**

The group did not preserve its original members, its Golden Era sound, its Platinum lineup or even formation dance as a permanent final form.

代わりに、オーディション、卒業、在籍期間の重なり、リーダー継承、楽曲再演、年号表記という更新手順を残した。

That is why Morning Musume in 2026 is not the same as Morning Musume in 1997.

**And because it is not the same, it has been able to continue under the same name.**

Turning that contradiction into an operating system may be the group's greatest invention.
