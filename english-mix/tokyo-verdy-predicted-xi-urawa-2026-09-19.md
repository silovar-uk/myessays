# 今日の東京V予想スタメン11人を、名前だけで終わらせない

## 前節と同じ3-4-2-1を予想。公式プロフィールと今季データから、浦和戦で「誰をどう見るか」を一人ずつ知る

On September 19, 2026, Urawa Reds face Tokyo Verdy at Saitama Stadium. キックオフは18時30分。At noon, the official starting XI has not been announced yet.

So the eleven players below are a **predicted XI**, not confirmed starters. 9月18日0時39分時点の情報を基にした予想では、東京Vは前節・千葉戦と同じ11人を継続する3-4-2-1。The predicted names match the previous official XI 11 for 11.

The predicted shape is as follows.

- GK：マテウス
- 3CB：井上竜太、林尚輝、鈴木海音
- WB／中央：溝口修平、齋藤功佑、平川怜、新井悠太
- 2シャドー：福田湧矢、平尾勇人
- CF：染野唯月

## マテウス｜「安定感」を武器と言い切るGKが、最後尾の基準点

Matheus was born in 1993 and is 190cm tall. ブラジルのコリンチャンスのアカデミーを経て、2020年から東京Vでプレーしている。He experienced the 2023 promotion playoff final and then played all 38 J1 league games in both 2024 and 2025. He has watched a large part of Verdy's recent history from the goalmouth.

On his official profile, he names **安定感, stability**, as his main weapon. It sounds almost too plain. でもGKの「安定感」はかなり全部入りに近い。It requires saves, positioning, decisions, cross management, and communication to stay reliable week after week.

He has started all seven league matches this season. 東京Vは今季のアウェーリーグ戦、岡山戦とC大阪戦をともに0-0で終え、まだ敵地で失点していない。If Urawa spend long periods attacking, do not watch only for spectacular saves. Watch how Matheus handles crosses and controls the space behind the back three. That is where “stability” becomes visible.

## 鈴木海音｜林とのパス交換が多い、3バックの接続役

Kaito Suzuki was born in 2002 and is 182cm tall. ジュビロ磐田の育成組織からトップへ上がり、栃木SCへの育成型期限付き移籍を経て、2025年に東京Vへ完全移籍した。He was also part of Japan's U-23 setup around the 2024 AFC U23 Asian Cup.

In his JFA profile, Suzuki names **interceptions and covering** as his strengths. ボールを持ったときの派手さより、相手の次のプレーを先に読み、危険な場所を消すタイプとして見ると分かりやすい。

But this season his role is not only defensive. Football LAB's pass-exchange data shows a high volume of passes from Hayashi to Suzuki, making him an important receiver in the back-three circulation. 「何回目立ったか」より、**林から受けて、次にどこへ動かすか**を見る。A centre-back can do excellent work while barely appearing in the highlight reel. Football sometimes has a deeply inconvenient evaluation system.

## 林尚輝｜「守備」が武器なのに、今季2得点でチーム得点王

Naoki Hayashi was born in 1998 and is 182cm tall. 大阪体育大学から鹿島アントラーズへ入り、東京Vへの期限付き移籍を経て定着した。His official profile gives one wonderfully direct answer for his main weapon: **守備, defending**.

And yet, in the 2026/27 league season, he has scored twice in six appearances. チーム総得点が3なので、9月14日時点では林が単独のチーム得点王。Against Chiba on September 13, he scored the 90+2 equaliser. His registration says DF, his strength says defence, and the scoreboard says top scorer. The departments are clearly not sharing one spreadsheet.

Still, “goal-scoring centre-back” is not the full picture. Football LAB also shows Hayashi heavily involved in passing with Suzuki and Inoue. 浦和戦で見るべきはセットプレーだけではない。Watch **who he feeds from the middle of the back three, and how far he steps forward to defend**. The goals are loud; the foundation is 90 minutes long.

## 井上竜太｜Jデビューの相手が東京V。今はその東京Vで3CBを組む

Ryota Inoue was born in 2000 in Saitama and stands 186cm tall. 立正大学からツエーゲン金沢、ブラウブリッツ秋田を経て、2025年夏に東京Vへ加わった。He describes his strengths as **one-on-one defending and joining the attack**, a useful combination for an outside centre-back.

There is a nice loop in his career history. His J.League debut came on February 19, 2023, when he played for Kanazawa away to Tokyo Verdy at Ajinomoto Stadium. プロのリーグ戦が始まった相手が、数年後の所属クラブになった。His CV had foreshadowing.

This season, passes from Inoue to Hayashi rank high in Verdy's internal pass network. つまり「対人で止める」だけでなく、奪ったあと中央へつなぎ直して、チームをもう一度前へ向けるところまで仕事。Against Urawa, watch **the space behind him immediately after he steps out to challenge**. That moment shows not only Inoue's judgement, but how the entire back three works together.

## 新井悠太｜166cmの「推進力」が、WBから試合を前へ運ぶ

Yuta Arai was born in 2003 and is 166cm tall. 前橋育英高校、東洋大学を経て東京Vでプレーし、2025年のJ1では37試合に出場した。On his official profile, he names **推進力, forward drive**, as his main weapon.

If you see 166cm and immediately file him under “small technical player,” you miss the point. Football LAB has him third on the team in attacking CBP, first in cross CBP, and second in dribble CBP. 前節の千葉戦では右WBで先発し、縦へ運ぶ役割を担った。

Do not count only pretty touches. **ボールを受けた地点より、プレーが終わった地点がどれだけ前か**を見る。Carry outside, cut inside, draw a defender, release the next player. Those movements are what “forward drive” looks like. Wing-backs are often reduced to running distance; with Arai, watch the direction of that running.

## 齋藤功佑｜「走力・予測・技術」の3語で、中盤の仕事がほぼ説明できる

Kosuke Saito was born in 1997 and developed at Yokohama FC. 2023年に東京Vへ加入し、J1昇格後の2024年は38試合、2025年は37試合に出場した。In this predicted XI, he partners Rei Hirakawa in central midfield.

His self-described weapons are **走力、予測、技術 — running, anticipation, technique**. It looks like a greedy three-item set, but midfield asks for all three. 走れるだけでは遅れ、読めるだけでは届かず、触れるだけでは前へ進まない。The position punishes any missing piece.

Saito started in central midfield against Chiba. 東京Vは森田晃樹の負傷離脱もあり、中盤の組み合わせを調整している。Against Urawa, pay attention not only when Saito has the ball, but **where he moves immediately after Verdy lose it**. Does he close the lane, win it, or slow the attack? Anticipation is often clearest in the few seconds when he never touches the ball.

## 平川怜｜ラストパス15本。中央で「次の一手」を作る

Rei Hirakawa was born in 2000. FC東京の育成組織からプロになり、鹿児島、松本、熊本、磐田を経て東京Vへ加入した。The club history reads a little like a J.League transfer map, but that variety of environments matters for a midfielder who must read many different situations.

He lists **technique and duels** as his strengths. 今季はリーグ7試合すべてに出場し、Football LABではラストパス15本でチーム最多。Zero goals and zero assists look quiet, but the player who most often plays the final pass before a shot has a different kind of footprint.

Against Urawa, do not wait only for one decisive through ball. **攻撃の向きを変える一つ前のパス**にも注目したい。If one side is blocked, switch it; if the forward lane closes, recycle. A central midfielder creates value not only by finding brilliant answers, but by abandoning bad attacks early.

## 溝口修平｜DF登録でも武器は「チャンスメイク」。1得点1アシストがそれを裏づける

Shuhei Mizoguchi was born in 2004 and is left-footed. 鹿島アントラーズの育成組織からトップへ上がり、今季東京Vでプレーしている。His first J.League goal came on August 9, 2026, against Kawasaki Frontale in Verdy's league opener.

He is officially registered as a defender, but his self-described weapon is **チャンスメイク, chance creation**. That alone tells you not to read the registration too literally. Football LAB has him at one goal and one assist, second on the team in attacking CBP, and second with seven last passes.

Do not wait only for a left-footed cross. **どの高さで受けるか、相手をどこまで押し下げるか、内側の味方にどんな角度を作るか**まで見る。His registration says DF, but the job description points forward. In football, the position label can sometimes feel more like a department name than an occupation.

## 福田湧矢｜0得点0アシストでも攻撃CBP首位。「数字がない」を雑に読まない

Yuya Fukuda was born in 1999. ガンバ大阪で長くプレーし、2025年から東京Vに所属している。His official answer for his main weapon is simple: **dribbling**. In this predicted XI, he operates close to Someno as one of the two attacking midfielders.

As of Football LAB's September 14 update, Fukuda has six appearances, zero goals, and zero assists. それでも攻撃CBP9.66でチーム1位、ドリブルCBP3.36も1位。He has also taken ten shots, second-most on the team. The score column looks empty; the process column is busy.

One caution: CBP is Football LAB's own metric, not a universal “ability rating.” パス、クロス、ドリブルなど、チャンス構築への寄与を定量化した指標として読むのがよい。そのうえで福田を見るなら、**何人抜いたかより、ドリブル後に守備の配置を何人分ずらしたか**を見る。Goals and assists are outcomes; deforming the opponent's shape is work that happens before them.

## 平尾勇人｜日本大在学中でJ1のシャドー候補。肩書きと仕事場の距離がすごい

Hayato Hirao was born in 2005. セレッソ大阪U-15、四日市中央工業高校を経て、現在は日本大学に在学している。He is already committed to join Tokyo Verdy in 2027, and this season he can play for the first team as a JFA/J.League Special Designated Player.

He started the previous league match against Chiba, and Football LAB lists four league appearances this season. 大学在学中で、今日の予想勤務地が埼玉スタジアム。That CV has unusual pressure. But the special designation is not a sightseeing pass: eligible players can appear in official J.League matches while staying registered with their university or school.

After scoring his first J.League goal in 2025, Hirao said that attacking the box for crosses was one of his characteristics and finishing there was a weapon. 染野が中央で収め、福田やWBが相手を動かしたとき、**最後にペナルティーエリアへもう一人入ってくる役**として見ると分かりやすい。His run before touching the ball can be the clearest introduction to who he is.

## 染野唯月｜今季12本0得点。それでも浦和相手には今年2戦連発

Itsuki Someno was born in 2001 and is 181cm tall. 鹿島アントラーズつくばJrユース、尚志高校、鹿島を経て東京Vへ。His official profile lists **post play and shooting** as his main weapons. In the current 3-4-2-1, he is the central striker.

He has appeared in all seven league matches this season. Football LAB records a team-high 12 shots but zero goals, plus one assist. 同サイトの浦和戦プレビューも、今季のポストプレー向上と、まだ得点がない点を同時に挙げている。So “a striker who has not scored” is true, but incomplete.

There is also an awkward fact for Urawa. In the 2026 special-format league, Someno scored the winner in Tokyo V's 1-0 win on March 14 and scored a penalty in the 1-1 match on April 12. 今季リーグの0得点と、今年の浦和戦2試合連続得点が同居する。The sample is small, so this is not proof of a special matchup. Still, watch **who he lays the ball off to while holding off a centre-back**, not only the moment he shoots. That is often where Verdy's attack begins.

## 11人をまとめて見ると、東京Vは「登録ポジション」と「仕事」が少しずれたチーム

Put the eleven players together and a pattern appears. 東京Vは「誰がエースか」を一人決めるより、**登録ポジションと実際の仕事が少しずつずれていること**が面白い。

Hayashi is a registered defender but the current top scorer with two goals. Mizoguchi is a registered defender with one goal, one assist, and heavy chance-creation involvement. 0得点0アシストの福田が攻撃CBP首位で、平川は0アシストでもラストパス15本。Someno has no league goal yet but remains the reference point up front, while university student Hirao attacks the box around him.

This does not mean “numbers are unreliable.” むしろ、**一つの数字だけでは仕事の途中までしか見えない**という話だ。Goals, assists, CBP, last passes, self-described strengths, and positioning each reveal a different layer. Stack them together and the selection starts to make more sense.

From an Urawa viewing perspective, you do not need to track only Someno. 福田が運び、平川が向きを変え、WBが高い位置を取り、林がセットプレーで前へ出る。Verdy's goal total is low, but the jobs before the goal are distributed across the eleven.

Once the official lineup is announced, that becomes the correct reference. 予想が外れても、この11人を知っておく価値は残る。Some may enter later, and the profiles still show the roles Verdy are trying to connect.

Do not memorize eleven names before kickoff. **11個の「見るポイント」**を持っておく。Then, once the game starts, names begin to turn into people.
