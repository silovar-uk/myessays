# 試作品は動いた。Can you make 10,000?
## Deep Techの本当の難しさ――From the lab to the real world

Imagine a small research lab. A scientist makes a remarkable new material. The prototype works. Everyone applauds. Then a potential customer raises a hand: **“Great. Can you deliver 10,000 units by next month?”**

研究室で一つ作れたとしても、同じ品質のものを一万個作れるとは限らない。And even if production works, customers may not buy it. これは実話ではなく、考えるための**hypothetical thought experiment（架空の思考実験）**だ。

So what makes deep tech “deep”? 「技術が難しい」だけでは説明しきれない。The real challenge often appears when scientific discovery must become a reliable, affordable and usable product. This article compares primary sources, challenges its own assumptions, and tests a simple production thought experiment. 情報確認日：2026年10月18日。

## 1. そもそも「ディープテック」とは何なのか

There is no single universal definition. 欧州イノベーション会議（European Innovation Council, EIC）は、cutting-edge science, technology and engineeringに根差し、研究現場と継続してつながる変革的な技術を重視する。It distinguishes deep tech from “high tech,” which is often described by R&D intensity. [EIC definition](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-frequently-asked-questions/faqs-eic-pre-accelerator_en).

日本の新エネルギー・産業技術総合開発機構（New Energy and Industrial Technology Development Organization, NEDO）は、長い研究開発期間、大きな資金、高いリスク、社会課題への寄与を重視して支援対象を説明する。[NEDO official programme](https://www.nedo.go.jp/activities/ZZJP_100250.html).

The distinction matters: **scientific novelty and commercial value are not the same thing.** A useful reservation app built with existing algorithms may create great customer value without a new scientific breakthrough. 新しい光学センサーには科学的な価値があっても、まだ買い手がいないことがある。Neither example is automatically a better business.

![Three gates from research to society](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/deep-tech-three-gates-2026.svg)

*図1：The three gates—technology, production and adoption. 筆者作図。これは公式なディープテック認定基準ではなく、分析のためのモデル。*

## 2. この言葉はいつからあるのか。技術そのものは、もっと古い

The label is relatively new; the phenomenon is not. 「ディープテック」の現代的な用法は、2014年にスワティ・チャトゥルヴェディ（Swati Chaturvedi）が使ったと紹介される。ただし、this attribution does not prove the very first use of the phrase. [History of the term](https://whatisdeeptech.com/about/) / [Industry overview](https://www.infoworld.com/article/2338478/what-is-deep-tech-life-after-consumer-apps.html).

Consider semiconductors. In 1947, John Bardeen and Walter Brattain developed an early transistor at Bell Telephone Laboratories. In 1958, Jack Kilby demonstrated an integrated circuit; in 1959, Robert Noyce developed an important planar circuit concept. [Nobel Prize educational resource](https://educationalgames.nobelprize.org/educational/physics/transistor/history/index.html) / [Japanese semiconductor history archive](https://www.shmj.or.jp/integred-circuits/index.html).

部品一つの発明と、多数の部品を小さく安定してつなぐ技術は別の問題だった。The integrated circuit changed not only what electronics could do, but **how components could be manufactured at scale**. 新しい名前より先に、地道な製造上の突破が存在していた。

## 3. 量子だけではない。分野名で分類すると外す理由

Quantum computing, biotechnology, new materials, fusion, robotics, optics, semiconductors and space technology are common examples. 欧州委員会は、物理・生物・デジタルの領域を組み合わせる革新にも注目する。[European Commission innovation agenda](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A52022DC0332).

But a sector label does not prove a scientific breakthrough. 「AIを使う」だけで、必ず新しい基礎技術があるとは言えない。The better questions are: What new scientific or engineering knowledge does the product require? What makes it difficult to reproduce? And what prevents real-world adoption? **Classify the uncertainties, not just the industry name.** この三つは筆者の評価軸であり、公的な統一分類ではない。

## 4. 技術が完成したかを測る九段階と、そこで測れないもの

Technology Readiness Level (TRL) is a nine-level framework developed by the US National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA). It describes technological maturity, from observing basic principles at level 1 to proven operational use at level 9. [NASA introduction](https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/somd/space-communications-navigation-program/technology-readiness-levels/) / [Formal NASA definitions](https://nodis3.gsfc.nasa.gov/displayDir.cfm?Internal_ID=N_PR_7123_001D_&page_name=AppendixE).

ざっくり言えば、１～３が原理や概念、４～６が試験環境での検証、７～９が運用環境での実証に近い。ただし分野ごとに判定条件は異なる。Here's the trap: **TRL is not a customer-demand score or a profitability score.** 技術成熟度が高くても、顧客が支払う価格、供給費用、保守の成立は未確認かもしれない。

## 5. 90％の成功が10回続くと、なぜ35％しか残らないのか

Let's overthink a very small number. Imagine ten production steps, each with a 90% pass rate. Assume outcomes are independent and there is no rework. For 100 units, the expected number passing every step is **100 × 0.9¹⁰ ≈ 34.9**.

![Illustrative 90 percent yield across ten steps](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/deep-tech-yield-experiment-2026.svg)

*図2：A mathematical thought experiment, not an industry benchmark or measured manufacturing result.*

「各工程は九割成功」と言われたら安心しそうなのに、10工程を通せば約35個。Real factories may have correlated failures, inspections and repair loops, so this simple equation is not a general factory forecast. But it reveals a real conceptual issue: **a successful experiment and a successful chain of operations are different achievements**. ここでの歩留まり（yield）とは、投入に対して良品が得られる割合。量産は単なる複写ではない。

## 6. 「大金がかかる」の中身は、設備購入だけではない

Buying a bigger machine does not automatically solve everything. What about raw material quality, equipment calibration, staff skills, inspection costs, field repairs and alternative suppliers? 試作品一つの製作費だけを見ても、生産した製品の原価や安定供給力は分からない。

A serious production plan separates initial capital expenditure, yield, cost per usable unit, maintenance, capacity and lead time. 欧州投資銀行（European Investment Bank, EIB）の2025年調査も、試験・開発・規模拡大を支える技術基盤と資金を検討している。[EIB technology infrastructure report](https://www.eib.org/en/publications/20250208-unlocking-innovation-addressing-the-funding-needs-of-eu-technology-infrastructures).

**Owning an invention is different from being able to supply it reliably.** 特許を持っていることも、安く安全に作れることの証拠にはならない。

## 7. 医療機器では「動いた」の後に、人間の安全が現れる

Medical devices make the distinction sharper. The US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) describes stages including discovery, preclinical studies, regulatory pathways, review and post-market safety monitoring. [FDA device development process](https://www.fda.gov/patients/learn-about-drug-and-device-approvals/device-development-process).

米国の医療機器はすべて同じ許可を要するわけではなく、risk classification and intended use affect the regulatory path. [FDA market guidance](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/device-advice-comprehensive-regulatory-assistance/how-study-and-market-your-device). A prototype may work and still require evidence on safety, clinical performance and correct use. **Technically possible does not automatically mean safe or legally marketable.** 現実の人へ使うための検証は、技術実証とは別の責任を伴う。

## 8. 投資家はなぜ資金を出しにくいのか

Some software companies can test demand with a relatively cheap early product. 素材や医療技術では、顧客が判断できる状態になる前に、設備や試験への支出が必要な場合がある。Costs arrive before decisive market evidence.

Investors must consider technology risk, production risk, regulation, customer adoption and the next funding round. 資金消費率（burn rate）は現金が減る速度、資金余力（runway）はその条件で活動を続けられる期間の目安。

A 2024 EIB study found that EU scale-ups in its sample had raised roughly half as much capital by age ten as comparable San Francisco companies. **Important limitation:** that is a study of innovative growth firms broadly, not a deep-tech-only failure or success statistic. [EIB scale-up gap](https://www.eib.org/en/publications/online/all/the-scale-up-gap). Technology value and investability are related, but they are not identical.

## 9. 日本の支援制度は、何を「進捗」としているのか

Japan's NEDO has a Deep-Tech Startups Support Program (DTSU) with three phases: early commercialization R&D (STS), later commercialization R&D (PCA), and mass-production demonstration (DMP). 2026年10月5日更新資料によると、DTSUの事業期間総額は877億円（2023～2032年度）、別の環境転換分野向け事業は378.7億円（2026年４月時点総額）。[NEDO official record](https://www.nedo.go.jp/activities/ZZJP_100250.html).

It lists **119 selected companies** in the first nine DTSU rounds and **19 selected companies** in the first six rounds of the separate environmental programme. 採択は支援を受ける対象に選ばれたという意味であり、商用化に成功したという意味ではない。A grant, a patent, a prototype, a paid contract and positive operating profit must be tracked as different milestones.

## 10. 「ディープテック」という呼び方への三つの批判

**First: definition inflation.** 「難しそうなAI企業」から本格的な材料研究まで、同じ言葉で呼ばれる可能性がある。BCG and Hello Tomorrow used patent and publication evidence in one 2019 identification method, but that is not a universal standard. [BCG methodology](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2019/infographic-deep-dive-into-deep-tech-investing).

**Second: technology-first thinking.** A groundbreaking technology does not prove that customers need the product. 既存技術の安い解決策が十分なら、科学的に先進的であるだけでは勝てない。

**Third: social and ethical costs.** Public funding has opportunity costs. 軍事や監視にも転用できる技術では、用途によって評価が変わり得る。Who receives the returns from publicly supported research? Who carries safety and environmental burdens? **The label “deep tech” should begin questions, not stop them.**

## 11. 思考実験：本当に「深い技術」である必要はあるか

Imagine two imaginary sports-motion analysis companies. Company A uses standard cameras and existing image processing. Company B develops a new optical measurement principle. 利用者が必要とするのが簡単な練習改善なら、Aで十分かもしれない。If the use case requires precision that existing technology cannot deliver, B's new science may matter enormously.

The questions become practical: What is impossible with current tools? What decision changes when accuracy improves? Who pays for that improvement? Is there a cheaper alternative? どちらが優れた企業かを技術の新しさだけで決めない。

The counterintuitive suggestion: **Before investing heavily in new technology, test the customer problem using existing tools or even a manual service.** 新技術を使わない実験の方が、必要な新技術の条件を明確にする場合がある。

## 12. 調査を三人の担当に分けると、見落としが減る

**The assumption challenger（前提を疑う担当）:** Is a new scientific breakthrough truly necessary? Could current tools solve the problem? 補助金なしの採算はどうか、見込客は実際の購入者か、と問う。

**The primary-source researcher（一次資料を調べる担当）:** Gather original papers, patent claims, test conditions, production records, cost estimates, customer contracts, regulatory documents and cash-flow statements. 特許件数だけで技術的な優位性を判断しない。

**The experiment designer（検証手順を作る担当）:** Identify the most dangerous uncertainty, select the cheapest informative test, define success and stop criteria, and assign an owner. 技術試験と実際の購買行動という異なる証拠を、両方揃えることが重要になる。

## 13. 追加調査計画を立てて、定義をもう一度疑う

The second research pass tested five questions: Is the definition consistent? Does TRL prove commercialization? Is manufacturing a separate challenge? Is the financing gap deep-tech-specific? Do all medical devices follow the same regulatory path?

再調査では、[EIC](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-frequently-asked-questions/faqs-eic-pre-accelerator_en)と[NEDO](https://www.nedo.go.jp/activities/ZZJP_100250.html)の定義は着眼点が違い、[NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/somd/space-communications-navigation-program/technology-readiness-levels/)のTRLは市場性を示さず、[EIB](https://www.eib.org/en/publications/online/all/the-scale-up-gap)の数値は分野限定ではなく、[FDA](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/device-advice-comprehensive-regulatory-assistance/how-study-and-market-your-device)の規制経路は一つではないことを確認した。

This changed the argument. The three gates are a **useful analytical model, not an official definition of deep tech**. すべての技術が量産を要するわけではなく、一般的な事業にも研究以外の障壁はある。A model becomes more useful when it includes its own limits.

## 14. 実務で試すなら、最初の30日で何を見るか

**Week 1:** Write down the new technical claim, the customer problem and the best existing alternative. 技術責任者と購買責任者へ別々に話を聞き、技術の証拠と予算の証拠を分ける。

**Week 2:** Test repeatability and customer behavior. Record failures as carefully as successes. 「欲しい」という言葉だけでなく、有償試験や具体的な購入条件を確認する。

**Week 3:** Estimate production or reliable service delivery, including yield, equipment, materials, maintenance and cost per unit. 第３週で初めて生産可能性の仮説を数字にするのではなく、それまでのデータと突き合わせる。

**Week 4:** Decide to continue, redesign or stop. Apply four tests: technical evidence, delivery evidence, buyer evidence and funding evidence. 安全性や倫理、法令順守が重要な分野では追加の専門審査が必要。The goal is not to launch deep tech in 30 days; it is to discover **which crucial unknowns remain**.

## 15. 知っておくと資料が読みやすくなる用語集

- **Deep tech（ディープテック）:** Frontier science or engineering-based innovation; no single worldwide definition.
- **R&D（研究開発）:** Research and development, from building knowledge to developing processes and products.
- **Basic research（基礎研究）:** Work to uncover principles without requiring an immediate product.
- **Applied research（応用研究）:** Research directed toward concrete uses.
- **Technology transfer（技術移転）:** Moving research results into practical use, including licenses and collaborations.
- **Spinout（研究室発の新会社）:** An independent venture built around research capabilities or results.
- **Proof of concept / PoC（概念実証）:** A small test of feasibility, not necessarily proof of demand.
- **TRL / Technology Readiness Level（技術成熟度）:** A nine-level technology evidence framework, not a business-success measure.
- **Yield（歩留まり）:** The share of inputs that emerge as acceptable outputs.
- **Manufacturability（製造可能性）:** Ability to produce repeatedly with acceptable quality and cost.
- **Scale-up（規模拡大）:** Expansion of production, service capacity or company operations, depending on context.
- **Feasibility study（事業化可能性調査）:** Structured assessment of technical and commercial feasibility.
- **Intellectual property（知的財産）:** Legally protected knowledge and creations; a patent is not automatic permission to use a technology.
- **Exit（投資回収）:** Recovering investment through sales of shares, listing or other transactions.
- **Burn rate / runway（資金消費率／資金余力）:** How fast cash is consumed / how long current cash might last.
- **Stage gate（関門審査）:** Evidence-based decision on whether to fund the next development stage.
- **Valley of death（死の谷）:** The difficult stretch between research and viable practical use.

## 16. 最初の「一万個ください」は、案外いい質問だった

Back to the lab. The invention is still real. Nobody has taken the scientific achievement away. But the customer’s request reveals a new set of problems: reliability, safety, cost, logistics and actual use.

「同じものを作れますか」「安全に使えますか」「誰が払いますか」。Simple questions, hard answers. What makes deep tech difficult is not just the complexity of the science. It is **the distance between discovering something and making it work for people repeatedly**.

The surprising part is how ordinary the bridge looks: test records, manufacturing methods, contracts, independent evidence and cash. 深い技術の社会実装を支えるのは、ときに驚くほど地味な仕事なのだ。
