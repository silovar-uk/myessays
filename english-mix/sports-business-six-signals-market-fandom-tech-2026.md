# スポーツ市場は伸びているのに、関心層は減っている
## Six signals from autumn 2026: spending, fandom, participation, rules and technology

At a stadium, people spend more on food and merchandise. Digital sports subscriptions are growing. Yet the share of people who say they like sports is shrinking. **Is the sports business growing—or are we measuring different things?** 2026年秋のsix storiesは、この問いを考える好例だ。

They cover different layers of the industry: consumer spending, fan relationships, exercise habits, competition rules, equipment design and grassroots data. 共通の問いは、**who creates value, who pays, and which behavior produces that value?** ただし、母集団も単位も違うため、数字は安易に足さない。

This essay was researched as of 2026年10月14日. Primary reports, secondary reporting and our own hypotheses are explicitly separated. The Athletic article and the JSAA event description could not be fully opened in the research environment; we do not invent their missing details.

## 1. 「8,743億円増加」の前に、何を市場と呼ぶか決める

On October 7, 三菱UFJリサーチ＆コンサルティング and マクロミル released the 23rd スポーツマーケティング基礎調査. 調査は2026年8月26～28日に、15～69歳のインターネット利用者2,000人へ実施。 Estimated stadium-spectator spending reached **8,743億円 (+13.9%)**; sports-related media spending reached **3,192億円**, a series high. [Announcement](https://www.murc.jp/news/news_release/news_release_261007/) / [Survey PDF](https://prtimes.jp/a/?f=d20489-189-ef5a1f4ce57e73c82136fe6bc421d26a.pdf)

まず定義を確認しよう。**Stadium spending is not club ticket revenue.** 観戦消費の総額は、クラブの収入そのものではない。 交通費、飲食、グッズ、記念品も含んでいる。Spending per spectator averaged 57,397円 annually and 13,106円 per visit. One-visit breakdown: ticket 4,322円, transport 3,283円, food 2,876円, merchandise 1,566円, memorabilia 1,059円. 電車代は、乗客がサポーターでもクラブの売上にはならない。

現地観戦率は19.6%から20.0%へ微増にとどまる一方、観戦経験者の年間観戦回数は4.2回から4.7回に増えた。 Per-visit food spending rose 15.7%; merchandise spending grew 21.9%. **More visits and more spending per visit** explain the pattern more plausibly than a massive rise in the number of first-time fans. But nominal spending may also reflect prices and changing consumer composition.

対照的なのが、スポーツ参加関連の支出だ。Total spending was **1兆6,999億円 (−0.9%)**. Equipment spending fell 21.3% to 2,841億円; facilities and membership spending fell 7.6% to 5,415億円. The sports-interest group also shrank to 63.7%. A booming spectator segment is not the same as a booming whole sector. [Report pp. 2–3](https://prtimes.jp/a/?f=d20489-189-ef5a1f4ce57e73c82136fe6bc421d26a.pdf)

Importantly, these are estimates from spending rates, average outlays and age-group populations—not a ledger of club P&Ls. **Market size and addressable revenue are different things.** A serious business case begins by tracing money to its recipient.

## 2. 3,192億円のメディア市場は、放映権の総額ではない

Within the 3,192億円 media market, paid internet streaming accounts for **1,154億円 (36.2%)**—about triple its 2020 level. Usage stands at 10.7%, while paid television usage is 7.4%. But these are measures on the consumer side, **not the total rights fees paid to leagues**. [Report pp. 5–6](https://prtimes.jp/a/?f=d20489-189-ef5a1f4ce57e73c82136fe6bc421d26a.pdf)

調査上のメディア市場には、書籍・雑誌、映像ソフト、有料放送、スポーツゲームソフトなども含まれる。 無料配信の広告収入、リーグの放映権販売、映像制作会社の請負売上をそのまま足せる市場ではない。When someone buys a streaming subscription, the streaming platform, rights holder and production contractor each have different revenue models.

The strategic question is therefore not merely, “How many streams can we generate?” It is **who owns the customer relationship, usage data, editable footage and secondary-use permissions**. Monetization follows the contractual architecture, not just the number of videos.

## 3. ファンの熱量は、人口とは別の商売をつくる

「スポーツファン調査2027」は2027年に実施された調査ではない。The research was conducted in July **2026**, with 2027 in the report title. ファンベースカンパニー and インテージ surveyed professional baseball, J1 football, League One rugby and B.PREMIER basketball fans, combining broad screening with a team-level survey of at least light fans. [Release](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000029.000055363.html) / [Free 27-page report](https://www.fanbasecompany.com/fansoken/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/sportsfan_freereport2027.pdf)

重要なのは、**recommendation intention（推奨意向）と retention intention（継続意向）は別物**という点。 Across the four leagues, over 60% of light-or-stronger fans would recommend the league. But the share intending to remain fans differs: baseball 80.9%, Jリーグ 67.4%, リーグワン 64.4%, Bリーグ 60.6%. Sharing enthusiasm and maintaining a lifelong attachment are related, but not interchangeable. [Report p. 10](https://www.fanbasecompany.com/fansoken/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/sportsfan_freereport2027.pdf)

Sponsorship is even easier to misread. **Among respondents who recognized their team's sponsor**, those who reported intentionally purchasing or using a sponsor's products or services represented 48.5% in baseball, 55.6% in football, 61.1% in rugby and 56.3% in basketball. The share saying sponsor recognition increased purchase intention ranged from 50.0% to 60.6%. これは「国民の6割がスポンサー商品を購入」とは違うし、survey-based attribution is not causal proof of incremental sales. [Report p. 22](https://www.fanbasecompany.com/fansoken/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/sportsfan_freereport2027.pdf)

Here lies a practical opportunity: move beyond logo exposure. A sponsor can create a relevant fan experience, a redeemable benefit, or a product trial. Then measure awareness, preference, purchase and repeat purchase as **separate stages**, instead of calling them all “sponsorship effect.”

## 4. 運動したい人の多さより、始められない理由に市場がある

ロイヤリティマーケティング surveyed 1,000 people aged 20–69 on September 2–3, 2026. **40.9% currently exercise**, while **60.4% want to increase their opportunities or time for exercise**. Walking leads among active exercisers at 59.2%; strength training is at 39.6%. [Original report](https://biz.loyalty.co.jp/report/207/)

ただし運動したい意思が、そのまま有料サービスの購買意思になるわけではない。 Men who wanted to exercise but had not started most often cited a **lack of trigger** (39.6%). For women, **fatigue** led (43.6%). People already exercising mentioned fixed days or times (43.8%), allowing short sessions (42.1%), and accessible locations near home (39.4%) as ways to continue.

That suggests a more grounded service hypothesis: **reduce friction before adding features**. 徒歩移動のついでにできる短時間プログラム、予約や準備が少ない地域活動、休んでも復帰しやすい設計。We need experiments to know whether these ideas increase sign-ups and retention. The survey doesn't establish willingness to pay.

It also finds positive correlations between exercising and self-reported physical or mental wellbeing. **Correlation is not causation**: people who feel healthier may find it easier to exercise in the first place.

## 5. MLBは、データで最適化された守備をルールで押し戻そうとしている

ファングラフス（FanGraphs）reported on October 6 that Major League Baseball was testing **outfield positioning restrictions** in the Arizona Fall League. This is a pilot, **not an MLB-wide adopted rule**. [On-site report](https://blogs.fangraphs.com/major-league-baseball-piloting-experimental-outfield-positioning-rules-in-arizona-fall-league/)

Field markings define anchor positions. The Hawk-Eye tracking system checks whether defenders stand within permitted ranges when the pitch is thrown; a display signals compliance. 内野には走者なしの場合の制限もあり、違反時の進塁は審判の裁量を含む。The exact boundaries and eventual outcomes are still experimental.

なぜ守備位置まで規制するのか。分析が進み、チームは守備配置を合理的に最適化してきた。 The league already restricted extreme infield shifts in 2023, yet deep outfield positioning continues to affect what becomes a hit. [MLB's 2023 rule changes](https://www.mlb.com/rule-changes-2023/)

This creates a fascinating tension. **Teams optimize to win; leagues design conditions under which winning stays entertaining.** Those two objectives can diverge. Better predictive models do not automatically make a more watchable sport. Whether this experiment improves the game remains an empirical question.

## 6. カブスのバット投資は、測定から製造へ進む野球技術の実例になる

The Athletic reported on September 26 that the Chicago Cubs had built a close relationship with **ヴーロ・バッツ（Vullo Bats）**, a small custom bat maker, including supply to minor-league affiliates. Some major-league players use individually tailored bats. [The Athletic article](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7627124/2026/09/26/mlb-bat-technology-cubs-vullo-bats/) / [Secondary coverage](https://malaysia.news.yahoo.com/cubs-bat-experiment-limit-october-201614666.html)

The full Athletic article was not directly accessible here. We therefore rely on corroborating reporting for production details, not imagined quotes from the original. A 2025 local interview documented how Vullo shapes and finishes bats to players' specifications. The manufacturer itself also documents its precision weighing, sanding, calipers and weight-tuning process. [The Spokesman-Review](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2025/oct/10/from-the-inland-northwest-to-professional-playoff-/) / [Manufacturer's process](https://vullobatcompany.com/our-process/)

A “torpedo bat” redistributes mass toward a hitter's typical contact zone. Data about swing path and impact location can inform design. Then the player tests the bat, producing new observations. **Measurement → design → manufacturing → retesting** becomes a development loop.

ただし個人仕様のバットが、そのまま得点増や勝率向上を保証するわけではない。 Some players may benefit, others may not. The broader lesson is that baseball technology is moving from observing performance toward **changing the equipment that shapes performance**. [Technical background and limitations](https://www.theringer.com/2025/03/31/mlb/new-york-yankees-bowling-pin-bats-torpedo-mlb-home-runs)

The comparison with the outfield-positioning pilot is revealing: one is **rule design** for the sport as a whole; the other is **equipment design** for an individual athlete. Both depend on measuring the game, but they solve different problems.

## 7. 地域リーグのデータは、誰の決断に使われるのか

日本スポーツアナリスト協会（JSAA）announced its 20th open seminar, 「2026年地域プロ野球リーグデータ活用総括 ～NPBドラフト会議感想戦～」, for **October 23, 2026, starting at 17:00**. [Event page](https://peatix.com/event/5210353)

The page's title and start time were verifiable through search, but its full contents could not be fetched. We therefore cannot confirm the speakers, fees, venue or specific presentations. The event is a prompt for inquiry—not evidence of results already achieved.

What should grassroots data actually enable? A scout might want comparable player video, a coach might need consistent event tagging, and a league might need permissions before sharing footage. **Collection is not the same as usable data**. 撮影、タグ付け、照合、共有、許諾のすべてがそろって初めて、誰かの判断に使える資産になる。

A promising question is whether smaller leagues can make player development legible to larger organizations without imposing unaffordable technology costs. It remains a hypothesis until actual cases, workflows and financial figures are examined.

## 8. 六つのニュースを、混ぜずにつなぐ四つの層

Four distinct layers help organize the six signals.

- **Demand and household spending** — attendance, subscriptions, memberships. Track people × frequency × price × retention.
- **Relationship and belonging** — club loyalty, hometown attachment, sponsor trust. Track fan transitions and real purchase behavior.
- **Competition and technology** — defensive rules, custom bats, measurable on-field outcomes. Track performance changes and side effects.
- **Infrastructure and rights** — footage, tracking, permissions, data products. Track acquisition costs, reuse, search time and decision value.

This is an analytical framework developed for this essay, not an established industry taxonomy. Its central principle is simple: **Where value is created and who captures revenue are different questions.** A stadium visitor buys food; perhaps a vendor records the revenue while the club earns a commission. Likewise, a league licenses media rights while a contractor earns fees for producing the content.

For a practical pilot, separate “new spectators × repeat rate × net ticket yield” from “venue spending × club revenue share.” For sponsors, compare awareness with actual purchase. For participation products, track first attendance and retention after 30 and 90 days. For video systems, track not just hours captured but **the proportion actually licensed and reusable**.

## 9. 反証を残しておくことが、事業機会の検討を強くする

最後に、未解決の問いを残しておきたい。 Does nominal spending growth imply more real consumption? How much sponsor-related purchasing is genuinely incremental? Which exercise barriers can a paid service remove? Will outfield limits improve gameplay without new problems? Do custom bats and regional data systems generate enough value to justify their costs?

Each question asks for **a counterfactual**: what would happen without the intervention? 大きな市場規模、熱烈なファン、精密な計測を見つけただけでは、投資判断には足りない。Good research narrows the claim until it can be tested.

スポーツの価値は、ファンが増えるときだけ生まれるのではない。The same spectator returns more often; a sponsor earns trust beyond signage; an athlete's equipment is redesigned; and the league adjusts the very rules under which performance is measured. **Growth is not a single upward line. It is a change in the combination of behavior, relationships, technology and institutions.**
