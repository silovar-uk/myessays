# 香港は小さいのに、なぜ歩くほど世界が変わるのか
*English Mix: A folded city — mountains, harbour, transit and the images we remember*

> [第1編：香港はなぜ中国でありながら、中国本土と違うのか](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/#/essay/hong-kong-1990-2026-boundaries-of-autonomy) ／ [第2編：香港の食卓には、なぜ二つの時計があるのか](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/#/essay/hong-kong-food-everyday-time-1990-2026)

## 要旨――都市の広さは、地図上の面積と同じではない

The image of Hong Kong is often a skyline: glass towers, neon signs, crowded trains. ところが陸地の約40％は郊野公園などに指定され、urbanized land（都市化された土地）は約4分の1にとどまる。[Government geography overview](https://www.hketosin.gov.hk/hong-kong-at-a-glance.php) Hong Kong is not a place where every square metre has become a building. It is a city where **a dense urban core sits close to mountains, coastline and islands**. To understand it, we must ask not only *How big is the territory?* but also *How do people move through it?*

## 1. まず「香港島・九龍・新界・離島」を区別しないと、地図が読めない

Hong Kong is a group of connected but different places. **Hong Kong Island（香港島）** faces **Kowloon（九龍）** across **Victoria Harbour（ビクトリア・ハーバー）**. **The New Territories（新界）** extend farther north, while **Lantau Island（大嶼山）** and many smaller islands form another part of the region. 政府資料には261の離島があると記される。[Hong Kong geography](https://www.hketosin.gov.hk/hong-kong-at-a-glance.php)

The skyline photographed from Tsim Sha Tsui（尖沙咀）is mostly the other side of the harbour. Central（中環）and Wan Chai（湾仔）lie on the northern shore of Hong Kong Island. 新界には大規模住宅地と山々があり、大嶼山には1998年開港の国際空港と漁村の両方が存在する。[MTR airport history](https://www.mtr.com.hk/archive/corporate/en/investor/sec/2005_form20f/d20f.htm) Once you see these parts separately, a skyline stops being a map of the entire city.

## 2. 高層ビルが並ぶのは、香港の土地すべてが建物になったからではない

Hong Kong covers roughly 1,100 square kilometres of land. Government figures describe about a quarter as developed and around 40% as country parks and special areas. 「残りは自由に使える土地」と計算してはいけない。Conservation status, mountains, land ownership and infrastructure limits are different constraints, not interchangeable categories. [Government facts](https://www.hkconnect.gov.hk/en/facts-figures.html)

The terrain includes steep hills and valleys. Hong Kong's Civil Engineering and Development Department describes how reclamation, quarrying, land formation and reservoirs reshaped the landscape. 平らな土地が限られることは密集の背景の一つだが、住宅価格や高層化のすべての原因ではない。Urban density depends on the **placement of usable land**, not population alone. [Government landscape research](https://hkss.cedd.gov.hk/hkss/en/publications-and-resources/educational-materials/hong-kong-geology/chapter-9/index.html)

## 3. 香港には「昔からの陸地」と「あとから造られた陸地」がある

Land reclamation（埋め立て）means creating land by filling coastal water or shallow areas. The government estimates that around 30.5 square kilometres were reclaimed between 1967 and 1991, and another 19 square kilometres between 1991 and 1995. 1990年代は歴史の教科書の遠い過去ではない。The outline of the city was still changing at a major scale in recent decades. [Government geological history](https://hkss.cedd.gov.hk/hkss/en/publications-and-resources/educational-materials/hong-kong-geology/chapter-9/index.html)

The new Hong Kong International Airport at Chek Lap Kok（赤鱲角）opened in 1998 together with the airport rail links. 空港移転は航空機だけのニュースではなく、鉄道、道路、都市の移動経路を組み直す出来事だった。[MTR corporate history](https://www.mtr.com.hk/archive/corporate/en/investor/sec/2005_form20f/d20f.htm) Reclamation also raises questions about habitat and coastline changes. Artificial land is still built inside a natural coastal system.

## 4. 中環のエスカレーターは、坂道を都市の交通に変えた

The **Central–Mid-Levels Escalator（中環至半山自動扶梯）** opened in 1993. It is an approximately 800-metre chain of covered escalators and moving walkways connecting commercial streets with hillside neighbourhoods. その方向は時間帯によって変わり、観光局の案内では朝は下り、日中から夜は上りになる。[Tourism Board information](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/jp/place-to-go/travel.guide-central-mid-levels-escalator.html)

Why does this matter? A short distance on a map can still be physically demanding if it involves a steep climb. **Vertical distance（高低差）** affects the everyday meaning of proximity. しかもこのエスカレーターはビクトリア・ピーク山頂に続く観光ロープウェーではない。Its usefulness comes from connecting people with ordinary streets and homes, not simply from being an attraction.

## 5. 港を渡るフェリーとICカードは、「距離」ではなく「接続」を設計した

The **Star Ferry（天星小輪）** links the Kowloon side of Victoria Harbour with Central and Wan Chai. A route across the water is both a practical connection and a way to see the city from a different angle.[Star Ferry guide](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-star-ferry-pier.html) 港は「街を分断する海」であると同時に、船が行き交う都市内部の空間でもある。

In September 1997, the **Octopus card（八達通）** introduced contactless stored-value ticketing for transport. 乗り換えるたびに支払い方法を変えなくてよい仕組みは、目に見えない移動の摩擦を小さくする。[MTR railway history](https://stationrailvoyageinfo.mtr.com.hk/en/years-on-the-rail) Geography tells us where places are; transport networks tell us how accessible they feel. But access still varies by fare, frequency and mobility needs.

## 6. 40％の緑は、手つかずの空白ではなく、都市の隣にある生態系である

Hong Kong has 25 country parks and 22 special areas with a combined designation of 44,842 hectares, according to its Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department. そこは単なる「開発されなかった残りの土地」ではなく、自然保全、教育、野外活動のために管理される場所だ。[Country parks information](https://enjoyleisure.afcd.gov.hk/en-US/thematic-experiences/country-parks-and-hiking-trails)

The 100-kilometre **MacLehose Trail（麥理浩徑）** and Dragon's Back（龍脊）show another side of the region. でも自然が近いからといって、すべての道が簡単で安全なわけではない。Heat, rain, path difficulty and access all matter. [Official tourism overview](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/attractions/your-guide-to-experiencing-hong-kong.html)

Look even further back in time. Around Sai Kung（西貢）, the Hong Kong UNESCO Global Geopark contains hexagonal volcanic rock columns formed about 140 million years ago. 高層ビルの背後にある岩の時間は、1990年代の埋め立てよりも、人間の歴史そのものよりも桁違いに長い。[Geopark geology](https://www.geopark.gov.hk/discover/attractions/high-island) A single territory can contain very different scales of time.

## 7. 大澳と長洲は、「島の香港」が別の暮らしを持つことを示す

Tai O（大澳）on Lantau Island is known for **stilt houses（棚屋）** built beside tidal waterways and for a history of fishing and preserved seafood. 写真では「昔の香港」に見えるかもしれないが、住民が現在も生活する場所である。[Tai O guide](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-tai-o-village.html) A picturesque village is not merely a set for visitors.

Cheung Chau（長洲）also has its own communal traditions, including the **Cheung Chau Jiao Festival（長洲太平清醮）**, featured on Hong Kong's local intangible heritage list. [Local representative list](https://www.icho.hk/en/web/icho/past_representative_list_of_the_ich_inventory.html) 都心以外の地域は都市の「例外」ではない。They are other centres of social life within the same geographic territory.

## 8. 1990年代以降の香港映画は、地図を感情の風景へ変えた

Wong Kar-wai's **Chungking Express（『恋する惑星』、1994年）** uses Central's escalators, corridors and the area around Chungking Mansions as cinematic space. 街は名所のカタログではなく、人物が近づいたりすれ違ったりする距離感を描く装置になっている。[Tourism Board: movie locations](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/themed-travel/hong-kong-good-take/love.html) That is a reading of film style, not an explanation of every Hongkonger’s emotional life.

Neon signs are another example of a city living differently in memory and reality. Many older signs have disappeared amid technological changes and the rise of LED alternatives, while artisans and artists work to preserve the craft. 映画や写真の中で繰り返し見られる香港の夜景は、今の街角の姿と必ずしも一致しない。[Neon sign history](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/arts/discover-hong-kong-iconic-neon-signs.html)

The actual Kowloon Walled City（九龍城寨）was demolished, yet it continues to appear in popular imagination, including the 2024 film **Twilight of the Warriors: Walled In（『トワイライト・ウォリアーズ 決戦！九龍城砦』）**. 映画は街への入り口になるが、再現されたセットを実際の生活史の証拠と混同してはいけない。[Walled City on screen](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/neighbourhoods/kowloon-city/hong-kong-on-the-silver-screen.html)

## 9. 2023年の豪雨は、美しい斜面が危険にも変わると教えた

Hong Kong has a humid subtropical climate and is exposed to heavy rain and tropical cyclones. On 7 September 2023, the Observatory Headquarters recorded **158.1 millimetres of rainfall in one hour**, the highest hourly figure since records began in 1884 at that time. 洪水や土砂崩れ、交通障害が起きた。[Hong Kong Observatory report](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202310/04/P2023100300488p.htm)

Hills are scenic, but rainwater also flows downhill. Drainage, slope safety and warnings are vital parts of urban life. 一つの豪雨だけから気候変動の個別の因果関係を断定することは避ける。The broader point is physical: a landscape is more than a beautiful view; it is a condition that people must live with.

## 10. 守破離――「面積が小さい」より、「行き来できる場所が偏っている」が重要だ

**守 / Facts.** Mountains, bays, islands, dense neighbourhoods and protected countryside all exist within Hong Kong. Transport and reclamation reshaped access through the 1990s. 香港には一つのスカイラインからは分からない複数の地形がある。

**破 / Interpretation.** A better way to understand a city than physical area alone is to examine **effective distance（実際に感じる移動距離）**: slopes, ferries, transfers, walking routes and time. 同じ1キロでも、海越え・坂道・駅直結では近さが変わる。This is an analytical interpretation, not a measured travel-time ranking.

**離 / Transfer.** Other cities can be read this way too. 「駅から徒歩10分」という言葉は、坂、階段、信号、身体条件を省略している。Maps of walking time and access barriers could reveal a more useful city than a flat map alone. Hong Kong reminds us that urban design is partly the art of editing routes through difficult terrain.

## 基礎用語と地名

- **Hong Kong Island（香港島）**: island containing Central and Wan Chai.
- **Kowloon（九龍）**: peninsula north of Victoria Harbour.
- **New Territories（新界）**: extensive northern and surrounding areas, with housing, mountains and villages.
- **Lantau Island（大嶼山）**: large island with the airport and Tai O.
- **Victoria Harbour（維多利亞港）**: harbour between Hong Kong Island and Kowloon.
- **Reclamation（埋め立て）**: creating land out of shallow coastal water.
- **Country park（郊野公園）**: legally designated area for conservation and outdoor activity.
- **Octopus card（八達通）**: contactless transport card introduced in 1997.
- **Central–Mid-Levels Escalator（中環至半山自動扶梯）**: hillside transport link opened in 1993.
- **Geopark（地質公園）**: area for protection and interpretation of geological heritage.
- **Stilt house（棚屋）**: dwelling raised over mudflats or water.
- **Kowloon Walled City（九龍城寨）**: demolished dense urban settlement, now remembered through a park, films and research.
