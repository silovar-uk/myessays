# ポケモンが女子サッカーを育てる国で、女子リーグは7年ぶりに戻ってきた
## Japan's coaching link, diaspora players, grassroots tournaments, and a fragile league restart

September 17, 2026. In East Jakarta, two Japanese World Cup winners, Mana Iwabuchi and Aya Miyama, stood on a pitch with 74 Indonesian girls. The event had an unlikely name: **MilkLife Soccer Challenge with Pokémon**.

ポケモンと2011年女子ワールドカップ優勝者、そして10〜13歳の少女たち。It sounds like a charming one-day event. しかし、約1か月後にインドネシアでは女子の全国トップリーグが再開された。The previous Liga 1 Putri was held in 2019. Seven years had passed.

Meanwhile, Indonesia's national team had recruited a Japanese coach, welcomed players with overseas backgrounds, and lifted a regional trophy. But it had also suffered crushing defeats against stronger Asian teams. **How can success, fragility, and rapid institution-building all be true at once?**

*As of October 23, 2026. Results, organisers' targets, interpretations and business hypotheses are kept separate. Sources are linked throughout.*

## 1. 女子サッカーを強くするには「代表を集める」だけでは足りない

A national team is a selection mechanism, not a complete football system. PSSI（インドネシアサッカー協会）は代表「Garuda Pertiwi」を編成するが、それだけで女子の競技人口や国内リーグが増えるわけではない。

Think of three layers. **Entry:** girls have a chance to play through schools and communities. **Continuity:** they find coaches, opponents, and regular training. **Destination:** there is somewhere to play as adults, potentially as professionals.

2019年のLiga 1 Putri以前にも女子大会の歴史はある。The issue was not that football for women appeared out of nowhere in 2026. It was that a stable national top-tier competition had been missing for years. [Historical record: RSSSF](https://www.rsssf.org/tablesi/indo-womchamp.html), [local assessment: Pandit Football](https://panditfootball.com/editorial/215710/PFB/261020/kompetisi-sepak-bola-putri-kembali-jangan-untuk-pergi-lagi).

## 2. 「優勝した」と「アジアで勝てる」の間には、大きな断層がある

The timeline looks contradictory, but that is precisely why it matters.

- **2024:** Indonesia won the inaugural AFF Women's Cup under Satoru Mochizuki.
- **2025 AFC Women's Asian Cup qualification:** a 1–0 win against Kyrgyzstan, a 0–2 loss to Pakistan, and a 1–2 defeat against Chinese Taipei. No qualification.
- **August 2025:** 0–7 against Thailand, then 0–7 against Vietnam at the higher-level ASEAN Women's Championship.
- **April 2026:** 1–7 against DR Congo and 4–2 against New Caledonia in the FIFA Series.
- **July 22, 2026:** a 5–0 final victory over Laos. A second AFF Women's Cup title.
- **October 2026:** 0–0 friendlies against Armenia and Jordan; a five-place climb to 105th in the October 20 FIFA ranking, according to AFC and local reporting.

Sources: [AFC Asian Cup qualifiers](https://www.the-afc.com/en/national/afc_womens_asian_cup.html/news/qualifiers-group-d-chinese-taipei-2-1-indonesia), [Thailand match](https://www.the-afc.com/en/more/asean_womens_championship.html/news/group-a-thailand-7-0-indonesia-vietnam-6-0-cambodia-2), [Vietnam match](https://www.the-afc.com/en/more/asean_womens_championship.html/news/group-a-cambodia-0-7-thailand), [FIFA Series report](https://www.fifa.com/ja/tournaments/series/articles/fifa-womens-series-matchday-three-round-up-ja), [AFF 2026 final](https://www.aseanfootball.org/v3/second-aff-womens-cup-for-indonesia/), [AFC ranking update](https://www.the-afc.com/en/more/fifa_world_ranking.html/news/japan-stay-top-in-asia-korea-republic-indonesia-and-chinese-taipei-climb).

The key distinction is competition level. **The AFF Women's Cup is not the same tournament as the top-tier ASEAN Women's Championship.** In 2026, the Cup involved Indonesia, Laos, Singapore, Cambodia, Malaysia and Timor-Leste. Thailand, Vietnam and the Philippines were not in that Cup field. [AFF's match list](https://www.aseanfootball.org/v3/aff-womens-championship-2018/).

優勝を矮小化する必要はない。しかし「東南アジア最強」という評価は証拠を超える。Different opponents test different capabilities. Winning among peers does not automatically close the gap to elite Asian teams.

## 3. なぜ日本なのか。日本人監督の招聘は、2023年の協会間協力からつながっている

In 2024, PSSI appointed **Satoru Mochizuki（望月聡）** as women's national-team head coach. He had played for clubs including Urawa Reds and held coaching experience with Japan's women's national team. PSSI president Erick Thohir explicitly pointed to Japan's women's football record as a reason for the decision. [AFF's appointment report](https://www.aseanfootball.org/v3/pssi-sign-mochizuki-as-new-womens-team-head-coach/).

But the link began earlier. In May 2023, JFA and PSSI renewed a five-year cooperation agreement covering women's football, coach and referee education, national-team friendlies, league cooperation and training-centre expertise. [JFA's Japanese primary source](https://www.jfa.jp/about_jfa/news/00032175/).

ここは因果を慎重に扱いたい。The agreement created an institutional channel, but we cannot claim it directly caused that specific hiring decision. What matters over time is not just importing a coach. **Can the skills and routines of coaching become local institutional knowledge?**

## 4. 海外育ちの選手を集めると、代表は早く変わる。国内の育成が早く変わるとは限らない

Local coverage often calls Indonesian-heritage players with international backgrounds *diaspora players*. ただし「ディアスポラ」は代表資格を定める法律用語ではない。Eligibility and nationality require player-by-player verification.

Consider Claudia Scheunemann. Born in Indonesia, she signed with FC Utrecht Women in August 2025 on a contract lasting until 2028. [FC Utrecht's announcement](https://fcutrecht.nl/nieuws/2025/augustus/claudia-scheunemann-tekent-bij-fc-utrecht-vrouwen/), [current squad](https://www.fcutrecht.nl/teams/selectie-vrouwen/).

The five scorers in the July 2026 AFF Cup final were Sydney Hopper, Isabelle Nottet, Estella Loupatty, Felicia de Zeeuw and Emily Nahon. Their international connections are part of the story of the national team. [AFF official report](https://www.aseanfootball.org/v3/second-aff-womens-cup-for-indonesia/).

The tension is straightforward: **Overseas-developed talent can raise the immediate level of a national squad without automatically improving the daily football environment for girls at home.** 海外組の代表参加と国内選手の成長を結ぶ場所が、今度はリーグになる。

## 5. 7年ぶりの女子リーグは、全国に広がるためではなく「まず途切れない」ために小さく始まった

I.League introduced Indonesia Women's League 2026/27 in August. The original launch date was October 17, but the operator updated it to **October 18, 2026**, with the opening fixtures at Jakarta's Stadion Madya rather than the originally announced Soemantri Brodjonegoro venue. [August announcement](https://ileague.id/press/detail/indonesia-women-s-league-2026-27-bersiap-bergulir-i-league-usung-konsep-sport-festival), [October 11 update](https://www.ileague.id/press/detail/indonesia-women---s-league-2026-27-dimulai-18-oktober--enam-klub-siap-bersaing).

Only six clubs joined: Persija Jakarta, Persita, Dewa United, Garudayaksa, Persikad and FC Bekasi City. All are from Greater Jakarta. Each club plays the others three times: **15 regular-season matches per club, 45 in total**, followed by playoffs.

Opening day scores were Garudayaksa 0–1 Persita, Persikad 0–1 Dewa, and Persija 1–1 FC Bekasi City. [Official fixture/results page](https://www.ligaindonesiabaru.com/fixtures/index/INDONESIA_WOMENS_LEAGUE_2026-27/1).

Why a small, centralised league in such a large country? The organisers prioritised manageable travel expenses and continuity, with player allocation and support for clubs. This is a plausible operational trade-off, not evidence that six teams are enough. [I.League's format explanation](https://ileague.id/press/detail/indonesia-women-s-league-2026-27-bersiap-bergulir-i-league-usung-konsep-sport-festival).

Another milestone came on October 15: **TVRI** was announced as the free-to-air broadcasting partner and **RANS Entertainment** as the digital streaming partner. Visibility becomes possible before a sustainable audience business has been proven. [Official broadcast announcement](https://www.ligaindonesiabaru.com/press/detail/iwl-2026-27-gandeng-tvri-dan-rans-entertainment-untuk-perluas-jangkauan-kompetisi).

Local criticism captures the central risk. Seven years passed since the last top-level league. If cost efficiency becomes a permanent excuse for geographic exclusion, a restart will not necessarily become a nationwide football system. [Pandit Football editorial, October 20](https://panditfootball.com/editorial/215710/PFB/261020/kompetisi-sepak-bola-putri-kembali-jangan-untuk-pergi-lagi).

## 6. もう一つの主役は、ポケモンよりも「普通の小学生が出場できる大会」である

The grassroots picture looks very different. MilkLife Soccer Challenge (MLSC), developed by Djarum Foundation's sports initiative and MilkLife, creates tournaments for girls at primary-school age.

For its September 1–6, 2026 opening series, organisers reported **1,037 players from 57 schools in Tangerang** and **1,091 from 63 schools in Semarang**: a combined 2,128 girls and 120 schools. Unlike the target figures often quoted in promotion material, these are reported participation results. [MLSC organiser report](https://milklifesoccer.com/news/522/milklife-soccer-challenge-2026-2027-resmi-bergulir-tangerang-dan-semarang-jadi-kota-pembuka).

The 2026/27 programme has a **15-city schedule**, including Jayapura and several other cities outside the capital. That is a plan, not yet proof of all events taking place. Programme director Teddy Tjahjono has spoken of a 2035 Women's World Cup ambition. **Ambition is not qualification.** [Djarum Foundation schedule](https://www.djarumfoundation.org/aktivitas/sd-tarakanita-bumijo-i-lahir-sebagai-juara-baru-milklife-soccer-challenge?lang=id).

At the next age brackets, PSSI and its partners promote **Piala Pertiwi U-14 and U-16**. In 2025 they announced participation targets of 5,760 girls, 120 female coaches and 62 female referees. These are targets, not verified final totals. [ANTARA's original reporting](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/4770365/piala-pertiwi-kembali-digelar-bidik-5000-talenta-muda).

Then comes Pokémon. On September 17, Iwabuchi and Miyama coached 74 girls in East Jakarta, with drills, playing time and questions. [Local event report](https://sport.liputan6.com/timnas-indonesia/read/8293889/2-pesepakbola-legendaris-putri-jepang-latih-talenta-muda-indonesia). An icon, a character brand, and an accessible school tournament can make an unfamiliar sport feel imaginable. **But a one-day clinic is an entry point, not a development pathway by itself.**

## 7. 三つの改革がつながるとは限らない。つなげるための指標が必要だ

We can now distinguish three overlapping strategies.

- **National team:** foreign coaching expertise, overseas-connected players and international fixtures.
- **Grassroots:** school tournaments, city rounds and youth competitions.
- **League and media:** senior club matches, broadcasting access and potential commercial relationships.

三つの取り組みは並走している。ただ、つながっていることまでは証明されていない。A girl's experience at ten needs a bridge to competition at fourteen, then sixteen, then senior football. Each step has a different set of gatekeepers and costs.

From a sports-business perspective, I would track five unanswered questions: annual retention among young players, progression from youth competitions into senior clubs, player contracts and travel expenses, attendance and repeat viewing, and the commercial ownership of media and sponsorship rights.

**These are proposed research questions, not reported market statistics.** We do not yet have reliable public evidence here for league profitability, broadcasting-rights fees, average crowds or player retention.

## 8. 2035年のワールドカップよりも、2027年にリーグが残っているか

Indonesia's ranking improved in October 2026. It has won two AFF Women's Cups. It has also suffered repeated heavy defeats to stronger opponents. None of those facts alone tells us whether a girl who starts playing at ten will still have a club to play for at twenty.

The 74 girls who met Iwabuchi and Miyama in Jakarta are not merely an uplifting image for a campaign. They are a test of continuity. **Can an event become a habit, a habit become a pathway, and a pathway become a viable football life?**

それは2026年の代表戦の結果より長い時間がかかる問いだ。The next important measure may be surprisingly mundane: whether the new women's league is still functioning next season, and whether more girls can actually reach it.
