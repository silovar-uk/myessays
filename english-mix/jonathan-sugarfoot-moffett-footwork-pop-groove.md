# シュガーフットは、足でベースを弾く
## Jonathan Moffettのキックから考える、Michael Jacksonと「踊れるドラム」

Watch a Michael Jackson live performance, and your eyes naturally go to Michael.

身体の止まり方、ターン、ポーズ。音楽そのものが身体になっているように見える。

But after a while, something strange happens.

**Michaelの足を見ているのに、耳は別の“foot”へ引っ張られる。**

That foot belongs to Jonathan “Sugarfoot” Moffett, the drummer behind the stage.

彼のbass drumは単に拍を刻んでいる感じではない。細かく動き、bass lineと絡み、ダンサーの踏み込みを下から押す。

The more you listen, the less useful the phrase “a drummer with a fast foot” becomes.

Sugarfootの面白さは、**drumming、bass playing、そして人を踊らせることが、彼の中でほとんど同じ問題になっている**ところにある。

## 1. “Sugarfoot” started with the foot

Jonathan Moffett is a drummer from New Orleans who began working with The Jacksons in 1979 and later spent decades performing with Michael Jackson.

公式サイトにはMadonna、Elton John、George Michael、Janet Jackson、Stevie Wonder、Quincy Jonesらとの仕事も並ぶ。

In a 2026 Cornell Daily Sun interview, Moffett explained the origin of the nickname “Sugarfoot.”

若いころのバンドでメンバー全員にnicknameを付けることになり、bandleaderが彼のbass-drum pedalの扱いを見て、足さばきが“sweet”だとしてSugarfootと名付けたという。

He did not even like the name at first.

もっと強そうな名前が欲しかった。でも、その嫌だった名前が、長いcareerそのものを表す言葉になった。

The important point is that the nickname was never only about speed.

彼の足は、手数を増やすためだけに動いていたわけではない。

[The Legend of Sugarfoot — The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/04/the-legend-of-sugarfoot-an-interview-with-michael-jackson-s-drummer)

## 2. He was listening to the bass before he became famous as a drummer

One of the most revealing comments Moffett has made is about the bass guitar.

子どものころ、彼はbass playerになりたかった。そのため曲を覚えるとき、drum partだけでなくbass partも聴く癖がついた。

He described trying to absorb both rhythms and express them through his bass-drum technique.

ここがかなり面白い。

Most drummers learning a song might ask, “Where is the kick? Where is the snare? What is the hi-hat doing?”

Sugarfootはそこへ、**“What is the bassist doing?”**まで入れる。

His right foot becomes more than one limb inside the drum kit.

キックがbassと重なったり、隙間へ入ったりしながら、low end全体のmovementを作る。

This leads to a useful way of hearing him:

**足が器用なのではない。足でbass lineを考えている。**

That sentence is my interpretation, not a direct quote from Moffett. But it grows directly from his description of learning both the drum and bass parts of a song.

[Interview With Jonathan “Sugarfoot” Moffett — Michael Jackson: A Living Legacy](https://valmaiowens.blogspot.com/2011/03/interview-with-jonathan-sugarfoot.html)

## 3. New Orleans: rhythm from the bottom up

In the same interview, Moffett connected his approach to New Orleans.

彼は現地のdrummerについて、リズムを **“bottom up”** で作る感覚や、bass drumの複雑なpatternとtechniqueに触れている。

That does not mean every New Orleans drummer plays the same way.

second line、brass band、R&B、funkなど、New Orleansの音楽には複数のtraditionが重なっている。

Still, it matters that Moffett himself uses New Orleans and “bottom up” when explaining his own playing.

ドラムを手から組み立てるのではなく、地面から立ち上げる。

The kick creates the floor. The snare and hi-hat live on top of it.

この感覚はMichael Jacksonのmusicと恐ろしく相性がいい。

Because Michael’s music often reaches the body before the brain has time to explain it.

## 4. The Jacksons audition: compressing several layers into one body

Moffett’s major professional break came in 1979, when he auditioned for The Jacksons.

公式Biographyによれば、彼は締切ぎりぎりでauditionの機会を得て、“Shake Your Body (Down to the Ground)”を演奏するよう求められた。

The recorded drum arrangement was described as involving multiple overdubbed layers.

Moffettはそれを知らず、「一人で全部演奏している」と思って練習していた。

So he solved the physical puzzle.

腕を交差させ、四肢を独立させ、複数のpatternを一つのbodyへ押し込んだ。

Years later, in Drumeo’s “Play That Beat Again,” he described mentally slowing the groove down and working out how every part could happen without losing the beat.

これは単なるchopsの話ではない。

**His real skill was compression: many musical jobs, one human body.**

[Jonathan “Sugarfoot” Moffett: The Story — Official Site](https://www.jonathanmoffett.com/post/jonathan-sugarfoot-moffett-the-story)

[Play That Beat Again — Drumeo](https://www.drumeo.com/beat/jonathan-moffett-play-that-beat-again/)

## 5. Michael Jackson was not only a singer. His body was part of the rhythm section

The relationship between Moffett and Michael becomes more interesting when we stop thinking in terms of “singer plus backing band.”

Moffettの公式Biographyには、初期liveでMichaelの即興的なstopやmovementにdrum accentを合わせ、Michael本人が「どうして次の動きが分かったのか」と驚いたという回想がある。

We should treat that as a recollection from Moffett’s side, not an independently verified scientific fact.

それでもliveを見ると、この話が示している関係は理解しやすい。

Michael does not merely dance on top of the beat.

足を置く。肩を跳ねさせる。turnを止める。身体をfreezeさせる。そこから急に動く。

He makes time visible.

その瞬間、drummerの役割はtempoを一定に保つことだけではない。

The drummer decides where the body should feel the emphasis.

Michaelがbeatを「見せる」なら、Sugarfootはそのbeatを床の下から支える。

That may be one reason the pairing worked so well.

## 6. Live drummer does not mean “he recorded every Michael Jackson hit”

This distinction matters.

SugarfootはMichael Jacksonを代表する**live drummer**の一人だが、有名曲のstudio recordingをすべて彼が叩いたわけではない。

Drumeo notes that Michael Jackson’s recorded catalog features many other major drummers, including Jeff Porcaro, Leon “Ndugu” Chancler and John “JR” Robinson.

だからSugarfootが“Billie Jean”や“Smooth Criminal”を演奏する映像を見ても、それをそのまま「原盤を作った人」と考えるべきではない。

The more interesting job is different.

**He had to turn an already-finished global hit back into a living physical event, night after night.**

巨大な会場で、歌、dance、lighting、映像、観客のreactionを一つのpulseへ戻す。

That is live drumming as reconstruction.

[Michael Jackson’s Drummer — Who Is Jonathan “Sugarfoot” Moffett? — Drumeo](https://www.drumeo.com/beat/jonathan-sugarfoot-moffett-drum-lessons/)

## 7. The difficulty of making simple 4/4 feel huge

Pop drumming can look easy on paper.

四つ打ち、2拍目と4拍目のsnare、一定のhi-hat。譜面だけなら、長いdrum soloより単純に見える。

But “simple” does not mean physically or musically easy.

Drumeoの解説では、“Wanna Be Startin’ Somethin’”や“Smooth Criminal”のgrooveに、細かなkick、hi-hat opening、accent、coordinationが組み込まれていることが示されている。

And in the “Smooth Criminal” lesson, precision in an arena setting becomes a key idea.

何万人もの観客がいる場所では、複雑さを増やせばいいわけではない。

You have to make the pulse obvious without making the playing boring.

歌手が踊り、lightingが動き、videoが切り替わる。その中心のtimeを毎晩同じ強度で置く。

**Technique is used to create clarity.**

Sugarfoot seems to be a master of that kind of technique.

[Study The Greats: Jonathan “Sugarfoot” Moffett — Drumeo](https://www.drumeo.com/beat/study-the-greats-sugarfoot/)

[The Grooves of Michael Jackson — Drumeo](https://www.drumeo.com/michael-jackson-grooves)

## 8. Great enough to be invisible

Moffett’s tour history extends far beyond Michael Jackson.

公式tour listにはMadonnaのVirgin Tour、Who’s That Girl Tour、Blond Ambition Tour、Elton JohnのReg Strikes Back Tour、George MichaelのCover to Cover Tour、Janet Jacksonのjanet. World Tourなどが並ぶ。

These are not concerts where most people bought a ticket to stare at the drummer.

中心にはstarがいる。songがある。danceがある。showがある。

The drummer’s job is to make the star look larger.

Sugarfoot自身は派手なcymbal moveや巨大kitを使うshowmanでもあるが、それ以上に重要なのは、主役を最大化できることではないか。

That is a strange form of mastery: being extremely good without demanding that the audience notice how good you are.

[Tour History — Jonathan “Sugarfoot” Moffett Official Site](https://www.jonathanmoffett.com/tours)

[NAMM Oral History: Jonathan “Sugarfoot” Moffett](https://www.namm.org/video/orh/jonathan-sugarfoot-moffett-full-interview)

## 9. Drums may not “keep time.” They may deliver time to bodies

Beginners are often told that drums keep the tempo.

間違いではない。でもSugarfootを見ていると、それだけでは足りない。

A drummer may be doing something more physical: distributing time to everyone else.

Kickが鳴る。bassが重なる。dancerが踏む。観客の肩が動く。次のsnareを待つ。

Everyone shares the same “next.”

このときdrumは時計ではない。

It is an interface that synchronizes bodies.

Sugarfootの右足は、その働きをとても分かりやすく見せる。

He listens to the bass line, builds the low end, reacts to movement, and projects a clear pulse into an arena.

小さなpedalから、巨大なstage全体の身体がつながっていく。

## 10. Not a drummer with a fast foot. A drummer who thinks with his foot

In the end, the nickname “Sugarfoot” was almost too accurate.

ただし「速くkickを踏める人」という意味では足りない。

Inside that right foot are several ideas at once:

- New Orleans and the “bottom up” feeling he describes
- bass playerへの憧れ
- learning the bass line as well as the drum part
- 複数のrhythmを一つのbodyへ統合する力
- reacting to a dancer’s movement
- 巨大会場でも輪郭を失わないprecision

So this may be the better description:

**Sugarfoot is not simply a drummer with a fast foot. He is a drummer who thinks with his foot.**

Michael Jackson made rhythm visible with his body.

その後ろでSugarfootは、見えないrhythmを床へ打ち込んでいた。

The audience may not have been watching his right foot.

でも、そのfootに動かされていた。
