# 「地に足をつける」に、土はいらない — Grounding Without the Ground
### What grounding is, how it differs from meditation, and why the evidence matters

The word **grounding** sounds remarkably physical. Ground means 地面。So do we need grass, bare feet, or a forest? The NHS recommends something much less dramatic: look at patterns on the ceiling, feel the chair, or notice the taste left by toothpaste. 地面は一切登場しない。The strange detail raises a useful question: **What exactly are we trying to bring back?**

身体は椅子に座ったままでも、attention can be trapped in an imagined future or a painful past. **Psychological grounding** is a set of ways to notice the environment and sensations available right now. It may help when anxiety or unwanted thoughts feel overwhelming. It is not a promise to erase them（[NHS guidance](https://www.nhsinform.scot/healthy-living/mental-wellbeing/breathing-and-relaxation-exercises/grounding-exercises/)、[NIH guidance](https://www.training.nih.gov/oite-careers-blog/grounding-skills/)）。

A crucial distinction first. **Earthing**, another practice sometimes called grounding, concerns physical or electrical contact with the Earth. これは別領域。Evidence for a psychological attention exercise cannot be used to prove medical claims about electrical contact（[research example](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31831261/)）。

## 1. 「今ここに戻る」とは、気分を消すことではなく注意の向き先を確かめること

What moves away when we say “come back to the present”? Not the body. **Attention** moves. メールを送ったあと、相手の反応を想像して落ち着かない。Your eyes still face the screen, but your mind is working on a future that has not happened.

Now notice the grain of the desk, the support of your chair, and one sound in the room. The message is still unanswered. The anxiety may remain. But attention has more than one destination. **Grounding does not have to make the mind quiet; it can help us notice where we are while the mind is noisy.** これは注意の制御（attentional control）という概念で説明できるが、実践によってその能力が必ず向上すると証明されたわけではない。

## 2. 瞑想は大きな領域であり、グラウンディングは場面に応じた対処法である

**Meditation** is not a single exercise or an order to stop thinking. It includes many practices focusing on breath, sounds, images, words, or bodily sensations. **Mindfulness** commonly means paying attention to present experience without immediately judging it（[NCCIH definitions](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/meditation-and-mindfulness-effectiveness-and-safety)）。

Grounding overlaps with mindfulness, but the two are not identical. Some meditation practices invite us to stay with an uncomfortable experience and observe it. In urgent distress, grounding may shift attention toward a chair, a window, or a nearby voice. 前者は経験との向き合い方を広く練習する営み、後者は「いま必要な注意の置き場」を選ぶ対処法として理解できる。They can also be combined.

You do not have to close your eyes. You do not have to focus on breathing if that feels uncomfortable. **An open-eyed glance around the room is a valid starting point.**

## 3. 有名な「5・4・3・2・1法」は、五感を使った現在地確認である

The famous **5-4-3-2-1 technique** gives attention a route. The UK NHS describes it this way（[NHS guide](https://www.nhsinform.scot/healthy-living/mental-wellbeing/breathing-and-relaxation-exercises/grounding-exercises/)）。

1. **See five things.** 時計、ペン、窓、壁の色、机の傷。
2. **Feel four things.** The pressure of your feet, the fabric on your skin, the chair, the weight of a cup.
3. **Hear three things.** 空調、車、足音。
4. **Smell two things.** Coffee or soap, if available.
5. **Taste one thing.** The lingering taste of a drink, perhaps.

The numbers look ceremonial. But they are not magic. Other NHS services describe variations, including different sensory counts（[Oxford Health](https://oxfordhealth.nhs.uk/camhs/self-care/sleep/relaxation/ground-yourself/)、[Kent NHS](https://www.kentcht.nhs.uk/leaflet/panic-attacks/)）。匂いが分からなければ省いてよい。**The purpose is orientation, not perfect counting.** And “five senses” is a practical everyday classification, not a complete scientific map of human sensation.

## 4. 身体・思考・安心感という三つの入口があり、足裏だけが方法ではない

The NIH describes three practical routes: **physical, mental, and soothing grounding**（[NIH, April 2025](https://www.training.nih.gov/oite-careers-blog/grounding-skills/)）。感覚を使うなら、ペンの重さを確かめる。思考を使うなら、100から7ずつ引く。安心感を支えるなら、好きな場所や大切な人を思い出す。

This reveals something important: grounding does not always mean scanning your inner body. If your heartbeat makes you more worried, consider the colours of nearby objects instead. **Interoception** means sensing internal bodily states; **exteroception** means sensing the outside world. この区別は学術語の暗記以上に実用的だ。It tells us why “focus on your breathing” is not the best instruction for everybody.

## 5. 古代の瞑想と、現代の心理的対処法を同じ起源にしてはいけない

It would make a neat story to say: ancient meditation began thousands of years ago and eventually became the five-four-three-two-one method. But a neat story is not necessarily a verified history. 古い瞑想と現代の対処技法には共通点がある。しかし、今回確認した一次資料からは、現代のグラウンディングに唯一の発明者を特定できない（[NCCIH](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/meditation-and-mindfulness-effectiveness-and-safety)）。

A documented milestone is **1979**, when **Jon Kabat-Zinn** started **Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction**, a medical and educational mindfulness programme in the United States（[UMass medical centre](https://www.ummhealth.org/services-treatments/center-mindfulness/mindfulness-programs/mbsr)）。That does not mean he invented grounding. Another clear document is a **2014 US trauma-informed care guide** that describes grounding for overwhelming emotions, memories, or dissociation（[clinical guide](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK207188/box/part1_ch4.box5/?report=objectonly)）。**Shared functions do not automatically prove a single historical lineage.**

## 6. なぜ使われるのか――「注意をそらす」ことにも場面によって意味がある

A **flashback** can make a past event feel as if it is happening again. **Dissociation** broadly refers to a sense of disconnection from oneself, memories, or the surrounding world. 日常的な心配と同じ意味ではなく、似た感覚が一度あっただけで病気と判断するものでもない。

The US Department of Veterans Affairs uses grounding to help people attend to their actual surroundings. Importantly, it also says to stop when the technique increases frustration（[VA guidance](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/professional/treat/care/toolkits/police/managingStrategies.asp)、[VA self-help tool](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/apps/ptsdcoachonline/tools/be-in-the-moment/pages/page-1.html)）。

Is shifting attention simply avoidance? Not necessarily. If you are crossing a road, noticing the traffic matters more than staying absorbed in a painful memory. **The value of shifting attention depends partly on what it allows you to do next.**

## 7. 科学的な根拠は、瞑想全般と「5・4・3・2・1法」単独で強さが違う

A technique appearing on hospital websites is not the same as a technique proven superior in high-quality trials. 複数の公的医療機関が5・4・3・2・1法を案内していることは確認できる。しかし、**the exact sequence of numbers has not been established here as uniquely effective** through high-quality head-to-head evidence. That is a limit on what we can claim, not proof that the method never helps.

There is broader clinical research on meditation and mindfulness, including possible benefits for anxiety, depression, and sleep（[NCCIH review](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/meditation-and-mindfulness-effectiveness-and-safety)）。But **evidence for a multi-week meditation programme is not direct evidence for a ten-second grounding exercise**. また、PTSD治療では、持続エクスポージャー療法、認知処理療法、眼球運動による脱感作と再処理法などの専門的治療が推奨される。Grounding is not a replacement for professional treatment when symptoms persist（[VA clinical overview](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/professional/treat/txessentials/overview_therapy.asp)）。

## 8. 「いつでも安全で、誰にでもよい」は言い過ぎである

Mindfulness and meditation are not automatically harmless for everyone. A **2020 systematic review of 83 studies and 6,703 participants** estimated negative experiences in **8.3%** of participants across varied meditation practices and study designs（[original review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32820538/)）。これは**グラウンディング単独の有害事象率ではない**。The figures also varied greatly by study type.

And the present moment is not always safe. If actual danger exists, moving to safety matters more than completing a sensory exercise. 英国の支援団体マインド（Mind）も、現在を経験することが常に安全とは限らない点に触れている（[Mind guidance](https://www.mind.org.uk/information-support/types-of-mental-health-problems/trauma/coping-with-trauma/)）。Grounding should support accurate awareness of reality, not demand acceptance of unsafe conditions.

## 9. 極端な四場面で試すと、守るべきものは数字ではないと分かる

Let's run an exaggerated **thought experiment**, not a clinical experiment. What survives when we change the conditions?

**A dark room.** Five visible objects may be impossible. Use sounds or touch instead. **A blocked nose.** Skip smell; notice the cup in your hand. **A noisy train station.** Use signboards and the line under your feet, but never stop attending to immediate safety just to finish counting. **An unsafe place.** Don't complete a mindfulness checklist. Leave and seek help.

These conditions reveal the essential distinction. **Completing the sequence is not the same as orienting yourself successfully.** 手順は目的ではない。「今何が起きていて、次に何が必要か」を見分けるための補助にすぎない。

## 10. 日常で使うなら「気づく・確かめる・戻る」で十分なことがある

Here is an **editorial suggestion**, not a validated standalone treatment. First, **notice**: “What's happening in my mind? Is it quiet right now?” 「静かではない」でも問題ない。Second, **check**: notice two objects you can see, one sound, or the texture of something you hold. Third, **return**: choose a next step, even a small one—write a sentence, drink water, or ask for support.

Do not turn it into another performance test. The question is not “Did I become calm enough?” but “Can I see my situation and choose what to do next?” つらさが増したら中止する。強い不安、フラッシュバック、解離、生活への支障が続くときは、医療・心理の専門家へ相談する。

## 11. 最大の批判は、「本人が気持ちを整えれば済む」に回収される危険である

What if the real problem is unsafe work, overwork, or a threatening relationship? Telling someone to count five objects does not remove the source of distress. **A coping tool can be useful without being a solution to the underlying problem.**

医療機関がグラウンディングを紹介していることは資料で確認できる。一方、「組織が対処の責任を個人へ押し戻す危険」は本稿の批判的な解釈であって、医療機関の公式な主張ではない。The distinction matters: helping someone through a difficult minute must not become an excuse to leave the difficult situation unchanged.

## 12. 地面ではなく、判断の足場を取り戻す

So why toothpaste? Because its taste belongs to the moment you are actually living in. The imagined future and remembered past may be vivid, but a simple sensation offers another reference point. 最初は滑稽に見えた細部が、いまは具体的な意味を持つ。

It won't solve your life. It may not even feel pleasant. You can change the exercise, stop it, or ask for help. The point is not obedience to a ritual; it is **recovering enough orientation to notice what you can choose next**. グラウンディングの地面とは、土のことではなく、判断するための足場のことなのだ。

## 用語の小辞典

- **Grounding／グラウンディング**：現在の環境や感覚へ注意を向ける対処法。
- **Meditation／瞑想**：心身と注意に関わる多様な実践の総称。
- **Mindfulness／マインドフルネス**：現在の経験を、評価を急がず観察する姿勢。
- **Attentional control／注意の制御**：注意を選び、切り替え、維持する働き。
- **Interoception／内受容感覚**：身体内部の状態を感じ取る働き。
- **Exteroception／外受容感覚**：外界の光、音、触覚などを感じ取る働き。
- **Flashback／フラッシュバック**：過去の体験が今起きているように感じられる現象。
- **Dissociation／解離**：自己・周囲・記憶などとのつながりが薄れる経験。
- **PTSD／心的外傷後ストレス障害**：トラウマに関わる症状が持続し生活に支障を及ぼす状態。
- **Earthing／アーシング**：地球との物理的・電気的接触を健康効果と結びつける別領域。
