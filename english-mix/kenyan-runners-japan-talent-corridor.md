# 「ランナーの聖地」Itenから、日本組がほとんど出てこないのはなぜ？

## 1. If you want fast runners in Kenya, just go to Iten — or so I assumed

ケニアの長距離走について少し調べると、ItenやEldoretという地名に何度もぶつかる。World-class runners gather there, train at altitude, and work around coaches and agents from abroad. To an outsider, if a Japanese team wants to scout Kenyan runners, this seems like the obvious place to begin.

ところが、人類学者Michael Kentaro Petersが2013年から2023年まで日本とケニアで行った民族誌調査を読むと、かなり変な事実が出てくる。Among the Kenyan runners in Japanese high schools, universities and corporate teams that he studied, almost none came to Japan through the famous Iten/Eldoret running hub. [Michael Kentaro Peters, “The endurance of hope: Kenyan runners in Japan”](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14725843.2024.2381493)

It is one of the most famous concentrations of running talent in the world, yet it is not the main gateway to Japan.

速い人が多い場所と、日本へ行く人が多い場所が一致しない。

That mismatch raises a larger question: how does the Japanese ekiden world actually “buy” or recruit talent from Kenya?

## 2. The Kenya–Japan connection is much thicker than it first appears

最初に規模感を置く。Peters estimates that in a typical year roughly **150 Kenyan distance runners** are attached to Japanese high school, university and corporate teams, about two-thirds of them men. His estimate combines official team rosters and competition results. [Peters 2024](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14725843.2024.2381493)

さらに歴史をさかのぼると、1988年から2008年までのオリンピック男子マラソンでメダルを取ったケニア人選手は、全員が日本で走った経験を持っていた。Douglas Wakiihuri took silver in 1988, Erick Wainaina bronze in 1996 and silver in 2000, and Samuel Wanjiru gold in 2008. World Athletics itself described Wanjiru’s career as “from Kenya to the world via Japan,” tracing his path from Sendai Ikuei to Toyota Kyushu. [World Athletics](https://worldathletics.org/news/news/wanjiru-from-kenya-to-the-world-via-japan)

つまり日本は、ケニア人ランナーにとって偶然の留学先ではない。It has operated for decades as a distinct career market.

Yet that market does not simply pull the fastest runners evenly from all over Kenya.

ここから「市場」という言葉のイメージが少し崩れ始める。

## 3. Who reaches Japan changes depending on where the scout stands

Petersの研究で最も面白い部分の一つが、主要なリクルーターたちの活動地域を地図に置いた分析である。The three major recruiters he anonymizes were concentrated mainly around Laikipia County and Kisii County. [Peters 2024](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14725843.2024.2381493)

すると、日本へ来る選手の民族的・地域的な構成もその地理に引っ張られる。Around Laikipia, many identified runners were Kikuyu; in Kisii County, many were Kisii. Peters argues that the geography of Japan-oriented recruiting networks strongly shapes which individuals — and therefore which regional or ethnic groups — eventually reach Japan.

ここで重要なのは、誰かが「Kikuyuを優遇しよう」「Kalenjinを外そう」と会議で決めたわけではないことだ。

Scouts search where they have trusted races, coaches, training groups and personal ties. Candidates are found there, introduced to Japanese schools or companies, and successful placements reinforce those same local relationships.

偏りは、差別的な方針がなくても、**探索ネットワークそのものから自然に生成される。**

## 4. Near Eliud Kipchoge, there was barely a doorway to Japan

この構造を象徴するエピソードがある。Peters also spoke with Eliud Kipchoge, whose training base at Kaptagat lies near the Iten/Eldoret running ecosystem. Kipchoge told him that, in his area, there had not been people taking runners to Japan. [Peters 2024](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14725843.2024.2381493)

Iten周辺には、世界を目指すランナーが何千人もいる。Yet the “Japan option” was not a strong route in that particular place.

逆に、世界的にはそれほど有名でない地域でも、日本とつながるエージェントの近くで走っていれば、発見される可能性が上がる。Peters describes a Japan-based runner who was noticed not simply because of his home area, but because he happened to attend school within the operational reach of an influential recruiter.

ここで「才能」の意味が変わる。

Performance is necessary. But **being located where performance can be observed by the relevant network becomes almost another prerequisite.**

## 5. A scout standing 100 meters away can change a career

極端な思考実験をしてみる。Runner A runs 5000m in 13:30. Runner B runs 13:45. A is clearly faster.

ただしAはItenで、欧米向けのエージェントが多い練習グループにいる。B competes at regional meets frequently watched by a Japan-oriented recruiter. If a Japanese university coach asks for a candidate list, B may appear first.

これは「遅い選手を選ぶ」という話ではない。Real recruitment still considers times, age, potential, temperament and team fit. A 2025 interview with a Japan–Kenya running agent describes local staff making candidate lists, Japanese-side evaluation through race footage and visits, and then negotiations over conditions. [Alpen Online](https://store.alpen-group.jp/Page/Feature/running_250930_01.aspx)

The crucial stage comes before that shortlist.

「世界中の選手から最適な一人を選ぶ」のではない。**A market is first built from the people a network can see, and only then does selection choose among them.**

## 6. Why does Japan go all the way to Kenya for runners?

需要側を見ると、駅伝という競技の特殊さが出てくる。Peters points to a robust Japanese market for Kenyan runners because high-school, university and corporate ekiden competition can bring both competitive results and national media exposure. [Peters 2024](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14725843.2024.2381493)

International Institute for Asian Studiesの記事では、東京国際大学の留学生エースが箱根駅伝で前方を走り続けることにより、大学名が長時間テレビ画面に映る広告価値まで論じられている。 [IIAS](https://www.iias.asia/the-newsletter/article/ekiden-new-year-maendeleo-and-gendered-expectations-kenyan-runners-japan)

Of course, reducing recruitment to “advertising” would be crude. Competitive improvement, internal stimulation, international exchange and educational opportunity can coexist.

ただ、駅伝では一人の走りが学校名や企業名と結びつき、全国中継で何十分も可視化される。

**Speed can be converted almost directly into organizational media exposure.** That conversion device gives Japanese institutions a competitive and economic reason to extend scouting networks overseas.

## 7. Reaching Japan is not the finish line; it is another narrow funnel

ケニアから日本へ来ること自体が狭き門だが、そこから先も安定しているわけではない。Peters notes that high-school and university runners live under time-limited athletic scholarships and temporary residence arrangements, with graduation and the possibility of securing a corporate-team contract creating another major fork. [Peters 2024](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14725843.2024.2381493)

企業チームでも、契約更新はパフォーマンスに左右される。Injury or poor performance in important ekiden races can contribute to a return home. Even Olympic-level achievement does not automatically guarantee a permanent place.

しかも主要駅伝では外国人選手の起用数や区間が制限される。Hakone permits up to two foreign student athletes on the entry list but only one to run. The 2026 New Year Ekiden allows at most one foreign athlete in the team, while since 2025 the foreign-athlete-eligible section has been the 7.6km fourth leg. [関東学生陸上競技連盟規約](https://www.kgrr.org/pdf/about-kgrr/kiyaku.pdf)／[TBS ニューイヤー駅伝2026](https://www.tbs.co.jp/newyearekiden/outline/)／[住友電工 ニューイヤー駅伝2025](https://sumitomoelectric.com/jp/trackfield/ny2025)

つまり日本向けスカウト網に見つかり、日本のチームに選ばれ、来日し、チーム内競争を勝ち抜き、さらに大会の外国人枠に入って初めて、正月のテレビ画面に現れる。

What viewers perceive as “a fast Kenyan exchange student” is **the final output of multiple layers of selection**.

## 8. But “Iten runners do not come because Japanese scouts are absent” is still too simple

ここで因果を単純化すると、また間違える。The scarcity of Iten/Eldoret runners in Japan is not explained only by where Japanese recruiters happen to search.

世界的に評価の高いKalenjin系ランナーは、欧州や米国のロードレース、トラック大会、国際的なエージェント網に乗る機会が多い。Peters also notes that elite runners around Iten often orient toward European or American racing circuits rather than Japan. [Peters 2024](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14725843.2024.2381493)

So the corridor is not built by demand alone. Japanese schools and companies have places that are easier for them to recruit from, while athletes compare options: Japan may offer scholarships and stable team environments; Europe or the United States may offer different prize and professional opportunities.

エージェントはその間をつなぐ。

The geography of Kenyan runners in Japan is therefore **not a map of talent itself, but a map of transport routes created where both sides’ options overlap**.

## 9. A global market was not an open field; it was a collection of narrow corridors

調べる前、私はスポーツのスカウト市場を巨大なランキング表のように考えていた。Kenya’s fastest runners line up in order, and Japanese schools simply choose the athlete they want from near the top.

実際はもっと物理的で、人間くさい。Who watches which race? Which coach knows which agent? Who can send footage? Who can arrange a passport and school admission? Who will help when a teenager has trouble after arriving in Japan?

Each connection builds a thin corridor between Kenya and Japan. Talent near a corridor crosses the ocean; talent a little farther away enters another market or may never become visible to this one.

前作では、来日した外国人留学生を日本の大会がどの区間で走らせるかを見た。But upstream lies an invisible institution that helps decide **who arrives in Japan in the first place as a “foreign student runner.”**

A 3km restriction or one-runner rule is visible in competition regulations. A scouting network has no such public rulebook.

And precisely because it is less visible, it can shape the market even more strongly. **Talent is not simply discovered; it becomes “discovered talent” when it enters a corridor capable of carrying it.**
