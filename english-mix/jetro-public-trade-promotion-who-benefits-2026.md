# JETROとは誰のための組織か――Why Public Money Supports Exports
## History, services, costs and additional impactを読み解くEnglish Mix

A company exports its products and makes a profit. Then why should the government help? この素朴な疑問からJETROを調べ始めると、意外なほど広い組織が現れる。It supports exports, attracts foreign companies into Japan, facilitates partnerships, and even houses an institute studying developing economies. 国境を越える商売の、何を公共的な仕事と見なすかが問われる。

The key is to distinguish shared information from private business execution. 規制や商習慣の基礎情報は、多くの企業に役立つ。一方で個別の営業、契約交渉、顧客獲得は企業自身の仕事である。This distinction does not justify every subsidy, but it helps us ask a better question.

調査は2026年10月16日までの公開情報を対象とした。Main primary sources are the [JETRO profile](https://www.jetro.go.jp/jetro/profile.html) and the [FY2025 business report](https://www.jetro.go.jp/ext_images/jetro/profile/annual_report/jigyou2025.pdf). 公表された実績と独立した因果効果は区別する。

## 1. JETRO is not a company that sells exports for you

JETRO stands for Japan External Trade Organization. 日本語の正式名称は独立行政法人日本貿易振興機構。It became its present legal entity in October 2003 and operates under public policy objectives overseen by the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry. 「独立行政法人」は民間企業という意味ではなく、法定の公共目的のもとで活動する法人制度である。[組織概要](https://www.jetro.go.jp/jetro/profile.html)

Its services include market intelligence, information on regulations, trade consultations, business matching and inward investment support. ただし無料相談は営業代行ではない。JETRO expressly excludes tasks such as recommending particular trading partners, translating documents, making legal judgments on contracts, and executing business decisions. **Public support cannot replace your own sales team.** [相談範囲](https://www.jetro.go.jp/services/advice/)

## 2. The history begins in 1951, not 2003

The institutional roots go back to the Overseas Market Research Association established in March 1951. 1954年に海外貿易振興会が発足し、1958年に特殊法人日本貿易振興会となった。The Institute of Developing Economies was established in 1960, merged with the trade organization in 1998, and the current independent administrative agency started in 2003. JFOODO followed in 2017. [公式沿革](https://www.jetro.go.jp/ext_images/jetro/profile/annual_report/jigyou2025.pdf)

These changes show how the agenda expanded: from export promotion to business expansion, inward investment, research and international collaboration. ただし年表は変化の記録であって、各改革の原因まで直接証明するものではない。

An especially surprising detail is IDE-JETRO. 「アジア経済研究所」という名称だが、研究対象はアフリカ、中東、ラテンアメリカにも及び、政治や社会も扱う。Business support and academic research appear distant, yet both build knowledge of foreign societies. [アジ研の活動指針](https://www.ide.go.jp/Japanese/Info/ActivityGuidelines.html)

## 3. Four directions of support explain today's JETRO

First, outward expansion: Japanese companies seek customers and partners abroad. Second, inward FDI: foreign companies establish or grow operations in Japan. Third, overseas promotion of Japanese food products. Fourth, research and information services for business and policy. この四方向に、人材や地域経済の支援も重なる。[事業報告書](https://www.jetro.go.jp/ext_images/jetro/profile/annual_report/jigyou2025.pdf)

Inward foreign direct investment (FDI) is not an export. 海外企業が日本で事業を行うための投資である。JETRO helps with regulations, taxation, incorporation and cooperation with local institutions. The policy goal is wider than selling Japanese goods. [対日投資事業](https://www.jetro.go.jp/jetro/activities/fdi/)

JFOODO, created inside JETRO in 2017, focuses on consumer-facing promotion of Japanese food. 商談相手を見つけても、現地消費者が欲しがらなければ売れない。Market access and demand creation are different challenges. [JFOODO](https://www.jetro.go.jp/jfoodo/about.html)

As of April 2025, JETRO reported 76 overseas offices in 56 countries. As of April 2026, its staff numbered 1,980, including 1,278 in Japan and 702 abroad. 拠点数や職員数は組織の規模を示すが、そのまま経済効果には置き換えられない。[組織概要](https://www.jetro.go.jp/jetro/profile.html)

## 4. Reported success is different from proven impact

The FY2025 business report records 20,127 successful overseas-expansion cases for mid-sized and smaller companies, 113 foreign companies establishing or expanding activities in Japan, and 19,027 food-related business-meeting support cases. It also reports 7,169 invited overseas buyers for Japan Street and 15.97 million views of research articles. これらはJETROが公表した事業実績である。[2025年度報告書・2ページ](https://www.jetro.go.jp/ext_images/jetro/profile/annual_report/jigyou2025.pdf)

The same report's medium-term targets refer to success cases including expected outcomes. 「成功件数」がユニーク企業数や確定した輸出契約数と同じとは限らない。Definitions matter more than impressive-looking numbers. [同報告書・36ページ](https://www.jetro.go.jp/ext_images/jetro/profile/annual_report/jigyou2025.pdf)

A useful sequence is Input → Activity → Output → Outcome → Impact. 予算や人員、相談件数、商談機会、成約・継続取引、そして支援による追加的な変化。この階層を混同すると、活動の忙しさがそのまま経済効果に見えてしまう。

## 5. A budget is not revenue, and costs are not interchangeable

The FY2026 budget shown in the FY2025 report totals ¥71.986 billion, or 719億8,600万円. Its revenues include ¥49.036 billion in operating grants, ¥10.865 billion in national subsidies, ¥7.725 billion in commissioned income and ¥4.222 billion in business income. 財源は複数に分かれている。[事業報告書・50ページ](https://www.jetro.go.jp/ext_images/jetro/profile/annual_report/jigyou2025.pdf)

For FY2025, the reported administrative cost was ¥53.642 billion (536億4,200万円). Different year, different accounting definition. したがって2026年度予算と2025年度行政コストの差を「節約額」と呼べない。Always ask what a number measures before comparing it.

## 6. The economic argument is about market failure

Imagine a small food producer exploring an unfamiliar overseas market. Research into regulations, labeling and distribution requires upfront fixed costs. しかも調べて分かったことの一部は、ほかの事業者にも利用できる。

This suggests possible market failure: private firms may underinvest in information whose benefits spill over to others. 「情報の非対称性」は当事者間に知識の格差があること。「外部性」はある企業の行動が他者にも影響することだ。Shared public information can sometimes reduce duplicated costs. But proving a market failure is not the same as proving that a particular program is cost-effective.

A 2014 study of export promotion agencies in Japan and Korea found a positive statistically significant association with exports after controls, but the estimated effect became much smaller when endogeneity was addressed. もともと輸出が伸びやすい相手国で支援機関が活動していた可能性を考慮する必要がある。The paper does not prove that every JETRO service pays for itself. [Hayakawa, Lee and Park (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1111/deve.12048)

## 7. Additionality is the difficult test of public support

Suppose 60 out of 100 first-time exporters used JETRO. Did JETRO cause 60 new exports? Not necessarily. もともと輸出する準備のある企業ほど相談した可能性がある。This is selection bias.

The central concept is the counterfactual: what would have happened without support? 同じ企業について、支援を受けた現実と受けなかった現実を同時には観察できない。So evaluators may compare similar supported and unsupported firms, or use Difference-in-Differences to compare changes over time.

Criticisms worth testing include deadweight funding for activities that would occur anyway, unequal access across regions, crowding out of private consultants, and tension between policy missions and research independence. これらは検証の問いであり、JETROに不正があったと断定するものではない。JETROの監査資料と経済産業省の業務実績評価は公開されているが、評価制度の存在と因果効果の証明は別だ。[監査情報](https://www.jetro.go.jp/disclosure/info/inspectional_info.html)／[経産省評価](https://www.meti.go.jp/intro/koueki_houjin/a_index_14.html)

## 8. Practical use starts with a precise business question

Take a hypothetical Japanese sports-video analytics company entering a new market. The first questions are not “Can we export?” but “Who is the buyer?”, “Who controls video rights?”, “What privacy and data-transfer rules apply?”, and “Which country should we test first?” こうして調査対象を絞る。

JETRO asks users of its online consultation service to narrow targets to up to three countries or regions. 一枚のメモに対象国、顧客、サービス、契約方式、規制上の疑問を書いてから相談する。Use free guidance for general facts, then hire legal or commercial experts for decisions that the public service does not cover. [相談の案内](https://www.jetro.go.jp/services/advice/)

Some intensive hands-on programs are available after screening, with certain travel costs paid by JETRO. 無料という表記だけで誰でも対象になると解釈せず、募集要領を読む必要がある。[専門家支援](https://www.jetro.go.jp/consortium/expert/partner.html)

JETRO and JICA also have different primary roles. JICA focuses on development cooperation, including technical assistance and development finance. 貿易振興と国際協力には接点があるが、目的は同一ではない。[JICA概要](https://www.jica.go.jp/about/basic/jica/)

## 9. The vocabulary helps us read policy without being misled

**Export**は輸出、**Import**は輸入。**Inward FDI**は海外から日本への直接投資。**Trade policy**は通商政策、**EPA**は経済連携協定。Definitions are not decoration; they define what is being counted.

**Output**は活動によって直接生み出されたもの。**Outcome**は企業や市場で生じた結果。**Additionality**は支援がなければ起きなかった追加効果。**Counterfactual**はその比較のための反実仮想。**Crowding out**は公的支援が民間サービスの機会を圧迫すること。These terms help separate activity, impact and the public rationale.

## 10. The most important result is the invisible difference

The available sources establish JETRO's history, service boundaries, reported outcomes and budget. They do not provide a firm-level causal estimate of every program. 企業別の匿名化された継続売上、利益、非支援企業との比較や、全事業の費用対効果を取得したわけではない。

A stronger study would follow consultation → meeting → contract → repeat sales → profit, while comparing firms with similar starting conditions. 事業ごとの支出、採択基準、民間の代替策も必要だ。A reusable research instruction is: “Check official JETRO reports, audit records and program rules. Separate activity counts, reported success and estimated additional impact. Verify definitions and dates, identify missing data, and compare supported with similar unsupported firms where possible. Keep Japanese and English Mix versions consistent in figures and sources.”

Why should the state help private business cross borders? Because some information and relationships are hard for individual firms to build alone. しかしその公共的な理由が、すべての支援に当てはまるわけではない。The final question is how much value would not have existed without the support. 華やかな成功事例より、その見えない差分が評価の核心になる。
