# 20本でつかむ、ビールの味覚地図
## Crisp / Hop / Yeast / Malt / Sourを実在ビールで覚える

## 要旨

Trying to memorize beer styles one by one is a fast way to get lost.

Pilsner、Helles、Pale Ale、IPA、Hazy IPA、Weissbier、Witbier、Saison、Dubbel、Tripel、Porter、Stout、Gose、Gueuze。

The list keeps growing.

そこで今回は逆に考える。

**Don’t memorize flavors from names. Put the names onto a flavor map.**

使う地図は5つだけ。

- **Crisp / Clean** ―― すっきり、キレ、クリーン
- **Hop** ―― citrus, pine, tropical fruit, bitterness
- **Yeast / Spice** ―― バナナ、クローブ、胡椒、果実、発酵香
- **Malt / Roast** ―― bread, caramel, chocolate, coffee, roast
- **Sour / Wild** ―― 酸味、乳酸、果実、ファンキーな熟成香

For every beer, check the same four dimensions.

1. **Fermentation:** Lager, Ale, or Wild / Mixed?
2. **Dominant flavor:** 味の主役は何か
3. **Color:** Pale, amber, brown, or black?
4. **ABV:** 軽い、標準、強いのどこか

With these four axes, even an unfamiliar beer becomes predictable.

なお、ここで扱う20本すべてを厳密な意味でcraft beerと呼ぶわけではない。

Some are large or historic brands such as Asahi, Guinness, and Pilsner Urquell.

目的はcraft認定ではなく、**taste landmarks――味覚の基準点**を作ることだ。

---

## 1. Zone A ―― Crisp / Clean

### 1. Asahi Super Dry

- Style：International Pale Lager
- Fermentation：Lager
- Dominant：Clean / Dry
- Color：Pale
- Style ABV：4.5–6%

This is a useful starting point because it gives us a familiar definition of “clean.”

BJCP places Asahi Super Dry among examples of International Pale Lager, a style built around high attenuation, carbonation, refreshment, and restrained flavor intensity.

つまり「味がない」のではない。

It is designed so that malt, hops, and fermentation character do not dominate.

### 2. Pilsner Urquell

- Style：Czech Premium Pale Lager
- Fermentation：Lager
- Dominant：Malt + Hop balance
- Color：Gold
- Style ABV：4.2–5.8%

Now the same lager family becomes much more expressive.

パンのような麦芽感と、spicy / herbalなホップがはっきり見える。

**Lager does not mean flavorless.**

Pilsner Urquell is the landmark that proves it.

### 3. Weihenstephaner Original Helles

- Style：Munich Helles
- Fermentation：Lager
- Dominant：Soft Malt
- Color：Pale Gold
- Product ABV：5.1%

Helles looks close to Pilsner, but its center of gravity is different.

Pilsner puts more attention on bitterness and hop definition; Helles gives more room to smooth malt.

公式では5.1% ABV、21 IBU。

A useful contrast is:

**Pilsner = crisp + hop / Helles = soft + malt.**

---

## 2. Zone B ―― Hop

### 4. よなよなエール

- Style：American Pale Ale
- Fermentation：Ale
- Dominant：Citrus Hop + Malt balance
- Color：Amber Gold
- Product ABV：5.5%
- IBU：35

A very useful Japanese landmark for American Pale Ale.

Cascade hops give a citrus-like aroma, while gentle malt sweetness keeps the beer balanced.

IPAの一歩手前として覚えると分かりやすい。

**Hop-forward, but not all-in on bitterness or alcohol.**

### 5. Sierra Nevada Pale Ale

- Style：American Pale Ale
- Fermentation：Ale
- Dominant：Pine / Citrus Hop
- Color：Amber Gold
- Product ABV：5.6%
- IBU：38

This is one of the key reference points in American craft beer history.

公式ではCascade hopによるpineとcitrus、caramel maltがその土台を作る。

Compare it with よなよなエール and you start noticing that **the same style can speak with different hop accents.**

### 6. Bell’s Two Hearted Ale

- Style：American IPA
- Fermentation：Ale
- Dominant：Hop
- Color：Gold–Amber
- Style ABV：5.5–7.5%

This is where Pale Ale moves into IPA territory.

ホップの香り・味・苦味がさらに前へ出る。

The important shift is not simply “more bitter.”

**The whole balance of the beer moves toward hops.**

### 7. Tree House Julius

- Style：Hazy IPA
- Fermentation：Ale
- Dominant：Fruit-forward Hop
- Color：Pale / Hazy
- Style ABV：6–9%

Hazy IPA should not be remembered as merely “cloudy IPA.”

BJCP describes the family through intense fruit-forward hop aroma, soft mouthfeel, haze, and lower perceived bitterness than a traditional American IPA.

つまり、

**American IPA = dry / bitter / sharp寄り**

**Hazy IPA = juicy / soft / fruit-forward寄り**。

Same Hop zone, different direction.

---

## 3. Zone C ―― Yeast / Spice

### 8. Weihenstephaner Hefeweissbier

- Style：Weissbier / Hefeweizen
- Fermentation：Ale
- Dominant：Yeast
- Color：Pale / Hazy
- Style ABV：4.3–5.6%

Banana and clove.

この二語だけでWeissbierの輪郭はかなり見える。

The crucial point: those aromas are driven mainly by **yeast**, not hops.

### 9. Hoegaarden White

- Style：Witbier
- Fermentation：Ale
- Dominant：Wheat + Spice + Yeast
- Color：Very Pale / Hazy
- Style ABV：4.5–5.5%

It may look like Weissbier, but the aroma architecture is different.

Witbier traditionally brings coriander and orange peel into the picture.

覚え方はシンプル。

**Weissbier = banana / clove from yeast.**

**Witbier = citrus / coriander from spice plus fermentation.**

### 10. Saison Dupont

- Style：Saison
- Fermentation：Ale
- Dominant：Dry + Spicy Yeast
- Color：Pale Gold
- Style ABV：standard 5–7%

Saison is easier to understand through sensation than through the phrase “farmhouse ale.”

非常にdry、高炭酸、そしてpeppery / fruityな酵母感。

Saison Dupont is a classic reference point; the brewery’s technical sheet lists 6.5% ABV.

### 11. Duvel

- Style：Belgian Golden Strong Ale
- Fermentation：Ale + bottle refermentation
- Dominant：Fruit / Yeast + Dryness
- Color：Pale Gold
- Product ABV：8.5%

It looks light. It is not light in alcohol.

Duvel is 8.5% ABV, yet high carbonation and strong attenuation keep it remarkably lively.

ここで覚えることは一つ。

**Color and strength are separate axes.**

### 12. Westmalle Tripel

- Style：Belgian Tripel
- Fermentation：Ale
- Dominant：Spicy Yeast + Dry Malt
- Color：Gold
- Style ABV：7.5–9.5%

Put it next to Duvel and the Belgian map becomes clearer.

Tripel tends to show a little more rounded malt and spicy / phenolic yeast character, while Belgian Golden Strong Ale is often paler, lighter-bodied and more ester-driven.

強いのにdrinkable。

That contradiction is part of the style.

---

## 4. Zone D ―― Malt / Roast

### 13. Chimay Red

- Style：Belgian Dubbel
- Fermentation：Ale
- Dominant：Dark Malt + Fruity Yeast
- Color：Amber–Brown
- Style ABV：6–7.6%

Dubbel is dark, but it is not primarily a roast beer.

Think caramel, bread, raisin, plum, and dark fruit.

黒さではなく、**malt + Belgian yeast**の組み合わせを見る。

### 14. Fuller’s London Porter

- Style：English Porter
- Fermentation：Ale
- Dominant：Malt / Chocolate
- Color：Brown–Dark Brown
- Style ABV：4–5.4%

Porter and Stout overlap historically, so rigid borders are risky.

学習上は、London Porterをchocolate、toast、brown malt方向の基準点として置くと分かりやすい。

Dark does not automatically mean coffee-like.

### 15. Guinness Draught

- Style：Irish Stout
- Fermentation：Ale
- Dominant：Roast / Coffee
- Color：Very Dark Ruby–Black
- Style ABV：3.8–5%

This beer destroys the idea that “black means strong.”

Irish Stout emphasizes roast, coffee-like dryness, and relatively modest strength.

見た目より軽い。

That contrast is one of the most useful beer lessons.

### 16. Samuel Smith Oatmeal Stout

- Style：Oatmeal Stout
- Fermentation：Ale
- Dominant：Roast + Soft body
- Color：Dark Brown–Black
- Style ABV：4.2–5.9%

Now compare it with Guinness.

Oats can add smoother texture, grainy or nutty impressions, and enough sweetness to soften roast.

同じStoutでも、dryとsoftは別方向。

### 17. Aecht Schlenkerla Rauchbier Märzen

- Style：Rauchbier / Smoked Märzen
- Fermentation：Lager
- Dominant：Smoked Malt
- Color：Dark Amber
- Product ABV：5.1%

Smoke is neither hop nor yeast.

Schlenkerla uses its own smoked malt and brews a bottom-fermented Märzen; the brewery lists 5.1% ABV and 30 bitterness units.

ここでMalt axisが一気に広がる。

Malt can give bread, caramel, chocolate — and even **smoke**.

---

## 5. Zone E ―― Sour / Wild

### 18. Rodenbach Grand Cru

- Style：Flanders Red Ale
- Fermentation：Mixed fermentation / aged
- Dominant：Tart fruit + Wood
- Color：Red–Brown
- Style ABV：4.6–6.5%

Think wine-like acidity, red fruit, wood, and age.

Flanders Red shows that beer can build structure with acidity rather than hop bitterness.

ここまで来ると「ビール＝苦い」の定義は完全に崩れる。

### 19. Cantillon Classic Gueuze

- Style：Gueuze
- Fermentation：Wild / spontaneous tradition
- Dominant：Sour + Funk
- Color：Gold
- Style ABV：5–8%

Gueuze blends Lambics of different ages and gains complexity through aging and refermentation.

酸味だけではない。

Leather, hay, earth, fruit, and other **funky** notes can form part of the experience.

### 20. Original Ritterguts Gose

- Style：Gose
- Fermentation：Ale + lactic acidity
- Dominant：Sour + Salt + Coriander
- Color：Pale
- Style ABV：4.2–4.8%

Gose is one of the easiest sour styles to identify conceptually.

酸味にsaltとcorianderが重なる。

Compared with Gueuze, it is usually a brighter and more immediately readable flavor structure.

---

## 6. Put all 20 onto one map

### Crisp / Clean

Asahi Super Dry → Pilsner Urquell → Weihenstephaner Original Helles

Clean can branch toward dry, hop, or malt.

### Hop

よなよなエール → Sierra Nevada Pale Ale → Bell’s Two Hearted → Tree House Julius

Pale Ale moves toward IPA, then splits again between sharper bitterness and softer haze.

### Yeast / Spice

Weihenstephaner Hefeweissbier → Hoegaarden White → Saison Dupont → Duvel → Westmalle Tripel

“Fruity” can come from completely different mechanisms.

### Malt / Roast

Chimay Red → Fuller’s London Porter → Guinness Draught → Samuel Smith Oatmeal Stout → Schlenkerla Märzen

Dark fruit, chocolate, coffee, oats, smoke.

「濃色」は一つの味ではない。

### Sour / Wild

Rodenbach Grand Cru → Cantillon Gueuze → Original Ritterguts Gose

Wine-like, funky, salty-citrusy.

Acidity has grammar too.

---

## 7. Five questions for any unknown beer

When you see an unfamiliar name on a menu, ask only five things.

**Q1. Lager or Ale or Wild?**

発酵の大分類。

**Q2. What is the star?**

Hop, Malt, Yeast, or Sour?

**Q3. Pale or Dark?**

色。ただし色から強さを決めつけない。

**Q4. Dry or Sweet / Soft?**

What happens in the finish?

**Q5. ABV?**

最後に強さを見る。

These five questions turn a menu into a map.

---

## 8. Practice

### 1

“6.5% / hazy / tropical / soft mouthfeel / dry hopped”

→ **Hazy IPA** is the first hypothesis.

### 2

“5% / banana / clove / wheat / high carbonation”

→ **Weissbier / Hefeweizen**.

### 3

“4.2% / black / coffee / dry / nitro”

→ **Irish Stout**.

### 4

“8.5% / golden / highly carbonated / fruity / dry”

→ **Belgian Golden Strong Ale**.

### 5

“4.5% / pale / tart / coriander / salt”

→ **Gose**.

Memorizing names is useful.

But **reverse-engineering a style from its description** is far more powerful in real life.

---

## 9. The main idea

Beer becomes confusing when you classify it only by color.

「AleかLagerか」だけでも足りない。

Style names alone are not enough either.

Use two layers.

**Fermentation × Dominant Flavor × Color × ABV**

and

**Crisp / Hop / Yeast / Malt / Sour**.

Once you have those layers, an unfamiliar beer is no longer completely unfamiliar.

「たぶんこの辺にいる」と置けるようになる。

Expertise is not memorizing every name.

**It is being able to place the unknown on a map you already understand.**
