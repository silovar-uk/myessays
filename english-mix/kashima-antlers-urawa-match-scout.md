# 鹿島はいま、13 scored / 10 conceded。『強い』より『殴り合いに強い』が近い

The first number that jumped out at me was not “4 wins from 5”.

**13 goals scored, 10 conceded.**

鹿島は3位、4勝1敗。That sounds like a normal strong Kashima start. でも失点数まで並べると、かなり違う顔が見える。

**They score a lot. They also allow games to become very open.**

2026年9月6日18時、浦和はメルカリスタジアムで鹿島と対戦する。今の鹿島を一言で言うなら、a perfectly controlled champion というより、**a team that can survive chaos and still hit harder at the end** に近い。

And Leo Ceara has eight goals in five league matches. チーム総得点13の約62％。

Eight in five is ridiculous.

## Start with the five league games

![鹿島の2026/27 J1開幕5試合。横浜FMに4-3、名古屋に2-1、福岡に3-2、東京Vに2-0、水戸に2-4。4連勝のあと初黒星。](assets/kashima-2026-09-06/recent-five.svg "Figure 1｜Winning, yes. Calm, not really.")

- 7 Aug: Yokohama F. Marinos 3-4 Kashima
- 15 Aug: Kashima 2-1 Nagoya
- 22 Aug: Kashima 3-2 Fukuoka
- 29 Aug: Tokyo Verdy 0-2 Kashima
- 2 Sep: Mito 4-2 Kashima

Four straight wins, then the first defeat. 鹿島は勝点12で3位。That works out to **2.6 goals scored and 2.0 conceded per match**.

J.League's club stats list 648.8 passes per game and 58.3% average possession. 鬼木達監督のチームらしく、自分たちでボールを動かし、相手を押し込む時間を作れる。

But the official match preview also points to an uncomfortable pattern: runners getting in behind too easily and duels being lost. Five games, ten goals conceded.

So this is not simply “classic Kashima, defend and win 1-0”.

**The ability to move the game is a strength. The risk is that the game moves too much.**

## Strength 1: Leo Ceara keeps bringing a goal into the match

Eight goals in five matches, top of the league scoring chart.

He scored twice against Yokohama FM, once against Nagoya, once against Fukuoka, twice against Tokyo V, twice against Mito. In other words, **he has scored in every league match so far.**

The patterns are varied too.

- set-piece finish
- attacking a cross
- finishing after Yuma Suzuki's lay-off
- reacting to a loose ball
- quick finishing inside the box

浦和側からすると、cutting the final pass to Leo is not enough. Once the ball enters the box, the defence has to finish the phase — including second balls and rebounds.

## Strength 2: Yuma Suzuki creates the action before the action

If you only watch Leo, you miss half of Kashima's attack.

Against Mito, Gaku Shibasaki played forward, Yuma Suzuki cushioned the ball with his chest, and Leo finished. Against Fukuoka, Suzuki assisted Cavric.

鈴木優磨の価値は、CBと競ることだけではない。He receives, attracts pressure, protects the ball and gives the next player time to face forward.

**Leo is often the final strike. Yuma helps create the shape in which that strike becomes possible.**

That creates an awkward decision for Urawa. If a centre-back follows Suzuki too aggressively, space can open behind or around him. If midfield drops too deep to help, Shibasaki gets more room to look up.

## Strength 3: Shibasaki + Matheus Bueno give them repeated access forward

The recent base shape looks close to a 4-4-2, although positions change during possession.

![鹿島の基準配置イメージ。前線にレオ セアラと鈴木優磨、中央に柴崎岳とマテウス ブエノ、両翼にエウベルと松村優太。サイドバックの小川諒也と広瀬陸斗も高い位置へ出る。](assets/kashima-2026-09-06/attack-map.svg "Figure 2｜A reference shape, not a prediction of today's XI.")

J.League described Kashima's win over Tokyo V as a game in which they played through a tight press with fluent passing, highlighting the double pivot of Shibasaki and Matheus Bueno.

The chance-creation totals as of 2 September are also telling: Ryoya Ogawa 10, Shibasaki 10, Leo 10, Yuta Matsumura 9, Suzuki 8.

**Creation is distributed.** It does not live only in one No.10.

So if Urawa press high, the question cannot just be “who do we stop?” It has to be: **where do we want Kashima to pass, and where do we plan to win the next ball?**

## Strength 4: the bench can still score

The opening match against Yokohama FM is the loudest example.

Kashima were 1-3 down. Leo scored in the 81st minute. Then substitute Aleksandar Cavric scored at 90+9 and 90+12. 4-3.

Cavric already has three league goals.

So surviving the starting front line does not mean the attacking threat is over. **The scoring threat can remain late into the game.**

For Urawa, even a lead entering the final 20 minutes would not make this a simple “close the match” situation.

## Where can Kashima be attacked?

They lost 2-4 at Mito in the previous round.

It is tempting to call four goals conceded a derby anomaly. But the official J.League preview connects it to issues already visible from the opening round: space behind the line and problems winning decisive duels.

Mito's second goal came from a counter after Kashima had moved forward, with goalkeeper Tomoki Hayakawa also caught high. Other goals came from a second phase after a corner and a central dribble into shooting range.

![鹿島がボールを持つときのリスク。サイドバックと中盤が高く出た背後でボールを失うと、センターバック脇と最終ライン背後に大きなスペースが生まれる。浦和は奪った直後の1本目を前向きに出せるかが鍵。](assets/kashima-2026-09-06/transition-risk.svg "Figure 3｜The strength and the risk come from the same thing: committing players forward.")

This is the key tension.

鹿島はボールを持てる。That allows full-backs and midfielders to move higher.

But **being able to go forward is not the same as being ready for the instant the ball is lost.**

For Urawa, the important moment may be the first few seconds after regaining possession — before Kashima can restore their forward-facing defensive shape.

## Three things to watch from an Urawa perspective

### 1. Do not let Yuma Suzuki receive the first ball comfortably

Stopping Leo alone is insufficient. 誰が鈴木に競り、誰が挟み、誰がsecond ballを拾うのか。The whole sequence matters.

### 2. Attack the space behind Kashima's full-backs immediately

Ogawa and Rikuto Hirose going high helps Kashima create. At the exact moment possession is lost, that same positioning can create an exit for Urawa.

Can Urawa play forward with the first pass? Can they release into the channel before Kashima's block resets?

### 3. Treat the final 30 minutes as a different match

Cavric and Koki Tagawa can change the front line. 前半を耐えれば終わり、ではない。If the score is level or separated by one goal after 60 minutes, the bench becomes part of the tactical problem.

## And then there is Mercari Stadium

Before kick-off today, Kashima are **unbeaten in 21 consecutive official matches at Mercari Stadium**.

浦和も直近5回のリーグ対戦で鹿島に勝てていない。Recent examples include 0-1 in September 2025, 2-3 in February 2026 and 0-1 in April 2026.

So yes, the venue and matchup history are heavy.

But this version of Kashima also gives opponents something clearer to attack than a pure control team would.

Urawa arrive with their own chaos. They started with three defeats, then produced two straight comeback wins against Yokohama FM and Fukuoka. Their defence has conceded 15 in five, which is even worse than Kashima's ten.

That is why today's game may not be decided by which side looks more “complete”.

**It may be decided by which team converts its own instability into the opponent's problem first.**
