# Zero, One, or Two Shots?――マリノス対ヴェルディの「二つの沈黙」

## 10月21日の国立決戦。Two teams, two different problems in front of goal

How many shots did Tokyo Verdy take against FC Tokyo on October 17? **Zero, one, or two?** ヴェルディ公式の試合後会見では質問者が「0本」、相馬監督が「公式記録は1本」と述べている。FC東京の公式記録はone shot。ところがＪリーグの試合ページはtwoと表示する。[Verdy's official interview](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/info/2026101708/report) / [FC Tokyo's match data](https://www.fctokyo.co.jp/match/schedule/result/20261017/) / [J.League match record](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/101708/)

This is a small but revealing data problem. 記録時点や判定基準など、差異の原因はまだ特定できていない。Rather than choosing the most convenient number, we should preserve the disagreement. The robust finding is simpler: Verdy generated very little attacking threat and lost **0–4**, after playing most of the match with ten men.

That is our entry point. 横浜F・マリノスも直近のリーグ2試合では得点していない。But the shared outcome—zero goals—does not imply a shared cause. This essay separates confirmed facts, tactical interpretations and questions to test while watching the match. 情報基準日は**2026年10月19日**。No independent full-match video coding has been conducted.

## 1. When, where, and which competition?

The fixture is **Wednesday, October 21, 2026, at 19:00 JST**, at **MUFG Stadium (the National Stadium)**. It is matchday 11 of the **2026/27 J1 League**, with Yokohama F. Marinos officially designated as the home club. DAZN and NHK BS are scheduled to carry the match. 春の「Ｊ１百年構想リーグ」と混同しないこと。[Official Marinos fixture](https://www.f-marinos.com/matches/20261021/event/) / [Verdy schedule](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/schedule/) / [J.League broadcast information](https://www.jleague.jp/j1/tv/stream/search-list/?club=yokohamafm&enddate=2026-10-31&period=custom&startdate=2026-10-01)

After ten matches, **Marinos sit eighth: 16 points, 4 wins, 4 draws, 2 losses, goal difference +4**. **Verdy are twentieth: 4 points, 0 wins, 4 draws, 6 losses, goal difference −14**. [Official table](https://www.f-marinos.com/matches/tables)

The gap is real. だがleague position is not a complete model of current performance. It tells us what has happened, not how either team might respond to the next opponent.

## 2. Marinos: six unbeaten, yet struggling to score

Marinos have gone **six consecutive J1 matches without defeat** since their August 29 loss to Urawa: two wins and four draws. They beat Mito 2–0 on September 19, then drew **0–0 with Cerezo Osaka on October 10 and JEF United Chiba on October 17**. They have kept six clean sheets in their first ten league games. [Official results](https://www.f-marinos.com/matches/results) / [Post-match reporting](https://www.soccerdigestweb.com/news/detail/id%3D198603)

A clean sheet is a defensive achievement, not proof of a complete attacking system. Against Chiba, Marinos struggled at first when pressed high up the pitch. The second half improved, but no goal followed. Importantly, they did score in recent domestic cup matches. **Two consecutive league blanks are not an all-competitions scoring drought.** [Marinos results](https://www.f-marinos.com/matches/results)

Head coach **Steve Corica**, appointed for this season, praised his team's defensive work after the Chiba match while acknowledging problems with progression against pressure and the quality of final actions. **Kaina Tanimura** is a major goal threat, but the team also needs contributions from surrounding attackers. [Coaching appointment](https://www.f-marinos.com/news/team/10188) / [Official coaching interview](https://www.f-marinos.com/tricolore_plus/article/1562) / [Press report](https://news.livedoor.com/article/detail/32591281/)

**Working hypothesis:** Marinos have strengthened the platform from which to attack. Next comes the harder part: creating better decisions for defenders rather than simply passing the ball more often. ヴェルディが積極的に前へ出るか、深く引くかで必要な打開策は違う。

## 3. Verdy: the manager changed; the winless run did not

Verdy have **four league goals and 18 conceded in ten matches**—just **0.4 goals scored per game**. They lost **0–2 to Sanfrecce Hiroshima on October 11** and **0–4 to FC Tokyo on October 17**. The broader run matters more than one disastrous derby. [Official Verdy fixture list](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/schedule/)

On **October 5**, the club ended its contract with **Hiroshi Jofuku**, who had led the team back to J1 in 2023. On **October 8**, it appointed **Naoki Soma** as head coach. 監督交代は新しい解決策を探す行動であると同時に、積み重ねてきたやり方を組み替える行動でもある。[Jofuku announcement](https://www.verdy.co.jp/news/15272) / [Soma announcement](https://www.verdy.co.jp/news/15277)

Under Soma, the starting formation has shifted from a **3-4-2-1** to a **4-4-2**, according to the J.League's match preview. These numbers describe a starting shape, not a fixed pattern for every minute. [Official J.League preview](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/101708/)

Two games are a very small sample. Losing twice does not establish that the new formation failed. More useful questions are whether the team can regain possession at the intended height, move it forward, and avoid immediately giving it away. The new coach's impact should be judged in these repeated actions—not by the chalkboard numbers alone.

## 4. The 0–4 defeat changed completely in minute 18

Against FC Tokyo, Verdy's forward **Itsuki Someno was sent off in the 18th minute**. FC Tokyo scored after 41 minutes and added three goals in the second half. Soma described a sequence involving Someno's dismissal, an injury to Yuya Fukuda, a set-piece concession and a penalty for handball. [J.League match](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/101708/) / [Verdy post-match interview](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/info/2026101708/report)

It would be wrong to project the **ten-man 0–4** directly into the next **eleven-a-side** match. But it would also be wrong to excuse the entire winless start because of one red card. The structural scoring problem existed before the derby.

Listen to the players. **Kai Shibato** explained that early long passes behind the defence were deliberate and shared within the team. Captain **Koki Morita**, returning as a substitute, argued for retaining the ball instead of endlessly clearing it back to the opposition. Soma wanted faster entry into the attacking half after regaining possession. These ideas can coexist: **direct play only helps if someone can secure the next action**. [Official player and coach interviews](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/info/2026101708/report)

One change is confirmed, not speculative. Soma explicitly stated that **Someno will not play in the next match**, because of suspension. The October 17 lineup included **Kim Hyun-woo**, with **Kazunari Ichimi** later appearing as a substitute. **Kanya Fujimoto**, recently returned on loan, and new defender **Cho Yu-min**, who made his league debut, are among the personnel worth watching. Their October 21 starting status was not confirmed on October 19. [Verdy interviews](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/info/2026101708/report) / [Fujimoto signing](https://www.verdy.co.jp/news/15235) / [Cho signing](https://www.verdy.co.jp/news/15239)

The question is no longer just "Can Verdy score?" It is **Who provides the outlet, who supports it, and how can the team retain possession long enough to create a second action?**

## 5. The 1993 opener is history, not a predictive model

On **May 15, 1993**, the first match in J.League history was **Verdy Kawasaki vs Yokohama Marinos**, played at the old National Stadium. Meyer scored the league's inaugural goal; Everton and Ramón Díaz scored as Yokohama won **2–1**. [J.League's historical goals video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aFp84q7amOM) / [J.League interview with Kazushi Kimura](https://www.jleague.jp/special/anniversary/25th/interview/kimura.html)

The October 2026 match also takes place at the National Stadium site. ただし現在の競技場は建て直された施設であり、1993年の旧国立と同じ建物ではない。Its current naming-rights title is MUFG Stadium. [Stadium operator notice](https://jns-e.com/news/20260101-436/)

A closer historical result is February 28, 2026, when Marinos beat Verdy **3–2** in the separate **J1 100 Year Vision League**. Tanimura and Someno were both among the scorers. [Official match record](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/022801/)

The temptation is to expect another five-goal thriller. Yet competitions, managers, player availability and form have changed. **Head-to-head history is context, not a forecast.** The same club names can house very different working teams.

## 6. Five questions to test during the match

Do not simply watch the scoreboard. Try these five observational questions at roughly ten minutes, half-time and after substitutions. They are **hypotheses**, not statements about confirmed formations.

1. **Where does Verdy's press begin?** Do they attack the Marinos centre-backs or wait in a compact mid-block? The first line of defence changes the build-up problem.
2. **Who moves Marinos forward?** Can Riku Yamane, Jun Amano or Kosuke Matsumura receive facing upfield? How many runners support Kaina Tanimura beyond the wide players such as Ryo Miyaichi or Tomoki Kondo? These are recent participant names, not a confirmed starting XI. [Marinos October 17 lineup](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/101707/lineup/)
3. **What happens after Verdy regain the ball?** Does the first pass reach a teammate? Can that player hold it and find a second pass? This is especially revealing without Someno.
4. **How do the teams react to the first goal?** An opener may force the other side to alter its risk tolerance. Or the game may remain scoreless; both outcomes must remain possible.
5. **What does a substitution actually change?** Does it alter a running pattern, passing route, defensive shape or simply freshen the legs? Watch the five minutes before and after.

A good observation is not always a correct prediction. 試合を見て仮説が外れたなら、それは収穫だ。A live match should challenge the viewer's model, not merely confirm it.

## 7. Three roles: skeptic, researcher, process designer

**The skeptic** asks us to reject easy shortcuts. An eighth-place side is not automatically efficient in attack; a 0–4 loss with a red card is not a clean measurement of eleven-player quality; two games cannot isolate a new manager's effect; 1993 nostalgia cannot determine 2026 tactics.

**The researcher** goes back to primary sources: official schedules, full result lists, the coaching announcements, player testimony and disciplinary consequences. For the conflicting 0/1/2 shot figures, the correct status is **unresolved discrepancy**, not a fabricated consensus.

**The process designer** turns the preview into a repeatable observation workflow: verify matchday lineups and absences; revisit the five questions during the first half, second half and substitutions; after the game, separate **prior hypothesis / observed action / revised interpretation / remaining unknown**. No measured pressing intensity, passing tempo or shot quality should be claimed without adequate underlying video or event data.

This is the difference between a pre-written match narrative and a useful preview. 予習の役割は勝負の結論を決めることではない。It is to prepare questions the match can answer.

## 8. Two kinds of silence at the National Stadium

Back to the strange 0/1/2 disagreement. Verdy struggled to generate shots in the derby. Marinos, by contrast, have become harder to break down but have drawn their last two league games without scoring. These are **two different attacking problems**, linked by the same empty goals column.

Verdy need to build a functioning route from recovery to attack under Soma, without Someno. Marinos need to turn defensive security into a more productive attack under Corica. Neither challenge is captured by the league table alone.

In 1993, these two clubs represented the beginning of professional league football in Japan. In 2026, the question is less grand and more immediate: **can each side find the next useful action when an opponent disrupts its plan?** That is what I would watch for before counting the goals.
