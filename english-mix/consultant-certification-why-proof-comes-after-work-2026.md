# 資格を取る前に、5年働いてください――What does a consulting certificate prove?

「Please certify me as a consultant.」そう言って認定制度のページを開くと、応募条件は**five years of consulting experience**。資格を取る前に、もう5年働いている。The order feels backward. まずそこが気になる。

これは実在する制度だ。全日本能率連盟の「認定マネジメント・コンサルタント（J-CMC）」は、five years of professional consulting practiceを前提とし、書類、小論文、面接などで審査する。上級のJ-MCMCではten yearsが前提となる。これは**learning to become a consultant**ではなく、**recognition of experience already gained**に近い。[公式制度](https://www.zen-noh-ren.or.jp/certification/mc-2/overview/)

資格とは普通、study → exam → certificateだと思っていた。ところが最初の例はwork → evidence → recognition。The sequence is reversed. そこで資格取得と、実際に顧客の問題を解く能力の関係を調べ直す。情報確認日：2026年10月18日。Confirmed facts, interpretations, thought experiments, and proposalsは分けて扱う。

## 1. 資格には「学習」「証明」「機会」という別々の役割がある

資格の価値は少なくとも三つに分けられる。**Learning**は知識を身につける価値、**signaling**は能力を他者へ伝える価値、**access**は仕事や機会に入る価値。同じcertificateでも三つの比率は違う。

会計のknowledgeは資格証がなくても使える。逆に経験審査型のcertificateは、新しい技能の教材とは限らない。Suppose certificate holders earn more. それでも資格が給与を上げたとは言えない。Experienced people may be more likely to obtain it. この混同を**selection bias（選択による偏り）**と呼ぶ。

Before ranking certificates, decide what you want to buy: knowledge, a credible signal, or access. 目的が変わればevidenceも変わる。試験範囲を読むのか、採用担当者に聞くのか、求人要件を数えるのか。その違いが重要だ。

## 2. 有名な資格を並べると、試験の対象が驚くほど違う

候補は七つ。中小企業診断士、日商簿記2級、統計検定2級、**Project Management Professional（PMP）**、ITストラテジスト、**Professional Scrum Master I（PSM I）**、**Entry Certificate in Business Analysis（ECBA）**。ECBAの日本語名「ビジネスアナリシス入門認定」は説明のための訳で、公式和名と断定しない。

A single label, “consulting certification,” hides different exams. 診断士は経営全体、簿記2級は記帳と原価、統計検定2級は推測と分析。PMP focuses on project leadership; ITストラテジストはbusiness and IT strategy、PSM IはScrum、ECBAはbusiness analysisの基礎を扱う。[診断士](https://www.chusho.meti.go.jp/shindanshi/2026/260406shindanshi.html)／[簿記](https://www.kentei.ne.jp/calendar_2026)／[統計](https://www.toukei-kentei.jp/grade/grade2/)／[PMP](https://www.pmi.org/certifications/project-management-pmp)／[ITストラテジスト](https://www.ipa.go.jp/shiken/kubun/st.html)／[PSM I](https://www.scrum.org/assessments/professional-scrum-master-i-certification)／[ECBA](https://www.iiba.org/ecba)

Prices can mislead too. 2026年度の簿記2級は5,500円、統計検定2級は7,000円。PSM I costs US$200 per attempt; ECBA lists a regional package of US$395 or less. これは**exam or advertised package prices**, not total costs. Training, materials, retakes, renewals and study time still matter.[簿記](https://www.kentei.ne.jp/calendar_2026)／[統計](https://www.toukei-kentei.jp/grade/grade2/)／[PSM I](https://www.scrum.org/assessments/professional-scrum-master-i-certification)／[ECBA](https://www.iiba.org/ecba)

## 3. 2026年の試験改訂は、古い比較表を簡単に壊す

First, PMP changed on **9 July 2026**. The **Business Environment** share rose from 8% to 26%, while People moved from 42% to 33% and Process from 50% to 41%. AI, sustainability, stakeholder engagement and business outcomes received greater emphasis. 旧版の解説書だけで判断すると、今の試験像を見誤る。[PMI](https://www.pmi.org/certifications/project-management-pmp/new-exam)

Eligibility matters more than an attractive ranking. 大卒者の通常経路では、**36 non-overlapping months** of leading projects within the past ten yearsと、原則35時間のeligible trainingが必要。さらに2026年12月1日からlive instructor-led trainingの提供主体要件が変わる予定だ。イベント運営の経験が自動的に認定されるわけではない。[PMP requirements](https://www.pmi.org/certifications/project-management-pmp)

Second, the 中小企業診断士 examination abolished its second-stage oral examination from 2026. 第1次受験料は14,500円→17,200円、第2次は17,800円→15,100円。The combined fee stayed the same. 試験合格と登録完了は別段階であり、登録にはadditional practical requirementsがある。[中小企業庁](https://www.chusho.meti.go.jp/shindanshi/2026/260205shindanshi.html)

Third, the ITストラテジスト examination sits before a planned 2027 reform. IPA proposes a **Professional Digital Skills Examination** with management, data/AI and systems domains. 新制度の名称・区分はまだ検討事項を含む。This is a planned transition, not permission to describe every draft as final.[IPA](https://www.ipa.go.jp/shiken/minaoshi/index.html)

## 4. あえて極端な比較をする。資格欄以外が同じ二人なら

Now a deliberately extreme **thought experiment**. Two applicants have identical work history, outcomes, portfolio and interview performance. Only one holds PMP. If evaluators favor that applicant, the certificate provides a signal under those conditions. 条件を固定することで、資格欄の影響だけを考える。

Change the setup. A has a certificate but vague achievements. B has no certificate but can explain budgets, stakeholders, decisions and before-and-after metrics. Now multiple variables change, so we **cannot isolate the causal effect** of the credential. しかし現実の採用では、こうした複合的な比較が起こる。

Important: **neither experiment was actually run with recruiters**. We also have not completed the proposed 45-job-posting dataset. Real validation would classify requirements as required, preferred or absent, control for seniority and domain, and test anonymized résumé variants with assessors. 数を調べていない以上、資格の市場評価を実証済みとは言えない。

## 5. 試験勉強をやめるのではなく、試験と実務を競わせる

The answer is not “never take exams.” A well-designed syllabus can be a useful **learning scaffold**. 統計では偶然の変動と施策効果を区別する。簿記ではrevenue and cash flowが違う理由を考える。These skills matter whether or not you buy the exam ticket.

Imagine a fixed budget of **90 study hours**. Plan A: 90 hours of exam preparation. Plan B: 90 hours of realistic case work. Plan C: 45 hours each. The number is arbitrary; it is **not an estimated certification study requirement**. We have not observed which plan wins. The point is to compare the opportunity cost of each route.

Measure all three plans on the same dimensions: factual accuracy, issue decomposition, evidence discipline, execution design and communication. Also record exam scores, but do not let them define the only outcome. **Blind evaluation** by more than one reviewer would reduce some bias. これは提案したresearch designであり、実施済みの比較試験ではない。

## 6. 調査の途中で、ランキングではなく条件分岐が必要になる

A conditional view, not a popularity ranking: PMP may fit project and PMO leadership if eligibility is met; 中小企業診断士 fits broad management knowledge; 簿記2級 fits accounting gaps; 統計検定2級 fits analytical inference. ITストラテジスト connects technology and strategy; PSM I covers Scrum; ECBA introduces business analysis. These are **fit hypotheses**, not measured labor-market rankings.

Sometimes **not taking the exam yet** is rational. You may be ineligible, the syllabus may not match your immediate work, or practical skills may be more urgent. 制度変更を待つ価値もある。The options include taking the exam, learning without certification, building a portfolio, or postponing the decision.

Compare practical transfer, personal skill gaps, market signal, total burden and future options. But weights are subjective. A person seeking a new job and a person trying to deliver better work may rationally choose different routes. **Sensitivity analysis** asks whether the recommendation survives changes in those weights.

## 7. 次の調査では「資格が役立った話」ではなく反証を探す

The next research step should seek **disconfirming evidence**, not testimonials. Who succeeds without certificates? When does a certificate fail to help? What changes between required and preferred credentials? 誰が、何を、どの基準で評価したかまで追う。

Proposed follow-up: collect **45 job postings**, 15 each from strategy/new business, project and operations transformation, and data/IT. Log date, firm, seniority, required experience, credential status, salary and job content. Control for duplicates and source bias. このdatasetは**not yet collected**であり、推定結果をここで作らない。

Another route is a **skills diagnostic** using publicly released syllabi and questions. Classify errors into knowledge gaps, quantitative analysis, reading the question and exam-specific procedure. Respect copyright and exam rules. そうすると、必要なのがpass the examなのか、do the job betterなのかを分けられる。[IPA official resources](https://www.ipa.go.jp/shiken/minaoshi/index.html)

## 8. 90日間で、資格取得の是非を小さく決着させる

A proposed **90-day decision cycle**: Days 1–14, define target work, test sample questions and check eligibility. Days 15–30, trial exam study against practical work. Days 31–60, produce a financial model, project plan or analysis report and ask others to review it. Days 61–90, compare improvement and total cost; decide to sit the exam, continue learning without certification, or pause. It does **not** promise certification within 90 days.

Write the **stop rules** before studying. If eligibility cannot be confirmed, costs remain opaque, or learning fails to improve relevant work, reconsider. If concrete feedback identifies a skill gap, job requirements demand the credential, or a concept visibly improves a deliverable, continuation has a stronger case. These are proposed decision rules, not universal thresholds.

## 9. 名刺の一行ではなく、仕事の変化を測る

Back to the strange opening: **work five years before being certified as a consultant**. It sounds circular. But some credentials help you learn before practice, while others validate practice after it happened. Ranking them on one scale mixes different products.

A new line on a résumé can have value. But its cost is time you could have used to build judgment and demonstrable results. The more useful question is: **Which decision, in which real project, will I make better afterward?** When you can answer that, a certificate stops being a collectible and becomes a tool.
