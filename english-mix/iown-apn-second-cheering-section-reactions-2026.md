# 拍手は同期した。でも観客の反応は？

*The Second Cheering Section — technology is not yet demand*

Imagine a stadium crowd clapping. Miles away, another crowd claps at almost the same moment. This is a surprisingly human way to describe a telecom experiment. 通信網の性能を説明するのに、なぜ拍手なのか。Because latency becomes tangible when people try to share one moment.

But here's the odd part: we can read a great deal about synchronized applause, and far less about what the participants actually thought. The network connected two places. Did the experience connect with an audience? **Technical feasibility is not the same as customer demand.**

対象は2026年5月14日に公表されたNTT西日本グループと毎日放送（MBS）の実験。本稿は2026年10月11日を基準に、official claims, media coverage, independent commentary, and first-person reactionsを区別する。Not finding publicly indexed feedback does not prove that no feedback exists. [Official release](https://www.ntt-west.co.jp/news/2605/260514a.html)

## 1. 「第二の応援席」は、ただの巨大テレビではない

The experiment took place in late March 2026 and combined three screens of over 300 inches, wide-angle 8K images, low-latency optical transmission, and individualized audio commentary. アストロデザインが撮影と表示補正、NTTソノリティが開放型音声機器「ヌーム・ワン（nwm ONE）」で協力した。[NTT West](https://www.ntt-west.co.jp/news/2605/260514a.html)

The central technology is APN, the All-Photonics Network, under NTT's IOWN vision. APNは大容量で遅れの少ない拠点間通信を目指す全光通信網。Latency is the gap between an event and its appearance on a remote screen. Even a small delay can disrupt a shared cheer, so synchronized clapping is a useful metaphor. [NTT technology overview](https://www.rd.ntt/iown/0002.html)

Yet low latency does not mean literally zero latency. The May release does not publish a detailed end-to-end delay measurement, a conventional-network comparison, or a quantified audience response. **A successful demonstration is one kind of evidence; audience satisfaction is another.**

## 2. 反応は三層に分かれ、肝心の「観客の声」は公開情報が薄い

First, media coverage. 『ビジネスネットワーク』が5月14日、『新Screens』が15日に報じ、ASCII.jpなどにも紹介記事が載った。But multiple placements do not equal multiple independent endorsements. Several texts mainly report the release or reproduce its contents. [BUSINESS NETWORK](https://businessnetwork.jp/article/34493/)／[Screens](https://www.screens-lab.jp/article/31159)／[ASCII.jp](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/402/4402104/)

Second, industry commentary. DEINKO's May 14 newsletter highlights the intuitive power of matching claps while raising questions about network deployment and cost. This is a conditional positive assessment, not a user survey. さらに同記事の通信網の状況は5月時点の見立てであり、10月時点の提供状況と区別したい。[DEINKO](https://note.com/deinko/n/nc47250ab32a1)

Third, the audience itself. We could not verify detailed first-person participant reviews, the number of attendees, repeat-visit intention, or willingness to pay for this particular trial from the publicly accessible material checked. 非公開の調査や検索されない投稿まで「存在しない」とは言わない。The evidence gap is the finding. **The press reaction is visible; the customer reaction is not yet clearly documented.**

## 3. 「世界初」を字面どおり読むと、2022年の銀座でつまずく

A three-screen immersive sports event was not invented in 2026. KDDI organized such public viewings in Tokyo's GINZA 456 in September 2022, featuring former football and baseball players as guests. 実施報告には参加者の声や質問の場面も残っている。[2022 announcement](https://kyodonewsprwire.jp/release/202209166670)／[Post-event account](https://home.kingsoft.jp/news/pr/prwire/202210067746.html)

So what exactly is the claimed world's first? The footnote defines a narrow combination: IOWN APN's low latency plus acoustic extended reality using open-ear devices, mixing ambient venue sound with individual commentary at a remote public viewing. It is explicitly marked as company research as of March 2026. [NTT's footnote](https://www.ntt-west.co.jp/news/2605/260514a.html)

The innovation claim concerns **the architecture of the combination**, not the invention of group viewing or panoramic screens. 自社調べによる限定的な新規性と、独立機関による世界全例の検証も別物だ。Being precise about “first” helps us see what was actually engineered.

## 4. 耳をふさがない実況は、8Kよりも観客を選ばない発明かもしれない

A shared sports venue holds different audiences. One visitor wants the crowd noise; another wants detailed tactical commentary. Traditional sealed headphones can isolate the listener, while loud public commentary can overpower the room. 開放型の音声機器は、会場音と隣の人の声を残したまま解説を重ねる。[Official description](https://www.ntt-west.co.jp/news/2605/260514a.html)

Think of it as **one shared picture, multiple possible explanations**. Beginners could receive a short primer, experienced fans more technical analysis, and international visitors another language. These are proposals, not features confirmed in this experiment. ここにある価値は、単なる解像度ではなく「違う楽しみ方の人が同じ場所にいられる」という包摂性かもしれない。

## 5. 毎日放送との関係を遡ると、「観戦」より前に「制作」を変えていた

The collaboration has a production-side prehistory. In December 2025, MBS used IOWN APN during a high-school rugby broadcast experiment, relocating functions such as AI-assisted slow-motion generation and captions to a data center. 2026年1月にNTT西日本が公表した。[January release](https://www.ntt-west.co.jp/news/2601/260108a.html)

For broadcasters, the question is where production resources must sit. For spectators, it is where the feeling of being at the event can happen. Same network, different part of the value chain. **First reorganize production, then potentially reorganize attendance.**

A later update matters: on September 18, 2026, NTT West announced an expansion of its commercial All-Photonics Connect service across its western Japan service area. That does not guarantee immediate provision at every address or disclose the cost of this particular event. 5月の「通信網が限られる」という評論を、そのまま10月の事実として引用するのは危険だ。実際の拠点と見積条件に置き直す必要がある。[September announcement](https://www.ntt-west.co.jp/news/2609/260918b.html)／[Service overview](https://business.ntt-west.co.jp/service/network/iown/)

## 6. 観戦の反応を知りたければ、「画質がよいですか」だけでは足りない

Here is a proposed experiment rather than a claimed result. Compare ordinary large-screen public viewing with versions that add personalized audio and the complete low-latency experience. Ask about immersion, synchronized cheers, ease of conversation, intention to return, and willingness to pay. 可能なら「画面だけ」「画面＋個別音声」「全部入り」の条件を分ける。Otherwise the team cannot tell which expensive component creates value.

Segment by use case, not just average satisfaction. A supporter who cannot travel, a family member new to the sport, and an analyst-minded fan may value the same feature differently. **Who gains what over a normal viewing, and how much would they pay for the difference?** それが技術実証の次の検証課題だ。

## 7. クラブが売るのは通信回線ではなく、遠征できない日の応援席だ

Potential payers include attendees, venue operators, municipalities, and sponsors. But potential payers are not verified customers. Revenue could involve seating, food, merchandise, sponsorship, and event rentals. Against that sit imaging equipment, production, dedicated connectivity, staff, and media rights. 既存施設を使う場合でも機材は無料ではないし、試合映像の上映には権利処理が要る。[J.League video rules](https://www.jleague.jp/corporate/services/videoutil/)

For perspective, Yokohama DeNA BayStars' THE LIVE connects screenings with food and merchandise; Cosm in the US combines specialized filming, a dome screen, content partnerships, and venue operations. Neither is an identical IOWN implementation. Their common business question is more useful: **why would people leave home to watch a screen together?** [THE LIVE](https://dena.com/jp/news/5296/)／[Cosm](https://www.cosm.com/)

提案するなら、一度きりの派手な技術ショーではなく、遠征できない日の小さな応援拠点から。Tie the event to local sponsors, member benefits, F&B, and repeatable production workflows. Shared infrastructure may improve the economics, but profitability remains an untested hypothesis.

## 8. 実証の次に要るのは、拍手を揃える装置ではなく「また来る」という証拠だ

Synchronized applause is an elegant proof of low latency. It makes a complex network legible to humans. Yet press attention should not be mistaken for market validation. That distinction becomes sharper, not weaker, as we investigate the reactions.

A “second cheering section” becomes real when people return for the next match, after the operators have dealt with cost, rights, and everyday operations. 技術の問いは「拍手を揃えられるか」。事業の問いは「その場所で、次も一緒に応援したくなるか」。**What we most need next is not a new speed claim, but the audience's reason to come back.**
