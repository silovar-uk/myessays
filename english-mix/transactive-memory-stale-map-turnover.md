# 一人だけ入れ替える方が、全員入れ替えるより危ない

## Strong teams can get lost in an old map.

前作で、team memoryを「全員の頭の中にある知識の合計」と考えるのをやめた。What matters is also the **directory of who knows what, and the retrieval paths leading to those people**. すると次の問題は自然に出てくる。そのdirectoryに載っている人が異動したり退職したりしたら、チームは何を失うのだろう。

直感では、loss should scale with headcount. 一人抜けるより三人抜けるほうが痛く、全員入れ替わればもっと痛そうに見える。But transactive-memory research breaks that simple arithmetic. **A team replacing only one member can perform worse than a team rebuilt from scratch.**

This is not a story about weak newcomers. むしろ逆で、**既存メンバーがうまく協調できていたからこそ、その協調の前提が古くなったときに壊れやすい**という話である。Team memory contains expectations such as “this person handles this kind of problem.” 人が替わったのに期待だけ残れば、memory is not merely missing; it becomes wrong.

## 1. What disappears first is the accuracy of the address.

2007年、Kyle Lewis、Maura Belliveau、Benjamin Herndon、Joshua Kellerは、membership changeとgroup cognitionの関係を実験した。They compared intact groups, totally reconstituted groups, and groups in which only one member was replaced. ポイントは単なる能力比較ではなく、already-existing TMS structureとnew memberのexpertiseがどう噛み合うかを見ることだった。

The result was strange. 一人だけ新しいメンバーが入ったグループは、安定したグループだけでなく、**全員を組み直したグループよりも成績が悪かった**。Incumbents kept using the division of cognitive labor built with the old member and expected the newcomer to occupy the same slot. しかし当然、新任者のknowledge and strengthsは前任者と同じとは限らない。

What happened was not simply a vacancy but a misdelivery. 「この論点はBさん」が常識だったのに、Bさんが抜けたあともthe query keeps going to the same coordinate. 後任者が別の強みを持っていても、古いmapの上では見つからない。So turnover loss cannot be measured only by how much knowledge leaves; it also depends on **how convincingly the old retrieval path continues to look correct**.

この見方をすると、「属人化」の意味も少し変わる。Personal dependency is not only a state in which one person alone holds information. 組織全体が「あれはあの人」というsingle routeに慣れすぎて、routeを更新する方法を持っていない状態も属人化である。The risk lies not only in concentrated knowledge, but in **frozen destinations for questions**.

## 2. If a successor is treated as a predecessor-shaped blank, new expertise disappears.

引き継ぎでは、つい「Aさんがやっていた仕事をBさんへ渡す」と考える。It imagines work as a box that can be lifted and handed to the next person. 分かりやすいが、Lewisらの結果を通すと別の危険が見える。The box also contains the predecessor's judgment habits, strengths, weaknesses, consultation partners, and tacit division of labor.

Suppose the predecessor handled both contract review and the history of negotiations with a client. 後任者は法務知識には強いが、past contextは知らない。このとき「契約関係は全部後任者」というmapを維持すると、legal judgment and historical context remain bundled into one imaginary role. 実際には「審査は後任者」「過去経緯は古参メンバー」「数字は経理」と分解したほうが正確かもしれない。

Then onboarding has to change too. 新人に旧来のmapを覚えさせるだけでは足りない。Existing members must relearn **what this new person actually knows**. Onboarding is therefore not one-way adaptation by the newcomer; it is also **the organization updating its own memory structure around the newcomer**.

## 3. A brief act of reflection reduced the damage of the old map.

ここまで読むと、every membership change seems to require a giant knowledge-management project. しかしLewisらの補足研究には、もっと地味で興味深い結果がある。When incumbents were asked to **explicitly reflect on their collective knowledge** before beginning the task, the process inefficiencies associated with partial membership change could be avoided.

No lost knowledge magically returned. 前任者の頭を復元したわけでも、新任者へ大量のmanualsを読ませたわけでもない。The team simply stopped replaying the old division of labor automatically and reconsidered who should handle what now. それだけで、an outdated map moved closer to present reality.

This has a strong implication for handoffs. 引き継ぎ資料を増やすことはinformation volumeを増やす。しかし古いretrieval pathsを温存したまま資料だけ厚くすると、「どこを見るか」「誰に聞くか」の誤りは残る。What may be needed is not only information transfer but **a deliberate moment to break the old role hypothesis**.

だから最初の問いは、「前任者は何をしていたか」だけではない。It should also be: “Given the team we have now, who should know and own what?” 前任者中心の文章から現在のチーム中心の文章へ主語を替えた瞬間、handoff becomes redesign rather than preservation.

## 4. More connections do not always make a team more resilient to turnover.

では、普段から全員がよく話すチームならmapの更新も速いのだろうか。Not necessarily. Linda Argote、Brandy Aven、Jonathan Kushが109組の4人チームで行った研究では、the effect of turnover depended on the **shape of the communication network**.

With stable membership, fully connected decentralized networks were strong. 直接話せる経路が多いため、members can learn who knows what and develop a strong TMS. ところが一人が入れ替わると、その強みが逆向きに働いた。Incumbents communicated less with the newcomer and tended to fall back on the previous coordination logic.

Hub-and-spoke networks were less flexible under stability, but turnover changed the picture. 「まず中央へ伝える」というsimple coordination rule was easy to see, dyadic communication with the newcomer increased, and the knowledge map could be updated more effectively. The researchers concluded that communication networks condition the effect of turnover. Source: <a href="https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/orsc.2017.1176">Argote, Aven & Kush (2018)</a>.

So virtues like “flat” and “open” have a time dimension. 安定時にlearningを最大化する構造と、membership change時にreconfigureしやすい構造は同じとは限らない。A resilient team may not be one that keeps the same structure forever, but one that can temporarily make its coordination logic visible when change occurs.

## 5. The answer is not “document everything.”

If TMS is vulnerable to turnover, the obvious reaction is “remove human dependency and write everything down.” しかしLinda ArgoteとYuan Renは、organizational knowledgeの二つのrepositoryとして**routines**と**transactive memory systems**を比較している。Routines are relatively resilient to turnover, while TMSs are more vulnerable; meanwhile TMSs can be especially valuable for uncertainty and innovation. Source: <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0191308516300065">Argote & Ren (2016)</a>.

A monthly invoice process is a good candidate for a routine. 反対に、「契約上は可能だが、この文脈では別の配慮がいる」「この障害は仕様書の原因だけでは説明できない」といった例外は、multiple experts may need to be combined. A 2026 study again examined how routines mediate TMS and team performance in small dynamic teams, and discussed how explaining existing routines may help newcomers adapt. Source: <a href="https://academic.oup.com/icc/article/35/4/955/8419670">Industrial and Corporate Change (2026)</a>.

So “de-personalization” does not mean pushing every bit of knowledge into documents. 反復できる判断はroutinesへ結晶化し、例外や新しい問題はexpert networkへ残す。その境界を意識するほうがorganizational memoryは丈夫になる。A handoff is also the act of deciding **which knowledge should live in people and which should live in systems**.

## 6. Bus Factor is better understood as lost routes, not only lost people.

Software engineering has the slightly grim concept of Bus Factor or Truck Factor: how many key developers would need to disappear before a project stalls. 近年の研究では、code historyだけで「誰が知っているか」を推定する方法には限界があり、reviewsやmeetingsなど複数のknowledge channelsも見る必要がある。A study involving 269 engineers argued that important knowledge generation and distribution channels extend beyond version-control systems. Source: <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.01523">Jabrayilzade et al. (2022)</a>.

Applied to ordinary organizations, counting only people starts to feel strange. 一人が辞めても、そのknowledgeへのalternate routesが三本あれば耐えられるかもしれない。逆に五人が同じ情報を持っていても、everyone still needs one key person to validate a decision, so the real single point of failure remains one.

A 2023 review synthesized 91 empirical studies on knowledge loss induced by organizational member turnover. 影響や予防策は文脈に依存し、単純な「退職者の知識を保存せよ」では足りないことが見える。Sources: <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/org/science/article/pii/S0969647423000587">Galan (2023, Part I)</a> and <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/org/science/article/pii/S0969647423000551">Part II</a>.

What we really want to measure may be network fragility. 誰かが消えたとき、どのknowledge domainが孤立するのか。Which judgments become unverifiable? Which consultation path has no backup? **A dependency audit looks less like a list of names and more like a list of arrows we cannot afford to lose.**

## 7. Put one “new map” at the end of the handoff document.

ここまで調べると、what we leave in a handoff also changes. 従来の引き継ぎ書は、担当業務、操作手順、取引先、年間スケジュールを並べる。それらは必要である。ただ、そこへ「現在のチームで何を誰に聞くか」「一人で完結しない論点はどこか」「前任者と後任者で得意分野が違う箇所はどこか」を一枚追加すると、the document becomes a search map rather than only an instruction manual.

That map should not be treated as finished. むしろ最初の一か月で書き換わる前提のほうがよい。As the successor discovers “this issue belongs with C” or “this decision needs data expertise,” the arrows are redrawn. 既存メンバーも同じmapを見て、自分たちが無意識に前任者へ向けていたarrowsを発見できる。

Then a good handoff is no longer measured by page count. 前任者がいなくなったあともquestions reach the right placeか、新任者のnew expertiseがmapへ反映されたか、例外時に誰と組むべきかが分かるか。その三つが生きていれば、一部のinformationを忘れてもteam can retrieve or reconstruct it.

前作では、「覚えていないのに、誰に聞けばいいかを知っている」こともmemoryだと考えた。This sequel makes that idea slightly more dangerous. **Knowing who to ask is memory; continuing to know who to ask exactly as before can become false memory.** 引き継ぎとは、knowledgeを過去から未来へ運ぶ作業ではない。It is the act of doubting an inherited map and redrawing it for the people who actually exist now.
