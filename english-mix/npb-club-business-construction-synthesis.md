# 球団経営という一つの全体をどう組み立てるのか — Assembling a Club's Business as a Whole
## Revenue structure・fan・sponsorship・stadium ownershipを、一つの意思決定システムとして読む

第11回から第14回まで、球団経営を支える4つの土台を、一つずつ読んできた。

- Revenue structure (Episode 11)
- Fan (Episode 12)
- Sponsorship (Episode 13)
- Stadium ownership (Episode 14)

第14回の最後に、こう問いを置いた。

**So how do these four foundations actually combine into one whole — a club's business as a going concern?**

今回はここから始める。これは、第5〜9回の5つのmechanismを第10回で統合したのと、構造的に対になる回である。

> **Information basis: 2026年9月1日**
>
> This article synthesizes what Episodes 11–14 already established rather than pursuing fresh primary research. It doesn't evaluate or rank any specific club's management.

---

## 1. SHORT ANSWER — the four foundations aren't four separate stories

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
球団経営を理解する出発点は、収益構造・ファン・スポンサーシップ・球場所有が、**not four independent topics but one system that shapes each other's conditions**だという点である。
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
Episode 11's revenue structure (club-by-club by default, league-level only for the Japan Series) is actually generated by fan behavior from Episode 12 — attending, watching, joining a fan club. That attention feeds into Episode 13's sponsorship (uniform ads, in-stadium signage, naming rights), and who owns the stadium that hosts all of it, as Episode 14 showed, sets the boundaries of what a club can decide on its own.
<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
The naming-rights negotiation layer from Episode 13 changing with stadium ownership structure (Episode 14) is concrete evidence that these four aren't independent.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
つまり、4つの土台は「収益構造→ファン→スポンサーシップ→球場所有」という一直線の話ではなく、**stadium ownership sets the range of what's possible for the other three**という、階層のある関係になっている。
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
球団経営を読むには、4つを個別に理解するだけでなく、**which one is currently constraining the others**を見る必要がある。

---

## 3. WHO DECIDES — stadium ownership bounds what the other foundations can do

As Episode 14 showed, whether a stadium is publicly-owned/club-operated, club-owned, or acquired changes how much a club can decide entirely on its own.

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
That decision-making range **spills over into the other foundations — revenue structure and sponsorship**.
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
Under public ownership with club operation, signage rights or part of naming-rights authority tend to need coordination with the municipal owner. Under club ownership or after an acquisition, the club can decide over a wider range on its own.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
つまり、同じ「スポンサーシップを増やしたい」というNEEDに対しても、球場所有の型によって、球団が単独で実行できる施策の範囲が異なる。
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
第10回のCONSTRAINTが支配下枠・タイミングという編成上の制約だったのに対し、球団経営レベルのCONSTRAINTは、**the ownership structure of a physical asset — the stadium**から生まれている。

---

## 4. WHO PAYS / WHO BENEFITS — mapping four stakeholders

それぞれの土台には、異なる利害関係者が関わっている。

| Stakeholder | What they bear | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| Parent company (Ep.11) | Covering deficits, etc. | Advertising value (including the tax treatment) |
| Fans (Ep.12) | Ticket price, membership fees, time | The experience of watching, a tie to the home club |
| Sponsor companies (Ep.13) | Ad fees, naming-rights fees | Exposure, a brand association |
| Facility owner (Ep.14) | Capital burden for construction/major renovation (if owner) | Usage fees, regional draw |

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
この表が示すのは、**running a club's business means pulling resources — money, attention, authority — from these different stakeholders and converting them into the product that is a baseball game**だということである。
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
球団自身は、これらの利害関係者の「間」に立ち、それぞれとの関係を調整する立場にある。

## 5. Club Business Construction Audit — four questions

ここまでの議論を、第10回のRoster Construction Auditと対になる形でまとめる。

球団経営のニュースや動きを見るとき、次の4つを確認する。

### 1. FOUNDATION
Which of revenue structure, fans, sponsorship, and stadium ownership is the central variable right now?

### 2. WHO DECIDES
How does this club's stadium ownership structure shape its decision-making range?

### 3. WHO PAYS / WHO BENEFITS
What does the parent company, the fans, the sponsors, and the facility owner each bear, and each get?

### 4. LINK BACK TO ROSTER
How does this club's business standing connect to Episode 10's budget CONSTRAINT?

## 6. LINK BACK TO EPISODE 10 — closing the loop on where CONSTRAINT comes from

第10回では、契約更改やFA・トレードで提示できる条件を「the budget-shaped CONSTRAINT」と呼んだ。当時は、その発生源を「球団単位の収益構造」とだけ述べていた。

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
第11〜15回を経て、そのCONSTRAINTの発生源をより具体的に言えるようになった。**Budget grows out of a combination of all four foundations — revenue structure (Ep.11), fan attention (Ep.12), sponsorship (Ep.13), and stadium ownership (Ep.14).**
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
How strong each foundation is can differ club by club, but budget isn't set by any single one of the four channels alone.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
つまり、第10回のRoster Construction AuditのCONSTRAINTと、第15回のClub Business Construction Auditは、別々のframeworkではなく、**one system stacked on top of the other**である。
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
PLAYER SYSTEM（Episode 1〜10）とBUSINESS SYSTEM（Episode 11〜15）は、ここで初めて明示的に接続された。

## 7. A minimal worked example

たとえば、ある球団が「有力な外国人選手と契約したい」というNEED（第10回）を抱えているとする。

- FOUNDATION: maybe sponsorship revenue is up this year, or owning its own stadium has widened its revenue discretion — which foundation is moving changes the picture.
- WHO DECIDES: if the stadium is club-owned, new attendance drives or event revenue are easier for the club to decide on its own, making revenue easier to forecast.
- WHO PAYS / WHO BENEFITS: additional support from the parent company, a new sponsorship deal, rising attendance — several channels can be working at once.
- LINK BACK TO ROSTER: together, these shape how much room there is under the CONSTRAINT for what the club can offer in contract renewal, FA, or a trade.

This isn't an evaluation of any specific club's actual management decisions — just a minimal example of how to use the frame.

---

## 8. WHAT THIS SYNTHESIS DOES NOT MEAN

### Synthesis ≠ a simple causal link between a good roster and revenue

"A good roster always increases revenue" or "revenue alone builds a good roster" are not claims this article makes. The four foundations and the CONSTRAINT condition each other; they aren't a simple one-way causal chain.

### Synthesis ≠ evaluating a specific club's management

The Club Business Construction Audit isn't a scorecard for any specific club. It's a set of questions for reading the news.

### The PLAYER/BUSINESS symmetry ≠ the series' overall design principle

That Episode 10 and Episode 15 mirror each other structurally is one observation, not evidence that the whole series was designed around this symmetry.

## 9. TAKEAWAYS

- Revenue structure, fans, sponsorship, and stadium ownership aren't four separate stories — they're one system that shapes each other's conditions.
- Stadium ownership structure (Episode 14) bounds what's possible for the other foundations (revenue structure, sponsorship).
- Four stakeholders — parent company, fans, sponsors, facility owner — sit in different burden/benefit relationships.
- The Club Business Construction Audit (FOUNDATION / WHO DECIDES / WHO PAYS-BENEFITS / LINK BACK TO ROSTER) lets you read club-business news as one system.
- Episode 10's budget CONSTRAINT grows out of the combination of these four foundations — PLAYER SYSTEM and BUSINESS SYSTEM are now explicitly connected.
