# 市場規模とは、結局だれの財布を数えているのか
## The market isn't one number. It depends on what you count.

市場規模が500兆円。Wow, that sounds like an enormous business. But what if the same ingredient is sold several times before it reaches the final customer? **One product can generate several transactions.** だから「市場規模が大きい」と「最終消費者がそれだけ払う」は、同じではない。

A real illustration: 日本の経済産業省が2025年8月に発表した2024年の国内電子商取引市場は、**B-to-C（消費者向け）が約26.1兆円**、**B-to-B（企業間）が約514.4兆円**。Same country, same year, same ministry, radically different transaction boundaries. これは誤植ではない。何をcountするかが異なる。[経済産業省・2024年対象の調査](https://www.meti.go.jp/press/2025/08/20250826005/20250826005.html)

This essay starts from a simple question: **What exactly are we counting?** 調査の歴史、公的統計、会社の売上、価値の二重計上、新規事業の推計、倫理的な限界を順に見ていく。We will also run an intentionally over-serious hypothetical experiment. 実在する統計と架空の計算は明確に分ける。

## 1. そもそも市場とは、何が起きる場所なのか

A market is not necessarily a physical place. 朝市なら売り手と買い手が同じ場所にいる。しかしbroadcast rights、software subscriptions、撮影業務の委託契約には、共通の市場の建物がない。Markets are relationships among buyers, sellers, products and possible substitutes.

したがって **market boundary（市場の境界）** が重要。Consider coffee. 「飲料全体」「喫茶店のコーヒー」「自販機の缶コーヒー」では違う市場になる。You must specify the product, customer, geography, period and transaction stage. さらに、コーヒーからお茶へ乗り換える人がいるように、substitutes（代替商品）も境界を曖昧にする。

Market size（市場規模）は、defined marketにおける取引・需要の大きさだ。It may be measured in yen, customers or units. **An annual spending figure and a customer count are not interchangeable.** 「何円か」以前に「何を、どの期間で数えるか」を決める。[米国中小企業庁の市場調査ガイド](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/plan-your-business/) ／ [カーネギーメロン大学教材](https://www.cmu.edu/swartz-center-for-entrepreneurship/education-and-resources/project-olympus/images/strategiesstoriespdfn.pdf)

## 2. 「市場規模」の中には、少なくとも六種類の数字が住んでいる

The phrase “market size” hides several different measurements. 日本語で整理しておこう。

- **Consumer spending（消費者支出）**：生活者が買ったもの・体験へ支払う金額。Sport spectators might pay for tickets, transport and food, not just admission.
- **Revenue／sales（売上高）**：企業が売った商品・サービスから得る売上。売上税などの取扱いをそろえる。
- **Gross transaction value（取引総額）**：商流で成立した取引の総額。A marketplace's GMV is not the platform's own fee revenue.
- **Production value／shipments（生産額・出荷額）**：産出や出荷の尺度。They are related to—but not always identical to—sales.
- **Value added（付加価値額）**：produced value minus intermediate inputs（生産した価値から原材料や外注サービス等の中間投入を引いた部分）。
- **Potential demand（潜在需要）**：まだ成立していない購入を含めた可能性の推計。It rests on assumptions rather than observed transactions.

The US Census Bureau explicitly uses different measures across industries. 欧州連合統計局（Eurostat）もturnover、production value、value addedを区別する。The OECD defines value added as output minus intermediate consumption. **Different numbers can all be legitimate, but they answer different questions.** [米国国勢調査局](https://www.census.gov/topics/business-economy/production.html) ／ [欧州連合統計局](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/EN/sbs_h_esms.htm) ／ [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/data/indicators/value-added-by-activity.html)

最後にstock versus flow（残高と期間中の流れ）。10万人の登録者はある時点の人数、50億円の年間支出は一年間のflowだ。Multiply customers by purchase frequency and price to estimate annual sales—but don't silently mix units.

## 3. コーヒー一杯を三回売ると、市場は三倍に膨らむのか

Let's invent a very small supply chain. これは会計の構造を見せるための架空例で、実際のコーヒー原価ではない。

- Farmer → wholesaler: **100円**
- Wholesaler → café: **150円**
- Café → final customer: **500円**

Add every sale and you get **750円**. But the final customer spends only **500円**. What happened to the other 250円? Nothing mysterious: intermediate transactions are counted again at later stages. 取引の合計と最終的な消費額は別の指標なのだ。

For a simplified value-added calculation, assume no outside input for the farmer and only the coffee bean itself as input for the next stages. Farmer adds 100円; wholesaler adds 50円; café adds 350円. **Total value added = 500円.** 実際には電気・水・家賃のサービス部分・輸入・税など、さらに多くの調整が必要だ。

Does this mean 750円 is “wrong”? No. If you're selling software to wholesalers, trade volume matters. If you're studying final household consumption, the 500円 matters more. The lesson is not “always use GDP.” It is **choose the transaction stage before you calculate**. 国際通貨基金（IMF）は、国内総生産の生産面を各段階の付加価値から説明している。[IMFのGDP解説](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/fandd/issues/series/back-to-basics/gross-domestic-product-gdp)

## 4. 人間はいつから、市場を「調査」しはじめたのか

Merchants have watched customers for centuries. But systematic market research expanded with mass production, national advertising and the need to understand people the seller had never met. 顔の見える常連客だけでは、全国で何が売れるか分からなくなったのである。

In 1911, Charles Coolidge Parlin（チャールズ・クーリッジ・パーリン）began professional research for a publishing company, studying industries such as agriculture and automobiles. In 1923, Arthur C. Nielsen（アーサー・C・ニールセン）founded his company; retail and media measurement expanded in the following decades. Sampling, audience measurement and survey methods helped turn intuition into repeatable evidence. [統計学系雑誌『Significance』の歴史解説](https://academic.oup.com/jrssig/article/18/2/12/7038128) ／ [ニールセンの沿革](https://sites.nielsen.com/timelines/our-history/)

日本の経済センサス―活動調査（Economic Census for Business Activity）は、企業や事業所の売上、費用などを体系的に把握する。2026年6月にも実施された。Yet a census is not the same thing as a perfect database; coverage exclusions, reporting and adjustment still matter. [総務省統計局](https://www.stat.go.jp/data/e-census/2026/index-2.html)

The historical shift matters: measuring past sales is one problem; estimating demand for a product that doesn't exist yet is another. **They may use similar spreadsheets, but not the same quality of evidence.**

## 5. 公的統計でさえ、「業界」の境目に悩んでいる

An industry classification is like a map. It helps comparisons, but new businesses cross boundaries. スポーツ配信なら、競技団体、映像制作、配信、広告、情報サービスが交わる。

In Japanese business statistics, **enterprise industry（企業産業分類）** reflects an enterprise's main activity, while **establishment industry（事業所産業分類）** refers to the activity at a location. **Business activity（事業活動）** and **product（生産物）** are additional lenses. The industrial classification used for the survey changed from its 13th to its 14th revision for 2024 onward. 同じ名称の時系列でも、分類が変われば比較に注意が要る。[統計局・用語の解説](https://www.stat.go.jp/data/kkj/kekka/yougo.html)

A census aims to cover its defined population; a sample survey estimates from a subset. どちらでもnonresponse（無回答）、coverage gaps（対象漏れ）、estimation adjustments（推計補正）が問題になる。日本の経済構造実態調査も、回答されたデータ等から全体値を推計する方式を明示している。[推計方法](https://www.stat.go.jp/data/kkj/kekka/index.html)

Public statistics are valuable for transparent, comparable definitions; specialized surveys can be more relevant for a very narrow new service. **Authority does not replace fitness for purpose.** 偉い数字より、問いに合う数字を選ぶ。

## 6. 新規事業で使う三つの市場――全体・提供可能・獲得可能

Startups often use three nested market sizes. まず意味を理解してから略語を覚えよう。

- **TAM — Total Addressable Market（理論上の全体市場）**：the total theoretical revenue opportunity for the defined product if all relevant potential buyers purchased it.
- **SAM — Serviceable Addressable Market（提供可能市場）**：the part reachable with your current product, geography, regulations and sales channels.
- **SOM — Serviceable Obtainable Market（獲得可能市場）**：the part you could realistically win over a specified period after competition and execution limits.

There are variations in how practitioners expand and define these terms. ここでは上のmeaningに固定する。[アマゾン広告のTAM・SAM・SOM解説](https://advertising.amazon.com/ja-jp/library/guides/tam-sam-som) ／ [大学の市場規模教材](https://www.cmu.edu/swartz-center-for-entrepreneurship/education-and-resources/project-olympus/images/strategiesstoriespdfn.pdf)

「大きな市場の1%を取ればよい」はbeautiful arithmetic, but weak strategy. **Who signs the contract? Who holds the budget? Why would they choose you?** それに答えられなければ、one percentはまだ売上計画ではない。

## 7. わざとやりすぎる実験――架空の映像サービスを三通りに数える

Let's invent a sports-video workflow service: organizing, checking and delivering match footage for Japanese sports organizations. **Everything in this experiment is hypothetical.** 実在する競技団体の購入予算や、市場の調査結果ではない。

**Method A: bottom-up estimation（積み上げ法）.** Assume 200 possible customers, 12 paid jobs a year and 150,000円 per job.

> 200 × 12 × 150,000円 = **360,000,000円／年（3億6,000万円）**

Easy to calculate, easy to challenge. Where did 200 come from? Do all organizations really order 12 times a year? Is 150,000円 a price customers will accept? 積み上げ法は、疑うべき仮定をはっきり見せる。

**Method B: top-down estimation（絞り込み法）.** Suppose the total relevant spending pool is 1.2 billion円（12億円）, and the service addresses 30%.

> 12億円 × 30% = **3億6,000万円**

Same result. But that is no validation: both 12億円 and 30% were invented to land at the same number. **Agreement between two calculations is meaningless if their assumptions are not independent.** 同じ結論を後付けで作っただけなら、検証のふりをした計算だ。

**Method C: apply business constraints.** Of the assumed 200 organizations, imagine only 80 are serviceable under your present offering; perhaps 15 can actually be contracted in the planning horizon.

- **TAM:** 200 × 12 × 15万円 = **3億6,000万円／年**
- **SAM:** 80 × 12 × 15万円 = **1億4,400万円／年**
- **SOM:** 15 × 12 × 15万円 = **2,700万円／年**

これも現実の市場規模ではなく、考え方の練習。To justify SOM, you need evidence about leads, conversion rates, procurement cycles, channel reach and actual contracts. Comparing independent estimates is called triangulation（複数手法による照合）. それは数字を三つ並べる儀式ではなく、**異なる証拠をぶつけ、食い違いを調べる行為**である。[米国中小企業庁の市場調査ガイド](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/plan-your-business/)

## 8. もう一段疑う――そもそも年間12件、15万円で買われるのか

A spreadsheet quietly assumes that every “potential customer” behaves like a real buyer. しかし、need（必要性）、intent（買いたい気持ち）、budget（予算）、procurement authority（発注権限）は別だ。

For sports footage, the user may be a club, the buyer a league, the operator a production company, and the rights holder yet another entity. **One workflow, several organizations, several wallets.** 利用者が困っているだけでは、発注者の予算は生まれない。

Try a different question: which existing cost would this service replace? Maybe overtime, external production fees, incident recovery, manual quality checks or a missed revenue opportunity. 価格の検証ではwillingness to pay（支払い意思額、WTP）も問う。ただし「便利だと思う」というアンケート回答と、実際に発注する行動は違う。

A large report might tell you a sector is big. A small paid pilot may tell you whether this specific service is a business. **A market becomes actionable when its buyer and budget have names.**

## 9. 誤差は数字の後ろではなく、前提の中に潜んでいる

What if our assumptions are wrong? Let's run three fictional scenarios.

| シナリオ | 団体数 | 年間件数 | 一件単価 | 年間市場 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 慎重 / Low | 100 | 6 | 10万円 | 6,000万円 |
| 標準 / Base | 200 | 12 | 15万円 | 3億6,000万円 |
| 楽観 / High | 300 | 18 | 20万円 | 10億8,000万円 |

That's a very wide range. It does **not** prove the real market is unstable; it shows the model is highly dependent on assumptions. This is a scenario analysis（シナリオ分析）because several inputs change together. Sensitivity analysis（感度分析）typically changes one assumption while keeping the others fixed.

Observed statistics bring other risks: sampling error, nonresponse bias, shifting industry classifications and later revisions. Eurostat insists that metadata（統計の付帯情報）— definitions, methods and quality information — accompany the figures. **Read the footnotes before admiring the decimals.** [欧州連合統計局のメタデータ解説](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/en/web/metadata) ／ [改定の情報](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/en/web/national-accounts/database/data-revisions)

## 10. 市場の成長率が高くても、客が増えているとは限らない

Market value is roughly quantity multiplied by price. If you sell 100 units at 100円, revenue is 10,000円. Next year you sell the same 100 units at 120円: revenue grows by **20%**, but the number of units does not grow.

That's the difference between nominal values（名目値）and a real or constant-price view（実質値、物価変動を調整した値）. Even currency conversion changes how a global market appears. [世界銀行・名目と実質](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/114942-what-is-the-difference-between-current-and-constan) ／ [英国国家統計局・物価調整](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/inflationandpriceindices/methodologies/deflatorsandhowweusethemineconomicestimates)

CAGR means Compound Annual Growth Rate（年平均成長率）. A value rising from 100 to 150 over five years has CAGR = (150÷100)^(1÷5)−1 ≈ **8.45%**. It is a smoothed rate connecting the first and last years, not proof that every year grew at that rate.

A report stating “20% growth” is only the beginning. **Growth of what? Price, volume, customers, currency value or the classification itself?** 問いが違えば、事業上の意味も違う。

## 11. 「市場がない」ことと「困りごとがない」ことは違う

A market can be small because the technology is missing, regulation prevents transactions, or customers lack budgets. It can also be small because a free alternative works perfectly well. **No revenue today does not necessarily mean no problem exists.**

ここではneed（必要）、purchase intention（購入意向）、effective demand（購買力を伴う需要）、realized transactions（成立した取引）を区別したい。This is an explanatory framework, not a universal official statistical classification.

Some entrepreneurs create new categories rather than entering an existing one. だが「将来大市場になる」と言うだけでは証拠にならない。What will customers stop buying? Which spending line will move? Who approves the new purchase? That is where a new category becomes a business.

There is an ethical limit too. A deep human need may produce a small monetary market when the people affected have little purchasing power. **Market size is not a measure of human worth.** 市場調査の歴史を研究する論文は、消費者を購買力で理解することが社会の価値観に与えた影響も扱う。[市場調査史の批判的研究](https://www.emerald.com/jhrm/article-abstract/1/2/200/218915/Capitalism-early-market-research-and-the-creation)

## 12. 批判的に読むための、十の問い

Use these ten checks whenever someone puts a huge market size on a slide.

1. **Boundary:** どのproduct、customer、geography、periodを数えたか。
2. **Buyer:** 誰が使い、誰が契約し、誰のbudgetから払うか。
3. **Metric:** Revenue, spending, gross transaction value, output, value added or potential demand?
4. **Double counting:** 同じ取引が供給網の別段階で繰り返し数えられていないか。
5. **Evidence status:** Observed data, estimated current value or future forecast?
6. **Population:** どの母集団、どの年、どの分類、どの除外条件か。
7. **Price versus volume:** 名目売上の増加を利用者増と誤認していないか。
8. **Triangulation:** Independent sources or just recycled assumptions?
9. **Obtainability:** 自社が本当に取れる市場はどこまでか。
10. **Decision:** Which actual decision changes if the estimate is true?

An estimate that doesn't change any decision may be informative, but it is not yet strategic. The point is to reveal the constraints, not decorate a presentation.

## 13. 調べるときの手順――再利用できる七工程

A practical market-sizing workflow can be built in seven steps.

- **1. Define the decision.** 参入、営業先の選択、投資額、価格のどれを決めたいか。
- **2. State the market in one sentence.** 「どこで、誰が、何を、何年間に買うか」。
- **3. Find observed evidence.** 公的統計、産業団体、企業の財務資料、入札、価格、顧客調査。Check definitions and scope.
- **4. Calculate bottom-up.** Number of buyers × adoption × frequency × price, adjusted for overlap and retention.
- **5. Cross-check independently.** 供給者の売上合計、調達予算、既存の業務量など。
- **6. Model uncertainty.** Downside, base and upside scenarios; key assumptions; unknown contracts.
- **7. Return to the decision.** 次に誰と話すか、何を試験販売するかまで決める。

For a report, always preserve your unit, scope, year, formula, sources, exclusions and assumptions. These are not decorative footnotes; they're the reproducibility of the model.

## 14. 用語集――市場規模の資料で出会う言葉

- **Market size（市場規模）**：the amount of transactions, demand or buyers within a defined boundary.
- **Revenue／sales（売上高）**：the seller's business income from goods and services.
- **GMV／Gross Merchandise Value（取引総額）**：a measure of gross marketplace transactions; not the platform's net revenue.
- **Value added（付加価値額）**：output minus intermediate consumption.
- **Final demand（最終需要）**：demand for consumption, investment and other final uses.
- **Population（母集団）／sample（標本）**：the complete target group and the subset studied.
- **Estimation（推計）／forecast（予測）**：estimating unknown values versus projecting future ones.
- **Market share（市場占有率）**：a firm's portion of a consistently measured market.
- **TAM（理論上の全体市場）**：the full theoretical addressable revenue opportunity.
- **SAM（提供可能市場）**：the part the business can actually serve.
- **SOM（獲得可能市場）**：the part it may realistically win over a defined horizon.
- **Bottom-up（積み上げ法）／top-down（絞り込み法）**：estimating from individual units versus narrowing from an aggregate.
- **Triangulation（複数手法による照合）**：checking an estimate against independently derived evidence.
- **Sensitivity analysis（感度分析）**：testing how changes in assumptions affect the result.
- **Scenario analysis（シナリオ分析）**：testing combinations of assumptions.
- **CAGR（年平均成長率）**：a compound annualized growth rate.
- **Nominal／real（名目／実質）**：measures at current versus price-adjusted values.
- **WTP／Willingness to Pay（支払い意思額）**：what a customer is prepared to pay, not necessarily what they will actually purchase.
- **Metadata（統計の付帯情報）**：scope, definitions, quality and methods accompanying a statistic.

## 15. 結論――市場規模は「夢の大きさ」ではなく、取引の地図である

Return to that giant number: 500 trillion yen. At first it seems to describe a single enormous opportunity. Now we can see multiple layers: B-to-B transactions, consumer spending, company revenue, value added and hypothetical future demand. **They are not six names for the same pile of money.**

Market sizing is useful because it forces a choice. **Who pays, for what, at which stage, and how often?** その一問を丁寧にたどると、巨大な市場のはずが、自社で狙える部分は小さくなるかもしれない。That is not a failed estimate. It is better information.

A market becomes understandable when the abstract number can be traced back to a customer, a payment, a contract and a budget. 調べる前は「大きな市場がある」と思っていた。調べた後は、「その大きさを成り立たせる取引はどれか」と問える。In business, that is a more useful kind of ambition.
