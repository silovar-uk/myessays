# ドーパミン中毒――The Baseline We Can't Simply Reset

*English Mix／Research date: October 26, 2026. 日本語正本と論点・文献・数値をそろえた派生版。Not medical advice.*

## スマホを置いた直後、またスマホを探している。いったい誰が命令したのか

You finish one video. You're not even enjoying it anymore. Yet your thumb keeps scrolling. 「次に特に見たいものはない」と思いながら、次を探している。この感じ、something is off.

The popular explanation is “dopamine addiction” or “a raised dopamine baseline.” 刺激を浴びすぎた脳の目盛りが上がり、普通の日常では満足できなくなる、という話だ。Sounds scientific. But **what exactly is this baseline measuring?** そして下げれば本当にやめられるのか。

研究を読んでいくと、the key distinction is not simply high versus low dopamine. **Wanting and liking can come apart.** 「楽しい」と「また見たい」は、同じものではない。[Berridge & Robinson（2016）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5171207/)

## 守｜ドーパミンは「幸福の液体」ではなく、行動を調整する神経伝達物質である

Dopamine is a neurotransmitter（神経伝達物質）, a chemical messenger involved in how neural circuits operate. 脳全体に幸福を振りまく液体ではない。Its role depends on the brain region, receptors（受容体）, and timing.

It is involved in learning, motivation, movement, and effort allocation. 2020年の研究では、薬理学的な操作が難しい認知課題に挑む際のcost-benefit balance（便益と負担の比較）に作用した。Dopamine can change how worthwhile an effort feels, not merely how pleasant a reward feels.[NIHの研究紹介](https://beta.nih.gov/news-events/nih-research-matters/dopamine-affects-how-brain-decides-whether-goal-worth-effort)

So eliminating dopamine is neither possible as a sensible everyday goal nor desirable. 問題は量の善悪ではなく、どの行動がどんな条件で生まれるか、である。

## 守｜ドーパミン研究は、運動の研究から「予想外の報酬」の研究へ広がった

In the 1950s, Arvid Carlsson（アルヴィド・カールソン）and colleagues established dopamine as a neurotransmitter in its own right. 当初の大きな話題は運動の調節だった。その発見はパーキンソン病の研究にも結びつき、Carlsson received a share of the 2000 Nobel Prize.[研究史](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35944478/)

In 1997, Wolfram Schultz（ヴォルフラム・シュルツ）and colleagues described **reward prediction error（報酬予測誤差）**. Unexpected reward? A burst of neural response. An expected reward? Less surprise. An expected reward that fails to arrive? A dip. This is a learning signal, not a simple pleasure meter.[1997年の研究](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9054347/)／[1998年の研究](https://www.nature.com/articles/nn0898_304)

Imagine a bell predicting candy. 最初はお菓子に驚く。やがてベルが次の報酬を知らせる合図になる。The brain learns what comes next. この区別が分かると、通知を見る前から気持ちが引っ張られることも、少なくとも考察しやすくなる。ただし通知を見た人の脳内活動をここで直接測ったわけではない。

## 守｜「ベースラインが上がる」は、三つの違う現象を一語に詰め込んでしまう

Neuroscience distinguishes **tonic activity（持続性活動）** from **phasic activity（一過性活動）**. ただし神経細胞の発火、細胞外濃度、受容体への作用は、different measurements. Even the neat equation “tonic = motivation, phasic = learning” is scientifically contested.[Berke（2018）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6358212/)／[伝達の時空間的な研究](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8220193/)

In everyday speech, “my baseline has gone up” may refer to three different things. One: higher baseline dopamine activity or concentration. Two: reduced satisfaction from a familiar stimulus. Three: feeling bad without stimulation. ①は生理学の測定、②は適応や学習、③は主観的な気分の問題だ。They may interact, but they are not synonyms.

That means “scrolling raised my dopamine baseline” is not automatically a scientific finding. 「下げたい」の正体は、perhaps the frequency of urges or the difficulty of stopping, not a directly observed chemical number.

## 守｜依存症には適応や耐性があるが、脳の数値が一方向に上がるわけではない

**Tolerance（耐性）** means that repeated exposure can weaken the effect of the same amount. **Withdrawal（離脱）** refers to symptoms that can appear when an established dependence is interrupted. 依存症には報酬・ストレス・行動制御の複数の回路が関わる。[神経生物学の概説](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK597351/)

But there is no universal rule that addiction simply pushes baseline dopamine upward. 測定する場所、物質、経過、方法によって変わる。Nor should results about powerful drugs be transferred directly to ordinary phone use.

If everyday life feels flat, sleep loss, stress, mood, and environment are alternative explanations to consider. 「脳の刺激量だけが原因」という説明は、sometimes hides the very conditions we need to change.

## 守｜「好き」と「欲しい」は別だから、面白くない動画も次々と開けてしまう

Kent Berridge（ケント・ベリッジ）and Terry Robinson（テリー・ロビンソン）separate **liking（快感）** from **wanting（欲求・誘因の顕著性）**. The latter is not always a conscious decision; a cue can make a reward suddenly feel compelling. ドーパミンは特にwantingに関わる。[2016年の総説](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5171207/)／[2023年の論考](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10527990/)

You may find the next video dull but still feel pulled toward the next one. この場面は理論を理解するための類推であり、スマホ利用の全例に同じ神経機序が証明されたわけではない。Nevertheless, the question changes: **Why does wanting persist after liking fades?**

Use time alone doesn't answer that. 開く直前の合図と、終えた後の満足度の差まで観察すると、習慣の構造が少し見えてくる。

## 守｜「ドーパミン中毒」は便利な比喩だが、正式な診断名ではない

The WHO recognizes gaming disorder in ICD-11（国際疾病分類第11版）. It requires impaired control, growing priority over other activities, continued play despite harm, and significant functional impairment, normally over at least 12 months.[WHOの診断説明](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/addictive-behaviours-gaming-disorder)

The American Psychiatric Association still places Internet Gaming Disorder among conditions requiring further research in DSM-5-TR（精神疾患の診断・統計マニュアル第5版改訂版）. General social media or smartphone use is not simply the same diagnosis.[米国精神医学会](https://www.psychiatry.org/patients-families/internet-gaming)

Long screen time is not automatically addiction. 使う目的、制御の難しさ、生活への支障が重要になる。A behavior can be inconvenient without being a disorder, and a disorder can't be diagnosed from one metric.

## 破｜ドーパミン断食は「物質を減らす方法」ではなく「行動を見直す方法」として始まった

“Dopamine fasting” is a catchy name for a practice associated with psychiatrist Cameron Sepah（キャメロン・セパー）and ideas from **cognitive behavioral therapy（認知行動療法）**. It was never meant as literally emptying dopamine from the brain. ハーバード大学医学部の解説も、刺激を避ければ脳内ドーパミンが洗い流されるという解釈を否定する。[Harvard Health（2020）](https://www.health.harvard.edu/blog/dopamine-fasting-misunderstanding-science-spawns-a-maladaptive-fad-2020022618917)

CBT looks at links among thoughts, feelings, and actions. 通知が鳴ったらすぐ開く、という一連の行動に、別の選択肢を入れる。That's behavioral change, not chemical cleansing.

Perhaps the appeal of “detox” is the promise of a clean reset. しかし、人が見直すべきものが生活の習慣なら、a better question is how to redesign the conditions of choice.

## 破｜本当に「刺激を断つ」と効くのか。肯定・否定の実験を並べてみる

Let's deliberately compare studies that disagree. These trials measured well-being, anxiety, sleep, or attention—not a direct reset of participants' brain dopamine.

In 2022, Lambert（ランバート）and colleagues randomized 154 participants to take a one-week break from social media or use it as usual. The break group showed improvements in well-being, depression, and anxiety measures.[2022年の無作為化試験](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35512731/)

Yet another randomized study in 2022, with 93 participants, found no clear causal effect on anxiety or sleep quality after a one-week social media suspension. 同じ「一週間の休止」という看板でも、methods and outcomes matter.[対照的な研究](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36194912/)

In 2025, Noah Castelo（ノア・カステロ）and colleagues tested blocking mobile internet on smartphones for two weeks, while keeping calls, texts, and computer internet available. Attention and psychological outcomes improved. The research also suggested that more offline socializing, exercise, and time outdoors partly explained the results.[2025年の研究](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11834938/)

A 2024 research review found that reducing use sometimes appeared more promising than total abstinence, while emphasizing inconsistency and limited evidence. つまり、there is no one-size-fits-all digital detox protocol.[2024年のレビュー](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11422191/)

**Interpretation:** Restricting access can help under some conditions. But it does not prove a chemical baseline has been reset. What may matter is which activities regain our time and attention.

## 破｜もう一歩やりすぎるなら、「禁欲」と「環境変更」を別々に試したい

**Proposal, not an experiment already conducted.** Suppose your problem is late-night short-form video. まず7日間は通常の使用を観察し、次の7日間は通知を消す、アプリをホーム画面から外す、寝室の外で充電する。This is a before-and-after self-observation, not a randomized clinical trial.

Track five things: unintended app openings, bedtime use, mood before opening, satisfaction afterward, and effects on sleep or next-morning work. 利用時間だけでなく、why you opened the app and whether it delivered what you wanted. Record sleep and workload too, because they may confound the comparison.

A stricter ban might be another condition, but don't disrupt work or relationships just to follow a dramatic rule. The goal isn't to estimate a dopamine level. 目的は行動の合図と結果を確かめ、自分に効く変更を探すことだ。

## 離｜動画に勝つ意志を鍛える前に、動画が始まる条件を変えられる

Behavior is shaped by design: notifications, auto-play, app placement, and the effort required to stop. 「意志が弱い」の一言では、これらの条件が見えなくなる。Not every urge can be removed, but some triggers and friction can be changed.

**Practical proposal:** Specify the cue in one sentence. Reduce that cue. Replace an empty moment with a small alternative. 自分で始めた用事を済ませ、自分で終えられるかを評価する。Success is not merely a longer abstinence streak.

Severe substance use, gambling problems, or gaming-related impairment call for professional support. 長期間大量に飲酒している場合、急な断酒は危険な離脱を起こす可能性があり、医療的支援が必要になる。[NIAAA](https://www.niaaa.nih.gov/health-professionals-communities/core-resource-on-alcohol/alcohol-use-disorder-risk-diagnosis-recovery)

## 離｜集中できない問題を、すべて「快楽を求める脳」で説明してはいけない

What if the problem isn't an overpowering reward but an unclear next step? 目的が曖昧、失敗が怖い、疲れている、眠い。Different causes can produce the same observable behavior: reaching for a phone.

**Proposal:** Make the desired task startable in five minutes. Open one primary source. Write one heading. 完成させるのではなく、始める一手を具体化する。This is a planning technique, not evidence of a five-minute neural reset.

Reducing temptation and making meaningful work approachable are two sides of the same design problem. 誘惑を消しただけでは、戻る場所が遠いままかもしれない。

## 離｜本当に取り戻したいのは、ドーパミンの低さではなく、選択の余地である

Back to the thumb scrolling even after the fun is gone. 「好き」と「欲しい」が分かれ、合図から報酬を学び、環境が行動を後押しする。That story is more complex than “too much dopamine,” but it is also more useful.

You don't need to make every enjoyable stimulus disappear. 面白い動画は面白いままでいい。What matters is whether you can stop when you've decided to stop, and return to the things you actually value.

The outcome worth measuring may not be a hidden chemical baseline. **It may be regained agency（行動を自分で選び、終える余地）.** 「刺激のない脳」を手に入れるのではなく、刺激と暮らす方法を設計し直す。That is a far more concrete goal.
