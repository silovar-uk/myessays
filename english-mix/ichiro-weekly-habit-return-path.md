# 週1回は、年間313日サボっている――それでも「続ける」と言っていい
## A habit is not a perfect streak. It is a path you can return to.

週に1回、何かをする。

Do it for one year, and you get roughly 52 sessions.

逆に言うと、**365日のうち約313日はやっていない。**

That sounds less like discipline and more like an extremely generous holiday policy.

「週1で継続しています」と聞くと立派に聞こえる。でも数字にすると、313日休んでいる。

So here is the strange question: **Can we really call that “consistency”?**

ここで、イチローの話が効いてくる。

CW-Xの公式インタビューには、三日坊主で運動が続かない人からの質問がある。

> 典型的な三日坊主で、運動が長続きしません。どうしたら長続きしますか？

A contemporary article summarising the video highlights two ideas: **do not begin by demanding “every day” from yourself**, and start with something like once a week for a year.

イチローといえば、むしろ「毎日同じことをする人」の象徴みたいな存在だ。

And yet, for a beginner, he loosens the frequency first.

Why?

---

## 1. The man of daily routines does not start beginners with “daily”

まず、誤解を避けたい。

Ichiro was not arguing that daily repetition is pointless.

むしろ2025年のCW-Xイベントでは、毎日同じことを続けると体の違和感や変化を感じ取りやすくなり、コンディション維持につながるという趣旨を語っている。

So the two messages can be arranged like this.

```text
For the person who always quits
→ Do not make “daily” the entrance requirement.

For the person who already has a routine
→ Repeating the same action can become a sensor for change.
```

一見、逆方向。
But they may describe different stages of the same process.

**For a beginner, daily repetition is load. For an experienced practitioner, daily repetition can become measurement.**

初心者の「毎日」は義務。
熟練者の「毎日」はセンサー。

That distinction changes the whole story.

---

## 2. “Every day” quietly turns into a scoring system

新しいことを始めるとき、人はなぜか頻度を最大値にしがちだ。

- 毎日英語をやる — study English every day
- 毎日筋トレする — work out every day
- 毎日日記を書く — write every day
- 毎日本を読む — read every day

Four times a week would already be impressive. Still, we often choose seven before we have done one.

そして「毎日」と宣言した瞬間、goalが少し変わる。

英語力を上げたかったはずが、**Did I do it today?** が主目的になる。
筋力をつけたかったはずが、**protect the streak** が勝敗条件になる。

The metric starts eating the goal.

Then one missed day feels absurdly large.

30日続けて、31日目に休んだ。
Recordとしては30 wins, 1 loss.

That is excellent.

でも本人の感覚では、“My streak is back to zero.”

ゼロ？
Where did the thirty wins go?

---

## 3. How fragile is a perfect streak? Let us overcalculate it.

ここで少しだけ、無駄に数学を持ち込む。

Assume a person successfully completes each scheduled session 95% of the time.

かなり優秀だ。
Only one miss in twenty attempts.

But define success as **never missing once**, and the picture becomes ridiculous.

- 365 daily sessions, all successful: about **0.00000074%**
- 52 weekly sessions, all successful: about **6.9%**

もちろん、real behaviour is not an independent coin flip. Success probabilities change with context, fatigue, work, weather, motivation and hundreds of other things.

This is not a predictive model.

でも一つだけ構造が見える。

**If perfection is the scoring rule, higher frequency creates more chances to “fail.”**

Daily practice is not the problem.
The brittle rule is: **one miss means failure.**

---

## 4. Habit research does not say one miss erases everything

習慣形成研究でよく引用されるPhillippa Lallyらの2010年の研究がある。

Participants chose an eating, drinking or activity behaviour and repeated it in a consistent context over 12 weeks. Researchers tracked how automatic the behaviour began to feel.

ここで面白い点が二つある。

First, the time course varied enormously. Among participants whose data could be modelled, reaching near-asymptotic automaticity was estimated to take anywhere from **18 to 254 days**.

つまり「○日続ければ習慣になる」という一発の数字では片づかない。

Second, **missing one opportunity did not materially disrupt the habit-formation process.**

これはかなり救いがある。

But there is an important qualification.

この研究は「週1が科学的に最適」と証明したものではない。Participants were generally repeating behaviours in daily contexts.

So we should not glue Ichiro’s advice and this paper together and announce, “Science proves once a week is best.”

言えるのは、もっと地味で、でも大事なこと。

**Habit formation is not a game in which one absence deletes all previous repetitions.**

---

## 5. What is actually strong about once a week?

週1には当然、弱点もある。

There are fewer repetitions.
Practice volume is lower.

英語を週1回5分だけやって、来月ペラペラにはならない。
One weekly workout is not automatically optimal for every fitness goal.

So “once a week” is not magic.

Its strength may be elsewhere: **it is often easier to use as a frequency that keeps the relationship alive.**

たとえば「毎日30分走る」を3日でやめる。
Annual total: three returns to running.

一方で「毎週日曜に10分歩く」という弱そうな目標でも、1年続けば52回戻る。

Three heroic attempts versus fifty-two returns.

この差は大きい。

If we stop measuring continuity only by longest streak and instead ask **how many times did I come back?**, once-a-week practice looks completely different.

---

## 6. Maybe the first thing to build is not a habit, but a return path

「習慣」というと、歯磨きみたいな完成形を想像する。

Automatic.
Daily.
Strange not to do it.

But demanding that finished state from day one is odd.

道路で言えば、まだ獣道しかない場所に初日から高速道路を要求している。

The first path can be much thinner.

```text
Do it
↓
Leave a gap
↓
Return
↓
Leave another gap
↓
Return again
```

このreturnが何度も起きると、行動への道が少しずつ消えにくくなる。

Later, once a week may become twice a week.
It may become daily.

Or it may stay weekly because weekly is enough for the purpose.

**Frequency is a design variable, not a personality test.**

毎日できない＝意志が弱い、ではない。
It may simply mean that “daily” is currently too heavy a setting for the relationship between your life and that behaviour.

---

## 7. You can rest 313 days and still be continuing

最初の疑問に戻る。

A weekly practitioner does nothing on roughly 313 days of the year.

Can that still count as consistency?

調べる前は、週1は毎日の劣化版だと思っていた。

Daily = 100 points.
Three times a week = 70.
Once a week = 30.

A single vertical ranking.

でも、少し違って見えてきた。

Daily is not simply the superior version of weekly.

週1には、**行動との接続を切らさず、戻る動作そのものを練習する段階**という役割がある。

Once returning becomes normal, frequency can increase.

イチローが三日坊主の人には「毎日」を入口にせず、一方で自分自身は日々同じ行動を繰り返して変化を読む。

Those two messages may belong to two phases of one habit.

**Consistency does not have to mean never breaking. It can mean being able to return after a break.**

そう考えると、313日の「やっていない日」はサボりの証拠に見えなくなる。

They become the spaces between 52 successful returns.

52本の帰り道。
