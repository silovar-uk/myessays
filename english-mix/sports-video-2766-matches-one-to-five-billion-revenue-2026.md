# 試合が2,766もあるのに、なぜ映像事業で5億円稼ぐのは難しいのか — 2,766 matches, one difficult business

### 要旨

There are a lot of sports matches in Japan. 2026/27シーズンのJ.LEAGUEは1,140試合、B.LEAGUEは1,626試合。Together, 2,766 regular-season games. Surely a video company can make money from that volume?

The arithmetic is seductive. But a match is not a purchase order. **Scheduled matches, actual video workflows, and newly billable services are three different things.** この違いを忘れると、市場規模は簡単に膨らむ。This essay revisits league formats, club financial data, broadcast rights and operations, then asks who pays for what—year after year.

> **Scope note:** Official formats and disclosed cases are evidence. Our contract prices, capture rates and margins are scenarios, not observed results. Existing broadcast contracts and vendor assignments are not fully verified.

## 1. 2,766 games sounds like a market. It is only a starting point

J1, J2 and J3 each have 20 clubs playing 38 matches in 2026/27. That means 380 per division, or 1,140 combined. B.PREMIER has 26 clubs × 60 games ÷ 2 = 780 games; B.ONE 25 × 60 ÷ 2 = 750; B.NEXT 4 × 48 ÷ 2 = 96. Total B.LEAGUE volume: 1,626.

| League group | Clubs | Games per club | Total games |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| J1–J3 combined | 60 | 38 each | 1,140 |
| B.PREMIER | 26 | 60 | 780 |
| B.ONE | 25 | 60 | 750 |
| B.NEXT | 4 | 48 | 96 |
| **Total** | **115** | ― | **2,766** |

Sources: [J.LEAGUE format](https://www.jleague.jp/corporate/about_competitions/season/) and [B.LEAGUE format](https://www.bleague.jp/regulation/).

These are **scheduled regular-season volumes**, not confirmed completed productions. 公式試合があること、カメラが動くこと、そして新規事業者が契約を取れることは別問題。The existence of a workflow is not evidence that the workflow is available for sale.

![From match volume to billable operations](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/sports-video-funnel-2026.svg "Official match counts pass through video scope, buying authority and rights before they can become paid services. The ¥40,000 unit price is hypothetical.")

### Other sports add volume, but they don't make the data uniform

The earlier league-volume ledger includes other calculable regular-season counts: SV.LEAGUE 530 matches across men and women in 2026/27, League One 206 across three divisions, League H 320 across men's and women's leagues, and men's F.LEAGUE F1/F2 234. For 2026, Japan's NPB schedule implies 858 games including interleague play, and JD.LEAGUE has 232 in the earlier ledger.

| Competition | Year | Scheduled regular-season games | Caveat |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| SV.LEAGUE (men and women) | 2026/27 | 530 | 264 + 266, not completed video deliveries |
| League One (three divisions) | 2026/27 | 206 | 108 + 56 + 42 |
| League H (men and women) | 2026/27 | 320 | 210 + 110 |
| Men's F.LEAGUE F1/F2 | 2026/27 | 234 | 162 + 72; women's separate |
| NPB | 2026 | 858 | 143 games per club, no double count of interleague |
| JD.LEAGUE | 2026 | 232 | East/West accounting needs care |

Sources: [SV.LEAGUE](https://www.svleague.jp/ja/sv_men/topics/detail/23530), [League One](https://league-one.jp/news/6096), [League H](https://leagueh.jp/news/?id=22611), [F.LEAGUE](https://www.fleague.jp/league/outline.html), [NPB](https://npb.jp/games/2026/), [JD.LEAGUE](https://jdleague.jp/news/detail/11580/).

These are scheduled volumes—not verified recordings, delivered assets, or accessible contracts. 他競技の数字を足し上げても、契約の壁は消えない。The wider the catalogue, the greater the need for consistent definitions.

## 2. Counting the same match twice is surprisingly easy

Twenty J1 clubs each play 38 matches. 20 × 38 = 760 team appearances, but only 380 actual matches. ホーム開催も別の試合が増えるわけではない。One match may also trigger pre-game setup, file ingest, monitoring, archiving, multiple deliveries and rights review.

Suppose we define seven operational steps per match. Then 2,766 × 7 = 19,362 process occurrences. But **19,362 steps are not 19,362 billable transactions.** Several steps may already be included in one contract, and many are automated.

We need separate ledgers for scheduled games, completed video captures or streams, actual operational contracts, deliveries and separately paid tasks. 公開情報は予定や配信方針まで確認できても、委託・納品実績は別途記録が必要。Clean definitions matter more than large totals.

## 3. ¥40,000 per match leads to a rather uncomfortable result

Let's try the most optimistic scenario: every one of those 2,766 games becomes a new ¥40,000 order. Revenue would be ¥110.64 million. That barely clears ¥100 million, and still falls far short of ¥500 million.

At the same price, reaching ¥500 million requires 12,500 paid games a year. If only 20% of the 2,766 games are available as new paid work, roughly 553 × ¥40,000 yields about ¥22.13 million.

| Hypothetical scenario | Paid games | Price per game | Annual revenue |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Every game sold | 2,766 | ¥40,000 | ¥110.64m |
| 20% sold | ≈553 | ¥40,000 | ≈¥22.13m |
| Target ¥100m | 2,500 | ¥40,000 | ¥100m |
| Target ¥500m | 12,500 | ¥40,000 | ¥500m |

Numbers tell us something uncomfortable: price × volume is easy; proving **payable and accessible volume** is hard. 値上げすればよい、という話にもならない。Higher prices need a stronger value proposition and responsibility, not a wish for higher revenue.

## 4. Club turnover is not a video purchasing budget

J.LEAGUE publishes club financial information, and B.LEAGUE has published its 2024–25 club financial overview. Club revenues vary widely, yet a club's gross revenue is not a discretionary media-technology budget. Different fiscal years and the 2026/27 format transition also complicate comparisons.

[Club management disclosures](https://www.jleague.jp/corporate/about_jclubs/management_jclubs/) / [B.LEAGUE financial overview](https://www.bleague.jp/media_news/detail/id=540572).

A club might use video every day without having the right to procure a separate official-match delivery system. The league, production company or broadcaster may control that agreement. だから「利用者」「発注者」「支払者」「許諾者」は別々の列で管理したい。A list of teams is not yet a list of buyers.

## 5. J.LEAGUE already has its own rights and asset workflows

Here is an inconvenient fact for anyone pitching a brand-new central archive. J.LEAGUE has managed, stored and licensed match footage since 1993; since 2017 it has also developed official video production and ownership arrangements. Its digital asset initiative, **J.LEAGUE FUROSHIKI**, covers video, still images and other assets. Official rules distinguish primary from secondary use and set conditions for re-use.

Sources: [Rights and official content](https://aboutj.jleague.jp/corporate/activities/various_rights/), [video-use rules](https://www.jleague.jp/corporate/services/videoutil/), and [FUROSHIKI overview](https://aboutj.jleague.jp/seasonreview2023/management150/).

Therefore, "let's build a new archive" may mean competing with an existing system. ここは前提を変えたい。What if the opportunity lies around an established archive: checking ingest, verifying delivery specifications, tracking approvals and coordinating recovery? **A useful complement may be easier to justify than a total replacement.**

## 6. Observable cost reduction beats an impressive feature list

In a published case, Photron reports that its work with M2A Media reduced pre-match operational effort by 75%, match-day effort by 60%, and labor cost by 15% for a specific production workflow. These are vendor-reported results, not general performance guarantees.

[Photron × M2A Media case study](https://www.photron.co.jp/service/sports/case/m2a-media_2509/).

The lesson is not simply "automation works." It is that **a measurable recurring problem** gives sales teams something concrete to discuss. ただし利益を得る会社と支払う会社が違う場合もある。If the production vendor saves labor while the league pays a fixed fee, who has an incentive to fund the new tool? Every ROI claim needs a beneficiary and a buyer.

## 7. Youth competition expands volume, not necessarily budgets

The 2026 JFA U-18 Premier League has 24 teams, split into 12 in East and 12 in West, each playing 22 matches. That is 264 scheduled regular-season games. Regional Prince leagues add further competitions, but formats vary and cannot be multiplied uniformly.

[JFA official tournament format](https://jfa.jp/news/00036068/).

Yet professional academy teams and school teams don't share one budget structure. A coach may want video while a school, a club or a governing body pays for it. Rights, privacy and minors' data bring further constraints. 育成年代は大きな裾野だが、少額・多数顧客向けの販売モデルが必要かもしれない。A low-cost repeatable product is a different business from a high-touch league operation.

## 8. Mission-critical means more than being impressive

Immersive replays and AI highlights may be attractive. But an event can often proceed without them. It cannot casually ignore a failed transmission, an unauthorized delivery or an incident that no one owns.

So ask four questions: Does the work recur? Is failure costly? Does it cross organizational boundaries? And what happens when the service is removed? 重要なのは恐怖を煽ることではない。If the existing vendor already prevents failure cheaply, the new service has no automatic right to exist. **Indispensability must be earned through repeatable performance, not vendor lock-in.**

## 9. Redraw the market around the buyer who bundles workflows

Potential buyers include leagues, clubs, production and streaming companies, venues, youth organizations and recurring event promoters. しかし利用者を全て足しても顧客数にはならない。If a league hires a production company, the league and vendor should not be counted as two independent buyers of the same fee.

| Buyer | Recurring job | Sales question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| League or federation | Rights, standards and official supply | Where does the current process fail? |
| Production operator | Setup, monitoring and deliveries across events | What grows when it wins another event? |
| Club or venue | Event-specific and promotional media | Who actually controls the purchase? |
| Youth organization | Filming, analysis and sharing | Who signs and pays every year? |
| Event promoter | Live operations and post-event delivery | Can the sports workflow be reused? |

The first buyer hypothesis worth testing is a production or distribution operator managing many events. A relatively small number of customers may generate large recurring workloads. ただし内製が進んでいれば勝ち目は薄い。We must study existing systems and contract renewal terms before declaring a new market.

## 10. The product should do fewer things, but own a clearer outcome

Instead of another universal platform, consider a focused **Media Delivery & Operations Assurance** service: event schedules, ingest verification, destination checks, status monitoring, recovery steps and delivery logs.

This does not imply owning every camera, every archive or every rights decision. Annual pricing could depend on event volume, connected destinations, support hours and service-level commitments. 権利の最終判断は権利者に残す。If the customer can say, "Give this operator the job and our delivery will be traceable and reliable," we may have a reason for recurring payment.

The danger is equally clear: unlimited emergency response turns fixed revenue into an expanding labor bill. **Scope and price must be designed together.**

## 11. Twelve contracts for ¥100 million; sixty for ¥500 million

Another scenario shifts the unit from individual matches to annual contracts. For ¥100 million: two anchor contracts × ¥25 million plus ten standard contracts × ¥5 million. For ¥500 million: ten anchors × ¥20 million plus fifty standards × ¥6 million.

![Annual contract combinations for ¥100m and ¥500m](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/sports-video-sales-contracts-2026.svg "Hypothetical contract counts and prices. No customer willingness to pay has been verified.")

These combinations are mathematical examples, not bookings. Who has budget authority? When do contracts renew? How many independent prospects exist? How much does each implementation cost? Each question could invalidate the sales plan. 契約数が増えても、毎回特注なら単一商品の強みは消える。The true scaling question is whether operational complexity grows slower than revenue.

## 12. Gross margin reveals the hidden fragility

Suppose annual revenue is ¥100 million, gross margin 55%, and fixed operating expenses ¥48 million. That leaves only ¥7 million. At a 30% gross margin, the same fixed expense creates an ¥18 million shortfall. **These are deliberately hypothetical numbers, not a forecast.**

Cloud usage, transfer costs, 24-hour support, third-party licenses and custom incident handling can undermine the economics. Track contribution by customer, exceptional manual hours, implementation time, automatic processing rates and retention. 動画の本数より、顧客追加時に増えない仕事を探す。That is where a repeatable business begins.

## 13. The live-entertainment market offers a new temptation

Japan's 2025 live-entertainment market recorded 100,820 performances and estimated ticket sales of ¥856.4 billion, according to the Pia Research Institute. That is a much larger event count than a single sports league.

[Pia Research Institute, June 2026](https://corporate.pia.jp/sp/news/detail_live_enta_market20260617.html).

But 100,820 performances are not 100,820 video delivery contracts. Some are never filmed; some use locked-in providers; rights and production arrangements vary. 市場規模という魅惑的な数字へ、また飛びつきそうになる。The expansion criterion should be simple: can we reuse the same ingest, monitoring, delivery and audit components with minimal customization? Then sport may be the first training ground, not the final boundary.

## 14. Use the first 90 days to disprove the idea cheaply

Days 1–30: map one real event, its vendors, decision-makers, rights owners, tasks, time and costs. Days 31–60: test whether two or three independent customers share the same workflow and whether errors or manual handoffs can be measurably reduced. Days 61–90: show a price to actual budget owners, including support hours, incident responsibilities and variable charges.

Failure conditions matter: no rights to connect, no willingness to pay, too much bespoke work, or established providers already doing the work cheaply. その場合に大きな設備投資をしない。A failed pilot with a clear answer is better than a glossy, untestable growth forecast.

## 15. We counted matches. We should have counted promises

Initially, 2,766 sounded like a market. In reality it is merely a recurring calendar of events. Each event comes with obligations: start on time, deliver to the right party, honor rights, recover from failure, keep a reliable record.

What customers may pay for is not a match or an AI feature in isolation, but **the dependable fulfillment of those promises**. 調べる前は試合を何件増やせるか考えていた。Now the more interesting question is how many valuable, recurring promises one operation can fulfill without rebuilding itself each time.

- 2,766 refers to scheduled J.LEAGUE and B.LEAGUE regular-season matches in 2026/27, excluding playoffs and cups.
- Streaming plans are not confirmed completion counts or vendor-level revenue.
