# eスポーツチームは何で食べているのか――七つの収益源と利益の壁

*人気チームの事業拡張、公開決算、組織再編から採算を読み解く*

*2026年10月14日時点の公開情報に基づく。収益区分は筆者の分析であり、個社の未公開決算値は推測していない。*

## 競技チームを調べたら、服飾店や就職イベントまで出てきた

An esports team sounds like a roster plus coaches. 調べるとcreators, apparel, events, education and even recruitment servicesが出てくる。Why would a competitive team build all of this? The answer may lie in the economics of winning.

Three distinctions matter: audience is not revenue; revenue is not profit; parent-company benefit is not team P&L. 混ぜると、どのmodelが持続可能かを判断できない。

## 人気チームの本業は、試合日以外にファンが戻ってくる仕組みをつくることでもある

In a 2025 survey of young game-video viewers, Crazy Raccoon recorded 34.0% awareness and ZETA DIVISION 33.0%. 配信者グループ『ぶいすぽっ！』も上位だった。This is awareness among a defined audience, not the whole population. [調査](https://www.yano.co.jp/press-release/show/press_id/3957)

Crazy Raccoon runs its own events and merchandise; ZETA develops apparel, events and education. Both create fan touchpoints beyond competition days. ただしdirect monetizationとprofitabilityは、公開資料だけでは確定しない。[CR](https://crazyraccoon.jp/)／[ZETA](https://zetadivision.com/)

## リジェクトとフェンネルは、所属する人の人気を企業向けの仕事へ変えている

REJECT connects players and creators with branded appearances and marketing work, including a 2026 partnership with AnyMind Group. FENNEL runs events, apparel and gamer-focused recruitment programs. これはsponsorship logosを売るだけではない。[REJECT](https://reject.jp/)／[FENNEL](https://fennel-esports.com/)

A corporate client may buy reach, talent coordination, production or event operations—not just logo exposure. ただしB2B services require sales and delivery capabilities. A popular roster does not automatically provide them.

## デトネーションの決算は、優勝しても利益が残るとは限らないことを示す

GameWith's FY May 2026 esports-and-entertainment segment posted 11億6,200万円 in revenue and 1,600万円 operating loss, compared with 7億5,900万円 revenue and 2億2,200万円 loss previously. Prize money and corporate projects helped, but operating profit stayed negative. [開示](https://static.gamewith.co.jp/sc2026/content-2.html)

It would be inaccurate to call that the team's standalone loss; it is a segment number. 会社自身もcompetition resultsと収益力の連動に触れている。A more useful question is whether recurring revenue covers fixed costs in a weaker competitive year.

Revenue growth without durable margin can mislead. Prize income is volatile; recurring sponsor renewals, salary costs, production spending and B2B gross margins reveal whether the business can absorb a bad season.

## 2026年の運営会社再編は『強くなる』以外の経営合理性を持つ

In April 2026, telecom company QTnet absorbed the operating company behind QT DIG∞. Competition, local facilities and connectivity sit closer together in one organization. [公式](https://qtdig.com/news/)

In July, VARREL and CAG OSAKA announced integration, transferring the Rainbow Six Siege division into CAG by VARREL and exploring education cooperation. This is an organizational design question: where should talent, sales and training capabilities sit? [発表](https://varrel.jp/news/2026/07/01/4078)

Consolidation may lower duplicated costs, but integration creates its own complexity. 公表されたintegration rationaleと実際のoutcomeは別。Measure capacity utilization, recurring sales and costs after the deal.

## 地方チームの商売は、全国規模の人気順位とは別の物差しを必要とする

Nagoya OJA, Saishunkan Sol Kumamoto, Hiroshima TEAM iXA and FUKUSHIMA IBUSHIGIN illustrate regional models. Their value cannot be reduced to national follower rankings. [名古屋](https://www.nagoyaoja.com/)／[熊本](https://www.sskumamoto.com/)／[広島](https://ixa.yarukiman.com/)／[福島](https://www.ibsg.jp/)

A regional sponsor may value local events, employee participation or community relationships. These are hypotheses, not proven outcomes. 地域のpaid participation, sponsor retention and per-event marginを測定して初めて評価できる。

## 収益を七つの財布に分けると、競技と事業を別々に管理できる

Seven possible revenue streams: sponsorship, prize money, publisher or league payments, merchandise and memberships, talent appearances, event and video production, and adjacent services such as education or facilities. ただしevery team has a different mix, and accounting treatment matters.

The first three depend more directly on competition and publisher relationships. Merchandising and talent deals depend on audience attachment. B2B and adjacent services require operating capability. This separation clarifies where management should invest.

Suggested dashboard: revenue, direct costs, gross margin, fixed cost, recurring share, contract renewal and title concentration for each stream. さらにpayer and budget categoryを対応付ける。Don't invent undisclosed numbers before investigating contracts.

## 新規事業は、チームに機材を売るだけでなく共同で顧客を開拓する発想がある

A technology provider could treat the team not only as a buyer but also as a channel partner. 映像制作・データ計測を技術企業が担当し、team provides talent and audience access. A joint offer may be more viable than selling hardware alone.

Rights and contracts come first: footage reuse, game data access, sponsor conflicts, talent agreements and revenue sharing. Pilot metrics should include production time, gross margin, delivery quality and repeat orders. Growth alone is not proof of demand.

## 勝つための組織が、勝敗に左右されすぎない商売を探している

The strange discovery—an esports team running apparel shops and job events—now looks less strange. Winning creates attention, but lasting business needs revenue that survives quiet months and difficult seasons. Who captures value, and what remains after the match?
