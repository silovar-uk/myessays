# キティはレゴの棚を何センチ奪うのか――サンリオ決算から始まる、玩具売場の経済学｜English Mix

If Hello Kitty enters the LEGO aisle, how many centimeters of shelf space will Sanrio gain? そもそも、棚は簡単に増えない。アメリカの店で定規を出すのは少し suspicious だが、この小さな疑問から、キャラクター事業の成長をどう測るかという問題が見えてくる。

The starting point は2026年11月6日のサンリオ上期決算と、2027年以降に予定されるLEGO製品。This is not an earnings summary. 数字から玩具の売り方、棚の競争、新しい顧客への到達まで考える。Facts と hypotheses は明確に分ける。

## 1｜An odd toy-store puzzle――8 kinds of surprise in one product

Look at [MGA's Miniverse Series 4](https://shop.mgae.com/products/mgas-miniverse-make-it-mini-sanrio-hello-kitty-series-4). 全8種類には、CinnamorollのランチボックスやKuromi & My Melodyのティーセットがある。ただし、all eight are **not** inside one capsule. One capsule brings one surprise from eight possibilities. 公式の掲載価格は12.99ドル。

![MGAによるサンリオのミニチュア玩具「メイク・イット・ミニ」第4弾。メーカー公式の製品写真](https://shop.mgae.com/cdn/shop/files/247745-S4-Mini-SANRIO-Hello-Kitty1_1200x1200.jpg?v=1781200312)

Photo: MGA公式の製品画像。Inside the package, designs differ; on the shelf, shoppers see one capsule series. 樹脂を使う商品があり、manufacturer states ages 8+ and adult supervision required.

Customers enjoy character variety, while retailers may manage the assortment through a smaller set of product listings. ただし、actual shelf space and SKU management depend on the seller. 小さなカプセルにあるのは、kawaiiだけでなく、**variety と operational complexity を分ける**という発想だ。

## 2｜Sanrio's earnings point to toys as a North American growth area

Sanrio reported consolidated first-half revenue of 107.725 billion yen, up 22.9% year over year. 一方、[earnings deck](https://assets.minkabu.jp/news/article_media_content/urn%3Anewsml%3Atdnet.info%3A20261106567433/140120261106567433.pdf)では、US revenue and contribution profit were roughly flat in local currency, amid tariff and input-cost pressures. Toys and digital categories grew. Global growth and a North American slowdown can coexist.

Page 26 of the deck shows the FY ended March 2026 category mix: apparel 34%, toys & sports 29%, household products 12%, other 25%. Important: **29% covers toys AND sports**, and is not the latest half-year mix. 数字の粒度を間違えると、precision-looking forecasts become misleading.

![北米ライセンスカテゴリの構成比を示す著者作成図。アパレル34%、玩具・スポーツ29%、家庭用品12%、その他25%。](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/sanrio-lego-category-mix-2026.svg)

If toys & sports grew 20% while everything else stayed flat, the illustrative lift would be 29% × 20% = **5.8%**. This is a scenario, not company guidance. また海外セグメントの対象期間は2026年1〜6月。The October LEGO announcement has not yet produced reported sales in this half-year.

## 3｜Character popularity alone does not guarantee retail adoption

Three players matter: Sanrio is the **licensor** (権利を許諾する側), LEGO or MGA the **licensee** (商品化する側), and Target the **retailer** (販売する側). Their incentives differ. Retail sales at Target are not the same thing as Sanrio's royalty revenue.

Shelf space is finite. If LEGO Sanrio merely replaces another licensed Sanrio toy, the IP owner's gain may be limited. If it wins demand that previously went to a rival IP, the outcome differs. The question is not just 'How much did it sell?' but **'What did it replace?'**

Two concepts help: **cannibalization** (共食い) means the new item shifts demand away from an existing item. **Incremental sales** means sales genuinely added to the business. [NielsenIQ's assortment discussion](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/education/2022/the-value-of-incrementality-seeing-the-full-assortment-picture/) separates sales velocity from the value of incremental demand.

## 4｜A 300,000-yen hit can add only 50,000 yen

Try a simple thought experiment. LEGO Sanrio sells ¥300,000 a month. If ¥250,000 shifts from existing toys, the store gains only ¥50,000. If none is displaced, it gains ¥300,000. Same headline sales, very different incremental impact. The buyer may look immune to cuteness; perhaps they're simply doing the math.

![売れた額と増えた額の違いを示す著者作成図。30万円の新商品売上のうち25万円が既存商品の売上減少なら純増は5万円。](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/sanrio-lego-incrementality-2026.svg)

Real retailers also care about gross margin, shelf width, stock turns, markdowns, and returns. Sanrio and Target do not necessarily share the same incremental gain. So additional shelf space or more second-wave sets are signals, not proof. **Without a defined outcome, the same shelf photo can support opposite stories.**

## 5｜The LEGO deal may expand reasons to buy, not just space to sell

The [official announcement on October 6, 2026](https://corporate.sanrio.co.jp/news/20261006.html) confirms a multi-year deal and LEGO Sanrio products planned from 2027. Individual Cinnamoroll or Kuromi sets, launch counts, and wave-two plans are not yet confirmed. Excitement is fair; certainty is not.

[LEGO Animal Crossing](https://www.lego.com/en-us/themes/animal-crossing/about) provides a useful comparison: released in March 2024, it later expanded with more locations and characters. That does not guarantee Sanrio will follow the same schedule. The transferable idea is **a world that supports successive product waves**.

![レゴ公式の『どうぶつの森』博物館セット。シリーズの継続投入を説明する比較資料](https://www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/bltf0a7ea271067c206/77056_Prod.png)

Photo: LEGO official, Animal Crossing Blathers's Museum Collection (77056). これはSanrio LEGOの完成予想画像ではなく、継続展開の比較例。

The more interesting possibility is discovery: a family shopping for LEGO meets Hello Kitty without first visiting a Sanrio aisle. The **purchase occasion** changes. A toy could become a fan-acquisition touchpoint, not merely a royalty source. But new-customer growth requires buyer data; it cannot be inferred from product listings.

## 6｜To judge wave two, watch what remains—not only what arrives

Three scenarios matter. **Expansion**: new LEGO listings and stores without losing existing Sanrio toys. **Replacement**: LEGO grows while other Sanrio licensees shrink. **One-off demand**: the first wave fades and shelf presence contracts. LEGO unit sales alone cannot distinguish them.

Fewer SKUs in wave two do not automatically imply failure. Ten items selling 100 units each underperform five selling 300 each. Inventory costs differ, too. SKU count is easy to observe; it is not the goal.

[Target search results](https://www.target.com/s/sanrio%2Bmini) show MGA Series 3 and 4 as well as building toys from other Sanrio licensees. But online availability may include third-party sellers and does not prove physical distribution. The original insight—'the real shelf needs a store visit'—is valuable. Yet a photo still cannot reveal actual sell-through.

## 7｜A shelf allocates attention and purchase opportunities

Think of a shelf not as wood, but as a system allocating **discovery opportunities**. Eye-level placement, a core LEGO aisle, or a gift endcap attracts different shoppers. The same product meets different needs. That resembles ad placement and recommendations on streaming platforms.

Streaming has limited recommendation slots. Stadiums have limited ad surfaces; LED boards rotate creative over time. MGA's blind capsules place multiple character experiences behind a compact retail presentation. The mechanics and economics are different, but the shared idea is **more variety through constrained access points**.

This changes the definition of a competitor. A LEGO Sanrio gift may compete with plush toys, stationery, or flowers—not merely other bricks. Competition follows the **job the customer wants done**, not only the product category label.

## 8｜The causal arrow may run the other way

The usual story is: popular characters sell products. A different causal arrow is possible: a popular product format helps people discover a character. From **monetizing fans** to **creating fans through products**. This is a hypothesis, not an observed outcome, but it changes how to evaluate the deal.

Ecology offers the idea of an **ecological niche**: characters may serve different occasions instead of fighting directly. Real-options thinking suggests value in deciding later which character fits which market. These are analogies, not proven laws of branding. A large character catalog is useful only if it creates viable choices.

The deal may carry two kinds of value: near-term toy revenue and longer-term character discovery that could affect later purchases. We cannot yet quantify the second. That is exactly why **sales forecasts and audience-development hypotheses should be tracked separately**.

## 9｜Before visiting the shelf, design the comparison

'Could someone check a shelf in America next year?' Let's over-engineer it—carefully. This is a proposed field study, not a completed visit. Compare the same Target stores before launch, after wave one, and after the next product wave, with a few other locations for context.

![2027年以降の店頭観察フローを示す著者作成図](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/sanrio-lego-shelf-observation-2026.svg)

Record six things: **store coverage, LEGO vs. other licensees' SKUs, shelf width and facings, placement context, prices/markdowns/stockouts, and old-to-new assortment changes**. To infer sell-through and profit, POS and margin data would be needed where available.

Set counterexamples in advance: LEGO grows while other Sanrio SKUs shrink; or fewer products remain while total category profit improves. Both are plausible. Hunting only for photos that confirm the story is treasure hunting, not research. A useful test must allow **the evidence to change the hypothesis**.

## 10｜What matters may be a new entry point, not more centimeters

At first, I wanted to measure centimeters of shelf space. Now I want to know where the purchase came from: an existing Sanrio fan, a regular LEGO shopper, or someone choosing a gift. Same product, different business meaning.

A shelf reset is a retailer's bet on future demand. But a single photo cannot reveal that bet in full. Assortment, displacement, distribution, buyers, and economics must be read together.

If someone brings a ruler into an American toy aisle next year, ask what they're really measuring. Perhaps not the shelf at all, but an unfinished experiment in **how character awareness becomes incremental purchases through distribution**.

## Research note｜Facts, hypotheses, and open questions

**Verified facts:** 2026 earnings release, FY2026 North American category mix, October 2026 LEGO partnership, MGA's eight random variants, and ongoing Animal Crossing products. **Hypotheses:** new purchase occasions, displacement of existing products, and fan discovery. **Unknowns:** LEGO Sanrio assortment, future physical distribution, sales, and incremental profit.
