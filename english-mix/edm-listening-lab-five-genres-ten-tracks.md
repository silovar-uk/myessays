# EDM Listening Lab
## 5ジャンル10曲を、Kick・Bass・Energyの3問で聴き比べる

## 要旨

前編「EDMは、ひとつの音ではない」では、House、Techno、Trance、Dubstep、Drum & Bassを、ジャンル名より先に**聴く場所**から整理した。

This Listening Lab takes the next step.

目的は、10曲のジャンル名を暗記することではない。

When you hear an unfamiliar track, you should be able to ask three questions first.

1. **Where is the kick? ―― キックはどこにある？**
2. **What is the bass doing? ―― ベースは何をしている？**
3. **How does the energy move? ―― エネルギーはどう動く？**

The learning path is simple.

**Knowledge → Listening → Comparison → Recognition**

知る。

Listen.

比べる。

Then notice the structure for yourself.

---

## 1. 最初のルール――ジャンル名を当てる前に、1曲を3回聴く

Listen to each track at least three times, but change your target each time.

### Pass 1：Kickだけ追う

Follow the low “thump.”

- Is it on every beat: 1, 2, 3, 4?
- Does it disappear at some points?
- How does it interact with the snare or clap?
- Does the rhythm feel fast, or does your body feel it at half speed?

ジャンル名はまだ考えない。

Just watch the floor beneath the track.

### Pass 2：Bassだけ追う

Now follow the low end.

- Does the bass hit with the kick?
- Does it move between the kicks?
- Is it a long sub-bass tone?
- Does it make a repeating phrase?
- Is bass the main event, or mostly a foundation?

低音が聞き取りづらければ、スマホの小さなスピーカーよりヘッドホンやイヤホンのほうが分かりやすい。

### Pass 3：Energyだけ追う

Finally, zoom out and follow the whole track.

- Are layers slowly added?
- Does the same loop change in tiny ways?
- Is there a breakdown where the drums disappear?
- Is there a large return of the beat?
- Does the track keep nearly constant pressure?

ここまで聴いてから、初めて「何ジャンルっぽいか」を考える。

The goal is not simply to guess the label. The goal is to explain **why** it sounds that way.

---

## 2. BPMは答えではない――数字が食い違うこと自体が教材になる

We will use BPM as a guide, not as a judge.

同じ曲でも、BPMデータベースやDJストアで数字が違うことがある。

There are several reasons.

- Original Mix, Radio Edit, and remix versions may run at different speeds.
- Analysis algorithms can disagree.
- One database may count 85 BPM while another treats the same pulse as 170 BPM.
- Older tracks may have different metadata across reissues and edits.

だからBPMは**判定器**ではなく**補助線**。

Feel the speed first. Check the number second.

# HOUSE

## 3. Frankie Knuckles & Jamie Principle – Your Love

「Your Love」はChicago Houseの初期を聴く入口として非常に分かりやすい曲。

Preservation Chicago places the 1986 Frankie Knuckles / Jamie Principle version of “Your Love” in the important history surrounding The Warehouse and early Chicago House.

BPM data varies by version and analyzer, but it is often listed around 119 BPM.

### Pass 1：Kick

Count the regular four-on-the-floor pulse: one, two, three, four.

その上に、ほかの音がどう乗るかを見る。

### Pass 2：Bass

Do not isolate only the bass instrument. Listen to how the repeating synth movement works with the low-end foundation.

House often builds a stable floor with the kick and then creates movement around it.

### Pass 3：Energy

Do not wait only for one giant drop.

反復する素材に音が加わり、引かれ、grooveが育つ感覚を追う。

**After listening:** “A stable floor, with movement growing on top of it.”

### Source

- Preservation Chicago, The Warehouse  
  https://www.preservationchicago.org/the-warehouse-most-endangered-2023/
- Insomniac, Jamie Principle & Frankie Knuckles – “Your Love”  
  https://www.insomniac.com/music/jamie-principle-frankie-knuckles-your-love/

## 4. Mr Fingers – Can You Feel It

Larry HeardのMr Fingersによる「Can You Feel It」は、少ない要素からHouseの設計を見るのに向いている。

Roland describes it as a house blueprint built with the JUNO-60 and TR-909.

版によってBPM表記は異なるが、おおむね116〜119 BPM前後の範囲で見つかる。

### Pass 1：Kick

Notice how long the regular four-on-the-floor pulse remains your physical reference point.

### Pass 2：Bass

低音は派手に暴れるより、反復するpulseとして機能する。

Listen to how kick and bass leave enough space for groove.

### Pass 3：Energy

Ask not only “what happened?” but “what changed inside the repetition?”

大きな展開がなくても、音色、コード、パーカッションの変化だけで時間が進む。

**House comparison question:**

> 同じ四つ打ちでも、どこで身体が揺れ始めた？

### Source

- Roland, Can You Feel It: Mr. Fingers  
  https://articles.roland.com/can-you-feel-it-mr-fingers/
- Beatport, Mr. Fingers tracks  
  https://www.beatport.com/artist/mr-fingers/25487/tracks

# TECHNO

## 5. Cybotron – Clear

“Clear” is useful not as the one perfect definition of Techno, but as a **bridge from Electro toward Detroit Techno**.

Craft Recordings presents Cybotron’s *Enter*, including “Clear,” as a highly influential record in the development of Detroit Techno. Beatport, meanwhile, classifies “Clear” as Electro.

このズレが面白い。

Genres are borders drawn after the music already exists, and historical tracks often cross those borders.

Beatport lists the Original 1983 Mix at 125 BPM.

### Pass 1：Kick / Drum machine

Do not ask only whether it is four-on-the-floor.

syncopationや電子的なdrum patternそのものを聴く。

### Pass 2：Bass / Sequence

Listen to the repeating electronic sequence as a whole, not only to “the bass guitar part.”

### Pass 3：Energy

Follow how machine patterns create pressure through repetition.

**Important:** “Clear” is not the only sound of Techno. It is a track for learning the border.

### Source

- Craft Recordings, Cybotron – Enter  
  https://mediakits.concord.com/p/enter/press-release.html
- Beatport, Cybotron – Clear (Original 1983 Mix)  
  https://www.beatport.com/track/clear/18413032

## 6. Model 500 – No UFO’s

Juan AtkinsのModel 500による「No UFO’s」は、1985年のDetroit Techno初期を考えるうえで重要な曲。

Beatport lists it at 125 BPM, and it repeatedly appears in historical Techno selections.

### Pass 1：Kick

Follow the drum-machine pulse.

Houseとの違いを「暗い／明るい」ではなく、リズムの重心と反復の質で探す。

### Pass 2：Bass

Ask yourself: does the bass make the track sing, or does it make the machine move?

正解は一つではない。

### Pass 3：Energy

Instead of waiting for a huge chorus, listen for tiny changes in loop and texture.

### House vs Techno

続けて「Can You Feel It」と「No UFO’s」を聴く。

Their BPM ranges are close enough that speed alone will not save you.

- House：groove、コード、身体の揺れへ耳が向くか
- Techno：sequence、texture、machine-like repetitionへ耳が向くか

This is not a law. It is a comparison exercise.

### Source

- Beatport, Model 500 – No UFO’s  
  https://www.beatport.com/track/no-ufos-vocal/14413531

# TRANCE

## 7. Energy 52 – Café del Mar

“Café del Mar” is one of the classic reference points of 1990s Trance.

DJ Mag treats the 1993 track as a trance classic that has continued to generate major remixes.

Three ’N One Remixなど主要版では約133 BPM前後のデータが見られるが、版によって数字は異なる。

### Pass 1：Kick

The four-on-the-floor kick matters, but do not make it the star of this listen.

長い展開を支える床として働いているかを見る。

### Pass 2：Bass

Does the low end come to the front, or does it support melody and arpeggio?

### Pass 3：Energy

ここが中心。

Follow how melody appears, layers expand, the track pulls back, and the beat returns.

Ask: **where did the emotional level rise?**

### Source

- Energy 52, Café del Mar  
  https://energy52.bandcamp.com/album/caf-del-mar
- DJ Mag, Energy 52’s trance classic ‘Café Del Mar’  
  https://djmag.com/news/energy-52s-trance-classic-cafe-del-mar-remixed-orbital-and-michael-mayer

## 8. System F – Out of the Blue

Ferry CorstenのSystem F名義による「Out of the Blue」は、1999年Tranceの代表曲の一つ。

Beatport lists the Original Mix at 140 BPM, and DJ Mag has covered its creation with Ferry Corsten himself.

### Pass 1：Kick

Feel the fast four-on-the-floor pulse.

But kick alone could still point you toward Techno.

### Pass 2：Bass

ベースは、kickとsynthの間で前進力を作る様子を見る。

### Pass 3：Energy

Follow melody, build-up, breakdown, and the return of the beat.

「どこで感情が上向いたか」を自分で指差せれば十分。

### Techno vs Trance

Compare “No UFO’s” and “Out of the Blue.”

Both repeat, but repetition does different work.

- Techno：**loopそのものがどう変形するか**
- Trance：**melodyがどこへ連れていくか**

Techno can be melodic, and Trance can be hard and mechanical. The point is to notice the role of repetition.

### Source

- Beatport, System F – Out of the Blue  
  https://www.beatport.com/track/out-of-the-blue/13482067
- DJ Mag, The Making of System F – Out of the Blue  
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5DN2YllaxCg

# DUBSTEP

## 9. Skream – Midnight Request Line

Use this track to temporarily erase the idea that “Dubstep = loud wobble bass.”

The Guardian describes “Midnight Request Line” as a track many people saw as embodying the still-young Dubstep genre.

Beatportでは140 BPM。

### Pass 1：Kick / Snare

The number says 140, but your body may not feel like it is running at 140 all the time.

大きなアクセントの間隔を取り、**half-time feel**を感じる。

### Pass 2：Bass

Follow the sub-bass.

メロディより低音が空間そのものを変える感覚を見る。

### Pass 3：Energy

Notice that fewer sounds can sometimes make a section feel heavier.

EDMのenergyは、音数が多いほど高いとは限らない。

### Source

- The Guardian, Skream interview / dubstep history  
  https://www.theguardian.com/music/2011/dec/03/skream-magnetic-man-dubstep-global
- Beatport, Skream – Midnight Request Line  
  https://www.beatport.com/track/midnight-request-line/823394

## 10. Benga & Coki – Night

Benga & Cokiの「Night」は、Dubstepの低音と反復するsynth motifを聴くのに向いている。

In The Guardian, BBC Radio 1Xtra’s MistaJam names “Night” as a track that helped him understand the connection between Dubstep and Garage.

BPM metadata itself becomes part of the lesson here.

BeatportのRadio Editには140 BPM表記がある一方、別のOriginal Mixでは異なる数値が表示されることがある。

Trust your body before trusting the metadata.

### Pass 1：Kick / Snare

Feel the space between the major accents instead of expecting continuous four-on-the-floor kicks.

### Pass 2：Bass

sub-bassと上側のsynth motifを別々に追ってみる。

Do not reduce Dubstep to one spectacular bass sound.

### Pass 3：Energy

Find the moments where **weight** matters more than numerical speed.

### Source

- The Guardian, MistaJam: the 10 tracks that shaped my career  
  https://www.theguardian.com/music/2012/aug/09/mistajam-10-tracks-1xtra
- Beatport, Benga & Coki – Night (Radio Edit)  
  https://www.beatport.com/track/night/10888522

# DRUM & BASS

## 11. Goldie – Inner City Life

“Inner City Life” is useful because Drum & Bass is not only “fast and aggressive.”

DJ Mag’s 30th-anniversary making-of coverage treats the track as a classic that helped Drum & Bass reach a wider audience.

Beatport掲載版は155 BPM。

### Pass 1：Drums

Here, the kick alone is not enough.

breakbeat全体を追い、細かく切られたドラムがどれほど前へ走るかを見る。

### Pass 2：Bass

Listen for the longer low-end foundation under the busy drums.

上が忙しいからこそ、下が安定して聞こえる瞬間がある。

### Pass 3：Energy

Vocal and wider harmonic textures give the track emotional range beyond pure speed.

**速い＝常に攻撃的**ではない。

### Listen / Source

- Goldie – Inner City Life (official video)  
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vHkOI-Z7lY
- Beatport, Goldie – Inner City Life  
  https://www.beatport.com/track/inner-city-life/12957498

## 12. Roni Size / Reprazent – Brown Paper Bag

This is also a perfect lesson in why BPM metadata is not absolute truth.

BeatportのOriginal Mixには85 BPMと表示される。

But in Drum & Bass listening, you can easily feel a double-time drum grid around 170 BPM.

85 or 170?

どちらか一方が絶対というより、half-time / double-timeで拍をどう数えるかの違いがある。

### Pass 1：Drums

Follow the rapid breakbeat and ignore the temptation to call an 85 BPM label “slow.”

### Pass 2：Bass

印象的なbass motifを追い、その周囲を高速のdrumが走る構造を見る。

### Pass 3：Energy

Hear two speeds at once: a fast surface and a relatively stable low-end foundation.

### Dubstep vs Drum & Bass

Compare “Midnight Request Line” with “Brown Paper Bag.”

Both are bass music, but your body counts them differently.

- Dubstep：大きな間、half-time feel、sub-bassの重さ
- Drum & Bass：高速breakbeat、細かい前進、低音との二重速度

Numbers are useful, but physical feel tells you more.

### Listen / Source

- Roni Size / Reprazent – Brown Paper Bag (official video)  
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cwI0gbGEyuI
- Beatport, Brown Paper Bag  
  https://www.beatport.com/track/brown-paper-bag/20687401

## 13. 30分で一周するListening Lab

You do not need to understand everything in one session.

30分なら、こう回す。

### 0–5分：House

“Your Love” → “Can You Feel It”

**Where did you start to feel the groove?**

### 5–10分：Techno

“Clear” → “No UFO’s”

**What repeated, and what changed slightly?**

### 10–15分：Trance

“Café del Mar” → “Out of the Blue”

**どこで感情が上向いた？**

### 15–20分：Dubstep

“Midnight Request Line” → “Night”

**Where did you hear space, not sound?**

### 20–25分：Drum & Bass

“Inner City Life” → “Brown Paper Bag”

**ドラムとベースは同じ速さに感じた？**

### 25–30分：Cross Comparison

Choose one track from the genre you understood best and one from the genre that confused you most.

Then write only three lines.

- Kick / Drums：
- Bass：
- Energy：

That is enough.

## 14. Listening Log――答え合わせより、観察記録を残す

Use the same small template for every track.

```text
Track:

Kick / Drums:

Bass:

Energy:

One word:

Genre guess（3回聴いたあと）:

比較して初めて気づいたこと:
```

You do not need perfect technical vocabulary.

たとえば、

```text
Kick / Drums: ずっとドンドンドンドン
Bass: キックの間を動く
Energy: 一発で爆発せず、じわじわ変わる
```

でも十分。

The important move is turning what your ear noticed into your own words.

## 15. ありがちな誤判定を先につぶす

### 「四つ打ちならHouse」

No. Techno and Trance also use four-on-the-floor constantly.

### 「暗ければTechno」

No. Mood is not a reliable genre detector.

### 「メロディがあればTrance」

No. Techno and House can be strongly melodic too.

### 「wobble bassならDubstep」

Too narrow. Listen for sub-bass, syncopation, half-time feel, and space.

### 「速ければDrum & Bass」

No. Listen to the relationship between breakbeat and bass.

### 「DropがあればEDM」

No. A drop is not a requirement for all EDM.

### 「BPMデータベースの数字が正解」

No. Version and analysis method matter.

**分類より観察。 Observation before classification.**

## 16. なぜ今すぐジャンル当てQuizを作らないのか

It is tempting to build a four-choice “What genre is this?” quiz immediately.

でも、今はまだ早い。

A quiz can train label recognition very efficiently.

今回育てたいのは、**音の構造を観察し、その根拠を説明する力**。

First, make ten Listening Logs.

その記録を見ると、自分がどこで迷うかが分かる。

- House vs Techno
- Techno vs Trance
- Dubstep vs Drum & Bass speed perception

Then the quiz can target your real confusion instead of generic textbook distinctions.

---

## まとめ――Name the structure before the genre

You do not need to name the genre first.

知らない曲を再生する。

Find the kick.

次にベースを見る。

Follow the movement of energy.

そして、

> **Kick is doing this. Bass is doing this. Energy moves like this. So the track sounds closer to this family.**

と言えれば十分だ。

The label can come later.

**Name the structure before the genre.**

ジャンルを当てる前に、構造を言葉にする。

Once you listen in this order, EDM stops sounding like one mass of similar electronic sounds.

リズム、低音、反復、空間、感情の設計が違う音楽として見え始める。

There is only one next step.

**この10曲でListening Logを10件作る。**

Build the quiz after that.
