# 構造化は、考えることそのものなのか
## ミントのピラミッド原則を、思考・説明・AI時代の構造批評として読み直す

“Start with the conclusion.”

仕事では便利な助言だ。But it becomes useless when you do not know the conclusion yet.

売上低下、SNS流入減、若年層減少、広告量減、既存客維持。If these are all you have, the real work is not moving an answer to the top. **It is discovering what deserves to be called the answer.**

Barbara Mintoのピラミッド原則が面白いのは、この「書く前」の段階に踏み込むからだ。

Minto herself describes the pyramid as a tool for finding out what you think, and her official material covers thinking and problem solving as well as writing.

ただし、ここで一段慎重になりたい。**The Pyramid Principle is not the same thing as thinking itself.**

It is a practical method for arranging ideas and exposing their relationships. It is not a comprehensive cognitive theory proven to describe how human thought works.

だから本稿では、構造化を思考の完成形ではなく、**思考を外へ出し、検査可能にする技術**として読む。

[McKinsey — Barbara Minto on MECE and the Pyramid Principle](https://www.mckinsey.com/alumni/news-and-events/global-news/alumni-news/barbara-minto-mece-i-invented-it-so-i-get-to-say-how-to-pronounce-it)
[Minto Books International — The Minto Pyramid Principle](https://www.barbaraminto.com/)

---

## 1. ピラミッドは「答えを先に言う」より、上下の論理を検査する道具

The simplest pyramid has one governing thought, supporting ideas beneath it, and more concrete grounds below them.

でも「理由を3つ並べる」だけでは足りない。Minto’s rules are stricter: the upper point should summarize the points below, ideas in one group should be logically comparable, and the group should have a meaningful order.

That is why the method is useful before prose polishing. It makes broken connections visible.

結論と根拠が混ざっている。同じ階層に異質な項目がある。上の一文が下を要約できない。Those defects are easier to see once the argument is externalized.

Minto later recalled that, while repeatedly rebuilding reports into pyramid form, “the problem was the thinking, not the language.”

The strength of the pyramid is therefore not cosmetic neatness. **It is the ability to expose where your logic does not connect.**

---

## 2. 「まとめる」は圧縮ではなく、上位の意味を仮置きすること

Suppose you know four things:

- 若年層の新規来場が減った
- SNSからの流入が減った
- 若年層向け広告のimpが減った
- 既存客の継続率は前年並み

A short summary can compress them. Structured thinking goes one step further and proposes a higher-level meaning:

> 新規顧客の獲得部分に問題が集中しているのではないか。

That sentence is not raw data. **It is an interpretation created by grouping observations.**

だから、構造化で生まれた上位概念はfactではなくhypothesisとして扱うほうが安全だ。

A different grouping may generate a different interpretation. The pyramid helps produce meaning, but it cannot automatically decide which meaning is true.

Good structuring means making the hypothesis explicit enough to compare with alternative groupings and disconfirming evidence. **Do not freeze the first elegant structure too early.**

---

## 3. MECEは「世界がMECEである」ことを保証しない

MECE means Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive. It helps reduce overlap and omission inside a chosen classification.

たとえば売上を「客数 × 客単価」に分けるのは、探索の入口として強い。But a clean classification does not guarantee a good problem definition.

You can divide visitors perfectly by age and still miss the decisive distinction if the real difference is first-time versus repeat attendance.

つまりMECEが答えるのは、**“Is this partition internally coherent?”** であって、**“Why is this the right partition?”** ではない。

MECE is best treated as a local audit tool for a chosen axis, not as a certificate that the world itself has been divided correctly.

---

## 4. SCQは、答えの前に「何に答えるのか」を整える

Minto Books International presents the SCQ Framework as a way to identify the question already forming in the reader’s mind.

- Situation：共有されている状況
- Complication：変化・問題
- Question：そこから自然に生まれる問い

SCQの価値は、結論をドラマチックに見せることではない。**It aligns the answer with the question the reader actually needs answered.**

If retention is stable but new customers are falling, the complication naturally creates a question such as “where should we intervene?” Only then does a conclusion about acquisition become meaningful.

Answer-first communication fails when the listener cannot see why that answer matters now.

So before moving a conclusion to the top, make sure the question exists underneath it.

[Minto Books International — Concept / SCQ Framework](https://www.barbaraminto.com/)

---

## 5. 探索・整理・説明を一つの型へ押し込まない

When the problem is well defined, evidence is sufficient, and the answer is largely known, a pyramid can be extremely powerful.

一方、何が問題か自体が争点なら、早すぎる構造化は探索を閉じることがある。

Rittel and Webber’s discussion of wicked problems is useful here: problem definition and solution can shape each other, so the “correct” formulation may not be fixed in advance.

地域活性化、孤立、組織文化。Depending on whose perspective you adopt, even the problem itself changes.

It helps to separate at least three activities:

- **Exploration** — 重要な変数や問い自体を探す
- **Organization** — 情報同士の関係を仮置きする
- **Explanation** — 読者が追える順序へ変換する

The Minto method is especially strong in organization and explanation. It can support exploration, but the first pyramid should remain provisional.

Before structuring, ask: **Am I communicating an answer, or still searching for one?**

[Rittel & Webber (1973) — Dilemmas in a General Theory of Planning](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01405730)

---

## 6. 「Mintoは効く」は、実務普及と実証研究を分ける

The Pyramid Principle has had enormous practical influence in consulting and corporate communication.

But wide adoption is not the same thing as controlled causal evidence.

2026年にPsychArchivesへ登録された研究計画は、Minto-based trainingがargumentative structuringへ与える効果をcluster-randomized field designで検証しようとしている。その問題設定自体が、the method is widely used but has not been extensively validated through controlled empirical studies, という境界を置いている。

This does not mean the method is ineffective.

It means we should distinguish **practice evidence** from **causal evidence**.

Long use by Minto, McKinsey, and organizations is a meaningful signal of practical usefulness. It does not by itself identify the causal effect on learning or decision quality.

だから、ピラミッド原則は「科学的に証明済みの思考法」と言うより、**strong practical track recordを持ち、なおempirical validationの余地があるstructuring method**として扱うのが妥当だ。

[PsychArchives — Evaluating the Minto Pyramid Principle Under Massed and Spaced Training](https://doi.org/10.23668/psycharchives.21791)

---

## 7. AI時代は「構造を作る能力」より「構造を批評する能力」が希少になる

Ask a generative AI to “organize this into three points,” “make it MECE,” “use SCQ,” or “lead with the answer,” and a polished structure appears almost instantly.

構造生成のコストは下がった。

But fluency does not prove that the grouping is correct. Why these three buckets? Does the evidence support the causal story? What alternative problem definition was omitted?

AI can cheaply produce multiple plausible storylines from the same material.

そのとき希少になるのは、pyramidを速く描く能力そのものではない。**It is deciding which structure to keep, where to doubt it, and what needs further verification.**

If AI helps structure the material, keep one final question in the workflow:

> **If this structure is wrong, where is it most likely to be wrong?**

---

## 8. 構造化の完成は、「壊せること」で確かめる

A practical sequence can be simple:

1. 事実・観察・違和感を出す
2. Group similar items
3. 「つまり何か」を一文にする
4. Check whether upper and lower levels really form summary-support relationships
5. MECEを局所検査に使う
6. Use SCQ to align the reader’s question
7. 別の切り方を一つ作る
8. Look for disconfirming evidence

A good structure is not one that looks impossible to break. **It is one that survives serious attempts to break it.**

Once a pyramid looks beautiful, we become attached to it. Alternative classifications, counterevidence, and alternative questions help distinguish explanatory power from mere neatness.

ミントのピラミッド原則は、答えを固定する型としてより、**a working structure that can be visualized, criticized, and rebuilt** として使うと強い。

---

## おわりに

「考える」とは、情報に階層をつくることなのか。

Only partly.

Hierarchy helps us see relations between concrete and abstract, conclusion and support, question and answer.

But the world does not arrive already shaped like one pyramid.

どの情報を入れるか。何を同じgroupとみなすか。何を上位概念にするか。そもそも何を問題とするか。

Those judgments remain outside the diagram.

So even when AI can generate endless clean pyramids, structured thinking does not disappear. The human advantage shifts toward **building structures and refusing to be fooled by them.**
