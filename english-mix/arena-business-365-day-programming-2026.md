# Arena Business: How to Program 365 Days, Not Just Game Nights

*English Mix / 30 October 2026. Japanese is the comprehension base; English is used where it clarifies how venue professionals actually talk. This is not a line-by-line translation.*

A sold-out basketball game looks like the perfect advertisement for an arena. But a building has **365 days**, and its home team uses only some of them.

つまり、施設経営の本質は試合日の売上だけではない。A venue is also a **giant booking calendar**. To host a concert on Friday, the production crew may need Thursday for *load-in*, the audience arrives on Friday, and the crew needs time for *load-out* before the next event.

The question isn't simply, “How can we fill more dates?” It is: **What mix of events maximizes financial returns, guest experience, and community value?**

## 守｜1. アリーナビジネスとは何を経営することか

An arena is a large indoor venue that can switch between sports, concerts, comedy, family shows, and corporate events. “Arena” and “stadium” aren't strict worldwide categories, but the former often suggests an enclosed, multi-purpose venue.

大事なのは、**the venue operator is not necessarily the event promoter** という点。The owner, operating company, home team, concert promoter, performer, ticketing platform, caterer, and sponsors may all be different businesses.

When a customer pays ¥10,000 for a concert ticket, **¥10,000 does not automatically become arena revenue**. A promoter may control the box office, pay the artist and production suppliers, and rent the building. The venue may earn a rental fee, a share of ticket revenue, food and beverage income, premium-seat revenue, or sponsorship revenue. Revenue allocation depends on the contracts. [Live Nation annual filing](https://investors.livenationentertainment.com/sec-filings/annual-reports/content/0001335258-23-000014/lyv-20221231.htm).

### Eight possible revenue streams

| Stream | 何を売っているか |
| --- | --- |
| Venue rental | 催事日と施設サービス |
| Food, beverage, parking, merchandise | 来場者の付帯消費と契約上の会場取り分 |
| Premium seating | スイート・ラウンジ・特別席の利用権 |
| Naming rights and sponsorship | 名前・広告・提携・施設ブランド |
| Ticketing and facility fees | 発券等から会場に帰属する部分 |
| In-house promotion | 会場自らが興行主となる企画 |
| Secondary spaces | 会議室・広場・撮影・見学 |
| District development | 飲食店・商業・不動産の収益。ただし権利者次第 |

The key phrase is **revenue capture**: who actually keeps the money? A restaurant outside the building may benefit from a concert, but its sales aren't automatically the arena operator's sales.

## 守｜2. 歴史：体育館から年間興行と街区経営へ

The evolution of arenas is also a story of **commercializing space, time, and audience relationships**.

1990年代には、sports arenas increasingly incorporated *corporate suites*, *naming rights*, and multiple anchor teams. Toronto's current Scotiabank Arena, opened in 1999, was designed for the NHL's Maple Leafs and the NBA's Raptors. [Official history](https://www.scotiabankarena.com/venue-information/history).

The O2 in London opened as an entertainment destination in 2007, reusing the former Millennium Dome. The original dome had been created for the millennium exhibition but attracted criticism after missing its visitor target. A struggling public landmark eventually became a major entertainment district. [Institution of Civil Engineers](https://www.ice.org.uk/what-is-civil-engineering/infrastructure-projects/millennium-dome) / [AEG](https://www.aeg-international.com/portfolio/the-o2-arena).

Then came purpose-built or redeveloped projects: Chase Center (2019), Climate Pledge Arena (2021), Co-op Live (2024), and Intuit Dome (2024). Operators invested in acoustics, premium hospitality, technology, and the surrounding district. [Chase Center](https://chasecenter.com/news/economic-impact-report-estimates-over-4-billion-generated-by-chase-center-20240909/) / [Intuit Dome](https://www.intuitdome.com/about/about-us).

ただし、newer and larger does not automatically mean more profitable. Construction cost, debt, renewal investment, and demand must be considered.

## 守｜3. 稼働率には、似て非なる指標がある

This is where the word *utilization* gets tricky.

The International Association of Venue Managers (IAVM) distinguishes *events*, *event days*, *non-event days*, and *dark days*. Importantly, an **event day can include load-in and load-out**, not merely a performance day. Multiple performances can also take place on the same calendar date. [IAVM's performance framework, pp. 4–6](https://iavm.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/IAVMArenasPerformanceReportingHandbook_07302023.pdf).

- **Show count:** 公演の本数。One calendar date may host two performances.
- **Public event-day ratio:** 観客を迎える日数 ÷ 365日。
- **Contracted occupancy ratio:** 契約上の占有日数 ÷ 365日。May include setup and teardown.
- **Seat sell-through:** 販売席数 ÷ 販売可能席数。
- **Turnstile attendance:** 実際に入場した人数。Not the same as ticket distribution.
- **Contribution margin:** 会場帰属の純収入 − 催事別の直接変動費。

A three-day booking could involve one performance day, one load-in day, and one load-out day. Saying “200 shows equals 55% annual utilization” would therefore mix two different measures.

## 守｜4. 海外で何が起きているか――５つの異なる経営モデル

### The O2, London — events plus a destination

AEG reported **239 arena performances and over 2.9 million tickets sold in 2025**. The larger O2 district welcomed **over 11 million visitors** in the same year. [Arena performance](https://www.aeg-international.com/press-centre/the-o2-delivers-historic-record-breaking-2025-performance) / [District footfall](https://www.aeg-international.com/press-centre/another-record-year-the-o2-celebrates-best-ever-footfall-and-sales-in-2025).

The difference matters. Ticket sales and district footfall describe different populations. They cannot simply be added together.

示唆は **the arena can be dark while the district remains alive** ということ。Retail, dining, smaller music venues, and leisure attractions create reasons to visit even without a headline concert.

### Co-op Live, Manchester — artists and promoters are also customers

Opening in May 2024, this music-focused arena reported approximately **105 events and over one million tickets** during its first year. Its acoustics, capacity, and concert-first design are part of its pitch to touring artists and promoters. [Co-op Live's first-year report](https://www.cooplive.com/co-op-live-celebrates-first-year-as-top-arena-in-uk-and-europe-1zqr).

A venue competes for the **tour routing decision**. The agent asks not only, “Can we sell tickets here?” but also, “Can our trucks load in? Does the sound work? Can the production team trust this venue?”

もちろん、開業リスクもあった。Technical problems led to postponements and cancellations, including an incident involving ventilation equipment. A promising building is not a substitute for reliable operations. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2025/may/16/from-early-setbacks-to-a-storming-springsteen-show-co-op-live-a-year-on).

### Climate Pledge Arena, Seattle — a foundation of sports

The rebuilt arena opened in 2021 beneath a roof inherited from the 1962 World's Fair site. Its regular users include professional ice hockey and women's basketball. The arena describes itself as hosting about **200 events a year**. A Seattle city document reported approximately **1.73 million total event visitors in 2025**. [Venue FAQ](https://climatepledgearena.com/frequently-asked-questions/) / [City of Seattle](https://seattle.legistar.com/View.ashx?G=FFE3B678-CEF6-4197-84AC-5204EA4CFC0C&GUID=B5819CC0-082B-4B11-BC85-58A06ACDAF08&ID=1390743&M=PA).

Sports teams create a predictable calendar, while concerts fill additional dates. But the same floor must switch between hockey, basketball, and live music.

![Climate Pledge Arena外観](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Climate%20pledge%20arena.jpg?width=960)

*Photo 2｜Kurt Schlosser, 2021. [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Climate_pledge_arena.jpg), [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/), unmodified.*

### Chase Center, San Francisco — the arena and its neighborhood

Chase Center opened in 2019 as home of the Golden State Warriors, alongside the surrounding Thrive City district. A venue-commissioned report estimated **$4.2 billion in regional economic impact between July 2019 and June 2024**. [Chase Center's announcement](https://chasecenter.com/news/economic-impact-report-estimates-over-4-billion-generated-by-chase-center-20240909/).

But **economic impact is not profit**. The estimate includes activity across the local economy, not simply cash earned by the arena owner. Displaced spending and the assumptions behind multiplier effects also matter.

### The Kallang, Singapore — public value as an operating goal

The former Singapore Sports Hub combines a stadium, indoor arenas, swimming facilities, retail, and community spaces. The public-side operator reported **50% growth in event attendance during 2024 versus 2023**, along with more than **40,000 hours of national-athlete training**. [The Kallang](https://www.thekallang.com.sg/en/media-centre/singapore-sports-hub-celebrates-record-year.html).

A 2026 government statement referred to **more than 4,000 event days across the precinct** since public takeover in late 2022. That is a multi-facility total, **not** a single arena operating more than 365 days each year. [Singapore government answer](https://www.mccy.gov.sg/about-us/news-and-resources/rebranding-of-the-kallang/).

Here, schools, athletes, and local residents are not merely “low-paying customers.” Public access can be one of the project's intended outcomes.

## 破｜5. 稼働率をどう上げるか――10の実務的な打ち手

**The first lever is booking access.** Become part of the touring circuit. Oak View Group markets calendars, technical information, venue networks, and routing support to promoters, agencies, and producers. [OVG Booking & Content](https://www.oakviewgroup.com/our-solutions/booking-content/).

実務的には、次の10策を別々のレバーとして管理できる。

1. **Win the tour routing decision.** 巡業都市間の移動・物流・曜日に適合する営業。
2. **Secure anchor tenants.** 定期的なスポーツ日程を基礎需要にする。ただし日程の優先権も管理。
3. **Convert one show into multi-night runs.** 設営費を複数公演で吸収できるか検証。
4. **Use different deal structures.** 貸館だけでなく共同主催、自主興行。ただしリスクは増える。
5. **Offer flexible capacities.** 必要に応じて客席・フロアを小規模化。
6. **Reduce conversion time.** 競技仕様とライブ仕様の変更時間を短縮。
7. **Segment dates by demand.** 平日法人イベント、週末ライブ、学校・地域利用などに分ける。
8. **Monetize secondary spaces.** メイン会場以外のラウンジ・広場・会議室を活用。
9. **Package premium access.** 複数興行を束ねた法人利用・年間席を販売。
10. **Track lost bookings.** 失注理由を日程・設備・採算・競争に分類する。

The surprising opportunity may be **operations, not marketing**. FIBA notes that movable flooring can allow a venue to change uses within hours, sometimes overnight. An Atlanta arena manager described reducing basketball conversion time from roughly 12 hours to four to six. [FIBA Venue Guide](https://www.venueguide.fiba.basketball/venue-operations) / [Atlanta Journal-Constitution](https://www.ajc.com/business/2025/10/inside-the-team-that-transforms-state-farm-arena-overnight-from-games-to-concerts/).

つまり、営業案件を増やす前に **create more sellable dates** という発想がある。もちろん安全基準や従業員の労働環境を犠牲にしてはいけない。

## 破｜6. 年間カレンダーは「会場の在庫管理」である

Think of each calendar date as inventory. Unlike a hotel room, however, a large show can block surrounding dates.

次は**説明用の架空モデル**で、実在施設の実績ではない。

| Use | Days |
| --- | ---: |
| Sports games / 有観客試合 | 55 |
| Concert performances / ライブ | 80 |
| Other public events / その他興行 | 30 |
| Corporate and community events / 企業・地域 | 20 |
| Additional load-in and load-out / 追加準備撤去 | 55 |
| Practice, maintenance, unavailable / 練習・保守 | 45 |
| Unbooked / 未利用 | 80 |
| **Total / 計** | **365** |

Under these assumptions, there are **185 performance or event days (50.7%)** and **240 contracted event-related occupancy days (65.8%)**. The categories do not overlap. These are not actual data from any overseas venue.

The 80 unbooked days are not necessarily 80 *sellable* days. Some are poor-demand weekdays; others are constrained by maintenance or provisional dates held for sports.

**Management KPI:** report both occupancy over 365 days and the sales conversion rate over genuinely sellable dates. Showing only the second metric can hide inconvenient dates.

A useful funnel is: **inquiries → qualified booking requests → feasible dates → contracts → performance days → occupied days → venue net revenue → contribution margin → annual operating performance**.

## 破｜7. 「イベントを１本増やす」採算はどう計算するか

The central concept is **contribution margin**: how much an event contributes after its direct incremental costs.

**Event contribution margin = venue-attributable net revenue − direct variable event costs.**

Suppose an event creates ¥3 million in venue revenue and ¥1.8 million in direct costs. Its contribution margin is **¥1.2 million**. Twenty similar events would generate **¥24 million** in incremental contribution, assuming no cannibalization or additional fixed costs.

日本語で注意すると、これは営業利益でも投資回収額でもない。Debt service, maintenance investment, depreciation, staffing overhead, and other costs still matter.

For annual cash planning, one simplified view is: **venue cash inflow − operating cash outflow − maintenance capital expenditure − debt service**, with further adjustments for taxes, subsidies, and financing terms.

Booking decisions should ask five questions: What existing event might this displace? How many total days does it occupy? What costs does it add? Which revenues actually belong to the operator? What impact does it have on fans, the team, and the community?

**Margin per occupied day** can be more informative than margin per performance, although opportunity cost is lower on dates that nobody else wants.

## 破｜8. 誰が稼働を決めるのか――営業力の背後にある交渉力

A venue sales team is not just selling square meters. It is selling **reliable execution inside a touring network**.

The promoter may be the direct business customer; the audience is the ultimate user. A booking agent must consider geographic routing, available dates, technical riders, production costs, and expected ticket demand.

この関係を「アリーナは興行のインフラを卸売りする」と考えると分かりやすい。A well-connected operator can propose not just one date, but a sequence of compatible cities. That creates bargaining power beyond the physical building. [OVG](https://www.oakviewgroup.com/our-solutions/booking-content/).

## 守｜9. 重要用語を、実務で使う意味から覚える

- **Anchor tenant:** 定期的な基礎需要を持つホームチーム。
- **Booking:** 会場の公演日程を仮押さえ・確定する営業。A hold is not a signed booking.
- **Tour routing:** ツアー都市・移動・曜日・機材物流の組み方。
- **Load-in / load-out:** 搬入・設営／撤去・搬出。
- **Venue conversion:** 競技・コンサート間の会場転換。
- **Dark day:** 催しのない日。ただし契約占有中の暗転日もある。
- **House nut:** イベント対応に必要な施設側の直接経費。
- **Rental / co-pro / in-house:** 貸館／共同主催／自主興行。Risk and reward are allocated differently.
- **Premium seating:** 法人スイート・特別席など、高単価の利用権。
- **Naming rights:** 会場の名称等を使用するスポンサー権利。
- **F&B per capita:** 飲食売上÷来場者。Gross sales are not the same as the operator's share.
- **Net revenue:** 会場に実際に帰属する収入。See the [IAVM definitions](https://iavm.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/IAVMArenasPerformanceReportingHandbook_07302023.pdf).

## 破｜10. 批判：満員の会場は、街を豊かにしたのか

It is easy to confuse **gross economic activity** with **net public benefit**.

A concert attracts restaurant spending, but some of that money may simply move from another local business. Public subsidies, transport infrastructure, noise, emissions, and future renovation costs also belong in the evaluation.

More than 130 studies are reviewed in a major academic survey. Its conclusion is that broad local economic effects of professional sports venues tend to be limited relative to large public subsidies. [Bradbury et al., Journal of Economic Surveys](https://doi.org/10.1111/joes.12533).

A case study of Brooklyn's Barclays Center found that early event frequency and attendance promises were met, while other claims relating to employment and development proved problematic. It does not establish the performance of every arena. [Published case study](https://doi.org/10.1108/IJPSM-01-2018-0002).

だから、公共施設の評価には **two scorecards** が必要になる。One measures financial sustainability; the other measures local accessibility, educational use, additional tourism, quality of life, congestion, labor conditions, and environmental burden.

## 離｜11. アリーナは「ホテル」「空港」「メディア」の交差点にある

These are **analogies and hypotheses**, not verified laws of arena economics.

**Like a hotel:** Unsold inventory expires. A vacant Tuesday cannot be stored and sold next year. But unlike a hotel room, a single arena booking can consume several adjacent dates.

**Like an airport:** Slots and operational constraints matter. An arena has its own “slots” for games, concerts, load-in, maintenance, and safety inspections.

**Like a media company:** The annual events calendar resembles *programming*. Big-name events attract attention, while smaller events create repeat relationships and broader audience coverage.

**Like real estate:** The event can generate neighborhood spending, but the operator captures real estate profits only when it has relevant ownership or contractual rights.

**Like a public library:** Some activities are worthwhile not because they maximize revenue, but because they improve access, learning, and community participation.

## 離｜12. これからの予測：競争するのは座席数ではない

**Hypothesis A — Booking networks become more valuable.** As more arenas compete for large tours, agency relationships, flexible booking dates, and technical reliability may matter more than simply adding seats.

**Hypothesis B — Mid-sized venues specialize.** Rather than competing head-on with large arenas, they may focus on corporate events, local tournaments, recordings, or formats that can be produced efficiently.

**Hypothesis C — Revenue management becomes cross-functional.** Successful optimization may connect calendar slots, ticket allocation, premium spaces, food and beverage, and operational labor. Data rights still need careful governance.

**Hypothesis D — Public venues need better public-value measurement.** Attendance alone is not enough. School hours, access for residents, noise, transit, and community benefits may increasingly shape investment decisions.

いずれも2026年10月時点の**予測・作業仮説**であり、確定した未来ではない。

## 実務編｜13. アリーナの改善診断をするなら、まず何を集めるか

Before launching a large research program, collect **raw operating data** for the past 24 months:

1. Daily calendar of shows, load-in/out, maintenance, practice and vacant dates.
2. Event category, promoter, show count, attendance and ticket records.
3. Inquiries, tentative holds, final bookings and loss reasons.
4. Contract structures and the operator's actual revenue share.
5. Security, staffing, cleaning, power and conversion costs by event.
6. F&B and merchandise per-capita sales and retained margin.
7. Premium seat, naming-rights and sponsorship contracts.
8. Loading docks, rigging, sound, flooring and seat-configuration constraints.
9. Protected home-team dates and release deadlines.
10. Transport, access and commercial district relationships.
11. Capital spending, maintenance obligations and financing.
12. Community-use, complaint, safety and environmental records.

おすすめの順番は **calendar heat map → lost-booking Pareto analysis → margin by event and occupied day → calendar optimization under sports constraints → a field experiment**。

### A 90-day improvement cycle

**Days 1–30:** Define the metrics and clean the calendar. Separate confirmed bookings from tentative holds. Align cost data with occupied dates.

**Days 31–60:** Identify the biggest reasons why events were lost. Test whether demand, dates, technical specifications, or economics are the actual constraints.

**Days 61–90:** Pilot only three interventions, such as midweek events, multi-night concert proposals, and faster release of unused protected dates. Measure incremental margin, occupied days, promoter satisfaction, and safety.

## 結び｜アリーナは「空いている日」があるからこそ、経営が難しい

A successful arena is not a building with its lights on every night.

試合を開催する日、転換のために働く夜、子どもがコートを使う朝、企業が顧客を招くラウンジ。それぞれが別の価値を生む。

The business is to **orchestrate these different uses without confusing attendance, revenue, profit, and public value**.

The best calendar is not necessarily the fullest calendar.
