# なぜ牛乳ブランドが女子サッカーを育てるのか――MilkLife's sports ecosystem in Indonesia

### From Pokémon and Nadeshiko legends to 15 cities, coaching pathways, and the Djarum connection

**Research cutoff: October 23, 2026.** インドネシア語の現地記事、公式発表、行政・リーグ記録に基づく。We distinguish **verified events, organizers' claims, interpretation, and proposals**. 参加者数はunique individualsとは限らない。

## 1. ポケモンが来た。But the girls were already playing before Pikachu appeared

In September 2026, Mana Iwabuchi（岩渕真奈）and Aya Miyama（宮間あや）, both members of Japan's 2011 Women's World Cup-winning team, arrived in Jakarta. そこへポケモン、さらに乳製品ブランド「MilkLife」が並ぶ。It sounds like a pitch deck where three unrelated ideas were somehow approved together.

But the sequence matters. ポケモンが女子サッカー大会を一から作ったのではない。**MilkLife and the Djarum Foundation had already built a recurring grassroots programme. Pokémon joined an existing platform.**

The Pokémon Company announced its **MilkLife Sports Project with Pokémon** on September 14. It covers girls' football and badminton; former world No.1 badminton star Kento Momota（桃田賢斗）also appeared at the announcement. On September 17, Iwabuchi and Miyama coached **74 girls aged 10–13** at KingKong Arena in Cijantung, East Jakarta. 彼女たちは翌年のAll-Starsを目指す「MilkLife Soccer Extra Training」の参加者だった。This was a documented coaching session, not merely a future plan.

[ジャカルタ日報](https://www.jakarta-nippo.com/14838/)／[Pokémon official Indonesian release](https://asia-press.portal-pokemon.com/press-release/Proyek-Baru-di-Indonesia-Untuk-Tahun-Fiskal-2026-2027/)／[Media Indonesia on-site report](https://mediaindonesia.com/sepak-bola/934697/legenda-jepang-berbagi-ilmu-sepak-bola-di-mlsc)

![MilkLife Soccer Challengeの参加者（Djarum Foundation公式写真）](https://djarumfoundation.org/webcore/files/gallery/68932d7ebc882.webp)

*Source: [Djarum Foundation official gallery](https://www.djarumfoundation.org/gallery/milklife-soccer-challenge-kudus-seri-1-2025-2026/photo). This photo shows a football tournament, not the September 2026 Pokémon clinic.*

## 2. MilkLife is a dairy brand first. スポーツは食品事業と別の場所から始まった

MilkLife is produced by **PT Global Dairi Alami**, a business unit described on **Savoria Group's official website**. 製品は牛乳や超高温瞬間殺菌乳（UHT乳）で、乳糖分解タイプ、子ども・十代向け商品もある。The customer is not just an athlete. It is a household buying everyday dairy products.

The company operates in Subang, West Java. In October 2023, it signed a cooperation agreement with LPDB-KUMKM, a government-linked financing agency, concerning local dairy cooperatives and feed-crop supply. 牛乳を生産し、流通させ、家庭へ届くまでには複数の接点がある。Sports activity could strengthen relationships with children and families, but **no verified sales uplift or marketing ROI is publicly available** in the reviewed sources.

[Savoria company structure](https://savoria.co.id/about-us)／[MilkLife product portfolio](https://www.milklife.id/)／[LPDB-KUMKM agreement](https://www.lpdb.go.id/bersama-milklife-lpdb-kumkm-kembangkan-ekosistem-sapi-perah-berbasis-koperasi/)

Savoria also lists HYDROPLUS isotonic drinks, other food brands and distribution operations. この後に出てくるHYDROPLUS Soccer Leagueが、単なる名前の偶然ではないことを理解する手がかりになる。However, the internal marketing budgets and formal cross-brand contracts remain unknown.

## 3. From one city to fifteen: 大会はスポーツ参加の基盤になりつつある

The **MilkLife Soccer Challenge (MLSC)** began in 2023 in Kudus, Central Java. 運営の中心はMilkLifeとダルム財団のスポーツ支援部門。Importantly, the programme involves both ordinary primary schools (**SD**) and Islamic primary schools (**MI, Madrasah Ibtidaiyah**). Local government reporting documented the participation of these religious schools as early as 2023.

The footprint grew from **8 cities in 2024** to **10 in the first 2025–26 series**, then **12 during 2026**. The announced 2026–27 calendar covers **15 cities**, including newly added North Jakarta, Garut and Jayapura. **Fifteen is a planned coverage figure**, not fifteen completed events as of this article.

[Kemenag Central Java](https://jateng.kemenag.go.id/kemenag-kudus-dukung-sepak-bola-putri-melalui-milklife-soccer-challenge/)／[RRI 2025 announcement](https://rri.co.id/semarang/sepak-bola/1739444/milklife-soccer-challenge-2025-2026-digelar-di-10-kota)／[official 2026–27 opener](https://milklifesoccer.com/news/522/milklife-soccer-challenge-2026-2027-resmi-bergulir-tangerang-dan-semarang-jadi-kota-pembuka)

The first September 2026 events drew **1,037 girls from 57 schools in Tangerang** and **1,091 from 63 schools in Semarang**. That is **2,128 participant entries across two cities**. U10 and U12 use **seven-a-side football without offside**. Early rounds are played in two ten-minute halves; semifinal and final games last two fifteen-minute halves. Smaller children can enter through **Festival SenengSoccer**, aimed at roughly ages six to eight.

「最初から本格的な選手だけを集める」のではなく、**lowering the barrier to first participation** is part of the programme design. 地域の実施体制が続けば、競技を始める機会そのものが増えうる。ただし、増加分のうち大会に起因する割合は未測定だ。

## 4. The numbers are impressive. But entries are not unique players

In March 2026, state news agency ANTARA published the organizer's participation series:

- **5,163** participant entries in 2024 Series 1.
- **10,051** in 2024 Series 2.
- **17,492** in 2025–26 Series 1.
- **32,706** in the sum of those three series.

The first-series figure increased by about **3.39 times** between 2024 and 2025–26. 2026年3月にはPSSI Awardsで「Grassroots Development Program of the Year」を受賞した。[ANTARA source](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5501242/milklife-soccer-dedikasikan-penghargaan-untuk-keluarga-hartono)

But **32,706 is not a verified count of distinct girls**. 同じ選手が複数大会に出場している可能性がある。There is also an unresolved discrepancy: one [Liputan6 report](https://www.liputan6.com/bola/read/6218958/milklife-soccer-challenge-sukses-ciptakan-fondasi-pembinaan-usia-dini) gives **17,365**, not 17,492, for the 2025–26 first series. The 127-person difference has not been independently reconciled.

参加数が増えたことと、女子の競技人口が増えたことは同義ではない。We would need first-time participation, unique registrations and twelve-month retention before making that stronger claim.

## 5. The hidden story is coaching continuity, not the one-off star clinic

A useful way to read the programme is as a **participation pathway**:

**Try it:** Festival SenengSoccer at younger ages.  
**Play:** regular U10 and U12 competitions through schools.  
**Improve:** Skill Challenges in passing, shooting and ball control.  
**Develop:** Extra Training and regional All-Stars.  
**Support the adults:** teacher and coach education.

最後の部分が見落とされがちだ。From September 29 to October 5, 2025, the Djarum Foundation, MilkLife and PSSI organized a national **D-licence coaching course for 30 physical education teachers and coaches** in Kudus.

[RRI coaching-course report](https://rri.co.id/semarang/sepak-bola/1881196/privacy.html)／[local coverage with explanation](https://ipol.id/2025/10/djarum-foundation-dan-pssi-selenggarakan-pelatihan-dan-sertifikasi-lisensi-d-nasional/)／[ANTARA on the football pathway](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5261089/milklife-soccer-challenge-permudah-pssi-siapkan-bibit-sepak-bola-putri)

A tournament lasts days; a teacher may shape practice for years. **What happens between tournaments?** そこに競技育成の本質がある。ただし、この30人の最終的な資格取得者数、指導改善、学校への波及効果は別途検証しなければならない。

## 6. Not only football: MilkLife also appears in badminton and archery

In Solo on October 1, 2026, **480 children from 16 primary schools** attended the MilkLife Festival SenengMinton. The participants were first- to third-graders, and activities were designed to introduce badminton through play. 女子だけの大会ではなく、男女の低年齢層への体験機会を広げる。[ANTARA local report](https://jateng.antaranews.com/berita/647143/ratusan-siswa-sd-di-solo-ikuti-milklife-festival-senengminton-2026)

Archery is another piece of the puzzle. In May 2025, a Kudus youth event drew **428 entrants**. Later that year, the national club archery championship organized with PERPANI drew approximately **1,360 participants from 116 clubs**, ranging from youth to adult categories. [Media Indonesia youth archery report](https://mediaindonesia.com/olahraga/770727/milklife-archery-challenge-2025-cari-bibit-pemanah-muda-)／[PERPANI official event announcement](https://www.indonesiaarchery.org/2025/10/16/pendaftaran-kejurnas-antar-club-milklife-archery-challenge-2025/)／[kumparan on-site report](https://kumparan.com/kumparansport/1-360-peserta-dari-sumbar-papua-berjibaku-di-milklife-archery-challenge-2025-26OzTGS83CZ)

The brand's **sports touchpoints are broader than girls' football**: structured football competitions, introductory badminton activities and archery tournaments. 乳業会社の関与を一競技の協賛と考えると、このportfolioが見えなくなる。

## 7. Pokémon adds a new reason to join an existing network

The Pokémon Company's Indonesian announcement also mentioned **Wayang**, a traditional performance art, a themed run, digital games and other fan events. スポーツ単独ではなく、インドネシアの文化・家庭・娯楽に接点を増やす構想の一部だった。[Official announcement](https://asia-press.portal-pokemon.com/press-release/Proyek-Baru-di-Indonesia-Untuk-Tahun-Fiskal-2026-2027/)

Our interpretation is a simple division of roles. **MilkLife and the Djarum Foundation contribute recurring access and operations; Pokémon contributes an engaging intellectual property; Iwabuchi and Miyama contribute football expertise and visible role models.** これは分析上の整理であり、契約上の責任分担や費用負担を示すものではない。

An important nuance: the September clinic's **74 girls were already enrolled in Extra Training**. ポケモンがゼロから74人を競技へ勧誘したわけではない。The event offered an additional experience to an existing development group. [Media Indonesia](https://mediaindonesia.com/sepak-bola/934697/legenda-jepang-berbagi-ilmu-sepak-bola-di-mlsc)

## 8. The Djarum connection raises questions of capacity and ethics

The Djarum Foundation provides a strong organizational context for this story. スポーツ施設、イベント運営、地域の関係者との接点があり、現地メディアはハルトノ家の長年のスポーツ支援も伝えている。MilkLife belongs to a food and beverage portfolio within the wider group context. Yet these links do not reveal exactly who pays which invoice.

[Savoria official information](https://savoria.co.id/about-us)／[ANTARA on the Hartono family](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5501242/milklife-soccer-dedikasikan-penghargaan-untuk-keluarga-hartono)

There is also a legitimate ethical issue. ダルムはたばこ事業に結び付いた企業名だ。In 2019, Indonesia's Child Protection Commission (KPAI) challenged branding practices in the group's badminton talent-scouting programmes. Djarum defended the programme's contribution to athlete development. Academic research published in 2020 examined the company's sports CSR, branding and tobacco-advertising regulations.

[The Jakarta Post on the 2019 dispute](https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2019/02/18/child-commission-accuses-cigarette-firm-of-exploiting-children)／[Siahaya & Smits, academic paper](https://doi.org/10.1080/17430437.2020.1764537)

**This is not evidence that the current MilkLife girls' football programme is illegal tobacco advertising.** スポーツ支援の価値と企業のbranding interestsは同時に存在しうる。That is why child privacy, consent, nutritional claims, brand visibility and governance deserve direct scrutiny rather than either celebration or blanket condemnation.

## 9. Beyond U12: a new professional league began in October 2026

On October 18, 2026, the **Indonesia Women's League 2026/27** kicked off. The official organizer listed **six clubs and a planned 45-match regular season**. Opening results: Persita beat Garudayaksa 1–0, Dewa United beat Persikad 1–0, and Persija drew 1–1 with FC Bekasi City.

[I.League official release](https://ileague.id/press/detail/indonesia-women---s-league-2026-27-dimulai-18-oktober--enam-klub-siap-bersaing)／[Official results](https://ileague.id/fixtures/index/INDONESIA_WOMENS_LEAGUE_2026-27/1)

This league is **not the same competition or organizer as MLSC**. ただし、子どもがサッカーを続けるにはU12の先にU15、U18、成人の競技環境が必要になる。The Djarum Foundation's June 2026 **Women's Soccer Trilogy** paired U12 MilkLife All-Stars with U15/U18 HYDROPLUS events and a U16 international tournament. ここで年齢ごとの接続を意識した設計が確認できる。[RRI on Women's Soccer Trilogy](https://rri.co.id/semarang/sepak-bola/2509199/womens-soccer-trilogy-2026-sistem-pembinaan-berjenjang)

Another factual connection: Teddy Tjahjono, reported as the MLSC programme director, is also identified in the league's official statement as CEO of PT Liga Indonesia Putri. **A shared executive does not establish shared funding or data systems.** 人的接点と組織上の一体性は分けて考える必要がある。

## 10. Measuring impact: count next year's players, not only today's crowd

Here is our **analytical proposal**, not a description of MilkLife's internal KPIs. A useful **measurement framework** has three layers.

**Participation metrics:** unique first-time players, number of schools, repeat entries, geographic access. 延べ人数と実人数を区別し、参加の入口が広がったかを測る。

**Development metrics:** six- and twelve-month retention, practice frequency, licensed coaches, U12-to-U15 progression, and available clubs. 大会と大会の間に起きる行動こそ重要だ。

**Brand and community metrics:** family awareness, purchase changes, matched-area comparisons, child safety and consent, local spending measured with actual data. 宣伝効果や経済効果を、関係者の発言だけで立証したことにはしない。[ANTARA on economic-impact claims](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5745501/milklife-soccer-challenge-berikan-dampak-ekonomi-kepada-daerah-sekitar)

A **difference-in-differences** study could compare matched cities before and after an MLSC introduction, while following participants and non-participants. ただし自己選択バイアス、他の育成プログラム、地域差を統制しなければ因果推定はできない。Youth data governance is a separate non-negotiable design issue.

## 11. Back to the milk carton: what is the real product of this project?

At first the story was almost absurd: milk, Pikachu and two World Cup winners on a football pitch. 調べてみると、その奥には学校、宗教学校、7人制の試合、コーチ養成、選抜練習、年齢別大会、新しい女子リーグが並んでいた。

**The more interesting output may be repeated opportunities to play, not a one-day promotional crowd.** もちろん、これは公開された活動からの解釈であり、MilkLife社内の戦略を知っているという意味ではない。The unresolved question is what will remain if budgets or brand priorities change.

**Will the same girls still be playing next year? And will their coaches, teammates and competitions remain when a sponsor's logo disappears?**

牛乳のマーケティングを調べるつもりだった。We ended up asking how a society builds lasting opportunities for girls to play sport. 予想以上に大きな話になった。

## Research notes: what is known and what remains unverified

- **Confirmed:** MilkLife dairy is produced by Global Dairi Alami; Djarum Foundation co-organizes the sports programme; Pokémon announced a partnership; the 74-person September 17 coaching clinic actually took place.
- **Organizer plans:** 15 cities for 2026–27 and an ambition to help Indonesia reach the 2035 Women's World Cup; these are not completed outcomes.
- **Data caveat:** 32,706 is the sum of three participation reports, not verified unique participants. A 17,365 vs 17,492 discrepancy remains unresolved.
- **Unverified:** sponsorship budget, project profitability, milk purchase conversion, long-term athlete retention, contract terms and data-sharing arrangements.
- **Ethical distinction:** past criticism of Djarum-related youth sports advertising is relevant context, not proof of misconduct by the present MilkLife programme.
