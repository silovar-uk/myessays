# Immersiv.ioはなぜ10年間スポーツXRを続けられたのか
## From FIFA AR to BBC 3D — the business behind the experience

> Research date: November 10, 2026. **Evidence / Interpretation / Hypothesis** を区別する。これは日本語原本のEnglish Mixで、数字と根拠は共通。

### Abstract

2022年カタールW杯で、ファンはスマホをピッチに向けると選手のデータを見られた。It looked like the future had suddenly arrived. でも開発元Immersiv.ioは、すでに2016年からXRに取り組んでいた。2026年にはBBCとDAZN向けに、競技データから試合を3Dで再現する技術を提供した。

The surprising part is the financial story. 同社の2022年売上高は約190万ユーロ。Net profit moved from roughly €510,000 in 2022 to losses in 2023 and 2024, before turning positive again in 2025. **Famous customers do not automatically mean a smooth growth curve.**

![2025 Club World Cup tabletop 3D experience, provided by Immersiv.io](https://www.immersiv.io/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/DAZN-Meta-Club-World-Cup-Tabletop.png)

*Source: [Immersiv.io / DAZN 2025 case](https://www.immersiv.io/portfolio/dazn-club-world-cup-2025/).*

## 守1｜Not a consumer app company, but a B2B technology partner

【Evidence】登記上の会社名はIMMERSIV、SIRENは**821 284 882**。Founded on June 24, 2016, in Paris. The legal president is Stéphane Guérin, while Emmanuelle Roger is registered as Directeur général. 対外的にはRogerがCEO、GuérinがCTOと紹介される。Legal title and operating title are different things. [French business register](https://annuaire-entreprises.data.gouv.fr/entreprise/821284882) / [About us](https://www.immersiv.io/aboutus/)

In February 2025, Sports Business Journal reported a team of **20 employees**. 現在の正確な実人数は、情報源の対象時点が異なるため確定しない。[SBJ interview](https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2025/02/24/10-most-innovative-sports-tech-companies-immersivio-takes-extended-reality-to-the-field-of-play/)

【Interpretation】Users open FIFA+, BBC Sport, or DAZN, not an app branded Immersiv.io. The company works behind the scenes. その立ち位置は **white-label B2B**。顧客のブランドで技術が提供される、という仕組みだ。

## 守2｜They knew sports broadcasting before they founded the company

【Evidence】2017年のフランス語インタビューでRogerは、創業前からGuérinとともに世界的なスポーツイベントや大手テレビ局向けのmobile appsを作っていたと話している。Guérin handled technology; Roger focused on product direction. [Augmented-Reality.fr, August 2017](https://www.augmented-reality.fr/2017/08/reinvente-experience-supporter/)

2017年にはFrance TélévisionsのRoland-Garros実証でHoloLensを使い、テニスの情報とライブ映像を組み合わせた。In 2018, the company also showcased an experience connected to FFF and TF1. [Roland-Garros case](https://www.immersiv.io/portfolio/roland-garros-france-tv-ar/) / [FFF-TF1 case](https://www.immersiv.io/portfolio/fff-tf1-ar/)

【Interpretation】The founders were not starting from a blank sheet. 彼らはスポーツ権利者や放送局向けの開発工程を知り、そこにARという新しいinterfaceを持ち込んだ。技術実証は同時に、顧客との関係を築く機会でもあった。

## 守3｜An international business before the FIFA breakthrough

【Evidence】Roland Bergerの2020年French SporTech調査は、Immersiv.ioをfan segment / multi-sport / B2B / white-labelに分類。The study listed revenue above €1m, a team of 10–25, and **€0.0m in reported cumulative funding at that point**. This is a 2020 snapshot, not proof of no funding since then. [Roland Berger report, p.32](https://www.rolandberger.com/publications/publication_pdf/RB_PUB_20_018_COP_French_SporTech.pdf)

2020年の会社売上は約**€1.41m**、そのうち輸出売上は約**€1.09m, or 77%**と登録資料の集約サイトが示す。[Origami Entreprises](https://origami-entreprises.fr/entreprise/immersiv-821284882/)。The Bayern Munich / Deutsche Telekom stadium AR demonstration also took place in 2020. [Case study](https://www.immersiv.io/portfolio/bayern-munich-deutsche-telekom-ar-stadium/)

【Interpretation】The 2022 World Cup was a global spotlight, not the birth of international sales. すでに国外のB2B clientsから収入を得ていたことが分かる。ただし顧客別売上は非開示。

## 守4｜FIFA 2022 scaled an existing capability

![FIFA+ Stadium Experience](https://www.immersiv.io/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Illus3-FifaPlus.webp)

*Source: [FIFA+ case study](https://www.immersiv.io/portfolio/_fifa-world-cup/).*

【Evidence】FIFA+ Stadium Experience let fans point a phone at the pitch to see players' statistics and real-time insights. The company reports **26 million TikTok post views**, **2 million reactions**, and **12% FIFA+ traffic growth**. ただしSNSの再生数はAR機能の利用者数と同じではなく、traffic increaseもAR単独の効果だとは立証されていない。[FIFA case](https://www.immersiv.io/portfolio/_fifa-world-cup/)

同じ大会で、TSN/RDSには**295,000 unique users**を記録した自宅観戦向けAR/3D体験を提供。MagentaTVではTV音声認識を使って、リビングに置いたmini pitchをライブと同期させた。[TSN case](https://www.immersiv.io/portfolio/_tsn-rds-world-cup/) / [Audio synchronization](https://www.immersiv.io/blog/augmenting-tv-experience/)

【Interpretation】It is misleading to tell a simple story of 'stadium AR failed, so the company pivoted home.' 2022年にはスタジアムと家庭向けが並行していた。同じデータを複数のscreens and contextsに展開する発想の方が正確だ。

## 破1｜From AR overlays to a 3D reconstruction platform

【Evidence】2023年はF1 Canadian Grand Prix向けにinteractive 3D course visualization。2024年はDFLとMLSでApple Vision Pro。2025年にはDAZNとMeta Quest向けにClub World Cupの**63 matches**を3D再現。Company-reported figures were **73,000 users** and **50 minutes average time spent**.[F1](https://www.immersiv.io/portfolio/_tsn-formula/) / [2025 DAZN case](https://www.immersiv.io/portfolio/dazn-club-world-cup-2025/)

![Business evolution map](/myessays/assets/immersivio-business-evolution-2026.svg)

2026年、ARISEはlive **skeletal tracking data**（選手の身体各部位の座標）を使い、3D avatarsを動かす。The tracking itself is performed by separate data providers. Immersiv.io handles reconstruction, correction, and synchronization with broadcast audio. [TVBEurope interview](https://www.tvbeurope.com/media-consumption/bringing-the-fifa-world-cup-to-life-in-immersive-3d)

BBC deployed live 3D and replays; DAZN Japan used 3D views in its coverage and replay functions. **DAZN’s help center says its Immersive View is for highlights and full-match replays, not live viewing.** 顧客ごとの機能を混同してはいけない。[BBC](https://bbc.com.im/sport/football/articles/c20y70lg0j3o) / [DAZN Help](https://www.dazn.com/en-JP/help/articles/36661068089629-immersive-view-a-new-way-to-watch-replays)

A September 2026 Paris visit reported **around 3 million BBC unique users**, citing the company. It also reported around **1–2 seconds** of 3D reconstruction delay. Those are company-reported figures, not independently audited metrics. [PRONEWS, November 9, 2026](https://jp.pronews.com/column/202611090908797418.html)

【Interpretation】The product is becoming less about putting labels on reality and more about making sports action **replayable and editable from different viewpoints**. Here, data integration matters as much as the interface.

## 破2｜The financials interrupt the success story

![IMMERSIV reported net profit](/myessays/assets/immersivio-net-profit-2022-2025.svg)

【Evidence】Net profit disclosed in corporate filings: **2022 +€510k, 2023 −€138k, 2024 −€277k, 2025 +€424k**. Cash at end-2025 is shown as about €978k on Pappers. Revenue is not consistently disclosed for recent years. Do not turn a missing field displayed as zero into zero revenue. [Pappers](https://www.pappers.fr/entreprise/immersiv-821284882) / [OpenBase](https://openbase.fr/entreprise/immersiv-821284882)

2020 sales were around €1.41m and 2022 around €1.9m in historical datasets. But **there is no reliable public year-by-year revenue series for 2023–25** in the reviewed materials.

【Interpretation】We cannot say the FIFA contract caused the 2022 profit. We also cannot say XR development caused the 2023–24 losses. 数字を使った企業分析で最も大切なのは、数字が教えていないことも明示することだ。

【Hypothesis】A small technical team must manage the tension between bespoke projects and reusable platform investment. The evidence needed is contract renewals, gross margin by project, and development reuse rate. 会社の知名度や顧客ロゴの数だけでは見えない部分である。

## 破3｜The integration layer may matter more than the 3D graphics

【Evidence】France Télévisions explained in 2020 how Hawk-Eye data, SMT stats, and broadcast video were integrated in an Immersiv.io Unity app. In 2026, skeletal data must be cleaned up and synchronized with broadcast audio. [France Télévisions](https://www.francetelevisions.fr/groupe/le-lab/en/r-d/roland-garros-2020-augmented-tennis-for-future-tv-37404) / [TVBEurope](https://www.tvbeurope.com/media-consumption/bringing-the-fifa-world-cup-to-life-in-immersive-3d)

【Interpretation】Anyone can demonstrate a beautiful 3D scene. Harder work may be connecting different sports, data providers, rights holders, devices and timecodes reliably. ここに積み上がるknow-howが、同社の強みかもしれない。ただし優位性の確定には競合比較が必要。

## 離1｜The company sells a new way of seeing sport without owning the broadcast

Why can a team of around 20 people work for global platforms? Because it need not own the event, the broadcasting rights, or a huge consumer audience. Immersiv.io provides an intermediate layer: **turning authorized tracking data into an experience that broadcasters can distribute**.

しかしtracking dataも自由に使えるわけではない。In a 2026 on-site interview, the company explained that rights arrangements differ by competition and contract. [PRONEWS](https://jp.pronews.com/column/202611090908797418.html)

【Hypothesis】Future growth depends not just on headset sales but on **data licensing and repeat adoption by broadcasters**. Demo budgets are different from recurring product budgets.

## 離2｜A technology company as a kind of translator

座標だけのデータは、一般のファンには意味が伝わらない。A good translator changes not only the language but the form in which meaning becomes understandable. Immersiv.io does something similar: from skeletal coordinates to a goalkeeper's point of view, from tracking to tactical insight.

【Interpretation】That changes the peer group. The relevant competitors include sports analytics firms, broadcast graphics systems, game engines, and internal development teams—not just AR studios.

【Proposal】For a technical SME considering new ventures, first look for the **missing translation layer** between a major customer's raw assets and an experience their audience would pay for or use repeatedly. ただし有償の継続利用と技術デモの反響を混同しない。

## 結論｜A durable problem, not a durable headset

2017年はHoloLens、2022年はstadium smartphones、2025年はMeta Quest、2026年はBBC's mobile and web experience。Devices changed repeatedly. The problem did not: **How can raw sports data become a fan experience?**

Immersiv.io may be better understood not as a company that perfectly predicted one AR device, but as a team that kept solving the same difficult translation problem while changing delivery channels. Its commercial sustainability still needs more evidence. That distinction is precisely what makes the business worth studying.
