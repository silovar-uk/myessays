# ロージークロニクル史――なぜ最も新しいグループが「年代記」を名乗ったのか
## From Kenshusei Unit '23 to nine members, major debut, First Page!, BOOKMARK and Blueprint: reading Rosy Chronicle as **future history promised in advance**

### 要旨

Rosy Chronicle is not yet a long-surviving group.

The nine-member group was officially formed on June 16, 2024 and made its major debut on March 19, 2025. As of August 2026, the same nine members remain active. Compared with Morning Musume, ANGERME, Juice=Juice, Tsubaki Factory or even OCHA NORMA, its institutional history is still very short.

So it would be premature to ask, with the same confidence used for older groups, **“what survived?”**

But that shortness is exactly what makes the name interesting.

The official explanation combines **Rosy** — rosy, hopeful, full of happiness — with **Chronicle**, a historical record of events arranged through time. The stated wish was that the members would create a rosy history worth being told for years.

つまり、長い歴史を持ったから「Chronicle」と呼ばれたのではない。

**Before the history existed, the group was already named after the act of making history.**

That reversal is the key to Rosy Chronicle.

This essay therefore uses **chronicle as commitment**: a chronicle not as accumulated achievement, but as a promise made before the pages are filled.

---

## 1. 2023――The story begins before the official group exists

The prehistory of Rosy Chronicle runs through Hello Pro Kenshusei Unit '23.

On May 23, 2023, Hello! Project announced that Hinoha Yoshida and Rena Kamimura would join the trainee unit. The same announcement said that the unit was expected to debut as a new Hello! Project group in 2024.

But it was still not a completed group.

The official wording said the organization would continue preparing for debut while **solidifying the group composition**.

That phrase matters.

**People and shared time came before the final name.**

The members performed through trainee concerts, opening acts and other live settings before the public received a definitive group identity.

In November 2023, Kenshusei Unit '23 even appeared as the opening act for Morning Musume '23 at Yokohama Arena.

まだ正式なグループ名はない。

でも、大舞台での経験はもう始まっている。

That in-between condition — not yet an official group, but already accumulating collective experience — later becomes part of the Rosy Chronicle logic.

---

## 2. 2024年6月16日――Seven plus two becomes one official nine

On June 16, 2024, two Hello Pro Kenshusei members, Hana Shimakawa and Yume Soma, were added to the seven members who had been active as Kenshusei Unit '24.

On the same day, the nine-member group received its official name:

**Rosy Chronicle.**

The official definition was unusually explicit.

- **Rosy**: rosy, hopeful, filled with happiness
- **Chronicle**: a historical record of events organized through time

The wish was for the group to create a rosy history that would continue to be told.

This is not a name that explains the past.

**It functions as an instruction for the future.**

Morning Musume acquired institutional meaning through decades of additions and graduations. Berryz Kobo's shared-time symbolism became richer after years of the same cohort growing together.

Rosy Chronicle, by contrast, was told on day one:

> You are a group whose history should become worth recording.

That is a beautiful name, but also a demanding one.

A chronicle cannot be produced by branding alone. It only becomes meaningful if events accumulate and remain readable later.

---

## 3. Why all nine are treated as the founding lineup

On the current official profiles, all nine members — Honoka Hashida, Hinoha Yoshida, Karin Onoda, Ayana Murakoshi, Hasumi Uemura, Yulia Matsubara, Hana Shimakawa, Rena Kamimura and Yume Soma — have June 16, 2024 listed as their Hello! Project joining date, identified as the Rosy Chronicle formation date.

Of course, their personal histories started earlier.

Some had long Kenshusei careers. Yoshida and Kamimura had already worked in Kenshusei Unit '23. Shimakawa and Soma were added only at the final stage before official formation.

Yet the organizational clock is synchronized.

**Their prehistories remain unequal, but the official chronicle begins together.**

This resembles OCHA NORMA's hybrid origin, but not exactly.

OCHA NORMA integrated long-term trainees and open-audition winners from clearly different recruitment routes.

Rosy Chronicle is more heavily rooted in the Kenshusei system. The organization spent a longer period adjusting the trainee-unit composition and then effectively said:

**this nine-member point is Page One.**

それは、年代記を書くときに「正史をどこから始めるか」を決める編集行為に近い。

History is never only what happened. It is also where we decide the official narrative begins.

---

## 4. 2024年11月――A “Chronicle” before major debut

On November 11, 2024, Rosy Chronicle held its first solo event.

The title was **“Chronicles of ロージークロニクル ～ミライハジマリ～.”**

Again, the language of chronicle appeared before the major debut.

At this event, the group announced that its debut single would be released on March 19, 2025.

The order is revealing.

They did not debut first and then start telling the story.

**The storytelling architecture existed before the commercial starting line.**

Chronicle was already becoming a grammar for describing their activities, not merely a decorative English word in the group name.

---

## 5. 2025年3月19日――A debut that leaves room for multiple futures

Rosy Chronicle made its major debut with the double A-side single:

**「へいらっしゃい！～ニッポンで会いましょう～／ウブとズル」**

The first song was written by Aoi Yamazaki and composed by Sho Hoshibe; the second was written by Shoko Omori and composed by KOUGA.

The two songs point in noticeably different directions.

「へいらっしゃい！」 is bright, outward-facing and theatrical.

「ウブとズル」 handles more complicated romantic emotions.

Rather than establishing a single immutable debut character, the first release leaves several directions open.

That fits the chronicle metaphor.

**Page One does not need to contain the whole ending.**

A chronicle acquires identity through accumulation, not through one definitive first sentence.

---

## 6. 2nd and 3rd singles――Refusing to close the image too early

On July 23, 2025, the second single arrived:

**「夏のイナズマ／ガオガオガオ」**

「ガオガオガオ」 became the theme song for the TV anime *Kaiju Sekai Seifuku*.

Then on March 18, 2026, the group released its third single:

**「Misery ～愛の天秤～／なんとかなるでしょ」**

“Misery” was written and composed by Sho Hoshibe; “なんとかなるでしょ” by Takui Nakajima.

The regular editions also included tracks titled:

- 「記念日未満」
- 「バラ色アンソロージー」

Not every title needs to be forced into a grand branding theory.

Still, it is striking how often the vocabulary around Rosy Chronicle touches **anniversaries, anthologies, pages, records and memory**.

The name begins to generate a semantic field around itself.

---

## 7. “First Page!”――Turning the first tour into a readable chapter

In November 2025, Rosy Chronicle launched its first live tour:

**「First Page!」**

It is almost too perfect for the name.

Not simply “our first tour,” but **the first page**.

The live itself becomes something imagined as readable later.

Footage from the November 8 Shinjuku ReNY performance was then included with the special edition of the 2026 third single.

That creates a simple but important chain:

**event → record → archive**

一度きりだったはずの現場が、次の作品の中へ埋め込まれる。

This is how a chronicle becomes more than metaphor: moments are repeatedly turned into retrievable objects.

---

## 8. 2026――BOOKMARK, Blueprint and “Hello Pro Chronicle”

By 2026, the naming language becomes even more explicit.

The September live tour is called:

**BOOKMARK**

The autumn tour beginning in October is:

**Blueprint**

And in August, the group also holds:

**「ロージークロニクル 夏イベント 2026～ハロプロクロニクル～」**

A bookmark remembers where you are.

A blueprint describes what you intend to build.

A chronicle records what has accumulated.

These terms point both backward and forward.

**Archive language and design language coexist.**

It would be too strong to claim that every title is centrally engineered as one master branding plan. But the pattern is still useful: Rosy Chronicle increasingly describes itself with words about recording time and designing what comes next.

---

## 9. 2026年現在――Do not force a survival story too early

As of August 2026, the group still has the same nine members.

Honoka Hashida is the leader, alongside Hinoha Yoshida, Karin Onoda, Ayana Murakoshi, Hasumi Uemura, Yulia Matsubara, Hana Shimakawa, Rena Kamimura and Yume Soma.

They have not yet gone through the kind of structural rupture that defines many older Hello! Project histories.

No large-scale graduation cycle.

No major reorganization.

No rename.

No crisis-driven reconstruction.

So this essay should not pretend that Rosy Chronicle already has a proven **survival model**.

The previous fifteen histories could examine institutions, repertoire, community, shared time, capability pipelines, reconstruction and other things that demonstrably remained after change.

Rosy Chronicle is different.

We can observe not what has survived, but **what the brand has promised to keep making**.

So instead of a mature survival model, the current formation hypothesis is:

### `chronicle_as_commitment`

**A brand that calls itself a chronicle before it has a long history, thereby committing itself to accumulating, recording and making future time narratable.**

---

## 10. Why this group belongs at the end of the individual-history series

Across Hello! Project history, different things have remained.

Morning Musume preserved a system.

Taiyo to Ciscomoon left repertoire.

Country Musume / Country Girls repeatedly redefined a brand.

Melon Kinenbi preserved community.

Mini-Moni left a portable format.

Aya Matsuura reinterpreted one powerful persona across time.

Berryz Kobo turned shared time into content.

℃-ute left a performance standard.

Buono! turned a side project into an autonomous musical identity.

ANGERME inherited the capacity to renew itself.

Juice=Juice built a capability pipeline.

Kobushi Factory constructed a second identity after rupture.

Tsubaki Factory layered cohorts to reduce discontinuity.

BEYOOOOONDS made recombination itself into identity.

OCHA NORMA unified different origins into one founding period.

And Rosy Chronicle?

**It does not yet know what will survive.**

Instead, the cover of the notebook already says *Chronicle*.

That makes it a fitting final chapter for a series about almost thirty years of Hello! Project.

The newest group begins with awareness that today's ordinary activity may become tomorrow's archive.

過去を受け継ぐだけではない。

**They begin by knowing that they themselves may become history.**

---

## 結論――History can be a decision before it becomes an achievement

Rosy Chronicle has not yet proven longevity.

It has not yet demonstrated a complete succession system or a recovery model.

That is why the useful question today is not, “What is their final form?”

The useful question is what kind of relationship with time the name asks them to build.

> **We are going to make history worth reading back.**

Open the First Page.

Place a BOOKMARK.

Draw the Blueprint.

Continue the Chronicle.

The newest group in a nearly thirty-year-old cultural system was given a name about historical memory before its own history existed.

That is both respect for the past and pressure from the future.

**History does not begin only after something survives for decades.**

**It begins when people decide that the present is worth making readable later.**
