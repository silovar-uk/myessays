# China Toy Expo 2026――IP、Collectibles、Experience、Commerceの接続

## 要旨：From a toy fair to the infrastructure of fandom

会場で目立ったのは food-inspired characters、squishy toys、limited-edition miniature cars、trading-card vending machines、photo booths、custom-made 3D figures、live commerce。そして52TOYSのcharacter worldsだった。

A simple hypothesis emerges: **Toy companies increasingly compete on more than manufacturing. They compete on turning affection into products, experiences, collections, and repeat purchases.** ただし、現地で目立った展示が中国市場全体の趨勢を代表するとは限らない。

本稿では、【Field observation】現地メモ、【Verified fact】公式資料等で確認できた情報、【Hypothesis】推論、【Unverified】未確認事項を分ける。Names transcribed from photographs may be inaccurate, and a displayed IP is not proof of a specific licensing agreement.

---

## 1. 守｜What kind of fair was this?

### Four exhibitions, one industry ecosystem

2026年10月21〜23日、Shanghai New International Expo Centreで四展が同時開催された。CTE = toys & trendy collectibles、CLE = licensing、CKE = baby products、CPE = preschool education。これはconsumer eventだけではなく、rights owners、licensees、manufacturers、retailersが取引相手を見つける**B2B marketplace**でもある。

【Verified fact】The Shanghai government reported an exhibition area of **230,000 m²**, 2,667 exhibitors, 5,504 brands and more than 2,700 licensed IPs. Early promotional material used rounded figures such as 2,700 exhibitors and 5,600 brands. A projected professional visitor number of 110,000+ should not be treated as a verified final attendance count.

[Shanghai government: opening report](https://www.shanghai.gov.cn/nw15343/20261022/05b56db4475c430b979b1ef5d5ed2279.html)

東京ビッグサイトの屋内16ホール総展示面積は115,420㎡。Thus the area ratio is about **1.99×**. This is a floor-area comparison, not a comparison of actual attendance density or exhibition productivity.

[Tokyo Big Sight facility data](https://www.bigsight.jp/visitor/company/business/management.html)

### Learn the vocabulary of licensing

**Licensor** はIP権利を許諾する側。**Licensee** は商品化などの利用許諾を受ける側。**Licensing agent** は両者をつなぐ代理事業者。**Minimum Guarantee (MG)** は契約で決めた最低保証額。**Royalty** は売上等に応じた利用対価。**Approval workflow** はデザインや品質の監修プロセスだ。

Crucial distinction: **2,700 IPs do not mean 2,700 licensees or 2,700 deals.** One character may be licensed to several manufacturers, and one licensee may work with multiple characters.

### Traditional toys vs trendy collectibles

中国語の「潮玩」はtrendy / designer / collectible toysに近い。伝統的な玩具と、大人も収集するdesigner toysでは需要が異なる。

The 2026 industry white paper reported 2025 China retail sales of **RMB 103.53 billion** for toys excluding trendy toys (+5.8%) and **RMB 67.69 billion** for trendy and collectible toys (+45.4%). In its survey, 56.8% of respondents cited IP as the most important factor when selecting trendy toys. 数字は調査範囲と定義に依存するため、2市場を無条件に合算して一つのターゲット市場と呼ぶべきではない。

[China Toy & Juvenile Products Association white paper](https://www.toy-cta.org/detail?id=37582)

## 2. 守｜The field notes, regrouped into ten observations

1. **Original character worlds**：BALALA（巴啦啦小魔仙）、Pleasant Goat and Big Big Wolf、GLO-UP GirlsやKreative Idolsなど。Idol, magical-girl and everyday character formats coexist. K-POP DEMON HUNTERSという展示名を中国発IPと断定しない。
2. **Food as character design**：BREAD TIME、Pleasant Goat Bakery、MOKAU、Bao Clubなど。Food shapes are instantly recognizable and may translate well into plush toys or charms. ただし本当に増えているかは年次展示リストとの比較が必要。
3. **Local and international IPs side by side**：POP MART、bilibili、LINE FRIENDS、SEGA、講談社、Sanrio、Chiikawa、mofusandなどがメモに登場。Who owns the IP and who distributes it are separate questions.
4. **52TOYS' independent character displays**：NOOK、LITTLE BUNSや判読不確かなキャラクター表記。The point is not simply shelf space: the displays tried to communicate individual **character identities and fictional worlds**.
5. **Tactile and participatory products**：squishy toys、slime、SQUAREPALS、アイロンビーズのその場での制作。The product creates value through touch or making, even before a story is added.
6. **Collectibles**：limited 1/64 cars and long queues. A queue signals interest or operational friction—but it does not prove high sales volume. 具体的な車種の文字起こしには不確実性がある。
7. **Trading cards and vending machines**：UCARDSHAREなど、実在人物のカードパックも観察。Collection can turn an isolated purchase into a recurring habit. 肖像権・写真利用許諾は重要。
8. **Alifish and global licenses**：阿里魚の展示でサンリオ、ちいかわ等のIPが見られた。Display presence does not establish the territorial scope of each agreement.
9. **Photobooths and customization**：JUST.FOTO、SCLAでの撮影会、ライブコマース、photo-to-figurineの全彩3Dプリント。Here the customer becomes part of the experience or even the product.
10. **Established toy makers and lookalike products**：Takara Tomy's Licca, Punirunes, TOMICA, micropets and T-SPARK; FUNKO products themed around sports and music. たまごっち／シルバニアファミリーに似て見える商品もあったが、**similarity is not proof of infringement**。

## 3. 守｜Alifish as a licensing infrastructure company

Alibaba's Alifish is an IP operation and commercialization platform founded in 2016. The official site describes an **IP2B2C model**: from IP owners, via companies, to consumers. Services include IP assets, product design approval, supply support and integrated marketing. サンリオ・ちいかわ等を含む200超のIPを紹介しているが、各ライセンスの品目や地域条件は個別に異なる。

[Alifish official description](https://ip.alibaba.com/about)

The business insight is **transaction-cost reduction**. 企業が商品化するまでには、権利元探し、交渉、監修、発売、販売分析などの手間がある。A common platform could lower that friction and speed up product launches. But it may also concentrate bargaining power and customer data in the middle layer.

**IP owner → licensee → product → retail channel → consumer → demand feedback.** This loop is as important as the character itself.

## 5. 破｜Five purchase engines

### Engine A: Buy meaning

Character-specific displays and magical-girl IPs invite emotional identification. **Hypothesis:** Stronger attachment may increase repeat purchases. ただし好意度と購買頻度は別に測定する。

### Engine B: Buy a sensation

Squishy toys, slime and bead workshops deliver tactile satisfaction or the pleasure of making. **Hypothesis:** Hands-on trial boosts conversion. 比較実験で参加者当たりの増分粗利を見る必要がある。

### Engine C: Buy the missing piece

Trading cards, limited cars and character series rely partly on collecting. **Hypothesis:** A continuous series can raise purchase frequency. Yet scarcity can also create resale speculation and consumer frustration. 在庫回転、苦情、ユニーク購入者数を追う。

### Engine D: Become part of the product

Photo booths, meet-and-greets and custom 3D figures convert personal memories into merch. **Hypothesis:** Creating and sharing a personal artifact increases engagement and referral traffic. 個人写真・未成年のデータは、同意と保管期限が必須。

### Engine E: Buy at the moment of desire

Livestream shopping, vending machines and integrated e-commerce shorten the path from discovery to payment. **Hypothesis:** Less friction may improve conversion. But **contribution profit**, not gross sales, is what matters.

These engines overlap. A limited collectible with an emotional story, a hands-on booth, and a fast purchase link can activate all five at once.

## 6. 破｜What the second research pass changed

### Sports licensing is no longer peripheral

【Verified fact】The 2026 licensing industry midyear report found **sports-IP licensing collaboration counts up 157.1% year on year** within the tracked dataset. The same report discussed global sports, city-level sports, club IP and racing. This is **growth in counted collaborations, not revenue growth**.

[Official licensing trends report](https://www.toy-cta.org/en/detail?id=37975)｜[Licensing Magazine interpretation](https://www.licensingmagazine.com/2026/10/28/chinas-licensing-market-in-2026/)

The NBA display, miniature race cars and personality cards observed on site are therefore useful clues to investigate—not proof that a particular licensing partnership caused the reported growth.

### Domestic IP is not just imitation

The same report cites **1,146 collaborations for Chinese-origin IPs**, +94.2% year on year; 361 for gaming IPs and 254 for animation/comics. 日本のIPを販売する中国企業という一方向の構図だけでは足りない。A reverse flow—Chinese characters expanding abroad—is plausible, but requires country-level sales and distribution evidence.

### AI toys need a sustainability test

The fair introduced an AI+smart ecosystem hall and hosted AI-related programs. **This shows industry interest; it does not prove product-market fit.** Success requires retention, child safety, content controls, privacy, battery performance and cloud inference costs to work together.

[Shanghai government announcement](https://www.shanghai.gov.cn/nw15343/20261022/05b56db4475c430b979b1ef5d5ed2279.html)｜[Alibaba Cloud event notice](https://toy-cta.org/detail?id=37970)

## 7. 破｜Business applications to sports and entertainment

**A. Matchday collectibles.** Limited player cards, numbered items and event-specific memorabilia can make match attendance collectible. Measure unique buyers, repeat purchase rate, margins, and unsold inventory—not just units sold. 選手肖像や大会関連権利の確認が先。

**B. Stadium photo experiences.** Personal photo frames, photo booths and made-to-order figures could turn the fan into the subject. Start with inexpensive photo products before adding 3D manufacturing. 個人データの処理方針と納期の設計が不可欠。

**C. Museum and regional licensing.** Historic artifacts, local food and cultural stories may become characters or souvenirs. Cultural value needs careful treatment, not simply mass production.

**D. Licensing services for smaller IPs.** Match rights owners with manufacturers and channels, standardize approvals and speed up launches. KPIs should include concluded agreements, time-to-market and repeat partnerships.

**E. Sell an integrated fan journey.** A stadium operator might sell the whole experience—discover, touch, take a photo, share, buy—rather than isolated hardware.

One simplified formula: **incremental contribution = participants × purchase conversion × average order value × gross margin − staffing − equipment − logistics − royalties − returns**. It is useful only when the purchases truly would not have happened without the activation.

## 8. 離｜Associative detours

### Food characters and local tourism

A food-themed character can cross into bakery packaging, plush toys, social media and souvenirs. **What if a local specialty became a character before it became a souvenir?** ただし食品の形だけでは差別化しにくく、独自の物語がいる。

### Photo booths and self-centered memorabilia

The consumer buys not only an object but evidence that **“I was part of this moment.”** A concert, match or trip may be remembered through personalized products. The competing alternative is often a free smartphone photo, so willingness to pay must be tested.

### Trading-card vending machines as media

A card vending machine is a shop, a distribution channel and a reason to visit again. A club could treat match dates as a **release calendar** for collectible cards. しかしランダム性の過度な利用、転売、購買トラブルには注意が必要だ。

### AI companions and service economics

A traditional toy remains mostly the same after purchase. An AI-enabled companion can change through interaction. That transforms a product into something closer to an ongoing service, with retention and support costs. **The harder question is not whether it can talk, but whether it deserves continued trust.**

### Museums and football clubs

Both own meaningful stories, symbols, places and memories. Licensing is not simply putting a logo on a product. It is translating cultural meaning into new experiences **without destroying its original context**.

## 9. Limits and the next research plan

Major warnings: eye-catching booths are not representative samples; licensed IP counts are not deals; growth in collaborations is not growth in revenue; Chinese distributors can represent foreign IP; visually similar goods are not automatically counterfeits; conference topics are not evidence of commercial success.

To validate the hypotheses, collect: yearly exhibitor catalogs and IP lists; SKU-level sales and inventory; purchase cohorts; booth participation versus transaction logs; rights clearance costs; and 30/90-day AI-toy usage metrics. Use year-on-year category coding, controlled on-site experiments, cohort analysis and contribution-margin models.

**Best first test:** combine one limited collectible with a simple photo experience at a sports venue, measure incremental contribution profit against a comparable event, and review fan feedback alongside financial results.

## 10. Conclusion

Shanghai revealed a possible future in which **IP is not merely a picture on merchandise**. It is the shared language connecting stories, manufacturers, interfaces, fans and retail channels. The most valuable capability may be the ability to design this whole connection.

For sports, this means moving beyond “How many merchandise items can we make?” to **“What experiences make fans want to return, collect, participate and share?”**
