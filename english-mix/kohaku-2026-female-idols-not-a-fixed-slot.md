# 紅白の「女性アイドル枠」は何席？
## The chairs keep moving――Juice=Juiceと=LOVEを比べる前に

How many seats are there for girl groups at NHK Kohaku? 「今年は三枠」などと言いたくなる。It sounds like musical chairs: three chairs, seven hopeful groups, one dramatic final round. 紅白というより体育の授業だ。

But there is a problem with the metaphor. そもそもNHKは「女性アイドル固定三枠」という制度を公表していない。And the category itself is slippery: Japanese idol groups, K-pop groups, female vocal-and-dance acts, and special performances are not interchangeable. 椅子の数より、誰を参加者に数えるのかが揺れている。

2026年の数字はさらにややこしい。Juice=Juice has roughly **600 million views across SNS-related videos** according to its label. =LOVE sold **417,068 copies of a single in its first week**. Both numbers look enormous. But they measure different things. One is video reach around a song; the other is sales of a physical product. そのまま人気指数として比べるのは、東京タワーの高さと新幹線の速さを比べるのに似ている。どっちもすごいが、何を決めるのか。

This article separates **verified facts, interpretations, and subjective forecasts**. 情報は2026年10月19日時点。出演の正式決定を意味しない。

## 1. 椅子を数える実験――First define who counts

Before predicting a quota, count actual performers. 便宜的に二つの定義を試す。Narrow: Japanese female groups rooted in domestic idol culture. Broad: female pop and dance groups from Japan and overseas. Neither category is an official NHK selection rule.

In 2023, the announced lineup included 乃木坂46、櫻坂46、NiziU、MISAMO、LE SSERAFIM. NewJeans was added later as a special project. In 2024, 乃木坂46、櫻坂46、ME:I、ILLIT、TWICE、LE SSERAFIM appeared. In 2025, 乃木坂46、FRUITS ZIPPER、CANDY TUNE、ILLIT、aespa、HANA were on the initial list; AKB48 joined later in an anniversary performance. [2023 lineup](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2302371/full/) / [NewJeans special addition](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2308877/full/) / [2024 lineup](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2354842/full/) / [2025 lineup](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2418686/full/) / [AKB48 song listing](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2425968/full/).

| 年 | Narrow: domestic idol examples | Broad: additional female groups |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2023 | 乃木坂46、櫻坂46 | NiziU、MISAMO、LE SSERAFIM、NewJeans（special project） |
| 2024 | 乃木坂46、櫻坂46、ME:I | ILLIT、TWICE、LE SSERAFIM |
| 2025 | 乃木坂46、FRUITS ZIPPER、CANDY TUNE、AKB48（additional project） | ILLIT、aespa、HANA |

This is a **classification exercise, not a complete performer count**. HANA is a female group but should not automatically be treated as a conventional Japanese idol. Likewise, special projects should not be mistaken for ordinary slots.

The result matters. In 2025, FRUITS ZIPPER and CANDY TUNE both debuted on Kohaku. A rigid "only one group from this scene" rule cannot explain it. Competition exists, but **coexistence is possible when different performances serve different programming purposes**. The first question, then, is not "who steals whose seat?" It is "what performance does the show need?"

## 2. A popularity chart is not a casting memo――NHKの選考とは

NHK has historically explained its selection through three considerations: **activity during the year, public support, and the program's concept or production**. Sales, streaming, videos, karaoke, live events, and public surveys can inform the overall decision. [Reporting on NHK's selection criteria](https://encount.press/archives/244327/).

These are three different questions. How widely is a song consumed? Who do viewers want to watch on New Year's Eve? And what kind of television moment can the artist create? 同じ数字に還元できるとは限らない。Ten times more views does not mathematically imply ten times the chance of selection. そんな単純な制度なら、番組編成の会議には電卓一台で十分になる。

We cannot observe NHK's current internal survey, invitations, artist decisions, or negotiations. したがってここでできるのは、public evidenceを整理し、**a plausible programming story** を推測するところまでだ。「内定」を装うことはしない。

## 3. Juice=Juice――600 million views, but views of what?

On May 1, 2026, Up-Front Works announced approximately **600 million total views of SNS-related videos** around 「盛れ！ミ・アモーレ」. It also announced the June 24 EP 『MORE! MORE! EP』 including 「クラクラ☆クライマックス」 produced by 広瀬香美. [Record label announcement](https://up-front-works.jp/news/20390/).

The important phrase is **SNS-related videos**. これは「楽曲が配信サービスで6億回聴かれた」という意味ではない。Short clips, user-generated posts, and various video contexts may be involved. The published statement alone does not provide fully comparable platform definitions or a complete duplication audit. Viral reach and sustained song listening are different measurements.

Other evidence helps. The EP sold **51,271 copies** in its first week on Billboard JAPAN's album sales chart, ranking third. Juice=Juice performed at ぴあアリーナMM in May and appeared on NHK's 『Venue101（ベニュー・ワンオーワン）』 on September 5. Its official fall schedule includes 日本武道館 on November 6 and ぴあアリーナMM on November 25. [Billboard album sales](https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/162971/2) / [NHK MUSIC announcement](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bOPIl99wrDk) / [Official concert schedule](https://helloproject.com/event/ac7ec0bd437f3af1f9fcdc9c579f31312e9112fe13471d9fbae91c755990d6f2/).

Yet Juice=Juice was not among the top 100 artists on Billboard JAPAN's 2026 midyear Artist 100. [Midyear ranking](https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/162134/2). That's not proof that people don't care. It is evidence that **different indicators tell different stories**.

The question has shifted: not "is 600 million a big number?" but **can a viral discovery turn into a recognizable song and a memorable national-TV performance?** That translation is what makes Juice=Juice fascinating.

## 4. =LOVE――a stronger case across multiple measures

=LOVE offers a different evidence profile. Its April 2026 single 『劇薬中毒』 sold **417,068 copies in the opening week**, topping Billboard JAPAN's singles sales chart. The group ranked **No. 20** on the midyear Artist 100. [Singles sales](https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/160234/2) / [Artist ranking](https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/162134/2).

In the first-half Hot 100, 「とくべチュ、して」「劇薬中毒」「ラブソングに襲われる」 all appeared. Several recognizable songs create more than a one-hit story. [Billboard midyear coverage](https://www.billboard-japan.com/special/detail/5309).

It would be tempting to compare **417,068 / 51,271** and declare that one group is eight times as popular. But one item is a single and the other is an EP, a shorter album. Pricing, bundling, purchase motivations, and recording categories differ. The arithmetic may be correct; the inference is not.

Instead compare like with like: chart position over the same months, duration on composite song charts, streaming and video components, and NHK exposure. その見方では=LOVEの総合的な公開指標は強い。一方、Juice=Juiceには2026年の「新発見」の物語がある。It's not a contest between a real hit and a fake hit. It is a contest between **different forms of reach and recognition**.

## 5. Incumbents and newcomers are different questions

An incumbent has evidence a newcomer cannot have: prior selection. 乃木坂46 appeared in 2023, 2024, and 2025 and ranked **33rd** on the 2026 midyear Artist 100. Simply replacing that track record with a short-lived viral spike would be a poor model. [2023](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2302371/full/) / [2024](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2354842/full/) / [2025](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2418686/full/) / [Midyear chart](https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/162134/2).

FRUITS ZIPPER and CANDY TUNE also have prior-selection evidence, though only from 2025. They ranked 58th and 52nd respectively in the 2026 midyear artist chart. A 2025 debut is no automatic ticket for 2026. Still, the prior year's television presence changes the question from "why introduce them?" to "why continue or replace them?" [FRUITS ZIPPER's official debut notice](https://fruitszipper.asobisystem.com/news/detail/61442) / [Billboard midyear](https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/162134/2).

HANA, meanwhile, ranked **second overall**. ILLIT ranked 34th and XG 36th. Their potential roles involve more than domestic idol culture. If you classify them all as the same "girl-idol slot", you lose the distinction between a Japanese idol act, a female pop-and-vocal act, and internationally oriented performers. [Midyear chart](https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/162134/2).

So there is no single queue. Instead, several routes lead onto the show: **returning performer, nationwide song, fresh cultural phenomenon, strong live act, international relevance, or special-programming fit**. Predicting Kohaku requires modeling those routes, not just ordering names.

## 6. Conditional predictions――出場可能性は一点より幅で読む

These are **subjective scenario estimates for a distinct vocal appearance on December 31**, not just the first press announcement. Rounded to 5-percentage-point steps, they are **not calibrated probabilities or statistical confidence intervals**. Do not sum them to infer the number of available slots.

| ILLIT* | 65% | 42–82% | Prior appearance; No. 34 midyear |

| 櫻坂46 | 40% | 20–62% | Appeared in 2024, not 2025 |

| CUTIE STREET | 30% | 12–52% | Needs stronger comparable evidence |
| AKB48 | 30% | 13–52% | 2025 anniversary project differs from a regular slot |

*HANA, ILLIT, and XG are included as broader female-group comparators, not as proof of the size of a narrow Japanese idol quota.*

In an earlier single-artist assessment, I placed Juice=Juice around 65%. After comparing the full field and correcting for incompatible metrics, this article uses **55% as a central judgment**. That does not mean a new negative fact was discovered. It means the methodology changed. **A forecast should document why it moves.**

## 7. The overly serious thought experiment――もし三席しかなかったら？

Let's imagine NHK had only three slots for female groups. This is an invented restriction, not an inside report. Ranking strictly by midyear Artist 100 would favor HANA (No. 2), =LOVE (No. 20), and 乃木坂46 (No. 33). [Billboard JAPAN](https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/162134/2).

Change the criterion, however, and the list changes. Prioritize continuing a recent hit and FRUITS ZIPPER or CANDY TUNE gains ground. Prioritize a fresh 2026 discovery and =LOVE or Juice=Juice looks attractive. Prioritize a combined performance and several acts might coexist—as 2025's programming already shows. [2025 performance coverage](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2428055/).

The model's output is sensitive to the assumed number and purpose of "slots". 「誰が椅子を奪うか」の前に、「誰が椅子を三脚だと決めたか」を確認せねばならない。What looked like a fierce competition may partly be a classification error.

## 8. Research, re-research, and the missing test

The first research pass gathered previous appearances, sales, streaming-related claims, and live activity. That immediately exposed two problems: **600 million SNS video views cannot be added to physical sales**, and the category "female idol" changes depending on whether HANA and international groups are counted.

A second pass cross-checked 2023–2025 lineups, 2026 midyear rankings, official tour schedules, and NHK appearance evidence. This removed the unsupported fixed-quota assumption and separated newcomers from returning artists.

What remains undone is a proper **backtest**. Reconstruct what an analyst could have known by October in each of the previous years, predict the final lineup, and evaluate the result without peeking at December outcomes. Then examine **calibration**: did artists assigned a 70% chance actually appear about seven times out of ten across comparable cases? Use a **Brier score** to measure the error between predicted probabilities and outcomes.

The next data collection should therefore include: historical October snapshots of sales, streaming, videos and karaoke; comparable week-by-week song-chart placements; NHK performance history; year-end schedules; and first-announcement versus later-addition outcomes. Compare a simple previous-year baseline, a chart-only baseline, and a programming-aware model. **More complexity is useful only if it beats a simpler forecast.**

Neither the backtest nor the probability calibration has been completed for these published percentages. NHK's unpublished polling and negotiations are also not available. These are limits of the analysis, not facts to fill in with confidence.

## 9. So, will Juice=Juice perform at Kohaku?

My current answer is **possible, competitive, but not confirmed**: a central scenario of **55%**, with a broad 30–75% range. Sustained song-chart improvement or further evidence of nationwide song recognition would move that estimate up. A lack of comparable traction through year-end would move it down.

=LOVE looks stronger on published composite metrics. 乃木坂46 brings a different kind of evidence: repeated selection. FRUITS ZIPPER and CANDY TUNE have continuation cases. None of this establishes that only one of them may appear.

So, how many chairs are there? We began by trying to count them. Now the better question is **why each chair would be placed on the stage**. Kohaku is not a televised spreadsheet reading. It is a show that tries to turn a crowded year of music into a few hours of shared memory. The most useful forecast predicts that act of editing.
