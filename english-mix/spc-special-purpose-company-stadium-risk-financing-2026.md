# Why create a company just to create another company?
## SPCという「目的しかない会社」――国立競技場とサッカービジネスから考える

*Research date: October 23, 2026. The correct abbreviation here is **SPC**, not SCP. Facts and hypothetical figures are kept separate.*

Every company has a purpose, right? なのに金融の世界には、わざわざ**特別目的会社（Special Purpose Company / SPC）**という名前がある。A company whose special feature is… having a purpose? That sounds suspiciously ordinary.

But the question gets stranger. Four established organizations can create one additional company just to run a single project. The newly formed company signs the contracts, receives the revenue, and becomes responsible for the deal. なぜ既存の会社で直接やらないのか。To understand that, follow the money and responsibility, not the company name.

## 1. SPC, SPV, TMK――似ている略語を区別する

An **SPC (Special Purpose Company)** is a company set up for a specific purpose or project. 例えば一つの不動産を保有する、特定の事業を運営する、クラブの株式を買収する。It describes the *role* of the company, not a single uniform legal form. [Cabinet Office glossary](https://www8.cao.go.jp/pfi/pfi_jouhou/tebiki/yougosyuu/yougo_04.html)

An **SPV (Special Purpose Vehicle)** is a broader term: a dedicated *vehicle* that may be a company, a trust, or another arrangement. そのうち会社という形をとるのがSPC、と覚えると分かりやすい。ただし業界によってはSPCとSPVをほぼ同じ意味で使う。[National Tax Agency](https://www.nta.go.jp/about/organization/ntc/kenkyu/ronsou/37/kubota/hajimeni.htm)

**特定目的会社（TMK）** is something narrower and more precise: a Japanese legal entity under the Asset Securitization Act. 「特別」と「特定」は別物。A TMK is one legal route for asset securitization; not every SPC is a TMK. [Japanese law](https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/410AC0000000105)

The useful question is therefore not “What is this company called?” but **“What rights and risks have been put inside it?”**

## 2. 一社増えると、契約関係はむしろ整理される

Suppose four firms want to operate a stadium. Each could sign separate agreements. でも誰がチケット・施設利用収入を受け取り、誰が警備費を払い、誰が修繕を判断するのか。It quickly becomes complicated.

A dedicated SPC becomes the **single contracting party**. 出資者は株式を持ち、SPCは運営契約を結び、必要な業務を専門会社へ委託する。This is called **ring-fencing**: placing an activity within a defined legal and financial boundary.

One more company on the organization chart can mean **less confusion in the allocation of responsibility**. ここがSPCの面白さだ。

## 3. 国立競技場は本当にSPCが運営している

The operator, **Japan National Stadium Entertainment**, was established on September 3, 2024, and began operating the stadium on April 1, 2025. 会社自身が「特別目的会社」と明記している。[Official operator announcement](https://jns-e.com/news/20250401-421/)

Published shareholdings are:

- NTT Docomo: **58%**
- Maeda Corporation: **34%**
- SMFL MIRAI Partners: **5%**
- Japan Professional Football League (J.League): **3%**

[Infroneer Holdings investor presentation, slide 31](https://www2.jpx.co.jp/disc/50760/140120241108516928.pdf)

These partners bring different capabilities: communications, infrastructure, finance, and football connections. ただしownership percentages alone do not reveal all decision-making rights, guarantees, or operational duties. Those depend on contracts.

Another trap: the SPC did **not simply buy the stadium building**. It operates under a **concession (公共施設等運営権)**, a right to operate a public facility under agreed conditions. The bid announced a proposed concession payment of **¥52.8 billion (528億円)**. That figure is not annual revenue or a purchase price for the building. [Japan Sport Council](https://www.jpnsport.go.jp/corp/chotatu/tabid/2948/Default.aspx)

The original published period ended in March 2055, but an official 2025 announcement extended the end date to **March 31, 2056**. This small detail illustrates why a term sheet, press release, and final updated contract must not be treated as identical documents.

## 4. Bankruptcy remoteness is not a magic shield

A popular phrase is **bankruptcy remoteness（倒産隔離）**. It means designing the project so that a sponsor's financial trouble does not automatically disrupt the specific project. 事業の資産や債務を分け、独立した契約関係を作る発想だ。[Cabinet Office explanation](https://www8.cao.go.jp/pfi/pfi_jouhou/tebiki/jitsumu/jitsumu02.html)

But **creating an SPC does not automatically erase the sponsor's risk**. 親会社が保証していれば保証責任を負う。The sponsors can lose their equity; lenders may have security; accounting rules may require consolidation depending on control and applicable standards.

So the test is practical: **Who guarantees the debt? What is pledged? Who must inject more capital if the project fails?** “There's an SPC” is not an answer to any of those questions.

## 5. 架空のサッカークラブ買収で試してみる

Imagine a hypothetical **¥10 billion (100億円)** football club acquisition. Three investors establish an acquisition SPC and contribute **¥3 billion in equity (30億円)**. The SPC borrows another **¥7 billion (70億円)** and purchases the club's shares.

This is only an illustration, not the finances of an actual club. The club's ticket revenue is **not automatically available** to repay the holding company's debt. The cash must travel through lawful distributions or other permitted arrangements, subject to the club's obligations, financing covenants, and applicable rules.

The **waterfall of payments** matters. 仮にある年の元利返済前に使える現金が10億円、利息3億円、元金返済5億円なら、差引き2億円。ただし税金、積立金、配当制限を無視して、その2億円を投資家の利益と呼ぶことはできない。

**DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio / 元利返済余裕率)** = cash available for debt service ÷ interest and principal due. In this scenario, 10 ÷ (3 + 5) = **1.25 times**. The metric shows debt-service headroom, not a guaranteed return. [Cabinet Office glossary](https://www8.cao.go.jp/pfi/pfi_jouhou/tebiki/yougosyuu/yougo_04.html)

A clever structure does not remove the loss. **It defines the path through which a loss reaches each stakeholder.**

## 6. 資産を保有するSPCと、事業を運営するSPC

There are at least two useful categories. An **asset-holding or securitization SPC** owns assets such as real estate and raises finance against the cash flows. 日本ではTMKや、合同会社と匿名組合を組み合わせた**GK-TK方式**が出てくる。[Industry association](https://www.t2fifa.or.jp/investors/financial-products/fund-equity/)

A **project or operating SPC** undertakes a specific project. PFI (Private Finance Initiative / 民間資金等活用事業) and concessions are typical settings. なお、内閣府はすべてのPFI案件にSPC設立を求めているわけではないと説明している。This is a design choice, often required by an individual procurement, not a universal law. [Cabinet Office practice guide](https://www8.cao.go.jp/pfi/pfi_jouhou/tebiki/jitsumu/jitsumu02.html)

In one case, the dedicated company mainly **holds an asset**. In the other, it coordinates **delivery of a service**. 同じ略語でも、ビジネスの中心はかなり違う。

## 7. エンロン事件は「箱を分けたら透明になる」という幻想を壊した

The US energy company **Enron** used special-purpose entities (SPEs) in structures that raised serious accounting and disclosure issues. SEC records describe transactions designed for off-balance-sheet treatment and improper reporting. [SEC testimony](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/121201tsrkh-testimony-recent-events-relating-enron-corporation) / [SEC complaint](https://www.sec.gov/litigation/complaints/comp17762.htm)

Using a special-purpose entity is not itself misconduct. 問題は、法律上の分離と経済的な実態が食い違ったことにある。A structure can clarify risk, but it can also obscure risk when control, guarantees, and reporting are misrepresented.

The lesson: **Separate legal entities require better disclosure, not less scrutiny.**

## 8. 次の商談で使える7つの問い

When someone says “We will use an SPC,” ask:

- **Purpose** — What specific deal is it built for? 何の案件か。
- **Asset** — Does it own property, shares, or operating rights? 何を持つか。
- **Equity** — Who owns and controls it? 誰が出資・支配するか。
- **Debt** — Who lends, guarantees, and takes security? 誰が融資・保証するか。
- **Cash flow** — Who pays in, and who gets paid first? 現金の流れは。
- **Operations** — Who actually performs the work? 実務の担い手は。
- **Exit** — How does the project end, and who bears a loss? 出口と失敗時の責任は。

最初は「目的しかない会社」が妙に思えた。でも大きな投資や共同事業では、**a single project needs its own boundary, accountability, and financial logic**. そのために新しい会社を置くことがある。

The better final question is not “Why create another company?” It is **“Whose risks are inside, whose risks remain outside, and who can prove the difference?”**
