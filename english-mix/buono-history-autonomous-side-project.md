# Buono!史――借り物の3人は、なぜ独立した「本体」になれたのか
## しゅごキャラ！、ホントのじぶん、Dolce、初恋サイダー、パリ、横浜アリーナから読む派生ユニットの自立

### 要旨

Buono! started as a side project in the most literal sense.

Berryz工房の嗣永桃子と夏焼雅、℃-uteの鈴木愛理。All three already had a primary group.

2007年に結成され、デビュー曲「ホントのじぶん」はテレビアニメ『しゅごキャラ！』の主題歌になった。

So the original architecture was simple: **borrow three performers from existing groups and connect them to an anime project.**

Yet ten years later, Buono! filled Yokohama Arena with about 15,000 people for its final concert.

The question is therefore not why a temporary unit became popular.

**How does a side project become an autonomous identity?**

Buono!’s answer was to build something its parent groups did not provide: a dedicated rock repertoire, a three-voice structure, and a live environment with the band Dolce.

---

## 1｜2007年――目的別キャスティング

Buono!は2007年、嗣永桃子、夏焼雅、鈴木愛理の3人で結成され、10月31日に「ホントのじぶん」でCDデビューした。

The lineup crossed organizational boundaries: two members from Berryz Kobo and one from ℃-ute.

This was not simply a smaller version of one parent group. It treated Hello! Project itself as a **talent pool**.

大人数では役割の一部だった嗣永のキャラクター性、夏焼の音色、鈴木の歌唱力が、3人になると一気に前へ出る。

A side project can work as a magnifying lens: it makes capabilities visible that the parent organization does not always foreground.

---

## 2｜「ホントのじぶん」――tie-in song becomes self-description

「ホントのじぶん」は『しゅごキャラ！』のための主題歌だった。

But the title later became strangely autobiographical.

Buono! was precisely the place where the three members created another version of themselves outside their home groups.

公式クレジットは作詞・岩里祐穂、作曲・木之下慶行、編曲・西川進。

Buono! quickly developed a guitar-forward pop-rock language through external writers and arrangers.

「恋愛♥ライダー」「Kiss!Kiss!Kiss!」「ロッタラ ロッタラ」と続くことで、the tie-in did not trap the unit. **It gave the unit an initial musical constraint from which its own identity could emerge.**

---

## 3｜Cafe Buono!――from songs for an anime to songs for a live act

2008年の1stアルバム『Cafe Buono!』には、「泣き虫少年」「ロックの神様」「Last Forever」などが入った。

This mattered because a unit with only tie-in singles remains dependent on the external project.

Buono! began accumulating songs whose main purpose was different: **to make a Buono! concert work.**

「ロックの神様」はその象徴で、のちのライブではBuono!自身の物語を語る曲になった。

The purpose gradually reversed.

**Anime needed Buono! songs → Buono! needed songs for its own world.**

---

## 4｜2009年――Dolce creates a dedicated live environment

2009年2月、日本青年館で初ワンマン「Buono! ライブ2009 ～ハイブリッド☆パンチ～」を開催。

The backing band Dolce became central to Buono!’s stage identity.

生バンドになると、3人は振付だけでなく、音圧、テンポ、客席との呼吸へ反応しなければならない。

This changed the product.

Buono! was no longer merely “three Hello! Project idols singing rock-flavored songs.” It became a **live rock-idol act with a dedicated performance environment**.

The parent groups did not offer exactly the same experience.

That difference is what autonomy looks like.

---

## 5｜Three members means nowhere to hide

3人という人数は、能力を濃縮する。

A large group can distribute lines and roles. In a trio, every voice is exposed.

鈴木の高音、夏焼の色気のある中低域、嗣永の輪郭の強い声と客席掌握力。

Their differences became structural rather than decorative.

Buono! was not important simply because it gathered strong members.

**The trio format forced each member to carry visible responsibility.**

Small teams concentrate talent, but they also concentrate risk.

---

## 6｜タイアップ終了後も続く――the first test of autonomy

『しゅごキャラ！』はBuono!誕生の入口だったが、2010年代に入ると活動はアニメだけでは説明できなくなる。

2010年にはベスト盤『The Best Buono!』、2011年には映画『ゴメンナサイ』へ3人で主演、2012年にはDolceを従えたツアーでSIAM SHADE「1/3の純情な感情」までカバーした。

A useful test for side-project autonomy is simple:

**Does the project still make sense after the original external purpose disappears?**

Buono! passed that test.

The brand itself began generating its next context.

---

## 7｜「初恋サイダー」――a gateway that needs no parent-group explanation

2012年1月18日、「初恋サイダー／DEEP MIND」を発売。

「初恋サイダー」は、ほとんどイントロを置かず鈴木愛理の歌から始まる。

You do not need to know Berryz Kobo.

You do not need to know ℃-ute.

You do not need to know Shugo Chara.

You do not even need to know why Buono! was formed.

**The song can recruit its own audience.**

That is a critical moment in the independence of a derivative brand.

2012年には日本武道館「第一回ゆび祭り～アイドル臨時総会～」にも出演。その後「初恋サイダー」はハロプロ内外で繰り返しカバーされ、2025年にはSNSやカバーをきっかけに再び注目を集めた。

The repertoire began traveling farther than the organization that created it.

---

## 8｜Paris――Buono! becomes the name on the ticket

2012年2月12日、Buono!はパリで初の海外単独公演を開催。約1000人を動員し、チケットは即日完売だった。

Hello! Project’s broader overseas fandom clearly mattered, so this should not be mythologized as an isolated conquest.

Still, the billing matters.

**The ticket said Buono!, not Berryz Kobo + ℃-ute.**

A borrowed lineup had become a standalone destination.

---

## 9｜The structural weakness: time still belongs to the parent careers

音楽的に独立しても、3人はBuono!専属ではない。

Their calendars belonged partly to Berryz Kobo, ℃-ute, Country Girls, and later individual careers.

This is the structural weakness of a successful side project.

**Identity can become independent before resources become independent.**

2012年以降に活動間隔が広がっても、Buono!の音楽人格自体は崩れなかった。

That resilience is part of what made the later return possible.

---

## 10｜2017年――the parent groups become guests at the side project’s finale

2017年5月22日、横浜アリーナで「Buono!ライブ2017 ～Pienezza！～」を開催。約1万5000人が集まった。

The guest structure almost summarizes the entire history.

℃-ute、カントリー・ガールズ、PINK CRES.がBuono!のステージへ出演した。

At the beginning, Buono! had borrowed people from larger organizations.

Ten years later, **the surrounding organizations entered Buono!’s own concert as guests.**

The dependency relationship had symbolically reversed.

最後は「ホントのじぶん」。A song created for an anime had become a song capable of closing the group’s own ten-year story.

That is not a tie-in anymore.

That is canon.

---

## 11｜2025年――archive discovery without prerequisite knowledge

2025年1月、Buono!の旧譜がハロプロ過去曲のサブスク解禁対象になった。

Buono! appeared alongside Berryz Kobo and ℃-ute as its own artist name.

Later in 2025, “初恋サイダー” gained renewed attention through covers and social media.

A younger listener can now enter through one song, then work backward through the catalog without knowing the original organizational map.

**An autonomous identity has its own discoverability.**

---

## 12｜派生ユニットが自立する6条件

Buono!の歴史から、side project autonomy の条件を整理できる。

1. **Give it a role the parent groups do not already own.**
   - Buono! received a distinct rock identity.

2. **Build enough dedicated repertoire.**
   - アニメ曲だけでなく、アルバム曲とライブ曲を蓄積した。

3. **Create a dedicated live environment.**
   - Dolceとの生バンドがBuono!だけの現場を作った。

4. **Amplify capabilities hidden in the parent groups.**
   - 3人編成が各人の声と役割を露出させた。

5. **Survive the end of the original external purpose.**
   - 『しゅごキャラ！』後も映画、海外、単独ライブへ進んだ。

6. **Own a gateway work that needs no backstory.**
   - 「初恋サイダー」が出自説明を不要にした。

When those conditions accumulate, **a side project stops feeling secondary.**

---

## 結論｜borrowed people can build an unborrowed identity

Buono!には専属メンバーが一人もいなかった。

But it had dedicated songs, a dedicated live sound, dedicated memories, and eventually a dedicated audience.

所属はBerryz工房と℃-uteから借りていた。

The identity was not borrowed.

個別史シリーズでの生存モデルは、

**`autonomous_side_project_survives`**

と呼べる。

A side project can outgrow its original organizational explanation when it creates something its parent groups cannot substitute.

> **所属は借りられても、アイデンティティは自分たちで作れる。**

Buono! spent ten years proving exactly that.
