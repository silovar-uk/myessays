# 注目はどうやって契約に変わるのか — How Attention Turns Into a Contract
## Uniform ads・stadium signage・naming rightsから、スポンサーシップの交渉構造を読む

第12回では、ファンの注目そのものが、スポンサーにとっての価値の土台になっているという構造に触れた。

しかし、その注目が実際にどんな契約を通じてスポンサー収入という具体的な収益になるのかは、まだ扱っていなかった。

第12回の最後に、こう問いを置いた。

**What institutional and contractual structure does the relationship between a club and its sponsors actually rest on?**

今回はここから始める。

> **Information basis: 2026年9月1日**
>
> Naming-rights parties and figures used here are only what the sponsor company itself disclosed publicly. No club's undisclosed financials are guessed at. A full comparison of every club's sponsorship contract amounts is also not the point of this article.

---

## 1. SHORT ANSWER — sponsorship isn't one kind of contract

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
「スポンサー」と一言でまとめると見落としやすいのは、**a sponsorship deal isn't necessarily struck between just the club and a company**という点である。
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
NPBのスポンサーシップには、少なくとも次の3つの形態がある。
<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
Uniform ads on a player's uniform, cap, or helmet; signage inside the stadium and on the big screen; and naming rights, where a company's name gets attached to the stadium itself.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
このうち命名権は、多くの場合、球場を所有する自治体等の施設所有者が契約の一方の当事者になる。球団単独の契約ではない場合がある。
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
つまり、スポンサーシップを理解するには、「**what gets advertised on**」だけでなく「**which parties the contract is actually between**」を見る必要がある。

---

## 2. SPONSORSHIP FORM — three types, three negotiating layers

| Form | Where it appears | Who typically negotiates |
|---|---|---|
| Uniform ads | Player's uniform, cap, helmet | Club-level |
| In-stadium signage | Stadium signage, big screen | Club-level (tied to home-game rights) |
| Naming rights | The stadium's name itself | The facility owner (e.g., a municipality), sometimes with the club too |

Uniform ads・in-stadium signageは、第11回で見た「放送権は球団単位」という構造と同じレイヤーにある。一方、命名権は**a layer belonging to the facility itself**にあり、球団単位の話とは少し違う階層で交渉される。

---

## 3. RULE LIFECYCLE — the Central/Pacific gap in uniform ads

Uniform advertising has a gap between the two leagues in when it started and how far it reaches.

At a 2000 professional baseball executive committee meeting, uniform ads became allowed under conditions limiting things like size.

- **Pacific League**: started in 2000.
- **Central League**: started in 2006 — six years behind the Pacific League.
- **Central League**: in principle, home uniforms only.
- **Pacific League**: also permitted on visitor uniforms.

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
このセ・パ差は、**pointing the same direction as the broadcast-rights tendency seen in Episode 11**。
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
第11回では、歴史的にマスコミ関連企業を親会社に持つ球団が多いセ・リーグが、地上波放映権を比較的厳格に管理してきた経緯があるとされることを見た。
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
ユニフォーム広告の導入がセ・リーグで6年遅れ、範囲もホーム限定にとどまっている点は、この傾向と方向性が一致しているように見える。
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
ただし、これはシリーズ上の示唆であり、なぜセ・リーグがビジター用を認めないのか、その具体的な理由を明記した情報源は確認できていない。断定はしない。

---

## 4. WHO NEGOTIATES — naming rights aren't always the club's own deal

命名権を具体例で見る。

In 2024, Mazda Corporation announced a naming-rights deal for the Hiroshima Municipal Stadium (MAZDA Zoom-Zoom Stadium Hiroshima). The contract is between **Mazda, the City of Hiroshima, and the Hiroshima Toyo Carp** — three parties — worth 220 million yen a year (before tax) over a five-year term from April 2024 to March 2029, the fourth consecutive renewal.

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
この事例が示すのは、**naming-rights negotiations can involve the facility's owner, not just the club**ということである。
<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
When a municipality owns the stadium and the club is simply its home tenant, the naming right — a right over the facility itself — belongs to the owner.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
一方、球団自身が球場を所有・建設しているケースでは、命名権交渉の当事者構成は異なり得る。本稿ではその網羅的な比較はしないが、**who owns the stadium changes which layer naming-rights negotiation sits at**ことは押さえておきたい。
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
これは、マツダスタジアムのような三者契約がNPB全球場の標準であることを意味しない。球場ごとに所有構造が異なるため、命名権の交渉構造も一様ではない。

---

## 5. WHAT IS SOLD — the kind of exposure differs by form too

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
スポンサーが得ているものも、形態によって性質が異なる。
<!-- level:2 role:description -->
Uniform ads ride along with a player's own performance, reaching news coverage, social media, and print, not just the broadcast itself. In-stadium signage reaches whoever attends or watches a broadcast or stream. Naming rights tie a company's name to the place itself, continuously.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
つまり、ユニフォーム広告は「選手の活躍」という不確実な変数に連動する露出であり、看板広告は「観戦・視聴という行為」に連動する露出であり、命名権は選手の成績や観客数に直接左右されにくい、より長期的なブランド上の結びつきである。
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
第12回で見た「ファンの注目がスポンサー価値の土台になる」という関係は、ユニフォーム広告・看板広告では比較的直接的だが、命名権ではやや間接的である。

---

## 6. CONNECTING TO EPISODE 12 — linking attention to contracts

第12回のREVENUE LINK（ファンの行動→収益カテゴリー）を思い出す。

- Buying a ticket and watching in person → ticket revenue
- Watching a broadcast or stream → broadcast rights
- Joining the fan club → stabilized ticket revenue

ここに、今回の視点を加える。

- Attendance and viewership drawing a crowd → **the foundation of in-stadium signage and uniform-ad value**
- A player's performance getting widely covered → **the foundation of uniform-ad exposure value**
- Regional interest in the venue itself → **the foundation of naming rights' longer-term value**

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
つまり、第12回で見た「ファンの注目」は、単一の収益へつながるのではなく、**feeds several different sponsorship forms in different ways, each with its own kind of exposure**。
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
「注目→契約」という一本の矢印ではなく、注目の種類（観戦・視聴・報道・地域的な関心）と、契約の形態（ユニフォーム・看板・命名権）を、それぞれ対応づけて読むほうが正確である。

---

## 7. WHAT SPONSORSHIP DOES NOT MEAN

### Sponsorship ≠ always a two-party deal between club and company

Naming rights can bring the facility owner in as a third party, as seen above.

### A disclosed sponsorship figure ≠ proof of advertising effect

Even where a contract amount is publicly disclosed, it doesn't prove any particular advertising effect or sales impact. This article doesn't attempt effect measurement.

### One disclosed example ≠ the standard for every stadium

The Mazda Stadium three-party deal is one disclosed case. Ownership structures differ stadium by stadium, so naming-rights negotiation isn't uniform across NPB.

---

## 8. TAKEAWAYS

- Sponsorship comes in at least three forms — uniform ads, in-stadium signage, naming rights — each negotiated at a different layer.
- Uniform-ad adoption differs between leagues (Pacific League 2000, Central League 2006, Central League home-only), which appears to point the same direction as the broadcast-rights tendency from Episode 11, though no source confirms the causal link — treated as a suggestion, not a fact.
- Naming rights can involve the municipality that owns the stadium as a party, so it isn't always the club's contract alone (e.g., Mazda Stadium's three-party deal).
- The kind of exposure a sponsor buys differs by form: uniform ads ride on player performance, signage rides on attendance/viewership, naming rights tie to the venue over the long term.
- Episode 12's fan attention doesn't feed one single revenue line — it feeds several sponsorship forms differently.
