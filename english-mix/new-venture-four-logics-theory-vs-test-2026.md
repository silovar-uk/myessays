# 新規事業の始め方は、なぜ四種類もあるのか――Four logics, one uncertain future

「まず何をしますか」というsimple questionに、三つのanswersが返る。「Ask customers」「Start with what you have」「Form a bold theory」。どれもrightに見えるのに、first moveは三方向へ分裂する。会議室で最初に誕生するstartupは、productではなく付箋のpopulationである。

しかしmethod choiceは、単なる好みではない。A market that does not yet existを現在の顧客に問い続けても答えは出にくい。逆にwilling buyersが目の前にいるのにgrand theoryだけ練れば、初回売上を逃す。Which method to use is itself an investment decision.

本稿はLean Startup、Effectuation、Creation Theory、Theory-Based Viewのfour approachesを中心に比較する。最後は架空のsports video archive businessを使い、Monday morningに何をすべきかまで落とす。The key: 方法論は「正しい順番」ではなく、**how we act under uncertainty**の違いである。

> 【Research note】原典で確認できるfacts、本稿のinterpretation、実務へのproposalを区別する。Sports businessの記述はthought experimentで、実在企業へのcustomer researchではない。

## 1. Research identifies four different logics of venture creation

2024年の[Four Approaches to New Venture Creation](https://doi.org/10.1177/01492063241264226)が出発点。Combs、Gruber、ZahraはLean Startup、Effectuation、Creation Theory、Theory-Based Viewを比較した。注意したいのは、four approachesが完全に排他的なplaybooksではない点。現象を説明するdescriptive theoryと行動を導くnormative guidanceが混在する。

One-line synthesisは、Leanならtest and learn、Effectuationならact with available means and partners、Creation Theoryならenact opportunities、Theory-Based Viewならbuild a causal explanation of unseen value。これは本稿のsummaryであって、各理論のofficial slogansではない。

![四つの事業創出の論理を、それぞれが問うこと、最初の行動、確かめる証拠で比較した図](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/new-venture-four-logics-map-2026.svg)

This diagram is not a ranking. It is a question generator for the same venture idea. Four lensesを重ねると、which unknowns remain invisibleが分かる。

## 2. Lean Startup tests whether a business can actually work

Eric RiesのLean StartupはBuild–Measure–Learnとvalidated learningを中心に置く。A Minimum Viable Product（MVP）は完成品の小型版とは限らず、learningに必要な最小の実験手段。Positive commentsではなく、actual usageやpaymentを観測してpivot or persevereを判断する。[Official explanation](https://leanstartup.co/about/principles/)

Strengthはobservable customer behaviorからfast feedbackを得られる点。Limitは、radically new valueやinstitutional changeを含むfuture marketに対し、today's responseがfuture demandの直接証拠とは限らない点。Don't stop asking customers. Ask what the evidence actually proves.

## 3. Effectuation starts with means, not a fixed destination

Saras SarasvathyのEffectuationはexpert entrepreneursのdecision-making研究に由来する。Instead of starting with a fixed destination、who I am / what I know / whom I knowというmeansから動き、stakeholder commitmentsに応じてgoalを共創する。[Research background](https://effectuation.org/effectuation-background-research)

- Bird in Hand：who I am / what I know / whom I knowから始める。
- Affordable Loss：expected returnより、何を失ってよいかを先に決める。
- Crazy Quilt：self-selecting partnersとのcommitmentsで可能性を広げる。
- Lemonade：surprisesをopportunitiesへ変える。
- Pilot in the Plane：predict the futureより、what we can controlを増やす。

Effectuationはnot an excuse for no planning。公式資料もstartupのearly logicとして位置付け、causal planningとの両立を示す。[Official principles](https://effectuation.org/effectuation-101?page_id=4055&principle=crazy-quilts)。A networking dinner alone is not a partnership: commitments of time, money or resourcesが必要になる。

## 4. Creation Theory questions whether the opportunity existed beforehand

Sharon AlvarezとJay Barneyが2007年に問うたのは、Is an opportunity discovered or created? という根本問題。Creation Theoryは、entrepreneurial actionを通じて機会自体が形成される場合を説明する。[Original paper](https://doi.org/10.1002/sej.4)

Example: 複数クラブが持つfootageについてrights clearanceとshared standardsを共同で作れば、previously untradeable videoが売買可能になるかもしれない。Market researchだけでなく、rules and commitmentsが市場の条件を作る。これは本稿のillustrationであり、original studyのスポーツ事例ではない。

Creation TheoryとEffectuationは似ているが、the question differs。前者はontology of opportunity、後者はlogic of entrepreneurial action。If we merge them, 「市場を作る」と「誰とどう動く」を混同してしまう。

## 5. Theory-Based View starts from a causal claim against conventional wisdom

Theory-Based Viewは、いわゆる「理論型事業創出」に対応する研究概念。ただしthe Japanese label is not a single standardized translation。FelinとZengerは2017年、unique strategyを、what problem to solve / what assets to combine / why value emergesというfirm-specific causal theoryとして捉えた。[Original paper](https://doi.org/10.1287/stsc.2017.0048)

「AI analysis will sell」はweak theory。もっとstrongなclaimは、high rights-clearance and search costsがarchive reuseを妨げている、therefore standardizing rights and metadata reduces transaction costs、so the archive could become a marketplace。これはcausal chainであって、verified factではない。

A 2024 comparison argues that Lean emphasizes early feedback while Theory-Based View emphasizes contrarian beliefs, counterfactual thinking, and theory-specific experiments。It does not reject validation; it questions whether interviews are always the best first test。[Comparison paper](https://doi.org/10.1177/01492063231226136)

A longitudinal study of nine consumer platform ventures over up to five years traces how a rough thesis evolves into an actionable theory through theorizing and data generation。A theory is not a beautiful story to protect. It is a model to revise with evidence。[Study](https://doi.org/10.1287/stsc.2024.0178)

## 6. Useful tools are not automatically rival theories

A common classification trap: Design Thinking、Customer Development、Business Model Canvas、Discovery-Driven Planning、Blue Ocean Strategyを「rival schools」として横並びにすること。But problem discovery、business description、investment planning are different tasks。A spoon, a pan and a recipe are all useful; they are not the same kind of tool.

- Design Thinking：human needsを観察し、right problemを再定義する。[IDEO](https://designthinking.ideo.com/introduction)
- Customer Development：repeatable and scalable business modelをsearchする。[Steve Blank](https://steveblank.com/2012/03/29/nail-the-customer-development-manifesto/)
- Jobs to Be Done：customer demographicsより、customers' progress in a situationを見る。[HBR](https://hbr.org/podcast/2016/12/the-jobs-to-be-done-theory-of-innovation)
- Business Model Canvas：nine building blocksでvalue creation and captureを可視化する。Filling boxes is not proof。[Strategyzer](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-business-model-canvas)
- Discovery-Driven Planning：critical assumptionsを明示し、milestonesでinvestmentを判断する。[McGrath & MacMillan](https://hbr.org/1995/07/discovery-driven-planning)
- Blue Ocean Strategy：competition factorsを描き直し、new value curveを考える。[Official](https://www.blueoceanstrategy.com/tools/strategy-canvas/)

The point is not to ban combinations. Use JTBD research inside Lean experiments; use Business Model Canvas to examine a Theory-Based strategy. What must be banned is mistaking a completed worksheet for validated evidence.

## 7. One sports archive idea, four very different Monday mornings

【Thought experiment】A venture manager wants to turn long-term sports footage storage into recurring revenue。Assume archives have costs while monetization requires rights clearance, metadata, buyers and a sales channel。This is a hypothetical case, not evidence about any particular league or company.

### Lean: find the buyer and the payment

Interview actual footage buyers about their last failed search and actual spend。Then offer a manual paid pilot using about ten rights-cleared clips。Evidence is quote approval, paid order, repeat purchase—not 'sounds useful'。The count is illustrative, not a research-backed benchmark.

### Effectuation: turn relationships into committed means

Start with existing video capability, production partners and clubs。If two stakeholders commit to rights review and trial use, co-design the offer around those commitments。Set an affordable loss before expansion。The evidence is contributed time, resources and obligations.

### Creation Theory: build the missing rules of exchange

The problem may not be absent demand but absent transactability。Agreeing on licensing windows, permitted use and revenue sharing may create a market that could not function before。But joining a meeting is not the same as legally valid rights clearance.

### Theory-Based View: write the causal chain and a disconfirming test

Theory: High search and rights costs suppress small transactions; shared metadata and standardized licensing could reduce transaction costs and unlock long-tail demand。First test? Not UI popularity, but real cost breakdowns and rights approval constraints。If licensing costs remain high for each transaction, the causal theory fails.

![スポーツ映像アーカイブ事業を四つの方法論で考えた場合の最初の検証行動を比較する図](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/new-venture-four-logics-case-2026.svg)

All four can be necessary. A paid customer without legal clearance is not a viable business. A licensing system without buyers is not a viable business either. Success under one lens does not remove risks visible under another.

## 8. A second literature check removes the fantasy of a universal method

【Verified】A 2024 paper separates Lean from Theory-Based View by the role of contrarian beliefs and startup-specific causal logic—not simply by experimental speed。Today's customer answers are not direct observations of an unseen future. Nor does Theory-Based View reject customer evidence。[Felin et al.](https://doi.org/10.1177/01492063231226136)

【Verified】A randomized controlled trial of 116 Italian startups found better early performance and more pivots among founders trained to formulate and test hypotheses scientifically。But this is not a four-way tournament. Population, intervention and observation period limit generalization。[Camuffo et al.](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2018.3249)

【Verified】Effectuation identifies patterns in expert entrepreneurs' reasoning。Describing expert behavior is not equivalent to proving a universal success formula。[Research background](https://effectuation.org/effectuation-background-research)

【Interpretation】The decisive question is not merely whether the future is uncertain. Ask what can be observed, controlled, negotiated, or falsified now。Purchase behavior is observable. Partner commitments are negotiable. Rights rules can be co-created. Causal hypotheses can be split into tests.

## 9. Build a sequence of questions, not loyalty to one school

【Proposal】Do not assign one employee to each theory. Maintain an assumption ledger: (1) customer problem, (2) available means and stakeholder commitments, (3) market-making rules, (4) contrarian causal model, (5) unit economics and repeatability。This is our integrated operating proposal, not an official five-step framework.

- Week 1：Break the hypothesis into buyer, pain, willingness to pay, and why this market has not existed。Check legal constraints first。
- Week 2：Separate buyers, rights holders and operators。Collect observed examples, existing costs and approval constraints。
- Week 3：Secure a small pilot commitment and set affordable loss limits。
- Week 4：Observe paid behavior or actual workflow improvement。A concierge test may precede software。
- Week 5：Recalculate rights, acquisition, delivery and sales costs per transaction。
- Week 6：Decide persevere, pivot or stop。Document evidence, remaining unknowns and the next investment gate。

Choose decision metrics carefully. Twenty interviews measure activity. Number of paid orders, days to rights clearance and hours of manual delivery test viability。A positive comment, pilot participation, binding commitment and cash received are different strengths of evidence.

## 10. The sticky note is innocent; the hidden assumption is not

Back to the opening. The customer interviewer, partnership builder and theory maker were not necessarily wrong. They were looking at different maps of the same unknown future。The danger is holding only one map and mistaking its blank areas for empty land.

Venture building is not about reciting your favorite framework。Ask what customers cannot yet answer, what stakeholders must co-create, and what causal claim only you believe。Then design cheap tests for the most dangerous errors。Before sticking a note on the wall, label the unknown it represents. Now the wall starts to become a map of a business.
