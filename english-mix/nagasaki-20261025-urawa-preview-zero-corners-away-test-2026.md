# Four Goals, Zero Corners――長崎は埼玉で再現できるか
*Written October 24, 2026. Pre-match preview for Urawa Reds vs V-Varen Nagasaki, Round 12.*

10月21日、長崎は首位・広島に4-0で勝った。But the match sheet looks strange: **zero corners** for Nagasaki, ten for Hiroshima. Shots were 10 to 13. A four-goal win with a ten-corner deficit. クラブとＪリーグ、二つの公式記録を突き合わせても数字は変わらなかった。[長崎公式記録](https://www.v-varen.com/gamedata/2026102115)／[Ｊリーグ公式記録](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/102115/)

That does not automatically prove superiority. 広島は66分に退場し、長崎の後半2得点は11対10の局面で生まれた。Yet Nagasaki were already 2-0 up before the dismissal. **The reason to discount the score and the reason to respect the performance coexist.** ここから浦和戦を読みたい。[Ｊリーグ試合経過](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/102115/)

The match is set for **October 25, 15:00, Saitama Stadium 2002**. 第11節終了時点、浦和は8位・勝点18、長崎は14位・勝点12。This is Round 12 of the **2026/27 J1 season**, not the special competition played earlier in 2026. 以下は10月24日時点のpre-match analysisである。[Ｊリーグ対戦ページ](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/102503/)


## 守｜昇格組・長崎の現在地は、4-0より11試合で測る

長崎は2025年J2で2位となり、2018年以来のJ1に復帰した。Its home also moved to PEACE STADIUM. ただし、J2、2026年春の特別大会、2026/27リーグをone continuous datasetとして扱うのは危険だ。Higher-level opponents change the time and space available to the same players.[クラブ公式沿革](https://www.v-varen.com/club/profile)／[公式スタッフ登録](https://data.j-league.or.jp/SFIX05/search?divisionValue=1&exDivisionId=1&exTeamId=47&teamId=47)

Across 11 league games, Nagasaki have **3 wins, 3 draws, 5 losses; 15 goals for, 18 against**. The Hiroshima game alone contributes roughly 27% of those goals. 4-0だけを見ればfearsome attack、直前10試合だけなら11得点。The evaluation changes dramatically with the observation window.[Ｊリーグ公式出場記録](https://data.j-league.or.jp/SFPR01/search?competition_frame_id=1&competition_frame_id_ex=1&competition_id=725&competition_id_ex=725&competition_year=2026&competition_year_ex=2026&dataSize=1&pageStartNo=0&selectedCompetitionName=%EF%BC%AA%EF%BC%91%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%82%B0&selectedCompetitionYear=2026%E5%B9%B4&selectedTeamName=%E9%95%B7%E5%B4%8E&team_id=47&team_id_ex=47)

A simple home-away split is revealing. **Home: 3W-2D-1L, goals 11-7. Away: 0W-1D-4L, goals 4-11.** 公式の11試合を再集計した数値だ。This is not causal proof: opponents and sample sizes differ. しかし、試合会場が埼玉である以上、home performanceをawayに移植できるかは直接の論点となる。[Ｊリーグ公式全試合結果](https://www.jleague.jp/club/nagasaki/)

So neither “a weak promoted side” nor “the team that destroyed the leaders” is enough. 長崎が示したのは**a high ceiling in one match**, not yet proof of repeatability across opponents and venues. ここから戦い方を分解する。


## 破｜広島戦の4点を分解すると、先制までの時間が主役になる

The scoring timeline matters more than the headline: **36' Onaga, 45' Matheus Jesus, 73' and 90+7' Yasuda**. 広島の退場は66分。Two goals came before numerical advantage and two after it. 先制の再現性と、数的優位下の追加点は別の問いとして扱うべきだ。[公式試合経過](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/102115/)

At 45', Jonathan Junior advanced from the right; a cleared cross reached Matheus Jesus, who finished. At 73' and 90+7', Matheus supplied the final passes for Yasuda. 幅を取る前進、second-ball reaction、final pass、味方のrun。Different actions were connected. Matheus is not only a dribbler or scorer; he can be a **link between ball progression and the final run**.[公式速報](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/102115/)

Yasuda said the squad already shared an expectation about Matheus receiving the ball in a diagonal area. That changes the interpretation. 得点はindividual inspirationだけでなく、**shared timing between passer and runner**の結果でもある。If Urawa close down Matheus, they must also track what his teammates do when he faces forward.[保田の試合後コメント](https://www.v-varen.com/gamedata/2026102115)

Hiroshima still recorded 13 shots and ten corners. Nagasaki did not erase all opposition attacks. 高木監督はopponent-specific preparationと澤田崇の今季リーグ戦初先発を評価した。The hypothesis is more precise: **you do not need to win every phase if you win the decisive sequence**. 浦和戦でそれが続くかを観察する。[長崎公式監督コメント](https://www.v-varen.com/gamedata/2026102115)


## 破｜浦和との勝負は、同じ3-4-2-1から異なる時間が生まれるところにある

Both sides used a **3-4-2-1** in their latest league games, according to official match records. ただしformation is a starting map, not a behavioral explanation. Press high, defend deep, or attack vertically after regaining possession: identical nominal shapes can create different games.[長崎―広島](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/102115/live-commentary/)／[浦和―千葉](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/102116/live-commentary/)

Takagi's pre-match comments are unusually specific. 曺貴裁監督の浦和は中央をcompactに保ち、横パスへ素早くpressするという。He mentioned pass speed, first touch and preparation before receiving. つまり長崎側のidentified threatは**the speed of Urawa's pressure**, not merely individual marking. Whether their solution works remains an on-pitch question.[高木監督会見](https://www.v-varen.com/team-comments/251305)

Urawa beat Chiba **3-2** on October 21. Kaneko, Nemoto and substitute Sasa scored; Chiba pulled one back at 90+1'. The official preview highlights Kaneko and Nagasaki's Hasegawa in chance creation. Possession alone may miss the key: **who receives the first useful pass after a regain, and whether the next attack is completed**.[浦和公式](https://www.urawa-reds.co.jp/topteamtopics/9029770.html)／[公式対戦データ](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/102503/)

So the test is not whether Matheus touches the ball at all. 浦和が彼をbackward-facingにして外へ追い込めれば、the attack slows. 長崎がfirst pressureを外してMatheusやHasegawaを前向きにできれば、浦和の背後にspaceが生まれ得る。**Aggression is an asset when pressure works and a liability when it is bypassed.**


## 破｜今季初のアウェイ勝利を阻むものは、環境なのか、試合運びなのか

Nagasaki's five away results are Okayama 0-1, Kashiwa 2-4, Kobe 1-3, Chiba 1-1 and Kashima 0-2. Two scoreless games, two matches conceding multiple goals. しかし、この結果を“travel mentality”へ短絡させる証拠はない。**A home-away gap is an observation; its cause is still a hypothesis.**[Ｊリーグ公式戦績](https://www.jleague.jp/club/nagasaki/)

There are at least three candidate explanations: opponent strength, score state and timing, and pressure during buildup. 浦和戦で最も観察しやすいのはthird oneだ。Count not only final-third entries but how often Nagasaki **escape the first wave of pressure**. Without progression, attacking talent may never be activated.

The calendar adds uncertainty: Hiroshima on October 21, Urawa on October 25. 大南は広島戦22分に交代したが、そこからinjury statusや出場可否は推測できない。Yasuda starts? Sawada retains his place? Still unknown. **Adaptability to available players** matters as much as a fixed best eleven.[長崎公式記録](https://www.v-varen.com/gamedata/2026102115)


## 離｜ピーススタジアムで生まれる強さを、敵地へ持ち運べるか

Away from tactics, PEACE STADIUM sits inside Nagasaki Stadium City, opened in October 2024, alongside an arena, hotel, shops and offices. 勝敗への因果は未検証だが、home-away performance gapを見ると別の問いが立つ。**Can a club carry its home-built momentum beyond the place that helped create it?**[施設公式](https://www.nagasakistadiumcity.com/project_outline/)／[長崎市観光公式](https://www.at-nagasaki.jp/spot/65386)

The Hiroshima fixture was also a **Peace Match** between clubs rooted in cities marked by atomic bombing. 長崎は平和祈念ユニフォームを着用し、平和学習や発信を続ける。This should not be used as a causal explanation for victory. 平和活動はscoring mechanismではない。**The club carries responsibilities that cannot be reduced to the scoreline.**[長崎公式・平和祈念活動](https://www.v-varen.com/peace)

Saitama Stadium will not reproduce Nagasaki's home setting. Crowd, tempo and game state can differ. その場所でも自分たちの攻撃を成立させられれば、Nagasaki's progress travels. できなければ、the boundary of its current strength becomes clearer. これは勝敗だけでは測れない90分だ。


## 観戦ガイド｜最初の15分と、マテウスが前を向く瞬間を見逃さない

Use four observation points. **①First 15 minutes:** can Nagasaki escape Urawa's first press? **②Matheus receiving:** backward pass or forward-facing progression, with runs from Yasuda and Hasegawa? **③Wide channels:** in two 3-4-2-1 shapes, who covers behind the wide player? **④After the first goal and late in the game:** can Nagasaki maintain progression when game state changes? これはpredictionsではなくtestable observationsである。

For Urawa, the decisive moment may come **just after a failed press**, not at the tackle itself. 山口蛍の周辺からMatheusへ前向きの時間を渡さなければ、Urawa can attack vertically. If Nagasaki break the first wave, watch how far the Urawa back line retreats. That reveals more than possession percentage alone.

For Nagasaki, count **the number of players connected to one chance**, not only total attacks. Matheus' pass, a supporting player and a third-man run can create danger even with few shots. ただし、the finishing efficiency seen against Hiroshima is not guaranteed to repeat.

If taking notes, record receiving zones, failed presses and changes before/after the opening goal—not just shots by 15-minute interval. 長崎が押されながら先制すれば広島戦との共通性が増える。If it concedes early and cannot progress, a different explanation deserves testing. **Decide what evidence would change your mind before the match starts.**


## 結論｜4-0の翌日に必要なのは、勢いの物語より再現性の検査である

I began with the odd fact: **four goals, zero corners**. But the score hides different mechanisms: two goals before the red card, combinations between Matheus and Yasuda, ten corners allowed, and five away games without a win. The 4-0 score is both evidence and camouflage.

My central question for Urawa–Nagasaki is not shot total or possession. **When Urawa press, can Nagasaki use the next player and face forward?** 広島戦では、その瞬間が得点につながった。If it happens again in Saitama, we learn something more durable than a single upset. If not, we learn exactly why that 4-0 demanded skepticism. Either way, the 90 minutes matter.
