# トレードとは何を交換する制度なのか
## FAとの違いから、選手契約の譲渡・事前同意・player mobilityを読む

「トレード」と聞くと、player for playerの交換を思い浮かべやすい。

But the visible movement is not the whole mechanism.

第8回ではFAを、**the right to expand the market of clubs you can negotiate with**として読んだ。

すると今回はこう聞ける。

**A trade and free agency can both move a player between clubs. So what is actually different?**

> **Information basis: 2026-08-31**
>
> 最新公開の野球協約は2025年度版。2026年の運用確認にはNPBの2026 trade noticesと7月31日のtransfer-window announcementを使う。

---

## 1. SHORT ANSWER — A trade moves an existing contract relationship

2025年の最新公開野球協約第105条では、球団はexisting player contractを他球団へ譲渡できる。

When the contract is assigned, the club's rights and obligations under that contract move to the receiving club.

[日本プロ野球選手会「野球協約・統一契約書」](https://jpbpa.net/contract/)

[日本プロフェッショナル野球協約2025](https://jpbpa.net/wp-content/uploads/jpbpa-pdf/ag2025.pdf)

So the safest mental model is:

**TRADE = contract assignment, not ownership transfer of a person.**

選手本人を「売買する」という理解ではなく、**the club-side counterparty to an existing contract changes**と考える。

---

## 2. SAME RESULT, DIFFERENT MECHANISM

FAでは、

**ELIGIBILITY → DECLARATION → NEGOTIATION → AGREEMENT**

だった。

The player earns the right and decides whether to exercise it.

トレードでは、already existing contractが球団間の手続で別球団へ移る。

Both may end with:

**Player A: Club X → Club Y**

But:

**SAME DESTINATION ≠ SAME PATH**

制度を見るときは、結果ではなくmechanismを見る。

---

## 3. INITIATOR — Who starts the movement?

FAはplayer-right-based mobilityとして理解できる。

トレードは、通常、clubs negotiate the assignment of an existing contract.

That does not mean the player has zero agency.

ただし、FAのように「選手が自分の権利行使でmarketを開く」構造ではない。

The key is to separate **initiative** from **consent**.

---

## 4. CONSENT — When is consent given?

野球協約第106条では、選手は選手契約が他球団へ譲渡されることを、**統一契約書であらかじめ同意**する。

This produces an important distinction.

### Wrong simplification 1

“Players are traded without consent.”

Not quite.

There is **general prior consent** built into the standard contract structure.

### Wrong simplification 2

“Every trade requires fresh destination-by-destination approval.”

That is also not how the published standard framework is written.

The better question is:

**When was consent given, and what exactly did it cover?**

今回のkeywordは、

**CONSENT TIMING**

である。

---

## 5. RIGHT — What actually moves?

Article 105 is clear about the core object: the **existing player contract**.

契約に関する球団のrights and obligationsが、receiving clubへ移る。

Think of it as:

**OLD CLUB — CONTRACT — PLAYER**

becoming

**NEW CLUB — CONTRACT — PLAYER**

The contract relationship continues, while the club-side counterparty changes.

だから、

**TRADE ≠ terminate first, sign from zero later**

と考える方がよい。

---

## 6. TRADE ≠ LOAN

第107条では、player loan、recall right、conditional assignmentを禁止している。

So an ordinary NPB trade is not the same mechanism as a football loan.

**TRADE ≠ LOAN**

この区別は地味だが重要。

---

## 7. TIMING — There is a transfer window

第108条では、選手契約を譲渡できる期間を、season終了翌日から翌年7月31日までとしている。

Waiver-based transfers are an exception.

そしてNPBは2026年7月31日にも、その日でtransfer periodが終了したと公式に発表した。

[NPB「選手契約、譲渡可能期間終了のお知らせ」](https://npb.jp/news/detail/20260731_02.html)

This is a good source-vintage example:

**LATEST-PUBLISHED RULE TEXT + CURRENT-YEAR OPERATION**

を組み合わせる。

---

## 8. PROCEDURE — Club agreement is not the final step

A trade is not complete just because two clubs announce an agreement.

第110条では、receiving clubが契約譲渡協定書などをコミッショナーへ提出して承認を申請する。

Then the commissioner removes the player from the old club's controlled-player registration and registers the player with the new club.

第111条では、その公示日からnew clubのための野球活動へ従事できる。

So the process is roughly:

**CLUB AGREEMENT → COMMISSIONER PROCEDURE → REGISTRATION / PUBLIC NOTICE**

Episode 6の支配下登録がここでretrievalされる。

---

## 9. 2026 CASE — A trade is not always one-for-one

2026年5月13日のNPB公示では、DeNAから山本祐大がソフトバンクへ、ソフトバンクから尾形崇斗・井上朋也がDeNAへ移っている。

[NPB「トレード 2026年度公示」](https://npb.jp/announcement/2026/pn_traded.html)

One player on one side, two on the other.

That already tells us:

**TRADE ≠ one-player-for-one-player only**

ただし、公示だけを見てcomplete economic termsを推測しない。

**PUBLIC NOTICE ≠ COMPLETE CONSIDERATION**

If the official source does not disclose cash or other terms, do not invent them.

---

## 10. TRADE vs FA — Compare with the same variables

| Variable | TRADE | FA |
|---|---|---|
| INITIATOR | club-to-club contract assignment mechanism | player earns and exercises a right |
| RIGHT | existing contract and club-side rights/obligations move | market of possible contracting clubs expands |
| CONSENT | general prior consent in the uniform contract | player decides whether to exercise FA |
| DESTINATION | determined through club transaction | player negotiates and agrees; re-signing is possible |
| TIMING | Article 108 transfer window | FA eligibility / declaration calendar |
| AFTER | existing contract continues with a new club counterparty | new agreement follows negotiation |

The point is not to rank freedom.

The point is:

**Same player movement, different institutional path.**

---

## 11. PLAYER AGENCY — “club-controlled” is useful but incomplete

“FA is player-controlled, trade is club-controlled” is easy to remember.

But it is too rough if used alone.

選手は統一契約書でcontract assignmentへ事前同意している。

At the same time, Article 109 penalizes collusion designed to force a transfer.

So an ordinary trade is not a player-held right to demand movement to a chosen club.

But that does not mean players cannot communicate preferences or discuss their situation in practice.

Separate:

**FORMAL RIGHT**

from

**REAL-WORLD COMMUNICATION**

制度記事ではこの境界を守る。

---

## 12. SYSTEM VIEW — Trade as roster reallocation

シリーズ上のeditorial frameとして、tradeは**roster reallocation mechanism**と読める。

Draft allocates rookie negotiation rights.

Farm systems develop talent.

Contract renewal converts evaluation into contract outcomes.

FA expands a player's negotiation market.

Trade moves an existing contract between clubs.

Together, these mechanisms form **roster construction**.

That is the larger question beginning to appear after Episode 9.

---

## 13. WHAT TRADE DOES NOT MEAN

### TRADE ≠ FA

FA changes negotiation freedom.

Trade assigns an existing contract.

### TRADE ≠ FREE CONTRACT STATUS

自由契約では、選手が公示後にany clubと契約できる状態になる。

Trade keeps an existing contract relationship alive while changing the club-side party.

### TRADE ≠ LOAN

Article 107 prohibits the ordinary loan / recall-right structure.

### TRADE ≠ ACTIVE PLAYER DRAFT

Both can move players, but the governing mechanism is different.

Same outcome does not mean same rule.

---

## 14. 30-second Mobility Mechanism Audit

When you see a trade headline, ask:

1. **INITIATOR** — 誰が動かした？
2. **RIGHT** — 何のcontract / rightが動く？
3. **CONSENT** — 同意はいつ与えられた？
4. **DESTINATION** — 移動先はどう決まった？
5. **TIMING** — transfer window内？
6. **COMPARE** — If this were FA, what would be different?

最後の比較が、制度の暗記をsystem understandingへ変える。

---

## 15. TAKEAWAYS

- NPB tradeの中心は**existing player contractのassignment**。
- Club rights and obligations under that contract move to the receiving club.
- Player consent exists as **general prior consent** in the uniform contract structure.
- Ordinary transfers have a deadline; 2026もJuly 31終了が公式確認されている。
- FAとtradeは同じplayer movementを生み得るが、INITIATOR / RIGHT / CONSENT / DESTINATION / TIMINGが違う。
- Ask not only **“Who moved?”** but **“Which mobility mechanism moved them?”**

ここまで、

**DRAFT → DEVELOPMENT → CONTRACT → FA → TRADE**

を見てきた。
