# スマイレージ／アンジュルム史――別物になり続けることは、なぜ「らしさ」になったのか
## 4人のエッグ、2期加入と離脱、武道館、改名、大器晩成、リーダー継承、2026年から読む「自分を壊して続く」組織

### 要旨

ANGERME is difficult to define by a single stable image.

「夢見る 15歳」の4人組と、2026年のアンジュルムは、人数も中心人物も作家もステージの空気もかなり違う。

The group even changed its own name.

Ordinarily, that much change would suggest a different group.

Yet the history from S/mileage to ANGERME suggests the opposite.

What survived was not a fixed number of members, a permanent center, or one musical genre.

**What survived was the capacity to redesign the group before the current form hardened into doctrine.**

モーニング娘。が加入と卒業を制度化したグループなら、スマイレージ／アンジュルムはさらに一歩進み、**identity itself became editable**.

This essay reads that continuity as **renewal capacity**.

---

## 1. 2009――A small four-member Egg unit

スマイレージは2009年4月、ハロプロエッグから選ばれた和田彩花、前田憂佳、福田花音、小川紗季の4人で結成された。

The official history explains the name as a combination of “smile” and “age/generation.”

It was not a large rotating system at first.

むしろ4人の若さ、近さ、声、キャラクターがそのまま輪郭になるユニットだった。

After indie releases, the group was given a public challenge in 2010: collect at least 10,000 photos of smiling people to earn a major debut.

They exceeded the target and made their major debut on May 26, 2010 with 「夢見る 15歳」.

The important point is not only the number.

**The process of becoming major was itself part of the product.**

ファンは完成品を見るだけではなく、デビュー条件の達成に参加した。

Early S/mileage therefore sold proximity, youth, smiles, and visible upward movement.

### Key Moment 1
**1万人の笑顔――Debut as Participation**

---

## 2. 2011――Breaking the successful four-member form

A year after major debut, the safest strategy would have been to preserve the successful quartet.

But S/mileage opened a new-member recruitment process.

新加入者はすぐ正規メンバーになるのではなく、まず「サブメンバー」として活動し、中西香菜、竹内朱莉、勝田里奈、田村芽実の4人が正メンバーとなった。

This was not simply a one-day addition announcement.

**Joining became a visible probation, training, and evaluation process.**

At the same time, the original lineup changed sharply.

小川紗季が2011年8月に卒業し、前田憂佳も12月31日に卒業した。

Two of the original four were gone within months.

In the 2014 announcement that later introduced the name change, Ayaka Wada and Kanon Fukuda themselves described this period as a crisis for the survival of the group.

The question became unavoidable:

> Was S/mileage the four people?
> 
> Or could S/mileage continue after the four-person chemistry broke?

The group chose the second answer.

That was its first major act of **self-disruption**.

### Key Moment 2
**2期加入と初期メンバー卒業――The First Identity Break**

---

## 3. The six-member era――Do not restore the old normal; build a new one

2012年以降、和田彩花、福田花音、中西香菜、竹内朱莉、勝田里奈、田村芽実の6人が中心となった。

This period matters because it was not merely an interim stage.

Instead of recreating the early four-person mood, songs such as 「好きよ、純情反抗期。」「寒いね。」「新しい私になれ！」「ヤッタルチャン」 built a different six-member S/mileage.

The title 「新しい私になれ！」 almost reads like an organizational instruction.

そして2014年7月15日、スマイレージは初の日本武道館公演を実現した。

This was not the untouched momentum of the debut quartet.

It was an achievement reached **after the group had already been reconstructed**.

Success could have frozen the six-member lineup into a new sacred form.

Instead, the group disrupted itself again.

### Key Moment 3
**2014日本武道館――Success Does Not Freeze the Form**

---

## 4. 2014――Succession before departure

On September 24, 2014, Wada and Fukuda announced two major changes at once: adding third-generation members from Hello! Pro Kenshusei and changing the group name.

This announcement is central to understanding ANGERME.

二人は、2期メンバーが「初期メンバーが卒業したらグループをどうしていこう」と不安を口にすることがあった、と公式文面で説明している。

So they consulted staff and decided to bring in new members.

This was more than emergency rebranding.

**The founders began designing a group that could survive the founders while the founders were still present.**

In organizational terms, this is succession before departure.

室田瑞希、相川茉穂、佐々木莉佳子が3期として加入。

Then in December, after more than 10,000 name proposals, the group became **ANGERME**, a coined word combining the French *ange* (angel) and *larme* (tear), proposed by second-generation member Kana Nakanishi.

That detail matters.

The new name was not imposed by the original four.

**Brand authorship had already moved beyond the founders.**

### Key Moment 4
**スマイレージ→アンジュルム――Succession Before Departure**

---

## 5. 「大器晩成」――The production system changes too

ANGERME’s first single under the new name arrived on February 4, 2015: 「大器晩成／乙女の逆襲」.

The transformation was not cosmetic.

「大器晩成」 was written and composed by Takui Nakajima.

「乙女の逆襲」 had lyrics by Ameko Kodama and music by Hiroshi Kawanabe and Tadashi Ueda.

The early S/mileage catalog had been strongly centered on Tsunku’s songwriting.

Now, **multiple writers could participate in defining what ANGERME sounded like**.

The title 「大器晩成」 also fit the moment almost suspiciously well.

A quartet had broken, a six-member group had rebuilt itself, the lineup had expanded to nine, and even the name had changed.

Instead of apologizing for instability, the group made delayed growth its banner.

同年の日本武道館映像作品は冒頭から「S/mileage→ANGERME」と明示する。

The past was not erased.

**The old history was attached to a new operating identity.**

### Key Moment 5
**大器晩成――New Name, New Authors, Same History**

---

## 6. After 2015――Never declare a final lineup

The real significance of becoming ANGERME was not simply becoming a nine-member group.

It was refusing to treat those nine as the final answer.

上國料萌衣が2015年、笠原桃奈が2016年に加入。

In the 2017 Hello! Project reorganization, Musubu Funaki joined from Country Girls while Ayano Kawamura was promoted from Kenshusei.

その後も伊勢鈴蘭、橋迫鈴、川名凜、為永幸音、松本わかな、平山遊季、下井谷幸穂、後藤花、長野桃羽と加入は続く。

The recruitment routes also diversified:

- open auditions,
- Kenshusei promotion,
- transfer / concurrent membership from another group.

ANGERME became less a system that asks “which generation number comes next?” and more a system that asks **“what kind of person does the current group need, and where can we find them?”**

But flexibility has a cost.

人数が増えれば関係性を把握する難度は上がる。

Repeated graduations can also create emotional fatigue: the form a fan has just learned to love changes again.

Moe Kamikokuryo acknowledged this directly in her graduation announcement, noting that the short interval between graduations might be painful for fans.

Institutionalized change does not remove the pain of change.

**It creates a culture capable of carrying that pain into the next formation.**

---

## 7. Ayaka Wada graduates――Founder exit without brand collapse

On June 18, 2019, Ayaka Wada graduated at Nippon Budokan.

She was the final original S/mileage member.

This could have been an identity crisis.

But the succession work had started five years earlier:

- add a third generation,
- change the name,
- diversify writers,
- keep recruitment open.

By the time Wada left, ANGERME was already larger than “Ayaka Wada’s group.”

竹内朱莉がリーダーを引き継いだ。

A second-generation member inherited the organization from the founder generation.

**The 2014 succession design had become operational reality.**

### Key Moment 6
**和田彩花卒業――Founder Exit Without Brand Collapse**

---

## 8. Takeuchi → Kamikokuryo → Ise――Leadership moves beyond founder memory

Akari Takeuchi graduated at Yokohama Arena on June 21, 2023.

Moe Kamikokuryo then became the third leader.

ここで重要なのは、上國料がスマイレージ時代を経験していないことだ。

She joined in 2015, after the ANGERME name already existed.

So direct memory of S/mileage was no longer a qualification for leadership.

上國料も2025年6月18日に横浜アリーナで卒業した。

As of 2026, the official lineup lists Layla Ise as leader and Shion Tamenaga as subleader.

The nine current members are:

- 伊勢鈴蘭
- 為永幸音
- 橋迫鈴
- 川名凜
- 松本わかな
- 平山遊季
- 下井谷幸穂
- 後藤花
- 長野桃羽

**No current member performed as S/mileage.**

The people who decided the name change are gone too.

The original nine-member ANGERME lineup is gone.

Yet ANGERME functions as an ordinary active group.

2026年7月には37thシングル「BaBaBa Burning Love！／愛が愛のままでいられますように」をリリースした。

This is a twelve-year-later answer to the 2014 fear: *what happens after the founders leave?*

### Key Moment 7
**伊勢鈴蘭体制――The Successor No Longer Needs Founder Memory**

---

## 9. How this differs from Morning Musume

Morning Musume and ANGERME both use joins and graduations.

But their continuity architectures are different.

モーニング娘。は、**名前と基本制度を強く固定し、人を入れ替える**。

S/mileage / ANGERME has been willing to change:

- people,
- group size,
- name,
- songwriting network,
- live image,
- leadership style.

If Morning Musume resembles a durable operating system whose users change, ANGERME resembles **an operating system that can update its own architecture**.

There are fewer sacred components.

That makes identity harder to define, but it also makes more things replaceable.

The inheritance is not a fixed answer.

**The inheritance is an update procedure.**

---

## 10. “ANGERME-like” became an attitude, not a shape

It would make little sense to ask the 2026 lineup to reproduce the exact mood of the 2009 quartet.

Preserving 「夢見る 15歳」 as a museum object is not the same as preserving the organization.

What has actually been inherited is more abstract:

**make the current members visible as individuals;**

**treat graduation as the entrance to a new configuration;**

**change the number, sound, and structure when necessary;**

**build the present group’s answer instead of merely reenacting the seniors’ answer.**

After enough cycles, a paradox appears:

> **The most stable ANGERME trait is the willingness to become different again.**

---

## Survival model: `renewal_capacity_survives`

Across this series, we have asked what remains when people, groups, or eras end.

For ANGERME, the durable asset is not one stable form.

**The durable asset is the ability to redesign the form.**

Call this `renewal_capacity_survives`.

Definition:

> **A group does not sanctify its current brand, size, production model, or leader; instead, it introduces successors before crisis, dismantles even successful forms when necessary, and creates continuity through repeated redesign.**

This resembles Country Musume’s `brand_survives_by_redefinition`, but the mechanism is different.

Country allowed operational gaps and preserved a name that could later receive new meaning.

ANGERME stayed active and **rewrote its meaning while still running**.

This is not preservation followed by reboot.

It is continuous transformation.

---

## 結論――ANGERME perfected the art of not being finished

S/mileage began with four people.

Those four people were compelling enough to become a permanent answer.

But that is not what happened.

The group added members.

Members graduated.

Six people reached Budokan.

More members joined.

The name changed.

The songwriting network widened.

The founder graduated.

A second-generation member became leader.

A member who had never been in S/mileage became leader.

And in 2026, a lineup with no S/mileage-era member continues as ANGERME.

The history suggests a broader idea:

> **Succession is not only the preservation of an old form.**

Sometimes succession means:

> **the previous generation dismantles its own “completed” form so the next generation has room to build another one.**

The greatest work created by S/mileage / ANGERME over roughly seventeen years may not be one lineup or one song.

It is **an organizational culture that can begin the next unfinished version as soon as the current version starts to look complete.**
