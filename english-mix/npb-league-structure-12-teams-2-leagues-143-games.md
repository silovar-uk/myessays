# なぜNPBは12球団・2リーグ・143試合なのか / Why NPB Has 12 Teams, Two Leagues and 143 Games
## Read the league format as an architecture of competition

第1回で覚えたNPBの骨格は、**12 teams / two leagues / 143 games**だった。

Until now, we treated those numbers as givens.

今回は、その前提を疑う。

**Why 12? Why two? Why 143?**

The key is that these numbers do not share one origin.

- **two leagues** came from the postwar reorganization in 1949.
- **12 teams** emerged after expansion and mergers through the 1950s.
- **143 games** is today's scheduling formula.

NPB's current shape is not a law of nature.

It is a **league format that has been designed, revised, and negotiated over time**.

> **Information baseline: August 31, 2026.**
>
> Historical sections use archived sources. Current game-count and postseason rules use 2026 NPB information.

## 1. SHORT ANSWER — 12, two, and 143 have different histories

一番大事なのは、

**two leagues did not automatically create 12 teams and 143 games.**

1950年、最初の2リーグシーズンはCentral 8 teams and Pacific 7 teams — 15 in total.

試合数も固定ではない。2005年は136試合、2014年は144試合だった。

So we read the system in this order:

**HISTORY → TEAM COUNT → SCHEDULE → POSTSEASON**

## 2. 1949年までは「8球団・1リーグ」だった

In 1949, Japanese professional baseball still had one league with eight clubs.

NPB's historical standings place the Giants, Braves, Stars, Hawks, Dragons, Tigers, Flyers and Robins in one table.

今なら別リーグにいる球団の系譜が、同じ順位表で競っていた。

[NPB 1949 yearly standings](https://npb.jp/bis/yearly/yakyuremmei_1949.html)

That one-league system changed after the 1949 season.

## 3. なぜ2リーグになったのか

The Baseball Hall of Fame and Museum in Japan explains that, as professional baseball regained popularity after the war and more teams wanted to join, an American-style two-league structure came to be seen as desirable for further expansion.

1949年11月26日、太平洋野球連盟が結成。12月15日にはCentral Leagueが7球団で発足し、翌1月に国鉄が加わって8球団となった。

[Baseball Hall of Fame Museum: one-league era](https://baseball-museum.or.jp/collection_list/poster/)  
[NPB Pacific League history](https://npb.jp/pl/history.html)  
[NPB Central League history](https://npb.jp/cl/history.html)

But do not reduce this to a simple efficiency story.

人気拡大、新規参入、企業間の利害、player recruitment competition などが絡む再編だった。

For this article, the important point is simpler:

**the league boundary itself was redesigned.**

## 4. 1950年、2リーグは「8＋7＝15球団」で始まった

The first two-league season did not look like today's NPB.

Central League: **8 clubs**.  
Pacific League: **7 clubs**.

Total: **15**.

[NPB 1950 Central League](https://npb.jp/bis/yearly/centralleague_1950.html)  
[NPB 1950 Pacific League](https://npb.jp/bis/yearly/pacificleague_1950.html)

This corrects an easy misconception:

**the birth of two leagues was not the birth of the 12-team system.**

## 5. 15球団は、1958年までに「6＋6」へ収束した

The 1950s were unstable in terms of club count.

- 1950: Central 8 / Pacific 7
- 1951: 7 / 7
- 1953: 6 / 7
- 1954: 6 / 8
- 1957: 6 / 7
- 1958: 6 / 6

Mergers involving Nishinippon and Nishitetsu, Taiyo and Shochiku, Takahashi and Daiei, and Mainichi and Daiei gradually changed the map.

[Baseball Hall of Fame Museum: professional baseball in the 1950s](https://baseball-museum.or.jp/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/OB-NEWS-64.pdf)

So 12 was not a number selected first as a proven optimum.

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
現在の12球団制は、a historical outcome shaped by entry, mergers, and business continuity として読む方が正確である。
<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
The first two-league season had 15 clubs, and the total changed repeatedly before reaching six plus six in 1958.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
That means league membership is an institutional variable, not a permanent natural condition.
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
To ask why NPB has 12 teams is also to ask who can own, enter, finance, and sustain a professional club.

That business question returns later in the series.

## 6. 2004年にも「12球団」は揺れた

The 12-team structure was challenged again in 2004.

オリックス・ブルーウェーブと大阪近鉄バファローズの統合が承認され、日本プロ野球選手会は史上初のストライキを行った。

Later that year, Rakuten's new club was approved, and the 2005 season again opened with six clubs in each league.

[NPB Pacific League history: 2004–2005](https://npb.jp/pl/history.html)

We are not doing the full 2004 realignment story here.

The lesson is that **12 teams is a rule-shaped structure, not an untouchable constant**.

## 7. では、143試合はどこから来るのか

Now we switch from history to arithmetic.

For 2026:

### Same-league games

Five league rivals × 25 games each.

**5 × 25 = 125**.

### Interleague play

Six clubs in the other league × 3 games each.

**6 × 3 = 18**.

Therefore:

**125 + 18 = 143 games.**

[NPB 2026 Central schedule note](https://npb.jp/games/2026/schedule_note_cl.html)  
[NPB 2026 Pacific schedule note](https://npb.jp/games/2026/schedule_note_pl.html)

There is no mystical meaning in 143.

It is the total produced by the current **schedule architecture**.

## 8. 25試合は奇数なので、ホームとビジターは完全に半分にならない

Twenty-five is odd.

So one club hosts 13 games in a league matchup, while the other hosts 12.

2026年はleague home gamesが62または63。そこにinterleague home games 9試合が加わり、total home gamesは71または72になる。

That means 143 games do not split perfectly into equal home and road totals every year.

The allocation is balanced across seasons.

This is the kind of design detail hidden by the headline number “143.”

## 9. そもそも143試合も、ずっと同じではない

Current format is not ancient tradition.

2005年は136試合制。

In 2014, Central League clubs played 144 regular-season games, including 24 interleague games.

From 2015, interleague play moved to 18 games per club, creating the structure that leads to today's 143-game total.

[NPB Pacific history](https://npb.jp/pl/history.html)  
[NPB 2014 Central standings](https://npb.jp/bis/2014/stats/std_c.html)  
[NPB 2015 interleague format](https://npb.jp/interleague/2015/information.html)

So 143 is also a policy choice.

**If it is designed, it can be redesigned.**

## 10. 交流戦は、分かれていた2リーグをつなぐ

Interleague play began in 2005.

The first edition gave every team 36 interleague games, 216 games league-wide.

当時の両リーグ会長コメントでは、普段対戦しない相手との新鮮さ、パ・リーグの存在感、プロ野球人気の拡大、新しい魅力への期待が語られている。

[NPB Interleague 2005](https://npb.jp/interleague/2005/)  
[NPB 2005 league presidents' comments](https://npb.jp/interleague/2005/news1.html)

Interleague did not erase the two-league structure.

It created a bridge across it.

That is an important design option: **keep categories, but allow limited crossover**.

## 11. クライマックスシリーズは「143試合の後」に、もう一つの競争を置く

The Climax Series began in both leagues in 2007.

[NPB history](https://npb.jp/archives/japanesebaseball150th/history/)

This creates two competitive layers:

**REGULAR SEASON**  
143 games decide the standings.

↓

**POSTSEASON**  
The top three clubs enter a shorter elimination path toward the Japan Series.

A long season and a short postseason reward slightly different things.

That tension is part of the league format.

## 12. 2026年のCS改定を見ると「何を公平とするか」が見える

From 2026, the Final Stage rules changed in certain cases.

Normally, the first-place club begins with a one-win advantage.

But if either condition applies — a regular-season gap of 10 games or more, or a First Stage winner below .500 — the first-place club receives a two-win advantage and the Final Stage can extend to seven games.

[NPB 2026 Climax Series rules](https://npb.jp/games/2026/info_cs.html)

The interesting question is not simply “good rule or bad rule?”

It is the system-design tension:

- **How much should a 143-game first-place finish be rewarded?**
- **How much postseason upset potential should remain?**

League design has to answer both.

## 13. 日程は、ただのカレンダーではない

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
A schedule is not merely a calendar. It is part of the competitive rules.
<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
In 2026, clubs play 125 same-league games but only 18 interleague games, so exposure to opponents is intentionally uneven by category.
<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
That makes repeated same-league matchups the main engine of league standings, while interleague play becomes a limited cross-league comparison.
<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Game allocation therefore influences not only competitive structure but also which rivalries and stories are repeatedly produced.

From a sports-business angle, one more game also means one more inventory unit for ticketing, food and beverage, sponsorship exposure, media and content.

But **more games do not automatically mean more profit**.

Player workload, demand, travel, broadcast scheduling and calendar density also matter.

We will test those economics later in the Business phase.

## 14. 30秒でリーグ構造を読むなら

Use four questions.

1. **WHO — Who can participate?**  
   12 clubs.

2. **WHO PLAYS WHOM — Who plays whom, and how often?**  
   125 same-league games + 18 interleague games.

3. **WHAT COUNTS — What determines the standings?**  
   The 143-game regular season.

4. **WHAT HAPPENS NEXT — How is the final representative decided?**  
   Climax Series, then Japan Series.

With these four questions, a league stops looking like a list of teams.

It becomes an **architecture of competition**.

## 15. Takeaways

1. **Two leagues and 12 teams did not arrive at the same time.** The 1950 season began with 15 clubs; six plus six emerged by 1958.
2. **143 is today's schedule formula, not a permanent tradition.** 125 same-league games + 18 interleague games.
3. **League format is a designed rule system.** Interleague play and postseason rules show that who plays whom, how often, and how regular-season performance is rewarded can all change.

## Next Question

Now we know a league designs:

**who participates, who plays whom, how often, and how winners are determined.**

The next question is about the most important scarce resource on the field.

**How are players allocated among clubs?**

第5回は、

**「ドラフトとは何を配分する制度なのか」**。

We will read the draft not merely as a player-selection event, but as a system for **allocating scarce talent across competing clubs**.
