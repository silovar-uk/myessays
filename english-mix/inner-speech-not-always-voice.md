# Is Your Inner Voice Really a Voice?
## 頭の中のことばを捕まえる

いま、この文章を読んでいる。

Can you hear it inside your head?

自分の声かもしれない。

Maybe it has no clear voice at all.

あるいは、文字の意味だけが入ってきていて、nothing is actually “speaking.”

I used to treat thinking and talking in my head as almost the same thing.

ところが、inner experienceをランダムな瞬間で切り取る研究を見ると、その前提が崩れる。

In a 2008 study, Christopher Heavey and Russell Hurlburt sampled 10 random moments from each of 30 college students.

**Inner speech appeared in 26% of sampled moments on average.**

個人差は0〜75％。

Five participants reported no inner speech in any of their ten sampled moments.

That does **not** mean humans think verbally only 26% of the time.

30人×10回というspecific sampleで、Descriptive Experience Samplingという特定のmethodを使った結果にすぎない。

But it is enough to break one assumption:

**thinking is not always the same as silently saying sentences.**

[Heavey & Hurlburt, “The phenomena of inner experience,” 2008](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1053810008000032)

---

## 1. What psychologists mean by “inner speech” / 内言とは何か

Psychology usually calls the phenomenon **inner speech**.

2015年の包括的reviewでは、声として外に発音されないlanguage experienceとして扱われている。

You may also see terms such as verbal thinking, inner speaking, covert self-talk, internal monologue, and internal dialogue.

すでに名前が多い。

And the experience itself is not one thing either.

たとえば、

「明日、朝一でメールを返して、そのあと資料を直そう」

というfull sentenceに近い思考。

Or maybe just:

「メール、朝、先」

The review distinguishes **expanded inner speech** from **condensed inner speech**.

前者はordinary speechに近い。

The latter can be compressed—meaning arrives without a fully formed sentence.

So the word “voice” may already be misleading.

内言は必ずしも、頭の中のaudio trackではない。

[Alderson-Day & Fernyhough, “Inner Speech: Development, Cognitive Functions, Phenomenology, and Neurobiology,” 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4538954/)

---

## 2. Sometimes the mind talks back / 頭の中は一人語りとも限らない

Inner speech can also be **dialogic**.

「これでいいか」

“No, not yet.”

「何が足りない？」

Maybe one mind can temporarily hold multiple conversational positions.

2016年のneuroimaging studyでは、monologic inner speechとdialogic inner speechを分けて調べている。

Questionnaires about inner speech have also distinguished features such as dialogicality, evaluation or motivation, other people's voices, and condensation.

つまりinner voiceは、必ずしも「自分が自分に説明する声」ではない。

It can argue.

It can rehearse someone else's words.

It can collapse into fragments.

“Voice” starts looking less like a person and more like a **flexible language format**.

[Alderson-Day et al., “The brain’s conversation with itself,” 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4692319/)

[“The Varieties of Inner Speech Questionnaire – Revised,” 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6204885/)

---

## 3. Asking “How often do you have an inner voice?” may be the wrong method

There is an obvious research problem.

内言は外から見えない。

You cannot record it with a microphone.

So researchers often have to ask the person.

でも、人は自分の1日をそんなに正確には覚えていない。

“I always think in words” may be a self-image rather than an accurate count.

そこでHurlburtらが使ってきたのが**Descriptive Experience Sampling, or DES**.

Participants carry a device that beeps at random moments.

合図が鳴ったら、その直前に直接経験していたものを記録する。

Later, researchers interview them about that exact moment.

It is almost like trying to catch thought **red-handed**.

もちろんDESにも限界がある。

Interviewing, interpretation and introspection all create methodological problems.

Still, experience-sampling research suggests that broad questionnaires can sometimes produce higher estimates of inner speech than moment-by-moment sampling.

「普段の自分はこうだ」と「いまの一瞬に実際に何があった」は、同じ質問ではない。

[Hurlburt, “Exploring Inner Experience”](https://hurlburt.faculty.unlv.edu/sampling.html)

[“Toward a phenomenology of inner speaking,” 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4410513/)

---

## 4. What if someone barely has inner speech? / ほとんど内言がない人

A 2024 paper gave this question a memorable name.

Nedergaard and Lupyan proposed **anendophasia** for the experience of little or no inner speech.

自己報告でinner speechが少ない46人と、多い47人を比較した。

The low-inner-speech group performed worse on a verbal working-memory task and had more difficulty with rhyme judgments.

一方、task switchingや一部のperceptual categorizationでは差がなかった。

That matters.

Low inner speech does not mean “low thinking.”

Rather, silent language may be useful for particular cognitive jobs—holding verbal material, comparing sounds, rehearsing words.

But the strongest claim remains debated.

2025年には、「low inner speech」は示しても、**zero inner speech**を実証したとは言えないというcommentaryが出た。

In 2026, Hurlburt responded by arguing that studying the fidelity of lived experience requires methods different from validating a psychological construct, and suggested that mostly anendophasic individuals do exist.

So “anendophasia” is useful, but the science is not finished.

人をcleanly「inner voiceあり／なし」に二分する段階ではない。

[Nedergaard & Lupyan, “Not Everybody Has an Inner Voice,” 2024](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/09567976241243004)

[Lind, “Are There Really People With No Inner Voice?,” 2025](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/09567976251335583/)

[Hurlburt, “Fidelity Versus Validity Using Anendophasia as an Example,” 2026](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/09567976251413525/)

## 5. Can thought exist without words? / 言葉なしで考えられるか

This question is annoying in a beautiful way.

「言葉では考えていなかった」と説明した瞬間、we have already turned it into words.

Hurlburt and colleagues use the term **unsymbolized thinking** for experiences in which a person reports having a specific, differentiated thought without experiencing words, images, or other symbols at that moment.

たとえば「彼はいま何をしようとしているんだろう」というmeaningは明確にある。

But the sentence itself was never internally spoken.

This idea is controversial and tightly connected to debates about introspective method.

だから「unsymbolized thinkingは確実にこういうものだ」と決めつけるべきではない。

Still, it reminds us of something simple:

**no inner sentence does not automatically mean no thought.**

思考はlanguageだけでなく、images, spatial relations, sensations, emotions, bodily statesなど多様な形式を使える。

[Hurlburt & Akhter, “Unsymbolized thinking,” 2008](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1053810008000524)

## 6. A one-day experiment / 自分のインナーボイスを10回だけ捕まえる

Instead of asking,

“Do I have an inner voice?”

もう少し細かく聞いたほうがよさそうだ。

For one day, set 10 random alarms.

鳴ったら、**直前**に頭にあったものだけを書く。

- Was it a full sentence? / 完全文だったか
- Just words or fragments? / 単語や断片だけか
- Was there an audible quality? / 声らしい音質はあったか
- Were you speaking or hearing? / 話す感じか、聞く感じか
- Monologue or dialogue? / 独白か対話か
- Image, feeling, body sensation? / 言葉以外だったか
- Meaning without a clear form? / 内容だけある感じか

This is not a scientific diagnosis or test.

DESを雑に借りたself-observationにすぎない。

But it may be better than asking your memory to summarize an entire life.

---

## 7. The inner voice may be a tool, not a resident / 頭の中の住人ではなく道具

At first, I imagined an inner voice as a tiny narrator living inside the head.

ずっと自分に向かってしゃべっている存在。

Now it looks different.

Inner speech can be full or compressed.

独白にも対話にもなる。

Its frequency varies dramatically.

ほとんど使わない人もいる可能性が高い。

And thought does not disappear when words do.

So maybe “inner voice” sounds too permanent.

It may be better understood as a **language mode that thought can recruit**.

考えを保持する。

Give yourself an instruction.

会話を予行する。

Argue with yourself.

言葉を並べ替える。

At those moments, thought borrows the shape of speech.

だから面白い問いは、

**“Do I have an inner voice?”**

ではなく、

**“When does my thinking choose the form of a voice?”**

なのかもしれない。

I went looking for a person inside the head.

見つかったのはpersonではなかった。

It was one format among many that thinking can use.
