# Three months after Europe, 6 of Villa's starting XI are already gone

## 今のAston Villaは完成形の続きではなく、same operating system with a rebuilt cast

May 20, 2026. Aston Villa beat Freiburg 3-0 in Istanbul and won the UEFA Europa League.

The starting XI was Martínez; Cash, Konsa, Pau Torres, Digne; Lindelöf, Tielemans; McGinn, Rogers, Buendía; Watkins.

September 19.

**Six of those eleven players are no longer Aston Villa players.**

Emiliano Martínez moved to Chelsea. Ezri Konsa to Arsenal. Lucas Digne to Paris Saint-Germain. Youri Tielemans to Manchester United. Morgan Rogers to Chelsea. Ollie Watkins to Al Hilal.

That leaves five of the Europa League final starters. A simple headcount gives a retention rate of about 45%.

This is the fastest route to understanding Villa right now.

They are still a strong club built under Unai Emery. But they are not simply continuing with the same team and adding one or two upgrades.

**The manager and the footballing ideas remain. Many of the people executing those ideas have changed.**

今季のヴィラは、この二つを同時に見る必要がある。

---

## 1. FACT｜Last season was fourth place plus a European trophy

まず、what exactly are they rebuilding from?

Villa finished the 2025/26 Premier League season fourth with 65 points. Four days before the league season ended, they beat Freiburg 3-0 to win the Europa League, the club's first major trophy in 30 years.

They also finished the Europa League as the competition's top-scoring side with 31 goals.

[Aston Villa: Bizot on an “absolutely unbelievable” season](https://www.avfc.co.uk/news/2026/may/25/postmatch-bizot-on--absolutely-unbelievable--season/)
[UEFA: Freiburg 0-3 Aston Villa](https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuropaleague/news/02a5-20ac3759bba4-7f91e47690b9-1000/)

So this is not a rebuild that followed obvious failure.

**A major reset arrived immediately after major success.**

That creates the confusing part. The label says “fourth-place side, Europa League winners.” The passing relationships, defensive references and attacking connections underneath that label are much less continuous.

---

## 2. ANALYSIS｜They did not only lose six players; they lost connection points from goalkeeper to striker

Six departures sounds like a transfer-window statistic.

Look at the roles and it becomes more meaningful.

- GK: Emiliano Martínez
- CB: Ezri Konsa
- LB: Lucas Digne
- CM: Youri Tielemans
- AM/FW: Morgan Rogers
- CF: Ollie Watkins

A goalkeeper who starts possession. A centre-back and a left-back. A midfielder who progresses the ball. An attacker who receives and carries between lines. The central striker.

**The changes run through the whole pitch.**

これは解釈であって、the club has not publicly described these six departures as one coordinated replacement package.

But role by role, it explains why “the players are good, so it should work immediately” is too simple.

A new goalkeeper has to learn where the centre-backs want the ball. New midfielders need to recognise the receiving angles. A new striker has to know when the pass is coming into feet and when it is coming into space.

Football is not only a sum of individual quality.

It is also **prediction between teammates**.

---

## 3. OVERKILL RESEARCH｜In May, Manzambi was trying to beat Villa. In September, he started Villa's first league win

Here is the strangest detail.

In the Europa League final on May 20, Johan Manzambi started for Freiburg.

Against Aston Villa.

About two months later, he signed for Aston Villa.

Then on September 19, on his first Premier League start, Manzambi scored just before half-time against Tottenham. Nicolas Jackson and Emi Buendía added two more, and Villa won 3-2 for their first league victory of the season.

So:

**A player who tried to stop Villa becoming European champions scored the opening goal of Villa's first Premier League win 122 days later.**

移籍市場、展開が速すぎる。

[Premier League: Manzambi squad number confirmed](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4679970/manzambi-squad-number-confirmed)
[Reuters: Manzambi, Jackson and Buendia fire Villa to first win](https://www.reuters.com/sports/soccer/wrapup-soccer-manzambi-jackson-buendia-fire-villa-first-win-spurs-misery-deepens-2026-09-19/)

It is more than trivia.

今のヴィラは、last season's team plus a few additionsではない。They are building a new combination so quickly that one of last season's final opponents is already inside the new version.

---

## 4. ROSTER｜The rebuild is spread across every line

The 2026/27 Premier League squad list looks very different.

Zion Suzuki is in goal. Defensive options now include Matteo Ruggeri, Taylor Harwood-Bellis and Aaron Wan-Bissaka. Midfield includes João Gomes, Leon Goretzka and Manzambi. Nicolas Jackson has joined the forward line, while Alejandro Garnacho arrived on loan.

[Premier League: 2026/27 squad lists](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4706139/see-all-the-202627-premier-league-squad-lists)

It would be too early to say, “this player replaces Tielemans” or “that player replaces Rogers.”

Emery's teams are role-sensitive. Even when the formation graphic says 4-2-3-1, the actual positions change with the opponent and phase.

事実として言えるのは、new personnel have arrived at goalkeeper, defence, midfield and attack.

The interpretation is that Villa do not need one replacement.

**They need several new relationships to become reliable at the same time.**

---

## 5. RESULTS｜Four points from five league games, but a Champions League away win

Has the rebuild worked yet?

In the Premier League, the early answer is: not consistently.

Villa opened with a 4-0 defeat at Brighton. They then lost 1-0 to Arsenal, drew 0-0 at Hull, and lost 2-1 at home to Nottingham Forest.

September 19 finally brought the first league win: Tottenham 2-3 Aston Villa.

After five matches, Villa had one win, one draw and three defeats: four points. Immediately after the Spurs match, that put them 15th.

[Reuters: Villa first Premier League win at Tottenham](https://www.reuters.com/sports/soccer/wrapup-soccer-manzambi-jackson-buendia-fire-villa-first-win-spurs-misery-deepens-2026-09-19/)

But Europe tells a different early-season story.

On September 8, Villa won 3-2 away at Club Brugge in their Champions League opener. John McGinn scored the first goal of the entire 2026/27 Champions League league phase.

[UEFA: Champions League opening scorers](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0219-0e8c384ee79a-e94c0f9968ee-1000--champions-league-opening-scorers-promise-david-scores-firs/)
[UEFA: Matchday 1 round-up](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/02a9-218b8c00ab1e-0e070e496ca8-1000--champions-league-matchday-1-highlights-and-round-up-real-mad/)

Three league games without a goal. Three goals away in Europe. Then three goals at Tottenham.

この振れ幅を、“actually good” or “actually bad” の二択で急いで片付ける必要はない。

At this point, the cleaner conclusion is:

**the new version has not become repeatable yet.**

---

## 6. TACTICS｜The Emery operating system is still recognisable

The largest piece of continuity is Unai Emery.

UEFA's technical analysis of Villa's 4-0 Europa League semi-final win over Nottingham Forest highlighted verticality, forward play, immediate pressure after losing possession and tactical flexibility.

Villa's nominal 4-2-3-1 often became a 3-2-5 or 2-3-5 in possession. Narrow attacking midfielders occupied central spaces, full-backs provided width, and the team looked to accelerate through lines after drawing the opponent toward the ball.

[UEFA: Emery's tactical adjustments](https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuropaleague/news/02a5-20997d1f4081-9b506d718627-1000--europa-league-performance-insights-how-unai-emery-s-tactical/)
[UEFA: Villa's speed in attack and change of tempo](https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuropaleague/news/02a5-20936ad2131d-349a0cb7f8a3-1000--europa-league-tactical-analysis-aston-villa-s-speed-in-a/)

The Premier League has also described a recurring Emery pattern: invite pressure in deeper areas, then suddenly play vertically once the opponent commits.

[Premier League: A year under Emery](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/3750635)

でも、last season's tactical diagram cannot simply be pasted onto this season's squad.

Tielemans, Rogers and Watkins are gone. Even if the geometry is similar, the player who creates time, receives between lines or attacks the space may be different.

So rather than asking only “Is it 4-2-3-1?”, watch the functions:

- Can Villa draw the press without losing control?
- When the vertical pass arrives, are the next two movements connected?
- Is the rest defence ready when an attack breaks down?
- Do the new forwards recognise Emery's moment to accelerate?

**The formation is the screenshot. The timing is the software.**

## 7. ZION SUZUKI｜For a Japanese viewer, the rebuild has a very direct entry point

There is one particularly interesting route into this team from Japan: Zion Suzuki.

Villa signed Suzuki permanently from Parma in August. Reuters noted that the 23-year-old Japan international began his professional career at Urawa Red Diamonds, then played for Sint-Truiden before making 59 appearances across two seasons at Parma.

[Reuters: Villa bolster squad with Suzuki and Ruggeri](https://www.reuters.com/sports/soccer/villa-bolster-squad-with-arrivals-suzuki-ruggeri-2026-08-19/)

Martínez later moved to Chelsea.

つまり鈴木が入った夏は、単なるbackup goalkeeper signingではない。The position that had contained one of Villa's strongest personalities and most important leaders changed.

For an Emery team, goalkeeper work is not only shot-stopping. The goalkeeper participates in how the side invites pressure and chooses the first route forward.

So with Suzuki, watch more than saves.

Where does he stand? Does he go short or long? How long does he hold the ball before releasing it? What distance does he keep from the centre-backs?

**He is one of the clearest places to watch the new Villa learning itself.**

---

## 8. WATCH｜Four connections tell you more than a list of new signings

If you want to understand this Aston Villa week by week, watch four connections.

### ① Goalkeeper → centre-backs → midfield

How does the new back unit create the first progression after the Martínez era?

### ② Midfield → attacking midfield

After Tielemans and Rogers, who delivers the ball between lines, and who can turn forward?

### ③ Attacking midfield → striker

Watkins used to combine running behind, link play and finishing. How do the new forwards divide those functions? Against Tottenham, the three goals were shared by Manzambi, Jackson and Buendía.

### ④ Attack → counterpress

The best Emery Villa could attack while already preparing for the next defensive action. Are enough players close to the ball when possession is lost?

The goal is not to become last year's Villa again.

**The goal is for the 2026/27 version to become coherent on its own terms.**

---

## 9. DISCOVERY｜This is not simply “European champions in bad form”

Before looking closely, current Aston Villa can feel contradictory.

Fourth in the Premier League last season. Europa League winners. Unai Emery still in charge.

Yet this season began without a league win, and without a goal in the first three league matches.

What happened?

“Bad form” is true at one level, but incomplete.

Six of the Europa League final starters left. The goalkeeper changed. A centre-back changed. The left-back changed. The midfield distributor changed. A major attacking carrier changed. The record Premier League scorer left.

At the same time, Emery's footballing logic survived: attract, progress vertically, change speed, press immediately after losing the ball.

So the real question is not whether Villa can reproduce last season's finished product.

It is:

**How quickly can the same operating system create a different finished product with new people?**

On September 19, Manzambi scored the opening goal of Villa's first league win.

Three months earlier, he was standing on the other side of the Europa League final.

That is current Aston Villa in one image.

Not a completed great team simply continuing.

Not a failed team starting from zero.

**A successful football idea rebuilding its human connections at extremely high speed.**

完成していない。だから今見ると面白い。
