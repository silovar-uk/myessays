# 六つの「fu」――Cantonese and Mandarin, Same Characters, Different Voices

*English Mix／調査基準日：2026年10月26日。Sources include original language-policy documents, official census data, and peer-reviewed linguistics. This is a desk study, not a speaker experiment.*

「夫・虎・副・扶・婦・父」。Six characters, one Roman spelling—**fu**. But the Linguistic Society of Hong Kong writes them **fu1, fu2, fu3, fu4, fu5, fu6**. The numbers mark six different tones, not item numbers. 一つの「fu」に六つの意味を押し込んだというより、声の高さや動きを語の一部として聞き分けている。[Jyutping scheme](https://lshk.org/jyutping-scheme/)

This small surprise opens a bigger puzzle. 広東語と北京語は、漢字を広く共有している。Why, then, can't we assume that two speakers will understand each other's conversation? Because **shared script, shared written comprehension, and mutual spoken intelligibility are three different things.**

## 守｜「北京語」と「普通話」は、厳密には同じ名前ではない

First, name what we are comparing. Cantonese（広東語・粵語）here primarily means the Guangzhou–Hong Kong varieties of Yue Chinese. 「北京語」は日常的な日本語の呼び方だが、この記事では主として**普通話（Putonghua、標準的な中国語の話し言葉）**を指す。Beijing's local dialect and the nationally codified standard are related, but not identical.

The 1956 State Council definition chose Beijing pronunciation as the standard, northern speech as its base, and exemplary modern vernacular prose as a grammatical norm. **A standard is selected and codified; it is not simply everything spoken in a city.** [1956 State Council instruction](https://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xwfb/xw_fbh/moe_2128/moe_2326/moe_1144/tnull_14344.html) / [History of standardization](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-asian-studies/article/what-is-mandarin-the-social-project-of-language-standardization-in-early-republican-china/7AAA7A1CB930C41E850E515EFE89DF14)

Mandarin can mean Standard Mandarin or the wider Mandarin dialect group（官話系統）. Cantonese, too, is not one perfectly uniform accent. The Yue group includes multiple regional varieties. 名前が一つだから、実態も一つとは限らない。[Oxford Handbook of Chinese Linguistics](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/38607/chapter-abstract/334719596)

## 守｜「方言」という一語では、会話の通じなさを説明できない

Both belong to the Sinitic family（シナ語派・漢語派）. They share historical roots and a substantial written tradition, yet they differ in sound systems, vocabulary, and grammar. **Mutual intelligibility（相互理解可能性）** asks whether speakers can understand one another without prior learning or exposure. For Cantonese and Putonghua, that cannot simply be assumed.[Chinese dialect research overview](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/38607/chapter-abstract/334719596)

Does that make them different languages or dialects? Linguistically, treating them as distinct languages has strong grounds. Socially and institutionally, they are also widely classified as Chinese dialects. The label therefore reflects both linguistic distance and history, institutions, and identity. 「方言だから、何となく分かるだろう」は成り立たない。

A critical distinction: **unlearned intelligibility is not the same as acquired bilingual ability.** 香港の人が学校や仕事で普通話を習得することはある。Exposure can bridge a linguistic gap; it does not erase the gap that existed before learning.

## 守｜同じ「中国語」でも、声の高低は装飾ではなく単語の一部になる

Standard Mandarin is commonly described as having four major lexical tones, plus a neutral tone. Cantonese is often described with **six tones** in modern Jyutping, the Cantonese Romanization scheme of the Linguistic Society of Hong Kong. Its famous fu1–fu6 sequence is an official illustration.[Jyutping: tones](https://jyutping.org/en/jyutping/)

But why do some books say Cantonese has *nine* tones? The traditional description counts three **checked-tone categories（入声）** associated with syllables ending in **-p, -t, or -k**. In Jyutping, those are conventionally represented with the corresponding tone numbers 1, 3, and 6. **Six versus nine is largely a matter of phonological classification**, not a sudden discovery of extra everyday tones.[Tone-system comparison](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/13670069231181508)

Cantonese retains many final stop consonants found in historical Middle Chinese（中古漢語）that are no longer preserved the same way in Standard Mandarin. This is valuable historical evidence. It does **not** prove that Cantonese is “the original Chinese” or that Mandarin is somehow an inauthentic replacement. Both have changed over centuries.[Historical phonological discussion](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.langcom.2020.10.004)

## 守｜漢字が同じでも、食事を勧める言葉は驚くほど違う

An academic study discusses a food-waste message written in colloquial Cantonese. Compare two lines:

> **Colloquial written Cantonese:** 食唔晒都唔好嘥  
> **Mandarin-based Standard Written Chinese:** 吃不完也不要浪費  
> **Meaning:** 食べきれないからといって、食べ物を無駄にしないで。

Notice what we did: both lines use **traditional characters（繁体字）**. Yet the words and their structure differ: 食 / 吃, 唔 / 不, 嘥 / 浪費. **The contrast is not merely simplified versus traditional script.** A literate Mandarin speaker may still find colloquial written Cantonese difficult to understand.[Peer-reviewed study of Cantonese vocabulary](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13670050.2022.2116267)

Take a smaller phrase: **我不知道** in Mandarin-based writing, **我唔知** in colloquial Cantonese. Both mean “I don't know.” The same 我 and 知 appear, but negation changes. 目ではつながりが分かる。Yet shared characters do not automatically supply the missing spoken grammar.

## 守｜広東語は、普通話を別の発音で読んでいるだけではない

Differences reach into grammar. Research from the Chinese University of Hong Kong describes characteristic Cantonese features involving **aspect markers（動作の完了・経験などを表す相標識）**, sentence-final particles（終助詞）, comparative constructions, and post-verbal elements. [CUHK grammar research](https://research.cuhk.edu.hk/en/publications/aspects-of-cantonese-grammar-2/)

In other words, translation is not a sound-replacement operation. 広東語の口語には「佢」「哋」「咗」「嘅」などの文字も現れる。Written Cantonese is a socially developed way of expressing actual Cantonese words and sentence patterns—not simply “incorrect standard Chinese.” [Study of written Cantonese](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/de/document/doi/10.1515/glochi-2018-0006/html)

## 守｜同じ香港人でも、話すときと書くときの「中国語」がずれる

Hong Kong reveals a crucial distinction between **spoken language and written register（書き言葉の規範）**. A person may speak Cantonese at home and use Standard Written Chinese for school or official documents. That written Chinese may even be read aloud with Cantonese pronunciation. 一方、広告、会話文、オンラインでは、話し言葉に近い書面広東語もよく使われる。[Donald Snow on written Cantonese](https://benjamins.com/catalog/japc.18.2.05sno)

Simplified versus traditional Chinese is principally a **script-form distinction（漢字の字体の区別）**. Cantonese versus Mandarin is a distinction in linguistic varieties. Taiwan generally uses traditional characters while its standard speech is called 國語（国語）. The two contrasts are not one-to-one.

## 守｜共通語は自然に勝ち残っただけでなく、近代国家が選び取った

A national common language is both linguistic and institutional. China's 1956 directive promoted Putonghua through schooling, publishing, and broadcasting. The **Law on the Standard Spoken and Written Chinese Language**, effective from 2001, specifies Putonghua and standardized Chinese characters for various public uses.[1956 directive](https://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xwfb/xw_fbh/moe_2128/moe_2326/moe_1144/tnull_14344.html) / [National language law](https://en.moe.gov.cn/documents/laws_policies/201506/t20150626_191388.html)

A common language can improve communication across regions. But public utility does not make a local language inferior. **Language status is shaped by institutions and opportunities, not grammatical merit.** 普通話を広める政策と、広東語の文化的・社会的価値の評価は、別の問いとして扱う必要がある。

## 守｜香港では広東語が多数派だが、「話せる」と「いつも使う」は違う

The **2021 Hong Kong Population Census** gives us an important test of assumptions. Among residents aged five or above, **88.2%** reported Cantonese as their usual spoken language; **2.3%** reported Putonghua. But ability to speak either as a usual or additional spoken language reached **93.7%** for Cantonese and **54.2%** for Putonghua.[Official census](https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/data/stat_report/product/B1120109/att/B11201092021XXXXB0100.pdf)

```text
2021, Hong Kong residents aged 5+
                  Usually spoken      Able to speak
Cantonese              88.2%              93.7%
Putonghua               2.3%              54.2%
```

The two columns answer different questions. Everyday use is not ability, and ability is not exclusive mother-tongue status. These are 2021 figures, **not a real-time measure of 2026 or a forecast of language extinction**.

Hong Kong's education policy aims for **biliteracy and trilingualism（両文三語）**: literacy in Chinese and English, and spoken Cantonese, Putonghua, and English. The legally designated official languages are Chinese and English—not a simple official-language listing of Cantonese versus Mandarin.[Education Bureau](https://www.edb.gov.hk/en/edu-system/primary-secondary/secondary/index.html) / [2024 government reply](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202411/06/P2024110600276p.htm)

## 破｜漢字を残して音だけ消すと、どこまで理解は残るのか

A thought experiment: What happens if we remove the sound but keep the writing? We can compare four conditions without pretending to have tested actual respondents.

1. **Standard Written Chinese:** 吃不完也不要浪費 — vocabulary likely more familiar to a Mandarin-only reader.
2. **Colloquial written Cantonese:** 食唔晒都唔好嘥 — shared characters, but less familiar words.
3. **Cantonese audio:** no visual character clues for a Mandarin-only learner.
4. **Jyutping romanization:** tones are encoded, but symbols only help if the reader has learned them.

Hypothesis: **written comprehension and spoken comprehension will not match perfectly**. This is not an empirical result. Education, contact with Cantonese media, and familiarity with either script can change the outcome. A real experiment would randomize presentation modes and control participants' learning histories.

Shared writing is a bridge—but also a potential source of false confidence. 「何となく読める」と「本当に理解できる」の差を、言語の近さそのものが見えにくくする可能性がある。That psychological claim remains a research question, not a tested fact here.

## 破｜「広東語は古い」「普通話は優れている」は、どちらも危うい近道になる

It is true that Cantonese retains certain historical phonological features, especially final -p, -t, and -k. It does not follow that the whole language is an unchanged fossil. Conversely, Putonghua's administrative reach does not mean it is intrinsically more developed than Cantonese. **Historical retention, social prestige, and linguistic complexity are different dimensions.** [Cantonese grammar](https://research.cuhk.edu.hk/en/publications/aspects-of-cantonese-grammar-2/) / [Written-standard study](https://benjamins.com/catalog/japc.18.2.05sno)

The analytical trap is to turn **description into ranking**. 言語を優劣で並べるより、いつ、誰が、どの場面で使い、どのように知識や文化を伝えられるかを見るほうが具体的である。

## 離｜言語を「声・文字・制度」の三層で見ると、日本語の常識も揺らぐ

Other societies also negotiate a distance between regional speech and common written forms: Arabic, German-speaking areas, and even Japanese dialects offer partial parallels. But the historical situations and degrees of mutual intelligibility differ. **Analogy is useful, equivalence is risky.**

Three layers help: **voice（音声）**, **script and writing（文字・書記）**, and **institutions（制度）**. Ask what sound distinctions matter, what texts can be shared, and which language is used in schools or public life. Mixing these layers produces bad shortcuts: same characters means same language; official means superior; dialect means unwritable.

The practical lesson for travelers is modest. 普通話で覚えたフレーズが香港の全会話をそのまま開く鍵だとは思わないこと。ただし香港には普通話や英語で話せる人も多い。Ask what works, rather than deciding what someone must speak.

Finally, the six fu words. At first they looked absurd: six characters, one Roman spelling. Now they reveal a limit in our listening habits. **The tones were always part of the words. It was our definition of “a sound” that had left them out.** 六つの単語が奇妙だったのではない。こちらの物差しが小さかったのだ。

## 資料・方法・未検証の点

Research sequence: verify the official tone notation; check grammar and written-language studies; compare the 1956 policy text and 2021 census; then construct a controlled *desk comparison* using equivalent written examples. No human comprehension testing was conducted. **Beijing dialect ≠ perfectly identical to Standard Mandarin** and **six tones ≠ nine tones as a direct contradiction** were key editorial corrections.
