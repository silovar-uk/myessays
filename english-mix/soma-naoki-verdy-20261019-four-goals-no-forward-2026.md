# Four Goals, No Forward Scorer――相馬直樹は今のヴェルディをどう変えるのか

## まず、得点者の名前が不思議だった

2026/27の東京ヴェルディは開幕10試合で**4得点**。But the names on the scoresheet are **Naoki Hayashi (2), Shuhei Mizoguchi (1), and Yuta Arai (1)**. 前線の主力選手はまだリーグ戦の得点者にいない。[J.League club statistics](https://www.jleague.jp/club/tokyov/)

It is tempting to call this a striker problem. しかしシュートを打てる場所までボールが届かなければ、個人のシュート力は測れない。We need to look at the **process before the finish**, not simply the scorer.

As of **October 19, 2026**, Verdy are **20th, with 0 wins, 4 draws, 6 defeats, four scored and 18 conceded**. 城福浩監督との契約を10月5日に解除し、相馬直樹監督が8日に就任。His first J1 matches ended **0–2 vs Hiroshima** and **0–4 vs FC Tokyo**. [Club results](https://www.jleague.jp/club/tokyov/) / [Coaching change](https://www.verdy.co.jp/news/15272)

この論考は、公式データとクラブ会見を使った事前分析である。No private dressing-room information or full-match independent tracking is claimed.

## 1. A coaching change does not automatically mean a rejection of Jofuku's football

On **October 5**, Verdy ended **Hiroshi Jofuku's** tenure. 城福監督は2022年から指揮し、2023年のＪ１復帰を実現していた。At the time of the change, the club had **four draws, four defeats, four goals and 12 conceded after eight matches**. [Official club announcement](https://www.verdy.co.jp/news/15272)

Yet sporting director **Atsuhiko Ejiri** did not describe a complete ideological break. In the October 9 press conference, he stressed the club's past strengths: several attackers pressing repeatedly, a compact defensive shape, and quick attacks after winning the ball high up the pitch. 相馬監督には、むしろその方向を**継承し、機能させ直すこと**を期待した。[Appointment press conference](https://www.verdy.co.jp/beleza/news/15291)

The board also described a longer-term ambition: regain an aggressive collective press, score from quick transitions, then become more effective when keeping the ball in the attacking half. これは**計画**であって達成済みの成果ではない。[Club statement](https://www.verdy.co.jp/news/15278)

だから検証すべき問いは「新しい戦術を導入したか」だけではない。**What was worth keeping, and which connections between players needed repair?** 交代の目的を正確に読むと、評価の仕方も変わる。

## 2. Soma's past gives experience, not a guarantee

**Naoki Soma**, born July 19, 1971, played **58 senior international matches for Japan**, including the 1998 World Cup. 2002年には東京ヴェルディ1969でもプレーした。As a manager he has led Machida, Kawasaki, Kashima, Omiya and Kagoshima. [Official profile](https://www.verdy.co.jp/staff/98)

In **2018**, Machida finished **fourth in J2 with 21 wins, 13 draws and eight defeats** under Soma. 鹿児島では2025年にゼネラルマネージャーと監督を兼任し、シーズン後に退任した。Both the club and Soma cited disagreements over recruitment-organisation management rather than an explicit disagreement over playing style. [Machida record](https://www.jleague.jp/news/article/13386/) / [Kagoshima announcement](https://kufc.co.jp/2025/11/20/soma-3/)

過去の実績は経験の証拠だが、今回の成功を保証しない。また一クラブでの組織方針の不一致から、本人の性格や他のクラブとの関係を断定してはならない。

At his Verdy unveiling, Soma introduced the phrase **"NO LIMIT!"**. 彼は選手の可能性だけでなく、自分の従来の考え方を修正する必要にも言及している。[Unveiling press conference](https://www.verdy.co.jp/beleza/news/15291) The slogan is a commitment, not a performance indicator.

## 3. The 4-4-2 changes responsibilities, not just positions on a diagram

Jofuku had mainly used a **3-4-2-1**. Soma opened against Hiroshima in a **4-4-2**. 背後の人数を3人から4人へ、前線の基準位置を2人へ変更した。ただし試合中の陣形は守備と攻撃で変わる。[J.League preview](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/101708/)

The two forwards were **Itsuki Someno and Kim Hyun-woo**. 新井悠太は左サイドバックへ。Soma explained that he valued Arai's one-on-one defending and his ability to move forward, even though full-back was not his usual role. Kim was chosen partly for his movement against the opponent's last line. [Hiroshima lineup](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/101103/lineup/) / [Coach's match interview](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/info/2026101103/report)

Two forwards may create more passing outlets, but only if the ball reaches them and support follows. 前線へ長く送っても、その次のこぼれ球を相手に回収されれば、攻撃は終わる。The shape must be judged through **connections between players**, not by the numbers 4-4-2 alone.

## 4. Why a team that wants to press may sometimes choose not to press yet

Here is an apparent contradiction. The club promises a return to energetic pressing, yet defender **Naoki Hayashi** explained the new defensive style as inviting an opponent into an organised area before trying to win the ball. 最初のパスへ無条件に飛び出すより、奪う場所を揃えるイメージだ。[J.League tactical preview](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/101708/)

The two ideas can coexist. **Pressing is not only how hard you run; it is when and where you move together.** 特定のパスが出た瞬間に複数人で圧力を掛ければ、その前にはわざと相手に持たせる場面もある。

This is a **working tactical interpretation**, not a claim that Soma has officially named a particular pressing theory. プレス開始位置や発動回数を実際に測定したわけでもない。The practical question for the next match is straightforward: **what action triggers Verdy's collective press?**

## 5. The Hiroshima match exposed a second-half problem

Soma's first match ended **Verdy 0–2 Hiroshima on October 11**. The teams were level at half-time, but Hiroshima scored in minutes **47 and 58**. [Official match record](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/101103/)

Soma said Verdy showed forward intent in the first half. 後半には守備ラインが下がり、最初の攻撃のプレーで前へ進めず、後方へのパスが増えたと説明した。He also acknowledged the opponent's tactical adjustments. [Post-match conference](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/info/2026101103/report)

One coaching decision is particularly revealing. Forward **Kazunari Ichimi** came on and was later substituted off again. 相馬監督は選手の状態を読み違えたとして、起用判断について自分の問題を認めた。He also acknowledged that he had not yet learned every player's characteristics. [Verdy account](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/info/2026101103/report)

A newly appointed coach must do two jobs simultaneously: teach new principles and learn how his own players perform under match pressure. 戦術を知っていることと、どの選手に何ができるかを把握していることは違う。This is one reason why a two-game evaluation is so uncertain.

## 6. The 0–4 derby was not a normal eleven-versus-eleven test

Verdy lost **0–4 to FC Tokyo on October 17**, but **Someno was dismissed in the 18th minute**. The match therefore contains a long ten-man period. [FC Tokyo official record](https://www.fctokyo.co.jp/match/schedule/result/20261017/)

Soma modified wide defending at half-time and introduced Ichimi to provide an attacking outlet. 終盤には追加失点を防ぐため、守備を**5-3-1**へ変更したという。しかし前へ出る試みの後、最後の失点も喫した。[Soma match comments](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/info/2026101708/report)

Four conceded in one match is a serious result. But **you cannot isolate the effect of the new 4-4-2 from a match played mostly with ten players**. それでも退場以前から未勝利だった事実は残るので、すべてを退場のせいにすることもできない。

There is also a data-quality caution: shot totals appear differently in the post-match question, Soma's reply and some public statistical displays. 0本、1本、2本のうち、集計差の原因をまだ特定できていない。Do not invent certainty where the sources disagree. [Verdy interview](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/info/2026101708/report) / [J.League match page](https://www.jleague.jp/match/j1/2026/101708/)

## 7. The attack needs a second action, not just the first forward pass

Return to the four goals. None was credited to the leading forwards in the first ten league matches. シュート本数や位置の全情報がないため、これを単純に個人の技術不足へ変えてはならない。[J.League club statistics](https://www.jleague.jp/club/tokyov/)

After Hiroshima, midfielder **Kai Shibato** spoke of attacking the space behind the opposing defenders. **Kaito Suzuki** discussed the need for runners into space, players moving toward the ball, and more support for crosses. FC東京戦後には**Koki Morita**も、ボールを落ち着かせる時間の必要性に触れている。[Hiroshima interviews](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/info/2026101103/report) / [Tokyo derby interviews](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/info/2026101708/report)

These comments suggest a shared tactical problem: **win the ball → move forward → secure the second action → reach the box → shoot**. 縦に速く進むことと、ボールをつなぐことは矛盾しない。The first pass needs a receiver, and the receiver needs support.

Soma confirmed that **Someno will miss the next match**. His replacement was not confirmed as of October 19. [Coach's comments](https://www.verdy.co.jp/match/info/2026101708/report) More than the name, watch who makes the runs and who supports the first attacking action.

## 8. Judge the new regime at three different time horizons

**First: shared instructions and shape.** Can players execute the 4-4-2 and the new pressing triggers? **Second: repeatability.** Can they keep those actions in place after half-time and after opponents adjust? **Third: outcomes.** Do the patterns ultimately produce goals and points?

Soma himself stressed after the first week that players needed not only a shared understanding but also successful experience in real matches. 共有と結果を結びつける作業は、まだ始まったばかり。[October 15 media interview](https://www.verdy.co.jp/beleza/news/15305)

Intermediate improvements can be measured before results arrive. But this does **not** make points unimportant. 勝点を取る責任と、戦術の改善過程を理解する仕事は、同時に存在する。

## 9. Five testable questions for the next match

**Proposal—not a prediction.** For the October 21 fixture against Marinos, try five questions.

1. **Where does Verdy start pressing?** 最初から相手ＣＢへ行くのか、それとも決めた場所へ誘い込むのか。
2. **Can the 4-4-2 protect the wings?** サイドハーフとサイドバックの受け渡し、逆サイドへの展開をどう守るか。
3. **What happens after a recovery?** 最初の縦パスの後、二手目が味方へつながるか。
4. **Who replaces Someno's role?** 誰が背後へ走り、誰がボールを収め、誰がこぼれ球を拾うか。
5. **Does the forward intent survive after minute 60?** 広島戦の後半と同じ現象が続くか、相手や交代の条件と一緒に見る。

A simple observation exercise: choose **five clear ball recoveries per half**, note the location of recovery, the direction of the first pass, whether a second successful action follows, and whether the sequence reaches a shot. 5回では統計的な効果は証明できないが、観察と推測を区別する練習になる。

## 10. Skeptic, researcher, and process designer

**Skeptic:** A coaching change need not reject everything before it. 4-4-2 is not automatically attacking. Pressing does not mean rushing forward at every moment. A ten-man defeat cannot be interpreted as a normal tactical test. No forward goals do not alone prove poor individual quality.

**Researcher:** We rechecked the coaching statements, club statistics and both October match reports. The initial picture of an unconditional high press was revised after Hayashi's description of a more selective defensive trap. The interpretation of six goals conceded under Soma was qualified by the red card on October 17. 公式の発言、データ、解釈を同じ確実性で扱わない。

**Process designer:** Next time, code selected video sequences for pressing triggers, ball-recovery locations, first and second attacking actions, entry into dangerous areas, and match conditions. 比較対象と定義を固定したうえで、複数の観察者の分類が一致するか確認する。Unmeasured metrics should remain unmeasured.

## 11. From the scorer to the team that creates the shot

The original puzzle was strange: **four goals, none scored by the leading forwards**. The research shows why the next question should not be simply "Who is the striker?" 相馬監督は城福体制のすべてを消すためでなく、チームの共通した前進方向を取り戻すために呼ばれたとクラブは説明する。

The new shape appeared against Hiroshima, but its effectiveness faded later in the match. Against FC Tokyo, an early red card prevented a normal eleven-versus-eleven test. **Two defeats are facts; the independent causal impact of the coach remains unproven.**

得点者の名前は結果にすぎない。The deeper story lies with the player who presses, the one who receives after recovery, the runner who moves behind the defence, and the teammate who makes the second action possible. 相馬ヴェルディを知るとは、その連携を観察することなのだ。
