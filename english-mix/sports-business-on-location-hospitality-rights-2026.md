# 同じ試合、まったく違う価格：Who sells the experience?

*On Location—守破離で読む premium sports experiences. 情報基準日：2026年11月8日。*

> **Abstract**　On Location provides ticket-inclusive hospitality products: premium viewing, food, hotels, travel and special access. Created by the NFL, it expanded into global events and became part of TKO in 2025. Yet an exclusive right is not a guaranteed profit. The reported Paris 2024 loss and the record FIFA World Cup 2026 sales illustrate a basic principle: **Revenue is one thing; taking responsibility for future demand is another.**

## 1. 守｜A premium seat is not the whole product

Two people watch the same match, at the same stadium. One buys a normal ticket. The other buys a package that includes a lounge, food, a hotel and a dedicated entrance. The goal is still to see a match—but **the product is not the same**.

**Hospitality（観戦付帯サービス）**には、特別観戦席、飲食、宿泊、移動、限定の接遇などが含まれる。A sports fan may value access to the game itself, a traveler may value a trouble-free itinerary, and a company may value a client relationship. Different purposes create different **willingness to pay（支払意思額）**.

The enterprise began within the **NFL（米プロアメリカンフットボールリーグ）**. In 2015, investors including RedBird Capital, Bruin Capital and Carlyle acquired equity. Endeavor bought the company in **2020**, and in February **2025** IMG, On Location and Professional Bull Riders transferred to **TKO Group Holdings**. In 2024, its NFL partnership was extended through **2036**. [NFL partnership and history](https://onlocationexp.com/news/nfl-announcement) / [TKO transaction](https://tkogrp.com/news/tko-completes-acquisition-of-sports-assets-from-endeavor/).

A small source-audit lesson: Goldman Sachs' earlier sports report p.20 described the Endeavor transaction as 2022. The NFL's own public chronology says 2020, which this article adopts. **Official histories can contradict industry reports; check the dates.**

![The commercial chain behind an official hospitality package.](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/sports-business/onlocation-model.svg)

Why does official status matter? A normal travel agency can reserve hotels and arrange meals. However, it cannot automatically sell access covered by an event's **exclusive hospitality rights（公式ホスピタリティの独占販売権）**.

The International Olympic Committee appointed On Location for the **Paris 2024, Milano Cortina 2026 and Los Angeles 2028** Olympic hospitality programs. The IOC explains that authorized hospitality distributors are separate from the ordinary ticketing system. 独占されるのは、一般観戦券の全販売経路ではなく、指定された観戦券付きホスピタリティ商品だ。[IOC ticketing and hospitality](https://www.olympics.com/ioc/ticketing).

Pricing also requires care. **Price discrimination（価格差別）** means charging different prices for essentially the same product under different circumstances. **Product differentiation（商品差別化）** means creating different products. A package with transport and food is not just the original ticket sold at a higher price.

## 2. 守｜Why did the Paris Olympics produce a loss?

Sports Business Journal examined 2024 Olympic-related financial reporting and concluded that On Location suffered **at least $252 million in losses** on the Paris Games business during that year. It described approximately **$528 million in operating revenue against $780 million in expenses**. These figures come from its examination of former parent Endeavor's public filings; they are not a simple full-lifecycle profitability calculation. [SBJ investigation, February 2026](https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2026/02/02/challenges-with-olympics-hospitality-raise-concerns-about-iocs-new-model/).

さらに、関係者がイベントの直前までチケットを受け取れなかった事例や、商品価格への不満が報じられた。The company responded that tickets were delivered before the sessions and that some delays involved ticketing processes beyond its full control. Both the complaints and the company's response matter.

A one-stop package removes work for the buyer—but **fulfillment（契約内容の履行）** becomes more complicated for the seller. A ticket, hotel room, transfer and meal all have different suppliers and cancellation terms. If one component fails, the customer experiences the whole package as a failure.

The economic lesson is uncomfortable: **Exclusive selling rights can create exclusive exposure to unsold inventory and operational failure.** 権利を持つことは利益を持つことではない。

## 3. 破｜Why did the 2026 World Cup look different?

**Observation:** In May 2026 TKO announced that more than **500,000 World Cup hospitality packages had been allocated**. Allocation does not mean every package had been purchased by an end consumer. [TKO announcement](https://investor.tkogrp.com/news/news-details/2026/FIFA-World-Cup-2026-Hospitality-Sales-Program-Sets-Records-Sees-Strong-Momentum-Ahead-of-Tournament/default.aspx).

After the tournament, a November 2026 TKO earnings-call transcript reported approximately **$2.4 billion in total FIFA World Cup hospitality sales** and over **640,000 packages sold**. These numbers are **gross hospitality sales**, not On Location's net profit. [Earnings-call transcript](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tko/transcripts/722802-q3-2026/) / [TKO earnings release](https://investor.tkogrp.com/news/news-details/2026/TKO-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx).

**Abduction:** Why did the same firm have such different outcomes? Possible drivers include different rights payments, customer demand, event complexity, inventory risk, geographic market and product design. A soccer tournament involves one sport; the Olympic Games involve many sports and venues. But this fact alone does not explain profitability.

**Competing explanations:** Maybe North American purchasing power, event scale or the customer mix mattered more. To separate the hypotheses, we would need contract-level costs, contribution margin, unsold inventory, customer acquisition costs and delivery performance. それらが不明なまま「五輪は赤字、W杯は高収益」と決めつけるのは危険だ。

**Forecast:** Future hospitality providers may focus less on maximizing ticket face value and more on **matching commitments with verified demand**. Flexible contract terms, better demand forecasts and shared revenue mechanisms may reduce risk. 検証指標は販売総額だけではなく、利益、返品、受渡遅延、顧客満足度。

## 4. 破｜Premium service and public access can conflict

A high-priced sports package is not necessarily objectionable. People have different needs and willingness to pay. ただし、一般向けの座席が狭まり、情報が不透明になり、体験を選べる人が減るなら、公平性の問題が生じる。

The IOC pursued a more centralized official hospitality model partly to reduce fragmented ticket distribution. A centralized model can clarify official channels, but dependence on one provider can also amplify any operational disruption. [IOC](https://www.olympics.com/ioc/ticketing) / [SBJ's critical examination](https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2026/02/02/challenges-with-olympics-hospitality-raise-concerns-about-iocs-new-model/).

ここで分けるべきは**consumer access（観戦機会へのアクセス）**と**commercial profitability（事業採算）**。Both must be evaluated. Higher revenue does not by itself prove a better experience or a fairer allocation of tickets.

## 5. 離｜Airlines, travel agencies and the economics of promises

**Airlines:** One aircraft offers different cabins and ticket conditions. The shared core is transportation; customers purchase different comfort and flexibility. Stadium hospitality follows a similar logic. This is connected to **revenue management（収益管理）**, the discipline of allocating scarce capacity over time and across customer segments.

**Travel agencies:** A bundled trip reduces the buyer's coordination costs and anxiety. However, it concentrates responsibility. A missing ticket can destroy the value of the hotel and hospitality package. **Convenience for the customer becomes complexity for the operator.**

**Financial analogy:** When a company commits to rights or inventory before knowing future demand, it accepts market uncertainty. This resembles entering a forward commitment, although hospitality contracts are not necessarily financial derivatives. 重要なのは「誰が、いつ、需要変動のリスクを引き受けるか」という問いだ。

## 6. 日本市場への応用｜Sell a solved problem, not just a luxury seat

国内クラブが学べるのは、外国の価格表ではない。For distant fans, travel planning may be the biggest obstacle. For corporate guests, predictable service quality may matter most. For first-time viewers, clear information and easy access may be worth more than luxury.

A small pilot should estimate **incremental profit（既存売上の置き換えを除いた追加利益）**. Measure package price, actual delivery cost, cancellations, repeat purchase and satisfaction. Selling a costly product to an existing customer can raise apparent revenue without creating new value.

## 7. 結論｜The business sells certainty

At first, the mystery was why two spectators paid different amounts for the same match. After studying On Location, the question changes. The expensive part is not just access; it is the promise that every part of the experience will work.

Paris 2024 makes the cost of a broken operating model visible. FIFA World Cup 2026 demonstrates the scale available when demand materializes. The final lesson is simple but demanding: **Premium hospitality is not the art of charging more. It is the business of making a complicated promise reliable—and profitable.**
