# Black teeth, blue tins――化粧品の歴史は誰が美を決めるかの歴史

*Research date: 2026-10-24. This is a source-based essay, not fieldwork or a claim that today's cosmetic products are all alike.*

A strange question: why was it once respectable to make your teeth black? And why was the famous NIVEA tin not blue at first?

その答えを探すと、化粧品のhistoryは「more beautiful products」という単純な進歩ではないと分かる。It is also a story about society, chemistry, selling, and safety.

## 守｜化粧品を考える前に、化粧・製品・制度を分ける

Start with a strange fact: in old Japan, black teeth could be part of proper appearance. **Ohaguro** was connected to marriage and coming of age. Today, white teeth are often treated as a beauty goal. So the history of cosmetics is not only about new materials. **The meaning of beauty changes.** 出典：[化粧の文化史｜日本化粧品工業会](https://www.jcia.org/user/public/knowledge/history)

Two words must stay separate. **Kesho** means the act of grooming or decorating the body. **Cosmetics** is also a legal product category in modern Japan. It can include soap, shampoo, perfume and lipstick. Medicated cosmetics often fall under a different legal category, quasi-drugs (医薬部外品). We should not apply today's laws directly to ancient objects. 出典：[化粧品の定義｜医薬品医療機器等法第2条](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/web/t_doc?dataId=81004000)／[化粧品と薬用化粧品｜日本化粧品工業会](https://www.jcia.org/user/public/knowledge/glossary/cosmeceuticals)

## 守｜古代エジプトの目元の化粧は女性専用ではなかった

The Metropolitan Museum of Art keeps a tiny Egyptian **kohl** jar from around 1887–1813 BCE. Its materials include obsidian and gold. The museum says men and women used cosmetics, and that cosmetics could be linked to hopes for life after death. So makeup was not simply “something women do.” 出典：[コールの容器｜メトロポリタン美術館](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/543979)

But cultural importance does not prove medical safety. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration warns that some traditional kohl products can contain dangerous lead. We must separate **historical meaning** from **safety evidence**. 出典：[伝統的なコールの鉛中毒リスク｜米国食品医薬品局](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-products/kohl-kajal-al-kahal-surma-tiro-tozali-or-kwalli-any-name-beware-lead-poisoning)

## 守｜江戸の化粧は赤・白・黒で社会的な情報を伝えた

Three colors explain much of Edo-period makeup: **red rouge, white face powder, and black eyebrows or teeth**. Blackened teeth sometimes indicated marital status. Dark, iridescent red lips became fashionable late in the period. Makeup communicated social information, not just personal taste. 出典：[化粧の文化史｜日本化粧品工業会](https://www.jcia.org/user/public/knowledge/history)

A powder called **Bien Senjoko** was promoted with the help of woodblock-print publishers. This looks surprisingly modern: use a popular image to create interest in a product. Today's media differ, but the basic question survives: **How does an ideal image help sell something?** 出典：[化粧の文化史｜日本化粧品工業会](https://www.jcia.org/user/public/knowledge/history)

## 守｜明治期に美しさの規範と安全性の基準が同時に動いた

In 1870, a decree restricted tooth blackening and eyebrow shaving among the Japanese peerage. It did **not** instantly ban the practice for every person in Japan. Around 1877, concerns about lead-based powder encouraged research on lead-free products, commercialized in 1904. Social fashion and product safety changed for different reasons. 出典：[化粧の文化史｜日本化粧品工業会](https://www.jcia.org/user/public/knowledge/history)

The modern lesson is simple: “natural” and “traditional” do not automatically mean safe. A safety claim needs evidence about the substance, amount and how it is used.

## 守｜1911年のニベアは保湿よりも「安定して作れる」を変えた

Oil and water usually separate. **Emulsification** helps keep one dispersed in the other. In 1911, NIVEA used the emulsifier **Eucerit** to make a stable cream that could be produced widely. This was not the invention of moisturizing. It was a step toward **consistent industrial products**. Chemist Isaac Lifschütz, dermatologist Paul Gerson Unna and entrepreneur Oscar Troplowitz were involved. 出典：[ニベアの歴史｜バイヤスドルフ](https://www.beiersdorf.com/brands/brand-history/nivea)／[バイヤスドルフの技術史｜バイヤスドルフ](https://www.beiersdorf.com/about-us/our-history/milestones)

And the famous blue tin? It was not blue at first. The brand adopted the blue-and-white design in **1925**. A formula and a visual identity can change on different timelines. 出典：[ニベアの歴史｜バイヤスドルフ](https://www.beiersdorf.com/brands/brand-history/nivea)

## 守｜資生堂は薬局から始まり、化粧品の選び方まで商品にした

Shiseido began as a Western-style pharmacy in Ginza in **1872**. It released the lotion **Eudermine** in 1897, then **seven colors of face powder** in 1917. Its 1924 magazine gave readers beauty tips and lifestyle ideas. The company was no longer selling only a formula. It was helping people learn **how to choose and use** products. 出典：[資生堂の歴史｜資生堂](https://corp.shiseido.com/jp/company/history/)

But the seven-color launch was not exactly the same as today's debates about inclusive shade ranges. We should respect historical context.

## 守｜20世紀にはクリームだけでなく手入れの「手順」が商品になった

In the early 1900s, entrepreneurs such as **Elizabeth Arden, Helena Rubinstein and Madam C. J. Walker** sold systems of products, not only single jars. Skincare became a **routine**: cleanse, care, moisturize. A routine can create repeat demand and a long relationship with a brand. 出典：[スキンケア商品の歴史｜スミソニアン国立アメリカ歴史博物館](https://americanhistory.si.edu/ne/collections/object-groups/health-hygiene-and-beauty/skin-care)

But these businesses served different people. Beauty markets also reflected racial and class barriers. The important question is not only who could buy a product, but **who had been left out**. 出典：[スキンケア商品の歴史｜スミソニアン国立アメリカ歴史博物館](https://americanhistory.si.edu/ne/collections/object-groups/health-hygiene-and-beauty/skin-care)

## 破｜科学的に見える説明と、実際の根拠を混同してはいけない

A scientific-looking ad is not the same as scientific proof. There are separate steps: **ingredient research → formula → finished-product testing → advertising claim**. Evidence for one step does not automatically prove the next.

The same applies to skin scanners at a store. A precise-looking number does not prove that a product recommendation is correct. We need to know how the measurement works and whether the advice helps.

## 破｜規制が整っても、発売後の健康被害はなくならない

Regulation grew as cosmetics became everyday mass products. In Japan, full ingredient labeling became mandatory in **April 2001**. In the U.S., the **Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act of 2022** expanded the FDA's authority. But rules differ by country, and cosmetics do not all receive a government approval of effectiveness before sale. 出典：[化粧品の全成分表示｜日本化粧品工業会](https://jcia.org/user/business/guideline/ingredientlabelling)／[米国の化粧品規制｜米国食品医薬品局](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-laws-regulations/modernization-cosmetics-regulation-act-2022-mocra)

In **July 2013**, Japanese authorities reported a voluntary recall of certain medicated cosmetics containing rhododenol after reports of white patches on the skin. This is a reminder: innovation needs **post-market safety monitoring**, not only tests before launch. 出典：[ロドデノール配合製品の自主回収｜厚生労働省](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/houdou/2r98520000035xv0.html)

## 破｜白い肌への憧れは単純な東西の違いでは説明できない

Why have people valued lighter skin? There is no single answer. The **Wellcome Collection** shows that some ideals predate Western colonialism, while colonial systems also used appearance to rank people. Historical context matters. 出典：[美の崇拝展・解説｜ウェルカム・コレクション](https://wellcomecollection.org/guides/exhibitions/the-cult-of-beauty/captions-and-transcripts)

The **World Health Organization** warns about mercury in some skin-lightening products. That is not the same issue as every Japanese medicated product that uses a legally defined whitening claim. We must distinguish beauty norms, safety and regulation. 出典：[水銀を含む肌色を明るくする製品｜世界保健機関](https://www.who.int/initiatives/elimination-of-mercury-containing-skin-lightening-products)

## 破｜動物実験と環境負荷で、化粧品の評価対象は容器の外へ広がった

The **European Union** introduced animal-testing and marketing restrictions for cosmetics in stages, with a final marketing-ban phase in 2013. This does **not** mean every animal test under every chemical law disappeared. 出典：[動物実験規制｜欧州委員会](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/cosmetics/ban-animal-testing_en)

Now buyers may also ask about ingredient sourcing, packaging and waste. Product quality is becoming a question about more than what happens on the skin.

## 離｜化粧品史の五つの層は、今も一つの売場に同居している

Cosmetics history has at least five layers: **social identity, commerce, industrial chemistry, routines, and responsibility**. These are not steps where old roles simply vanish. We can still find all five on one beauty floor.

That explains why similar creams coexist. People may be buying comfort, habit, prestige or self-expression. The bottle is only one part of the value.

## 離｜百貨店の役割は「商品を揃える」から「選ぶ根拠を作る」へ広がりうる

What could a department store do now? A maker can explain its own products. A multi-brand retailer can help customers **compare different answers**. This is a business hypothesis, not a proven result for every store.

Ask what “I want better skin” really means: less irritation, less dryness, a new look, or a different mood. Good advice begins with the customer's actual question. The next opportunity may be **helping people choose**, not merely adding more items.

## 結論｜歯の色が逆転した歴史は、美しさに唯一の正解がないことを示す

Return to the black teeth. The old beauty rule did not simply become a new, objectively correct rule. Ideas about appearance changed. Even NIVEA's famous tin changed color. **Who decides what looks beautiful, and whose values shape that choice?**

A cosmetic is a physical product. But its history also carries science, social status, health, advertising and sometimes discrimination. To understand what is inside the market, we must look beyond what is inside the bottle.

## 用語｜Key terms

- **乳化 (emulsification)**: water and oil are dispersed to make a stable mixture. 水と油を細かく分散させる技術。
- **医薬部外品 (quasi-drug)**: a legal product category in Japan, separate from cosmetics. 医薬品と化粧品とは異なる法律上の区分。
- **処方 (formulation)**: the recipe and design of a product. 成分の組み合わせと製品設計。
- **全成分表示 (full ingredient labeling)**: rules for disclosing cosmetic ingredients. 日本では2001年4月に義務化。
- **化粧品の発売後安全管理 (post-market surveillance)**: checking safety information after a product is sold. 発売後に集まる健康被害などの情報を扱う仕組み。

## 検証と限界｜Method and limits

**Facts** were cross-checked against museum collections, official brand histories, regulations and public-health notices. **Interpretations** about identity, social expectations and retail strategy are arguments, not direct measurements. **Proposal:** evaluate modern cosmetics through formula, evidence, advice and social meaning, not one dimension alone.

The essay focuses on Egypt, Japan, Europe and the United States; it is not a complete world history. Corporate histories may emphasize successes, and legal systems differ by country.
