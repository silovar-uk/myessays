# 信号待ちの10秒は、動画の10秒より長いのか
## The present is not a mathematical point.

赤信号。

10. 9. 8.

長い。

A favourite video: suddenly ten minutes are gone.

The clock is perfectly fair. Experience is not.

時間を考えるとすぐ時計を思い浮かべる。でもphenomenology asks an earlier question:

**How is time experienced at all?**

---

## 1. If “now” were only a point, melody would disappear

Hear C, then D, then E.

If consciousness contained only the mathematically exact present tone, C would vanish completely when D arrived, and D when E arrived.

Then we would hear isolated sounds, not **C→D→E as a sequence**.

でも実際には流れを聞く。

This is a doorway into Husserl's analysis of time-consciousness.

The experienced present is not a zero-width point. 直前が薄く残り、次に来るものへの構えも含んで進む。

---

## 2. Retention, primal impression, protention

フッサールの時間意識は、よく三つの構造で説明される。

- **primal impression** — いま生起している局面
- **retention** — the just-past still held in the flow
- **protention** — これから来るものへの曖昧な先取り

Retention is not the same as deliberately remembering last summer.

メロディ中、直前の音を毎回思い出してはいない。It remains as a fading part of the present flow.

Protention is not the same as planning next week.

When a melody continues, we are already leaning toward what comes next. だからwrong noteやsudden stopに驚ける。

**To be surprised is, in some sense, to have expected.**

---

## 3. Over-serious experiment: cut “Happy Birthday” into pieces

頭の中でHappy Birthdayの冒頭を鳴らす。

Now imagine five seconds of silence between every note.

The pitches are still there, but the melody collapses.

逆に極端に速くしても、recognisable sequenceが崩れる。

A melody is not a list of notes. **Its temporal binding is part of what it is.**

言葉も同じ。

If each sound in 「げ・ん・しょ・う・が・く」 vanished completely before the next arrived, the word could never form.

We bind meaning through time.

---

## 4. So why can a red-light ten seconds feel longer?

ここは慎重に。

Husserl's phenomenology does not by itself explain all psychological mechanisms of boredom or duration estimation. Psychology and neuroscience study attention, emotion, arousal, and other factors.

The phenomenological lesson is more basic:

**Experienced time is not a simple internal copy of clock time.**

At the light, the coming green can dominate attention: not yet, not yet, not yet.

動画へ没入すると、clock time itself may recede.

Same ten seconds. Different experiential organization.

---

## 5. “Now” carries a little past and future

「今この瞬間」と言うと、knife-edgeの一点を想像しやすい。

Phenomenologically, the present has thickness.

The just-past remains. The next is anticipated. Melody, language, and action live inside that spread.

第1回のコップはhidden sidesを引き連れていた。

Time does something similar.

**The present carries a little of what is no longer and what is not yet.**

赤信号で待っている私は、まだ来ていないgreenへすでに張り出している。
