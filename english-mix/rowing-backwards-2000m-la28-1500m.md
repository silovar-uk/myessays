# ローイングは、後ろ向きに2000m進む。なのにLA28は1500mになる
## Row backwards, move forward｜艇種の暗号、full-body stroke、synchrony、そして橋

The first strange thing about rowing is simple.

**The rowers are not looking where they are going.**

漕手はboatの進行方向に背を向け、stern側を向いて漕ぐ。World Rowingの用語解説でも、rowers face the stern while coxswains face forwardと整理されている。

[World Rowing, “Port and Starboard – Defining Rowing Series Part 4”](https://worldrowing.com/2024/01/21/port-and-starboard-defining-rowing-series-part-4/)

Classic rowingのstandard distanceは2000m。

But at the Los Angeles 2028 Olympics, Classic Rowing will be raced over **1500m**.

Why?

Not because sports science found a better distance.

**Because there is a bridge.**

Long Beach Marine Stadium cannot fit a full 2000m six-lane course after a bridge was built, so LA28 will use 1500m for the Olympic and Paralympic regattas.

[World Rowing, “Los Angeles 2028 confirms venue for Classic Rowing”](https://worldrowing.com/2024/07/12/los-angeles-2028-confirms-venue-for-classic-rowing/)

Row backward. Race forward. Sync everyone. And sometimes, infrastructure changes the distance.

ちゃんと調べると、rowing is wonderfully specific and slightly strange.

---

## 1. FACT｜In Japan, the sport is officially “ローイング”

日本では名前も最近変わった。

The Japan Rowing Association changed its Japanese name from 日本ボート協会 to 日本ローイング協会 on January 1, 2023. 競技名も「ローイング」とされた。

[日本ローイング協会「団体名称変更のお知らせ」](https://www.jara.or.jp/info/2022/info1206.html)

さらに歴史をさかのぼると、1998年には「日本漕艇協会」から「日本ボート協会」へ改称している。

So the label moved like this:

**漕艇 → ボート → ローイング**

[日本ローイング協会「日本のローイング略史」](https://www.jara.or.jp/history.html)

“Boat” can mean almost any boat.

“Rowing” points directly to the act of propulsion.

これはinterpretationになるが、「何に乗っているか」より「どう漕ぐか」に視線を戻す名前として、競技の中身を理解しやすい。

---

## 2. FACT｜1x, 2-, 4x, 8+ look like equations

Rowing events come with strange labels.

**1x, 2x, 2-, 4x, 4-, 8+.**

At first glance, it looks like notation from a spreadsheet.

でもruleはかなりsimple。

日本ローイング協会の説明では、

- number = 漕手数
- x = sculling、1人2本のoars
- no x = sweep rowing、1人1本のoar
- + = with cox
- - = without cox

[日本ローイング協会「ローイング競技について」](https://www.jara.or.jp/about.html)

So:

- **1x** = single sculls
- **2-** = pair
- **4x** = quadruple sculls
- **8+** = eight rowers plus a coxswain

Once the code is decoded, the name tells you crew size, oar system, and cox status.

ローイングは難しいというより、the naming system is extremely compressed.

---

## 3. FACT｜It looks like arms, but the drive starts with the legs

映像ではarmsが一番目立つ。

But World Rowing breaks the stroke into four phases: Catch, Drive, Release, Recovery. The Drive starts with a strong **leg drive**.

[World Rowing, “Rowing 101”](https://worldrowing.com/2026/08/23/rowing-101-the-history-terms-and-tactics-to-know-ahead-of-amsterdam-2026/)

Roughly:

1. Catch：blade enters water
2. Drive：legs push, core transfers, arms finish
3. Release：blade leaves water
4. Recovery：body returns for next catch

競技艇のseatはrail上を前後に滑る。

That sliding seat allows the rower to use the legs.

つまりrowerは、オールを腕で引くだけではない。

**Push with legs, transfer through the trunk, finish with arms, fold back, repeat.**

And in a crew boat, everyone repeats the cycle together.

---

## 4. OVERKILL RESEARCH｜Is perfect synchrony really fastest?

Eight rowers move almost together.

It looks beautiful.

But from a physics view, there is a weird question.

If everyone applies force at the same time, boat speed rises and falls within each stroke. Would staggered strokes make speed smoother and more efficient?

実はasynchronous rowingは昔から議論されてきた。

World Rowing紹介のÉcole Polytechniqueの研究では、robot rowersとscaled model boatで比較した。

Asynchronous rowing reduced speed fluctuations.

**The boat speed became smoother.**

But average speed was still lower than with synchronous rowing.

[World Rowing, “Is synchronicity the best way to move a boat?”](https://www.worldrowing.com/2017/10/12/synchronicity-the-best-way-move-boat/)

This does not prove that every human crew has one perfect timing pattern.

ただ、important pointはある。

**Smoother speed is not automatically faster speed.**

Synchrony is not cosmetic.

It is part of performance.

---

## 5. FACT｜2000m is neither simple endurance nor simple power

Classic rowing is normally 2000m.

A 2025 systematic review of rowing ergometer studies analyzed 34 studies and 909 rowers. VO2max and peak power output were among the strongest and most consistent correlates of 2000m erg performance.

[PubMed, “The Evaluation of Physical Performance in Rowing Ergometer: A Systematic Review”](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41283544/)

Important distinction:

**Erg performance is not the same as on-water performance.**

水上ではbalance、technique、wind、water conditions、crew synchronyが入る。

Still, the evidence shows why rowing cannot be reduced to only endurance or only strength.

Another 2025 systematic review notes that typical 2000m rowing depends heavily on aerobic energy while anaerobic capacity also matters.

[PMC, “Associations Between Lactate Thresholds and 2000 m Rowing Ergometer Performance”](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11871166/)

So you need:

**power to launch**
+
**capacity to hold**
+
**power to finish**

全部いる。

---

## 6. FACT｜A 2000m race has phases

World Rowing describes race strategy through phases such as Start, Settling, and Middle Phase.

At the start, crews use a high stroke rate to accelerate.

Then they settle into a sustainable rhythm.

In the middle, efficiency matters.

Near the finish, crews lift again.

[World Rowing, “Rowing 101”](https://worldrowing.com/2026/08/23/rowing-101-the-history-terms-and-tactics-to-know-ahead-of-amsterdam-2026/)

Here, the roles of Stroke and Cox matter.

日本ローイング協会は、Strokeをpaceとrhythmのlead役、Coxをsteering担当として説明している。

[日本ローイング協会「ローイング競技について」](https://www.jara.or.jp/about.html)

Eight people are not eight independent engines.

**They are one timed system.**

## 7. CASE｜The 2026 men's eight final ended by 0.06 seconds

Concrete example.

2026 World Rowing Championships in Amsterdam.

Men's Eight A Final.

Netherlands won in:

**5:17.75**

Great Britain finished second in:

**5:17.81**

Gap:

**0.06 seconds.**

[World Rowing Data Platform, 2026 World Championships Men's Eight A Final](https://raceanalysis.worldrowing.com/race/bd3e84a9-17d5-4a4b-b8e6-46633426638f)

Simple calculation:

2000m / 317.75 sec ≈ 6.29m/s

That is about **22.7 km/h average**.

If 0.06 sec is converted using that average speed, it is roughly **38 cm**.

This is not the actual finish-line distance, because finish speed is not identical to average speed.

でも感覚は分かる。

**Five minutes of nine-person coordination can end in something like a few dozen centimeters.**

Suddenly, tiny timing differences stop looking tiny.

## 8. PROPOSAL｜How to watch rowing without memorizing everything

You do not need all the terminology first.

Try three passes.

### Pass 1: Watch only the 500m splits

Who leads at 500?

Who stabilizes at 1000?

Who moves at 1500?

Who changes the race in the last 500?

まずraceをfour segmentsとして見る。

### Pass 2: Watch one crew's Catch

Look at the moment all blades enter the water.

Are they together?

Does timing change under fatigue?

8人が、one cycleに見えてくる。

### Pass 3: Watch the Recovery

Beginners naturally watch the Drive.

But Recovery is where the crew returns for the next stroke.

How smoothly does the boat continue?

How much does the shell pitch or slow?

ここを見ると、rowing is not “pull harder, more times.”

It is a rhythm system.

## 9. FUTURE｜LA28 breaks the 2000m assumption

Back to the opening.

2000m is the standard distance for major classic rowing events.

[日本ローイング協会「ローイング競技について」](https://www.jara.or.jp/about.html)

But LA28 Classic Rowing will use 1500m because of Long Beach's physical constraints.

At the same Olympics, Beach Sprint Rowing will make its Olympic debut.

World Rowing confirmed 12 Classic medal events plus three Beach Sprint events: Men's Solo, Women's Solo, and Mixed Double.

[World Rowing, “World Rowing Welcomes Confirmation of Rowing Programme and Quotas for LA 2028 Olympic Games”](https://worldrowing.com/2025/04/09/world-rowing-welcomes-confirmation-of-rowing-programme-and-quotas-for-la-2028-olympic-games/)

So LA28 rowing will do two strange things at once:

**shorten the traditional straight race**
and
**add a new beach sprint discipline.**

The sport is old, but the format is not frozen.

---

## 10. DISCOVERY｜Rowing now looks like a sport of protecting the cycle

Before researching, rowing looked like:

“People pull very hard and make a boat go fast.”

That is true, but incomplete.

After looking at the sport, another picture appears.

One athlete needs power.

A crew needs timing.

Drive matters.

Recovery matters.

The start matters.

The middle matters.

The last 500m matters.

Cox handles direction.

Stroke anchors rhythm.

Wind and water disturb everything.

And at LA28, a bridge changes the race distance.

So rowing starts to look less like:

**“How hard can you pull?”**

and more like:

**“How long can the crew protect a fast, shared cycle?”**

選手は前を向いていない。

But everyone is moving toward the same finish.

At first, that looked strange.

After research, it looks like the point.
