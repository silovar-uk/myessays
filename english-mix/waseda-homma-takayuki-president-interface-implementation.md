# 「選挙で選ばれても、総長には決まらない」Wasedaの新総長は「interface」を研究してきた人だった
## 第18代総長・本間敬之を、選出制度、電気化学、2032年と2050年の構想から読む

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
On September 21, 2026, Waseda University selected Professor Takayuki Homma as its 18th President. ところが本間は、少し前の6月にも「総長候補者選挙」で選ばれている。Read quickly, and it looks as if the same person was “selected” twice.

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
Waseda had already made the distinction explicit in June: winning the election did not itself determine the next President. これはsemantic trickでも、大学特有の肩書問題でもない。The mechanism changed after Japan's revised Private Schools Act took effect in April 2025.

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
Follow that制度, and another boundary appears in Homma's career. His field is electrochemistry, especially reactions at solid-liquid **interfaces**. 総長人事を調べていたはずが、気づけば「25ミリメートル角に2兆個の磁性体を並べる」研究の話に着地する。We have travelled surprisingly far.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Yet the detour turns out to be useful. 制度ではelectionとboard、経歴ではresearchとuniversity management、大学の将来像では2032と2050、そして就任メッセージではvisionとimplementation。Tracing Homma reveals a university standing at several kinds of handoff at once.

>
> This essay mainly uses public material from Waseda University and Japan's Ministry of Education. 本間の研究分野と大学経営能力を直接結び付けるevidenceはないため、"interface" is used only as an interpretive lens, not as a causal claim.

## 1. 総長候補者選挙で選ばれても、総長を決めるのは理事会になった

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
Start with the apparent double selection. 2026年6月2日の開票で、本間は次期総長候補者に選出された。But the election selected a **candidate for President**, not the President himself.

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
According to Waseda, the Board of Councilors then appointed directors, including Homma, and on September 21 the newly constituted Board of Directors selected the President from among the directors while giving full consideration to the election result. 本間の任期は2030年9月20日までの4年間。

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
The background is the revised Private Schools Act, effective April 2025. 早稲田は従来の「総長選挙で総長を決める」方式から、「総長候補者選挙で候補者を選び、評議員会による理事選任を経て、理事会が総長を選定する」方式へ変更した。The ministry's guidance allows internal election results to inform the process, while keeping final legal responsibility with the statutory appointing bodies.

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
So the election did not disappear, but neither does it finish the appointment. 学内の意思を示すprocedureと、学校法人として責任を負うselection procedureが直列につながった。Writing “the election chose the new President” would miss the governance change that became operative in 2026.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Homma's appointment is therefore two events at once: a leadership transition and Waseda's first passage through this new selection structure. 新総長を理解する最初の入口は、本人の経歴より先に、大学のgovernance mechanismが変わったことだった。

## 2. 本間敬之は「理系から突然来た新顔」ではなく、16年以上大学運営に入ってきた研究者だ

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
Hearing that the new President is a science-and-engineering professor invites a neat story: “researcher turns into university executive.” でも経歴を見ると、その切り分けはかなり雑。

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
Homma was born in Tokyo in 1964. 1987年に早稲田大学理工学部応用化学科を卒業し、1992年に博士後期課程を修了して博士（工学）を取得。He joined Waseda as an assistant in 1991, became a professor in 2005, and from 2007 served as professor in the Faculty of Advanced Science and Engineering.

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
His university-wide administrative work goes back at least to 2010. 教務部副部長、教務部長、理事（教務担当）、常任理事（教学総括・副プロボスト）を歴任している。That means more than sixteen years of involvement in academic administration before becoming President.

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
The picture shifts. 本間は「研究室から総長室へ移った人」というより、research labとuniversity-wide managementを長く往復してきた人。The overlap matters more than the simple label “science President.”

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
So the available record does not support a dramatic “humanities-to-science turn” or an outsider-style reset. 確認できるのは、内部で長く教学と研究推進を担ってきた人物が、最終的なcoordination roleへ移ったというcontinuityである。

## 3. 本間の研究は、物質の「中身」より二つのものが接する「界面」で起きる反応を追う

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
What, then, has Homma studied as a scientist? The key word is **interface**. 日本語では「界面」。

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
His work analyzes reactions at solid-liquid interfaces in electrochemical systems, then develops processes for nanostructures and thin films and devices such as magnetic recording media, sensors, solar cells, and thermoelectric devices. 物質の塊そのものより、異なるものが接した場所で何が起きるかを細かく追う研究である。

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
A 2016 Waseda interview gives an almost absurdly concrete example. 本間の研究グループは、25ミリメートル角の中に2兆個という密度で、結晶の向きをそろえた強磁性体の微細構造を形成した。That corresponds to roughly two terabits per square inch of recording density.

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
Twenty-five millimetres square is roughly the width of a large postage stamp. そこへ「2兆個」と言われると、日常のnumber senseはほぼ役に立たない。You open a profile of a new university President and suddenly find yourself thinking about particles you could never track with the naked eye.

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
But Homma's point in that interview was not merely “we can make very small things.” 機能は分かるのに内部のmechanismやstructureが分からないblack boxを開き、resultだけでなくprocessまで理解しようとする姿勢を強調していた。

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Move one level below “what worked” and ask where, through which reaction, and by what sequence the result emerged. 総長就任とは別文脈のresearch habitだが、本間という人物を理解するには肩書より具体的な手掛かりになる。

## 4. 研究者時代から、本間は「専門を深めるだけでは足りない」と話していた

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
We do not need to force a metaphor between chemistry and management to find continuity in Homma's past statements. 専門性を社会との関係まで広げて考える姿勢には、以前からrecordがある。

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
In the 2016 interview, Homma described the need for both directions of reasoning: working backward from a desired function to materials, structures, and processes, and working forward from observed reactions and structures toward function. また、優れた研究やdeviceも社会に受け入れられる必要があり、performanceだけでなくcostとのbalanceも考える必要があるとして、specialistとgeneralistの両方の視点を語った。

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
At a 2025 forum for doctoral students, before becoming President, Homma again asked researchers to go deep while also considering what social value their work creates and how it might contribute to industry. 異分野の学生とのdiscussionを通じて、俯瞰して見る力とnetworkを広げるよう促している。

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
The two statements are about nine years apart, yet the common idea is not “be broad instead of deep.” 専門を深く掘ったまま、その成果が別のconditionや相手とconnectしたときに何が起きるかまで考える、という要求である。

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Research to society, expertise to other fields, output to use. その発想が総長としてどのpolicyに結び付くかはまだ分からない。But it is not language invented on inauguration day; it is a concern documented earlier in his research and administrative career.

## 5. ただし「界面の研究者だから、組織の接続もうまい」と言い切るのは、話がきれいすぎる

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
This is where the satisfying metaphor needs a brake. 「界面を研究してきた人が、大学内外のinterfaceをつなぐ総長になる」。The wording is almost too perfect.

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
Public sources do not establish that studying electrochemical interfaces improves university-management ability. ナノ構造をcontrolする技術と、数万人規模のuniversityを動かす技術は別物。Inferring personality or executive competence from a research topic would make a neat profile and a weak factual argument.

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
Still, the metaphor can remain useful if we change its job. 本間のcareerにはresearchとacademic administrationの両方があり、現在の早稲田のplan自体がresearch・education・social contributionをseparateに強くするだけでなく、mutually connectしようとしている。So “look at the interfaces” can be an observation framework, not a competence rating.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Fact: Homma has studied interface electrochemistry. Interpretation: Waseda now faces several organisational handoffs. この二つの間にcausal arrowを勝手に描かない。Keeping that boundary intact makes the later institutional analysis stronger.

## 6. 早稲田の150周年は2032年だが、大学が置いている時間軸は2050年まで伸びている

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
Look at the plan Homma inherits and 2032, Waseda's 150th anniversary, is important but not the endpoint. 大学のofficial horizonは2050年まで伸びている。

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
Waseda frames its 150th-anniversary project as reform toward becoming a university that contributes to humanity by 2050, beyond 2032. 柱はresearch、education、social contributionの三つで、それぞれを全学的なplatformで進め、分野や組織を越えた「総合知」につなげる構想を掲げる。

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
So 2032 is not merely a year for looking back at history. 現在のofficial explanationでは、2050年までのlong-term reformを加速する途中のmilestone。本間は前任の田中愛治の下で常任理事として研究推進などを担当し、この150周年構想のresearch sideを自ら説明してきた。

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
Again, the simple story “new President, new long-term vision” does not fit. すでに走っているlong-range planがあり、新総長自身もそのformationとpromotionに関わっていた。A leadership change does not restart the clock.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
The first question for Homma's presidency is therefore less “what new flag will he plant?” than “how will the inherited 2050 vision become concrete research, education, and contribution before 2032?” 就任時点から、vision creationよりimplementationの比重が大きいtermなのである。

## 7. 引き継ぐのは成功談だけではなく、国際卓越研究大学に認定されなかった改革案でもある

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
Treat the long-term plan as a smooth success story and one major fact disappears. 早稲田大学は国際卓越研究大学への再挑戦で、2025年12月にrecognitionを得られなかった。

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
Waseda's official statement said it took the result seriously, would reflect on and improve the points raised by the advisory board, and would continue the 2050 reform vision developed through the application process. つまりvisionをdiscardしたのではなく、external reviewで指摘を受けたplanをreviseしながら続ける判断をした。

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
A September 2026 Waseda retrospective similarly explains that, although recognition was not achieved, the process forced long-range discussion of research strength, finance, governance, and the university's 2050 form. これはuniversityによるself-assessmentなのでthird-party evaluationとは分ける必要があるが、reform planのoriginを知る資料にはなる。

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
The new President does not inherit a finished success model. 大きなexternal reviewに出し、selectedされず、それでも残すと決めたplanである。Here, “continuity” necessarily includes revision as well as continuation.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
This is not a judgment on Homma's competence; it is the starting condition. 新体制を追うなら、“what did he say that was new?”だけでなく、“what did the university revise, and what moved into execution?”を見る必要がある。

## 8. 就任メッセージで目立つのは、新しい理念より「理念を実践へ移す」という言葉だ

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
Now read Homma's inauguration message. What stands out is not a brand-new slogan but the promise to move inherited ideals into “more practical action.” 既存のvisionをimplementationへ進める言葉である。

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
Homma explicitly inherits Waseda Vision 150 and the direction described as a Waseda that shines globally. そのうえで、division、climate change、disasters、resources and energy、pandemics、artificial intelligenceによる社会変化を挙げ、knowledge creation through research、high-quality education、social contributionをintegrateする考えを示している。

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
The 150th-anniversary site likewise emphasizes connecting the three pillars rather than treating them as separate brands. 就任messageのvocabularyは、このexisting frameworkと大きく重なる。On day one, continuity plus acceleration is more visible than a strategic reset.

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
Personnel news tempts us to hunt for differences from the predecessor. しかし一次資料から明確に読めるdeltaは、goalsのtotal replacementではない。Homma first says the existing ideals will be inherited, then frames practical action and acceleration as the task.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
That changes the comparison axis. 言葉のnoveltyではなく、long-term visionがbudget、people、research organisations、education systems、collaborationへどれだけconvertedされたか。The message itself shifts attention from slogan to implementation.

## 9. 今後を見るなら、三つの「接続」が実際に動いたかを確かめると変化を追いやすい

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
From here, this is not Waseda's official policy but an observation framework proposed by this essay. 本間体制のsuccess or failureを先回りして判定せず、visionがpracticeへ移ったかを後から確かめるため、三つのconnectionを見る。

<!-- level:2 role:proposal -->
- **研究と教育**：重点研究へのinvestmentやresearch centersの成果が、student learningやdoctoral trainingにどう戻るか。
- **専門と社会**：research outputがindustry、public sector、local communitiesとのcollaborationへどうつながり、そのprocessで大学側のresearchやeducationもどう変わるか。
- **組織と組織**：研究・教育・社会貢献のuniversity-wide platformsが、学部・研究科やexternal organisationsと共同で動くmechanismをどこまで作れるか。

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
This approach neither asks us to accept “contribute to the world” at face value nor dismiss it as abstraction. 共同研究、education systems、doctoral careers、cross-unit projects、external partnershipsなど、connectionが生んだobservable changesへ降ろして確認できる。

<!-- level:1 role:evidence -->
The timeline adds another twist. 本間の現在の任期は2030年9月までで、創立150周年の2032年より約2年早く終わる。At least for his first term, he is not scheduled to preside over the anniversary itself; he is building the runway immediately before it.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
So the 2030 question cannot be “has the 2050 ideal been achieved?” 2032へ向かう大学が、visionを制度・研究・教育・collaborationへ渡せるroutesをどれだけ太くしたか。With long horizons, conversion capacity is more informative at an intermediate checkpoint than final completion.

## 10. 調べる前は「新しい総長の人事」だったが、調べた後は「理念を次の工程へ渡す人事」に見える

<!-- level:4 role:claim -->
Return to the opening puzzle. Why was Homma chosen in June but not President until September 21? 制度上、electionが選ぶのはcandidateで、final selectionをするのはBoard of Directorsだから。That is the literal answer.

<!-- level:3 role:analysis -->
But once the制度 leads into career, research, and the university's long-term plan, that single handoff starts to resemble several others. 研究からuniversity managementへ、専門知からsocial valueへ、2032年のanniversaryから2050年のuniversity visionへ、そしてidealsからpracticeへ。Homma takes office while all of these processes are already moving.

<!-- level:2 role:description -->
His research on interfaces does not guarantee success at organisational interfaces. 国際卓越研究大学のreview resultも、2050年構想のvalidityやfeasibilityを自動的に証明しない。What we can say now is narrower: the new President inherits a university already midway through a long reform process.

<!-- level:5 role:implication -->
Before researching it, this looked like a personnel story: “Waseda's top changes from Aiji Tanaka to Takayuki Homma.” 調べた後は少し違う。It looks like a story about **who takes responsibility at the point where Waseda's long-built ideals must pass into the next stage of practice**. The odd phrase “selected, but not yet decided” turns out to be more than procedural trivia; it is an entrance into the university's current condition.
