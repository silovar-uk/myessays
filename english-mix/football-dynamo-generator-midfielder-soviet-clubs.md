# サッカーの「ダイナモ」はなぜ発電機なのか――The engine behind the team

A football player can be called a **dynamo**. 発電機である。Not a battery. Not a lamp. A human generator, apparently.

UEFA described エンゴロ・カンテ（N'Golo Kanté）as a **midfield dynamo** in a 2026 profile. The article also says he breaks up opponents' attacks and starts his own team's moves. [UEFA](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/02a9-2182e99fa90d-4b9d760fe60e-1000--fenerbahce-in-the-2026-27-champions-league-what-are-their-/)

でもhuman beings do not create unlimited energy. Running makes us tired. なぜ発電機なのか。And why are there clubs named Dynamo Moscow and Dynamo Kyiv? 調べ始めると、electricity、football tactics、そしてSoviet historyが一本の言葉にぶら下がっていた。

This essay follows three questions: What does a dynamo do? Where does the word come from? And does running more always mean playing better?

> 【Research note】Verified facts come from dictionaries, football bodies, and club histories. The two-player comparison later is a **thought experiment**, not an analysis of a real match. The first use of “dynamo” for a footballer has not been established.

## 1. そもそもサッカーの「中盤」とは、どこの何をする人なのか

まずthe basics. Two teams try to score goals. The **goalkeeper** has a special role, while the other players share attacking, defending, and connecting duties.

A **midfielder（ミッドフィルダー）** normally plays in the middle part of the field. But a midfielder is not someone who merely stands in the middle. They receive passes, help move the ball forward, defend, and connect one moment with the next.

Imagine this: your team attacks, then loses the ball. The midfielder who offered a passing option a second ago now has to protect the goal. **Midfield is where attack and defence keep meeting.**

The word *dynamo* is not a formal position in the [Laws of the Game](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-players/). It describes how a player works, not a special registration category.

## 2. ダイナモは選手の住所ではなく、働き方を表す言葉である

A **midfield dynamo** is usually an energetic, hard-working player who contributes in both attack and defence. They keep moving, close down opponents, help teammates, and react when possession changes.

ここで大事なのは**position versus style**という違い。Central midfielder tells us *where* a player usually plays. Dynamo tells us *what kind of contribution* the player is known for.

UEFA's description of Kanté includes both winning the ball and launching attacks. So “dynamo” is not just a distance measurement. It is praise for sustained, useful action. [UEFA profile](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/02a9-2182e99fa90d-4b9d760fe60e-1000--fenerbahce-in-the-2026-27-champions-league-what-are-their-/)

That word compresses many visible and invisible jobs into a single compliment.

## 3. 語源をたどると、サッカーより先に古代ギリシャと電気があった

In English, a **dynamo** first means a machine that converts movement into electricity. A bicycle-powered light gives the idea. The machine transforms energy; it doesn't make energy from nothing. [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/dynamo)

The word goes back to Greek **dynamis**, meaning power or ability. In the nineteenth century, the word became associated with electrical generators. In 1866, ヴェルナー・フォン・ジーメンス（Werner von Siemens）developed a key dynamo-electric principle, building on earlier research. That does not make him the sole inventor of all generators. [Etymology](https://www.etymonline.com/word/dynamo) / [Siemens history](https://www.siemens.com/ja-jp/company/history-heritage/personalities/)

The English dictionary also lists a figurative meaning: an energetic person, or **human dynamo**. This metaphor exists beyond football. [Cambridge](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/dynamo)

So the route looks like this: **power → electric generator → energetic person → football player**. ただし、この語が最初に誰のサッカー記事で使われたかは不明だ。Etymology is not the same as finding the first football use.

## 4. なぜクラブの名前も「ディナモ」なのか。そこには政治史がある

Now the surprise: **Dynamo is also a club name**, with a different historical route.

ロシアの大百科事典によると、1923年4月18日、モスクワでディナモというスポーツ団体が発足した。It was connected to staff of the Soviet security service, the **GPU**. [Russian encyclopaedia](https://old.bigenc.ru/domestic_history/text/1956789)

The official history of ディナモ・キーウ（Dynamo Kyiv）says that a former worker from Moscow's Dynamo factory suggested the name. It represented energy, strength and movement. The Kyiv society was officially registered in 1927. [Dynamo Kyiv's historical account, 2026](https://fcdynamo.com/news/pochemu-13-maya-istoriya-sozdaniya-kievskogo-dinamo)

This matters because a football club's name can preserve the institutions of the society in which it was formed. It is not simply an advertisement for energetic football. Not every modern Dinamo club has the same history, either.

**The player's nickname and the club name share a word, but we have no evidence that one directly created the other.** 語源が同じでも、歴史の道筋は異なる。

## 5. ダイナモの一番大切な仕事は、ボールを持っていないときに起きる

We naturally watch the ball. But most players don't have the ball at any given moment.

Consider losing possession. Player A closes down the opponent: **pressing（相手に寄せて自由を減らす守備）**. Player B protects the space behind: **cover（後方の補助）**. Player C protects the other side: **balance（守備の均衡）**.

The English FA teaches these as connected defensive roles. One player running alone is not enough. Teamwork matters. [FA pressing guide](https://www.thefa.com/bootroom/resources/coaching/spotlight-on-pressing) / [Six pressing capabilities](https://www.thefa.com/bootroom/resources/coaching/the-big-six-pressing)

The question is not only **how fast** someone runs. It is **when, where, and with whom**. The true product of pressing is less freedom for the opponent.

## 6. 似た言葉を覚えると、ダイナモの輪郭がはっきりする

Football vocabulary becomes easier when you separate **location, task and description**.

- **Central midfielder（中央の中盤選手）**: a position description; plays mainly in the centre.
- **Defensive midfielder（守備的中盤選手）**: helps protect the defence and support possession.
- **Box-to-box midfielder（攻守往復型の中盤選手）**: contributes near both penalty areas, moving between defence and attack.
- **Ball winner（ボール奪取役）**: known for tackles and interceptions.
- **Anchor（中盤の底を守る選手）**: holds an important position in front of the defence.
- **Playmaker（組み立て役）**: sets the tempo and chooses useful passes.
- **Hard worker（精力的な働き手）**: a broad description of effort.
- **Dynamo（ダイナモ）**: a lively, influential worker who keeps the team connected.

A player can be both **box-to-box** and a **dynamo**. These are not competing official titles. One describes a role; the other is a metaphor.

## 7. 歴史を少し戻す。1970年代のサッカーは「誰が走るか」以上を考えた

In the early 1970s, Ajax played a famous style called **Total Football（全員で攻守に関わるサッカー）**. Players switched positions, attacked together and pressed hard under リヌス・ミケルス（Rinus Michels）and his successors.

UEFA describes ヨハン・ニースケンス（Johan Neeskens）as one of the early box-to-box midfielders. Yet the wider system was more important than one runner. Players moved into spaces left by teammates. [UEFA: Ajax 1971–73](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0253-0d7ff54908f6-5927d0a16600-1000--the-greatest-teams-of-all-time-ajax-1971-73/)

A clever runner can reduce the amount of running everyone else needs to do. **Good movement can prevent unnecessary movement.**

This history explains tactical development; it does not establish that Ajax invented the word *dynamo*.

## 8. やりすぎの思考実験：12キロ走った選手と10キロ走った選手、どちらが役立ったか

Let's test the popular image of a dynamo.

**Player A** runs 12 kilometres in 90 minutes. He chases almost every ball, but often leaves dangerous gaps. Teammates have to cover for him.

**Player B** runs 10 kilometres. He blocks passing lanes, presses with teammates, and knows when to hold his position. Sometimes he simply stands in the right place.

Who performed better? **We cannot tell yet.** Perhaps A forced many turnovers. Perhaps B allowed opponents too much time. Both numbers and impressions need context.

A better observation checklist is: When did they press? Did teammates support them? What could the opponent do next? Did regaining possession create an attack? Could they sustain the work throughout the match?

These are our editorial questions, not an official scoring formula. **Distance measures activity; it does not directly measure the value of that activity.**

## 9. 実際の数字はどうか。中盤は走るが、勝敗は走行距離だけでは決まらない

Now real evidence. The **CIES Football Observatory** analysed 7,855 matches in 31 leagues from 2020 or the 2020/21 season. Midfielders averaged about **10.6 km** per match; centre-backs about **9.2 km**. [CIES analysis](https://football-observatory.com/Distances-covered-SkillCorner-data-analysis)

FIFA's study of the 2022 World Cup describes midfielders such as マルセロ・ブロゾヴィッチ（Marcelo Brozović）and エリエス・スキリ（Ellyes Skhiri）who sometimes covered more than **13 km**. But physical data depends heavily on team roles, opponents, and match situations. [FIFA positional analysis](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/fwc2022/physical-analysis/what-distances-did-various-positions-cover.php)

FIFA also reported no association between its running-related team metrics and win percentages in that tournament's analysis. This does **not** mean fitness is irrelevant. It means we should not use one number as a shortcut to success. [FIFA contextual analysis](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/fwc2022/physical-analysis/how-do-team-demands-change-in-relation-to-various-contexts.php)

When reading a running statistic, ask: Is this total distance, high-speed distance, or sprint distance? With or without the ball? Over how many minutes? Against which team?

Better data literacy begins with **knowing what a number does not tell us**.

## 10. ダイナモ礼賛への反論。見えない仕事と、働かせすぎる問題

The idea of the tireless worker is appealing. But it can mislead us.

First, holding position can be more valuable than chasing. The FA stresses timing, positioning and teamwork in pressing. [FA](https://www.thefa.com/bootroom/resources/coaching/the-big-six-pressing)

Second, a team that rarely has the ball may need to run more in defence. FIFA uses Japan's 2022 World Cup matches to show how the opponent and possession affect running demands. [FIFA](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/fwc2022/physical-analysis/what-distances-did-teams-cover.php)

Third, work rate has a cost. Coaches and sports scientists also examine acceleration, deceleration, recovery and training load. High effort in one game is not a licence to demand maximum effort every day. [FIFA training-load summary](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/environment/research-brief/high-performance/train/quantification-of-in-season-training-load.php)

This is not an argument against fitness. **The criticism is about turning visible effort directly into a judgement of human or sporting value.**

## 11. 観戦で試す：「ボールを失った後の10秒」だけを見てみる

A simple watching exercise: replay the **ten seconds after your team loses the ball**. Ten seconds is a learning window, not an official tactical standard.

1. **Who presses first?** Does the opponent lose time or passing options?
2. **Who provides cover?** Is the space behind the pressing player protected?
3. **What happens next?** Can the opponent advance, or must they pass back?
4. **If the ball is won, what follows?** A counterattack, a safe pass, or another loss?
5. **Who stays still, and why?** Is it laziness, or smart positioning?

Try watching the *spaces between players*, not just the ball. Sometimes the most important action is preventing a pass that never happens.

## 12. 発電機という名前は、思った以上に的確だった

Back to the generator. A dynamo doesn't make energy out of nothing. It converts movement into electricity that can be used elsewhere.

Football's dynamo works in a similar metaphorical way. Running becomes valuable when it creates time, closes passing lanes, protects teammates, or starts an attack.

So perhaps the best definition is not **the player who runs the most**, but **the player who turns personal effort into collective advantage**.

調べる前は、ダイナモは汗の量への賛辞だと思っていた。Now I want to ask a different question: *Whose next move became possible because of that player's last move?* Even a player standing still may hold the answer.
