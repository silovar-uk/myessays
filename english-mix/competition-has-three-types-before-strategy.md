# 競争戦略を勉強していたら、競争が3種類あると言われた。先に言ってくれ
## Before choosing a strategy, diagnose what kind of competitive world you are actually in.

経営学の本を読みながら、I recorded a voice memo of things I wanted to check later.

The next day, the transcription said this.

**「愛用型とチェンバレン型と俊平型」**

愛用型。

It sounds like a theory about your favorite shampoo.

俊平型。

Suddenly, a classmate named Shunpei has entered management theory.

調べ直すと、「愛用型」は**IO型（Industrial Organization）**、「俊平型」は**シュンペーター型（Schumpeterian）**だった。The speech-to-text was wrong, but that was not the real surprise.

もっと変なことが書いてあった。

**Competition itself has three different ways of being understood.**

I thought I was studying competitive strategy. Instead, I was being told to check the kind of competition before choosing a strategy.

先に言ってくれ。

## 1. Barney was not asking “Which strategy wins?” He was asking whether “competition” means one thing

1986年、Jay B. BarneyはAcademy of Management Reviewに「Types of Competition and the Theory of Strategy」を発表した。

The starting point is almost suspiciously simple. Strategy research asks what firms should do to earn superior returns, but the word “competition” itself is used differently across microeconomic traditions.

Barneyが整理したのが、次の3つだった。

- **IO型**：industry structure strongly shapes firm conduct and performance
- **チェンバレン型**：differences in firm-specific resources and capabilities create different outcomes
- **シュンペーター型**：large changes in technology, markets, or demand can rewrite the basis of competition itself

ここで大事なのは、3つを「A業界はIO型、B業界はチェンバレン型」と永久に仕分ける話ではないことだ。

Barney explicitly notes that a firm may face all three forms of competition at the same time.

分類表というより、three lenses for looking at competition.

[Jay B. Barney, “Types of Competition and the Theory of Strategy: Toward an Integrative Framework,” Academy of Management Review, 1986](https://journals.aom.org/doi/10.5465/amr.1986.4283938)

## 2. IO competition — do not only become stronger; choose a landscape where strength pays

IO型で最初に見るのは、企業の内側より**industry structure**である。

How high are barriers to entry? How many rivals are there? How differentiated are products? How price-sensitive is demand? In Barney’s summary, these structural features shape conduct and returns.

この流れを戦略論に強く持ち込んだ代表がMichael Porterだった。

Very roughly, the IO question is this.

**「どう頑張るか」より、where and under what rules should we compete?**

Instead of climbing the same slope faster than everyone else, choose a slope that favors you. Sometimes strategy even means shaping that slope through barriers or differentiation.

だからポーターのポジショニングは、単なる「ライバル分析」ではない。

It is also about creating or occupying a structure where rivalry does not destroy all the returns.

## 3. Chamberlinian competition — look less at the terrain and more at what only you have

チェンバレン型になると視線が企業の中へ移る。

Barney’s reading of Chamberlin highlights differences such as technical know-how, reputation, brand awareness, and managers’ ability to work together.

同じ業界にいても、企業はまったく同じではない。

They do not own the same resources, knowledge, or organizational capabilities.

ならば、その固有性を最も生かせる戦略を選べばいい。

This is where the landscape begins to look close to the Resource-Based View.

IO型が「どの場所を取るか」なら、Chamberlinian competition asks「自分は何者か」。

Strong technology. Strong brand. Strong operations. Capabilities that are difficult to copy.

競争のルールがある程度続くなら、keeping those capabilities sharp can be extremely rational.

[Jay B. Barney, “Types of Competition and the Theory of Strategy: Toward an Integrative Framework,” Academy of Management Review, 1986](https://journals.aom.org/doi/10.5465/amr.1986.4283938)

## 4. Schumpeterian competition — one day the sharper sword is told, “We are not using swords anymore”

ここから急に怖くなる。

IO and Chamberlinian thinking usually assume at least some stability: enough of the competitive environment can be read for firms to analyze, anticipate, and respond.

業界構造を分析する。

Map your strengths.

脅威と機会を予測して、準備する。

Schumpeterian competition is different because innovations in technology, markets, products, or organization can change the basis of competition itself.

Barney argues that such changes cannot be perfectly anticipated and can redefine which assets and capabilities are strategically valuable.

昨日まで最高だった能力が、today may become strategically irrelevant.

これはかなり嫌な話である。

In a Chamberlinian world, “sharpen the sword” can be perfectly sensible.

シュンペーター型では、剣を20年磨いたあとに**“This round does not use swords.”** が起こる。

And you cannot perfectly know in advance what the next weapon will be.

[Jay B. Barney, “Types of Competition and the Theory of Strategy: Toward an Integrative Framework,” Academy of Management Review, 1986](https://journals.aom.org/doi/10.5465/amr.1986.4283938)

## 5. Put the same TV company into three competitive worlds

ここで少しやりすぎて、let the same television company hold three different strategy meetings.

これは実在企業の戦略を断定するものではなく、a thought experiment to make the three lenses easier to see.

- **IO型の会議**：“Distribution, scale, standards, brand, price position. Which terrain makes rivalry less destructive?”
- **チェンバレン型の会議**：「画質、製造技術、デザイン、販売力。What can we become uniquely good at?」
- **シュンペーター型の会議**：“In five years, will people still need a thing called a TV set to watch video?”

Only the third meeting has a hole in the floor.

しかも厄介なのは、you can be excellent at meetings one and two and still have no answer to meeting three.

Even if you hold the best position in an industry and own the best capabilities, the boundary of competition can move when customers solve the same job in a new way.

逆に、シュンペーター型だけを見て毎週「全部破壊される」と叫んでいても、existing IO and Chamberlinian competition does not disappear.

Barneyの面白さは、he did not turn any single lens into a god.

## 6. “Japanese firms are good at Chamberlinian competition” can describe both a strength and a mismatch

この3分類を日本企業論につないでいるのが、入山章栄の『世界標準の経営理論』や関連解説である。

Iriyama interprets sectors such as Japanese autos and electronics, where many firms competed and refined products over long periods, as close to Chamberlinian competition: technology, people, operations, and differentiation matter.

これは「日本企業は戦略がなかった」という単純な話ではない。

It means that a particular way of competing was powerful under a particular environment.

一方で入山は、when technology and customer needs move sharply, Schumpeterian competition becomes more important.

ここで見え方が変わる。

A company’s weakness is not always a lack of capability.

**It may be a mismatch between the kind of competition it is good at and the kind of competition now dominating the market.**

チェンバレン型で鍛えた「もっと良くする能力」は強い。

But when the question changes from “How do we improve this?” to “What should exist at all?”, that strength alone is not enough.

これは入山による日本企業への解釈であり、Barneyの1986年論文そのものが日本企業をこのように分類したわけではない。That distinction matters.

[ダイヤモンド社『世界標準の経営理論』](https://www.diamond.co.jp/book/9784478109571.html)

[DIAMOND ハーバード・ビジネス・レビュー「日本企業はグローバルで勝ち残るために『シュンペーター型』の競争を学べ」](https://dhbr.diamond.jp/articles/-/10226)

[DIAMOND ハーバード・ビジネス・レビュー「自社に必要な経営戦略を見極めるには『競争の型』を理解せよ」](https://dhbr.diamond.jp/articles/-/10228)

## 7. So what do you do when prediction is weak? This is where real options connects

シュンペーター型を読んでいると、一つ困る。

If the conclusion is only “the future cannot be perfectly predicted,” that is not much of a management meeting.

そこで別系統の研究をつなぐと、real optionsという考え方が効いてくる。

This is not a prescription Barney directly gave in the 1986 paper. It is a later connection to research on decision-making under uncertainty.

リアルオプションの基本は、**keep the right to move later without taking on the obligation to move now**.

Rita Gunther McGrath’s 1997 work applies real-options logic to technology positioning investments and examines uncertainty, option value, and the timing of exercise.

JanneyとDessも、a real option as a decision that creates the right, but not the obligation, to make a later decisionと整理している。

Do not try to predict the future in one giant bet.

小さく試す。

Learn.

伸びる兆候があれば追加投資する。

If it is wrong, exit.

不確実性が高いほど、the capability to be wrong without losing the next move matters alongside the capability to be right.

[A Real Options Logic for Initiating Technology Positioning Investments, Academy of Management Review, 1997](https://journals.aom.org/doi/10.5465/AMR.1997.9711022113)

[Can real-options analysis improve decision-making? Promises and pitfalls, Academy of Management Perspectives, 2004](https://journals.aom.org/doi/10.5465/AME.2004.15268687)

## 8. If you turn the three types into a personality test, you probably break the idea again

ここまで来ると、「自社はどの型か判定する診断テスト」が欲しくなる。

But if we push that too far, we leave the original argument behind.

Barneyは3つの競争を相互に関係するものとして扱い、a firm may face all three at onceとした。

だから実務で使うなら、ラベルを一つ貼るより、three questions are more useful.

- **構造の問い**：Are returns shaped mainly by barriers, scale, networks, distribution, regulation, or other features of the industry terrain?
- **固有性の問い**：差を生んでいるのは、technology, brand, people, knowledge, or organizational capabilities unique to the firmか
- **破壊の問い**：Could a near-future change make that terrain or those capabilities suddenly less valuable?

And all three can easily be “yes.”

その場合、必要なのは理論を一つ選ぶことではない。

You might earn from the existing business through IO and Chamberlinian logic while keeping small options open against Schumpeterian change.

戦略は一枚の地図ではなく、a map, equipment, and a seismograph at the same timeなのかもしれない。

## 9. Before researching this, I wanted the right strategy. After researching it, that question feels one step late

最初、音声メモに残したかったのは、SCP、RBV、ポーター、リアルオプションの関係だった。

Which one should I use?

何が今っぽいのか。

Which theory are Japanese firms missing?

しかしBarneyの1986年論文まで戻ると、there was a question that came earlier.

**What is the competition in front of us actually made of?**

業界構造なのか。

Firm-specific resources?

それとも、その両方を急に古くする変化なのか。

When you study strategy, it is tempting to memorize the names of weapons.

でも本当に先に見るべきなのは、the nature of the battlefield.

And once Schumpeterian competition enters the picture, the thought gets worse.

**Your competitor may not be the company next door. It may be the change that ends the game you are all playing.**

「愛用型と俊平型」という誤変換から始めたメモは、in the end became a note about diagnosing competition before choosing strategy.

Maybe the mistranscription was the better opening after all.
