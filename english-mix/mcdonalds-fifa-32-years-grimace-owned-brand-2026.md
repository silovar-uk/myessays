# Why Is Grimace on a World Cup Cup?――32年のスポンサー協賛が終わる理由

*調査・執筆：2026年10月24日。FIFA、日米マクドナルドの公式発表、企業決算、投資家説明会、報道記事と研究文献を照合した公開資料調査。非公開契約書・独自取材・店舗実測は含まない。事実・解釈・提案を区別した。*

**A purple creature appeared among football legends on a World Cup collectible cup.** サッカー選手でも監督でもない。その名はGrimace（グリマス）。

Then Adweek reported on October 23, 2026 that McDonald's would end its FIFA World Cup sponsorship at year-end after a relationship dating back to 1994. 32年間の協賛が終わるという。[Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/brand-marketing/mcdonalds-ends-32-year-fifa-world-cup-sponsorship-amid-marketing-overhaul/)

The puzzle is that the 2026 tournament reached vast audiences and McDonald's itself ran an enormous global campaign. なのに契約は更新しない方向だ。**Does a successful event necessarily create a successful sponsor?** 契約、earnings、brand assetsの順に追う。

## 守｜まず、記念カップに混じる紫色の生き物を見てほしい

McDonald's produced collectible cups for the 2026 FIFA World Cup. 日本版の6種類には、David Beckham、Ronaldinho、Thierry Henry、Son Heung-min、Lamine Yamal、そして紫色のキャラクター**Grimace（グリマス）**がいる。The US edition offered nine designs, adding players such as Christian Pulisic. 同じglobal campaignでも、国によってline-upが違う。And one member is not even a footballer. [日本版の発表](https://www.mcdonalds.co.jp/company/news/2026/0604a/)／[US campaign](https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/corpmcd/our-stories/article/US-FIFA-world-cup-2026.html)

An odd team selection. ただ、グリマスは大会後もマクドナルドの世界で使える**owned brand asset（自社ブランド資産）**だ。選手の人気がなくなるわけではない。What changes is control: who can keep using an asset, under which rights and costs? この紫のメンバーは、スポンサー終了ニュースを理解する入口になる。

## 守｜スポンサーは「ロゴを置く広告枠」だけを買っているわけではない

First, **sponsorship（協賛）** is not just paying for a logo on a stadium board. 企業は公式な関連づけ、商標利用、会場表示、販促などのrights（権利）を契約で得る。The exact rights depend on the sponsor tier and the agreement. それは普通の広告枠とは違い、大会とのofficial associationを買う行為でもある。[FIFA's sponsor categories](https://tickets.fifa.com/tournament-organisation/partners)

McDonald's has sponsored FIFA tournaments since 1994. 2023年の更新では女子W杯2023と男子W杯2026の**Official Restaurant Sponsor**と公表され、LED boards、Fair Play Trophy naming rights、hospitality opportunitiesなどを含んだ。It does not mean one unchanged contract ran continuously for 32 years. [FIFA announcement, May 2023](https://inside.fifa.com/tournament-organisation/commercial/news/fifa-and-mcdonalds-renew-long-standing-partnership-with-collaboration)

But buying the rights is only the start. 限定商品、CM、アプリ、店舗運営の追加活動を**activation（協賛権の活用）**と呼ぶ。Academic research suggests that good activation is not simply about spending a fixed multiple of the rights fee. 何に投資し、どう事業へ接続するかが問われる。[Research on sponsorship activation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1441352313000028)

## 守｜最大規模の販促をして、最大の成果にならなかった

The 2026 campaign was massive. Adweek reports a roll-out in more than 110 countries, with creative work from Wieden+Kennedy New York. 日本でも限定セット、collectible cups、「ワールドマック」の商品と広告が展開された。A big event received a very big push. [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/brand-marketing/mcdonalds-ends-32-year-fifa-world-cup-sponsorship-amid-marketing-overhaul/)／[日本の施策](https://www.mcdonalds.co.jp/company/news/2026/0604a/)

Then came the earnings call. 2026年第2四半期の**US comparable sales（米国既存店売上高）は前年比0.8％増**で、前年の2.5％増より伸びが低下した。Global comparable sales grew 1.3%. これは会社の店舗実績であり、W杯施策だけの効果測定値ではない。[Q2 2026 results](https://mcdonalds.mediaroom.com/2026-08-04-McDONALDS-REPORTS-SECOND-QUARTER-2026-RESULTS)

CFO Ian Borden attributed roughly **two-thirds of the US guest-count shortfall versus expectations** to problems executing value offers. 残る大部分は6月のFIFA campaignの期待未達によると説明した。He also said the campaign did lift the business. **Underperformed expectations does not mean zero impact.** [Q2 earnings-call transcript](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mcd/transcripts/662377-q2-2026/)

## 破｜決算の数字は「ワールドカップ全体の失敗」を証明していない

Stop the clock. Q2 ended on **June 30**. The tournament ran from **June 11 to July 19, 2026**. 8月の決算説明会は、少なくとも会計上は大会前半を含む6月の米国施策への評価だ。It does not prove that the entire global sponsorship lost money. [Quarterly results](https://mcdonalds.mediaroom.com/2026-08-04-McDONALDS-REPORTS-SECOND-QUARTER-2026-RESULTS)／[FIFA tournament data](https://inside.fifa.com/tournament-organisation/fifa-world-cup-2026-by-the-numbers)

Executives also described operational problems: uneven execution of value offers, limited awareness, reduced digital deals and too many deployments for restaurant staff. 来店客数の不足を説明する要因は複数ある。**The grounded claim is that the June US campaign missed internal expectations.** 長年の契約全体の投資回収率や国別の採算は、公開データだけでは確定できない。[Earnings call](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mcd/transcripts/662377-q2-2026/)

What about FIFA itself? 2026年大会には公式集計で約681万人が来場し、大会関連のFIFAプラットフォーム動画は**290億回再生**を記録した。Views are not unique people, and neither metric is restaurant traffic. スポンサーに必要なのはmassive reachをactual visits and repeat visitsへ変える道筋だ。[FIFA's official figures](https://inside.fifa.com/tournament-organisation/fifa-world-cup-2026-by-the-numbers)

## 破｜企業が借りる「文化の熱狂」と、自分で所有する「ブランドの記憶」は別の資産だ

So what is changing? In September 2026, McDonald's introduced **McDonald's > NEXT**. 商品、顧客、店舗、人材を通じて来店頻度と生産性を上げる戦略だ。The consumer pillar puts fan-led campaigns, brand characters, rituals and loyalty relationships at the center. 単発のbuzzよりもrepeat visitsを重視する。[Company strategy FAQ](https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/corpmcd/our-stories/article/what-is-mcdonalds-next.html)

At Investor Day, global marketing chief Morgan Flatley described **fewer, bigger campaigns** built around McDonald's own iconic assets rather than relying heavily on other people's IP. 長期的な基礎需要を育てる施策への投資をおおむね倍にする意向も示した。This is a stated strategy, not an already proven outcome. [Investor Day transcript](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mcd/transcripts/746101-investor-day-2026/)

Think of it as **borrowed attention versus owned memory**. ワールドカップの商標使用は契約期間・範囲に依存する。Big Mac、Happy Meal、Grimaceといった自社のブランド資産は別の大会がなくても使える。Owned IP is not free or automatically more effective. それでもcampaignの翌日に何が残るか、という問いが立つ。

## 破｜ただし、グリマスがワールドカップに勝ったわけでもない

Does this mean Grimace beat football? Not quite. McDonald's France remains the naming partner of **Ligue 1 McDonald's** under a deal running from July 2024 through the 2026–27 season. スポーツ協賛を全面撤退するのではなく、市場と目的ごとに選び直す可能性がある。[French McDonald's](https://www.mcdonalds.fr/ligue-1-mcdonalds)

Other companies have gone the other way. **Lay's** moved from a regional supporter in the 2022 men's tournament and a supporter in the 2023 women's tournament to an official sponsor of the 2026 and 2027 editions. 同じWorld Cupでも、business fitによってdifferent sponsorship decisions can be rational. [PepsiCo release](https://www.pepsico.com/newsroom/press-releases/2024/lays-named-official-sponsor-of-fifa-world-cup-26-fifa-womens-world-cup-2027)

A tempting story is that FIFA politics caused the breakup. しかし日本語の続報では、マクドナルド側は最近の論争との関係を否定したと報じられた。We have not seen the private rights fee, negotiation record or internal decision memo. 決算の期待未達だけを唯一の原因と決めつけるのも避ける。[Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/brand-marketing/mcdonalds-ends-32-year-fifa-world-cup-sponsorship-amid-marketing-overhaul/)／[Japanese report](https://news.mixi.jp/view_news.pl?from=recent_list&id=9001117&media_id=138)

## 離｜ワールドカップの選手より、紫のキャラクターが経営戦略を説明してしまう

Look at the collectible cup again. Beckham and Yamal carry football's global excitement. Grimace comes from the McDonald's brand world. **Borrowed cultural heat and owned brand memory sit on the same cup.** 広告制作者の意図を推測して断定するのではなく、経営のlensで見たときに現れる対比だ。[Japanese cup lineup](https://www.mcdonalds.co.jp/company/news/2026/0604a/)

McDonald's has already turned fan culture into brand experiences. In 2024, it brought **WcDonald's（ワクドナルド）**, a fictional McDonald's-like restaurant recurring in anime, into real-world packaging, sauce and short animations. 顧客が作った遊びをブランドが受け取り、また広げる。Owned assets can grow through participation, not only through top-down advertising. [Official 2024 campaign](https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/corpmcd/our-stories/article/welcome-to-wcdonalds-mcdonalds-bringsanime-fans-favorite-fictional-restaurant-tolife.html)

So this is not “Grimace defeats the World Cup.” 問いは、attentionを借り続けるのか、repeat visitsにつながるbrand culture and customer relationshipsへ投資を振り向けるのか。Grimace does not need a great scoring record. He needs to remain memorable after the final whistle.

## 離｜大会の客を「顧客」に変える経路を追えば、スポンサーの価値が見える

Reach is only the beginning. A useful funnel is **event exposure → brand recall → product interest → store visit → purchase → repeat visit**. 各段階のconversion（移行）が弱ければ、巨大なviewershipは売上に直結しない。Conversely, a smaller local league might connect to everyday store visits more naturally. これは今回実証された事実ではなく、testable hypothesisだ。

A serious evaluation needs rights fees, activation spending, store traffic, basket size, gross profit, app sign-ups, repeat purchase and operational burden. **Difference-in-Differences（差の差分析）**などを使い、参加店舗と比較対象の変化を点検する。But weather, pricing, competitors and match calendars can confound the estimate. 短期増分と長期のbaseline demandは分けて見る。

Imagine choosing between A, an official global sponsorship, and B, an always-on campaign built on owned characters. A can unlock broad reach; B can be iterated without renegotiating the same event IP rights. でもAには信頼・海外認知の価値があり、Bには話題が飽きられるリスクがある。**A rights-fee comparison alone misses strategic value.**

## 再検証｜今回わかったことと、まだ分からないことを分ける

**Verified facts:** FIFA and McDonald's date their tournament relationship to 1994; a 2023 renewal covered the 2026 men's event; the CFO said the June US FIFA campaign missed expectations; US Q2 comparable sales rose 0.8%; September's strategy centered owned brand assets and loyalty; and October reporting says the sponsorship ends at year-end. [FIFA](https://inside.fifa.com/tournament-organisation/commercial/news/fifa-and-mcdonalds-renew-long-standing-partnership-with-collaboration)／[Earnings](https://mcdonalds.mediaroom.com/2026-08-04-McDONALDS-REPORTS-SECOND-QUARTER-2026-RESULTS)／[Investor Day](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mcd/transcripts/746101-investor-day-2026/)／[Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/brand-marketing/mcdonalds-ends-32-year-fifa-world-cup-sponsorship-amid-marketing-overhaul/)

**Not established:** the single decisive reason for the split, lifetime return on sponsorship, incremental sales by country, or a total retreat from sport. 終了時期は報道とFIFA広報への取材を通じて確認されたものだが、契約本文や両社共同の終了リリースは今回確認できなかった。Missing data should stay missing.

**Next research steps:** track official statements from both parties, compare later quarterly earnings explanations, collect country-level campaign and loyalty evidence, and assess counterexamples such as Lay's and Ligue 1. 単に「なぜやめる？」で終わらず、**which metric improves afterward?**まで追う必要がある。

## 結論｜32年間続いた協賛の終了は、広告の価値ではなく「蓄積する場所」を問い直す

At first, Grimace just looked absurd on a football collectible cup. でも調べると、global football stars and a brand-owned purple character printed side by side capture both the power and the limits of sponsorship. The 2026 World Cup had enormous reach; executives still said the June US campaign fell short of expectations. 大会の成功と企業成果の間には距離がある。

Perhaps the ultimate goal is not to **rent attention**, but to build **customer relationships that remain afterward**. これは筆者の解釈で、スポーツ協賛一般を否定する事実ではない。A character, a repeat customer and a product preference can remain after the event. 紫のグリマスは、案外このニュースで最もbusiness-savvyな「選手」だったのかもしれない。
