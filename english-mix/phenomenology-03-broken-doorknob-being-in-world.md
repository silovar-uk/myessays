# 壊れたドアノブだけが、急に「物」になる
## Working tools disappear into what we are doing.

朝、家を出る。

Grip. Turn. Open.

この三秒で「銀色で円筒形の金属物体」と考えることはほぼない。

The doorknob is already there **as something to open**.

Then it breaks.

回らない。

Suddenly the object becomes loud: ガタつき、ネジ、抵抗、形。

The thing that was almost invisible becomes extremely visible through failure.

Martin Heidegger took this ordinary shift very seriously.

---

## 1. Do we first see an object and then add its use?

A simple model says: first we perceive a physical object, then recognize “hammer,” then remember its function.

でもDIY中、本当に毎回そんな順番だろうか。

The hammer is already “for driving this nail.”

Heidegger describes human existence as **being-in-the-world（世界内存在）**.

We are not detached subjects who first inspect a finished external world.

私たちは最初から、働き、移動し、気にかけ、人と関わるworldの中にいる。

---

## 2. A tool that works recedes from attention

うまく使えている道具は、道具そのものとして前景化しにくい。

When typing, you do not inspect every keycap. 自転車で曲がるとき、ハンドル径を考えない。箸で麺を食べるとき、two parallel sticksの運動として処理しない。

**The tool disappears into the activity.**

Then a key falls off. The chain slips. 箸が折れる。

The equipment jumps forward as a problem.

Broken things are philosophically noisy.

---

## 3. Over-serious experiment: try to explain a doorknob by itself

Describe only the knob:

“Metal. Rotates. Several centimetres wide.”

全然足りない。

To explain why it rotates, we need the door. To explain the door, we need rooms, corridors, entering and leaving, locks, ownership, security.

朝急いで回す理由まで追うと、work, appointments, train schedulesまで出てくる。

One doorknob, investigated too seriously, produces a society.

Heidegger's equipment analysis shows that a tool has meaning within **a network of references, purposes, and practices**.

The world looks less like a warehouse of objects and more like **a web of significance**.

---

## 4. Bad UI can create the same kind of breakdown

A good interface lets you save and move on.

「保存したい」→押す→終わる。

When a button is hidden, unresponsive, or ambiguous, the interface itself moves to the foreground.

Now you are not doing the task. **You are dealing with the tool.**

ハイデガーをそのままUX theoryへ変換するのは乱暴。でも、working tools tend to recede from explicit attention という観察は強い補助線になる。

---

## 5. We are involved before we observe

第1回ではコップを見た。第2回では判断を保留して現れ方を見た。

Heidegger destabilizes the picture further.

We do not begin as spectators.

We open doors. Catch trains. Work. Wait for people.

**Things appear within involvement.**

壊れたドアノブが急に「物」になるのは、普段それが活動へ溶けていたからだ。

In those ordinary three seconds, the knob was not mainly a metal object. It was part of **getting outside**.

明日、普通に回るドアノブまで少し変に見える。

**The tools that disappear most smoothly may be the ones most deeply woven into our world.**
