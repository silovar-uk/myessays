# 会社を買わず、未来に少しだけ出資する――The CVC Paradox

Picture a big corporation with its own R&D team and sales channels. その会社が、小さなスタートアップへ１億円を投資する。But it doesn't buy the company. It may own only 10%. 残りの90％は他の株主のものだ。

So what exactly did it buy? 技術の利用権なら契約で手に入る。A partnership doesn't always require equity. それでも株式を買う選択肢がある。This is **Corporate Venture Capital (CVC)** — and the apparent halfway measure is the interesting part.

**Research cut-off: October 16, 2026.** 本文は一次資料と学術研究を土台とする新規記事のEnglish Mix版。Facts, interpretations, and proposals are distinguished. なお、CVCという略語は投資会社CVC Capital Partnersの固有名詞とも重なるが、ここで扱うのはcorporate venture capitalである。

## 1. CVC is equity investment without necessarily acquiring control――VCとの違いは目的にある

**The basic idea:** an established corporation invests in external startups. 直接出資、専用子会社、ファンドなど複数の形がある。CVC usually involves minority equity investments rather than acquiring control of the entire company. [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/jp/ja/services/advisory/deal-advisory/strategy/corporate-venture-capital.html)／[Research review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2199853122011325)

An independent VC mainly seeks financial returns. CVC also asks a strategic question: **What does this startup change for our parent company?** 技術への接点、新市場の探索、事業生態系の強化などが候補になる。

But ownership does not automatically provide a license, customer data, or control over product decisions. 株式取得と業務上の権利は別の契約だ。That distinction matters more than the acronym.

## 2. Buying shares and using technology are separate transactions――出資しない選択肢もある

Imagine a fictional video equipment maker, Company A, meeting a sports-video analytics startup, Company B. A社が欲しいのはB社の解析機能だ。

There are at least five options: procure the product, sign an R&D agreement, build a distribution partnership, make a minority investment, or acquire the business. 最初の三つは持分取得を必須としない。Even after a CVC investment, A may still need a commercial contract.

**Test the counterfactual:** Could we achieve the same goal without buying shares? 経済産業省は2025年、CVCなどの活動が増える一方、実際の事業創出はまだ十分でないとし、製品・サービスの調達を重要な手段として取り上げた。Sometimes becoming a customer is a better first experiment than becoming a shareholder. [METI 2025](https://www.meti.go.jp/press/2025/04/20250430003/20250430003.html)

## 3. CVC has a boom-and-bust history――未来への投資が景気に振り回される

Corporate venture investing is not new. 米国では少なくとも1960年代から取り組みが見られ、投資熱と撤退が繰り返された。The late-1990s internet boom and the subsequent downturn are a particularly visible cycle.

Henry Chesbrough's 2002 essay examined why corporations repeatedly struggle to capture the value of external startup investments. [HBR, 2002](https://hbr.org/2002/03/making-sense-of-corporate-venture-capital)

**The timing problem:** parent companies are measured every quarter or fiscal year. Startup growth and exits may take many years. 未来の探索に必要な長さと、既存事業の評価期間がずれる。A corporate venture team can be strategically patient in theory but financially impatient in practice.

## 4. Intel, GV, and Sony chose different distances from the parent――組織設計は目的を映す

**Intel Capital** was founded in 1991. In January 2025, Intel **announced a plan** to separate the unit into a standalone fund and remain an anchor investor. これは2025年時点の発表であり、完了を確認する根拠ではない。[Intel's announcement](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/newsroom/news/intel-news-jan-2025.html)

**GV**, launched as Google Ventures in 2009, describes Alphabet as its sole LP. It emphasizes investment autonomy and the ability to back direct competitors of Google. 親会社の資金と、親会社の事業部による投資統制は別の設計要素なのだ。[GV About](https://www.gv.com/about)

**Sony Innovation Fund** began in 2016. ソニーの公式説明は、初期企業、成長段階の進んだ企業、環境、アフリカのエンターテインメント、事業上の親和性など、目的やステージに応じた六つのファンドを紹介している。One label, different investment vehicles. [Sony's official description](https://www.sonyinnovationfund.com/jp/about/)

None of these structures is automatically superior. **Organizational distance is a design choice, not a prestige ranking.**

## 5. Financial returns and strategic returns are two different scoreboards――成果を混ぜない

Financial return measures the investment as an asset: cost, valuation, realized gains, and losses. **Strategic return** asks whether the parent acquired useful capabilities, improved a product, reached a new market, or built a profitable service.

Research describes CVC objectives including strengthening core business, supporting an ecosystem, and exploring technologies or markets. [Research review, 2020](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2199853122011325)

A startup's valuation may double while the parent signs no commercial contract. 逆に株式の評価益がなくても、協業で価値が生まれる可能性はある。Four outcomes exist: both returns, financial only, strategic only, or neither.

Meeting counts and proof-of-concept counts are activity metrics, not necessarily outcomes. **Three PoCs completed** is different from **one product commercialized**. [MIT Sloan Management Review, 2026](https://doi.org/10.63383/Kqjn9904)

## 6. A small equity stake does not buy the future――持分は希薄化し、権利も限定される

A fictional example: pre-money valuation is ¥900 million. A corporation injects ¥100 million in new equity. Post-money valuation becomes ¥1 billion, giving the investor **10%** on this simplified assumption.

If a later round dilutes existing shareholders by 20%, that stake can fall from 10% to **8%**. 実際の経済的権利は株式の種類や優先条件でも変わる。

And equity ownership does not automatically create an exclusive right to technology. B社が競合へ販売する自由を制限するには、別の契約上の論点が生じる。The central question becomes: **How much control is reasonable for such a small stake?**

## 7. Protective terms can become destructive terms――囲い込みは成長を妨げ得る

Japan's Fair Trade Commission and METI issued startup collaboration and investment guidelines in 2022, revised in February 2026. 交渉力の非対称性、知的財産、過度な費用負担、株式の買取請求権などについて具体例を示している。[JFTC guidelines](https://www.jftc.go.jp/dk/guideline/unyoukijun/startup.html)

A redemption or share repurchase clause may impose burdens under particular conditions. ただし、条項の存在だけで一律に違法とはならない。The actual terms and negotiating context matter.

Rights of first refusal or preferential negotiation can also affect future financing or acquisitions, depending on their precise scope. 株式譲渡に関する権利と会社全体の買収に関する権利を同一視してはいけない。

**The paradox:** the investor may try to protect itself by restricting the startup's options, even though those options are part of what makes the startup valuable. [METI investment-contract guidance](https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/newbusiness/startup_investment_agreement_guidelines/startup_investment_agreement_guidelines.html)

## 8. Three parties may want three different futures――利害相反は例外ではない

The parent business unit wants rapid adoption. The CVC team may want attractive investment returns. The startup wants freedom to serve multiple customers and investors.

A 2022 systematic review identifies tensions between exploration and exploitation, corporate and VC identities, and the startup's perception of opportunity versus threat. [Jeon & Maula, 2022](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jbvent/v37y2022i4s0883902622000386.html)

Economic research also distinguishes complementary startup businesses from potential substitutes or competitors. 戦略的な関係が深いほど、常に同じ投資家が望ましいわけではない。[JFE, 2002](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X02000788)

CVC is therefore not just a technology scouting function. **It is a mechanism for managing partially aligned incentives.**

## 9. The case for not investing――仮想案件で代替策をテストする

Return to Companies A and B. Assume B does not urgently need funding, has other customers, and A wants to launch a sports service within two years. これは実在案件ではなく思考実験だ。

- **Option A: Procure.** 導入速度、機能精度、業務改善を測る。No equity return, but direct proof of value.
- **Option B: Invest through CVC.** 将来の持分価値に参加できるが、審査、追加投資、契約、売却の課題が増える。Commercial rights are still separate.
- **Option C: Acquire.** 経営権を得られるが、買収価格と統合コストを負担する。Control is expensive and integration is hard.

If time to market is the main objective, Option A may be the best first step. B社の資金不足が技術開発の障壁で、長期的な成長を共同で支えたいならOption Bの意味が強まるかもしれない。

The practical test is simple: **What would the investment make possible that procurement alone would not?**

## 10. Track value creation, not just deal counts――実務の運用指標

**Practical proposal:** start with the objective, then compare instruments. 事業課題か新市場探索か、財務収益かを明示する。

Before investing, define expected ownership, follow-on funding capacity, dilution, exit paths, and commercial milestones. 担当事業部、意思決定権者、顧客、期限を決める。Review IP, exclusivity, confidential information, ROFR, repurchase clauses, and conflicts of interest.

Measure two scoreboards separately: **financial performance** (cost, carrying value, realized proceeds, further commitments) and **strategic performance** (deployment, sales, cost reduction, learning converted into business). 活動量と成果を混同せず、「出資がなくても実現したか」を可能な範囲で比較する。

A tax note matters in Japan. 経産省の2026年６月更新の案内は、2026年度以降の税制内容について照会を求め、改正後ガイドラインは今後掲載と説明している。Do not assume a tax benefit without checking the current conditions. [METI tax guidance](https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/economy/keiei_innovation/open_innovation/open_innovation_zei.html)

## 11. Vocabulary that changes the investment conversation――知っておくべき言葉

- **LP / Limited Partner**：ファンドへの出資者。通常は日々の投資判断を担わない。
- **GP / General Partner**：ファンド運営を担う立場。制度上の責任は法域ごとに異なる。
- **Balance-sheet investment**：事業会社の資金を直接使った投資。
- **Sourcing / Deal flow**：投資候補との接点を作り、検討案件を集める活動。
- **Pre-money / Post-money valuation**：新規投資前／後の株式価値。
- **Due diligence (DD)**：投資前の財務・法務・事業・技術の調査。
- **Preferred shares**：普通株と異なる分配条件などを持つ株式。
- **Dilution**：新株発行などによって既存持分比率が下がること。
- **Follow-on investment**：後続の資金調達ラウンドでの追加投資。
- **Exit**：株式売却による投資資金・利益の実現。IPOやM&Aなどがきっかけになる。
- **ROFR / Right of First Refusal**：一定の取引機会に優先して応じる権利。対象と条件を必ず確認する。
- **Open innovation**：組織の外部にある知識・技術も活用して価値を生む考え方。

## 12. Owning a little may be a way of respecting what you cannot own――結論

Why buy only a small stake? 最初は中途半端な行為に見えた。But a corporation cannot build, predict, or acquire every promising technology.

Minority investing can preserve the startup's independence while creating a long-term financial relationship. ただし投資条件を厳しくして自由を奪えば、その利点は小さくなる。

So the real question is not **"Should we launch a CVC fund?"** It is **"What must change in our business, and does that change truly require equity?"**
