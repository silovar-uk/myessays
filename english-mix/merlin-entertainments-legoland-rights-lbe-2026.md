# レゴランドはLEGO社が運営していない――Merlin and the Business of Physical Worlds
## Who owns the IP, who runs the experience, and who carries the risk?

You enter LEGOLAND and see LEGO everywhere. Naturally, you might assume that the LEGO Group operates the park. **But the major LEGOLAND theme parks are operated by Merlin Entertainments**, a British attractions company. さらに面白いことに、Merlinは2026年2月、LEGOとLEGOLANDの屋内Discovery Centres **29 locations**をLEGO Groupに売却した。Large resorts stayed with Merlin; the indoor centres moved to the LEGO Group. 同じブランドが、施設の形態によって担当会社を分け直したのである。[2025 Annual Report](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/media/8353/motion-jvco-annual-report-and-accounts-2025.pdf)／[2026 Half Year Review](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/media/8891/2026-half-year-review.pdf)

Why does this make business sense? **Owning a brand and operating a real-world experience require different capabilities.** キャラクターを商品に印刷するのと、何十年も乗り物を安全に運営するのは別の仕事だ。Merlin's core role is to translate someone else's fictional world into a physical destination and run it commercially. 以下ではfact、interpretation、proposalを区別して、現場の仕組みまで調べる。

## 1. Merlinは知的財産の所有者というより、体験の開発・運営者である

Merlin operates theme parks, aquariums, observation attractions, wax museums and character-themed venues. At the end of 2025, its reported portfolio covered **131 attractions in 22 countries**, with **5,847 rooms**. Annual visits were **60.5 million**. ただし131という数字は、2026年2月に屋内LEGO施設29拠点を売却する前の数字だ。It must not be repeated as the current count.[2025 Annual Report](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/media/8353/motion-jvco-annual-report-and-accounts-2025.pdf)

Its portfolio had three principal **attraction formats**: LEGOLAND Resorts, Resort Theme Parks such as Alton Towers and Chessington, and smaller **Gateway Attractions** such as Madame Tussauds, SEA LIFE and Peppa Pig facilities. ただし2025年以降、社内の運営体制は地域別に再編されている。A portfolio category is not always an organizational reporting segment.

**IP means intellectual property**, including protected brand names, characters and creative works. Merlin does not own all the IP it uses. 権利者から必要な権利を許諾してもらい、施設の設計や運営で価値をつくる。**The rights owner and the operator are not necessarily the same company.**

## 2. 大型レゴランドを残し、屋内施設29拠点を売却した事実が分業の輪郭を示す

The annual report says Merlin has **exclusive worldwide rights to the LEGOLAND brand in the theme-park field**. Yet its indoor LEGO and LEGOLAND Discovery Centres were sold to the LEGO Group on **27 February 2026**. The 29-site transaction had a cash consideration of approximately **£200 million**.[Annual Report, pp. 3 and 97](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/media/8353/motion-jvco-annual-report-and-accounts-2025.pdf)

大型リゾートとshopping-mall-based indoor attractionでは、設備投資、滞在時間、リピートの仕方、小売との接点が異なる。ただし、この違いを売却の唯一の動機と断定できる資料はない。What we can say is that the two formats ended up with different operating owners. **The central question is not who owns the character, but who can sustain each format best.**

## 3. 権利の流れとお金の流れを分けると、Merlinの事業構造が見える

A typical location-based licensing arrangement has a **licensor** granting specified rights and a **licensee/operator** investing in design, staff, safety, ticketing and maintenance. 利用許諾の対価は契約によりfixed feesやrevenue-linked royaltiesなどさまざま。This is a general model, not a statement about the terms of every Merlin contract.[Merlin's partnership structures](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/about-us/new-business-development/)

Follow two flows. **Rights flow:** IP owner → licensed rights → operator → visitor experience. **Money flow:** guest spending → attraction business → operating costs and agreed rights fees. さらにlandlord、developer、JV partnerがいれば、家賃や分配金も加わる。**Brand ownership, real-estate ownership and operating P&L are separate dimensions.**

The numbers are striking. Merlin's 2025 related-party disclosure reports **£104 million in royalties to the LEGO Group** and **£17 million to LEGO Holding A/S**, a combined **£121 million** in the royalty column. ただしこれは特定の関連当事者との会計上の取引であり、Merlin全体のライセンス料でも、施設ごとのロイヤルティ料率でもない。It is concrete evidence that access to valuable IP has a measurable financial cost.[Annual Report, Note 6.3](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/media/8353/motion-jvco-annual-report-and-accounts-2025.pdf)

## 4. Merlinはライセンスを「受ける側」にも「与える側」にもなれる

Merlin itself describes several **partner structures**: fully owned operations, joint ventures, management contracts, franchises, licensing agreements and acquisitions. ライセンスを受けて他社の作品を施設化するケースもあれば、Merlin側のブランド・運営方式を現地パートナーへ提供するケースもある。[Merlin: New Business Development](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/about-us/new-business-development/)

So the same company can act as a **licensee, franchisor or operator**, depending on the deal. 「ライセンス企業」という名前だけで相手を分類すると、リスクと収益の負担者が見えなくなる。For any project, start with a simple question: **Who contracts with whom, and who is accountable for the site?**

## 5. ペッパピッグとMinecraftは、同じ体験型ライセンスでも商品が違う

**Peppa Pig** provides an example of a preschool-focused brand. Merlin operates dedicated theme parks, including sites in Florida, Germany and Dallas–Fort Worth. The Dallas–Fort Worth park opened in 2025 with an announced investment of **more than $40 million**. 小型の屋内型施設「Peppa Pig World of Play」もあり、家族の訪問目的と施設の規模に応じてformatを変えている。[Merlin's 2025 announcement](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/newsroom/news-releases/2025/peppa-pig-dallas-fort-worth/)

**Minecraft** poses a different design challenge. How can a creative, open-ended game become a real place? Merlin and Mojang Studios have announced **Minecraft World** at Chessington for 2027, describing it as a **£50 million** development including themed accommodation.[Minecraft World announcement](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/newsroom/news-releases/2026/minecraft-hotel-coming-to-chessington/)

At **Brand Licensing Europe 2026**, Xbox and Merlin representatives discussed the need for both **brand fidelity** and **a new guest experience**. IPを忠実に再現するだけでは、人の流れ、待ち時間、安全設備、施設内の行動は設計できない。The challenge is not copying pixels into buildings. It is translating an interactive world into a safe, repeatable experience.[License Global, 6 Oct 2026](https://www.licenseglobal.com/location-based-entertainment/lessons-from-the-minecraft-location-based-experience)

In 2026, Merlin also opened a **Bluey-themed ride** at Alton Towers and a **PAW Patrol land** at Chessington. 同じ「キャラクター活用」でも、ターゲット年齢、同行者、遊び方を同じにする必要はない。[2026 Half Year Review](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/media/8891/2026-half-year-review.pdf)

## 6. IP権利者に代わってMerlinが担うのは、完成後まで続く運営能力である

Merlin has an in-house design and delivery organization called **Merlin Magic Making**. It works on consumer insights, concepts, creative design, theming, construction and project management. つまりMerlinは、権利を借りて施設を発注するだけの中間業者ではない。It builds specialist capability for turning ideas into attractions.[Merlin Magic Making](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/about-us/merlin-magic-making/)

Once a site opens, the unglamorous work begins: **staffing, inspections, queues, cleaning, food service, complaints, repairs and emergency procedures**. 人気キャラクターでも、長すぎる列や故障には勝てない。Delivering magic repeatedly is an operations problem.

Merlin's 2026 Half Year Review mentions **multi-attraction visits, annual passes, data-led marketing and technology modernization**. 施設を完成させることと、毎日運営し続けることは別。**An IP partnership becomes a business only when guests keep finding reasons to visit and return.**[2026 Half Year Review](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/media/8891/2026-half-year-review.pdf)

## 7. 6,050万人の集客でも、業績は簡単には伸びない

Merlin reported **£1.999 billion** in 2025 revenue, down **2.8% as reported** or **1.6% at constant currency**. Visits declined from **62.8 million to 60.5 million**, a **3.6% decrease**. Yet **commercial revenue per capita** increased **8.6% at constant currency**. これは総合的なチケット客単価が8.6%上昇したという意味ではなく、施設内商業売上に関する指標だ。[2025 Financial Results](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/newsroom/news-releases/2026/merlin-entertainments-delivers-resilient-performance-in-2025-and-establishes-strong-platform-for-sustainable-growth/)

The 2025 accounts reported a **£58 million total operating loss** but **£571 million underlying EBITDA**. EBITDA measures earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, with the 'underlying' measure further adjusted under the company's definition. **It is not free cash flow.** 同年にはMadame Tussauds関連資産について**£262 million impairment**も計上した。A world-famous brand can still lose estimated asset value when expected performance changes.[2025 Annual Report](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/media/8353/motion-jvco-annual-report-and-accounts-2025.pdf)

The implication: **brand awareness attracts attention; it does not guarantee a return on invested capital**. 固定設備・家賃・人件費・維持更新・royalty paymentsを負担しながら、集客を持続させる必要がある。

## 8. 会社の成長史は、ブランドの所有より運営施設の束ね方を重視してきた

Merlin traces its formation to **1999**. It acquired the LEGOLAND parks business in **2005**, brought The Tussauds Group into its portfolio in **2007**, listed on the London Stock Exchange in **2013**, and returned to private ownership in **2019** through a transaction involving KIRKBI, Blackstone-related funds and the Canada Pension Plan Investment Board.[Merlin About Us](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/about-us)／[2019 acquisition announcement](https://www.lse.co.uk/rns/scheme-of-arrangement-becomes-effective-sgs4k4lv1d2d7zj.html)／[Annual Report](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/media/8353/motion-jvco-annual-report-and-accounts-2025.pdf)

KIRKBI's investment relationship does **not** mean LEGO Group and Merlin are the same legal entity. 同じ投資家との関係があっても、brand owner、operator、local subsidiaryは別々に分析する。The 2026 Discovery Centre sale shows that ownership structures can change even when the consumer-facing brand stays familiar.

## 9. 体験を売る会社への最も重い批判は、「事故も現実になる」ことだ

A defective character product may be recalled. A theme-park accident involves people physically present at the site. In June 2015, the **Smiler rollercoaster at Alton Towers** suffered a collision that caused severe injuries. The operating company was fined **£5 million** in September 2016 after a health-and-safety prosecution. これは英国安全衛生庁（HSE）の行政記録でも確認できる。[HSE official record](https://resources.hse.gov.uk/convictions-history/breach/breach_details.asp?SF=BID&SV=4444325001)／[court coverage](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2016/sep/27/alton-towers-owner-fined-smiler-rollercaster-crash)

The case concerned failures in risk assessment, safe working systems and supervision, not merely an isolated worker mistake. 現在のMerlinは安全点検、報告、管理体制を年次報告書で説明するが、開示されている管理体制と安全の完全保証は同じではない。**Real-world IP experiences come with real-world duties of care.**[Annual Report: Health, Safety and Security](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/media/8353/motion-jvco-annual-report-and-accounts-2025.pdf)

There is also **third-party IP dependency**. Merlin's own risk disclosure warns that a loss or weakening of important IP rights may harm visitor demand. 建設した施設は長く残るのに、使用許諾の条件は契約によって変わり得る。We cannot infer individual contract terms from public materials, but the **mismatch between physical capital and contractual control** is an important strategic risk.[Annual Report: Principal Risks](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/media/8353/motion-jvco-annual-report-and-accounts-2025.pdf)

## 10. 日本のレゴランドを見ると、権利・資本・現地運営の区別がさらに明確になる

**LEGOLAND Japan** opened in Nagoya in 2017. The official legal notice identifies **LEGOLAND Japan合同会社** as the seller of tickets and related services. 名古屋市の環境影響評価の公開記録には、開発段階の事業者名が複数記載されている。**A brand name, parent company, local operating entity and property owner need not coincide.**[LEGOLAND Japan legal notice](https://www.legoland.jp/about-us/specified-commercial-transactions-act/)／[Nagoya City documents](https://www.city.nagoya.jp/shisei/keikaku/1008387/1008424/1008434/1008528/1008542/1034164.html)

The annual report also discloses Japanese property leases involving KIRKBI-related entities. 顧客から見えるのは一つの施設でも、裏側では地代、ブランド使用料、運営費が異なる相手先へ流れる。**The visitor sees a unified world; accounting sees a network of contracts.**[Annual Report, Related Party Transactions](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/media/8353/motion-jvco-annual-report-and-accounts-2025.pdf)

## 11. 【提案】ライセンス事業を検討するときは、キャラクター人気より契約と運営を先に確かめる

For an LBE project, I would start with five due-diligence questions. **Rights:** Which media, territories, uses, approvals and terms are covered? **Investment:** Who funds construction, working capital and exit? **Operations:** Who owns safety, staffing and emergency response? **Demand:** What do travel time, families, repeat visits and weather imply? **Exit:** What happens to physical assets, inventory and data when rights expire? これらは一般的な事業検証の提案であり、Merlinの非公開契約を再現したものではない。

A basic analytical model is **annual visits × realized revenue per visit**, followed by explicit operating costs, maintenance, licensing fees and capital requirements. 楽観・標準・悲観のscenario analysisを行い、IPがある場合とない場合を比較する。**The goal is to separate brand-driven demand from operating excellence.** フィジカルな場所を使うスポーツ事業にも応用できるが、クラブ商標、選手肖像、リーグ商標、試合映像の利用権は別々に確認したい。

## 12. 知っておくべき用語――「使用する権利」と「運営する能力」は別物である

**Licensor（ライセンサー）**: 権利を許諾する側。**Licensee（ライセンシー）**: 許諾を受ける側。**IP / Intellectual Property（知的財産）**: ブランド、著作物、商標等に関する権利。**Royalty（ロイヤルティ）**: 契約に基づく権利使用料。**LBE / Location-Based Entertainment（体験型娯楽）**: 特定の場所で楽しむサービス。**Brand Extension（ブランド拡張）**: ブランドを別の領域へ展開すること。**Franchise（フランチャイズ）**: 事業方式やブランドの利用を認める形態。**Joint Venture（合弁事業）**: 当事者が共同で取り組む事業。**Underlying EBITDA（基礎的EBITDA）**: 調整後の利払い・税・償却前利益で、free cash flowとは異なる。[Partnership structures](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/about-us/new-business-development/)／[Financial glossary](https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/media/8353/motion-jvco-annual-report-and-accounts-2025.pdf)

At the entrance to LEGOLAND, children do not think about royalty payments, operating entities or contract expiry. ただその「夢の世界」は、権利をつくる人、使用を認める人、場所を用意する人、事故を防ぎ、毎日施設を運営する人によってできている。**An IP has value, but a visitor's day must be built and delivered.** ブランドを持つ能力と、ブランドを現実の体験に変える能力は、別の専門性なのである。
