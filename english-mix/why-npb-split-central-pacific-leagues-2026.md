# Why Did Japanese Baseball Split into the Central and Pacific Leagues?

*2026年10月29日執筆。This English Mix is a companion to the Japanese original: the historical claims, data, diagrams, and sources follow the same argument. 英語部分は逐語訳ではなく、文脈から読める自然な表現にしている。*

Why two leagues? In Japanese baseball, we say *Se* and *Pa* as casually as we say first base and third base. Yet the distinction is oddly hard to explain. It isn't East versus West, and it isn't a promotion-and-relegation system either.

1950年の球団数を見ると、まず驚く。The Central League started with **eight teams**, while the Pacific League started with **seven**. That's fifteen in total—hardly the perfectly balanced 6–6 structure we know today. If you were responsible for scheduling games, an odd-numbered league would not be your favorite gift. [Baseball Hall of Fame and Museum](https://baseball-museum.or.jp/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/OB-NEWS-64.pdf).

So the real question changes. It isn't merely *why were two leagues created?* It's **who had the authority to admit new teams, and who wanted to preserve influence?** That is where the story begins.

*Photo 1｜1946年、後楽園球場のスコアボード裏から観戦する人々。Source:『昭和 二万日の全記録 第7巻』（講談社、1989年）／[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Korakuen_1946.JPG)（撮影者不詳、日本における旧写真としての著作権保護期間満了の説明あり）。*

## 守｜Before the split, there was just one league

Japan's professional baseball competition did not begin with two leagues. The team that became the Yomiuri Giants was founded in 1934, and seven clubs formed the first professional league in 1936. After the war, an eight-team league resumed full-scale competition in 1946. [野球殿堂博物館](https://baseball-museum.or.jp/exhibitions/history/).

巨人も阪神も阪急も南海も、もともとは同じリーグのチームだった。What we now call an interleague matchup wasn't even a special category back then: there was no separate league to cross.

A league isn't just a list of clubs. It is a set of rules about admission, competition, schedules, titles, and commercial opportunity. だから「新球団を入れるか」という話は、参加者を一人増やすだけでは終わらない。It can change who controls the competition itself.

## 守｜In 1949, eight clubs became two camps of four

One pivotal figure was **Matsutarō Shōriki**, closely associated with the creation of the Giants and the Yomiuri newspaper business. He promoted the idea of expansion and a two-league structure. Companies expressed interest in joining, including **Mainichi Shimbun**. [国立国会図書館の調査記録](https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?id=1000197494&page=ref_view)／[パ・リーグ.comの歴史検証](https://pacificleague.com/news/2020/5/24468).

The existing eight teams disagreed. Hankyu, Nankai, Tokyu, and Daiei supported admitting new entrants; the Giants, Chunichi, and Taiyō Robins were on the opposing side. Hanshin initially supported expansion but switched sides. The result: a **4–4 deadlock**. [パ・リーグ.com](https://pacificleague.com/news/2020/5/24468).

ここは混同したくない。*Supporting two leagues* and *supporting the admission of particular new clubs* were closely linked, but not logically identical positions. Saying "four teams liked two leagues and four didn't" makes the commercial dispute sound like a philosophical debate about tournament formats.

On November 26, 1949, the previous league framework was dismantled and reorganized. The Pacific side dates its founding to that day; the Central League was formally established on December 15. By the opening of the 1950 season, there were **15 teams: eight Central, seven Pacific**. [NPB Pacific history](https://bis.npb.or.jp/pl/history.html)／[NPB Central history](https://npb.jp/cl/history.html).

## 守｜Why did the entry of a newspaper company matter so much?

A strange feature of this baseball story is how many newspapers appear in it. Yomiuri and Mainichi were not just information providers. In that era, newspaper companies could own baseball teams and report the games to readers.

テレビが普及する前、活字メディアは野球情報への主要な入口だった。A curator at the Baseball Hall of Fame, interviewed in the Pacific League's history project, emphasizes the importance of newspaper reporting in that period. [パ・リーグ.com／日刊スポーツの当時紙面](https://pacificleague.com/news/2020/5/24468).

**Interpretation, not a newly verified historical fact:** An entrant backed by a major newspaper could affect more than the number of fixtures. It could change media attention, the visibility of clubs, corporate prestige, and bargaining power.

「読売と毎日が仲悪かったから」で終えれば、話は分かりやすい。しかしそれでは単純化しすぎる。We cannot establish from the sources reviewed that a single newspaper owner's private motive explains every decision. What can be said with greater confidence is that **club expansion exposed a disagreement over membership and control**.

## 守｜The league names don't describe a map

*Central League* and *Pacific League* sound geographic. But the teams weren't divided into inland and coastal areas, or into eastern and western Japan.

セントラル側が「中心・本流」を、パシフィック側が「国際的視野」を意識したという説明は、歴史解説に見られる。The Pacific League's official chronology describes its 1949 founding with the phrase "international outlook." That supports the existence of this aspiration, though it does not fully document the complete naming debate. [NPB](https://bis.npb.or.jp/pl/history.html)／[日刊スポーツ](https://www.nikkansports.com/general/nikkan/news/202011220000316.html).

And why do we usually say *Central–Pacific* rather than *Pacific–Central*? One published explanation mentions alphabetical order. Yet the journalist explicitly treats it as a hypothesis, not an official rule. A familiar expression can outlive the records of how it became familiar.

## 守｜The first 15-team season was not the final design

The inaugural Japan Series produced an almost too-neat historical twist. The **Mainichi Orions**, representing the new Pacific League, beat the **Shochiku Robins** of the Central League, four games to two. The newspaper-backed newcomer at the center of the controversy became the first champion of the two-league era. [千葉ロッテ公式球団史](https://www.marines.co.jp/corp/history/1950.html).

But the fifteen-team arrangement did not last. Nishinippon and Nishitetsu merged in 1951; Shochiku and Taiyo merged in 1953. The Pacific side experienced new clubs and additional mergers. **Only in 1958 did both leagues settle at six teams each.** [野球殿堂博物館](https://baseball-museum.or.jp/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/OB-NEWS-64.pdf).

![Number of teams in each league during the 1950s](https://silovar-uk.github.io/myessays/assets/npb-league-team-counts-1950s.svg)

*Figure 2｜1950年代の球団数（抜粋）。Source: Baseball Hall of Fame and Museum; visual recreated for this essay.*

One practical detail is worth keeping. With seven teams, you cannot have all seven playing head-to-head simultaneously. Someone has to sit out. The museum explicitly cites scheduling difficulties in the seven-team Pacific League. 球団数は制度論の抽象的な数字ではなく、試合機会と興行運営に直結する。

## 守｜Two leagues today do not mean two isolated competitions

As of 2026, each league has six teams. Central: Giants, Tigers, Dragons, Carp, BayStars, and Swallows. Pacific: Hawks, Fighters, Eagles, Lions, Marines, and Buffaloes. There is **no automatic promotion or relegation between them**.

Each league runs its regular-season title race. The **Climax Series (CS)** determines which team advances to the Japan Series; that representative isn't necessarily the regular-season champion. 日本シリーズは両リーグの代表が対戦して日本一を争う舞台だ。

There is also **interleague play** (交流戦), introduced in 2005. In the 2026 season, each club has **143 regular-season games**: 125 within its league and 18 against the other league. These are official games, not exhibitions. [NPB Central schedule](https://npb.jp/games/2026/schedule_note_cl.html)／[NPB Pacific schedule](https://bis.npb.or.jp/games/2026/schedule_note_pl.html).

The important distinction is between *two ranking systems* and *two completely separate worlds*. Today's professional baseball is the former, not the latter.

## 守｜The designated hitter rule came much later

For decades, the easiest way to tell the leagues apart was the DH rule. In the Pacific League, a **designated hitter (DH)** can bat in place of a fielding player, typically the pitcher. Traditionally, the Central League did not use that rule.

でも、年を比べると答えは明快だ。The two-league system began in **1950**; the Pacific League adopted the DH in **1975**. The rule difference cannot have caused a split that happened 25 years earlier. [NPB commissioner statement](https://npb.jp/news/detail/20250804_06.html).

There is another change on the horizon. In August 2025, the Central League officially decided to adopt the DH **from the 2027 season**. It explained both its long attachment to nine-player baseball and the changing domestic and international environment. As of October 2026, the change is still ahead. [NPB announcement](https://npb.jp/news/detail/20250804_05.html).

競技ルールと組織の境界は、別々に設計できる。The two leagues can use the same batting rules and still remain two leagues. That is not a contradiction.

## 破｜In 2004, the two-league structure nearly disappeared

The arrangement came under serious pressure in 2004. Plans to merge the Osaka Kintetsu Buffaloes with the Orix BlueWave triggered discussions about reducing the number of clubs and possibly moving to a single league.

日本プロ野球選手会は球団削減などに反発し、9月18・19日に史上初の公式戦ストライキが行われた。The following season, the newly approved Tohoku Rakuten Golden Eagles joined, and the twelve-team, two-league system survived. [日本プロ野球選手会](https://jpbpa.net/reform/)／[NPB chronology](https://npb.jp/cl/history.html).

There is a striking symmetry: in 1949, expansion put the structure under stress; in 2004, contraction put it under stress again.

Would one league necessarily have been better? Not established. A single table might make the competition easier to understand and produce more different matchups. Two leagues, however, sustain two races and a final showdown between their representatives. **Those are design possibilities, not proof of higher revenue.** To judge them, we would need audience, scheduling, broadcast, and financial evidence.

交流戦の導入は、ある意味で第三の選択肢だった。You can keep the boundary while building bridges across it. In 2005, interleague play created official matchups without dissolving either league. [NPB interleague record](https://npb.jp/interleague/2005/).

## 破｜From rivalry to shared digital business

Another familiar line is 「人気のセ、実力のパ」—the Central League has popularity, the Pacific League has quality. But *popularity* and *quality* need measures. Attendance, television exposure, regional following, and competitive results are not interchangeable.

A more concrete institutional change took place in 2007. The six Pacific League clubs founded **Pacific League Marketing (PLM)**, a jointly owned company involved in league-level marketing and digital operations. Its founding date is May 14, 2007. [PLM company profile](https://www.pacificleague.jp/about/)／[PLM interview](https://www.pacificleague.jp/recruit/interview/).

The clubs still fight fiercely on the field. Off the field, however, they can share distribution infrastructure, marketing expertise, and sponsorship opportunities. 最初は新規加盟をめぐって分かれた球界が、やがてリーグ内で共同事業を育てた。The same institutional boundary can support both competition and cooperation.

## 離｜Competitors need one another to create the product

Most businesses can imagine winning by taking customers away from competitors. Sports have an unusual constraint: **without opponents, there is no match to sell**.

経営学では、この競争と協調の併存を *coopetition* と呼ぶ。Teams compete for wins, talent, fans, and sponsorship. Yet they may also cooperate on schedules, rights distribution, officiating, league-wide content, and international promotion.

The real questions become operational. **What should each club compete on? What is better shared? How should the joint value be distributed? And who gets to approve new entrants?**

Those questions apply beyond baseball—to football leagues, entertainment platforms, and businesses built on shared infrastructure. A league's organizational chart matters less than the incentives and decision rights hidden underneath it.

1950年のセ8・パ7という不ぞろいな数字は、最初は妙な史実に見える。After following the deadlock in 1949, the club mergers of the 1950s, the near-collapse of 2004, and the shared business founded in 2007, it looks different. **The line between the leagues was not a natural law of baseball. It was a negotiated institutional boundary.**

## 結び｜The boundary is historical, but its value must remain current

Why are the Giants in the Central League and the Hawks in the Pacific League? Not because one is inherently better, or because of geography. Their leagues emerged from historical decisions about membership and control.

2027年にはDHという長年の差もなくなる予定だ。If the teams still remain in two leagues, the justification cannot simply be "because they play different versions of baseball."

What value does the boundary create today, and what costs does it impose? That is a more useful question than treating the old division as something obvious. **History explains where the line came from; governance must explain why it should remain.**

## 役に立つGlossary

- **NPB (Nippon Professional Baseball)**：日本のプロ野球を運営する組織・競技体系を指す略称。リーグ名とは異なる。
- **Pennant race**：レギュラーシーズンのリーグ優勝争い。
- **League champion**：通常、レギュラーシーズン1位の球団。CS勝者とは分ける。
- **Climax Series (CS)**：日本シリーズ進出チームを決めるポストシーズン。
- **Japan Series**：両リーグの代表による日本一決定戦。1950年開始。
- **Interleague play**：交流戦。2005年からレギュラーシーズンに組み込まれる。
- **Designated hitter (DH)**：指名打者。パ1975年、セ2027年導入予定。
- **Franchise / home market**：本拠地。セ・パを地理的に分ける基準ではない。
- **Coopetition**：競争しながら協働する関係。スポーツリーグの共同事業を理解する言葉。

## Research note｜What is known and what remains open

**Confirmed facts:** the 1936 one-league start; the 1949 dispute and split; 8+7 teams in 1950; 6+6 by 1958; Pacific DH adoption in 1975; the 2004 restructuring crisis; interleague play in 2005; PLM's founding in 2007; and the decision to introduce Central League DH in 2027.

**Interpretation:** newspaper ownership may have made the admission dispute a contest over influence as well as membership. The institutional split also became a framework for both rivalry and cooperation. 個々の経営者の動機や各制度の増収効果まで実証したわけではない。

**Further research:** compare newspaper coverage from November–December 1949, map ownership and media relationships, and analyze attendance, broadcasting, club finances, and fixture patterns around the 2004–2005 reforms. The aim is not merely to tell a good story, but to test what the boundary actually changed.
