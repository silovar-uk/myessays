# FAとは何を自由にする制度なのか
## Eligibility, declaration, negotiation freedom, and compensation

「FAした」という言葉は、very easy to misunderstand.

It sounds like:

**FA = changing teams.**

でも、第8回で見るのは移籍結果ではない。

The better question is:

**What exactly became free?**

第5回ではドラフトを、

**PICK → NEGOTIATION RIGHT → CONTRACT**

と分けた。

第7回では、contract reservationの中で選手と球団の契約関係を見た。

So now:

**When does a player gain the right to negotiate with other clubs?**

> **Information basis: 2026-08-31**
>
> 日本プロ野球選手会が公開している野球協約のlatest public editionは2025。2026年度FA有資格者の公式名簿はまだシーズン中で未公示。本稿はNPB公式FA説明、選手会公開資料、2025年度公示、2026年の資格取得事例を組み合わせる。人的補償は2026年に改革協議中なので、current ruleとproposed future changeを分ける。

## 1. SHORT ANSWER — Free agency expands the negotiation market

FA means **Free Agent**.

NPBの公式説明では、国内FAはNPBのどの球団とも契約できる権利、海外FAは国外を含む球団とも契約できる権利として説明される。

[NPB「フリーエージェントについて」](https://npb.jp/announcement/2019/fa_about.html)

So:

**Free agency is not the same as changing teams.**

より正確には、

**FA expands the set of clubs a player may negotiate with.**

The key concept is not movement itself.

It is **negotiation freedom**.

## 2. BEFORE FA — The market is restricted before the right is exercised

Episode 5 showed:

**PICK → NEGOTIATION RIGHT → CONTRACT**

新人選手は最初からall 12 clubsと自由交渉する制度ではない。

Episode 7 then showed the contract-reservation structure.

最新公開の2025年野球協約では、契約保留選手について保留球団が次年度契約上の権利を持つ。

[日本プロ野球選手会「野球協約・統一契約書」](https://jpbpa.net/contract/)

A useful editorial frame is:

**RESTRICTED ENTRY**
→ **CONTRACT RELATIONSHIP**
→ **EARNED MOBILITY**

これはNPB official terminologyではなく、制度を読むためのframeworkである。

## 3. ELIGIBILITY — Having the right is not exercising it

The first distinction:

**ELIGIBLE ≠ DECLARED**

FA資格を取っても、自動的に移籍するわけではない。

2025年度はFA有資格選手104名に対し、実際のFA宣言選手は8名だった。

[NPB「2025年度フリーエージェント有資格選手名簿公示」](https://npb.jp/news/detail/20251022_03.html)  
[NPB「2025年度フリーエージェント宣言選手公示」](https://npb.jp/news/detail/20251112_01.html)

That gap matters.

**Eligibility is an option, not an automatic transfer.**

## 4. THE CLOCK — FA has a service-time clock

FA資格はcalendar yearsだけでは読めない。

NPBの公式説明では、年度選手権試合期間中に145日以上出場選手登録されたシーズンを1シーズンとして扱い、145日に満たないシーズンの日数も合算する。

So think in terms of:

**service time**, not just years since debut.

故障者特例や投手向けの登録日数加算などもあるため、actual calculationは単純な年数表より複雑になる。

The beginner takeaway:

**FA eligibility has a clock built from active-roster service.**

## 5. DOMESTIC FA — Freedom inside the NPB market

選手会資料では、2007年オフのドラフト以降に入団した選手について、国内FA初回取得の基本構造は、

- 高校出身：8 seasons
- 大学・社会人出身：7 seasons

と整理されている。

[日本プロ野球選手会「移籍の活性化」](https://jpbpa.net/transfer/)

But do not simplify this to:

“University player = automatically FA in year seven.”

The underlying clock is registration time.

## 6. OVERSEAS FA — The market becomes wider again

Domestic FA and overseas FA are different rights.

**Domestic FA** opens negotiation across NPB clubs.

**Overseas FA** opens the market to domestic and foreign professional clubs.

NPBの公式説明では海外FA資格は基本的に9シーズン。

2026年にも、通算9年の出場選手登録に達して海外FA資格取得条件を満たした選手が報じられている。

So ask:

**Which market became available?**

not simply:

“Does he have FA?”

## 7. DECLARATION — Eligibility must be exercised

FA資格を持つだけでは、negotiation marketは自動で開かない。

選手は所定期間に権利を行使する意思を表明し、FA宣言選手として公示される。

Then negotiation begins.

A useful sequence:

**ELIGIBILITY**
→ **DECLARATION**
→ **NEGOTIATION**
→ **AGREEMENT**

Each is a separate state.

## 8. FREEDOM — Ask what became free

“Free” is too vague by itself.

FAで中心的に変わるのは、

**who the player may negotiate with.**

Episode 7 used:

**PERFORMANCE → EVALUATION → CONTRACT CONDITIONS → NEGOTIATION → AGREEMENT**

FA changes the market around **NEGOTIATION**.

Instead of negotiating only inside the previous club relationship, the player may open negotiations across the relevant market after exercising the right.

This can also change **negotiation leverage**.

But it does not guarantee a higher salary or a transfer.

## 9. STAYING IS ALSO A CHOICE

**FA declaration ≠ transfer.**

2025年度公示では、FA宣言後に元球団と契約締結合意した選手もいる。

[NPB「2025年度フリーエージェント宣言選手」](https://npb.jp/announcement/2025/fa_filed.html)  
[NPB「2025年度フリーエージェント宣言選手契約締結合意」](https://npb.jp/announcement/2025/fa_signed.html)

A player can exercise free agency and still re-sign with the same club.

残留もchoiceの一つである。

## 10. COMPENSATION — Freedom can still create acquisition cost

Domestic FA is not frictionless mobility.

選手会公開資料では、旧所属球団の日本人選手内の参稼報酬順位で、

- A rank: 1–3
- B rank: 4–10
- C rank: 11以下

に分類される。

[日本プロ野球選手会「移籍の活性化」](https://jpbpa.net/transfer/)

Important:

**A / B / C is not a talent ranking.**

It is a compensation classification based on salary rank within the former club.

C rank is outside the compensation requirement, while A and B can involve monetary and/or player compensation under the current published structure.

So:

**more player freedom does not mean zero system cost.**

## 11. 2026 RULE CHANGE WATCH — Current rule vs future proposal

This is the most time-sensitive section.

In 2026, NPB and the players association have been discussing removal of player compensation and possible replacements.

A July 3 negotiation included proposals involving special draft selection rights in future drafts.

But by late July the parties were still discussing compensation details and the rank structure.

Therefore, as of 2026-08-31:

**CURRENT RULE ≠ PROPOSED FUTURE CHANGE**

A headline saying “abolition toward” does not mean the existing mechanism has already disappeared.

This distinction should remain in every future update.

## 12. FA ≠ 自由契約

FA and free-contract status can both lead to negotiation with other clubs, but the route is different.

FA is a right earned and exercised after eligibility conditions are met.

自由契約は、契約保留されないなど別の制度経路で発生する。

So:

**same-looking market access, different institutional path.**

## 13. SYSTEM VIEW — Player freedom and club continuity

FA includes more than one idea:

- eligibility clock
- declaration
- domestic / international market scope
- compensation
- re-signing with the former club

That makes it useful to read as a design tension between:

**PLAYER FREEDOM**

and

**CLUB CONTINUITY / COMPENSATION**

The existence of a rule does not prove the rule is optimal.

選手会が補償制度の見直しを求めていること自体、制度設計が現在進行形のnegotiationであることを示す。

## 14. 30秒でFAニュースを読む6問

### 1. ELIGIBILITY
Does the player have the right?

### 2. CLOCK
Domestic or overseas? What service time created the eligibility?

### 3. DECLARATION
Eligible only, or actually declared?

### 4. FREEDOM
Which negotiation market opened?

### 5. COST
What compensation or acquisition cost may apply?

### 6. CHOICE
Transfer, re-sign, or other options?

This changes the question from:

“Who is moving?”

into:

**“What changed in this player's negotiation market?”**

## 15. TAKEAWAYS

- Free agency is not the same as changing teams.
- Eligibility is not declaration.
- Declaration is not transfer.
- Domestic and overseas FA open different markets.
- FA eligibility depends on a service-time clock.
- Re-signing with the same club remains possible.
- A/B/C is a compensation classification, not a talent grade.
- FA and free-contract status are different institutional routes.
- 2026 compensation reform is still a rule-change watchpoint.
- The best question is: **What exactly became free, when, and across which market?**

## 16. NEXT QUESTION — Mobility can happen without FA

FA is one mobility mechanism initiated through a player's earned right.

But players also move without using FA.

So the next question is:

**トレードとは何を交換する制度なのか？**

That will let us compare **player-initiated mobility** with **club-controlled contract movement**.
