# 日本の主要eスポーツ16チームを、競技成績ではなく商売の形で比べる

*配信者、競技組織、企業グループ、地域クラブ――同じ「チーム」にある四つの顔*

*対象：2026年10月時点で公表情報を確認できる16チーム。分類は筆者による分析上の仮説。*

What exactly is an esports team? 有名配信者の組織、multi-title competitive club、telecom-owned operation、regional teamを同じ言葉で呼んでいる。本稿では16チームをtheir business logicで比較する。

These four groups are analytical labels, not an official taxonomy. 一つの組織がmultiple revenue modelsを持つし、rosters and divisions change frequently. The descriptions reflect information available by October 2026.

## 人気や配信の接点を事業資産にするチーム

### クレイジーラクーン（Crazy Raccoon）

Operated by Samurai Kobo. 競技選手だけでなくstreamers, CR Cup events and merchandiseも持つ。2026年には米国100 ThievesとのApex Legends共同事業を発表した。Its fan relationship extends beyond match days. [公式](https://crazyraccoon.jp/)

### ゼータディビジョン（ZETA DIVISION）

GANYMEDE operates ZETA DIVISION across competition, creator activities, apparel and events. 教育分野への進出も発表。The key hypothesis is brand extension, but profit by business line is not publicly established. [公式](https://zetadivision.com/)／[企業](https://gnmd.com/)

### リジェクト（REJECT）

REJECT combines competitive players, streamers and virtual creators. 2026年にはAnyMind Groupとの提携を公表し、commercial appearances and marketing servicesを拡充する。[公式](https://reject.jp/)

### フェンネル（FENNEL）

FENNEL mixes competitive rosters with apparel, music events and corporate-facing events including recruitment. 2026年にはLeague of Legends国内年間王者になったと公表した。Competition and services require separate P&L analysis. [公式](https://fennel-esports.com/)

### リドルオーダー（RIDDLE ORDER）

RIDDLE ORDER grew around creator-led activity and competitive rosters. 選手のcompetitive reachとstreamer audienceが重なるが、individual popularity does not automatically become team-owned recurring revenue. [公式](https://riddle.info/)

## 複数競技を運営し、勝利と営業を接続するチーム

### デトネーション・フォーカスミー（DetonatioN FocusMe）

DetonatioN FocusMe is operated by DetonatioN within the listed GameWith group. Unusually, public accounts reveal esports-and-entertainment segment performance, not the team's standalone P&L. [公式](https://team-detonation.net/)／[決算](https://static.gamewith.co.jp/sc2026/content-2.html)

### スカーズ（SCARZ）

SCARZ, operated by XENOZ, has a long multi-title competitive history. A broader roster can mean broader sponsor reach, but also higher management complexity. 部門ごとの採算は公開情報からは分からない。[公式](https://www.scarz.net/)

### ヴァレル（VARREL）

VARREL, operated by CELLORB, agreed to integrate with CAG OSAKA in July 2026 and took over the Rainbow Six Siege roster as CAG by VARREL. This is team restructuring, not merely recruiting players. [公式発表](https://varrel.jp/news/2026/07/01/4078)

### グッドエイトスクアッド（Good 8 Squad）

Good 8 Squad focuses heavily on fighting games, with player management and commercial activity. Specialist positioning can be an advantage; more game titles are not automatically better. [公式](https://good8squad.com/)

## 既存企業の事業や設備と組み合わせるチーム

### キューティーディグインフィニティ（QT DIG∞）

QT DIG∞ traces back to Sengoku Gaming. In April 2026, telecom company QTnet absorbed the operating subsidiary. Competitive activity, connectivity and facilities may reinforce one another, but parent-company benefits are not team revenue. [ニュース](https://qtdig.com/news/)

### カドカワ・ファブゲーミング（KADOKAWA FAV gaming）

KADOKAWA FAV gaming operates within the KADOKAWA group and competes in fighting games such as Street Fighter 6. Media and publishing capabilities may matter, but actual commercial integration should be evidenced separately. [公式](https://www.favgaming.com/)

### 福岡ソフトバンクホークス ゲーミング（Fukuoka SoftBank HAWKS gaming）

This esports operation sits alongside the Fukuoka SoftBank HAWKS baseball business and has participated in League of Legends competition. Existing sports assets may help, but baseball fandom and esports monetization are not interchangeable. [公式](https://gaming.softbankhawks.co.jp/)

## 地域企業と競技文化を結ぶチーム

### 名古屋オージャ（名古屋OJA）

Nagoya OJA is a regional multi-sport organization spanning esports and Baseball5. Its local identity creates a different hypothesis from purely national creator brands. [公式](https://www.nagoyaoja.com/)

### 再春館ソル熊本（Saishunkan Sol 熊本）

Saishunkan Sol Kumamoto is rooted in the Saishunkan Systems business. 競技成果だけではなくlocal partnerships, community events and the parent company's objectivesを調べたい。[公式](https://www.sskumamoto.com/)

### 広島チームイクサ（広島 TEAM iXA）

Hiroshima TEAM iXA is operated by game-development company Yaruki Man Tokkys. Beyond players, it has event and technical know-how; esports visibility and B2B service revenue should be evaluated separately. [公式](https://ixa.yarukiman.com/)／[企業](https://yarukiman.com/)

### 福島イブシギン（FUKUSHIMA IBUSHIGIN）

FUKUSHIMA IBUSHIGIN is a Fukushima-based fighting-game team with international tournament results. The business question is whether world-class performance becomes durable local sponsorship and community value. [公式](https://www.ibsg.jp/)

## 16チームを比較しても、チーム単独の利益はほとんど比較できない

There is a transparency gap. Team rosters, sponsor logos and shops are visible; gross margin, prize allocation and fixed costs usually are not. Even GameWith discloses a segment, not every roster's stand-alone P&L.

The key asset may be the team brand, game title, individual player or streamer. そのファン接点が誰のものか、移籍や引退後も継続するかは、commercial durabilityを左右する。
