# Yokohama Has More Than One Center――砂州から都市の四つの中心へ

*Research: 2026-10-24. English Mix Reading Mode; Japanese remains the canonical version. Desk research, not field reporting.*

**Yokohama Station is not where Yokohama began.** 今の駅は三代目。And the port named in the treaty of 1858 wasn't even called Yokohama. 都市を調べようとしたら、まず地名の位置がずれた。

The 1858 commercial treaty named **Kanagawa**, while the trading port opened at Yokohama village in 1859. The first station, opened in 1872, stood near modern Sakuragicho. 同じ名前が、different locations and different purposesを背負ってきた。[財務省](https://www.mof.go.jp/public_relations/finance/denshi/202109/pageindices/index76.html)／[鉄道の歴史](https://www.at-yokohama.net/features/411)

This investigation compares archival maps, city records, port history and population data. The question is not just what was built, but **who made the land, why the center moved and how ordinary life expanded**. 現地実測・専門家取材は行っていない。Evidence and interpretation are kept separate.

## 守｜そもそも横浜とは、地名・港・駅・行政区域が重なったもの

Yokohama is a government-designated city（政令指定都市）with 18 wards. 2026年10月1日の推計人口は**3,753,462人**、2025年10月の面積は**438.23 km²**。These are citywide numbers—not figures for the waterfront tourist area.[人口](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/city-info/yokohamashi/tokei-chosa/portal/jinko/maitsuki/saishin-news.html)／[面積](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/city-info/yokohamashi/tokei-chosa/portal/shuyo-shihyo.html)／[行政区](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/city-info/yokohamashi/ku-shokai/division.html)

But what is 'Yokohama' in a conversation? For sightseeing: Chinatown and the waterfront. For shopping: Yokohama Station. For government and historical memory: Kannai. For residents: neighborhoods such as Totsuka, Aoba, Tsuzuki and Kanazawa. **One name, several geographies.** この区別から始めると混乱しにくい。

The better question is not 'Where is the center?' but **'Center of what?'** Transport, work, memory and daily life do not need to share an address. 横浜の地理は、その役割分担を覚えると見えてくる。

## 守｜「横浜」という名前は、海に横たわる砂州から始まった

The original Yokohama lay on a **sandbar**（砂州）—a long accumulation of sand shaped by water. Its name is explained as a sideways stretch of beach. 関内・関外の周辺には入海があり、現在の海岸線はそのまま昔の海岸線ではない。[財務省](https://www.mof.go.jp/public_relations/finance/denshi/202109/pageindices/index76.html)／[市立図書館の古地図](https://archive.lib.city.yokohama.lg.jp/museweb/detail?cls=collect_03&pkey=00004264)

Yoshida Shinden shows the earlier transformation. A reclamation attempt began in 1656, failed after an embankment broke, and was completed in 1667. **新田** means new farmland; **干拓** means draining enclosed water; **埋立** means filling water with material. 海を土地に変える手法にも違いがある。Yokohama was an engineered landscape long before its international port.[横浜市立図書館](https://archive.lib.city.yokohama.lg.jp/museweb/detail?cls=collect_03&pkey=00004264)

![Map of Yokohama's newly opened port, 1859](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d2/Detailed_map_of_the_developed_port_of_Yokohama_%2815205894711%29.jpg)

*Image: Takashima Kazuyuki, 1859; UBC Library via Wikimedia Commons. This is an archival map, not a modern survey. [Image source and reuse terms](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Detailed_map_of_the_developed_port_of_Yokohama_(15205894711).jpg).*

An old map changes the question. The city was not merely rebuilt on existing streets: **the land itself was remade**. 不自然な川筋や道路の曲線にも、かつての海岸線や工事の痕跡がありうる。Buildings are only the uppermost layer of urban history.

## 守｜1858年の条約は「神奈川」と書き、幕府は横浜村に港を造った

In 1853 Perry's fleet arrived at Uraga. The **1854 Treaty of Peace and Amity** was different from the **1858 commercial treaties**, which provided for foreign trade and named Kanagawa among the ports. 「ペリーが横浜を開港した」と一言で済ませると、条約の役割の違いが隠れる。[横浜の歴史年表](https://yokohamajapan.com/media/ja/overview/basic/history/)

Why not build at Kanagawa-juku? The Ministry of Finance's historical explanation points to limited flat land and the shogunate's desire to keep foreign residents away from the Tokaido highway. 実際の貿易港は対岸の横浜村に造られ、1859年6月2日（旧暦）に開港した。[財務省](https://www.mof.go.jp/public_relations/finance/denshi/202109/pageindices/index76.html)／[横浜港案内](https://www.yokohamaport.org/pr/introduction/)

So the port became a gateway to international exchange, but the gateway's location was shaped by control as well as commerce. **Openness and restriction were present together from the start.** 横浜を単なる『自由な港町』として語るだけでは、この緊張が消えてしまう。

## 守｜関内という地名には、かつて人の往来を区切った関門が残る

Kannai and Kangai mean **inside and outside the checkpoint**. 開港場は水路に囲まれ、吉田橋付近で往来を管理した。Today these names feel like ordinary neighborhood labels, but they preserve a history of controlled movement.[財務省の解説](https://www.mof.go.jp/public_relations/finance/denshi/202109/pageindices/index76.html)

A **foreign settlement**（外国人居留地）was a designated area for foreign residents under the treaty system. **運上所** was a predecessor of customs. Ships, goods, merchants and residents needed both spaces and rules. 港町の街路は、単なる見た目ではなく制度の表現でもあった。

The surviving cityscape is visually appealing, but the street plan also tells a story about access. 港は人や物を通しながら、同時に通行を区切った。A casual walk through Kannai can turn into a lesson in political geography.

## 守｜横浜駅は二度引っ越した。港の終点から広域交通の要所へ

Japan's first railway formally opened between Shimbashi and Yokohama in 1872. The original station stood near today's Sakuragicho, then the second station opened near Takashimacho in 1915. After earthquake damage, the current third location opened in 1928.[駅史](https://www.at-yokohama.net/features/411)

The original terminal served the port well but required through trains to reverse. **頭端式駅** is a dead-end terminal; **スイッチバック** means reversing direction. As rail developed into a regional network, the ideal station site changed. 港の玄関としての便利さと、広域鉄道の便利さは同じではなかった。[横浜駅の歴史](https://www.at-yokohama.net/features/411)

A city's gateway is defined by function, not by the oldest address. 横浜駅の名前だけが残り、港の歴史的中心と交通の中心は離れた。The mismatch is evidence of how urban needs evolved.

## 守｜開港の栄光だけを読むと、震災・差別・空襲・占領を見失う

After the Great Kanto Earthquake of 1923, Yokohama and Chinatown suffered devastating damage. Chinatown's official history also records the killing of Chinese people by Japanese perpetrators. False rumors helped fuel violence against Koreans and others. 災害による被害と、差別に基づく暴力は区別して記録する必要がある。[中華街公式史](https://www.chinatown.or.jp/feature/history/past_present_01/)／[検証資料](https://www.factcheckcenter.jp/fact-check/history/verification-kanto-earthquake-korean-claims/)

The Yokohama air raid of May 29, 1945 killed **3,650** and affected more than **300,000**, according to city records. After the war, occupying forces **requisitioned**（接収した）important buildings and port facilities, disrupting recovery and forcing businesses to relocate.[横浜市の記録](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/city-info/seisaku/torikumi/kichi/sesshu/yokohhama-rekishi01.html)

A simple tale of imported goods and Western buildings is incomplete. The city also contains memories of violence, displacement and exclusion. **What a place celebrates—and what is easy to forget—is part of its history.** 横浜を好きでいることと、負の歴史を正確に見ることは両立する。

## 守｜現在の横浜は港だけでなく、郊外を計画して大きくなった

Yokohama expanded by incorporating surrounding towns and villages. By 1939, its territory was close to today's extent. The city grew from five wards in 1927 to eighteen by 1994. 市の広さは開港場の大きさから自然に決まったわけではない。[市域拡大](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/kurashi/kyodo-manabi/library/shuroku/map.html)／[区の歴史](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/city-info/yokohamashi/ku-shokai/division.html)

In 1965 Yokohama proposed its **six major projects**（六大事業）: central-city reinforcement, Kanazawa reclamation, Kohoku New Town, roads, a subway, and the Bay Bridge. The goal was to manage conflicts among port, industry and housing. 施設を個別に建てるより、都市の役割を再配置する構想だった。[横浜市中央図書館](https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/reference/show?id=1000290399)

Kohoku New Town covers a planned area of around 2,530 hectares in the hills of Tsuzuki Ward. Its principles included preventing uncontrolled development, protecting urban agriculture and resident participation. A **new town**（ニュータウン）is planned urban development for housing and services. 横浜の生活都市としての顔は、港から離れた場所に広がる。[横浜市資料](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/kurashi/machizukuri-kankyo/toshiseibi/jokyo/sonota/nertown/nt.html)

The working port did not disappear. Facilities expanded across Yamashita, Honmoku, Daikoku and Minami-Honmoku as ships and cargo systems changed. **A scenic waterfront and a working logistics port are different but simultaneous parts of Yokohama.**[横浜港埠頭](https://yokohamaport.co.jp/effort/)

## 守｜みなとみらいは、横浜駅と関内のあいだに新しい都心を造った

Minato Mirai 21 was built on former shipyard and railway-related land. The project, begun in 1983, aimed to connect the separate centers around Yokohama Station and Kannai–Isezakicho, adding offices, shops and cultural facilities. **Redevelopment**（再開発）means reworking the uses and functions of existing urban land.[事業沿革](https://yokohama-minatomiraihall.jp/aboutus/history.html)

![Minato Mirai at night seen from Osanbashi](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/48/Views_of_Minato_Mirai_21_from_Osanbashi_Pier_at_night_20211212-5.jpg/3840px-Views_of_Minato_Mirai_21_from_Osanbashi_Pier_at_night_20211212-5.jpg)

*Photo: Suicasmo, December 12, 2021. Wikimedia Commons, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Views_of_Minato_Mirai_21_from_Osanbashi_Pier_at_night_20211212-5.jpg). Unmodified.*

Yokohama Landmark Tower opened in 1993—more than 130 years after the port. [事業年表](https://www.mm21dhc.co.jp/company/history/) Yet the tower now appears in the familiar image of 'historic Yokohama'. **A city's symbol can be much younger than the history it represents.**

## 破｜「横浜の中心」を四つに分けると、街の矛盾が整理できる

Here is an analytical model, not an official classification: **Kannai = historical and institutional center; Yokohama Station = transport and shopping; Minato Mirai = redevelopment and city image; neighborhood hubs = everyday life.** 一つの都市に複数の中心があるのは不自然ではない。

A useful sketch is Kannai [port] → Yokohama Station [regional mobility] → Minato Mirai [connector], with 18 wards of everyday life around them. These are overlapping functions, not a sequence in which one replaces the last. 港湾も行政も鉄道も並行して続いた。The city specialized its centers rather than abandoning them.

Try a thought experiment: remove the recognizable skyline. The schools, people, port and rail network still exist. Remove the main transport interchange, and regional movement becomes harder even if the skyline remains. これは被害予測ではなく機能を分離する思考実験。**Fame is not the same thing as functional importance.**

## 破｜みなとみらいの来街者数は、計測範囲を変えると増減の見え方が変わる

The city reported about **82.6 million** visitors in 2024 and **101.8 million** in 2025. Yet the 2025 release says visitors were **8.3 million fewer year on year**. Wait, what? [2024 release](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/city-info/koho-kocho/press/toshi/2024/0327raigaisya.html)／[2025 release](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/city-info/koho-kocho/press/toshi/2025/0330mmraigaisyaR7.html)

The key is the **measurement boundary**. In the 2025 release, the city expanded its scope to the area east of Yokohama Station and used people-flow data. It would be wrong to subtract the two published totals as though the methods were identical. 比較可能な過去年値の詳細は本稿では未確認。Even numbers from the same institution need a definition check.[市の説明](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/city-info/koho-kocho/press/toshi/2025/0330mmraigaisyaR7.html)

The broader lesson is uncomfortable: **changing what counts as Yokohama changes what looks like success**. Visitor volume and residents' quality of life also measure different things. 数字の大きさより、まず分母・対象区域・測る目的をそろえることが重要だ。

## 破｜観光の中心が強くなるほど、郊外の暮らしを別の尺度で見る必要がある

Yokohama's population is not expected to grow forever. A **2024-published projection** based on the 2020 census estimated a decline from a 2021 peak of about 3.779 million to about 3.01 million in 2070. This is a scenario—not a fact about 2070 or the latest 2026 forecast.[横浜市の推計](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/city-info/seisaku/torikumi/shien/jinkosuikei.html)

Demographic change will not affect every ward in exactly the same way. 郊外では学校、医療、バス、商店街の持続可能性が問われる。More offices downtown cannot prove that daily life improved elsewhere. We need ward-level data rather than impressions.

Terrain is another reason the city cannot be treated as one uniform place. Coastal reclaimed land and inland hills or **谷戸**（small valleys in hills）face different local hazards. 個々の危険度は最新の公的防災地図で確認する必要がある。One postcard is not a risk assessment.

This reframes the management question. **Building an attractive center and maintaining an entire city are different jobs.** 海辺に集まる視線を、住宅地の日常とどうつなぐか。That is a more difficult measure of success.

## 離｜横浜らしさは、地形・制度・移動・再開発が重なって生まれた

Yokohama offers a wider lesson about place identity. A natural feature names the place, political boundaries expand, railways shift activity, and redevelopment creates a new postcard. **Urban identity is layered rather than fixed.** 横浜らしさは、何世代もの選択の結果として生まれた。

On your next walk, ask why a road bends, why a river becomes straight, or why the main station and city hall are far apart. These odd details may preserve old shorelines and institutional choices. 地図の違和感は、都市を読むための入口になる。

## 検証と次の調査｜分かったこと・分からないことを混ぜない

Five verification rules guided the piece: distinguish the treaty name from the port site; separate the three station locations; never merge earthquake damage with targeted killings; check changing visitor-count definitions; and separate observed population from projections. **Fact and interpretation are not interchangeable.**

Next research steps: overlay historical and present maps at the same scale; compare age structure and transit accessibility across all 18 wards; and find the rebased 2024 visitor figure behind the 2025 year-on-year claim. **These are proposed checks, not completed measurements.**

## 結び｜横浜駅と港の距離には、街が何度も生き方を変えた時間が挟まっている

At first this seemed like a naming problem: why is Yokohama Station so far from Yokohama's original port? Two station moves explain the distance. But the sandbar, treaties, war, suburbs and redevelopment change the question. **Why did the city need so many centers?**

**Yokohama did not simply grow around one permanent center. It repeatedly created centers for different jobs.** 横浜駅から港までの距離は、単なる移動距離ではない。It is a distance filled with the city's history.
