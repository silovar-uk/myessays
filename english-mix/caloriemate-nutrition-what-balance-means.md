# What Does CalorieMate Mean by “Balanced”?
## 400kcalの黄色い箱を栄養基準で分解する

Every time I eat CalorieMate, one small question comes back.

**「バランス栄養食」って、what kind of balance?**

ブロックタイプ4本入りは1箱400kcal。Otsuka says its 11 vitamins provide “about half” of a day’s required amount.

400kcal is only about 18% of the adult 2,200kcal nutrition-label reference value.

Yet the vitamins are around 50%.

数字だけ見ると、the vitamins are moving much faster than the calories.

So this time, instead of asking whether the yellow box “feels healthy,” I want to take it apart with numbers.

[大塚製薬「カロリーメイトの栄養」](https://www.otsuka.co.jp/cmt/nutrition/)

[消費者庁「食品表示法に基づく栄養成分表示のためのガイドライン」](https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/food_labeling/nutrient_declearation/business/assets/food_labeling_cms206_250403_12.pdf)

## 1. 400kcal, but about half a day of vitamins

まず、ブロックタイプのチーズ味4本、80gを基準にする。

It contains 400kcal, 8.4g protein, 22.2g fat and 42.7g carbohydrate. そのうち糖質40.7g、食物繊維2g。Calcium is 200mg, iron 2mg, magnesium 50mg, potassium 110mg by analysis, plus 11 vitamins.

Now compare that with Japan’s 2025 nutrient reference values for food labeling. この基準は18歳以上の日本人の食事摂取基準を人口で加重平均した、食品表示用の物差し。It is not a personal daily target, but it works as a common ruler.

One box gives about 18% of energy, 10% of protein, 32% of fat, 13% of carbohydrate, 10% of fiber, 4% of potassium and 29% of calcium.

そしてビタミンAは50％、B1は60％、B2は50％、B6は約54％、ナイアシンは50％、葉酸は50％、ビタミンCは50％、Eは約49％。Meanwhile, B12 is 30% and vitamin D about 31%.

So under the current reference values, this is definitely not a food where every nutrient arrives in the same 18% proportion.

むしろ、**400kcalの箱の中に、特定のビタミンやカルシウムをかなり濃く詰め、食物繊維やカリウムは少なめにした食品**と見るほうが近い。

I had imagined “balance” as something like an even pie chart. そのイメージは最初の計算でかなり崩れた。

## 2. “About half a day” changes when the denominator changes

ここでもう一つ、妙なことがある。

On Otsuka’s product information page, the claim that vitamins provide “about half” of a day is explicitly based on the **2015 nutrient reference values**.

ところが、food-label reference values were revised to reflect the 2025 Dietary Reference Intakes for Japanese.

For example, the reference value for vitamin D moved from 5.5μg to 9.0μg, and vitamin B12 from 2.4μg to 4.0μg. たんぱく質は81gから85g、脂質は62gから70g、食物繊維は19gから20gになった。

One box contains 2.8μg of vitamin D. Against the old 5.5μg value, that is about 51%. Against the new 9.0μg value, it is about 31%.

同じ2.8μgなのに、「half」に見えたり「three-tenths」に見えたりする。

This does not automatically mean the current wording is improper. 消費者庁は栄養素等表示基準値の改正について2028年3月31日まで経過措置を設けており、大塚製薬も基準年を注記している。

But it reveals something important: **“X% of a day” is not an absolute property of the food. It moves depending on the denominator.**

栄養表示、suddenly becomes a math problem.

## 3. “Balanced nutrition food” does not mean “one-box complete meal”

次に、the words themselves.

The block product is labeled 「菓子、栄養調整食品（固形タイプ）」.

But 「栄養調整食品」 is not a government certification meaning “nutritionally complete.”

消費者庁は、栄養補助食品、健康補助食品、栄養調整食品といった表示で販売されるものを制度上は「一般食品」と整理している。That is different from foods that sit inside formal systems such as special dietary use foods.

Otsuka, meanwhile, describes CalorieMate through the concept 「バランス栄養食」.

If those ideas are blurred together, it can sound like “balanced nutrition food = government-approved complete meal.” 実際にはそうではない。

And Otsuka’s own nutrition page says that meals with staple food, main dish and side dish are ideal, while CalorieMate can help when people are busy or cannot get enough nutrition from meals.

つまり、the product itself is not saying, “Eat only this and you are done.”

[消費者庁「特別用途食品制度に関する説明会」](https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/food_labeling/foods_for_special_dietary_uses/notice/assets/food_labeling_cms206_250204_01.pdf)

[大塚製薬「カロリーメイトの栄養」](https://www.otsuka.co.jp/cmt/nutrition/)

## 4. Its origin explains the strange distribution

カロリーメイトは1983年に発売された。

Its roots include the medical enteral/oral nutrition product HINEX-R, developed for people who could not eat normal meals, and Otsuka says the compact logic of space food also influenced development.

その後、一般生活者向けに、「忙しいときの朝食」などを想定したブロックタイプへ展開された。

Seen from that history, the design goal was not “reproduce an entire plated meal.” It was **put a meaningful amount of nutrition into something compact, portable and fast to eat**.

野菜のかさ、汁物の水分、食物繊維の量、料理としての多様性まで全部入れようとしたら、that 80g box would stop being that 80g box.

CalorieMate is not a scaled-down photocopy of a meal. 食事の中から「携帯しやすい栄養」を強く圧縮したものに近い。

That makes the high vitamin density and the low fiber and potassium look less like a contradiction and more like a design trade-off.

## 5. What if all 2,200kcal came from CalorieMate?

ここで、a calculation nobody asked for.

The adult nutrition-label reference is 2,200kcal. Since one block is 100kcal, **22 blocks, or 5.5 boxes**, would mathematically reach 2,200kcal.

チーズ味で計算すると、protein 46.2g, fat 122.1g, carbohydrate about 234.9g, fiber 11g, potassium 605mg and calcium 1,100mg.

Compared with the food-label reference values, that is about 54% for protein, 174% for fat, 73% for carbohydrate, 55% for fiber, 22% for potassium and 157% for calcium.

さらにエネルギー比で見ると、fat alone supplies about 50% of calories. 厚生労働省の2025年版食事摂取基準では、成人の脂質はおおむね20～30％エネルギー、炭水化物は50～65％、たんぱく質は多くの成人で13～20％エネルギーが目標範囲。

A 22-block day sits well outside that pattern.

当然、this is not an experiment proving “22 blocks are dangerous.” 栄養素等表示基準値は個人の必要量そのものではないし、100％を超えた数字が即座に有害という意味でもない。The point is to expose the product’s architecture.

Scale CalorieMate up to an entire day, and **it becomes obvious that it is not a miniature model of the whole diet**.

[厚生労働省「日本人の食事摂取基準（2025年版）」](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/kenkou_iryou/kenkou/eiyou/syokuji_kijyun.html)

[厚生労働省「食事による栄養摂取量の基準」](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/web/t_doc?dataId=78ab4652)

## 6. The situations where CalorieMate is strong are actually very clear

ここまで数字を並べると、one might swing to the opposite conclusion: “So CalorieMate is badly balanced?”

That is also wrong.

Its strengths are clear: 100kcal per block is easy to count, it is portable at room temperature, it can be eaten quickly, and it packs multiple vitamins and minerals into only 400kcal.

朝食を完全に抜く。昼食を菓子パンだけで済ませる。移動中で何も食べられない。

In those moments when a meal falls apart, CalorieMate can be quite rational.

逆に、カロリーメイトだけで一食を閉じるなら、足りにくいものも見えている。Fiber, potassium and protein leave room for fruit or vegetables and protein-rich foods such as beans, dairy, eggs, meat or fish elsewhere in the day.

The useful question is not “How many points does CalorieMate score?” It is **what kind of broken meal becomes better when you insert it**.

万能食として採点すると不思議。As a backup food, the design suddenly looks much cleaner.

---

## 7. Think about “balance” across the day, not inside the box

最初、自分は「バランス栄養食」を、one box contains every nutrient in an ideal ratio, くらいの意味で受け取っていた。

The numbers say otherwise.

400kcalに対して、vitamins are dense. Fat is relatively high. Fiber and potassium are low.

That looks less like a defect and more like the consequence of prioritizing portability, storage, speed and nutrient density.

カロリーメイトは、小さな箱の中に「食事そのもの」を完成させた食品ではない。

It is **a food that makes the nutrition hole smaller when a proper meal fails to happen**.

そう考えると、the unit of “balance” changes.

Do not ask only whether one box is perfect. 朝・昼・夜・間食まで含めた一日の中で、where does it fit?

The yellow box is not a miniature complete meal.

むしろ、食事というシステムが一時的に壊れたときに入れる、a surprisingly well-designed **nutrition patch**.
