# 原画ゼロの美術館――What Does FRAMELESS Really Sell?
## If there is no original painting, what are visitors paying for?

Imagine entering an art gallery in London and seeing **not a single original painting**. それでも目の前にはVan Gogh, Monet, Dalí。Images spill across the floor, climb the walls, and move above your head. ここは**FRAMELESS**。英国の法人登記では業種が**Museums activities**になっている。[Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/13515416)／[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/artanddesign/2022/oct/14/frameless-immersive-art-exhibition-london)

So what is the product? Not the physical painting. **The product is time spent inside a reconstructed visual world.** ただしこれは筆者のinterpretationであって、公式の定義ではない。

## 1. FRAMELESSは四つの常設展示室を自由に巡る、有料の没入型アート施設である

FRAMELESS opened on **7 October 2022** near Marble Arch. Its venue covers around **30,000 square feet**, or about 2,790 square metres. Four permanent galleries reimagine **40+ famous artworks**. Visitors can wander in any order. さらにfifth gallery **Blank Canvas**では期間限定企画も行う。[Opening announcement](https://marble-arch.london/news/frameless-london/)／[Official experience](https://frameless.com/the-experience/)

The galleries are organized by **ways of experiencing art**, not by a single artist. **Beyond Reality** explores surreal worlds with Dalí, Bosch, Munch and others. **Colour in Motion** turns Impressionist and related paintings into animated colour and responds to visitor movement. **The World Around Us** surrounds you with landscapes, cities and seas. **The Art of Abstraction** explores shape, form and colour with artists such as Kandinsky and Mondrian. 重要なのは、*not every room is interactive*. [Gallery guide](https://frameless.com/the-experience/)

## 2. もともと「大きな絵で人を取り囲む」商売は、映像よりはるかに古い

Immersion did not begin with projectors. In the late eighteenth century, Robert Barker developed the **panorama**, surrounding paying visitors with painted views. 1790年代のLondonでも、people paid to feel as if they were inside an enormous picture.[Historical biography](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Dictionary_of_National_Biography%2C_1885-1900/Barker%2C_Robert_%281739-1806%29)

The modern wave includes **Culturespaces**, which introduced digital image-and-music exhibitions in 2012 and opened Paris's **Atelier des Lumières** in 2018. That venue reports **1.2 million visitors in its first year**. Japan's **teamLab Borderless** also opened in 2018. But there is a crucial difference: teamLab primarily presents its own digital artworks, while FRAMELESS transforms historical paintings into a different experience. 同じdigital museumというラベルでは、who creates the artworkが見えなくなる。[Atelier history](https://www.atelier-lumieres.com/en/atelier-lumieres-yesterday-today)／[teamLab announcement](https://www.team-lab.com/news/borderlessopen/)

FRAMELESS did not invent immersion. It redesigned it as a **permanent, multi-artist, multi-room urban attraction**.

## 3. 芸術作品を巨大化するのは、プロジェクターを置くだけの仕事ではない

The 2022 equipment announcement described **more than 50 projectors**, approximately **479 million pixels in total**, **one million lumens of combined brightness**, and **158 loudspeakers**. These are equipment-level figures, not a measurement of the resolution or brightness seen by one visitor. The system also uses servers, calibration cameras and sensors. [Panasonic's technical announcement](https://eu.connect.panasonic.com/no/en/news/frameless-signs-panasonic-official-technology-partner-uks-biggest-permanent-digital-immersive)

Several specialist companies made the space work. **FiveCurrents** helped create the experience; **Luke Halls Studio** created projection content. Credits list 2D and 3D animators, interactive designers, audio specialists, curators and production staff. 映像を流すだけではない。If two overlapping projections have different colours or visible seams, the illusion breaks.[Production credits](https://lukehalls.com/case-studies/frameless/)／[FiveCurrents](https://fivecurrents.com/frameless/)

**Projection mapping** fits visuals to physical surfaces. **Edge blending** hides seams between projectors. **Motion tracking** detects movement and can change the display. これらは別々のtechnical functionsだ。One creates a picture, another makes the room coherent, and a third can make the visitor part of the system.

## 4. 美術の民主化を掲げても、無料の美術館とは価値交換が違う

A physical painting carries information that a projection cannot reproduce: materials, texture, brushwork, scale and traces of age. 一方FRAMELESSは、the painting can move, grow and surround you. They are different value propositions, not interchangeable copies.

As checked in October 2026, online advance adult tickets start at **£29.50**, varying by session. By comparison, the **National Gallery's permanent collection is generally free**. But price alone does not establish artistic quality. 一方で有料のFRAMELESSは、映像・音・設備・サービスを組み合わせた**time-based experience**を売る。[Ticket prices](https://frameless.com/tickets-and-prices/)／[National Gallery](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/)

Does immersion create understanding? Not automatically. FRAMELESS offers artist information via QR guides, including Japanese, and teaching materials for schools. **Availability of explanation is not the same as evidence of learning.** 体験したあと、絵を以前より深く見られるようになったかが本当の問いだ。[Official guides](https://frameless.com/the-experience/)／[School resources](https://frameless.com/school-groups/)

## 5. 「映え」と批判されるのは、来場者自身が展示の一部になるからだ

Contemporary reviews describe children running, crawling and playing within the projections. **London Art Roundup** liked much of the experience, yet wanted more integrated art-historical explanation and noted crowds using phones for photos. これはreviewer's observationであり、全来場者の統計ではない。[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/artanddesign/2022/oct/14/frameless-immersive-art-exhibition-london)／[London Art Roundup](https://www.londonartroundup.com/reviews/Frameless-London)

When the visitor enters the image, **taking a photo becomes part of the experience**. But one person's perfect photo can interrupt another person's quiet viewing. Participation and spectatorship compete for the same space. つまり、freedom to participateにはcrowd managementが要る。

Art historian **Kate Mondloch** places immersive Van Gogh shows within the **attention and experience economies**. Her research is about that genre, not an empirical evaluation of FRAMELESS itself. It provides a useful analytical lens: **What kind of attention does the venue reward?** [Academic article, 2022](https://doi.org/10.3390/arts11050090)

## 6. さらに厄介なのは、名画が古いからといって自由に投影できるわけではないことだ

A famous painting is not automatically free to use. Under the general UK rule, copyright in artistic works lasts until **70 years after the creator's death**, measured to the end of the year. Even when underlying art is out of copyright, photo sourcing, video production, sound recording, music, advertising and contractual permissions raise separate questions. ダリ財団も作品利用のrights proceduresを案内している。[UK copyright guidance](https://www.gov.uk/copyright/how-long-copyright-lasts)／[Digital-image rights](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/copyright-notice-digital-images-photographs-and-the-internet/copyright-notice-digital-images-photographs-and-the-internet)／[Dalí Foundation](https://www.salvador-dali.org/en/dali-foundation/services/rights-and-image/)

For a business case, separate **underlying artwork, source image, adaptation, music, public performance, promotion and reuse**. FRAMELESS's individual licence terms could not be independently confirmed. だから、"everything is public domain"とも"all works were licensed on the same terms"とも書かない。

## 7. 事業のもう半分は、名画を見せることではなく、空間を企業へ貸すことにある

FRAMELESS has a **B2C business** selling tickets and visitor services, but also a **B2B offering**. Its website promotes corporate receptions, conferences, dinners and brand activations. Galleries can display customized content. The 2026 sales brochure gives cases involving **Spotify**, **Clinique**, **Causeway** and **Global Witness**. 名画のスクリーンは、企業のイベントでは別のストーリーを映す。[Events](https://frameless.com/immersive-event-venue-london/)／[2026 sales brochure](https://cms.frameless.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/FRAMELESS_SALES_BROCHURE_2026.html)

Possible revenue categories are admissions, venue hire, customized content, food and beverage, and retail. **These are revenue categories inferred from published services, not verified revenue shares.** 常設施設の設備をdifferent customer groupsに活用できる点は事業上の特徴。ただし、profitableとはまだ言えない。

## 8. 本当に検証すべき批判は、芸術性だけでなく、刺激・環境・採算の三つにある

**Sensory risks.** The official accessibility page says there is no strobe lighting in the main four galleries, but moving imagery, light changes and audio above **85 dB** may affect visitors. Ear defenders and quieter **Chilled Sessions** are offered. 「没入」は全員に快適なわけではない。[Accessibility](https://frameless.com/accessibility/)

**Environmental costs.** Permanent high-brightness projection needs power, cooling and upkeep. FRAMELESS reports energy-saving systems and use of renewable electricity. But sustainability claims must be separated from independently audited annual electricity use and per-visitor emissions. **A policy is not an outcome measurement.**[Sustainability](https://frameless.com/sustainability/)

**Financial sustainability.** The operating company, **FRAMELESS LONDON LTD**, was incorporated on **16 July 2021**. Its former name was IMMERSIVE GALLERIES UK LTD, and Companies House lists accounts for the year ended 2025 filed on **2 October 2026**. However, this research did not verify the numbers in those accounts, so it makes **no claims about actual revenue, profit, attendance or payback period**.[Company record](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/13515416)／[Filing history](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/13515416/filing-history)

## 9. 企画・運営の実務では「何を映すか」の前に「何に対価を払うか」を分ける

**Proposal, not an observed FRAMELESS result.** When designing an immersive venue for sports, arts or entertainment, ask three questions.

- **Customer value:** Do people pay for learning, emotion, participation, memory, or social time? 何を既存の動画や美術館では代替できないのか。
- **Rights and production:** Who owns source images and audio? Who can edit, re-screen, advertise or export the content? 誰がIP riskを負うのか。
- **Operations:** What are capacity, realized ticket price, attendance, utilization, B2B bookings, maintenance costs and accessibility requirements? 開業後の運営をどう維持するか。

A useful distinction: **Admission revenue = paid visitors × realized average ticket price**. The public *from £29.50* price is not the realized average. Then compare total income and the full cost of keeping the venue open. Measurement should separate **fun, learning and repeat-visit intention**.

For learning impact, show visitors the original painting before and after the experience. Ask whether they notice more detail or want to visit the original work. さらにstandard videoやnormal art panelsと比較する。That tests what the immersive format adds, rather than assuming that a big image is educational.

## 10. 額縁を外したのは、絵画からではなく、体験の価値の測り方からだった

Why do people visit a gallery with no original paintings? Not because copies have become the same as originals. Because **people may value an experience the original object cannot provide**. Artists supply the visual starting point; digital designers create movement; engineers connect the space; operators turn it into a place to visit.

But the real questions remain. **What did a visitor see, understand, feel, and remember? Who had the rights to create it? Who paid to maintain it?** 額縁がなくなっても、その外側にはcontracts, projectors, people and a business modelが残る。

## 用語を押さえる

- **Immersive exhibition：** 観客を映像・音で包む展示。必ずしもinteractiveではない。
- **Projection mapping：** 立体的な壁や床に映像を合わせる投影技術。
- **Edge blending：** 複数の投影映像の境界を自然につなぐ技術。
- **Motion tracking：** 人の位置や動きを検知し、システムへ反映する仕組み。
- **Location-based entertainment：** 実際の施設に出かけて購入する体験型娯楽。
- **Public domain：** 著作権保護期間が原則終了した状態。素材調達や他の権利は別。
- **Realized average price：** 実際の有料入場者当たり売上。広告の最低表示価格とは別。
