# 小林萌花史――ピアノを「特技」で終わらせない
## English Mix｜Specialty → Integration → Shared Capability → Bridge

### Abstract

**Honoka Kobayashi entered BEYOOOOONDS with a clear specialty: piano.**

小林萌花は2018年、ピアノという明確な専門性を持ってBEYOOOOONDSへ加入した。

But her story is not simply about being “an idol who can play the piano.”

重要なのは、そのピアノが本人だけの特技で終わらなかったことだ。

It entered songs, concerts, orchestral collaborations, radio, and eventually her own classical recording.

**Her specialty became part of the group's vocabulary.**

専門性が、グループの表現言語へ変わっていった。

This essay follows that change:

**Personal Skill → Group Resource → Shared Capability → Bridge.**

---

## 1｜A specialty existed before the idol career

Kobayashi was born on August 16, 2000, in Tokyo.

本人によると、幼い頃に友人がピアノを弾く姿を見て、自分もやりたいと思ったことが始まりだった。

She later began serious lessons and competitions, and by childhood she was already thinking about music school.

つまり、ピアノはオーディションのために作った武器ではない。

**It was already a long-term discipline.**

長い時間をかけて育ててきた専門領域だった。

## 2｜ONLY YOU――the audition asked for more than singing and dancing

In 2018, Hello! Project announced the “ONLY YOU” audition.

その募集文はかなり特殊だった。

The organizers explicitly asked for people with a skill, talent, or ability they could be proud of.

「誰にも負けない特技」や「才能・技能」が募集条件の前面に出ていた。

Kobayashi later explained that she saw the audition and thought, in effect, **“I can play the piano.”**

This matters because the specialty was not accidental.

**The system itself was looking for difference.**

違う技能を持つ人を採り、その違いを生かそうとする採用だった。

[Hello! Project: ONLY YOU audition](https://helloproject.com/news/8552/)

## 3｜Expertise is not the same as being ready for a group

A specialist can enter an organization with one strong skill and still need to learn many shared skills.

小林はハロプロ研修生を経験していない。

Piano did not automatically teach her idol dancing, group positioning, MC work, or Hello! Project performance habits.

**Expertise needs integration.**

専門性を持ったまま、組織の共通言語も覚える必要がある。

That tension is important.

A specialist should not be forced to erase the specialty, but the specialty alone is not enough.

---

## 4｜Expertise requires maintenance

Kobayashi entered music university while continuing idol activities.

本人は、アイドルになるなら音大へ進みピアノも続けることを家族との条件として受け入れたと語っている。

This was not simply a story of “working very hard.”

**Expertise requires maintenance.**

ピアノは、昔できたから今もできる、という種類の能力ではない。

Practice, lessons, theory, history, reading scores, and performance preparation all take time.

In 2023, she wrote that her first year at university overlapped with BEYOOOOONDS' major debut and was so busy that she could hardly remember it.

大学生活は、加入時の専門性を保存する時間ではなく、更新し続ける時間だった。

[Her university graduation blog](https://ameblo.jp/beyooooonds/entry-12793122473.html)

## 5｜“Eiyuu”――piano enters the group's vocabulary

A major turning point came with **“Eiyuu ~Waratte! Chopin Senpai~.”**

The song uses Chopin's *Polonaise héroïque* as part of a BEYOOOOONDS pop song.

小林はそこで実際にグランドピアノを弾いた。

She told ONTOMO that she felt pressure when she learned she would perform the piece, but she was also excited to present a work she had loved since childhood **as a BEYOOOOONDS song**.

That phrase is the key.

**Piano was no longer only “Honoka's skill.”**

ピアノがBEYOOOOONDSの楽曲の中へ入った。

**Piano became group vocabulary.**

## 6｜A specialist can enlarge the group's options

In 2022, BEYOOOOONDS held its first solo concert at Nippon Budokan.

小林にとっては「武道館でピアノを弾く」という個人的な目標でもあった。

But the stage belonged to the whole group.

This is where individual expertise and organizational scale meet.

**A specialist does not only do a specialist's task.**

専門家がいることで、組織全体が選べる表現が増える。

A live show can include real piano.

A pop song can directly interact with classical repertoire.

A concert can be designed around abilities that already exist inside the group.

---

## 7｜SeasoningS and combinatorial identity

Kobayashi joined BEYOOOOONDS together with Miyo Hirai and Utano Satoyoshi through the same audition.

でも、3人の強みは同じではなかった。

Dance, movement, visual expression, piano — different strengths remained visible.

**The goal was not to make everyone identical.**

違いを消さずに組み合わせる。

This fits BEYOOOOONDS itself: a group whose identity often comes from combinations.

## 8｜Performance skill can become production resource

A specialty can also be useful when the person is not standing at the center of a stage.

コロナ禍では、小林のピアノやアレンジ能力は映像や企画にも使われた。

An instrument, music theory, score reading, and arranging ideas can produce content.

**Performance Skill → Production Resource.**

専門性は「見せ場」だけでなく「制作の選択肢」にもなる。

---

## 9｜BEYOOOOOPHONIC――from vocabulary to bridge

In 2023, BEYOOOOONDS performed with an orchestra in **BEYOOOOOPHONIC**.

小林はクラシックが好きで、ピアノを続け、BEYOOOOONDSへ入ったことが、この公演で一つにつながったと語った。

Later, she explained that the first BEYOOOOOPHONIC was the first time she had played with an orchestra.

At a later edition, she also performed part of Tchaikovsky's Piano Concerto No. 1 after making a request herself.

Now the specialty had another function.

**Bridge.**

BEYOOOOONDSとクラシック音楽の世界を接続する橋である。

This does not mean that Kobayashi alone created BEYOOOOOPHONIC.

The orchestra, arranger, staff, conductor, and all members created it together.

The better claim is this:

**Her expertise increased the number of things the team could realistically imagine doing.**

## 10｜Graduation from university did not force a choice

Kobayashi graduated from university in 2023.

A simple story would ask:

**Idol or pianist?**

でも、その二択にはならなかった。

She remained in BEYOOOOONDS while increasing her classical activities.

**Idol Pop ⇄ Classical Music.**

One side did not replace the other.

The interesting part is the movement between them.

---

## 11｜Honopiano――from group resource to independent work

In November 2025, she released the classical recording **Honopiano** under her own name.

It includes works by Chopin and Debussy.

2018: “Special skill: piano.”

2025: a classical work under her own name.

**Specialty → Independent Work.**

ただし、これは「アイドルを卒業して本当の道へ進んだ」という話ではない。

She released it while remaining a BEYOOOOONDS member.

The specialty expanded without replacing the group identity.

[Hello! Project: Honopiano](https://helloproject.com/release/7581/)

## 12｜“HonoKura”――expertise becomes explanation

In 2025, interfm started **“Kobayashi Honoka no HonoKura.”**

She said she wanted not only to introduce classical music, but also to stay close to people who play instruments and talk honestly about difficulties and mistakes.

弾くだけではない。

She began to explain, host, and translate a specialist world for listeners.

**Expertise → Translation.**

A specialist can perform knowledge, but can also make it understandable.

## 13｜2026――the bridge becomes larger

Hello! Project's 2026 schedule listed **Tabisuru Classic Premium Stage 2026** on August 25 at Sumida Triphony Hall.

Kobayashi appeared as pianist and navigator with violinist Ai Takamatsu, cellist Anri Tsukiji, conductor Kanako Hirota, and the Tacticart Orchestra.

The announced program included Chopin, Debussy, and the first movement of Chopin's Piano Concerto No. 1.

2018年の「特技：ピアノ」から、オーケストラとのコンチェルトとナビゲーションまで来た。

But it would be wrong to say she had “moved beyond idols.”

Her idol experience also travels in the other direction: stage communication, audience awareness, and public speaking can enter the classical context.

**Translation works both ways.**

[2026 premium stage announcement](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000195.000094653.html)

---

## 14｜NO PIANO――a specialist is more than the specialty

In 2026, Kobayashi wrote about performing at Solo Fes without piano.

She called it **“NO PIANO!”** and focused instead on making her own costume, using her newer interest in sewing.

これは人物史の最後に重要な話である。

A person can have a deep specialty without becoming only that specialty.

**Expertise is part of identity, not the whole identity.**

公式プロフィールでも、現在の趣味は洋裁になっている。

This protects the essay from a common mistake: reducing a specialist to one skill.

[Her Solo Fes production blog](https://ameblo.jp/beyooooonds/entry-12958315710.html)

## People × Music × System × Society

**People** — She maintained a specialist identity without allowing it to become her entire identity.

**Music** — Classical repertoire and idol pop repeatedly moved into each other's space.

**System** — ONLY YOU deliberately recruited people with distinct skills, and BEYOOOOONDS kept those differences visible.

**Society** — The idol is not only a generalist who can sing and dance. An idol can also carry a serious specialist field.

---

## Epilogue｜What changes when one specialist joins a group?

At first:

**My Skill.**

自分ができること。

Then:

**Our Resource.**

グループが使えるもの。

Then:

**Shared Capability.**

組織全体ができること。

And finally:

**Bridge.**

外の世界と接続するもの。

A specialist adds value not only because “this person can do something nobody else can do.”

専門家が一人いることで、チーム全体の発想が増えることがある。

We can use a piano here.

We can perform with an orchestra.

We can explain Chopin to an idol audience.

We can bring an idol audience into a classical hall.

**Her specialty did not remain hers alone.**

小林萌花のピアノは、小林萌花だけの能力から、BEYOOOOONDSの表現語彙、そして異なる音楽文化をつなぐ接続点へ広がった。

That is the most important change in her story.
