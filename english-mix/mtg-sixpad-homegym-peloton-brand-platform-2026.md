# The SIXPAD Company Is More Than a Fitness Company

*As of October 29, 2026. This essay separates verified facts, business interpretations, and hypotheses that still need testing.*

A premium exercise bike priced at **¥495,000** and a video membership at **¥990 per month** sounds like a very expensive way to receive motivational messages from the internet. But these prices belong to different stages: the former was the 2021 price of The Bike EX; the latter came with the 2024 CLUB revision. And the paid plan ended on June 30, 2026. 同じ商品の現在価格と誤解してはいけない。[MTG 2021](https://www.mtg.gr.jp/news/detail/2021/04/article_1936.html)／[SIXPAD CLUB 2026](https://club.sixpad.jp/news/20260323)。

Calling it *Japan's Peloton that disappeared* misses the distinction. SIXPAD did not disappear; what ended was the **paid mobile-video membership**. Device control, activity history, and product registration remain available in the free app. つまり「ブランドの終了」と「動画課金モデルの終了」は別の出来事である。[公式FAQ](https://faq.club.sixpad.jp/hc/ja/articles/16652650634895-7%E6%9C%881%E6%97%A5%E4%BB%A5%E9%99%8D-%E3%82%A2%E3%83%97%E3%83%AA%E3%81%AE%E7%94%BB%E9%9D%A2%E3%81%8C%E5%A4%89%E3%82%8F%E3%82%8A%E3%81%BE%E3%81%97%E3%81%9F)。

## 守｜MTG builds brands, not only EMS devices

MTG was established in 1996, is headquartered in Nagoya, and is listed under stock code 7806. Its founder and president is Tsuyoshi Matsushita. ReFa covers beauty and hair care; SIXPAD covers electrical muscle stimulation; Style concerns posture support; ReD entered recovery wear in 2025. 一つの器具メーカーより、**a brand-development company in beauty and wellness**と捉えた方が実態を説明しやすい。[沿革](https://www.mtg.gr.jp/company/history/)／[経営者情報](https://www.mtg.gr.jp/company/message/)。

In FY2025, consolidated revenue was **¥98.81 billion** and operating profit was **¥10.665 billion**. ReFa accounted for about ¥72.8 billion in brand sales, compared with approximately ¥14.2 billion for SIXPAD. In other words, hair care contributes more to MTG than the famous abs devices. SIXPAD is a highly recognizable door into a larger business.[MTG IR](https://www.mtg.gr.jp/ir/finance/recent.html)／[2025年ブランド別報道](https://www.fashionsnap.com/article/2025-11-12/mtg-2025/)。

A practical accounting trap: MTG's reportable segments primarily follow **distribution channels**—direct marketing, professional outlets, retail stores—rather than brands. "SIXPAD revenue" and "Direct Marketing revenue" are not additive categories. One asks *what* was sold; the other asks *through which channel*. 混ぜると二重計上の原因になる。[2026年3Q資料](https://financialfilings.com/filings/mtg-co-ltd/earnings-release/2026/49914014/)。

## 守｜From automobiles to ReFa and SIXPAD: the continuity is commercial

Matsushita became independent in an automotive business in 1994 and founded the company in 1996. The timeline then moves through Kirala water delivery (2004), ReFa (2009), SIXPAD with Cristiano Ronaldo (2015), and the Tokyo Stock Exchange Mothers listing (2018). 車、水、美容、筋肉。The categories change, but the recurring capability appears to be turning everyday aspirations into premium products.[History](https://www.mtg.gr.jp/company/history/)／[Founder interview](https://www.projectdesign.jp/articles/173929fa-f841-41be-af82-abd2902b2357)。

The 2014 launch of MDNA SKIN with Madonna reveals another pattern: celebrity collaboration can connect product development, differentiation, and attention. ただし、a famous partner is not clinical evidence. ブランドへの信頼と機器の効果は、異なる根拠で評価しなければならない。[MTG沿革](https://www.mtg.gr.jp/company/history/)。

ReD recovery wear launched in 2025. By 2026, filings describe growth across ReFa, SIXPAD, and ReD. A reasonable interpretation is **category expansion through brands and channels**, not reliance on a single technological invention. ただし、すべての新ブランドが同じ仕組みで成功したと証明されたわけではない。[公式沿革](https://www.mtg.gr.jp/company/history/)／[決算](https://financialfilings.com/filings/mtg-co-ltd/earnings-release/2026/49914014/)。

## 守｜EMS stimulates muscle; it does not replace every benefit of exercise

EMS stands for **Electrical Muscle Stimulation**. Electrodes apply impulses that make muscles contract. SIXPAD markets its own CMM Pulse waveform and has expanded from abdominal devices to legs, core, and full-body suits. 「筋肉の動きを補助する」ことと「運動のあらゆる効果を代替する」ことは違う。[MTGの技術説明](https://www.mtg.gr.jp/news/detail/2020/10/article_1889.html)。

A 2023 systematic review included **10 studies and 174 healthy participants**. Strength gains were reported, but improvements in strength-related functional outcomes were not found, and protocols varied substantially. This is evidence about EMS interventions generally—not a blanket verification of every SIXPAD claim.[Peer-reviewed review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36731008/)。

For assessment, separate **muscle activation**, **measured strength**, **daily-life function**, **fat loss**, and **long-term adherence**. An established mechanism does not automatically establish all downstream outcomes. 作用機序から購入者の生活改善まで、一足飛びにはつなげない。

## 守｜HOME GYM resembled a connected-fitness bundle

SIXPAD HOME GYM launched on October 20, 2020. The concept synchronized EMS stimulation with instruction, music, and video—branded **SIXPAD WAVE**—using a Powersuit and optionally a dedicated bike. The live studio was in Harajuku. 自宅で一人で運動する孤独を、connected fitness experienceに変える構想だった。[MTG 2020](https://www.mtg.gr.jp/news/detail/2020/10/article_1889.html)。

The 2021 offer listed a Powersuit at **¥118,800**, The Bike at **¥132,000**, The Bike EX at **¥495,000**, and the Standard Plan at **¥4,180 monthly**. These are historical prices. A large upfront purchase plus a recurring fee: that is the classic connected-hardware proposition.[MTG announcement](https://www.mtg.gr.jp/news/detail/2021/04/article_1936.html)。

## 守｜Peloton makes the subscription a central economic engine

Peloton is known for connected bikes and treadmills, instructor-led classes, activity tracking, and ongoing membership. 「画面付きバイク」という見た目よりも、whether the content and community make continued use valuableが比較の核心になる。

Peloton's FY2026 revenue was **$2.446 billion**, including **$1.6756 billion in subscriptions (68.5%)**. Subscription gross margin reached **71.4%**. Yet paid connected-fitness subscriptions ended at **2.553 million**, down **8.8% year on year**. Recurring revenue can be large without guaranteeing subscription growth.[Peloton FY2026 results](https://investor.onepeloton.com/news-releases/news-release-details/peloton-announces-q4-fy2026-financial-results/)。

HOME GYM also connected hardware and video. But the publicly checked material does not establish Peloton-like subscription scale at MTG. **Similar product architecture does not prove similar unit economics.** 写真比較だけでは企業の強みは判定できない。

## 破｜The move from ¥4,180 to free is clear; the exact cause is not

On April 22, 2024, HOME GYM became SIXPAD CLUB. The monthly price dropped **¥4,180 → ¥990**, while Bluetooth controls, training history, and warranty registration became free features. Live-streaming and content updates moved to an occasional schedule. これはprice cutだけではなく、the target user changed—from paid class members toward SIXPAD owners.[2024 official notice](https://www.mtg.gr.jp/news/detail/2024/03/article_2250.html)。

The paid tier ended on **June 30, 2026**. March was the final billed month; April–June were free for existing users. Selected videos moved to YouTube, but synchronized EMS video control and VOD-based activity logging were not retained. The Bike tablets received separate guidance. 有料サービスの終了と、機器アプリの消滅は別である。[Official notice](https://club.sixpad.jp/news/20260323)。

Did the strategy fail? We lack member counts, churn, content costs, and service-level profit. Plausible **hypotheses**, not findings, include insufficient subscription economics, a shift toward free owner services, or prioritizing product sales over video production. 公式説明は「サービス提供体制の見直し」であり、財務上の撤退原因を公表したものではない。

A useful thought experiment: **10,000 hypothetical subscribers × ¥990 × 12 months = ¥118.8 million annual gross billings**. This is not MTG's disclosed subscriber base. Without content, app, support, and acquisition costs, profit cannot be inferred. Subscription revenue is recurring; so is the obligation to deliver value.

## 破｜Strong company growth does not validate every product line

MTG reported **¥102.486 billion** in revenue and **¥12.667 billion** in operating profit for the first nine months of FY2026, increases of 46.3% and 35.8% respectively. Its full-year guidance was raised to ¥142 billion revenue and ¥16 billion operating profit. ここでの2026年9月期は、9月末までの通期実績ではなく、6月末までの累計実績と会社予想を含む。[2026年3Q filing](https://financialfilings.com/filings/mtg-co-ltd/earnings-release/2026/49914014/)。

The 2026 filing attributes growth to products such as ReFa hair-care items, SIXPAD Foot Fit, and ReD wear. MTG therefore has growth routes outside paid fitness content. **Corporate profit and HOME GYM unit economics are different questions.** 一方の好調だけで、もう一方の収益性を証明することはできない。[3Q filing](https://financialfilings.com/filings/mtg-co-ltd/earnings-release/2026/49914014/)。

Retail stores, salons, hotels, and e-commerce are not merely checkout counters; they can also create trials and recommendations. 評価すべきはbike specificationだけでなく、**distribution and discovery**の仕組み。This is an interpretation of MTG's channels, not proof that every initiative has positive ROI.

## 破｜The 2019 accounting case exposes a different kind of organizational risk

There is also a documented governance failure. On September 6, 2019, the Tokyo Stock Exchange imposed a public disciplinary measure and requested an improvement report. JPX described revenue recognized before meeting recognition requirements and false explanations to the auditor, affecting disclosures from FY2018 through the first half of FY2019. これは風評ではなく、取引所による公式公表である。[JPX notice](https://www.jpx.co.jp/news/1021/20190906-13.html)。

JPX described weakened controls: material deal terms were not properly shared, the accounting function was overridden, and senior leadership failed to respond adequately. Tokyo Shoko Research also noted a later disclosure of **possible** accounting issues in a Korean subsidiary. 注意したいのは、認定された中国関連の問題と、韓国関連の疑義開示を同じ確度で断定しないこと。[JPX](https://www.jpx.co.jp/news/1021/20190906-13.html)／[TSR](https://www.tsr-net.co.jp/data/detail/1189699_1527.html)。

This is not evidence of a new violation in 2026. It illustrates a distinction: **brand trust is not a substitute for internal control**. 企業研究には商品の魅力だけでなく、売上認識、承認、海外拠点管理、監査対応まで必要になる。

## 離｜Content can become customer service rather than a subscription product

The current free app retains device controls, activity history, registration, and warranty extension; selected videos remain on YouTube. One reading is service contraction. Another is a shift in **the job content performs**. コンテンツそのものへの課金から、製品利用を支える接客や継続利用の支援へ。[SIXPAD official](https://club.sixpad.jp/)／[2026 FAQ](https://faq.club.sixpad.jp/hc/ja/articles/16652650634895-7%E6%9C%881%E6%97%A5%E4%BB%A5%E9%99%8D-%E3%82%A2%E3%83%97%E3%83%AA%E3%81%AE%E7%94%BB%E9%9D%A2%E3%81%8C%E5%A4%89%E3%82%8F%E3%82%8A%E3%81%BE%E3%81%97%E3%81%9F)。

Consider a free technique video from a sports club. Its value may not be video revenue; it can help owners use equipment, avoid abandonment, or discover another product. その場合content is a service layer rather than a profit center. ただしSIXPADで実際に再購入率が上がったという公開実証は、今回確認できていない。

For a video-technology supplier, the question becomes **what behavior does video change?** not only **how much do viewers pay?** 映像サブスクの売上と、それが補助する機器販売を同じ市場金額として二重に足してはならない。

## 離｜Market sizing needs separate buyers and units of demand

This case suggests at least four analytical markets: **home exercise equipment**, **EMS devices**, **coaching video/content**, and **software for device control and customer records**. Each has different buyers, budgets, units of payment, and replacement cycles. 「デジタルフィットネス」でまとめると、何を誰が買うのかが見えなくなる。

A rough sizing template: hardware ≈ **eligible households × purchase rate × unit price ÷ replacement cycle**; subscriptions ≈ **eligible users × paid attach rate × monthly price × 12**; B2B software ≈ **eligible firms × adoption × annual contract value**. These are modeling structures, not estimates of MTG's actual TAM. 重複顧客と解約、買い替え周期を追加で検証する。

Useful terms: **D2C** means direct-to-consumer selling; **ARPU** is average revenue per user; **churn** measures cancellations; **attach rate** is the proportion of hardware buyers who subscribe; **VOD** is video on demand. これらは似たサービスを見た目ではなく、business mechanicsで比較するための言葉になる。

To test the business seriously, ask for device units sold, app registrations, paid members, 12-month retention, monthly content costs, acquisition costs, and device-support incidents. Most were not established in the public documents reviewed. **Missing data is a finding about the limits of the investment thesis**, not a license to invent a narrative.

## 結び｜The larger question is where continued use creates economic value

SIXPAD moved from abdominal EMS devices to a connected suit, premium bikes, and an online studio. The paid class model was repriced and later retired, while free owner functions remained. We started with muscles contracting under electrical impulses and somehow arrived at customer relationship management. 妙な寄り道に見えるが、これこそブランド経営の論点かもしれない。

Peloton-like models monetize continued participation through subscriptions. MTG's broader model may monetize repeat product choice through brands, experiences, and channels. The key difference is **who pays, for what, and how often**. その問いはスポーツ映像やリハビリ、教育の市場分析にも応用できる。

The 2019 accounting case belongs beside the brand-growth story. Making desirable products and governing an organization are separate capabilities. In the end, the interesting measurement is not only distance pedaled, but **what the company counts as revenue, retention, and value**. 会社を見る目を変えるのは、その定義の違いである。
