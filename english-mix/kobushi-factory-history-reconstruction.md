# こぶしファクトリー史――8人から5人へ、崩れたあとに何を作り直したのか
## 結成、レコ大、2017年の相次ぐ離脱、アカペラ、辛夷第二幕、無観客ラストライブから読む「危機後の再設計」

### 要旨

Kobushi Factory is not simply a story of early success followed by decline.

2015年に8人で結成され、同年9月にメジャーデビュー。年末には日本レコード大賞最優秀新人賞を受賞した。

Then, in 2017, three members left within a single year.

8人から5人へ。

If we stop there, the story looks like organizational collapse.

But the most interesting part begins after the rupture.

残った5人は、8人時代を完全再現しようとしなかった。

They did not simply refill old line allocations or search for three replacements. Instead, the group gradually shifted its center toward chorus work, harmony, and a cappella.

In other words, they changed the metric.

> **Recovery does not always mean restoring the previous organization.**
>
> **Sometimes recovery means redesigning the organization around the people who remain.**

こぶしファクトリーの後半は、その post-crisis redesign の記録として読むと非常に面白い。

---

## 1. 2015――an inherited name, an unfinished identity

2015年1月2日、ハロプロ研修生から8人の新ユニットが作られることが発表された。

藤井梨央、広瀬彩海、野村みな美、小川麗奈、浜浦彩乃、田口夏実、和田桜子、井上玲音。

2月25日、グループ名が**こぶしファクトリー**に決まる。

“こぶし” referred to the Japanese magnolia and also evoked a clenched fist: elegance plus strength.

The “Factory” part explicitly carried the wish to inherit the spirit of Berryz Kobo, which was about to suspend activities.

つまりこのグループは、完全な白紙から始まったわけではない。

It was already positioned inside a lineage: Hello! Project Kids, Berryz Kobo, ℃-ute, and then a new trainee generation.

But inheritance of a name does not automatically create identity.

**The operating concept still had to be discovered through work.**

---

## 2. 「念には念」と「ドスコイ！」――a brand that was easy to understand

結成直後の代表曲が「念には念」。

メジャーデビューは2015年9月2日。

「ドスコイ！ケンキョにダイタン」「ラーメン大好き小泉さんの唄」「念には念（念入りVer.）」のトリプルA面だった。

Kobushi Factory's early grammar was highly physical and instantly legible.

拳、押忍、ドスコイ、猪突猛進。

They were energetic, loud, forward-moving, and bodily.

年末には第57回日本レコード大賞最優秀新人賞を受賞する。

That was a remarkable launch.

But early success can also lock an organization into its first successful image.

> **The faster a brand becomes understandable, the faster expectations harden around it.**

「元気」「力強い」「拳」「がなる」。

These were assets, but later they would also become constraints to move beyond.

---

## 3. 2017――three departures, three different contexts

こぶしファクトリー史の最大の断層は2017年にある。

However, it is inaccurate to compress that year into a single story of “three members causing trouble and leaving.”

The contexts were different.

### 藤井梨央

2017年5月、学業に専念し、教師・保育士を目指すため夏のハロコンをもって卒業すると発表された。

In July, before that planned graduation, management announced the termination of her contract, citing violations of Hello! Project rules and failure to fulfill an agreement made during subsequent discussions.

### 小川麗奈

2017年7月、「不安神経症」と診断され活動を休止。

After continued treatment and discussion with her family and doctor, she graduated in September because a stable return date could not be identified.

This was a health-related departure and should not be folded into the causal story of the other cases.

### 田口夏実

2017年12月、事務所は本人や家族と話し合ったうえで、グループメンバーとしての自覚と責任を欠くと判断し、専属マネージメント契約を終了したと発表した。

The same official announcement also stated something crucial: management had spoken with the remaining five, and all five confirmed their intention to continue moving forward.

> **The five-member lineup was not a planned selection.**

It was the result of disruption.

だから後から「運命の5人だった」と神話化する必要はない。

What matters is what happened next: **five people who remained by circumstance eventually created a form that looked intentional.**

---

## 4. No replacement――turning shortage into redesign

3人が抜けたあと、新メンバーは補充されなかった。

広瀬彩海、野村みな美、浜浦彩乃、和田桜子、井上玲音。

This made Kobushi Factory structurally different from ANGERME or Juice=Juice, both of which continued to absorb new members.

Instead of replacing three missing slots with three new people, Kobushi had to redistribute existing responsibility.

歌割り、フォーメーション、MC、ライブ上の負荷。

Everything had to be reconsidered.

If you define a smaller team as “the old team minus three,” the task becomes hole-filling.

But if you define it as a new five-person system, new advantages become visible.

> **A smaller organization can expose individual roles more clearly.**

That condition became the foundation of Kobushi Factory's second act.

---

## 5. 「これからだ！／明日テンキになあれ」――future tense after rupture

5人体制初のシングルは2018年3月28日の

**「これからだ！／明日テンキになあれ」**。

The titles almost inevitably sounded autobiographical after 2017.

もちろん、曲名だけから制作側の意図を断定することはできない。

But reception matters too.

The five did not sing, “We will return to eight.”

The language pointed forward.

> **Reconstruction is not necessarily a return to the previous headcount.**
>
> **It can mean building a future form with the present team.**

That directional change leads directly into the group's most distinctive late-period skill: a cappella.

---

## 6. A cappella――“do something different from other groups”

5人体制のこぶしファクトリーを決定的に変えたのが、アカペラだった。

They began challenging a cappella in the autumn 2017 tour and kept developing it afterward.

Later, members explained that one motivation was simple: **they wanted to do something different from other groups.**

This is an important organizational statement.

After crisis, teams often try to restore their previous competitive advantage.

Kobushi chose a different path:

**learn a new capability in order to create differentiation.**

A cappella requires more than a strong lead vocal.

Bass, rhythm, harmony, vocal percussion, balance.

一人だけが目立てば成立する芸ではない。

You must listen to others, understand your own range and function, and adjust your output so the whole system works.

井上玲音も、ボイスパーカッションを始めた当初はできないことばかりで悔しかったと振り返っている。

So a cappella was not an ornament added after the team became good.

> **It was training in how five people make one sound.**

That is why it mattered beyond music.

---

## 7. Listening became a team capability

「歌が上手い」という表現だけでは、後期こぶしファクトリーを説明しきれない。

Solo singing primarily rewards control of your own voice.

A cappella adds another requirement:

**your own performance depends on accurately hearing everyone else.**

This had striking overlap with the needs of a five-person team after organizational shock.

- 自分の役割を知る
- 相手の音を聴く
- 全体が崩れそうなら調整する
- 主旋律でなくても機能を果たす

The musical skill and the organizational skill were almost the same.

人数減少によって一人ひとりの責任は重くなった。

A cappella turned that pressure into practice.

Through repetition, what may have begun as a differentiation experiment became a recognizable group capability.

---

## 8. 『辛夷第二幕』――naming the new organization

2019年10月2日、2ndアルバム**『辛夷第二幕』**を発売。

The wording matters.

Not merely “Part 2.”

**Second Act.**

「これからだ！」「明日テンキになあれ」「ナセバナル」「Oh No 懊悩」「開き直っちゃえ！」など、5人体制を象徴する曲が並ぶ。

And the limited edition B included a full bonus disc titled **「辛夷無伴奏合唱（アカペラ）集」**.

「念には念」「チョット愚直に！猪突猛進」「GO TO THE TOP!!」「桜ナイトフィーバー」「LOVEマシーン」などをa cappellaで収録した。

At this point, a cappella was no longer a concert trick.

**It became product architecture.**

危機後に身につけた能力が、公式ディスコグラフィーの中心に入った。

> **Recovery can be considered complete when a post-crisis capability becomes part of the official identity—not only when old numbers recover.**

---

## 9. Successful reconstruction did not guarantee permanence

それでも、グループは永続しなかった。

2020年1月8日、3月30日をもって解散すると発表。

According to the official explanation, leader Ayaka Hirose had first expressed in early 2019 that she wanted to graduate from Hello! Project and pursue a new path. Later discussions showed that Minami Nomura, Ayano Hamaura, and Sakurako Wada also had their own future goals.

This should not be flattened into “the 2017 crisis finally killed the group.”

The five-person team had already spent more than two years rebuilding itself.

『辛夷第二幕』まで完成させた後に、それぞれが次を考え始めた。

和田桜子は解散発表時、5人で活動すること、この5人であることに強い意味を感じていたと述べている。

That helps explain why the answer was not another member addition.

> **This second act was designed as a five-person team, not as an indefinitely inheritable institution.**

---

## 10. The audience disappeared from the final room

最後のライブは2020年3月30日。

**「こぶしファクトリー ライブ2020 ～The Final Ring！～」** at TOKYO DOME CITY HALL.

Because of the spread of COVID-19, the concert was changed to a no-audience performance.

The show itself happened and was broadcast live, with live-viewing arrangements also made, but there were no fans in the room where the five performed their final concert.

It was a cruelly ironic ending for a group whose second act had become so dependent on live vocal interaction and ensemble power.

Yet not everything vanished.

映像は残った。

The a cappella recordings remained.

The repertoire remained.

And Reona Inoue moved into Juice=Juice after Kobushi Factory ended.

**The organization closed, but capability migrated.**

---

## 11. The survival model――`reconstructed_identity_survives`

こぶしファクトリーは長寿グループではない。

約5年間の活動だった。

So its continuity cannot be explained by an enduring institution or brand reboot.

What survived most strongly was the **identity created after the rupture**.

Early Kobushi Factory was associated with:

- 8 members
- trainee selection
- physical power
- fists
- momentum
- a rookie award

Late Kobushi Factory became associated with:

- 5 members
- harmony
- chorus work
- a cappella
- distinct individual voices
- listening to one another

The second identity was not simply inherited from management at formation.

**It was learned.**

そこで本シリーズでは、こぶしファクトリーのモデルを

> **`reconstructed_identity_survives`**
>
> **When a team suffers a major rupture, it may survive not by restoring the old structure but by creating a new identity around the capabilities of those who remain. That second identity can become the version remembered most strongly.**

と定義する。

---

## 12. Juice=Juiceとの違い――pipeline vs. completed team

前回のJuice=Juiceと比較すると構造差が分かりやすい。

### Juice=Juice

Bring in new members.

Create overlap between seniors and juniors.

Give difficult work to newcomers.

Reproduce capability across generations.

→ **capability pipeline**

### こぶしファクトリー

新しい人を入れない。

Reassign the five who remain.

Learn a new group capability together.

Complete the team as those five.

→ **reconstructed identity**

Neither model is universally superior.

One optimizes for organizational continuation.

The other can optimize for the completion of a specific team.

---

## 13. After crisis, “back to normal” may be the wrong target

人が抜けた。

A project failed.

A central contributor left.

The market changed.

When that happens, organizations often ask how to return to the old state.

But sometimes the old state is unrecoverable.

Then the more useful questions are different:

- Who is here now?
- What becomes visible because the team is smaller?
- Which old strength no longer needs to be protected?
- What skill can this exact group learn that the previous group never needed?

こぶしファクトリーは8人へ戻らなかった。

They learned a cappella as five.

そして、その5人を「第二幕」と呼べるところまで作った。

That is the most important part of their history.

---

## 結論――not the five who remained, but what the five built

2017年だけを見れば、こぶしファクトリーは

**8人から5人になったグループ**

に見える。

But if we continue through 2018 and 2019, the meaning changes.

They became

**a group that created something possible only after becoming five.**

危機後の組織を見るとき、「何人残ったか」だけでは足りない。

The more revealing question is:

> **What did the remaining people learn after the crisis, and what new standard did they make normal?**

こぶしファクトリーにとって、その答えがアカペラ、ハーモニー、そして『辛夷第二幕』だった。

> **Reconstruction is not a return.**
>
> **It is the creation of a form that did not exist before the break.**
