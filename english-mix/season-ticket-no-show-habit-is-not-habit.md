# Season ticketを買って、72 matches全部来た人は約1%だった
## FC Basel 8,734人のbehavioral dataから読む、“sold seat, empty seat”問題

I used to assume that a season ticket holder is someone who comes to almost every match.

少なくとも「最も来場頻度が高い人たち」というイメージはある。They pay upfront, secure a seat or admission right, and commit to the club for a whole season. かなりstrong commitmentに見える。

Then a Swiss football dataset makes that image wobble.

2013年から2016年までの72 league matchesを通じて継続的に観察できたseason ticket holdersは8,734人。公開working paper版では、彼らは平均で4年間に約21試合、つまり年5〜6試合を欠席していた。そして**all 72 matchesに来場した人は約1%**だった。[CREMA Working Paper](https://www.crema-research.ch/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/football-spectator-no-show-behavior-in-switzerlandempirical-evidence-from-season-ticket-holder-behavior.pdf)

About one percent.

This is not merely “some season ticket holders skip a game.” むしろ、**never missing a match was unusually rare**という話である。

At first, this looked like a contradiction: “loyal, but absent.”

But after reading the paper and later studies, the contradiction moved. ファンの行動より、こちらの「what a season ticket is」という前提の方が怪しくなった。

## 1. DATA｜この研究は「何人来たか」ではなく、「誰が来なかったか」を追った

The central paper is Dominik Schreyer and Benno Torgler’s “Predicting season ticket holder no-show behaviour: more nuanced evidence from Switzerland,” published in *Applied Economics* in 2021. [Taylor & Francis](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00036846.2021.1925081)

出版社版が示すのは、an established Swiss Super League clubで、8,734 season ticket holdersが72試合で下したattendance / no-show decisionsである。公開working paperではクラブ名がFC Basel 1893と明記され、broader raw dataとして約2.09 million ticket-holder attendance decisionsがstadium access systemから取得されたと説明されている。[Taylor & Francis](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00036846.2021.1925081) [CREMA Working Paper](https://www.crema-research.ch/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/football-spectator-no-show-behavior-in-switzerlandempirical-evidence-from-season-ticket-holder-behavior.pdf)

That matters because this is not a survey asking, “Did you intend to go?”

It checks whether the person actually passed through the gate.

観客研究でaverage attendanceやtickets distributedのようなaggregate numbersだけを見ると、「同じ3万人」でも中身が隠れる。毎回来る3万人なのか、4万人が入れ替わりながらaverage 3万人なのかでは、クラブが解くべき問題が違う。

This study peels off one layer of aggregation and follows individual **attendance decisions**.

## 2. GAP｜チケットが売れた時点で売上は立つ。それでも空席は「終わった問題」ではない

公開working paperによれば、観察期間のFC Baselでは、distributed ticketsのうち実際には使われなかった割合、つまりno-show rateは平均約26.77%だった。Year by yearでは2013年25.81%、2014年23.41%、2015年26.61%、2016年31.27%とされる。[CREMA Working Paper](https://www.crema-research.ch/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/football-spectator-no-show-behavior-in-switzerlandempirical-evidence-from-season-ticket-holder-behavior.pdf)

At first glance, this is odd.

The ticket is already sold.

Then from the club’s point of view, revenue is already secured, and attendance can look like the buyer’s private choice.

それでも論文がno-showを問題にするのは、a seat carries value beyond its primary ticket priceからだ。Food, beverage, merchandise, parking, crowd atmosphere, sponsor/broadcast presentation, operations planning, and—when demand exceeds supply—the opportunity cost of an empty seat that another fan could have used all sit behind the same seat.[CREMA Working Paper](https://www.crema-research.ch/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/football-spectator-no-show-behavior-in-switzerlandempirical-evidence-from-season-ticket-holder-behavior.pdf)

So **sold and used are different KPIs**.

Ticket business does not fully end at checkout.

## 3. HABIT｜最も目を引くのは、「休んだ回数」より「休みが続いていること」だった

The loudest number in the paper is not age or price.

It is the absence streak.

working paper版では、直前に1試合欠席した人がnext home matchも欠席する割合は約47%。Two consecutive missesで約60%、threeで68%、fiveで79%、tenで89%、twentyで96%まで上がる。[CREMA Working Paper](https://www.crema-research.ch/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/football-spectator-no-show-behavior-in-switzerlandempirical-evidence-from-season-ticket-holder-behavior.pdf)

On paper, it looks like a slope.

Miss once. Miss again. Then again.

This makes a tempting managerial story: “No-show became a habit. Intervene early and break it.”

実際、著者たちも“emerging no-show habits”をimportant predictorとして扱っている。出版社版abstractでも、geographical distanceと並んで、no-show habitのrobust roleが確認されたとされる。[Taylor & Francis](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00036846.2021.1925081)

If the story ended here, management would be easy.

But the paper breaks its own easy story.

## 4. TWIST｜「来ない癖」が強く見えても、本当に“癖”とは限らない

Consecutive absences strongly predict another absence.

That is highly useful for **prediction**.

But it does not prove **causation**: that missing the previous match itself caused the next miss.

working paper版で著者たちは、長いabsence streakを単純な“growing disinterest”と読むことに慎重である。People may buy a season ticket for different reasons, including motives beyond securing a seat, such as supporting the club financially or feeling more involved.

Then they write a striking line.

> “there might be no habit to break.”

つまり、**壊すべきhabitそのものが存在しないかもしれない**。[CREMA Working Paper](https://www.crema-research.ch/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/football-spectator-no-show-behavior-in-switzerlandempirical-evidence-from-season-ticket-holder-behavior.pdf)

That is a major shift for no-show management.

A holder who missed ten straight matches is indeed likely to miss the next one. しかし、その人が「悪い習慣」に陥っているのか、そもそもevery match attendanceのために買っていないのか、生活条件が変わったのかは、behavior history aloneでは分からない。

**A strong predictor is not automatically a causal lever.**

If we forget this, more precise analytics can produce rougher interventions.

## 5. AGE｜年齢は一方向に効くのではなく、距離や気温と組み合わさって効き方が変わった

The phrase “more nuanced evidence” in the published title is probably earned here.

Age does not move in one simple direction.

最終掲載版abstractでは、3D contour plotsを使った分析から、おおむね35〜65歳のSTHsは、retirement-age holdersと比べて、distance from home to stadiumやtemperatureといったbarriersの影響を受けやすかったと報告される。一方、retirement-age groupではhabit patternがかなりrobustだった。[Taylor & Francis](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00036846.2021.1925081)

working paper版でも、ageとno-showにはinverted U-shaped nonlinear relationshipが観察され、単純な「young people skip more」「older people skip more」では捉えにくい。[CREMA Working Paper](https://www.crema-research.ch/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/football-spectator-no-show-behavior-in-switzerlandempirical-evidence-from-season-ticket-holder-behavior.pdf)

The lesson is not “target people in their 40s.”

同じ年齢でも、distance, weather, weekday scheduling, and life constraintsの組み合わせでattendance costは変わる。

Age is better read not as a person-label, but as a condition that can change **how strongly another barrier matters**.

## 6. PRICE｜高くすれば来る、安ければ休む、ではなかった

Price carries the same trap.

“Cheap season tickets make skipping feel less costly.” That intuition is easy to understand.

ところがworking paper版では、season ticket priceとno-showの関係はmonotonicではなく、1試合あたり約23 Swiss francs付近を谷とするU-shapeだった。Very low/free ticketsだけでなく、relatively expensive ticketsでもno-show rateが高かった。[CREMA Working Paper](https://www.crema-research.ch/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/football-spectator-no-show-behavior-in-switzerlandempirical-evidence-from-season-ticket-holder-behavior.pdf)

Of course, this does not mean “23 CHF is the universal optimal price.”

これはspecific club, specific years, specific product designで観察された関係である。

The useful point is that **price cannot be treated as one linear lever**.

Does price itself change behavior, or do different price bands contain people with different motives and constraints? 観測データだけでは、その二つを慎重に分ける必要がある。

## 7. CONTEXT｜スイスで当たった変数が、別のリーグでも同じ強さで当たるとは限らない

In a sense, this paper is itself a replication study.

それ以前のGerman market研究では、25 Bundesliga / 2. Bundesliga clubsを使った分析から、superstars, traditional away teams, and derbiesなどgame quality factorsがno-showと関連し、そのeffectsもfirst and second divisionsで異なると報告されていた。[Journal of Sports Economics](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1527002518784120)

Another 2021 study analyzed more than 5,900 season ticket holders of one professional team and found age, tenure, expenditure, prior attendance, viewing conditions, and game quality to predict individual attendance decisions.[Journal of Sport Management / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1123/jsm.2020-0284)

さらに2022年公表のsystematic reviewは、healthcare, education, sportsなどを横断した98 studiesを整理し、antecedents are context-dependentである一方、product characteristicsとthe opportunity costs of physical attendanceには共通性が見られるとまとめた。[Journal of Economic Surveys](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/joes.12534)

So rather than hunting for one universal “type of person who does not show,” separate **portable structure from club-specific context**.

Distance appears repeatedly.

But age, price, match conditions, and seating effects should not be copied straight from one club into another.

## 8. INTERVENTION｜2021年の論文が「試してみるべき」と書いたreminderは、2025年に実験された

Re-research moves the story forward.

2021年working paperはfuture researchとして、reminders, pricing/payment mechanisms, rewards or punishments, and easy ticket-sharing appsを挙げていた。[CREMA Working Paper](https://www.crema-research.ch/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/football-spectator-no-show-behavior-in-switzerlandempirical-evidence-from-season-ticket-holder-behavior.pdf)

Then in 2025, *Journal of Business Research* published a large field experiment with 13,911 season ticket holders of a German football team, testing reminders with reward opportunities.[Journal of Business Research](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0148296325001419)

The result was not a magic fix.

reminders had only a short-term effect; the condition with a monetary gift reward worked best, but the study found no persistent behavioral change in later matches.[Journal of Business Research](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0148296325001419)

Now the 2021 observation study and the 2025 intervention experiment connect.

**Predicting who is likely to miss and knowing how to increase attendance are different research questions.**

A highly accurate risk model does not automatically tell you which treatment will work.

## 9. THOUGHT EXPERIMENT｜同じ「空席1席」でも、実は三つのまったく違う問題かもしれない

Try an over-serious thought experiment.

All three people miss the next home game.

- A：45歳、lives far from the stadium. It is a weekday match and the weather is harsh.
- B：10試合連続で来ていない。ただしseason ticketを“seat for every match”より、club-supporting membershipに近い感覚で持っている。
- C：normally attends almost every time, but work blocks this one match. If releasing the seat were one tap, C would happily do it.

In the dataset, all three become the same value: **no-show = 1**.

But A has an attendance-barrier problem, B has a product-value-definition problem, and C has a seat-recovery UX problem.

If you send the same “please attend” reminder to all three, one intervention is being asked to solve three different problems.

**The empty seat is the same outcome, but not necessarily the same problem.**

A single no-show KPI erases that distinction.

## 10. PRACTICE｜実務では「予測・原因・回収・関係」を別々に持った方がいい

From here, this is my proposal, not the paper’s conclusion.

If a club manages season-ticket no-show, do not compress everything into one “absence countermeasure.” Separate at least four layers.

- **Predict｜予測**：Who is likely to no-show next? Use past attendance, distance, match conditions, and similar signals.
- **Explain｜原因仮説**：Why might they miss? Do not infer motive from logs alone; add surveys or interviews when needed.
- **Recover｜席の回収**：Even if absence cannot be eliminated, can release, transfer, or resale return the seat to another attendee?
- **Relationship｜関係価値**：Could a low-attendance holder still have high renewal, support, or broader customer value? Do not label low attendance as “bad customer” automatically.

There is a reason to keep these separate.

The easiest person to predict is not always the easiest person to change.

An absence may be hard to change but easy to recover as a seat.

And even a successfully recovered seat can create a different long-term cost if punishment damages the value of holding a season ticket.

Do not turn no-show management into only “reduce absence.”

**The larger goal is to increase the value actually used across seat, experience, and relationship—not merely the number of seats sold.**

## 11. METRICS｜「売れたか」だけでなく、「使われたか」「戻せたか」まで見る

As another proposal, shift the metric chain one step beyond season-ticket sales.

After sales, track actual entry rate.

After entry rate, track advance release rate among expected absentees.

After release, track how often returned seats are redistributed.

Then ask whether redistributed seats create new attendance, food/merchandise spend, membership, or future purchase.

すると、同じ“season ticket holder did not attend”でも、seat stayed emptyとsomeone else used itを分けられる。

These KPIs were not directly tested by the paper.

But they fit the working paper’s distinction between acting on the immediate cause of no-show and compensating for the consequences of unused capacity.[CREMA Working Paper](https://www.crema-research.ch/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/football-spectator-no-show-behavior-in-switzerlandempirical-evidence-from-season-ticket-holder-behavior.pdf)

There are ways to solve the problem other than forcing the original holder to attend.

## 12. LIMITS｜これはFCバーゼルの2013〜2016年であり、「サッカーファンの法則」ではない

The numbers are vivid, but they should not become universal laws.

対象はone major Swiss clubで、期間は2013〜2016年。当時のFC Baselはdomestically dominantで、stadium supply-demand balance, league format, pricing, and access conditionsも他市場とは違う。[CREMA Working Paper](https://www.crema-research.ch/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/football-spectator-no-show-behavior-in-switzerlandempirical-evidence-from-season-ticket-holder-behavior.pdf)

Also, the public working paper and final published article differ in title and level of detail. 本稿では、8,734人・72試合、distance、habit、age interactionsなどpublished versionで確認できるcore findingsを軸にし、26.77%、average ~21 missed matches、~1% perfect attendance、streak-specific rates、price turning pointなどのdetailはpublic working paperから引用した。

Most important: do not convert observational predictors into causal claims.

「連続欠席者へ介入すれば必ず戻る」「high priceなら値下げで来る」「40代だから来ない」とは、この研究からは言えない。

The paper teaches something quieter and more useful.

**No-show is not a single-cause phenomenon.**

## 13. DISCOVERY｜シーズンチケットは「18枚の試合券を束ねたもの」ではないのかもしれない

At the start, “only about 1% attended all 72” looked like a story about people not showing up enough.

After the research, it looks different.

If you define a season ticket as “a product bought by people who attend every match,” every absence becomes product failure.

しかしseason ticketにはseat access, price advantage, priority rights, belonging, support, and future attendance optionsなど、multiple valuesがbundledされている可能性がある。少なくとも著者たちは、every-match seat use以外のpurchase motivesがあり得ることを、habit interpretationへのcounterpointとして明示している。[CREMA Working Paper](https://www.crema-research.ch/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/football-spectator-no-show-behavior-in-switzerlandempirical-evidence-from-season-ticket-holder-behavior.pdf)

Then an empty seat is not simply “a hole left by a fading fan.”

**It is a sold right that was not exercised for that match.**

That changes the club’s design problem.

From blaming “why didn’t you come?” toward designing which rights are used, how unused rights return to circulation, and what other value they carry.

You do not need to define the 1% who attended all 72 as the only ideal season-ticket holders.

The harder job is not to flatten the other 99% into one type.

After reading this paper, an empty seat looks less empty. It carries more information.

## 14. PROMPT｜自クラブのno-showを「誰が来ないか」で終わらせずに分析する

Below is a reusable prompt for turning the paper into club-side analysis.

~~~text
あなたはスポーツクラブのticketing / fan behavior analystです。

目的：
season ticket holderのno-showを、
「loyaltyが低い」「来ないhabitがついた」と早合点せず、
prediction・cause hypothesis・seat recovery・customer relationshipの4層に分けて分析してください。

必須ルール：
1. predictorとcausal factorを分ける
2. past absenceがstrong predictorでも、それ自体をcauseと断定しない
3. age・price・distanceのeffectをlinearだと仮定しない
4. segment間のinteractionを確認する
5. 「absenceを減らす」以外にrelease・transfer・resaleでseatをrecoverする選択肢を検討する
6. attendance frequencyとrenewal・support motive・customer valueを同一視しない
7. intervention ideaはobservational dataの結論ではなく「testable hypothesis」と明記する
8. 可能ならA/B testやstaged rolloutでeffectを測る
9. 結果はFACT・INTERPRETATION・PROPOSALに分ける

分析順：
A. no-show definition and denominator
B. individual attendance history
C. absence streak vs next-game absence
D. distance / age / price / seating / match conditions
E. interactions and nonlinearities
F. survey / interview design to fill missing causes
G. release / resale UX
H. intervention experiment
I. long-term renewal and customer value

入力：
【対象クラブ／大会】{{club}}
【分析期間】{{period}}
【保有データ】{{data}}
【現在のseason ticket制度】{{system}}
【困っているno-show現象】{{problem}}
~~~

The purpose is not to rank “people who do not show.”

**It is to separate the structure that creates an empty seat from the mechanism that can recover that seat.**
