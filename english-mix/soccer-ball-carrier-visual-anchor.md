# Watch the Ball Carrier
## でも、そこだけを見ない

When I watch football, my eyes go to the player in possession.

誰が持っているか。

Where are they facing?

運ぶのか、蹴るのか。

It feels obvious: the next action starts there.

But visual-search research gives this instinct a strange answer.

**It is right, but it can also become a novice habit if the gaze gets stuck there.**

A 2020 in-situ eye-tracking study of elite 11-v-11 football found that players often fixated the player in possession and areas containing combinations of ball, opponents and teammates.

保持者は重要な中心である。

But the center is not the whole map.

[What Do Football Players Look at?, 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7596273/)

---

## 1. Novices look at the ball more / 初心者ほどボールとパサーに固着する

In a classic 1994 study, experienced and inexperienced players watched 11-v-11 sequences and predicted pass destinations.

経験の浅い選手はballとpasserをより多く注視した。

Experienced players used more peripheral parts of the display, including the positions and movements of other players.

A pass does not exist inside the ball carrier alone.

受け手が動く。

An opponent closes a lane.

別の味方が相手を引っ張る。

Space opens.

The option often exists before the pass does.

[Williams et al., 1994](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8047704/)

---

## 2. But better decision-makers still watch the ball carrier / それでも保持者は見る

This is where the story gets better.

A study of skilled youth players found that successful decision-makers spent **more time fixating the player in possession**.

ただし、それだけではない。

They also switched gaze more often between the ball carrier and other areas.

So expertise does not mean “stop watching the ball.”

It means:

**center → periphery → center.**

視線は点ではなく、往復運動になる。

[Vaeyens et al., 2007](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.3200/JMBR.39.5.395-408)

---

## 3. Fixation is not the same as information / 視線の場所＝情報の全部ではない

Where the eye points is not everything the player sees.

周辺視野がある。

In 1-v-1 studies, experienced defenders often used cues from an opponent's hips and knees rather than simply staring at the ball.

3対3の研究でも、experienced players depended more on information outside the ball or passer area.

The ball tells you what has happened.

身体や周囲の配置は、what may happen nextを教えることがある。

[Williams & Davids, 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9635326/)

[Nagano et al., 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15648495/)

---

## 4. The ball carrier is a coordinate origin / 保持者は座標原点

Maybe the purpose of looking at the player in possession is not to watch the player.

**It is to read what is possible from that point.**

センターバックが持っているなら、

- nearest presser / 最寄りの守備者
- closest outlet / 近い出口
- player between lines / 間に立つ選手
- far-side space / 逆側の空白

を見る。

Then the question changes from:

“What will he do?”

to:

**“What can he do, given this structure?”**

保持者は主役ではなく、その瞬間のtactical mapの座標原点になる。

---

## 5. Scanning means leaving the ball to understand the ball

Recent soccer research describes scanning as active head movement away from the ball to collect information before engaging with it.

ボールから目を離す。

But only so that the next look at the ball contains more meaning.

If you see only the passer, you discover the receiver after the pass.

周辺を見れば、receiverが動いた瞬間にpass possibilityが生まれる。

That is one beat earlier.

[Perceptual-cognitive skills in soccer: scoping review, 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12237070/)

---

## 6. A simple viewing drill / 観戦で「保持者＋三つ」

Watch the ball carrier, then check only three things:

1. **Nearest opponent / 一番近い相手**
2. **Nearest useful teammate / 一番近い出口**
3. **Far-side empty space / 逆側の空白**

Before the pass, predict one likely receiver.

外れたら、whyを確認する。

Did the player choose something unusual?

守備の誘導を見落とした？

Did an off-ball run create a new option?

The match becomes a sequence of hypotheses rather than a sequence of completed actions.

---

## 7. So yes: watch the player with the ball

My original idea survives.

サッカーは、ボールを持っている選手を中心に見る。

But “center” now means something different.

Look at the carrier.

相手を見る。

Look at teammates.

スペースを見る。

Return to the carrier.

What we really want to see is not the ball or the person.

**We want to see the shape of the available options.**

The player in possession is not the answer.

その瞬間の「現在地」だった。
