# From game film to fundraising――Hudlは何を売る会社なのか

*調査基準日：2026年10月26日。一次資料優先。本文は確認事実、解釈、提案を分けて記述する。*

## 守｜映像分析会社が、なぜ学校の募金まで扱うのか

In July 2026, Hudl acquired TeamUp, a fundraising platform for school sports. 映像分析会社が、なぜ募金まで扱うのか。The surprise is real: Hudl now spans film review, livestreaming, ticketing, team operations and fundraising. [Hudlの発表](https://www.hudl.com/blog/hudl-acquires-teamup-gamified-fundraising)。

The question is not simply whether its video software is good. **Why can a video company expand into so many sports workflows?** 映像は商品であると同時に、指導、分析、スカウティング、配信への入口にもなる。ただし、製品がすべて完全統合されたという意味ではない。

## 守｜Hudlは2006年に創業した、映像を中心とするスポーツ技術企業である

Hudl was founded in Lincoln, Nebraska, in 2006. Its first project helped the University of Nebraska football team work with game film. 最初は巨大なAI企業ではなく、コーチの映像確認を助けるソフトウェアだった。[公式情報](https://www.hudl.com/newsroom)／[Sports Business Journal](https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2025/11/24/sports-tech-market-undergoing-rapid-consolidation-whats-driving-it/)。

In November 2025, Sports Business Journal reported 325,000 teams across 40 sports and 3,200 employees in 17 countries. **Teams are not the same as paid contracts or unique users.** 非上場企業のため、最新の連結売上や製品別の利益を公開情報だけで断定できない。[出典](https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2025/11/24/sports-tech-market-undergoing-rapid-consolidation-whats-driving-it/)。

## 守｜試合映像は「撮る→切り出す→意味を付ける→判断する」で分析資料になる

Video analysis does not always mean AI making tactical judgments. First comes a simple workflow: record, tag events, collect clips, then discuss decisions. **Coding** means assigning labels and timestamps; a **playlist** groups relevant clips. 90分すべてを見るのではなく、問いに対応する場面を取り出す仕組みである。

Sportscode is a professional tool for coding, multi-angle synchronization and clip sharing. たとえば相手のセットプレーだけを抜き出し、スタッフで見直せる。Its 2026 release notes also describe an AI scripting assistant, but that is not proof of fully automatic tactical judgment. [製品](https://www.hudl.com/en_gb/products/sportscode)／[更新履歴](https://www.wyscout-apps.hudl.com/releases/sportscode)。

![Hudl Sportscode interface showing coded events and a timeline](https://static.hudl.com/craft/_1200xAUTO_crop_center-center_none/03_HSC_Capture-Mode.png?mtime=20220812125945)

Image: Official Hudl Sportscode support documentation, showing capture and coding tools. [Source](https://support.hudl.com/s/article/capture-mode-sportscode)。

**Event data** records actions such as passes and shots. **Tracking data** records positions over time. **xG** estimates scoring probability; it does not equal actual goals. StatsBomb also offers **OBV**, a model of the value added by on-ball actions.[製品情報](https://www.hudl.com/products/statsbomb)。

## 守｜Hudlは一製品ではなく、顧客の仕事に応じた製品群である

One brand, different jobs. 学校のコーチは撮影と振り返り、プロ分析官は高度なタグ付け、スカウトは他チームの選手探索、運営責任者は配信やチケットを必要とする。The buyer, budget and workflow can be different even under one Hudl brand.

A functional map helps: Hudl/Assist for review, Focus for capture, Sportscode for advanced coding, Wyscout for scouting, StatsBomb for advanced data, Hudl TV for streaming and Hudl Fundraising for donations. **These are not automatically one subscription or one seamless product.** [Hudl](https://www.hudl.com/newsroom)／[Wyscout](https://www.hudl.com/products/wyscout)／[Fundraising](https://www.hudl.com/blog/hudl-acquires-teamup-gamified-fundraising)。

## 守｜2006年から2026年までの買収は「顧客」と「分析材料」の両方を増やした

The acquisition timeline explains the business: 2015 Sportstec strengthened pro analysis; 2019 Wyscout added scouting and match libraries; 2022 InStat expanded video-data coverage; 2024 StatsBomb added deeper data models. 買収は単なる機能追加でなく、顧客基盤と情報資源の拡張だった。[Sportstec](https://www.hudl.com/blog/hudls-acquisition-of-sportstec-approved)／[Wyscout](https://www.hudl.com/blog/the-inside-view-of-hudls-wyscout-acquisition)／[InStat](https://www.hudl.com/blog/hudl-instat)／[StatsBomb](https://www.hudl.com/blog/hudl-statsbomb-press-release-en)。

In 2025 Hudl acquired Balltime, FastModel and Titan Sports, among others. In July 2026, Sports Business Journal described TeamUp as its 19th acquisition. **Acquisition count is not the same as integration success.** 実務の時間削減や製品間連携は別に検証すべきだ。[Balltime](https://www.hudl.com/blog/hudl-expands-volleyball-focus-through-game-changing-acquisition-of-balltime)／[FastModel](https://www.hudl.com/blog/hudl-acquires-fastmodel-sports)／[SBJ](https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2026/07/08/hudl-makes-19th-acquisition-with-youth-sports-fundraising-firm-teamup/)。

## 破｜最大の変化は「自チームを振り返る」から「他チームも調べる」への拡張にある

Reviewing your own match and scouting somebody else's league are fundamentally different businesses. Wyscout says it covers more than 1,000 competitions, using professional-use rights agreements. **Content rights, not just cameras, become a supply constraint.** [Wyscout FAQ](https://www.hudl.com/products/wyscout/faq)。

Wyscout describes an average of about 2,500 tagged data points per match and full review of at least 25% of matches. AI時代でも、イベントの定義、記録、品質検査は無くならない。Coverage and accuracy may still vary by competition. [Wyscout FAQ](https://www.hudl.com/products/wyscout/faq)。

Wyscout's own site features Urawa Reds as a scouting-use case. これはHudlの全製品導入を意味せず、効果の独立検証でもない。It is a concrete example of the recruitment workflow. [Wyscout](https://www.hudl.com/products/wyscout)。

## 破｜Hudlの収益は分析ソフト単体ではなく、年額契約と周辺サービスの組み合わせで理解する

Hudl's U.S. high-school pricing page lists annual single-program plans at $1,500, $2,500 and $4,000. Differences include storage, scouting data and turnaround time. 部活動全体向けは個別見積もりで、これを世界共通価格やプロ向け単価として使ってはいけない。[公式価格](https://www.hudl.com/pricing/high-school)。

Four revenue categories help: recurring video software, capture hardware and related services, advanced data/scouting, and fan or operational services. **A useful business-model map is not a disclosed revenue breakdown.** 学校向けパッケージが年額請求でも、全社ARRや粗利率は公開資料から復元できない。[公式FAQ](https://www.hudl.com/solutions/high-school/faq)。

## 破｜同じ映像をめぐる競争は、技術力だけでなく権利と乗り換えやすさで決まる

Focus automates recording and upload, making capture-to-review one workflow. The real switching cost may lie in video migration, staff habits, opponent exchange and contracts—not only the camera itself. ただし「必ずロックインされる」と断定するには契約と実装の確認が必要だ。[Focus](https://www.hudl.com/products/focus)／[FAQ](https://www.hudl.com/products/focus/faq/fixed)。

Hudl competes with different players in different jobs: Veo or Pixellot in capture, Catapult-related tools in elite analysis, and Teamworks-related products in operations. **The moat may be the workflow network, not one AI feature.** これは仮説であり、維持率や市場シェアの確認が要る。[市場資料](https://cdn.hl.com/pdf/2025/sports-market-update-hl-fall-2025.pdf)。

## 破｜巨大化への批判は現実にあり、独占の違法性はまだ判決で確定していない

In March 2026, rival QwikCut filed an antitrust lawsuit against Hudl. It alleges bundled discounts and practices that restrict competition. 訴状にある約99％というシェアは**原告の主張**で、確定した認定事実ではない。This article does not treat litigation as a verdict. [Court docket](https://dockets.justia.com/docket/new-jersey/njdce/2%3A2026cv02334/593111)／[Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/tech-and-telecom-law/hudl-accused-of-monopolizing-school-sports-video-analysis-market)。

A separate question is athlete data, especially for minors. Hudl's policies describe AI processing and different rights for customer footage, profiles and highlights. **Uploading video does not automatically mean Hudl owns every right.** 顧客はモデル学習、公開範囲、第三者への共有、契約終了時の扱いを個別に確認したい。[Privacy](https://www.hudl.com/privacy)／[Terms](https://terms.hudl.com/terms)。

## 破｜Synergy SportsをHudl傘下として数えると、競争地図を取り違える

Synergy Sports is **not a Hudl acquisition**. On October 7, 2026, Sportradar announced an agreement to sell its Synergy coaching and scouting business to Teamworks for $170 million. Completion was expected later in 2026; it was not announced as closed in that release. この区別を誤ると競争環境の分析が崩れる。[Sportradar](https://sportradar.com/content-hub/news/sportradar-announces-agreement-for-the-divestiture-of-coaching-and-scouting-business-of-synergy-sports-to-teamworks-innovations/)／[SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1836470/000110465926114201/tm2626951d1_6k.htm)。

The transaction separates team-side analytics from some retained production and computer-vision assets. 同じ映像技術でも、放送・データ配信・賭博関連と、チーム向け分析では買い手が違う。The market boundary should follow customers and rights, not buzzwords.

## 離｜映像が集まるほど価値が増すのか――条件を分解すると別の答えになる

A possible model is a **video-centered workflow platform**: capture creates footage, coding adds metadata, coaches use it, scouts search it, and fans may watch it. But more video is not automatically more value. Rights, discoverability, quality and actual use are necessary conditions.

Three mechanisms are often confused: **network effects** (more users help other users), **switching costs** (migration is difficult), and **learning effects** (repeated use improves performance). 同じ『囲い込み』という言葉で片付けると競争力の源泉を見誤る。

## 離｜参入を考える企業は、分析アルゴリズムより先に利用権と現場業務を調べる

For market entry, start with seven questions: who records, who pays, who owns rights, which workflow is painful, what integrations matter, what renewal terms apply, and what outcome improves. 技術のデモより先に購買構造と権利を確かめる。This is a proposed research method, not a disclosed Hudl playbook.

For a camera or video-technology company, replacing the entire Hudl stack may be the wrong starting point. Better hypotheses include solving hard-to-film scenarios, exporting useful metadata into existing tools, or serving specialized use cases. ただし、権利と連携仕様の確認が前提である。

The story returns to fundraising. Hudl did not simply jump from cameras to donations. It expanded from a routine coaching workflow toward the wider operations of sports organizations. **Whether it becomes a true sports operating system depends on integration, rights and real user value—not the number of acquisitions.**
