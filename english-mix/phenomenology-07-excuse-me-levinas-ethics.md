# 「すみません」の一言で、世界に責任が発生する
## The other arrives before my explanation is complete.

駅を歩いている。

“Excuse me.”

世界にtaskが一個増える。

Turn around. Ignore it. Give directions. Say you do not know.

何を選んでも、**the question of response has already appeared**.

相手の名前も職業も性格も知らない。

I do not understand this person yet, but the relation has already begun.

Levinas takes this order extremely seriously.

**We may be addressed before we fully understand.**

---

## 1. Phenomenology has asked how things appear

ここまで6回、現れ方を見てきた。

- A mug appears with hidden sides.
- エポケーで存在判断を保留し、appearanceを見る。
- A doorknob appears within practical involvement.
- 身体がworldのscaleになる。
- The present carries the just-past and the not-yet.
- 他人の視線が自分自身の現れ方を変える。

Levinas adds a discomfort.

If I only ask “how does the other appear to me?”, do I still risk reducing the other to **something contained inside my understanding**?

相手は、私が分析する対象で終わるのか。

This is where phenomenology bends toward ethics.

---

## 2. Levinas's “face” is not facial-feature analysis

レヴィナスで有名なのが**the face（顔／顔貌）**。

It does not mean measuring eyes, nose, or expression.

Rather, the other cannot be exhausted by a list of features.

“Thirty-something.”
「会社員っぽい」
“In a hurry.”
「感じがよさそう」

These descriptions may tell me something, but not everything.

**The other exceeds my classification.**

For Levinas, face-to-face encounter is not only acquisition of information. It is also a situation in which the other calls for response.

---

## 3. Over-serious experiment: split “excuse me” into half-second intervals

### 0.0 sec
“Excuse me.”

### 0.5 sec
自分宛てかもしれない、とattentionが向く。

### 1.0 sec
You turn. There is another person.

### 1.5 sec
「何だろう」というquestionが立つ。

### 2.0 sec
They ask for directions.

The strange point is that around 0.5–1.0 seconds, before the content is fully known, **you are already not entirely unrelated**.

もちろんdangerを感じるなら離れていい。This is not a rule saying every call must be obeyed.

We are examining a structure: another person's appearance can do more than add one more object to the scene. **It can make my action matter.**

---

## 4. “Ethics as first philosophy” is a large reversal

Philosophy often seems to move in this order: what exists? what can we know? then, how should we live?

Levinas unsettles that sequence.

他者とのrelation, responsibility, responseを、knowledgeの後の応用問題ではなく根本へ置こうとする。

Hence the phrase **ethics as first philosophy**.

This is not “good manners are the highest philosophy.”

The deeper point is that before I finish organizing the world around my own concepts, **there is already someone who is not me**.

他人を完全に分かったことにしない。

Simple sentence. Difficult ethics.

---

## 5. Do not turn Levinas into a universal kindness slogan

レヴィナスには解釈上の論争も批判もある。Face, infinity, responsibilityは日常語そのままではない。

It is also difficult to derive concrete political or institutional rules directly from these concepts.

So “be kind to strangers—that's Levinas” would flatten the philosophy.

このシリーズで残したいのは、もっと小さい違和感。

**Understanding the other and responding to the other may not happen in that order.**

相手を説明できる前に、もう相手はいる。

---

## 6. After seven episodes, the world became more troublesome

At the beginning there was simply a mug on a desk.

Now the mug carries hidden sides.
The doorknob belongs to a network of activity.
身体がworldを測る。
Ten seconds carries past and future.
A stranger on the train is another centre that can see me.

そして最後に、“Excuse me.”

The world is more troublesome than it looked.

It contains meaning, bodies, temporal depth, other perspectives, and demands for response.

Phenomenology may not simplify everyday life.

**It restores complexity that familiarity had deleted.**

だから駅もコップもドアノブも、前より少し変に見える。

That is enough.

Phenomenology does not necessarily give more answers.

**It raises the resolution of the ordinary.**

And higher resolution makes the world slightly harder—and much more interesting.
